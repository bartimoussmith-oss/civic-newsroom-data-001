MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council

Charles W. Bloom, AICP, Planning and Development Director
Subject: Agreement for public safety fees for Saddle Ridge 16th Filing final plat.
Date:
May 24, 2021

Background:

The item for consideration is an agreement for public safety fees associated with the Saddle Ridge 16th
Filing final plat.  The final plat is a separate item on this agenda.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral: May 24, 2021
•
Public Service Committee: June 8, 2021
•
Final consideration: June 14, 2021

On May 3, 2021, the Planning Commission approved a preliminary plat and recommended approval of
an expedited plat subject to specific conditions (as noted on the corresponding plat item also on this
agenda).  Condition 1.c. was as follows:

“Prior to approval of the final plat, the applicant shall: Enter into a development agreement for
public safety fees. Public safety fees shall be based on a January 2021 fee of $756 per unit and
shall be adjusted for inflation utilizing the construction cost index for twenty (20) cities, as
issued by the current Engineering News Record to the date of final plat filing, and this condition
shall be subject to modification by the Governing Body at the time of their consideration.”

This agreement satisfies condition 1.c. For the purpose of establishing the fee staff utilized a fee of $756
per unit that was deemed acceptable by the Governing Body in their recent approval of the Whitney
Range 3rd Filing Development agreement. This fee was adjusted for inflation utilizing the construction
cost index for twenty (20) cities, as issued by the current Engineering News Record to the date of final
plat filing.  The inflation-adjusted fee totals $793.89 per dwelling unit. The total fees for this
development will be $20,641.14 which will be placed in the Development Impact Fund earmarked
for the purpose of enhancing the City’s fire rescue infrastructure.

Staff Recommendation:

Staff recommends the Governing Body approve the agreement as it satisfies preliminary plat condition
1.c.

and shall be adjusted for inflation utilizing the construction cost index for twenty (20) cities,
as issued by the current Engineering News Record to the date of final plat filing;

WHEREAS, public safety fees shall be collected and deposited into the Development

WHEREAS, Developer has agreed to a public safety fee of seven hundred ninety-three

appropriate for the Development.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises of the parties hereto and other
good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of which are hereby acknowledged,
the Parties agree as follows:

1.
Recitals Incorporated.  The foregoing recitals are incorporated herein by reference
into this Agreement as though set forth at length.

2.
The Parties agree to the following public safety fees regarding public safety
improvements:

A. Public safety fees total forty-eight thousand four hundred and twenty-seven
dollars and seven cents ($20,641.05).

B. Public safety fees shall be collected by the City at the time of initial building
permit application within Saddle Ridge 16th Filing final plat within the
Development.  Public safety fees shall be based on subdivision plat
assumptions regarding number of units as provided in subsection A above.

C. Public safety fees shall be deposited into the Development Impact Fund and
segregated for the purpose of public safety and infrastructure.

D. The Developer may, at any time, request a report of the account activity in
the Development Impact Fund and the City Treasurer shall deliver said
report within thirty (30) days.

E. If the City implements a City-wide public safety fee requirement for
residential development with fees due at the time of building permit
application, lots within the Development shall be exempt from fee
requirements. Any funds paid into the Development Impact Fund in excess
of the adopted fee schedule shall be refunded to the Developer within sixty
(60) days of adoption of said public safety fee requirement.  Shall the City
enter into a subsequent development agreement requiring a lessor per unit
fee, the difference of funds paid into the Development Impact Fund in
excess of the subsequent development agreement per unit fee shall be
refunded to the Developer within sixty (60) days of adoption of said public
safety fee requirement. If the City does not implement a City-wide public
