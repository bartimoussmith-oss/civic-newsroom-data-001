MEMO

Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council

Charles Bloom, AICP, Planning and Development Director
Subject: A memorandum of understanding between City of Cheyenne and the Wyoming
Independent Citizen Coalition
Date:
May 23, 2022

Background:

The item for consideration is a Memorandum of
Understanding between the Wyoming Independent Citizen
Coalition, a 501(c)(3) and the City.

The Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition’s mission is to
support consultation, mediation, situational analysis, and
research to address hate-based and civil rights issues in
Wyoming.  In late 2021, representatives approached the
City regarding installation of two murals on the landings of
the Interstate 180 pedestrian bridge, approximately at 7th
Street. The art installation is for the purposes of
celebrating community, culture, and South Side history and
supports their mission. Local artist, Jordan Dean, has been
selected to install the mural in July of this year.

The City will be required to enter into necessary landscape
agreements with the Wyoming Department of
Transportation for the project and the Wyoming
Independent Citizen Coalition will be required to maintain
the display.  The City has no financial obligation regarding
this project, other than limited maintenance necessary on
the bridge. The City is investigating the potential of
installing lighting to illuminate the art.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN CITY OF CHEYENNE, A MUNICIPAL CORPORATION AND
WYOMING INDEPENDENT CITIZEN COALITION
THIS MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT made this ______ day of ___________, 2022,
by and between Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition (Wyoming Independent Citizen
Coalition), a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization, and City of Cheyenne (the City), provides that:

WHEREAS, Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition was formed as a non-profit entity in
2020 with a mission to support consultation, mediation, situational analysis, and research to
address hate-based and civil rights issues in Wyoming; and

WHEREAS, the Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition desires to design, create, and
install two murals (herein referred to as Public Art) on the landings of the Interstate 180 pedestrian
bridge for the purposes of celebrating community and culture to support their mission of which
concepts are generally shown in Exhibit A, comprised of 8 pages, which is attached hereto and
incorporated herein; and

WHEREAS, the City recognizes Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition’s role in the
community and supports their mission; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne recognizes that art is important to the quality of life in
the community and has adopted an Art in Public Places ordinance in Chapter 12.24 of the
municipal code; and

WHEREAS, the City will enter into necessary landscape agreements with the Wyoming
Department of Transportation for said Public Art; and

WHEREAS, the Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition shall be required to maintain the
Public Art; and

WHEREAS, both parties mutually agree that the Public Art celebrates community heritage,
instills civic pride, and beautifies the public realm.

NOW, THEREFORE, the City and Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition in
consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set forth herein hereby agree, covenant, and
represent as follows:

Terms

1.
The City grants permission to Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition to paint,
construct, or install Public Art on each pedestrian landing of the Interstate 180
pedestrian bridge. The Public Art must demonstrate a community and cultural
theme as generally depicted in Exhibit A.

5.
The City reserves the right to inspect Public Art installations during
creation/installation and at the time of completion. Should the City find the
installations do not meet the approved plans or specifications, the City may remove
said art at Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition’s expense.
6.
The City reserves the right to remove any Public Art installed in the right-of-way
for maintenance or other actions the City deems necessary. Removal due to
maintenance or aforementioned actions shall be at the City’s expense. The City will
not be held responsible for replacement of Public Art.

Wyoming Independent Citizen Coalition shall not assign or attempt to assign,
