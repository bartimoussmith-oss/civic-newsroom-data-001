MEMORANDUM
TO:

Governing Body, City of Cheyenne
FROM:

Renee Smith, Economic Resource Administrator
DATE:

NOVEMBER 6, 2024
SUBJECT:
GRANT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND WYOMING OFFICE OF
HOMELAND SECURITY TO SUPPORT EXPENSES AND EQUIPMETN FOR
THE CHEYENNE FIRE RESCUE RERT#7 TEAM

The City’s Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department has been awarded a Wyoming Office of Homeland Security
(WOHS) grant through the US Department of Homeland Security Federal Emergency Management Agency,
Grant Programs Directorate, State Homeland Security Program Grant FY2024.
The Regional Emergency Response Team #7 is working to meet the qualifications of a Federal Emergency
Management Agency Type II hazmat team which requires an increase in training requirements and equipment
needs. RERT #7 requested funds for training to maintain current WMD/CBRNE and hazardous materials
certifications. These funds will cover personnel costs, travel, instructors, and associated backfill. In addition
to training, RERT #7 also included a request for grant funds to maintain existing equipment as well as purchase
new equipment in order to fulfil these training requirements.
WOHS received over $11 million in requests and only had $3.6 million in available funds. The grant award is
$112,651 to support the expenses and equipment costs through a performance period of September 1, 2024 to
August 31, 2026. There is no match requirement for this grant award.
Please contact Renee Smith at rsmith@cheyennecity.org or Deputy Chief Andrew Dykshorn at
adykshorn@cheyennecity.org if you have any questions.
