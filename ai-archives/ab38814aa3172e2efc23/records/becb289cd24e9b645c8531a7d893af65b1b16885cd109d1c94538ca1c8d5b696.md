WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the need for improving the convenience of
life for existing state residents, prospective new residents, and visitors alike;

WHEREAS, one avenue for making daily life more efficient and convenient is to allow
Wyoming residents and visitors to this great state to purchase alcoholic beverages with an alcohol
by volume (ABV) of 5% at grocery stores and convenience stores, which stores can be easier to
locate and more convenient to patronize than retail liquor stores;

WHEREAS the Governing Body believes that the State's current statutory structure
governing the sale of alcoholic beverages creates confusion on the part of Wyoming residents who
cannot comprehend why alcoholic beverages may be sold at certain grocery stores and
convenience stores in the state, but not at all of them;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body further believes that the current statutory structure
governing the sale of alcoholic beverages is stifling economic growth in both larger and smaller
communities because the number of liquor licenses that may be issued is tied to arbitrary
population formulas specified by state statute;

WHEREAS the Governing Body recognizes the value and necessity of local control
regarding the issuance of liquor licenses by cities and towns, which are also charged with funding
and administering emergency services and law enforcement;

WHEREAS the Governing Body recognizes and appreciates the entrepreneurial spirit of
the businesses who apply to the City of Cheyenne seeking a liquor license. However, the current
statutory structure illogically constricts alcoholic beverage sales, placing governing bodies across
the state in a precarious situation of picking “winners and losers” amongst a variety of qualified
candidates. The Governing Body believes this is detrimental and antithetical to a free market
economy; and

WHEREAS the Governing Body has requested that the Wyoming Association of
Municipalities draft a resolution on this topic similar to the one herein, which resolution, among
other things, would also express support for grocery stores and convenience stores being able to
sell alcoholic beverages with an ABV of 5%.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body hereby requests that the Wyoming State
Legislature, and the appropriate legislative committees, review, consider, and adopt modifications
to the current statutory structure governing the sale of alcoholic beverages in the state's city and
