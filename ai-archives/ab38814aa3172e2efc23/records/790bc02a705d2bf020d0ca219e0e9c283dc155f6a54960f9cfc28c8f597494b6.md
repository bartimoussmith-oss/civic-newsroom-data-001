RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 16, 2019

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:30 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Cook, Mr.
Laybourn, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, and Mr. Shanor. Absent:  Mr. Case, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Luna,
and Mr. White.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Alessandra Fakelman, Senior Assistant City
Attorney; Ron Downey, Deputy Human Resources Officer, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, and
Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook live.
Mayor Orr explained it is the governing body’s intention to go into executive session for the
purpose of evaluating proposed remaining sections.

Mayor Orr advised the City is in agreement to sign off on Sections #32, 33, and 44.

Bill Hinz, Union President, advised the Union proposes the following:  Section 4-
agreement with a 2 year contract term, Section 15-add 3 members from 88 to 91 in FY 2020 and
3 additional from 91 to 94 in FY 2021, increasing shift levels from 26 to 27 for FY 2020 and from
27 to 28 for FY 2021, all beginning on July 1st of each year, Section 17-remain an open working
section, Section 18-accept City’s language amendment, Section 20-Present Contract Language
(PCL), Section 21-reducing work hours per week from 56 to 52 and 3.5% wage increases for both
FY 2020 and 2021 beginning July 1st of each year, Section 22-request 480 hours of E.O.D
compensatory time instead of 240 hours, Section 23 letter F-changing the acting roster for Day
Personnel positions to four years instead of three, and in Section 27-add Battalion Chief of the
Shift in Section G and adjust vacation hours in Section J, and Section 28-awarding day personnel
the same amount of sick time as line personnel 960 working hours.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Roybal moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body
present, the City Clerk, Senior Assistant City Attorney, City Treasurer, and Chief of the Fire and
Rescue Department would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio
recording of the special meeting was stopped; executive Session convened at 6:52 p.m. in the
Mayor’s Conference Room]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:56 p.m.  Mayor Orr advised the City proposes the following:  Section #4-leave open as a working
document, Section #15-decline and counter with PCL, Section #18-accept the change of battalion
Chief or designee and add “earned benefit” in the last sentence following the word “money”,
