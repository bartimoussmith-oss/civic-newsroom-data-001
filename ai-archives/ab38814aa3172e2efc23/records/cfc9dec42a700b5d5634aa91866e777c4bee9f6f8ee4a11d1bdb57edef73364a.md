Development District boundary. The Downtown Development District is described in section 2.76.040 of the
municipal code.
The procedure for inclusion of additional land in the district is outlined in Wyoming Statute § 15-9-223
which reads as follows:
“15-9-223. Inclusion of additional property into development area; procedure.
Subsequent to the organization of an authority, additional property may be included in the
development area. Proceedings for inclusion shall be initiated by petition to the board of the
authority signed by the owner or owners in fee of each parcel of land adjacent to the existing
area sought to be included. The petition shall include evidence satisfactory to the board
concerning title to the property and an accurate legal description thereof. If the board approves
the application, it shall submit the application to the governing body of the municipality. If the
governing body also approves the application, it shall then, at a regular or special meeting, by
amendment to the ordinance treating the authority, redescribe the development area so as to
include the additional property as described in the petition. From the eﬀective date of the
amendment the additional property shall be included within the development area and shall be
subject to any taxes thereafter imposed by the municipality for the use and beneﬁt of the
authority.”
With the redescription of the Downtown Development District, staﬀ intends to draft a legal description including
all adjacent rights-of-way to the existing district and added properties. This would include all adjacent rights-of-
way including but not limited to House Avenue, Central Avenue on the South Side, etc. This would allow DDA
funds to be used on infrastructure improvements within the adjacent rights-of-way.
Governing Body Review Schedule:
This item is considered in the form of an ordinance amending the municipal code to redescribe the Downtown
Development District area. This requires three readings by the Governing Body and referral to Finance
Committee twice.
•
Governing Body introduction of the vacation and expansion request: August 12th at 6:00 pm in Council
Chambers.
•
Council Committee: August 19th at noon at the City Building, room 104.
•
Second Reading: August 26th at 6:00 pm in Council Chambers.
•
Council Committee: September 3rd at noon at the City Building, room 104.
•
Final Reading: September 9th at 6:00 pm in Council Chambers.
Impact and Beneﬁts:
Inclusion of these properties can stimulate economic activities, attract new businesses, and create jobs within
the expanded district. The expansion can lead to infrastructure upgrades that the DDA can help fund through the
Capital Improvement Grant, enhancing the area's overall functionality and appeal. The inclusion supports the
DDA's goal of fostering a vibrant downtown community, contributing to a cohesive and thriving urban
environment. Expanding the district aligns with the long-term strategic vision, promoting orderly and planned
development.
