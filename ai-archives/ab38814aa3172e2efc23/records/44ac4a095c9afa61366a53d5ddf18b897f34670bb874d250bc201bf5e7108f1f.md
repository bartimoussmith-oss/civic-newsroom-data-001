PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
for
SANITATION REROUTING AND OPTIMIZATION PROJECT

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and
between the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
(“City”), and Univerus, Inc., located at 514 Kennett Pike, Chadds Ford, Pennsylvania 19317
(“Consultant”).  This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature
affixed hereto.

RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by
and between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the Sanitation Rerouting and
Optimization Project, in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal
(RFP) #4-24, incorporated herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted
proposal, incorporated herein by reference.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by January 31, 2025.

3.
COMPENSATION
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the
Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed amount
of One Hundred Forty-Two Thousand, Six Hundred Seventy-Six Dollars
($142,676.00) as stated in Exhibit A – Price Proposal.

4.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services
not meeting such a standard.

5.
COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.

corporation.
