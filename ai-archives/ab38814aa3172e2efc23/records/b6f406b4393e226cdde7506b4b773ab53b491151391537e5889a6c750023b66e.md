SUMMARY:
The applicant is requesting a zone change from Community Business (CB) to Heavy Industrial (HI) for a
vacant 2.15 acre sitelocated at the eastern edge of the City, north of Interstate 80, at the Campstool
Road and Interstate 80interchange.Currently the parcelis located within a Community Business zone
district(CB)but is surrounded by a Heavy Industrial zone district (HI) to the north, west and southeast
and a Light Industrial zone district (LI) to the southwest. Other CB zone districts are sprinkled
throughout the near vicinity.The subject property and surrounding vicinity are already platted and
established with streets, lots and blocks and arelargely served by necessary infrastructure such as
utilities, sewer and storm drainage.Other land uses in the area include a Caterpillar Dealership, Old
Dominion Freight Company, a John Deere equipment supply, a Pilot Travel Center and a hotel.The
majority of the CB zoned property in the area is vacant. The applicant is proposing to develop an
impoundment yard and truck washing facility on the site. Impoundment yards are only permitted in the
HI zone district and only after conditional discretionary review by the Board of Adjustment.In order to
enable the development of this use the applicant is requesting a zone change from CB to HI.If the zone
change is granted, the application would still require a conditional use approval by the Board of
Adjustment.TheHI district is intended for uses ofveryhigh intensity which should be isolated and

granted a zone change from HI to CB in anticipation of developing a commercial use. However, the
