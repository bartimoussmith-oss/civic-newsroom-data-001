MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
Date:
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, THE LARAMIE CO’
CONSERVATION DISTRICT, ROTARY CLUBS OF CHEYENNE,
AND FROG CREEK PARTNERS, LLC
1.
Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is made and entered into by and
between the City ofCheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation (the “City”), the Laramie County
Conservation District, a Wyoming Conservation District (“LCCD”), Rotary Clubs of Cheyenne
(“Rotary”), and Frog Creek Partners, LLC and its agents (“Frog Creek Partners”). The City, the
LCCD, Rotary and Frog Creek Partners are, at times, individually referred to as a “Party” and
collectively referred to as the “Parties.” The Parties’ addresses are set forth in Section 8(i) below.
2.
Purpose. Stormwater pollution is directly affecting water quality in Crow Creek, a perennial
stream listed for sediment and F. coli on the Wyoming Department of Environment Quality
(WDEQ) 303(d) impaired streams list. The LCCD and Rotary have purchased twelve (12) retrofit
Gutter Bin filtration systems (“Gutter Bins”) from Frog Creek Partners with a plan to install them
downtown in the Capitol Basin (the “Property” as shown in Exhibit A). The purpose of this MOU
is to set forth the City’s terms and conditions by which the LCCD, Rotary and Frog Creek Partners
may conduct installation and maintenance of the Gutter Bins (the “Project”).
3.
Term of MOU. This MOU shall commence upon the date last signed and executed by the
duly authorized representatives of the Parties to this MOU and shall remain in full force and effect
until the Project is completed, unless terminated earlier pursuant to Section 8(b) below.
4.
Payment. No payment shall be made by any Party to any other Party as a result of this
MOU.
5.
Responsibilities of the City.
a.
Subject to the conditions set forth in this MOU, the City permits the LCCD, Rotary
and Frog Creek Partners to enter upon the Property subject to this MOU for purposes ofconducting
installation and maintenance of the Project.
b.
The City agrees to consult with the LCCD, Rotary and Frog Creek Partners on
matters relating to the Project.
c.
The City must approve any and all final design plans relating to the Project.
d.
The City will clean all stormwater catch basins in which Gutter Bins are to be
installed prior to installation and on a schedule to be mutually agreed upon and weather permitting.
Cleaning of catch basins shall not unreasonably delay the Project.
e.
The City will accept captured pollutants from the Project at the City of Cheyenne
Landfill located at 1461 Happy Jack Road at no cost to the other Parties. The pollutants will meet
federal and state regulation for disposal and comply with Wyoming Department of Environmental
Quality Happy Jack Landfill Lifetime Permit SHWD File #10.080.
