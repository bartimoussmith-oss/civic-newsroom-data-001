MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Richard Kaysen, and City Council
FROM:

Sara Vasquez, Asst. Director of Purchasing
DATE:

March 29, 2013
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #M-10-13, for a contract for the Snyder Avenue
Reconstruction project between the City of Cheyenne, Engineering Department,
and Simon Contractors, Cheyenne, WY.

Three (3) bids were received for this project.  The bid tab is shown below

Bidder

Total Bid____________

Simon Contractors.

Base: $5,821,597.20
Cheyenne, WY

Reiman Corp.

Base: $6,321,417.30
Cheyenne, WY

S&S Builders LLC.

Base: $6,563,926.32
Cheyenne, WY

The bids include $125,000.00 for a “miscellaneous force account”, and $30,000.00 for Railroad
Insurance.

The bids were reviewed by Sam Berta, and his recommendation is to accept the low base bid and
alternate A, from Simon Contractors or Cheyenne, WY, for a total bid amount of $5,669,859.20.
Alternate A replaces some of the items in the base bid at a lower cost.

The engineer’s estimate of the base bid cost of the project was $5,352,132.52

Funding will be: 021-26-2616-42-43436 (1% Sales Tax 11-14)
