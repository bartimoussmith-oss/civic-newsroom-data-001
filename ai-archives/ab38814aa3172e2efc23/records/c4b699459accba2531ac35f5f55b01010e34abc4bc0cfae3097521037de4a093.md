UDC-22-00004
February 14, 2022
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

UDC 2.4.2 Annexations

PlanCheyenne

BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting annexing of 48.6 acres of land for future development  most likely residential.
The County Assessor presently shows the tract of land as a portion of a platted  lot, one ownership parcel.
The land is currently developed in the County with one single-family home. A zone change is proposed
concurrently with this annexation. Both applications are anticipated to come to final consideration before the
Governing Body at the same meeting.

Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. The petition to annex has been accompanied by a submission
to change the zoning from County MU Mixed Use to the City AG Agricultural zone. The purpose of this
annexation is to allow future urban development
 future zone change and
platting action is anticipated to create urban development patterns.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned MU Mixed Use in the County. The property is concurrently proposed to
be annexed and rezoned to the City AG Agricultural zone. The City AG zone is intended for low-intensity
operations, including agriculture, and is appropriate as a holding zone until such time urban
development patterns are finalized. Future development is anticipated to be largely residential with
possibly a mix of commercial uses (according to the comprehensive plan designation for the area).

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

To accept the petition to annex and approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make
the following findings required in WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below:

1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of the

UDC-22-00004
February 14, 2022
The annexation of this property will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the occupants of
future development once it is built. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to
City Fire and Police and sanitation services. The property, if occupied, will also be required to be
connected to City sanitary sewer instead of having septic systems.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a natural,
geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (ii)]

This property, once developed, will constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part
of the city. For all intents, the property is surrounded by the City (the only non-annexed area is
the railroad right-of-way). The contiguity on three sides and the proximity to surrounding urban-
style development makes the parcel a natural geographic part of the City. The development of
this property will benefit those who live within the City and will be a part of the social and
economic fabric that is Cheyenne.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city or town
and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of the city or
town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a)
(iii)]

The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection
will be extended upon annexation. City sewer lines exist in the vicinity and both water and
sewer lines would likely be extended with the development of the property.

The application complies with this review criterion.

4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or town, or
the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iv)]

The area proposed for annexation is about 75.1% contiguous, along all but the eastern edge.

The application complies with this review criterion.

5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the annexed area pursuant
to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)]

Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.

The application complies with this review criterion.

required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all landowners and affected public
utilities within the territory a summary of the proposed annexation report as required under

CONFORMANCE WITH APPLICABLE PLANS:

The project is located in an area covered by the following plans:

Plan Cheyenne

Policies within PlanCheyenne encourage the maximization of existing infrastructure and the reduction of
County pockets. This property is near existing urban infrastructure (although water lines would require
significant extension), and the annexation would effectively eliminate a County pocket.

The Future Land Use Map of PlanCheyenne, as updated by Connect 2045, anticipates this area of the
community to develop with a mix of urban residential and lower-intensity commercial uses. These areas
are expected to develop with full access to urban services  such areas are best served by being within
City limits.

STAFF REVIEW:

Through the review process, the following comments were received:
-
New septic systems will not be allowed.
-
Water and sewer are not immediately adjacent to this parcel and extensions will be needed
with development.
-
Annexation will require future development to comply with City-adopted regulations

CONCLUSION:

Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria and therefore recommends approval of
the annexation.

The proposed annexation is a natural extension of the City, is contiguous to the current City limits, and
most services typically available to urban areas are available to this property.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends approval of the annexation of the property.
