ORDINANCE NO. ___________
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 12.04.010, OBSTRUCTION ON
STREETS,
AND
SECTION
12.04.080,
CONSTRUCTION
AND
REPAIR–PERMITS REQUIRED–FEES–EXCEPTION, OF CHAPTER 12.04,
STREETS, SIDEWALKS AND DRIVEWAYS GENERALLY, OF TITLE 12,
STREETS, SIDEWALKS AND PUBLIC PLACES, OF THE CODE OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1.  That Section 12.04.010, Obstruction on streets, of Chapter 12.04, Streets, Sidewalks
and Driveways Generally, of Title 12, Streets, Sidewalks and Public Places, of the Code of the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, is amended as follows:
12.04.010
Obstructions on streets, ALLEYS, SIDEWALKS, OR DRIVEWAYS WITHIN
THE
PUBLIC
RIGHT-OF-WAY–DESIGNATION
OF
NUISANCE–
PROSECUTION AS A MISDEMEANOR.
A.
Except as otherwise provided by this code SECTION or other city ordinance
PROVISION OF THIS CODE, no person shall erect, maintain or in any way cause an obstruction of
any character in or upon any street, alley, or sidewalk OR DRIVEWAY within ANY PUBLIC
RIGHT-OF-WAY OF the city.
B.
A PERSON MAY OBSTRUCT A STREET, ALLEY, SIDEWALK OR
DRIVEWAY WITHIN A PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY TO THE EXTENT PERMITTED BY A
RIGHT-OF-WAY ENCROACHMENT PERMIT ISSUED BY THE CITY ENGINEER
PURSUANT TO SECTION 12.04.080, SECTION 12.04.150, SECTION 12.04.230, OR SECTION
12.12.010 OF THIS CODE.
C.
A PERSON MAY OBSTRUCT A STREET, ALLEY, SIDEWALK OR
DRIVEWAY WITHIN A PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY FOR THE STORAGE OR PLACEMENT OF
MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT, OR OTHER TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY THEREIN TO
THE EXTENT PERMITTED BY A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT ISSUED BY THE
CITY ENGINEER.
D.
THE OBSTRUCTION OF ANY STREET, ALLEY, SIDEWALK OR DRIVEWAY
WITHIN A PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY BY THE STORAGE OR PLACEMENT OF MATERIALS,
EQUIPMENT, OR OTHER TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY THEREIN WITHOUT
HAVING OBTAINED A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT FROM THE CITY ENGINEER,
OR FOLLOWING THE EXPIRATION OF SUCH A PERMIT, SHALL CONSTITUTE A
NUISANCE AND A MISDEMEANOR PUNISHABLE IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE
PENALTY PROVISIONS OF SECTION 1.24.010 OF THIS CODE.
E.
THE OBSTRUCTION OF ANY STREET, ALLEY, SIDEWALK OR DRIVEWAY
WITHIN A PUBLIC RIGHT-OF-WAY BY THE STORAGE OR PLACEMENT OF MATERIALS,
EQUIPMENT OR OTHER TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY THEREIN IN ANY MANNER
WHICH VIOLATES THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE
PERMIT ISSUED BY THE CITY ENGINEER IN ACCORDANCE WITH CHAPTER 12.04 OF
THIS CODE SHALL CONSTITUTE A NUISANCE AND A MISDEMEANOR PUNISHABLE IN
ACCORDANCE WITH THE PENALTY PROVISIONS OF SECTION 1.24.010 OF THIS CODE.
F.
THE PERFORMANCE OF ANY CONSTRUCTION, EXCAVATION, OR OTHER
WORK WITHIN OR UPON ANY SIDEWALK, CURB AND GUTTER, DRIVEWAY,
ROADWAY
OR
STREET
WITHOUT
HAVING
OBTAINED
A
RIGHT-OF-WAY
ENCROACHMENT PERMIT FROM THE CITY ENGINEER, OR FOLLOWING THE
EXPIRATION OF A SUCH A PERMIT, SHALL CONSTITUTE A NUISANCE AND A
MISDEMEANOR PUNISHABLE IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PENALTY PROVISIONS OF
SECTION 1.24.010 OF THIS CODE.

G.
THE PERFORMANCE OF ANY CONSTRUCTION, EXCAVATION, OR OTHER
WORK WITHIN OR UPON ANY SIDEWALK, CURB AND GUTTER, DRIVEWAY,
ROADWAY OR STREET IN ANY MANNER WHICH VIOLATES THE TERMS AND

FOR THE PURPOSES OF CHAPTER 12.04 OF THIS CODE, THE PHRASE
“MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT OR OTHER TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY” SHALL NOT
INCLUDE ANY “MOTOR VEHICLE” OR “VEHICLE” AS DEFINED IN WYO. STAT.
SECTION 31-1-101(A); NOR SHALL THE PHRASE INCLUDE REFUSE CONTAINERS
SMALLER THAN TWO (2) CUBIC YARDS IN SIZE PLACED IN ACCORDANCE WITH
CHAPTER 8.44 OF THIS CODE.
Section
2.

That
Section
12.04.080,
Construction
and
repair–Permits
required–Fees–Exception, of Chapter 12.04, Streets, Sidewalks and Driveways Generally, of Title
12, Streets, Sidewalks and Public Places, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is
amended as follows:
12.04.080
Construction, and repair, AND STORAGE IN RIGHT-OF-WAY–Permits
required– Fees–Exception.
A.
RIGHT-OF-WAY ENCROACHMENT PERMIT.  No person may construct,
remove or repair any sidewalk, curb and gutter, driveway, roadway or street, or perform any
EXCAVATION OR other work within the public right-of-way without first obtaining a right-of-
way encroachment permit.  Right-of-way encroachment permit and plan review fees for work in the
public right-of-way shall be based on the valuation of the work and paid in accordance with the
schedule of permit fees contained in Section 15.08.030.  If the work to be done in the right-of-way
is in conjunction with a building permit, a separate right-of-way encroachment permit is required.
B.
Exception. None, unless waived specifically by the city engineer.
B.
RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT.  THE CITY ENGINEER MAY ISSUE A
RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT FOR THE STORAGE OR PLACEMENT OF
MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT OR OTHER TANGIBLE PERSONAL PROPERTY THEREIN
SUBJECT TO SUCH TERMS AND CONDITIONS AS THE CITY ENGINEER MAY
DETERMINE TO BE NECESSARY FOR THE PROTECTION OF THE PUBLIC HEALTH AND
SAFETY.  AN APPLICANT FOR A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT SHALL COMPLETE
AN APPLICATION FORM PRESCRIBED BY THE CITY ENGINEER.  THE CITY ENGINEER
SHALL CHARGE OF FEE OF SEVEN DOLLARS AND FIFTY CENTS ($7.50) FOR EACH
DAY, OR PART OF A DAY, THAT THE PERMIT WILL BE IN EFFECT, BEGINNING WITH
THE DATE OF ISSUANCE THROUGH THE DATE OF TERMINATION STATED ON THE
PERMIT.
1.
THE CITY ENGINEER SHALL NOT HAVE THE AUTHORITY TO
ISSUE A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT TO AN APPLICANT WHO HAS
RECEIVED A RIGHT-OF-WAY STORAGE PERMIT AT ANY TIME DURING THE
PERIOD OF FIFTEEN (15) DAYS PRIOR TO DATE OF APPLICATION FOR A NEW
PERMIT, IF THE NEW PERMIT WOULD ALLOW THE STORAGE OR PLACEMENT
OF ANY MATERIALS, EQUIPMENT, OR OTHER TANGIBLE
PERSONAL
PROPERTY AT A LOCATION WITHIN THREE HUNDRED (300) FEET OF THE
PERMITTED LOCATION UNDER THE PRIOR PERMIT.
C.
EACH PERMIT ISSUED PURSUANT TO THIS SECTION SHALL BE
CONSPICUOUSLY POSTED AT THE SITE DESIGNATED IN THE PERMIT DURING THE
ENTIRE PERIOD THAT SAID PERMIT IS EFFECTIVE.
