RESOLUTION NO. ________________
ENTITLED :
 “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY TREASURER TO DISCHARGE UNCOLLECTIBLE
DEBTS IN THE AMOUNT OF $43,607.37 PURSUANT TO WYO STAT. §16-4-502.”

WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat.§16-4-502 provides for discharge of uncollectible debts upon proper
certification to the Governing Body; and
WHEREAS, the City Treasurer has prepared the required certification for uncollectible debts on
the City’s book, a copy of which is attached to and incorporated as part of this Resolution; and

WHEREAS, the debts have been reviewed by the appropriate City staff and collection efforts
have failed; and
WHEREAS, the City desires to remove the uncollectible debts from its books as expediently as
possible.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING, that the City Treasurer is authorized to discharge the uncollectible debts in the amount of
$43,607.37 from its books.
THIS RESOLUTION shall be in full force and effect from the after its passage in accordance with
the laws of the State of Wyoming.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _____ day of _______________, 2018.

____________________________________

Marian J. Orr, Mayor

(SEAL)
Attest:

______________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
