TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager

SUBJECT:
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Waste
Management of Colorado, Inc., for the Recycling Services Project
______________________________________________
***NOTE: This agreement was previously approved by the Governing Body on April 11, 2022.
At the time the agreement was presented to the Consultant, their legal team requested that an
Addendum be attached/included with the PSA.  As this was not included at the time it was
originally presented to the Governing Body, it is being brought forward again in its amended
form for consideration***
One (1) Responsive Proposal was submitted under Request for Proposal (RFP) #7-22, for the
Recycling Services Project.

The proposal was received from:
1. Waste Management of Colorado, Inc.,  Fort Collins, Colorado.

Upon review of the one (1) proposal by the evaluation panel (consisting of 3 members), the
evaluation panel has recommended acceptance of the proposal submitted by Waste Management
of Colorado, Inc., for a project award amount not-to-exceed $200,000.00.  This Agreement is based
on actual quantities recycled, and is billed in accordance with the Fee Schedule attached as Exhibit
A, to the Professional Services Agreement.  If future Contract renewals are executed after the
original term of the Agreement ends, additional funds may need to be added as well.

Basis for award was based on a Qualifications based review and scoring of proposals.  Waste
Management of Colorado, Inc., submitted the highest scoring proposal, therefore their sealed
pricing envelope was opened & reviewed.

Funding:
023-14-1423-43-21301 (Solid Waste Fund / Professional Services)

$200,000.00

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
for
RECYCLING SERVICES (RFP 7-22)

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and between
the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and
WASTE MANAGEMENT OF COLORADO, INC., located at 5887 S. TAFT HILL ROAD,
FORT COLLINS, COLORADO 80526 (“Consultant”).  This Agreement shall become
effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto.

RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by and
between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the above-mentioned project,
in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal (RFP) #7-22, incorporated
herein by reference, the Consultant’s submitted proposal, incorporated herein by
reference, and Exhibit B – Addendum to Professional Services Agreement for
Recycling Services.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by April 15, 2023.

3.
COMPENSATION
This is a unit price, not-to-exceed Agreement.  The City shall pay for actual services
rendered.  In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement,
the Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed
amount of Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000.00) as stated in Exhibit A - Fee
Schedule.

4.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services
not meeting such a standard.

This Addendum (the “Addendum”) is incorporated into City Contract No. ________
(Professional Services Agreement for Recycling Services) (the “Contract”).

1. QUANTITY AND QUALITY: During the term of the Contract, WM shall accept and
City agrees to deliver City Single Stream Materials. City Single Stream Materials
means materials collected by or on behalf of City from residents and entities in the
City which have been source-separated from solid waste for the purpose of being
recycled. Single Stream Materials includes both Recyclables and Non-Recyclables,
as defined in Exhibit A of this Addendum. City will provide the Single Stream Materials
in accordance with Exhibit A (“Specifications”).  Single Stream Materials specifically
exclude, and City agrees not to deposit or permit the deposit for collection of, any
waste tires, radioactive, volatile, corrosive, flammable, explosive, biomedical,
infectious, bio-hazardous, regulated medical or hazardous waste, toxic substance or
material, as defined by, characterized or listed under applicable federal, state, or local
laws or regulations, or chemical or other properties that are deleterious or capable of
causing material damage to any part of WM's property, its personnel or the public or
materially impair the strength or the durability of WM's structures or equipment, or any
materials containing information (in hard copy or electronic format, or otherwise) which
information is protected or regulated under any local, state or federal privacy or data
security laws, including, but not limited to the Health Insurance Portability and
Accountability Act of 1996, as amended, or other regulations or ordinances or other
waste not approved in writing by WM (collectively, “Excluded Materials”).  Title to and
liability for Excluded Materials shall remain with City at all times. Title to Single Stream
Materials provided by City to WM is transferred to WM upon WM’s receipt or collection
unless otherwise provided in this Agreement or applicable law.  WM reserves the right
at its sole discretion upon notice to City to discontinue acceptance of any category of
