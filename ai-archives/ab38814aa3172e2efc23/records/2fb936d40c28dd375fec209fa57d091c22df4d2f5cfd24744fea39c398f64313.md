WHEREAS, it is in the best interest of the City of Cheyenne to establish a uniform
procedure for its departments and workforce, including a delineation of fees, costs, and charges for
inspecting, copying, and producing public records; and
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Public Records Act, Wyoming Statutes §* 16-4-201 through 16-
4-205, provides authority and sets forth procedures for the City to follow with regard to responding
to requests for public records; and
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 16-4-204(c) requires any fees or charges assessed by a
custodian of public records to “first be authorized by duly enacted or adopted statute, rule,
resolution, ordinance, executive order or other like authority”; and
WHEREAS, the following definitions, procedures and fees shall apply when City staff
receives and responds to requests for public records:
1.

d.
“Electronic/digital public record” is a public record that is primarily or solely

e.

A request for public records shall be submitted in writing by the applicant either on
forms provided by the custodian or in a written format that contains sufficient information for the
custodian to determine the completeness of the information being request. A request may also be
submitted via electronic communication (Le. e-mail) or facsimile transmission.
b.
The custodian shall acknowledge in writing receipt of the request within seven (7)
business days of the request.

Production and Construction Costs. Pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 16-4-202(d)(i),
