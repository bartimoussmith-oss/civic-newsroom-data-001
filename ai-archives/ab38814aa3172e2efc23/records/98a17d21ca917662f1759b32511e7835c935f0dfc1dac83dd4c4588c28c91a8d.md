cover the Committee'scost toproduce the Calcutta.Proceeds from the CBR Calcutta will be
paid as follows: 85% to the bettors; 10% to bona fide charities and 5% to cover expenses.

action is required byyou, we would be happy to discuss the Calcutta with youif desired.During
the last twenty-twoyears, approximately $187,377 hasbeen distributed to Cheyenne area
charities from these Calcuttas.
