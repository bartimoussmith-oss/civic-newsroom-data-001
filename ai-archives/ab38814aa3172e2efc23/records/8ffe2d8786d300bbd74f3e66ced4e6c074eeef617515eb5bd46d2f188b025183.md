RESOLUTION NO. _____________

ENTITLED:  "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE ALL
NECESSARY DOCUMENTS RELATED TO THE TRANSFER OF A
SPECIFIED PORTION OF THE PROPERTY LOCATED AT 2014 DELL
RANGE BOULEVARD."

WHEREAS, on or about April 24, 1961, through the execution of a warranty deed: Frank
M. Cole and F. Louise C. Cole, husband and wife; Edward G. Cole, a single person; and Lester L.
Cole, a single person (the "Cole Family") conveyed to the City of Cheyenne (the "City") title to
2014 Dell Range Boulevard (the "Dell Range Boulevard property"), previously used by the City
for Fire Station No. 5 beginning with the construction of Fire Station No. 5 in 1963. The warranty
deed is attached and incorporated herein as Exhibit A;

WHEREAS, the Cole Family conveyed the Dell Range Boulevard property to the City
subject to a deed restriction, which prohibited the City from holding the property should it "ever
cease to be used" by the City "as a fire station". (See Exhibit A, at p.1);

WHEREAS, the conveyance by the Cole Family also included a reversionary clause
whereby if the City should cease to use the Dell Range Boulevard property as a fire station, "the
conveyed premises shall revert to the grantors, or their heirs." (See Exhibit A, at p.1);

WHEREAS, as a result of the 2021 November election and through the use of the voluntary
one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax, voters in Laramie County approved the use
of taxpayer funds for the relocation and replacement of three (3) fire stations, including Fire Station
No. 5;

WHEREAS, on or around March 21, 2024, the City dedicated a newly built Fire Station
#5 at 4200 Converse Avenue, which replaced the previous First Station #5 at 2014 Dell Range
Boulevard;

WHEREAS, following the March 21, 20024 dedication of the new Fire Station #5, the City
has formally moved Fire Station #5 to 4200 Converse Boulevard and now ceases to use the 2014
Dell Range Boulevard property as a fire station;

WHEREAS, because the City ceases to use the Dell Range Boulevard property as a fire
station, the City acknowledges that the property should revert back to the Cole Family under the
April 24, 1961 Warranty Deed; and

WHEREAS, to effectuate the conveyance of the specified portion of the Dell Range
Boulevard property to the Cole Family, the City intends to execute a Warranty Deed to the Cole
Family conveying fee simple title to the specified portion of the Dell Range Boulevard property.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor is authorized to execute all necessary documents
3:41 pm, Jun 05 2024
