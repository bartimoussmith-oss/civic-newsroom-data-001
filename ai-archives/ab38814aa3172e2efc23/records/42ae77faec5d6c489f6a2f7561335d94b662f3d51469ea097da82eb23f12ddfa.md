MEMORANDUM OF
UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
LARAMIE COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT NUMBER ONE AND THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE

1.
Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made and entered into
by and between Laramie County School District Number One ("District"), whose address
is 2810 House Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the City of Cheyenne, a
municipality organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, acting
through its Recreation Division ("City"), whose address is 1317 Parsley Blvd, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82001.

2.
Purpose. The purpose of this MOU is to provide transportation service for the
Cheyenne Parks and Recreation “REC Camp Program” by leasing school buses from the
Laramie County School District Number One.

3.
Term of MOU. This MOU shall, when approved and executed by the parties
hereto, remain in full force and effect from June 6, 2022, through August 12, 2022. The
payment and indemnification provisions of this MOU shall survive the termination
hereof.

4.
Payment. Upon receipt of an itemized invoice from the District and on or before
September 30, 2022, the City agrees to pay the District for the costs of transportation for
the duration of this MOU. Such costs shall include, but not necessarily be limited to
maintenance and depreciation, drivers’ salary and benefits, fuel, and disinfection supplies
as required by District COVID-19 protocols. Billing will include the following:

A. $2.27/mile to cover maintenance, depreciation, and administrative costs.

B.  Driver salary and benefits will be actual costs. The City will be responsible for
hourly wages and benefits for the drivers’ personal break time, as long as said break times
do not exceed legal limits.

C.  Fuel cost will be the average price for the month of fuel sold to the District
Transportation Department.

D. $.50 per gallon of Wex-cide disinfectant (includes cleaning rags and containers),
if required.

5.
Responsibilities of District.   The District shall furnish a maximum of four buses with
a minimum capacity of 72 passengers and a maximum of four drivers to operate the buses.
In case of emergency, bus drivers shall remain with the buses at all times.

By Stefanie Boster at 12:35 pm, May 11, 2022

6.
Qualification of Drivers. For the term of this MOU, the District shall ensure the
buses under this MOU are driven by qualified District personnel who possess valid
commercial driver's licenses for the type of vehicle operated and have passed a Wyoming
Division of Criminal Investigation background verification.

7.
Special Provisions.

A.
The City shall add the designated buses to the City’s existing automobile
insurance policies and will cover the buses for liability and property damage. It is expressly
agreed and understood that the City’s insurance policy is primary, not contributory. The City
shall name the District as an additional insured/loss payee on the City’s automobile
insurance policy and furnish the District with a certificate of insurance evidencing such
coverage.

B.
It is expressly agreed and understood that the City will fully release,
indemnify, and hold harmless the District and its officers, agents, employees, successors
and assigns from any and all causes of action, claims, liabilities, demands, or expenses
arising out of the use of the District school buses under this MOU.

C.
It is expressly agreed and understood that the City will be responsible for
the conduct of students being transported to City programs and will assume disciplinary
responsibility in the event of misconduct.

D.
It is expressly agreed upon and understood that, should they be in place,
District, county, state, and CDC measures and/or restrictions related to the COVID-19
pandemic will be followed by all drivers designated by the District to operate District buses
under the terms of this MOU. Said measures and restrictions may include, but are not limited
to, wearing personal protective equipment (PPE), restricting passenger numbers, social
distancing, frequent disinfection of equipment, and in-state/out-of-state. The City will
develop and furnish to the District, a transportation plan that is aligned and in compliance
with the District reopening plan and the Wyoming Department of Education’s Smart Start
plan.

8.
General Provisions.

A.
Amendments. Any party may request changes in this MOU. Any changes,
modifications, revisions, or amendments to this MOU which are mutually agreed upon
by and between parties to this MOU shall be incorporated by written instrument,
executed, and signed by all parties to this MOU.

B.
Applicable Law. The construction, interpretation and enforcement of this
MOU shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State of
Wyoming shall have jurisdiction over any action arising out this MOU and over the
parties, and the venue shall be the First Judicial District Court, Laramie County,
Wyoming.

Sovereign/Governmental Immunity. Laramie County School District

MOU, and each fully retains all immunities and defenses provided by law with respect

Third Partv Beneficiary Rights. The parties do not intend to create in
any other individual or entity the status of third-party beneficiary, and this MOU shall
not be construed so as to create such status. The rights, duties and obligations
contained in this MOU shall operate only between the parties to this MOU and shall
inure solely to the benefit of the parties to this MOU. The provisions of this MOU
are intended only to assist the parties in determining and performing their
obligations under this MOU. The parties to this MOU intend and expressly agree that
only parties signatory to this MOU shall have any legal or equitable right to seek to
enforce this MOU, to seek any remedy arising out of a party's performance or failure
to perform any term or condition of this MOU, or to bring an action for the breach
of this MOU.
