RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE GOVERNING
BODY FOR ACCEPTANCE OF REAL PROPERTY FROM STEPHANIE
WHITE"

WHEREAS, Stephanie White, through her corporation, Dozzer, LLC, is engaged in the
business of developing and redeveloping real estate;

WHEREAS, on November 16, 2022, the City of Cheyenne ("City") and Dozzer, LLC filed
a joint application for an Expedited Plat proposing the Back 40 Subdivision, Third Filing,
Cheyenne, Wyoming ("Subdivision");

WHEREAS, on February 27, 2023, the Governing Body adopted Resolution No. 6307
approving the Subdivision;

WHEREAS, approval of the Subdivision created Lot 1, Block 1 ("Lot") inadvertently
creating joint ownership between the City and Dozzer, LLC;

WHEREAS, the creation of the Lot with joint ownership was unintended and is not in the
best interest of the City and Dozzer, LLC;

Whereas, Dozzer, LLC, has agreed to donate certain real property to the City as described
in a Quitclaim Deed (attached an incorporated herein as Exhibit A) for use by the City for the
purpose of use as community parks and recreation;

Lot 1, Block 1, Back 40 Subdivision, Third Filing.

See plat of record recorded on June 29, 2023 in the Laramie County Clerk’s Office under
Reception No. 858500 in Book #13, Page #34.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne accept this donation of real property; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is hereby designated as the
authorized representative of the City to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all matters relating to the
donation of this real property and is authorized to execute any necessary agreements and associated
documents.

10:29 am, Oct 09 2024
