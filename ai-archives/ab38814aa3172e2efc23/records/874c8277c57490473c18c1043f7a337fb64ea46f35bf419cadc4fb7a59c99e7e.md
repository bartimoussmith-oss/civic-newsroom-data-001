Subject:
Change to Scope of Work for 25th and Carey Intersection
Dear Ms. Gerard:
Western Research & Development, Ud. (Western) has been providing design services for the
signalized intersection at 2S St and Carey Ave.
During the signal design, Western noticed the
asphalt conditions on 25th Street and the ADA ramps were in very poor condition. Western and City
of Cheyenne staff met on site on August 28, 2019 to inspect and discuss this issue.
Both parties
discussed the extent of road reconstruction, curb and gutter replacement, ADA ramp replacement,
and storm sewer inlet replacements required for the project.
Western’s understanding of the
additional scope of work of the project is as follows:
•
Reconstruction of 25th Street from the west side of Carey Avenue to the new access on
(west side of 2504 Carey Avenue property). Transition from existing to new asphalt will use a
wedge design that eases the impact on vehicles traversing the area. This area encompasses
approximately 6,000 square feet of asphalt road replacement (150 lineal feet of 40-foot-wide
road).
•
Curb and gutter will be replaced on the north side of 25’ in the same area as the road
reconstruction.
Curb and gutter replacement will continue north on Carey Ave to the
northeast corner of 2504 Carey Avenue.
This is approximately 200 lineal feet of curb and
gutter replacement.
•
Along with the curb and gutter replacement, the existing storm inlets on the northwest corner
of the Carey and 25 intersection are to be replaced (2 inlets).
•
ADA ramps on the west side of the intersection are to be designed.
These ramps will be
coordinated with the sidewalk and ramp construction on the east side of the intersection (part
of the Capital Reconstruction project).
A geotechnical investigation is not included in the proposed scope.
Western proposes that the
subgrade be reworked, covered with 6” of Grade W base, and a minimum of 3.5” asphalt concrete for
the road section. Western also recommends incorporating fly ash into the subgrade preparation.
This is a small additional construction cost that significantly adds to the structural stability of the road
reconstruction.
