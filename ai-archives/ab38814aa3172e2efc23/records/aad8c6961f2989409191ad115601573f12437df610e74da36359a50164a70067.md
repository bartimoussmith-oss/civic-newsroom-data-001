The applicant is requesting approval of a plat for 6.7 acres of land. The subject property is currently slated for
multifamily development on one lot. The purpose of this plat is to divide the property into multiple
multifamily lots (change in ownership pattern).

identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.
The future land use map within PlanCheyenne identifies this area as developing with mixed-use
commercial development. PlanCheyenne encourages responsiveness to market demands, and
the change in ownership pattern is in response to market demands.

The application complies with this review criterion.

Transportation Network (4.3):
This plat is occurring within an area of the community which has been previously platted.
There is limited opportunity to modify the block layout or street network. The proposed plat
is near a regional transportation route (College Drive) which would severely limit access.

Open Space (4.4):
This parcel is part of the Harmony Valley PUD, and a 5-acre park is being provided central to
the development to meet open space requirements. The park is located immediately to the
north of the eastern portion of this plat.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this
development. Easements are provided for BOPU utilities and for other utilities.
Developments are required to provide improvements adjacent to their development. The
engineering and construction of the public rights-of-way associated with this development
are already in process.

Public Safety Fees are required at $836.88 per dwelling unit. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public
Safety Fees are payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure.
