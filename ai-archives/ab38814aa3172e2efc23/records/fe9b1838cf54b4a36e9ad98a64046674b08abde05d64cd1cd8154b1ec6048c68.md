EXHIBIT A
Post and Associates will provide Employee Assistance Program (“EAP”) services as
follows:
DEFINITIONS.
a.
“Covered Person”
- any individual who is eligible for EAP benefits as an
employee or household member.
b.
“Employer”
- City of Cheyenne.
c.
“Participating Provider”
- a professional contracting with Referral Service
who furnishes EAP services to Covered Persons.
d.
“Referral Service”
- Jerry Post, Psy.D, P.C., Administrator of EAP and
President of Post & Associates
e.
“Session”
- any in-person consultation with a Participating Provider in
connection with a Covered Person’s health, mental health, emotional health,
or alcohol or substance abuse problems.
2.
TERMS OF SERVICES.
a.
Referral Service is the administrator of EAP and provides referrals for the
Employer to Covered Persons for short term counseling from Participating
Providers. Services will be provided from the date of execution to June 30,
2022.
b.
Referral Service does not enter into a Doctor-Patient relationship with
Covered Persons.
c.
The Referral Service enters into an agreement with a Participating Provider
to provide short term counseling for the above listed referral services; sets
up a Session for the Covered Person; and reimburses the Participating
Provider for each Session.
d.
Covered Persons are entitled to up to five (5) Sessions per issue at the
expense of the Referral Service. The Employer shall reimburse the Referral
Service in the amount of One Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars ($125.00) per
Session for up to five (5) Sessions per issue per Covered Person.
e.
If an employee requires more than the five (5) allotted Sessions per issue
with the Participating Provider, the Covered Person may continue to use the
Participating Provider at his or her own expense.
