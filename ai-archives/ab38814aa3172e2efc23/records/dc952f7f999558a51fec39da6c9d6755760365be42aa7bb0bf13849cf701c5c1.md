RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
February 11, 2015
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining with
Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters (I.A.F.F.), Local No.
279.  Present were:  MAYOR: Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Mr. Brown, Mr. Cook
(arriving at 6:07 p.m.), Mr. Johnson, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, Mr. Shanor and Ms.
Williams.  Absent:  Mr. Valdez.  Also present were: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Dan White, City
Attorney, and Lois Huff, City Treasurer.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.  Mayor Kaysen
explained procedures, including audio recording of the meeting, involving a quorum of the
governing body relative to special meetings.
Upon request by Mayor Kaysen, Dan White, City Attorney, presented and reviewed the
City’s proposed Ground Rules, titled Ground Rules for the 2015-2016 Collective Labor Agreement,
and advised the governing body’s spokespersons during the negotiation process would be himself
and Lois Huff, City Treasurer.  Mayor Kaysen requested Local No. 279 present its proposed Ground
Rules.  Jonathon Bertsch, President, I.A.F.F. Local No. 279, presented and discussed three concepts
or concerns by Local No. 279, and indicated he would like to address the issues prior to talking about
Ground Rules.  [City Clerk note: Mr. Cook joined the meeting in progress]  Comments followed
from Mr. White and Mr. Bertsch.  Mr. Bertsch submitted copies of a memo containing the concepts
he discussed.
Mr. White recommended the governing body move into executive session to review and
formulate a response to the Union’s concepts related to the City’s proposed Ground Rules.  Mr.
Cook moved that the governing body meet in executive session, seconded by Mr. Brown.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.  Mayor Kaysen advised the
governing body would meet in a conference room and that a meeting room was available for the
Union team to use if desired.  Mayor Kaysen asked Mr. White to identify City staff requested to be
present at the executive session in addition to the governing body, and requested the audio recording
of the meeting be stopped until the meeting was reconvened. [City Clerk note: Executive session
convened at 6:23 p.m. in the Mayor’s Conference Room]
Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting
at 7:09 p.m.  Mr. White reported the governing body’s position on the Ground Rules was to uphold
the rules as presented by the City with a modification to Section 8, which he then reviewed.  He
confirmed the governing body’s spokespersons would be himself and Ms. Huff.  David Evans,
attorney for I.A.F.F. Local No. 279, expressed his opinion on proposed Ground Rules relative to
direct dialogue with vs. spokespersons for the governing body, and negotiation meeting room
environment.  Mr. Evans stated the Union’s spokespersons would be Jonathan Bertsch, President,

Rules for Negotiations 2015-2017 Collective Labor Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and
I.A.F.F. Local No. 279, were distributed.
Mayor Kaysen acknowledged there were no Ground Rules in place at this time.  Mr. Brown
moved that the governing body meet in executive session, seconded by Ms. Williams.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present. Mayor Kaysen asked Mr. White
to identify City staff requested to be present at the executive session in addition to the governing
body. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was stopped; Executive session convened
in the Mayor’s Conference Room at 7:26 p.m.]
Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting
at 8:03 p.m.  Mr. White explained the position of the governing body is to negotiate in good faith
through designated spokespersons, and reviewed proposed changes to the City’s Ground Rules
involving incorporation of certain sections of the Union’s proposed Ground Rules.
Mr. Evans queried whether the negotiation meetings could be held in a work session
environment instead of an official special meeting with Mr. White responding with information
contained in the Public Meetings Act.  Mr. Evans and Mr. White each presented their position on
spokespersons for the governing body and the Union; interpretation of “corporate authority”, and
contract approval.  Mayor Kaysen reviewed governing body procedures that are followed in
approving a final contract, and advised audio recording availability for meeting rooms was needed.
Mayor Kaysen recessed the special meeting at 8:20 p.m. to allow Union representatives and
its attorney to confer on the Ground Rules. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was
stopped] Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting at 8:35 p.m.  Mr. Evans offered that the
Union will merge the City and I.A.F.F. proposed Ground Rules into one “set” and submit to Mr.
White on Friday (February 13, 2015) and suggested review of the combined Ground Rules continue
at a special meeting on Saturday (February 14, 2015).  Mr. Evans further suggested each party
exchange contract proposals at the current meeting with negotiations to follow approval (assuming
agreement can be reached) of the Ground Rules.  Mr. Roybal moved to meet in executive session,
seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was stopped; Executive session convened
in the City Council administrative office at 8:45 p.m.]
Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting at 8:50 p.m.; advised a quorum of the
governing body can be present for a special meeting to be held on Saturday, February 14, 2015
beginning at 8:30 a.m., and that it is acceptable to exchange contract proposals at the current
meeting.
Mayor Kaysen stated the purpose of the special meeting had been met, and there being no
further business previously noted for the special meeting, adjourned the meeting at 8:51 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
City Clerk
