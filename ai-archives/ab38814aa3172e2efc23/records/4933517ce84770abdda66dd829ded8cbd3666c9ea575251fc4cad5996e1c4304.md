```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

September 20, 2023
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-1-24 for furnishing Furniture for Three (3) New Fire
Stations, for the Cheyenne Fire Rescue department.
 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-3-24 was solicited for furnishing Furniture for Three (3) New Fire Stations, for the
Cheyenne Fire Rescue department.
Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bids were received
from:
1.
Indoff. Inc
2.
Retail Associates, Inc. Ashley Cheyenne
3.
Working Fire Furniture & Mattress Co. Inc.
The bids were reviewed by John Fitzgerald (CFR) and John Kopper (CFR) and their
recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid, for each product group.  This will result in
three (3) separate Purchase Orders, as shown on the Bid Tabulation (Highlighted in Green).

The total Bid Award amount is $113,884.98

Funding:
032-16-1640-41-43804 (SPOT / Fire Station Construction - 2021 Ballot)
```
