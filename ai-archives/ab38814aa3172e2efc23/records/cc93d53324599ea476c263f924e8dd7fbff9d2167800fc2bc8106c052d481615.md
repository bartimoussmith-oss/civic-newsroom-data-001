The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for 29.61 acres of land. The subject property is

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

This development is in conformance with the principles and future land uses identified in
PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.
The future land use map within PlanCheyenne identifies this area as developing with residential
uses. The applicant has requested a future land use amendment for Lot 1, Block 1 to become
Community Business. The plat provides larger lots than you would typically see for residential
development unless used for multi-family or to be replatted at a later date. The plat does
propose a commercial lot at a size that you would generally see.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2)

The proposed plat divides the land into three large parcels and a small remainder parcel. Two of
the large parcels are proposed to be for residential development and may be replatted in the
future. The plat does connect Ogden Road to Storey Blvd. to the north and also proposes
connecting Wild Bluff to Converse to the west.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
2.1.2.d.3)

Community Facility Fees (4.2):
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre.
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are
exempt from fees.

$8,540 ($350 per acre)

Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, the Land Acquisition & Infrastructure fee are payable at
the time of the signing of the final plat.

Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a
building permit for each structure.
Residential Enhancement Fee
$400 per dwelling unit
Commercial Enhancement Fee $1,100 per acre

Transportation Network (4.3):
The applicant proposes extending Wild Bluff to Converse and Ogden to Storey. These are
major road connections that we would expect to happen with the proposed plat. Further
road networks could be proposed with the replatting of Lot 1, Block 2 and Lot 1, Block 3.
There is a sliver of land that is between proposed Wild Bluff an Converse Avenue right-of-
way. The applicant will need to acquire an easement from the adjacent property owner
prior to signing of the plat to make sure the connection will happen.

Open Space (4.4):
The applicant has requested that open space be addressed at a later date. The reasons they
gave are as follows, Lot 1, Block 1 will have a site plan which will then address the open
space requirement, Lot 1, Block 2 and Lot 1, Block 3 will either be replatted in the future or
they too will have site plans. Staff is satisfied with the applicants request as open space
requirements will be required either with a site plan or when a replat is submitted.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this
development.
