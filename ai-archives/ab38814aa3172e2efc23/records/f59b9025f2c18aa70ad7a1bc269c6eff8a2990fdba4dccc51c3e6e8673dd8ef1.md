FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Monday, December 2, 2024, in Committee
Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Chairwoman; Bryan Cook; Richard Johnson;
and Jeff White (via Zoom).
AGENDA ITEMS
18.
ORDINANCE _2nd READING
Amending Chapter 2.16.010 of the Municipal Code of
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, requiring the City Treasurer to annually evaluate and
report to the Governing Body any outstanding debt owed the City by any City Board,
Council or Commission Member. (SPONSORS - DR. ALDRICH, MR. COOK AND MR.
JOHNSON)
Mr. Cook moved to approve on 2 reading, seconded by Mr. Johnson. Motion carried by
unanimous voice vote.
Ward III Councilman, Richard Johnson, and sponsor of the item, provided an overview of the
proposed ordinance. Mr. Cook and Mr. White thanked John Brodie, Deputy City Attorney, for his
work on the ordinance and the sponsors for bringing it forward.
24.
RESOLUTION
— Authorizing a one-time payment from Fiscal Year 2024 budget savings
given to full-time and permanent part-time employees of the City of Cheyenne (excluding
elected officials and employees of the Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities). (SPONSOR
—
DR. ALDRICH)
Mr. Cook moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Johnson.
Mr. Cook moved to amend by substitute
dated November 21, 2024, seconded by Mr. Johnson.
Motion to amend carried by unanimous
voice vote. Main motion, as amended, carried by unanimous voice vote.
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided a staff report and Mayor Patrick Collins provided
additional information. Chairwoman Aldrich provided information related to the substitute and
committee members Johnson and White commented on the item. Public comment was made by
Seth Lloyd, City employee.
28.
CONSIDERATION OF BIDS/PURCHASES/REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS:
c)
Consideration of Bid #5-7 -25 for an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and
Fencetrak Inc. dlb/a Full Tilt Services for the Belvoir Ranch Trailhead and Access
Roads Project. (Belvoir Ranch Recreation Fund and Wyoming Outdoor Recreation
Grant)
