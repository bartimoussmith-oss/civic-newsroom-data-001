Background:
The item for consideration is an Annexation. This is approved by ordinance.   This annexation is
comprised of five non-residential properties all 100% surrounded by the City. The City has maintained
contact with the County Planning Office to coordinate any pending permits or development reviews.
Currently there are no active building permits and there is one County Site Plan under review. The
approved Site Plan will be grandfathered and allowed to develop in accordance with its approval.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and referral February 27, 2023
•
Public Services Committee: March 7, 2023
•
Second Reading: March 13, 2023
•
Public Services Committee: March 21, 2023
•
Third and Final Reading: March 27, 2021

On December 9, 2022, City staff mailed a courtesy notice to all landowners included in this annexation
inviting them to a “Question and Answer” session on December 19, 2022, at the City Municipal Building.
Representatives of two of the parcels within the annexation area attended the meeting and did not
express opposition to the annexation. A media release was also released on December 15, 2022,
identifying the subject properties and indicating the City’s intent to initiate the annexation process.

City-initiated annexations do not require an owner’s signature. To initiate an annexation, the City must
adopt a Resolution certifying that the annexation complies with Wyoming State Statute § 15-1-402.
After the City adopts this Resolution, the annexation follows the typical schedule, including a public
hearing and the approval of an Ordinance which annexes the land. This resolution was adopted by the
Governing Body on December 27, 2022.

As with any annexation, City services will be extended to service the land contained in the annexation.
City utilities are already located in the vicinity. Police, fire, and sanitation services will be provided.  As
several parcels of land contained in this annexation area are developed, City sanitation service will need
to be established and landowners have been notified of this required change.  Infrastructure
improvements required related to the annexation are minimal. Future development such as subdivision
or new development, will require on- and off-site infrastructure improvements. Existing infrastructure
