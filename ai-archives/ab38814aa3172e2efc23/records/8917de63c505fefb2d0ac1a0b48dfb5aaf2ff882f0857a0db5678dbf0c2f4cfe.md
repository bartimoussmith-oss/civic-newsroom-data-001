PUDC-24-28

April 1, 2024
RECOMMENDED MOTION:

Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for land at 4204 Dell Range Blvd
to City MR Medium Density Residential as shown in the attached zone change map, noting that the
project meets the review criteria for approval.

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

•
UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning

BACKGROUND:
The applicant has petitioned for1.60 acres of land located at 4204 Dell Range Blvd to be annexed into city
limits. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established. Per UDC 2.2.8 a zone will be assigned
based on the underlying county zone and per the policy document adopted by the city. Should the applicant
wish to have a different zone at the time of annexation, they will be required to submit a subsequent zoning
map amendment, proposing a different zone. The applicant is not requesting a different City zone at this
time.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The assigned zone is the most analogous City zone to the current zone when considering
allowed uses. Zoning for standard County zones shall be assigned based on the policy
document, while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis. (UDC 2.2.8.d.1)

The underlying county zone is MR Medium Density Residential. The most analogous city zone is
MR Medium Density Residential according to the policy document. The Assigned Zoning policy
document is attached to the staff report as attachment number three.

Establishment of the City zone matches the policy document; therefore, the item complies with
review criterion 1.

STAFF REVIEW:

Through the review process, there were no comments.

CONCLUSION:

Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria set forth in UDC 2.2.8.(d).

ALTERNATIVES:

1. Recommend approval of the Assigned Zoning as submitted by the applicant (Staff
Recommendation).
2. Recommend approval of the Assigned Zoning subject to conditions or modifications.
