RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 6, 2017

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:05 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Bryan Cook, Richard
Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Dicky Shanor, and Jeff White.  Absent:
Rocky Case and Mike Luna.  Also present were:  Kylie Soden, Deputy City Clerk; Sylvia Hackl,
City Attorney; Alessandra McCoy, Assistant City Attorney, Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and Tom
Schingle, Interim Chief, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded, and stated that Wyoming State
Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings Act allows the governing body to meet in executive
session to consider or tender offers regarding wages, salaries, or other terms and conditions of
employment during negotiations with the fire fighters union and that any motion approved by the
governing body to move into executive session during the special meetings would be pursuant to
this section of statute.  Mayor Orr further advised if the governing body met in executive session,
they would be meeting in the City Council Administrative office, and that Fire Union
representatives were welcome to use Room 104 for the purpose of meeting to caucus.  She stated
that spokespersons for the City are herself and Dicky Shanor, and Jonathan Bertsch and Andrew
Dykeshorn are the spokespersons for the Union.  She noted that any collective labor agreement
that may be reached must be approved by the governing body with the agreement placed on the
city council agenda for approval.

Mayor Orr stated that the City would be reviewing revisions to the following sections:
Section 2, Definitions; Section 13, Management Rights; Section 15, Company Strength; Section
17, Severance Pay; Section 22, Classification of Employment and Basic Wages; Section 24,
Working in a Higher Job Rank (further clarification needed from the Union at some point); Section
27, Wellness/Fitness Program; Section 33, Uniforms, and Section 46, Wildland.  Mr. Johnson
moved to meet in executive session, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all
members of the governing body present.  [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was
stopped; executive session convened at 6:13 p.m. in the City Council Administrative office]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:19 p.m.  Mr. Shanor reiterated that the City’s priorities are overtime predictability, management
rights and sustainability of the service structure.  Mr. Shanor presented the City’s counteroffer on
the following sections, or portions thereof: Section 13, Management Rights; Section 15, Company
Strength; Section 17, Severance Pay; Section 22, Classification of Employment and Basic Wages;
Section 23, Overtime; Section 27, Wellness/Fitness Program; Section 33, Uniforms; Section 46,
Wildland, and Section 47, Labor/Management Relations.  He noted the City has no objections to
the Union’s proposals for Section 2, Definitions, and Section 28, Vacations, and requested

clarification of wording contained in Section 20, Inclement Weather Conditions, and Section 24,
Working in a Higher Job Rank.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Shanor explained the City wishes to strike the
Union’s proposed “Education Reimbursement” language in Section 22 in its entirety.  Mr. Bertsch
posed questions relative to how the City predicts overtime, prediction of overtime for the upcoming
year, and whether the City anticipates distribution changes or cuts in state funding.  He discussed
non-uniform vs. Union employee raises during the last three years.  Mayor Orr advised there have
been financial and administrative changes recently, and that the City is looking at a significant loss
in sales tax revenue.  Mayor Orr recessed the meeting at 7:37 p.m. to allow Union representatives
to caucus, and Dr. Rinne moved to meet in executive session, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.  [City Clerk note: Audio
recording of the meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 7:40 p.m. in the City Council
Administrative office]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
8:17 p.m.  Mayor Orr addressed the questions that were presented by Mr. Bertsch prior to executive
session.  She advised overtime is predicted by a review of overtime costs in prior years, analyzing
data and discussions with the Fire Chief; noted overtime costs for 2014, 2015 and 2016, and stated
overtime is anticipated to exceed $500,000 this year.  Mayor Orr indicated the City would like to
see predictability in overtime budgeting moving forward, and explained the City won’t know of
any loss in state funding until the legislative session next year, however, sales tax revenues to date
are more than $900,000 below projection.  The City provided a handout referred to as the
“$20/hour Analysis” (on file with the City Clerk’s office) which contained information on pay
raises since 2002 for uniformed and non-uniformed City employees.  It was noted that the handout
does not include specialty pay, sick leave bonuses, etc. that are contained in the agreement that
aren’t available to other City employees.  Mr. Bertsch presented and explained the Union’s
counteroffers on the following sections, or portions thereof: Section 13, Management Rights;
Section 15, Company Strength; Section 17, Severance Pay; Section 22, Classification of
Employment and Basic Wages; Section 23, Overtime; Section 27, Wellness/Fitness Program, and
Section 33, Uniforms.  He advised there would be a minor modification to Section 24, Working in
a Higher Job Rank, presented at the next special meeting.

Mayor Orr stated the next special meeting would be held on March 8, 2017.  She stated the
purpose of the special meeting had been met, and there being no other business previously noted
for the special meeting, adjourned the meeting at 8:32 p.m.

Submitted by,

Kylie Soden

Deputy City Clerk
