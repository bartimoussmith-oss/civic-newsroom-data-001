MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

May 21, 2024
SUBJECT:
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and The Design
Studio, Inc. (TDSi), for the Johnson Pool Evaluation and Design Project
 ______________________________________________
Two (2) Responsive Proposals were submitted under Request for Proposal (RFP) #8-24, for the
Johnson Pool Evaluation and Design Project.

The proposals were received from:
1. TDSI, Inc.
2. Stockwell Engineers

The Proposal submitted by Landmark Aquatics has been rejected due to non-conformance with
the scope requirements.

Upon review of the proposals by the evaluation panel, the group has recommended acceptance of
the proposal submitted by The Design Studio, Inc. (TDSi), for a project award amount of
$247,533.00.
At this time, the Scope of Work requests Sixty-Five Percent (65%) Design Development (DD),
but does not include completion of One-Hundred Percent (100%) Construction Documents (CD).
This may be considered at a future time.
Basis for award was based on a Qualifications and Cost based review and scoring of proposals.

Funding:
 032-17-1721-45-43113 (SPOT 2021 Ballot / Johnson Pool Project)
