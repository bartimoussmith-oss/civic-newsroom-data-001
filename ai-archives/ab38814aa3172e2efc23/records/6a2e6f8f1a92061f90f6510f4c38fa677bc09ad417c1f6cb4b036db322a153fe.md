BACKGROUND:
In 1982 the City, County, and south Cheyenne Water and Sewer District executed an intergovernmental
agreement to implement the 1982 Wastewater Facilities Master Plan. The plan and the IGA were
intended to satisfy the Federal Clean Water Act requirements and to protect the safety and quality of
the region’s groundwater from pollution, specifically from septic system failures.

The IGA requires connection to sewer systems for development of properties lying within the Urban
Service Area identified in the Plan. The Facilities Plan defines the Urban Service Area as the area where
“Municipal Sewerage Treatment services can be provided via gravity flow” [Section 1.0, Definitions of
the IGA]. The IGA further states that the City of Cheyenne is to limit the use of on-site wastewater
treatment systems within the Urban Service Area; this would include septic systems and package
wastewater treatment plants. The IGA states that the City and County will not approve any development
which is inconsistent with the Facility Plan unless granted an exception pursuant to Sections 4.0 to 4.7 of
the IGA.

The properties subject to this 201 Exception applied for and received an exception in 2012. The
agreement had a sunset clause on the City’s approval of 10 years or the re-construction of the
interchange (whichever occurred first). WYDOT has acquired the property and rents the facilities
thereon out to third parties. As the sunset clause from the original City approval has been reached, an
extension of the approval is needed to allow the properties to continue to be used without connecting
to sewer. According to the original agreement, extension is not be unreasonably withheld.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to approve the exception, the Commission must make the
following findings per Sections 4.0 to 4.7 of the 201 IGA. A detailed analysis is below:

Section 4.0: EXCEPTIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT THAT IS NOT CONSISTENT WITH THE PROPOSED FACILITY
PLAN:

4.1 Exceptions can be granted for the following reasons:
(a) The proposed exception conforms with the criteria for exceptions in The City of Cheyenne
Annexation Policies.
(b) The exception can be shown not to be detrimental to the public health, safety and welefare [sic].
(c) The exception will alleviate a clearly demonstrable hardship and compliance with the Facility Plan
will exacerbate this hardship. A hardship will be determined based upon a cost/effectiveness study of
sewer service provision to the property seeking the exception.
(d) The exception demonstrates substantial compliance with the proposed Facility Plan.

Reason (a): This property is annexed to the City and has sanitary sewer nearby. Off-site sanitary
sewer main extension requirements are applicable, and wouldn’t require a significant expansion;
however the site is slated to be significantly modified by the reconstruction of the I-25/I-80
interchange, and as such, the utility extension may not service any structures in the near future.

Reason (b): The facility proposed does discharge into the surrounding environment, however each
property has at least 5 acres, and as such, can meet the typical standards for new construction
septic systems.

Reason (c): The property has sanitary sewer readily available (within a few hundred feet of the
property line). The property is anticipated to be used for the rebuilding of the I-25/I-80 interchange,
and, as such, extension of permanent infrastructure to what effectively a temporary facility is a
hardship. This is what was determined with the original approval of the exception.

Reason (d): The facility would maintain the principal goal of the IGA – that is to prevent the
excessive environmental pollution of the land. In the Cheyenne area, typically a property needs 5
acres for a septic system and leach field, and both of the subject properties have this acreage.

4.2. Exceptions may only be considered if the proposal has been denied due to non-conformance with
the proposed Facility Plan.

The applicant applied for a permit to allow a self-contained septic holding tank in 2012. The permit
was denied in 2012, after which the applicant was eligible to apply for an exception pursuant to the
IGA.  The exception request was filed with the Cheyenne-Laramie County Health Department in
2012 and approved for a period of 10 years by the City. It has been 10 years and the interchange has
not yet been rebuilt, therefore an extension to the original City approval is requested.

4.3. The request for exception must be submitted to the Planning Commission for review.

This proposal is being submitted to the Planning Commission.
