```text
FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
Due to the New Years Day holiday, a meeting of the Finance Committee was instead held on
Tuesday, January 2, 2024, in Committee Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00
p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Jeff White, Chainrian, Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Ken Esquibel, and
Scott Roybal.
AGENDA ITEMS
12.
ORDINANCE _31X1 READING
— Amending Ordinance No. 4520 Defining the Terms and
Conditions of Employment for Sworn Officers of the Cheyenne Police Department for
Fiscal Year 2023-2024.
Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on 3” and final reading, seconded by Mr. Roybal. Motion carried
by unanimous voice vote.
Chief Mark Francisco, Police Department, provided a staff report. Stefanie Boster, City Attorney,
and Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided additional information. Committee members
Aldrich and Roybal commented on the item.
22,
RESOLUTION
— Authorizing the Mayor to execute a Settlement Agreement with Rodney
and Joyce Stone and Dean and Jayne Fertig to resolve a lawsuit regarding increased
flooding following the development of the Thomas Heights Subdivision. (SPONSOR
—
MR. WHITE)
Dr. Aldrich moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Esquibel. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Stefanie Boster, City Attorney, provided a staff report and Tom Cobb, City Engineer, provided
additional information. Committee members Aldrich and Esquibel commented on the item.
24.
CONTRACT MODIFICATIONS:
c)
Contract Modification #1 to Contract #7782 between the City of Cheyenne and
DI-IM Design Corporation, for utility locating services for the Reed Avenue Rail
Corridor Design Project. (2017 Specific Purpose Option Tax Fund)
Dr. Aldrich moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $39,640.00, seconded by Mr. Esquibel.
Motion carried unanimous voice vote.
Wes Bay, Engineering Storm Water Division, provided a staff report and committee members
Aldrich and Esquibel commented on the item.
```
