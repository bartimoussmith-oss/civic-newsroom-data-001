MEMORANDUM
TO:

City of Cheyenne Governing Body
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

November 20, 2024
SUBJECT:
One-Time Payment Resolution

On November 25, 2024, the Governing Body will be referring to the Finance Committee the
attached resolution authorizing a one-time payment for all permanent full-time and part-time
employees of the City of Cheyenne. This resolution proposes a $1,000 payment for permanent
part-time employees and a $2,000 payment for permanent full-time employees.  The goal of this
resolution is to recognize the outstanding contribution of City employees, particularly in achieving
significant cost savings during fiscal year 2024.
The estimated cost of this one-time payment is as follows:
• $1,026,000 for the General Fund.  This comprises 440 full-time and 77 part-time
employees and includes all employees paid for by the General Fund, as well as specific
exceptions including the Youth Alternatives Grant Fund, Juvenile Justice Fund and the
Law Enforcement Grants Fund.  These funds cannot sustain the cost of this one-time
payment or are not permissible by grant requirements.
• $428,000 for all other Funds.  This covers 151 full-time and 89 part-time employees of
various other funds, including Solid Waste, Transportation Planning, Transit, Weed and
Pest, Recreation Programs, Federal Grants, One Percent, Specific Purpose Option Tax,
Fleet Maintenance, Civic Center and Ice and Events Center.
Please note that these are estimates as we won’t know the final number of employees who qualify
until after November 30, 2024.
If you have questions, feel free to me a call at 637-6336 or email at rlockman@cheyennecity.org.

RESOLUTION NO. ___________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A ONE-TIME PAYMENT FROM
FISCAL YEAR 2024 BUDGET SAVINGS GIVEN TO FULL-TIME AND
PERMANENT PART-TIME EMPLOYEES OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
(EXCLUDING ELECTED OFFICIALS AND EMPLOYEES OF THE
CHEYENNE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES)."

WHEREAS, the success and financial health of the City of Cheyenne depends on the
dedication and innovative efforts of City employees;

WHEREAS, during the fiscal year ending June 30, 2024, City employees have
demonstrated exceptional commitment to reducing costs and improving operating efficiency;

WHEREAS, these cost-saving measures have resulted in a significant positive impact on
the City’s General Fund budget, achieving savings of Three Million, Two Hundred Twelve
Thousand, Eight Hundred Seventy Dollars ($3,212,870) in Fiscal Year 2024; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne’s Governing Body recognizes the importance of
acknowledging and rewarding City staff for these cost savings, and wishes to provide a one-time
payment of two-thousand dollars ($2,000) to each full-time employee and one-thousand dollars
($1,000) to each permanent part-time employee excluding elected officials and those employed by
the separate Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that all full-time employees, defined as working thirty (30) or
more hours per week, who are employed as of November 30, 2024, will receive a two thousand
dollar ($2,000) one-time payment in December 2024, subject to federal withholding requirements.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that permanent part-time employees will receive a one
thousand dollar ($1,000) one-time payment in December 2024, subject to federal withholding
requirements, if they meet the following guidelines:
1. The employee works less than thirty (30) hours per week and has received a paycheck
each month from November 2023 to November 2024; or

2. If an employee was hired after December 1, 2023, they must have received a paycheck
each month from their hire date to November 2024; and

3. The employee must be employed by the City of Cheyenne on November 30, 2024; and

4. The employee must not be a seasonal employee.
3:03 pm, Nov 20 2024
