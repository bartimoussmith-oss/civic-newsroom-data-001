RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

December 11, 2017

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were:  MAYOR – Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL
MEMBERS – Rocky Case, Bryan Cook, Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Mike Luna, Scott
Roybal, Dr. Mark Rinne, Dicky Shanor and Jeff White.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk,
and Sylvia Hackl, City Attorney. The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Upon request by Mr. Cook, and pursuant to support received by
Mr. Shanor and Mr. Case, Agenda Item #12 was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Upon request
by Mr. Case, and pursuant to support received by Mr. Cook and Mr. Shanor, Agenda Item #11 was
removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Case moved to approve remaining Consent Agenda items,
seconded by Mr. Roybal.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.

[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on November 27, 2017.  Motion
(per Consent Agenda) carried.

Mr. Roybal declared a conflict of interest (business interest in voucher payable to Cowboy
Dodge, Inc.) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Luna moved to approve payment of vouchers
as presented, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body with the exception of Mr. Johnson voting “no”.

ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a portion
of Section 9, all of the W½ Section 14, all of Section 15, a portion of Section 16, and all of Section
21; Township 13 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming (located to
the southeast of the intersection of E. College Drive and Avenue C) (postponed from 11/27/2017).
Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr. Roybal moved to approve on third and final reading,
seconded by Mr. Case.  Mr. Roybal moved to postpone to December 20, 2017 at the request of the
developer, seconded by Mr. Case.  Motion to postpone carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body.

ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Amending Section 1.12.010, Findings of fact, and
amending Section 1.12.020, Filing of plats and maps, of Chapter 1.12, Datum Plane, of Title 1,
General Provisions, of the code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Mr. Roybal moved to approve
on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Johnson.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members
of the governing body. (#4212)

ORDINANCE – 2nd READING – Amending various sections of Title 10, Vehicles and
Traffic, of the code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Mr. Roybal moved to approve on second
reading, seconded by Mr. Case.  Mr. Roybal moved to amend Section 10.60.010 by removing the
reference to 100 feet and replacing it with 150 feet, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Request was made for
City Attorney review of enforcement concerns that may arise involving contested violations and
the City’s defense relative to the referencing of a specific footage distance.  Motion to amend
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  Main motion as amended carried.
voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.

ORDINANCE – 1st READING – Pursuant to Sections 1.1.6 and 2.4.1 of the Cheyenne
Unified Development Code (UDC), amending Section 1.4.4.f., Industrial Use definition, of the
UDC to clarify what land uses are subject to the Moderate Industrial and the Industrial use
categories – referred to Public Services Committee. (Sponsor – Mr. Roybal)

RESOLUTION – Authorizing the City Treasurer to discharge uncollectible debts in the
amount of $58,535.43 pursuant to Wyo. Stat. §16-4-502.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr.
Luna moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. White.  It was clarified that a debtor listed on the Board of
Public Utilities’ uncollectible list involved unpaid charges incurred by a previous owner of the
Hitching Post, not the current owner.  Ron Downey, City Treasurer, confirmed the former owner,
Cheyenne Mantiff, filed bankruptcy many years ago and the debt was uncollectible.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body. (#5895)

RESOLUTION – Authorizing a one-time payment to each member of the bargaining unit
of Cheyenne Firefighters Local Union Number 279 in settlement of the grievance filed by the
Union against the City concerning health insurance benefits.  Mr. Luna moved to adopt, seconded
by Mr. Case.  Upon inquiry for the record, Sylvia Hackl, City Attorney, provided an explanation
of the intent of the resolution, which is the result of negotiations pursuant to the collective
bargaining agreement and the grievance process initiated by the Union concerning changes to the
City’s health insurance benefits plan.  Ms. Hackl reviewed the grievance process involving an
arbitrator and the City’s offer to settle to avoid formal arbitration and affiliated costs.  Mayor Orr
and Ms. Hackl provided information on eligible members who will receive the settlement payment,
and noted the City is not acknowledging any non-compliance of current contract provisions.
Comment was made on addressing the issue in the next collective bargaining contract.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Cook voting
“no”. (#5896)

Mr. Johnson provided information received from a constituent regarding concern with the
increase in crimes recently within the City.  Captain Nathan Buseck, Police Department,
acknowledged there have been some traumatic incidents over the past few weeks and stressed the
importance of maintaining good relationships between citizens and police officers.  Mayor Orr
expressed appreciation to all first responders involved with incidents.  General comments were
made that the increase does not constitute a trend at this time, and that support for community
rehabilitative programs is important.

There being no further business to come before the governing body, Mr. Case moved to
meet in executive session pursuant to Wyoming Statute Sections §16-4-405 (a)(vii) and (ix) to
consider the potential purchase of real property, and to consider or receive any information
classified as confidential by law, seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body.  Mayor Orr advised the executive session would be held in the
Mayor’s Conference Room and would not be open to the public.  In addition to governing body
members, Mayor Orr stated the City Attorney, City Clerk, Director of Planning and Development
and Director of Public Works would also be attending the session.  She noted following the
executive session further action by the governing body would not be taken, therefore, the regular
meeting of the governing body would be officially adjourned following closure of the executive
session.

The meeting was officially adjourned at 8:04 p.m. following closure of the executive
session.
