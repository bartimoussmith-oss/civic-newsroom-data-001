The proposed zoning allows for expansion of an existing business park and allows for
construction of a new data center. The proposed zone change is in accordance with the plans
and policies outlined in PlanCheyenne and complies with review criteria 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

The subject property proposed is located in an area with regional connectivity.   The location of
North Range Business Park takes advantage of the location adjacent to Otto Road and the access
to I-80 via Roundtop Road while maintaining a minimal impact on surrounding land uses.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criteria 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property is proposed to be annexed into city limits and will be assigned the AG, LR, MR, CB,
and HI zones. The applicant has requested to change the zone to AG and LI. The LI zone would
allow for Limited industrial uses, which includes the proposed data center. The rest of the
property would be zoned to AG which would be used as an interim holding zone.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The proposed area to be rezoned as LI is adjacent Roundtop Road and I-80 allowing sufficient
access to the parcel. No changes to the network are proposed. The remaining area to be
rezoned AG has no proposed development and the AG zone will act as a holding zone until a
development is proposed.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The application supports the intent of the LI zoning district intent and applicability. The
application falls on the edge of a Business Park as designated by the Comprehensive Plan and
will serve the greater Cheyenne area by accommodating a variety of uses. The LI district is
