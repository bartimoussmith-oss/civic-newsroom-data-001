RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
September 7, 2010
Due to the Labor Day holiday, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne instead
convened in regular session on this date at 12:00 p.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of acting
upon vouchers for payment as presented. Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker
and Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Dr. Rinne.  Also present:  Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Mr. Brown moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Spiker.
Mr. Collins declared a conflict of interest (business interest) due to a voucher payable to the Bicycle
Station, and vacated Council Chambers for the remainder of the meeting.  Upon inquiry concerning
a voucher payable to Mechanical Systems Inc. ($1,826.00) for removal of a pump and installation
of a new pump at the U.S. Hwy. 30 Greenway tunnel, Todd Feezer, Assistant Director of Parks and
Recreation, explained replacement of the pump was due to design failure not mechanical failure.
Mr. Feezer and Mayor Kaysen advised discussions are being pursued with the engineering company
involved in the design.  Pursuant to comment about tunnel lighting, Mr. Feezer stated he would
check on the status of the lighting.  Motion to approve carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present in Council Chambers.
Purpose of the meeting having been met, Mayor Kaysen adjourned the meeting at 12:04 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
City Clerk
This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request
Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle
September 13, 2010
