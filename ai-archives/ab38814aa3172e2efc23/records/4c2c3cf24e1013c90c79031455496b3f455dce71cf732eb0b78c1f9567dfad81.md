RESOLUTION NO. _________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF A CAPITAL
CONSTRUCTION LOAN APPLICATION TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT
BOARD ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
FOR THE PURPOSE OF FUNDING THE REPLACEMENT OF FIRE STATION #5.”

WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the Capital
Construction Loan program to assist in financing the replacement of Fire Station #5 in Cheyenne,
Wyoming; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for a new Fire
Station #5; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne agrees to maintain Fire Station #5 for
the life of the loan; and

WHEREAS, the Capital Construction Loan program requires that certain criteria be met, as
described in the State Loan and Investment Boards Rules and Regulations governing the program,
and to the best of our knowledge, this application meets those criteria; and

WHEREAS, the estimated cost for the project is $5,097,485.00 and the City of Cheyenne would
like to borrow this full amount; and

WHEREAS, the State of Wyoming will hold the mortgage for the land and any buildings added
to that land as loan security.  The City of Cheyenne anticipates it will use future 6th Penny Sales
Tax revenues or will use monies from the general fund for the loan repayment.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY FOR THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, that a loan application in the amount of $5,097,485.00 be submitted to the State
Loan and Investment Board for consideration at the June 6, 2019 meeting to replace Fire Station
#5.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Cindy Schneider, Grants Manager, and Ron Downey, City
Treasurer, are hereby designated as the authorized representatives of the City of Cheyenne, to act
on behalf of the Governing Body on all matters related to this grant application.

PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 25th day of February, 2019.

___________________________________

Marian J. Orr, Mayor

Chapter 38 Capital Construction Loan: Replacement of Fire Station #5

Fire Station #5 is in North Cheyenne and serves the Dell Range Blvd. commercial corridor.  The
Fire Station is 56 years old (constructed in 1963) and has numerous problems, as it is no longer
able to adequately serve Cheyenne’s citizens.  There has been expansive growth in North
Cheyenne’s commercial occupancies.   Residential growth to the north and east of the old station
has also exploded in the past 56 years, rendering the existing station inadequate to ensure proper
coverage to both commercial and residential areas.

The existing station is too small to accommodate more than three firefighters.  The very populated
service area of Cheyenne requires more firefighters than the three that can currently be housed in
the small old station.  Replacing Station #5 with a larger station will address this issue.  Further,
problems from sheer age and wear have found this station unfit for continued service.

The 56-year-old Fire Station was not built to accommodate the numerous advances in firefighting,
equipment and technology that now exist. A new station, to replace Station #5, will provide for
the care of firefighters, vehicles, and equipment.  It will be built to provide all that is needed for
firefighting in 2019 and for many, many years to come.

Given the poor condition of Fire Station #5, Cheyenne’s Fire Chief, Greg Hoggatt, noted “the only
repairs that should be authorized are those that keep the station operational until the new station
can be built.”

In 2017, Cheyenne Fire and Rescue Chief Greg Hoggatt directed his staff to conduct a thorough
examination of each fire station and to submit a report to him.    The chief condensed the report
and added his conclusions and recommendation into an executive summary in October 2017.
Regarding Fire Station #5, Chief Hoggatt states, “Begin the process to replace the station that will
meet the needs of the department in the future.”

If the Capital Construction Loan is approved, the State of Wyoming will hold the mortgage for the
land and any buildings added to that land, as loan security.  The City of Cheyenne anticipates it
will use future 6th Penny Sales Tax revenues or will use monies from the general fund for the loan
repayment.
