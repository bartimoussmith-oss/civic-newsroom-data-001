```text
ORDINANCE NO. ________

ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE MAJOR STREET PLAN OFFICIAL
MAP 2017, BY UPDATING THE ENTIRE MAJOR STREET PLAN
OFFICIAL MAP WITH NEWLY PLATTED AND BUILT ROADS AND
WITH THE MOST RECENT MASTER TRANSPORTATION PLAN
(CONNECT 2045) AND OTHER RECENTLY ADOPTED MASTER
TRANSPORTATION AMENDMENTS."

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That pursuant to authority set forth in Wyoming Statutes and Title 1, Chapter
1.20 of the Cheyenne City Code, the Governing Body hereby finds that:

A.
The Cheyenne Area Master Transportation Plan (Connect 2045) was certified on
December 14, 2020 by Resolution 6106; and
B.
The Cheyenne Area Master Transportation Plan was amended on March 14, 2022
by Resolution 6217; and
C.
The Cheyenne Area Master Transportation Plan was amended on October 24,
2022 by Resolution 6277; and
D.
Various new roads around the City of Cheyenne have been platted and/or built;
and
E.
The Cheyenne Planning Commission held a Public Hearing on Monday May 1,
2023 and recommended the approval of the changes as an update to the Official
Map.

Section 2.  That the City Clerk is directed to file an executed copy of this ordinance with
the Official Maps incorporated herein with the County Clerk.

Section 3.  The City Engineer or his designated representative shall alter or amend the
Major Street Plan Official Map of the City of Cheyenne.

Section 4.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage and
publication.

11:47 am, May 03 2023
```
