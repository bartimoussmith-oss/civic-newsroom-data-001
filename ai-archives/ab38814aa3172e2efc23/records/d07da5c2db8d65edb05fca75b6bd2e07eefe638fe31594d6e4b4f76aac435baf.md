Attachment A
Statement of Work
This document is intended as a Statement of Work (SOW) to identify and describe important milestones and
deliverables for the City of Cheyenne, Office of Youth Alternatives (Grantee) for the Foster Grandparent Program
(FGP) grant agreement for State Fiscal Year 2021.
Foster Grandparent Program
The core mission of the Foster Grandparent Program is to provide stipends and benefits to as many senior
volunteers over the age of fifty-five (55) as possible so they may continue to remain self-sufficient and
independent in their community for as long as possible. The scope of the program is fostering self-sufficiency and
independence in seniors over the age of fifty-five (55) by providing opportunities to engage in community
volunteerism that directly benefits school-aged children, while receiving a stipend and other benefits.
There are five (5) goals in the grant application that the Grantee must strive to achieve within the one (1) year grant
period (period of performance defined in Section 3 of the Agreement). All of these goals have methods of tracking
the progress on a monthly basis.
To be eligible for the program a client must be fifty-five (55) years of age or
older and residing in Wyoming, and, through an ongoing evaluation, be financially eligible to participate in the
program, (at least two hundred percent (200%) of poverty level or lower). The services provided for the service
area clients have specific tracking mechanisms and projected service goals to achieve during the one year grant
period.
This program serves Goshen, Laramie, and Platte counties.
Foster Grandparent Program
The service deliverables include maintaining or increasing by up to two percent (2%) the number of unduplicated
volunteers, volunteer hours, volunteer satisfaction and reducing the wait-list of volunteers, tracking the number of
U. S. veterans who are recruited and enrolled as volunteers, maintaining or increasing by one percent (1%) the
number of children who benefit from the program. The Foster Grandparent Program will strive to maintain the cost
efficiency of providing stipends and benefits per volunteer per year by tracking program costs and funds. The
provider will report all potential or suspected adult abuse, neglect or exploitation to the appropriate authorities.
Each month the Grantee shall submit an invoice, for services rendered in the prior month to the Community Living
Section (CLS) Program Manager by the fifteenth (15th) day of the month. The Grantee shall submit the quarterly
program and financial reports to the CLS Program Manager as outlined in the Timetable of Payments and
Reporting Requirements chart in this Attachment A.
After the grant application has been approved by the Foster Grandparent Program Manager, any revision to any
category (Personnel, Travel, Equipment, Consumable Supplies or Other) that exceeds 10% of the entire grant
amount (i.e. Entire Grant Amount
— $30,000 x 10%
= $3,000), must be submitted in writing to the Foster
Grandparent Program Manager. Revisions requests must be made by May 15, 2021. Once approved, the Grantee
will submit the categories budget sheets to the Foster Grandparent Program Manager. The approved changes will
become part of the approved grant application.
