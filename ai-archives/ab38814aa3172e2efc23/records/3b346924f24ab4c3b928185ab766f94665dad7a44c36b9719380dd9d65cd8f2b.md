```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

March 9, 2022
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-17-22 for furnishing Aggregates on an annual basis, for
the City of Cheyenne, Street & Alley Division
______________________________________________
Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Three (3) bids were received from:

1. Knife River d/b/a JTL Group Inc. (Cheyenne, WY)
2. Simon Contractors (Cheyenne, WY)
3. Croell Inc. (Cheyenne, WY)

The bids were reviewed by Randy Hickman (Street & Alley), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Croell, Inc. for the purchase of
Aggregates on an annual basis, for a total cost of $37,500.00 for 5,000 Tons.

*Note* The Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) Requested 6,000 Tons of Aggregate under this bid
also.  They will be awarding their portion ($45,000.00) separately.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-32303 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Sand & Aggregates)

$37,500.00
```
