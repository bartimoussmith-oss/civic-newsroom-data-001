September 6, 2022

RECOMMENDED MOTION:
Move to recommend the City Council approve the exception to the 201 Facilities Plan requirement for
sewer service to a new house at 516 Pontillo Drive.

The request for an exception must be submitted to the Planning Commission for review.  After
Commission review, the exception would be approved or denied by the City Council. If the property
seeking the exception is outside the Corporate Limits of the City and inside the 201 Service Area
Boundary, then County Commissioner approval is also required.

BACKGROUND:
In 1982 the City, County, and south Cheyenne Water and Sewer District executed an intergovernmental
agreement to implement the 1982 Wastewater Facilities Master Plan. The plan and the IGA were
intended to satisfy the Federal Clean Water Act requirements and to protect the safety and quality of
the region’s groundwater from pollution, specifically from septic system failures.

The IGA requires connection to sewer systems for development of properties lying within the Urban
Service Area identified in the Plan. The Facilities Plan defines the Urban Service Area as the area where
“Municipal Sewerage Treatment services can be provided via gravity flow” [Section 1.0, Definitions of
the IGA]. The IGA further states that the City of Cheyenne is to limit the use of on-site wastewater
treatment systems within the Urban Service Area; this would include septic systems and package
wastewater treatment plants. The IGA states that the City and County will not approve any development
which is inconsistent with the Facility Plan unless granted an exception pursuant to Sections 4.0 to 4.7 of
the IGA.

The applicant has already received approval for a 201 Exception for the current house at 516 Pontillo
Drive, and now wants to build a second house with a separate system. The applicant applied for a
permit to County Health. The permit was denied. After denial, the applicant was eligible to apply for an
exception pursuant to the IGA. The request for an exception must be submitted to the Planning
Commission for review. After Commission review, the exception would be approved or denied by the
City Council and the County Commissioners if the property seeking the exception is outside the
Corporate Limits of the City and inside the 201 Service Area Boundary.

The property is in the South Cheyenne Water Sewer District boundary, and therefore would not be
serviced by BOPU utilities. In analyzing this exception, consideration was taken for the cost of the
property tying into SCWSD facilities, not BOPU facilities.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to approve the exception, the Commission must make the
following findings per Sections 4.0 to 4.7 of the 201 IGA. A detailed analysis is below:

Section 4.0: EXCEPTIONS FOR DEVELOPMENT THAT IS NOT CONSISTENT WITH THE PROPOSED FACILITY
PLAN:

4.1 Exceptions can be granted for the following reasons:
(a) The proposed exception conforms with the criteria for exceptions in The City of Cheyenne
Annexation Policies.
(b) The exception can be shown not to be detrimental to the public health, safety and welefare [sic].
(c) The exception will alleviate a clearly demonstrable hardship and compliance with the Facility Plan
will exacerbate this hardship. A hardship will be determined based upon a cost/effectiveness study of
sewer service provision to the property seeking the exception.
(d) The exception demonstrates substantial compliance with the proposed Facility Plan.

Reason (a): This property is outside City limits and not adjacent to City limits. The subject property is
within the South Cheyenne Water and Sewer District and is not anticipated to connect to Board of
Public Utilities infrastructure.

Reason (b): The facility proposed is on a parcel that is roughly 10 acres in size. Throughout the
County, septic systems are allowed on 5-acre lots, and approving this exception would be consistent
with standard County development requirements.

Reason (c): The nearest SCWSD sanitary sewer line is over 7,000 feet away (per the contractor’s
estimate), and across South Greeley Highway. The estimated cost of extending a sewer main is
$795,000, and the estimated septic system cost is under $7,000.

Reason (d): The facility would maintain the principal goal of the IGA – that is to prevent the
excessive environmental pollution of the land. Typical county development allows septic systems on
5-acre parcels, and, if approved, this would establish two septic systems on a 10-acre parcel.
