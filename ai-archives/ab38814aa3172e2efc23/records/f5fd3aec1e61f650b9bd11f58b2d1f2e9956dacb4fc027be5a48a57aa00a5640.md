RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
December 22, 2010
Due to the holiday season, the regularly scheduled December 27, 2010 meeting of the
Governing Body was instead held on this date beginning at 5:30 p.m. in Council Chambers.
Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown,
Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also
present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.
The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and
support by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be
considered in its normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Collins moved to Consent Agenda items,
seconded by Mr. Brown.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on December 13, 2010.  Motion
(per Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Spiker.
Mr. Valdez declared a conflict of interest (business interest) due to a voucher payable to radio
station KYOY and vacated Council Chambers.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of
the governing body present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr.
Valdez returned to Council Chambers.
“An Ordinance amending Section 2.04.060, Meetings of the governing body, of Chapter
2.04, Mayor and City Council, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the City of Cheyenne
Code” -- referred to Finance Committee (sponsor - Mr. Collins).
[CA]
“A Resolution declaring as surplus three (3) City-owned properties located at 515 Morrie
Avenue, 916 East 5 Street and 922 East 5 Street, Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming,

th
together with all improvements thereon, and authorizing the City Clerk and City Purchasing
Director to proceed with proposed sale of said properties pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 15-1-112”.

“A Resolution designating local banks as depositories for municipal funds”.  Reporting

Mr. Collins.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the

Announcement was made that the Sine Die Meeting of the Governing Body will be held
at 3:00 p.m., and the First Regular Meeting of  the Governing Body at 3:15 p.m. on Mon., Jan. 3,
2011 in City Council Chambers, Municipal Building, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY.
Announcement was made that due to the Sine Die and First Regular Meetings of the
Governing Body on Mon., Jan. 3, 2011, the following meetings will instead be held on Tues.,
Jan. 4, 2011: Finance Committee will meet at 11:00 a.m., Room 122, and Vouchers meeting at
Noon, Council Chambers, Municipal Building, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY. There will
be no Public Services Committee meeting on Tues., Jan. 4, 2011 at Noon as there are no items for
the agenda requiring a meeting.
Under other business:
Barbara Laybourn thanked outgoing Council member Judy Case for her 12 years of
service on City Council and her representation of Ward I residents.  Ms. Case responded that she
has enjoyed serving her constituents.
Ms. Case commented on the practice of waiving fees, and inquired about the waiver of
fees associated with the new airline providing service at the Cheyenne Regional Airport with
Dave Haring, Airport Manager, providing information on certain fees that have been waived on a
temporary basis, which is a common practice by airports.  Upon inquiry, City Attorney Dan
White advised he would conduct research on the matter involving the airport fees and provide a
response.
Major Dorr expressed concern with the aesthetics of the “hole in the street” on west
Lincolnway (i.e. site of the downtown fire years ago) and suggested the excavation be filled in.
Mayor Kaysen advised ongoing discussions concerning the site are being held, noting the City
owns 1/3 of the property and a private party owns the other 2/3rds.
There being no further business to come before the governing body, Mayor Kaysen
adjourned the meeting at 5:47 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
