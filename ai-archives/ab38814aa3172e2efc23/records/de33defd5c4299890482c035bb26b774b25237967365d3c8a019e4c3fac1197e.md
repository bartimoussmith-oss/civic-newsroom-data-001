______________________________________________
Bid #E-24-22 was solicited for the Surplus Sale of City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department
Evidence Seized Firearms.

One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Bid was received from:
1. Proforce Law Enforcement.  Prescott, AZ.
The bid was reviewed by Kevin Malatesta (CPD), and Mark Francisco (CPD).  Their
recommendation is to accept the highest responsive bid from Proforce Law Enforcement, for the
Surplus Sale of City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Owned Firearms.  ProForce Law
Enforcement did submit a copy of their active Federal Firearms Licenses (FFL) with their Bid.

The total amount of revenue to the City, is $13,198.70.

Funding:
210-00-0000-36-07665 (Misc. Agency Funds / Police Training)
