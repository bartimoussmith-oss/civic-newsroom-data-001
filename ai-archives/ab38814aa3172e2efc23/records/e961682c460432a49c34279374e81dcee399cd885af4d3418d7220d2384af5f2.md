MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND
LARAMIE COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT NUMBER ONE
1.Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made and entered into by and
between the Laramie County School District Number One("District"), whose address is 2810 House
Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the City of Cheyenne, a municipality organized and
existing under thelaws of the State of Wyoming, acting through its Community Recreation &
Events Department ("City"), whose address is 1317 Parsley Blvd., Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.
2.Purpose.The purpose of this MOU is to provide transportation service for the Cheyenne
Community Recreation&Events“REC Camp Program”by leasing school busesfrom the Laramie
County School District Number One.
3.Term of MOU. This MOU shall commence on June 8, 2020, and shall remain in full force
and effect through August 14, 2020.
4.Payment.The City agrees that on or before September30,2020, it will pay the District for
the costs of operation of the buses for the duration of this MOU.Such costs shall include salaries,
benefits, fuel, depreciation and maintenance, as set forth in Attachment A. Both parties agree that
no profits or gains will be incurred pursuant to this MOU.
5.Responsibilities of District. The District shall furnish a maximum of six (6) buses with a
minimum capacity of seventy-two (72) passengers and a maximum of six (6) drivers to operate the
buses.
A.In case an emergency arises, bus drivers shall remain with buses at all times.
B.The City will be responsible for hourly wages and benefits for the drivers² personal
break time, so long as said break times do not exceed legal limits.
6.Special Provisions.
A.The City shall add the designated buses to the City's existing automobile insurance
policies and will cover the buses for liability and property damage.
B.It is expressly agreed and understood that the City's insurance policy is primary, not
contributory.
C. The City shall name the District as an additional insured/loss payee on the City's
automobile insurance policy andfurnish the District with a certificate of insurance evidencing such
coverage.
