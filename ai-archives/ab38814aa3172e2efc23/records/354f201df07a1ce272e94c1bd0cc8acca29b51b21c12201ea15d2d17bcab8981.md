The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning

statutory requirements had been met relative to certified mailing notification to landowners within

requesting annexation and  replatting of the property, which is to address a legal property trust issue.

the summary of annexation report (i.e. under Annexation Report Data (iii) contained in the
Annexation Report and Staff Report supporting documentation) include wording that extension of
services initiated by the property owner would be to the SCWS District, which would clarify that if
the property is annexed applicable services would be through the SCWD District and not the City’s
Board of Public Utilities.  Mayor Orr noted for those present that there would be no action taken on
the proposed annexation at the conclusion of the public hearing as the purpose of the hearing is to
allow the opportunity for public comment.  She further noted Agenda Items #13 and #14 are
ordinances related to the public hearing and would not be discussed or acted upon at the meeting as
they are on first reading and would be referred to Public Services Committee for review and
recommendation, including additional opportunity for public comment.  Mayor Orr advised the
Committee will meet January 18, 2017 at noon in Room #122, Municipal Building.  There being no
further comments or input and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Orr closed the public
hearing.
