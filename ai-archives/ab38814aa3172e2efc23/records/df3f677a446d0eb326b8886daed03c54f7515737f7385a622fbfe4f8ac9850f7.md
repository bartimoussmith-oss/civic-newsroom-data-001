RESOLUTION NO. ________________
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR PARTIAL VACATION OF
THE STOREY BOULEVARD EXTENSION FINAL PLAT.”
WHEREAS, on February 26, 2007, the Storey Boulevard Extension final plat was
approved by the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne;
WHEREAS, on May 3, 2007, the Storey Boulevard Extension final plat was
recorded with the Clerk of Laramie County as Record No. 474166, Book 9, Page 27; and
WHEREAS, on April 28, 2022, the City applied for partial vacation of the Storey
Boulevard Extension; and
WHEREAS, on May 16, 2022, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission
conducted a public hearing, reviewed the proposed partial vacation that is the subject of
this resolution, and recommended approval of said partial vacation; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne are the owners of Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1, Block
5; and Lot 1, Block 6, Storey Boulevard Extension; and
WHEREAS, Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1, Block 5; and Lot 1, Block 6 of the Storey
Boulevard Extension final plat are the only lots affected by the proposed partial plat
vacation; and
WHEREAS, the owners of Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1, Block 5; and Lot 1, Block 6 of
the Storey Boulevard Extension final plat have requested partial plat vacation vacating the
notes and callouts on said plat as shown in Attachment A which is attached hereto and
incorporated herein;
WHEREAS, the Unified Development Code of the City of Cheyenne is silent on
the plat vacation process and reliance on state statutes is necessary and appropriate; and
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat. § 34-12-108 allows any part of a plat to be partially
vacated; provided, such vacating does not abridge or destroy any of the rights and
privileges of other proprietors in said plat; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that vacating
portions of the note affecting Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1, Block 5; and Lot 1, Block 6 of the
Storey Boulevard Extension final plat does not abridge or destroy any of the rights and
privileges of other proprietors in said plat;
NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, THAT the partial plat vacation be and the
same hereby is approved and confirmed as presented, and that the Mayor and the City Clerk
be and are hereby authorized, empowered, and directed to execute this resolution partially
vacating the Storey Boulevard Extension final plat as shown in Attachment A; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the County Clerk of Laramie County is
hereby directed to mark the recorded Storey Boulevard Extension final plat to reflect this
partial plat vacation pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 34-12-110.

UDC-22-00157
May 16, 2022
RECOMMENDED MOTION:
Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the partial plat vacation to delete references in the
dedication statement and callouts on the face of the Storey Boulevard Extension final plat that dedicate
Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1, Block 5; and Lot 1, Block 6 to the public.

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

W.S. 34-12-106 though W.S. 34-12-111

BACKGROUND:
The Storey Boulevard Extension Plat was created by the City from City owned land specifically to dedicate
public rights-of-way (extending Storey Boulevard and Converse Avenue).  Not all the land owned by the City
was required to become public right-of-way. The excess land was turned into 6 lots. Lot 1, Block 3; Lot 1,
Block 5; and Lot 1, Block 6 were annotated on the plat as being “dedicated to the public” along with the
streets. Typically, dedication to the public is reserved for rights-of-way, not lots, and as the City retained
ownership, the City could reserve these for whatever use would best suit the public, without having this
note. The proposal is to remove this note and associated callouts from the plat.

REVIEW CRITERIA:
The Unified Development Code of the City of Cheyenne is silent on the plat vacation process so
defaulting to state statutes is appropriate.  W.S. 34-12-106 through 34-12-111 allows for plat vacation.
W.S. 34-12-106 [Vacation; generally]

This section allows a plat to be fully vacated under certain conditions. It also directs full and partial
plat vacations to be approved by the Governing Body. The plat vacation must be approved by all
owners affected by the plat vacation. The proposed plat vacation has all owners affected by the
vacation applying, and will come before the Governing Body.

W.S. 34-12-107 [Vacation; streets and alleys]

This section is not applicable to this request.

W.S. 34-12-108 [Vacation; partial vacation; when permitted]

This section allows any part of a plat to be vacated; provided, such vacating does not abridge or
destroy any of the rights and privileges of other proprietors in said plat; and does not close or
obstruct public highways. The proposed partial plat vacation does not propose vacating any streets,
and the property owners affected by the vacation do not have any rights removed.

W.S. 34-12-109 [Vacation; partial vacation; effect on streets]

This section is not applicable to this request.

W.S. 34-12-110 [Vacation; duty of county clerk]
