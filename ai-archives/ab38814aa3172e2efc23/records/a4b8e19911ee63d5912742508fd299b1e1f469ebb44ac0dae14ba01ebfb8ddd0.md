RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING WATER AND SANITARY SEWER
SERVICES, BY OUTSIDE USER WATER AND SANITARY SEWER
SERVICES AGREEMENTS, FOR DEAN BYRNE, FOR LOT 3, ENERGY
PARK ONE, SITUATED IN THE NE¼ OF SECTION 33, TOWNSHIP 13
NORTH, RANGE 67 WEST, OF THE 6TH P.M., LARAMIE COUNTY,
WYOMING.”

WHEREAS, Dean Byrne (Byrne) is the owner of the property in Cheyenne, Wyoming,
more specifically described as:

Lot 3, Energy Park One, according to the official plat filed for record on January
20, 2012 in Book 9, Page 173, Records of Laramie County, Wyoming, situated in
a portion of the NE¼ of Section 33, Township 13 North, Range 67 West, of the
6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming; and

WHEREAS, Byrne submitted application to the Board of Public Utilities (“BOPU”) for
water and sanitary sewer services outside of the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne for the
above-referenced properties; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 3577, BOPU was instructed to present to the
Governing Body for its review any application for the provision of water and/or sanitary sewer
services to any individual or entity outside the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, BOPU has reviewed and approved the application submitted by Byrne, and
BOPU and Byrne will execute Outside User Water and Sanitary Sewer Services Agreements
upon approval by the Governing Body pursuant to Resolution No. 3577; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body has reviewed the applications for water and sanitary
sewer services submitted by Byrne and has determined that approval is in the best interest of the
City of Cheyenne.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the application submitted by Byrne to BOPU for
water and sanitary sewer services outside of the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne for the
property described as Lot 3, Energy Park One, according to the official plat filed for record on
January 20, 2012 in Book 9, Page 173, Records of Laramie County, Wyoming, situated in a
portion of the NE¼ of Section 33, Township 13 North, Range 67 West, of the 6th P.M., Laramie
County, Wyoming, is hereby approved.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that BOPU and Byrne are authorized to execute Outside
User Water and Sanitary Sewer Services Agreements.
By Stefanie Boster at 1:00 pm, Mar 10, 2022
