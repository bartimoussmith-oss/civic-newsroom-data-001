BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1. That the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have) applied for a zoning
map amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map
Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).

Section 2. That on August 5, 2013, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted a
public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended approval of said zone change.

Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change that is
the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.

Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the aforementioned
application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property described as a portion of
the south half of Section 20, T.14N, R.66W, 6th P.M., Cheyenne, Wyoming, to be known as Lots 1 and
2, Block 1, Terre Pont, is hereby changed to CB Community Business.

Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as
described in Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are severable.
If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional, such
unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and
phrases.

Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and publication
and upon the official filing of the affiliated Terre Pont Annexation and Final Plat map with the County
Clerk and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for Laramie County, Wyoming.

PLAT ANALYSIS SUMMARY:
The plat appears to be in compliance with all applicable requirements of the Unified Development Code.
Staff recommends approval of the Preliminary Plat based on the criteria in UDC §2.1.2.d.
DESCRIPTION OF SITE AND SURROUNDINGS:
The subject property is approximately 8.45 acres of vacant land northeast of and adjacent to Frontier
Mall Drive and Prairie Avenue. On the east side of the property is the platted but unbuilt Cutoff Road.
The land raises several feet from Prairie Avenue, then slopes back down to where Rue Terre aligns.
Frontier Mall Drive is fully constructed including the sidewalk. Prairie Avenue is built, but the sidewalk
will need to be constructed as the property develops. Rue Terre and Cutoff Road are not constructed.

Looking northwest on Prairie Avenue. Subject property on right.
PROJECT DATA:
This plat proposes two lots; Lot 1 on the west being 3.1 acres and Lot 2 being 3.16 acres. One hundred forty
feet of right-of-way, or 2.19 acres, will be dedicated to extend Rue Terre. Right-of-way for Cutoff Road is in
place. Lot 1 is proposed for development of an extended-stay hotel. Rue Terre adjacent to the subdivision will
be constructed with development of the hotel, leaving an unconstructed section between where Cutoff Road is
platted and the existing Rue Terre to the southeast, north of K-Mart and a vacant lot behind K-Mart (Lot 2,
Block 1, Terre Fini Subdivision).
At first, both lanes on Rue Terre will use the south half of the right-of-way, the same as it is north of Frontier
Mall Drive behind the shopping center there. The other half of the road will be constructed with future
development to the north. Please the aerial map below for clarification.

4.4 Open Space Systems

4 || Phasing Amenities, Facilities - Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and

amenities and public facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to the anticipated timing
or the likelihood of future phases being constructed as planned.
No development is proposed for Lot 2 at this time, but street improvements will be made to Rue Terre
adjacent to both lots. As mentioned, there will still be a gap southeast of the subject property north of the
Terre Fini lot. As noted in comments below from Engineering, this gap will create pressure on the City
to absorb the cost and construct the remaining portion of Rue Terre in this area. Since the City has not
established impact fees on development for off-site street improvements, any such construction by the
City would be paid for by all taxpayers rather than the development. This conflicts with PlanCheyenne
Foundation 7, which promotes development paying for improvements necessitated by the development.
However, whether the City or the developer pays for the improvements, a reimbursement requirement
could be filed against the adjacent properties so that when they develop they will be required to pay their
share of the improvements.
When development north of Rue Terre occurs Rue Terre will need to be widened to the full width.
No improvements to Cutoff Road are proposed with development of the hotel.
5 || Studies and Reports - The impacts identified in specific studies or technical reports that were
prepared or requested to be prepared in association with the application, and whether the findings and
recommendations of these reports, or any modifications or conditions on approval of the application,
can mitigate these impacts.
A Traffic Impact Study will be required prior to approval of a site plan. Drainage improvements include
extension of an existing stormwater line along Rue Terre. Further review will occur with site plan submittals
when more detailed design plans are submitted.
6 || Zoning - All proposed lots shall be buildable lots according to the existing or proposed zoning
district standards, and result in site design and development patterns that meet the Intent and
Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning district. All parcels not proposed as
