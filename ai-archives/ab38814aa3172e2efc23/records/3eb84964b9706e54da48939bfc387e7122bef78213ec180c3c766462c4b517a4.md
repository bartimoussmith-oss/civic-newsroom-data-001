WHEREAS, City leaders have always looked to the future to secure a reliable water supply
for Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, the Western United States is currently facing unprecedented drought
conditions with short-term and long-term impacts to the City of Cheyenne, including possible
Colorado River curtailment;

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) manages a complex
water supply system and delivers water to meet Cheyenne’s demands. That system is finite;

WHEREAS, future community growth depends on careful stewardship of our water
resources in cooperation with public and private entities; and

WHEREAS, the formation of the Wise Water Action Committee (WWAC) will allow for
a review of best practices and identify those most practical to develop a water conservation
roadmap to ensure a sustainable future for Cheyenne.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, the WWAC shall be comprised of representatives from BOPU,
the Department of Community Recreation and Events, Department of Planning and Development,
and members of the City Council, which representatives will regularly meet and provide clear
communication and direction for all water stakeholders; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the WWAC will look to City leaders for support of
their recommendations to implement water conservation policies and programs in Cheyenne.

PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED this _____ day of ___________________, 2022.
