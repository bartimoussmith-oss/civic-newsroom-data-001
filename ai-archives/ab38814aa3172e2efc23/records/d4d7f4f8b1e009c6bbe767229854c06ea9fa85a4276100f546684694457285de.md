CITY CONTRACT NO. ________
THIS AGREEMENT made and entered into this 1 day of July, 2013, by and between the
st
City of Cheyenne, a municipal corporation of the State of Wyoming, hereinafter referred to as “City,”
whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and Bill G. Hibbler, P.C., an
attorney licensed to practice law in the State of Wyoming, hereinafter referred to as “Hibbler,” whose
address is P.O. Box 2143, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82003-2143.
W I T N E S S E T H:
WHEREAS, the City has a need for the assistance of outside legal counsel for various
services and representation; and
WHEREAS, the City wishes to enter into a contract with an attorney licensed in the State of
Wyoming to perform these services; and
WHEREAS, Hibbler is willing to provide legal services to the City.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions herein
contained, it is hereby agreed as follows:
1.
Hibbler hereby agrees to provide outside legal counsel to the City beginning July 1,
2013, and ending June 30, 2014.
2.
As compensation for services rendered by Hibbler, the City shall pay Hibbler at the
rate of One Hundred Ten Dollars ($110.00) per hour.  The City shall pay Hibbler for expenses
incurred during the performance of this Agreement.
3.
An attorney/client relationship is created between Hibbler and the City.  All matters
involved in the rendering of legal services shall be considered privileged and confidential.  Hibbler
is an independent contractor and nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed to create
a relationship of employer/employee.  The City shall have no right to control or direct the details,
manner or means by which Hibbler performs services under this Agreement, provided that such
services shall be performed to the City’s reasonable satisfaction.  Hibbler agrees to pay all taxes and
fees, including income and social security, that he is obligated to pay as an independent contractor.
