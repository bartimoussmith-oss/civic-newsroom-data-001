BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 8.87 acres of land. The subject property
is developed at the north end with multi-family style development. The proposed plat would subdivide the
parcel into 76 lot consisting of 74 cottage lots, 1 common lot and a lot for the existing development. The
proposed plat is utilizing the Cottage Lot section of the Unified Development Code (6.6.5). There is a
concurrent zone change with this platting action proposing to change the zoning of the proposed cottage lots
to NR-2, Neighborhood Residential – Medium Density. The zone change is PUDC-24-17.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

On the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject land and surrounding area has
been designated as Urban Residential. The Urban Residential designation encourages a range of
more intensive residential uses, although the intent is not to change existing stable
neighborhoods. The existing neighborhood has a mix of housing types and densities, and the
proposed development fits within the range of the existing neighborhood, while providing a
different housing option.
