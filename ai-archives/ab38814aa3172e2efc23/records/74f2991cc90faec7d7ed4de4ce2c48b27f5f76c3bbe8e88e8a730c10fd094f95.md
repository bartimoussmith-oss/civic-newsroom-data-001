RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO CONSENT TO THE

ROUNDHOUSE WIND ENERGY FACILITY PARTIALLY LOCATED ON

WHEREAS, Roundhouse Renewable Energy, LLC ("Roundhouse") is an indirect
subsidiary of NextEra Energy Resources, LLC, the world's largest generator of renewable energy
from wind and sun;

WHEREAS, in 2019, Roundhouse constructed the Roundhouse Wind Energy Facility,
which facility is located in Laramie County and operates through a wind energy lease with the City
of Cheyenne on sixteen thousand seven hundred (16,700) acres of the Belvoir Ranch;

WHEREAS, the Roundhouse Wind Energy Facility is a commercial-scale wind facility
valued at three hundred thirty-five million dollars ($335,000,000.00) spanning a total of thirty
thousand (30,000) acres of public and private land in Laramie County ("Project");

WHEREAS, the Project was sited in two (2) phases by the Department of Environmental
Quality Industrial Siting Council. Phase one of the Project included eighty-two (82) General
Electric wind turbines capable of generating up to two hundred twenty-five (225) megawatts of
clean, renewable energy. The intent of the Project is to generate wind power for northern Colorado
communities. The power generated from phase one of the Project was sold to Platte River Power
Authority under a power purchase agreement for a period of twenty-two (22) years beginning in
2020;

WHEREAS, Roundhouse is in the process of building phase two of the Project. The second
phase will include up to thirty-eight (38) General Electric wind turbines capable of generating up
to one hundred six (106) megawatts of energy. Cheyenne Light, Fuel and Power Transmission has
committed to purchasing power from the Project through a purchase power agreement;

WHEREAS, City of Cheyenne and Roundhouse are parties to a Wind Energy Lease
Agreement dated September 18, 2012, which lease was amended on November 27, 2018, and
memorialized by a Memorandum of Wind Energy Lease Agreement dated April 8, 2019
("Agreement"). The term of the lease agreement is for a period of thirty (30) years;

WHEREAS, over the course of the thirty (30) year lease, it is estimated that the City of
Cheyenne will receive approximately thirty-nine million dollars ($39,000,000.00) in operating
payments;

WHEREAS, Roundhouse would like to partially assign the Agreement to a newly created
subsidiary, Roundhouse Renewable Energy II, LLC ("Roundhouse II") and to establish a co-
tenancy relationship with Roundhouse II; and
