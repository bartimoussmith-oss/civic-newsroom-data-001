25 - 550
Warren Livestock, LLC, Vacation and Modest Plat, Bell
Pasture Subdivision, 3rd Filing
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said AVI, PC, on behalf of Warren Livestock, LLC, submitted
a modest plat application for Bell Pasture, 3rd Filing to replat of Tracts 21 and 22, Bell
Pasture Subdivision, 2nd Filing. He said the subject property is located at 1501 and
1603 Wineglass Rd. Arnold said the purpose of the application is to change the
internal lot line common to Tracts 21 and Tract 22 so both lots will have property
abutting the parcel to the south currently owned by the State of Wyoming.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval with the
adoption of findings of fact a and b with two conditions: 1) All agency review
comments must be addressed prior to recordation of Bell Pasture and 2) The
vacation of Tracts 21 and 22, Bell Pasture Subdivision, 2nd Filing, will become
effective upon the recordation of Bell Pasture, Subdivision, 3rd Filing.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.  Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Zwonitzer, seconded by
Commissioner Hollingshead, to approve a vacation of Tracts 21 and 22,
Bell Pasture Subdivision, 2nd Filing, located in a portion of Sect 36, T15N,
R67W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, and approve the modest plat for
Bell Pasture, 3rd Filing, with two conditions and adopt the findings of fact
a and b of the staff report. The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:

Board of County Commissioners
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
November 18, 2025
Swan Ranch Properties, LLC, Vacation and
Subdivision Permit and Plat, Swan Ranch Rail Park,
19th Filing
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said AVI, PC, on behalf of Swan Ranch Properties, LLC,
submitted an application for approval of the Swan Ranch Rail Park, 19th Filing,
subdivision permit and plat located at Lot 2, Block 1, Swan Ranch Rail Park, 3rd
Filing. He said the subject property is located at 6355 Clear Creek Pkwy. Arnold said
the proposed subdivision will create two tracts from the 5.42-acre parcel.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the adoption
of findings of fact a and b with one condition: All agency comments will be addressed
and/or corrected prior to recordation.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Hollingshead, seconded by
Commissioner Zwonitzer, to approve the vacation of Lot 2, Block 1,Swan
Ranch Rail Park, 3rd Filing, located in a portion of Sects 27 and 34,
T13N, R67W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, and approve the
subdivision permit and plat for Swan Ranch Rail Park, 19th Filing, with
one condition and adopt the findings of fact a and b of the staff report.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:
