Department
Two(2)bidders responded to this bid, and thebid tabulation is attached.
A Mandatory Pre-Bid Meeting was held, and Spradley-Barr, Wireless Advanced
Communications/Bearcom, and Recreational Electric were in attendance.
The bids were received from Fremont Motors, Casper, Wyoming (Submitted a“NO BID'), and
Sp radley Barr Motors, Cheyenne, Wyoming.Sp radley Barr Motors submitted their bid with two
(2)separate body up fitter options, Wireless Advanced Communications/Bearcom($455,715.00)
and Recreational Electric($463,379.00).
Uponfurther review of thebid, it was identified that the model of light bar submitted by the low
bidder body up fitter, Wireless Advanced Communications/Bearcom, was alinear style light bar.
During the mandatory pre-bid meeting, the brands and manufacturers of add on equipment to be
accepted was increased to allowfor fair and equitable bid submissions by different body upfitters.
By allowingfor upfitters to submit alternate brands, the light bar that was originally specified was
opened to submission by alternate brands.The original bid specification called for a 51"Federal
Signal, Police Valor series light bar.The Police Valor light bar is a low profile, non-linear“V-
angles than a typical linear light bar.(Representative depictions of lightbar styles are included
below).Upon review of the linear style light bar and non-linear style light bar, The Cheyenne
Police Department has requested an upgrade to thelightbar to ensure officer safety andvehicle
visibility.
The City of Cheyenne contacted the low bidder body upfitter, Wireless Advanced
Communications/Bearcom, to request a price for an upgrade to the Police Valor‘V-Shape’Light
Bar.Wireless Advanced Communications/Bearcom provided a price of $2,737.00/lightbar. The
original price submission for the non*V-Shape’ linear lightbar was $1,991.00/lightbar.
