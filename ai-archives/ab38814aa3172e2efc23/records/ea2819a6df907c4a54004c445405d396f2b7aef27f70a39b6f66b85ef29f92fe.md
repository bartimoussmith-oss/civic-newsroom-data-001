Zone Change Application/UDC-18-00150
3
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application proposes changing an entire block under the same ownership from MR
Medium-density Residential to MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis. These lots total about 1.6 acres of
land. This land is currently occupied by 16 dwelling units in 8 structures. The structures were originally
built in the 1950’s. At the time the application for the zone change was submitted, the site was actively
used as a residential facility.

The applicant proposes a mix of multi-family and commercial uses at this site. The current MR zone only
allows very limited commercial uses, and multi-family development requires a Conditional Use approval.
The MUB zone allows a mix of uses, but expects that no individual block exceed 50% residential.
Apartments and a variety of commercial uses are allowed in the MUB zone.

As a smaller, walkable block near a commercial corridor and existing residential neighborhoods, this
property meets the intent of the MUB zone district. Development patterns to the south include
commercial development set close to the street with a higher design standard – although zoned PUD.
Area currently zoned MUB is located nearby, but is not contiguous to this block. Contiguous zones do
include a commercial PUD and CB Community Business zoning, but also LR Low-density Residential.

Policies in PlanCheyenne both support and oppose the proposed zone change. PlanCheyenne encourages
the maximization of existing infrastructure and MUB zoning in Mixed-use Activity Centers. PlanCheyenne
also encourages the protection of existing residential neighborhoods and non-commercial zoning in areas
designated Urban Residential.

While MUB zoning is encouraged in Mixed-use Activity Centers, the location of the designated activity
center is focused on the intersection of Western Hills/Storey and Yellowstone – making this property on
the edge of the Activity Center. While the property is designated Urban Residential, property immediately
adjacent is designated Mixed-use Commercial, and the designations on the Future Land Use Map are not
intended to strictly pre-determine zones and uses. On the balance, Staff feels the proposed zone change
is in accordance with the plans and policies outlined in PlanCheyenne

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change.

ZONE CHANGE DATA:  In reviewing, providing comments, and making recommendations and decisions
on a proposed Zone Change Request, the Staff, Planning Commission and City Council shall use the
following criteria of Section 2.2.1.d:

1. PLANCHEYENNE - The application is in accordance with
the development policies and physical patterns identified
in the Comprehensive Plan.

PlanCheyenne is mixed on this zone change, but on the
balance, Staff feels the proposed zone change is in
accordance with the plans and policies outlined in
PlanCheyenne.

PlanCheyenne encourages maximization of existing
infrastructure and redevelopment or revitalization of
underutilized areas. This property is currently developed
in an area where infrastructure is already installed. The
current development on this property is a medium-high
density of residential development. Properties near
