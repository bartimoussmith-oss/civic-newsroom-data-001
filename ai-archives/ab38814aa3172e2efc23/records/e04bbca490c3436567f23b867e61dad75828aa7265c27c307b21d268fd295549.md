RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
January 7, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in “Sine Die” Session on this date at 2:30
p.m. in the Cheyenne Civic Center.  Those present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and
Mr. Valdez.  Also present were Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, and Dan White, City Attorney.
Following the pledge of allegiance, Mayor Kaysen welcomed those in attendance and stated the
purpose of the session was to ceremonially recognize outgoing Council members for their years of
service on City Council.  He commented generally on community leadership and governing body
achievements; expressed appreciation to outgoing Council members for their time, effort and dedication
to the community, and recognized the supporting roles of families, friends and co-workers.
At Mayor Kaysen’s request, Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, read a ceremonial resolution in its
entirety honoring outgoing Council member Amber Ash, entitled as follows:  “A Ceremonial Resolution
in Recognition of the Honorable Amber Ash for her Dedication and Outstanding Contributions to the
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and its Residents.”  Mayor Kaysen noted Ms. Ash was sworn in as a
County Commissioner earlier in the afternoon and explained, pursuant to state statute, Ms. Ash was
ineligible to hold both the position of City Council member and County Commissioner at the same time,
which was why she was not seated at the dais.  He advised Ms. Ash had submitted her resignation as
a member of City Council prior to being sworn in at the County.  Mayor Kaysen presented Ms. Ash with
an engraved plaque noting her 4 years of membership on City Council and thanked her for her service
to the community.  Ms. Ash briefly commented on her years of service, expressed appreciation for being
able to serve the community as a City Council member, and stated she was looking forward to
continuing her service to the community as a County Commissioner. (#5461)
At Mayor Kaysen’s request, Ms. Intlekofer read a ceremonial resolution in its entirety honoring
outgoing Council member Patrick Collins, entitled as follows:  “A Ceremonial Resolution in
Recognition of the Honorable Patrick Collins for his Dedication and Outstanding Contributions to the
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and its Residents.”  Mayor Kaysen presented Mr. Collins with an engraved
plaque noting his 12 years of membership on City Council and thanked him for his service to the
community. Mr. Collins briefly commented on his years of service as a City Council member,
expressing appreciation to the voters of Ward II, co- City Council members who had served with him,
City staff, and members of his family for their support. (#5464)
At Mayor Kaysen’s request, Ms. Intlekofer read a ceremonial resolution in its entirety honoring
outgoing Council member Don Pierson, entitled as follows:  “A Ceremonial Resolution in Recognition
of the Honorable Don Pierson for his Dedication and Outstanding Contributions to the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, and its Residents.”  Mayor Kaysen presented Mr. Pierson with an engraved plaque
noting his 20 years of membership on City Council and thanked him for his service to the community.
Mr. Pierson briefly commented on his years of service as a City Council member and thanked the voters
of the community for allowing him to serve them. (#5462)
At Mayor Kaysen’s request, Ms.  Intlekofer read a ceremonial resolution in its entirety honoring
outgoing Council member Jack R. Spiker, entitled as follows:  “A Ceremonial Resolution in
Recognition of the Honorable Jack R. Spiker for his Dedication and Outstanding Contributions to the
