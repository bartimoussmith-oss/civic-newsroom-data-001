2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2)

The proposed plat divides eight lots into eighteen lots. The northern lot (currently Lot 1, except
the North 12 feet, Block 3, Centennial Heritage) is proposed to be divided into three (3) AD1
lots, each with 25-foot frontages. The southern lots (Centennial Heritage, 2nd Filing Lots 1 thru 5,
Block 1), are proposed to be divided into fifteen (15) lots (a mix of AD and SD lots).

Townhomes mixed with single-family and duplex development creates a mix of housing options
within an area which is considered beneficial from an urban design perspective.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
2.1.2.d.3)

Community Facility Fees (4.2):
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre.
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are
exempt from fees.

payment. Centennial Heritage, 2nd Filing is assumed to have paid all applicable plat fees at
the time it was filed; however, since this plat adds previously exempt land, a portion of the
required fees will still be charged with this platting action.

Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a
building permit for each structure.
Residential Enhancement Fee
$400 per dwelling unit
Commercial Enhancement Fee $1,100 per acre

Transportation Network (4.3):
The applicant does not propose any extensions or new connections. Townhome
(“attached”) lots are not allowed front access if the lot is less than 36’ wide. The plat does not
provide an access easement or alley for access to the central lots. These lots would need relief
from UDC 4.3.6.c to allow front access. Block 1 is too large; however, Staff recommends the
Planning Commission or City Council approve an exception due to the railroad proving a
low potential for an effective connection through this property (per UDC 4.3.3.c.2).

The applicant will need to provide an alley for rear-loading townhomes or provide a
subdivision standards waiver. The plat proposes front access for townhome (AD1) lots,
which is prohibited by UDC 4.3.6.c. The applicant has submitted a subdivision standards
waiver to request relief.

Open Space (4.4):
With the Centennial Heritage, 2nd Filing plat, a parcel of land south of this proposed
development was dedicated to the City Greenway and this proposed infill project will
provide a connection to the Greenway trail.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat does appear to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this
development. However, the preliminary plat shows water services set between sanitary
services, which could present future undesired maintenance access issues (at the corner of
Raleigh Drive and Taft Avenue). Water services will also need to be discussed for civil plans.
These issues will be coordinated and resolved with construction.

The submitted plat does not show an ‘escape’ easement for Black Hills Energy to make its
system ‘whole’ as the development is built (for which an 8-foot-wide easement along the
west property line of Lot 8, Block 1 was requested). The applicant and Black Hills Energy
need to coordinate to resolve the issue and add any required easements to the plat.

Public Safety Fees (4.6):
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any commercial building or
residential building on any of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees
are payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure.

The application complies with this review criterion, with conditions.

No specific phasing is proposed with this project.

Review criterion not applicable.

serve as a blanket easement for open space, drainage, utilities, and public or private shared
facilities. This lot will be established by equal, undivided interest of all owner(s) of fee-simple
Lots 1 thru 14, Block 1.

The application complies with this review criterion.

8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development for
adjacent property considering the existing zoning and Comprehensive Plan policies for that
property.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.8)

This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. The lots are
proposed for future single unit residential development which is similar to what is in the area as
well.

The application complies with this review criterion.

9) The application is not harmful to the public health, safety, and welfare. (UDC 2.1.2.d.9)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed plat would not adversely affect the public health, safety,
