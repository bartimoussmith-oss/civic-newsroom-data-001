EXHIBIT A

Landfill Tent Building Project
1441 Happy Jack Rd
Cheyenne Wy

I.
PROJECT DESCRIPTION

The Cheyenne City Landfill has a 31,500 square foot tent building that has sustained severe
structural damage from ensuing windstorms. There is a total of sixteen trusses, spanning 150
feet, that will need to be removed and recycled. The Engineering and Consulting Alliance (ECA)
will create and oversee a work plan to remove the existing fabric and trusses, without damaging
the existing concrete pad and push walls. The Engineer will design and manage the construction
of a new cost-effective structure to be placed on the existing foundation.

II.
SCOPE OF WORK
The project consists of the following tasks:
Kickoff Meeting:  The meeting will be held at the Landfill, between ECA the and the City of
Cheyenne representative(s). The meeting should last no longer than one hour and will cover the
project approach, schedule, and discussion regarding the deconstruction of the existing
structure and construction of a conex metal structure.
Conceptual Design: Based on input from the Kick-Off Meeting, the Consultant sill develop a
conceptual design for the conex tent and other supporting infrastructure.
Design and Specifications: Based off the kickoff meeting, the Consultant will design and prepare
construction documents for the deconstruction of the existing tent building. Consultant will also
design a new cost effective that can withstand sustained winds of 120 mph for a minimum of 12
hours.
The following should be considered:
➢ Consultant will collect all necessary data related to the project, including but not limited
to, Surveying and Geotechnical work and any other work that would be necessary to
produce the plans and specifications for the project.
➢ Design will be completed in three phases: 60%, 90% and Final. The 60% and 90% will
include periods for City review. Conference call, or meeting, will be held after each
review period.
➢ 60% Design Services: The services provided in the 60% design phase shall include the
following:
o Refinement of design scope based on results of Conceptual Design
