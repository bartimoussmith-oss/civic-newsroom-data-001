MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

April 18, 2022
SUBJECT:
Re-Appropriation to be considered for approval

On May 9, 2022, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee to
re-appropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2022 City of Cheyenne Budget per W.S. 16-4-112
and 16-4-113.  Below is more detailed information regarding this re-appropriation request.

GENERAL FUND RESERVES REQUEST
Public Defender Budget Increase
The City has budgeted a total of $100,000 in Fiscal Year 2022, which includes an additional
$50,000 approved by Council on January 10, 2022; however, public defender costs have continued
to exceed expectations with the significant increase from $4,500 per month last fiscal year to an
average of nearly $11,000 per month this fiscal year.  A total of $97,929 has been spent through
the end of March.  I am proposing an additional increase to this General Fund line item of $27,000
to get through the remainder of the fiscal year.
Nuisance Abatement Budget Increase
The City partnered with the Wyoming Department of Transportation and contracted with S&S
Builders to install rip rap and concrete to prevent homeless population habitation under the
Lincolnway and Missile Drive Crow Creek underpass structure.  This will prevent bridge damage
and promote a safer community.  A $31,250 increase to the nuisance abatement budget is being
requested to pay for the work completed.
Shelter Housing and Care of Animals
Per City Contract #7464 with the Cheyenne Animal Shelter (CAS), the City must pay CAS a daily
rate of $45 per animal for continued shelter housing and care services beyond ten (10) days as
ordered by the court.  On January 4, 2022, Animal Control received a dispatch call requesting
assistance at the Candlewood Suites regarding an animal cruelty case.  They found 12 adult dogs
and puppies in a wooden box on top of a trailer and the temperature was below freezing.  The

Cheyenne Police Department Non-Insured Loss
The Cheyenne Police Department (CPD) has had ten (10) incidents so far this fiscal year occur
where the repair expenses are less than the City’s deductible limit.  Therefore, the CPD must pay
for the repairs directly.  An $11,955 increase to the CPD’s non-insured loss budget is being
requested to pay for repair expenses for the following incidents:
• An employee was at a gas station and had to back up because the exit was blocked by a
tow truck. While backing up, this employee backed into a dumpster causing damage to the
rear of the vehicle.
• An employee was transporting a vehicle to BearCom Inc. in Greeley, Colorado, and noticed
that the front passenger side door glass was cracked.
• An employee's vehicle was parked in the driveway of his home when the back window was
broken out.
• An employee was escorting American Medical Response (AMR) to the flight for life pad
which he believed was still located off Evans Ave. The employee pulled in front of the
ambulance to block traffic. The flight for life pad is no longer located on Evans Ave. and
the ambulance continued straight through the intersection and the employee's vehicle was
struck by the ambulance.
• An employee was turning onto the north entrance to the CPD parking structure when he
observed some damage to the east side of the valley pan at the entrance. The employee
tried to avoid it and turned into the west side of the valley pan and he hit a pothole which
caused damage to the front right tire, skid plate, bumper cover, grille and possibly the hood.
• An employee was pursuing a suspect when a spare tire flew out of the bed of the suspect’s
vehicle and landed in the middle of the street. The employee tried to miss the tire but hit it
on the driver side of the vehicle.
• An employee was conducting surveillance when she heard a loud noise and then observed
the rear passenger window on the vehicle had been damaged. The window sustained an
approximate one-inch hole and the rest of the window was cracked.
• An employee found damage to the front fender of his vehicle. He believes the damage
occurred after a snowstorm when he drove through snow and ice in an alley.
• An employee was in a vehicle pursuit and ran over spike strips in the roadway. Damage
occurred to the front right tire.
• An employee was driving westbound on Lincolnway when his passenger mirror hit the
driver's side mirror of a parked Fire Department vehicle.

Increase in Jail Costs
The costs to house inmates at the county jail are exceeding the anticipated budget.  The CPD has
paid the county $394,840 for inmate housing services for the months of July 2021 through March
2022.  With a quarter of the fiscal year remaining, it is anticipated these expenses will exceed the
budgeted amount of $466,000.   I am requesting a $75,000 increase to the CPD’s budget for these
expenses.
Hydraulic Power Unit for Elevator at Jack Spiker Parking Garage
Facilities Maintenance staff made an unanticipated, urgent repair to the elevator at the Jack Spiker
Parking Garage due to the failure of the current hydraulic power unit.  The cost to replace the failed
hydraulic power unit is $42,665.
City Contract with Cheyenne Animal Shelter (CAS)
The City budgeted $450,000 for the Cheyenne Animal Shelter (CAS) FY 2022 contract; however,
this amount was budgeted prior to the final negotiations and contract execution in December 2021.
The increased contract is $528,000 for shelter services.  Therefore, an additional $78,000 is being
requested to cover expenses for the remainder of the fiscal year.

The City of Cheyenne has received the following new grants.  They are as follows, by fund:

• $2,500,000 – Wyoming Business Council – BRC Project Bison Microsoft Data Center
