WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 2.64.020ofthe Cheyenne City Code, the Governing Body
improvements and landscape features having historic character or special historical or aesthetic
interest orvalue...is a public necessity"; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is the owner of the property and structure known as the
Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station located at 1504 Dillon Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne provides ongoing basic maintenance of the Cheyenne
Auxiliary Pumping Station property and structure; and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station has historical value to the downtown
development area known as the West Edge; and
WHEREAS, a 2014 study of the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station by the Tatanka
Historical Association indicated that,“while the structure has deteriorated and been substantially
altered, a careful restoration could make it eligible for National Register of Historic Places
designation"; and
WHEREAS, a 2016 study ofthe Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station by TDSiof Cheyenne
indicated “the Pumphouse is one of the most important historic facilities owned by the City of
repurposing"; and
WHEREAS, the National Trust for Historic Preservation and its affiliate Main Street
Wyoming promote the revitalization of older and historic commercial buildings in downtown areas
andhas offered assistance with preserving the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station; and
WHEREAS, Historic Cheyenne, Inc., was formed as a nonprofit entity in 2012 in order to
promote historic preservation andto assist inthe protection of historic properties, buildings, and
structures in Cheyenne and is interested in the continuing welfare and preservation of the Cheyenne
Auxiliary Pumping Station; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body has determined that it is in the best interests of the
community to preserve the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station with the cooperation and assistance
of Historic Cheyenne, Inc.
NOW, THEREFORE, BEIT RESOLVEDBY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THECITY
OFCHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne recognizes the historical interest and value
of the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station located at 1504 Dillon Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming,
and directs the City of Cheyenne to proceed with the steps necessary to protect and enhance this
historic structure.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City of Cheyenne shall accomplish the following:
1. Stimulate greater governmental and public awareness and appreciation of the
importance of the Cheyenne Auxiliary Pumping Station to the residents of Cheyenne.
