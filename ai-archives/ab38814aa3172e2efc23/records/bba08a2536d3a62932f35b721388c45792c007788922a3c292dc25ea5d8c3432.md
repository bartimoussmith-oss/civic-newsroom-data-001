```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

November 5, 2024
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-4-25 for Three (3) Fire Apparatus for Cheyenne Fire
Rescue
 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-4-25 was solicited for Fire Apparatus for Cheyenne Fire Rescue.

One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1.
Front Range Fire Apparatus (Frederick, CO)

The bids were reviewed by John Kopper, Andrew Dykshorn, Neil Durante, John Fitzgerald, Ryan
Anderson (All - CFR), and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance) and their recommendation is to accept
the lowest responsive bid from Front Range Fire Apparatus.

The total requested bid award, is for Three (3) Fire Apparatus, and acceptance of Three (3) Trade-
Ins, for an amount not to exceed $5,023,363.63.

Delivery for the Two (2) Type 1 Pumpers is 1445-1536 Days (3.96 – 4.21 Years)

Delivery for the One (1) Ladder Unit is 1383-1475 Days (3.79 – 4.04 Years)

This Bid included pricing options for Pre-Payment discounts, however, the City has opted to not
provide any Pre-Payment at this time.

Funding:
Future Special Purpose Option Tax (6th Penny) Ballot Item
```
