RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
June 11, 2012
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson,  Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker
and Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Mr. Allen.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Upon request by Mr. Collins, and pursuant to support received by
Mr. Pierson and Mr. Brown,  agenda item #23(d) was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Upon
request by Mr. Pierson, and pursuant to support received by Mr. Collins and Mr. Brown, agenda
item #23(a) was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Collins moved to approve remaining
Consent Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Brown.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of
the governing body present.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on May 29, 2012.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest in voucher payable to Ameri-
Tech HVAC Services) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Valdez moved to approve payment of
vouchers as presented, seconded by Ms. Ash.  Upon inquiry regarding a voucher payable to AE Com
Technical Services, Inc. for an enhancement plan for Cheyenne area classified roadways, Matt
Ashby, Planning Services Director, advised services to be provided will involve the gathering of
information on best practices utilized by other communities to manage and maintain street scape
amenities along the public rights-of-way, noting vendor services will be paid through MPO
(Metropolitan Planning Organization) funding.  A request was made that a follow-up memo from
Tom Mason, MPO Director, be submitted to clarify professional services that will be provided.
Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.
Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown returned to Council Chambers.
Ordinance approving and establishing revised water rates, sewer rates, tap fees, system
development fees, administrative fees, service charges and penalties and septic and commercial
dump rates, as recommended by the Board of Public Utilities (BOPU).  Reporting for Finance
Committee, Mr. Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Collins. Upon
inquiry, Tim Wilson, BOPU Director, provided information on certain water and sewer rates as
compared with other municipalities in Wyoming and Colorado.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” --
all members of the governing body present. (#3948)

of the Cheyenne Civic Center for public performances and rehearsals.  Motion (per Consent

Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Symphony Orchestra  for use
of the Cheyenne Civic Center for Youth Education Concerts.  Kim Lovett, Executive Director,
Cheyenne Symphony Orchestra (CSO), introduced members of the board present in the audience,
and explained the agreement is for a community project that involves all 5th and 6th grade students
within the City.  She stated the purpose of the concerts are to provide cultural literacy, music
appreciation and general musical knowledge to students.  Anna Marie Hales, Cheyenne Symphony
Board member, expressed appreciation for past City funding for the project and encouraged
continued funding.  Mr. Brown moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $6,453.00, seconded
by Mr. Valdez.  Comments followed on funding participation level for the concerts by the City,
Cheyenne Symphony and the school district with concern expressed relative to whether funding
assistance for this purpose is part of the City's mission as City funds are not expended for other types
of school district educational programs, such as math or science education.  Ms. Hales discussed the
contract arrangement the Civic Center has with the school district.  During comments, Mayor
Kaysen clarified the previous agenda item involving CSO use of the Civic Center provides revenue
to the City and is not a City expenditure amount.  Upon inquiry, Ms. Lovett provided information
on concert costs and funding assistance received, which includes in-kind donations, to help cover
the cost.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present with the
exception of Mr. Collins and Dr. Rinne voting "no". (#5860)
