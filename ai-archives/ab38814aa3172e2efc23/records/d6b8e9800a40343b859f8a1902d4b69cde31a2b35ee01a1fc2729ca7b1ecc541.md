SUBJECT:
Railcar Storage Agreement between Cheyenne Rail Hub, LLC and the City
of Cheyenne

Please find the attached proposed Railcar Storage Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and
Cheyenne Rail Hub, LLC being initiated by the City Engineering and Planning and Development
Departments.

Reason for Agreement:

The primary reasons for the Railcar Storage Agreement:
▪ Implementation of the Tourism Master Plan.
In 2021, Cheyenne approved a new Tourism Master Plan that included the development
of a new railroad attraction as one of its top goals.  The main objective of that project is
to showcase Cheyenne’s railroad heritage.   As a part of implementing that plan, the
Union Pacific Railroad (UPRR) is donating fifteen (15) passenger railcars and a caboose
which will require temporary storage.
▪ Temporary storage of up to fifteen (15) rail cars.
The railcars will be stored in an inconspicuous portion in the northwest portion of the rail
park not directly visible or accessible to the public.
▪ Potential rehabilitation and/ or asbestos abatement staging area.   Provide a potential
area for the purpose of either renovation and/or asbestos abatement of some of the
railcars.

RAILCAR STORAGE AGREEMENT
This Railcar Storage Agreement “Agreement” dated ____________, between Cheyenne
Rail Hub, LLC (“Storage Facility”) and the City of Cheyenne, (“City”), individually and
collectively referred to as “Party” or “Parties”.  The purpose of this Agreement is to provide for
the temporary storage by Storage Facility of railcars owned, leased, or managed by the City.
In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by
and between the parties as follows:
I.
STORAGE OF RAILCARS
A.
The City desires to store up to fifteen (15) rail cars on the ground near, but
not on, the Storage Facility’s tracks for the purpose of renovation and
asbestos abatement.  The City of Cheyenne will be responsible for loading
and unloading of the rail cars either on or off the tracks or on or off a
vehicular freight transport.
B.
If the City desires to store its railcars on the Storage Facility’s tracks, it will
notify the Storage Facility of the number of railcars it wishes to store and
the dates on which the railcars will be tendered for storage.  The Storage
Facility will promptly advise as to whether, and to what extent, it can
accommodate the City’s request.
II.
FEES
For each railcar which is stored pursuant to the term of this Agreement, Storage
Facility will receive the following fees:
A.
Storage of the rail cars will be without cost to the City of Cheyenne
for a period of one year from the execution of this agreement.
B.
After the one-year period, a ten (10) dollar per railcar, per day for
each day railcar or fraction thereof is on Storage Facility's tracks or
property for the purpose of storage pursuant to this Agreement
(“Monthly Fee”).
C.
All outside costs incurred from the Class One rail Operator Union
Pacific Railroad (UPRR), or the Short Line Operator (SRRR),
moving into or out of the Storage Facility will be passed onto, or paid
by the City of Cheyenne.
III.
INVOICING; PAYMENT OF CHARGES
All fees shall be invoiced monthly by Storage Facility to the City.
IV.
FREEDOM FROM LIENS
Storage Facility covenants and agrees that no claim of lien, statutory or otherwise
will be made against City's rail cars stored by Storage Facility but that Storage
Facility will make and collect all charges for storage as an open unsecured account;
