WHEREAS, the Fiscal Year (FY) 2022 Assistance to Firefighters Grant ("AFG") is a grant
program from the Department of Homeland Security ("DHS") and the Federal Emergency
Management Agency ("FEMA"). The primary goal of the AFG grant is to meet the firefighting
and emergency response needs of fire departments and non-affiliated emergency medical service
organizations;

WHEREAS, AFG has helped firefighters and other first responders obtain critically needed
resources necessary for protecting the public and emergency personnel from fire and related
hazards.  Since 2001, AFG has helped firefighters and other first responders obtain critically
needed equipment, protective gear, emergency vehicles, training and other resources necessary for
protecting the public and emergency personnel from fire and related hazards;

WHEREAS, the AFG Program requires that certain criteria is met as described in the AFG
application guidance materials the City of Cheyenne’s application met that criteria;

WHEREAS, On August 11, 2023, FEMA awarded the City of Cheyenne three hundred
eighty-seven thousand forty-three dollars and sixty-three cents ($387,043.63) through its safety
and operations program; and,

WHEREAS, as a condition of this grant, the City is required to contribute non-federal funds
equal to or greater than ten percent (10%) of the federal funds awarded, or thirty-eight thousand
seven hundred four dollars and thirty-seven cents ($38,704.37) for a total approved budget of four
hundred twenty-five thousand seven hundred and forty-eight dollars ($425,748.00).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne Governing Body approve the receipt of
three hundred eighty-seven thousand forty-three dollars and sixty-three cents ($387,043.63) in
federal funds for the AFG program and authorizes the contribution of ten percent (10%) of the
federal funds awarded, or thirty-eight thousand seven hundred four dollars and thirty-seven cents
($38,704.37), from fifth and sixth penny funds in support of this program for the purpose of
purchasing breathable air fill stations.

4:39 pm, Aug 24 2023
