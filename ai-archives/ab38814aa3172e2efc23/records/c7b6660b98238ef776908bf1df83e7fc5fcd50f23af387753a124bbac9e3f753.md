RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
February 14, 2015
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 8:30 a.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining with
Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters (I.A.F.F.), Local No.
279.  Present were:  MAYOR: Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Mr. Brown, Mr. Cook, Mr.
Johnson, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, and Mr. Shanor.  Absent: Mr. Roybal, Mr. Valdez and Ms. Williams.
Also present were: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Dan White, City Attorney, and Lois Huff, City
Treasurer.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.  Mayor Kaysen advised that any executive session of
the governing body held during the meeting was pursuant to authority provided under the Public
Meetings Act.
Mayor Kaysen requested Local No. 279 present the proposed Ground Rules as combined (i.e.
City’s and Union’s proposed rules) by the Union.  Copies of the combined rules, titled Ground Rules
for the 2015-2017 Collective Labor Agreement, were distributed.  Jonathon Bertsch, President, I.A.F.F.
Local 279, introduced O’Kelley Pearson, attorney on behalf of I.A.F.F. Local 279.  Ms. Pearson
reviewed sections contained in the proposed combined rules.  It was noted the proposed rules, as well
as the Union’s contract proposal, are for a two year term.
Dan White, City Attorney, requested, as the combined Ground Rules are being presented for the
first time at the meeting, the governing body move into executive session to discuss.  Mr. Cook moved
that the governing body meet in executive session, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Motion carried.  Voting
“yes” -- all members of the governing body present.  Mayor Kaysen asked Mr. White to identify City
staff requested to be present at the executive session in addition to the governing body. [City Clerk note:
Audio recording of the meeting was stopped; Executive session convened at 8:52 a.m. in the Mayor’s
Conference Room] During the executive session, Mr. Shanor, citing Wyo. Stat. §16-4-405, voiced
objection that wording in Section 14 of the proposed Ground Rules forces the governing body to take
action in executive session without the ability to make statements on that action in an open meeting
session in violation of Wyo. Stat. §16-4-403(a).
Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting at
10:05 a.m.  Upon request by Mayor Kaysen, Mr. White reviewed the governing body’s directive on
desired changes to the title and certain sections contained in the combined Ground Rules.  Clarification
of wording regarding proposed changes was provided through dialogue between Mr. White and Ms.
Pearson.  Mr. Bertsch and Ms. Pearson acknowledged agreement to the Ground Rules with the City’s
requested changes as reviewed by Mr. White.  Mayor Kaysen recessed the special meeting at 10:16 a.m.
to allow for the Ground Rules to be prepared in final form for signatures to be acquired prior to moving
forward with proposed contract negotiations. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was
stopped]
Mayor Kaysen reconvened the special meeting at 10:55 a.m. and thanked the parties for
