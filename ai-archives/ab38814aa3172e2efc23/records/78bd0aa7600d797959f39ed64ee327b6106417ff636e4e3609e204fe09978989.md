18. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a zone change from AR - Agricultural
Residential to MU - Mixed Use for Tracts 1, 2, 3, and 5, Gleich Tracts,
Laramie County, WY.
25 - 055
Gleich Tracts, Zone Change
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said AVI, PC, on behalf of Lance E Gleich and Mindy
Grunewald, submitted an application for a zone change for Tracts 1, 2, 3, and 5 Gleich
Tracts. He said the application was submitted to change the zoning district from AR -
Agricultural Residential to MU - Mixed Use to accommodate the expansion of the
water well business and allow the existing residence to remain. Arnold said a
concurrent application was also submitted with a site plan for a shop, office, and
employee and equipment parking. Arnold said the site plan will address comments
from Public Works regarding deed restrictions and easement notes noted on the
Gleich Tracts plat, and the site plan will be approved administratively and included in
the report for information.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the zone
change with the adoption of findings of fact a and b with no conditions.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Heath, to approve the zone change for Tracts 1, 2, 3, and
5, Gleich Tracts, Laramie County, WY, from AR to MU with no conditions
and adopt the findings of fact a and b of the staff report. The motion
carried by the following vote:

19. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a subdivision permit and plat for Recco
Ranch, situated in the NW1/4 of Sect 11, T14N, R65W of the 6th PM,
Laramie County, WY.
25 - 059
Recco Ranch, Subdivision Permit and Plat
Proposed Change
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of Recco, Inc,
submitted a subdivision permit and plat application for property in the SW1/4 of the
NW1/4 of Sect 11, T14N, R65W. He said the application was submitted to subdivide
83 acres into 14 residential lots of around six acres each.
Shane Hansen, agent for the applicant, said the applicant would like the third
condition removed and to build the remainder of the access easement to a 24-ft wide
fire apparatus because it is a family exemption. Commissioner Thompson asked if
the access easement could be dedicated as a public road. County Attorney Mark
Voss said it would need to go through Title 24, the road petition process.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the
subdivision permit and plat with the adoption of findings of fact a, b, c and d with three
conditions: 1) A non-adverse Chapter 23 DEQ recommendation is received by the
Planning Dept prior to plat recordation; 2) The developer of the subdivision shall build
Railway Ln from Railroad Rd as shown on the plat up to the access of the exempt
subdivision to the north to county standards and shall build Recco Ranch Rd and
Raymond Trl as shown on the plat to county standards; and 3) The developer shall
build the remainder of the private access easement duly recorded at Bk 2421, Pg
1003, to county road standards.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Zwonitzer, move to approve the subdivision permit and plat
for Recco Ranch with the three conditions outlined in the staff report and
adopt findings of fact a, b, c and d of the staff report.
Commissioner Thompson asked about the agent's request not to build the access
easement to county standards. He added the entire portion needs to be built to county
standards because, at some point, the northern lots will sell.
Chairman Malm asked if the applicant was aware of a proposed change in county
road standards that would reduce right-of-way width by approximately 6 ft.  He asked
if the proposed change, under consideration by the board, was more appealing as a
condition to the applicant. Taft Love, applicant, said it would be a great compromise,
