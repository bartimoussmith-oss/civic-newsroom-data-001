MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

March 20, 2023
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-11-24 for Six (6) Trucks for the City of Cheyenne (Mid-
Size, 1 Ton, Cab & Chassis)
 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-11-24 was solicited for Six (6) Trucks for the City of Cheyenne (Mid-Size, 1 Ton, Cab &
Chassis).

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1. Ken Garff Cheyenne
2. Fremont Motor Company – Submitted ‘No Bid’

The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Steven Trowbridge (Weed & Pest),
Steve Gaer (Facilities), and Bill Gonzalez (Street & Alley), and their recommendation is to accept
the lowest responsive bid from Ken Garff Cheyenne.

The total requested bid award is for $364,251.00 for the purchase of Six (6) Trucks.

Funding:
Weed & Pest:  $86,514.00, less Trade (-$7,500.00) = $79,014.00
010-17-1702-44-43102 (Weed & Pest / Equipment (over $5,000))

Facilities Maintenance:  $78,997.00, less Trade (-$5,000.00) = $73,997.00
020-26-2615-40-43102 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Equipment (over $5,000))

Fleet Maintenance:  $84,647.00, less Trade (-$6,500) = $78,147.00
020-26-2615-40-43102 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Equipment (over $5,000))

Street & Alley:  $106,938.00, less Trade (-$15,000.00) = $91,938.00
020-26-2616-42-43102  (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Equipment (over $5,000))

Parks – Greenway:  $41,155.00
