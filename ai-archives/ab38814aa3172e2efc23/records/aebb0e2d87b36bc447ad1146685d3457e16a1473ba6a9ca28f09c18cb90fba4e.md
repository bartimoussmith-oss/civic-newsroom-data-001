Planning Commission
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
November 14, 2024
Heidi Larson, Sam Farstad, and Neal Rickner, representing AirLoom Energy, from
Laramie, Wyoming, were present to introduce their company. Founded in 2016 the
company is developing a wind energy device that is not a horizontal wind turbine but is
based on a track employing engineering methods and standard for the roller coaster
industry making it safer and creating less noise and disruption. 100 foot in height with
limited view shed impact at this time it can produce 1 MW of energy per track. It also
has the advantage of being able to be located near areas with height restrictions and
areas difficult to access.
AirLoom Energy's wind turbine design utilizes sites with lower wind, are made using
low-cost mass manufactured components from recyclable materials, materials can be
moved by standard transportation, and are easy to install and maintain. This is in
contrast to the disadvantages of the large scale turbines that are made of
non-recyclable materials, difficult to transport and handle, expensive to make, and
restrict views.
Completing their prototype demonstration in 2023, AirLoom Energy secured funding
for developing a pilot project that will be located in Laramie County. The site will be 20
to 40 acres in size, have a 20 meter track height, and will target 400kW of nameplate
capacity. It will not be connected to the grid since it is strictly for product testing and
demonstration. In the next few weeks they will be submitting a permit application to
Laramie County Planning & Development for their pilot project.
Land Use:Variances/Board App./Plats
3.
PUBLIC HEARING regarding the review and recommendation of the
Maverick Corner, 2nd Filing Subdivision Permit and Plat.
24 - 529
PZ-24-00084 PC 11.14.24 Mtng Pkt
Attachments:
Cate Cundall, Associate Planner, said that the Maverick Corner, 2nd Filing intends to
divide a 28.18 acre parcel into three residential tracts. The subject property is located
in the LU zone district and is currently vacant agricultural land. The surround area
consists of a mixture of subdivisions and agricultural land. Water and sanitary sewer
will be provided by individual wells and septic systems. The subdivision will be
accessed off Klipstein Road, with Tract 3 being accessed via an 80 foot easement on
the west side or a 40 foot easement on the east side of the parcel.
Cotton Jones, applicant's agent, was present to answer any questions.
Vice Chairman opened the Public Hearing for comments. Hearing no comments the
hearing was closed and a motion and discussion was called for.
Natacha Gasper moved, seconded by Mark Smith to recommend approval
of the Subdivision Permit and Plat for Maverick Corner, 2nd Filing, to the
Laramie County Board of Commissioners and adopt the findins of facts a
and b of the staff report with no conditions. Vote 3-0.

Murray Horse Creek Zone Change.
24 - 530
PZ-24-00086 LCPC STAFF REPORT PKG 11.14.24
Attachments:
Bryce Hamilton, Associate Planner, said that Murray Development, LLC submitted a
Zone Change application for Tract 1, Murray Hill Estates, 7th Filing Subdivision that
lies on the southwest side of the I-25 Interchange at Horse Creek Road. The property
is currently zoned A-1-Agricultural Residential and they wish to change to a
CB-Community Business zone district to prepare the parcel for commercial
development. The project meets the zone change criteria since amending the zone
district map is consistent with plan and policies of Laramie County.
Mark Christensen, applicant's agent, spoke on the project and said that this zone
change request to Community Business is to bring this 20 acre parcel more inline with
the future land use map that has designated this corner as mixed use commercial.
There is currently no proposed development for this site so there is no specific site
plan.
Vice Chairman Watkins opened the Public Hearing for comments. Hearing no
comments the hearing was closed and a motion and discussion called for.
Mark Smith moved, Jack Stadel seconded to adopt the findings of facts a
and b of the staff report and to recommend approval of a Zone Change
from A-1 to CB for Tract 1, Murray Hill Estates, 7th Filing, Laramie County,
WY to the Laramie County Board of Commissioners with no conditions.
Vote 4-0
