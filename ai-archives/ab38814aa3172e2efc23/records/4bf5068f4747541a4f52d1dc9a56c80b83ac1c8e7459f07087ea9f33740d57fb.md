MEMORANDUM
TO:
The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:
September 12, 2024
SUBJECT:
Reappropriation to be considered for approval
On September 23, 2024, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance
Committee to reappropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025 City of Cheyenne Budget per
W.S. § 16-4-112 and 16-4-113.  Below is more detailed information regarding this re-
appropriation request.
ALL FUNDS - ENCUMBRANCES AND ROLLOVER ACCOUNTS (RESERVES)
Each September my department prepares a large budget reappropriation request that includes
carryover balances and encumbrances from the previous fiscal year for various City funds,
including the One Percent Optional Tax Fund, Specific Purpose Tax Fund, and others.
When the annual budget is approved each June, you may have noticed that numerous city funds
are not included.  This is because funds available to carry over are not known until after the fiscal
year ends.  Therefore, I have included carryover balances in this reappropriation request.
I am also including General Fund line items that have unspent balances from FY 2024 to be carried
over to FY 2025 for a total of $1,053,750.  This includes:
•
$48,475 - City Council Ward funds
•
$25,750 - Police Department targeting system at the shooting range
•
$411,441 - Community Recreation and Events Capital Lottery Funds
•
$350,000 - Thomas Heights drainage improvement project
•
$58,084 - Parking lot and roadway surface repair at Lions Park
•
$160,000 - Cheyenne-Laramie County Public Health remodeling funds
Finally, I am including more detailed information on the purchase order (PO) encumbrances (see
attached reports) from FY 2024 for a total of $1,196,572 in the General Fund, and $29,096,132
in all other city funds.  Encumbrances included in this report are simply purchase orders that
were created for goods and services ordered prior to, but not received until after, June 30, 2024.
