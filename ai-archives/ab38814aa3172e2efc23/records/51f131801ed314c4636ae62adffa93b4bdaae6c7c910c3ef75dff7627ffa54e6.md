RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 June 23, 2014
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez and
Ms. Williams.  ABSENT: Mr. Cook.  Also present:  Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received. Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Shanor moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded
by Mr. Luna.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.
Presentation of Wyoming Association of Municipalities (WAM) 2014 Innovations Awards
and Community Hero Awards.  Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, reviewed awards the City of Cheyenne
recently received at the annual WAM convention as follows: Innovation Awards (projects/programs)
-- Development Standards Help Accommodate New Development, Mayor’s Youth Council Bullying
Project, Sanitation Routing Software, Roundabout Education, and Cone Zone Map; Community
Hero Awards (community enhancement projects) -- Cheyenne Police Department for its Citizens
Police Academy and Citizens on Patrol programs, Cheyenne Greenway Foundation, and the North
Cheyenne Community Park Partners for the soft surface trail system (Community Partners involved
with funding and materials assitance were: Paul Frauendienst, owner, Frauendienst Quality Homes;
Dan Gregg, owner, Gregg Construction; Larry Sutherland, real estate agent, #1 Properties, and Frank
Cole, local developer.  Mayor Kaysen recognized City staff and members of the community present
at the meeting who were involved in the projects and programs.

Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on June 9, 2014.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.

ORDINANCE - Amending the City of Cheyenne’s Solid Waste fee schedule established by
Ordinance No. 4013 (postponed from 6/09/14).  Dino Moncecchi, Spartan Management, and George
Gliem, Cheyenne Landlord Association, spoke in opposition to the proposed fee increases, citing
concerns with multi-family units vs. single family residential rates, financial burden on tenants
residing in multi-family housing units, and cost of sanitation services vs. water and sewer services.
Mr. Moncecchi suggested that fee increases be “frozen”.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr.
Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Discussion included
the following topics: Consultant solid waste services rate study  and recommendations involving an
8% fee increase vs. staff’s proposed 3.4% increase, which is based on the Consumer Price Index
pursuant to ordinance (provisions require staff to prepare annual increases for governing body
consideration based upon the Index); anticipated July time frame when the rate study information
will be provided to City Council members; funding needed for the Happy Jack Landfill closure and
expansion and Transfer Station expansion projects; increased routine operational and maintenance
costs; rates assessed to multi-family units such as duplexes and apartment buildings; current status
of the City’s application for a landfill (expansion) variance permit through the state; demonstrating
City efforts to fund the costs of solid waste operations relative to applying for state grants or loans;
content and comprehensiveness of elements included in the consultant’s study; meeting solid waste
community needs; City Council decision to not approve rate increases last year and changes
approved in assessing commercial account rates resulting in reduced revenue; City Council’s
responsibility relative to determining fee increases and timing of same; concern with increased rates’
effect on individuals with fixed incomes, and waiting to decide upon any increases until the
consultant’s study information, including staff comments and recommendations, have been received
and reviewed.  During discussion, Jim Elias, Director of Public Works, provided detailed
information in response to queries, and Mayor Kaysen advised the pending Chamber of Commerce’s
development fee study will not specifically include all of the sanitation cost areas.  Motion to
approve failed (City Clerk note: Passage of an ordinance on third and final reading requires the
affirmative vote of the majority (i.e. 6 “yes” votes) of the qualified members (10 members) of the
governing body).  Voting “yes” -- Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Dr. Rinne and Mayor Kaysen.
Voting “no” -- Mr. Luna, Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez and Ms. Williams.
