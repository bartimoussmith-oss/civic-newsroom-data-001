MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Patrick J. Collins, City of Cheyenne
Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Erin Gates, PE, Staff Engineer

DATE:
September 18, 2024

SUBJECT:
A resolution authorizing hate City Engineer to execute Temporary Construction
Easements on behalf of the City of Cheyenne.

Please find attached a proposed resolution that provides authorization to the City Engineer for the
execution of Temporary Construction Easements associated with the Storey Boulevard and
Summit Drive Extension project and the East Dell Range Construction project. It is anticipated
that both projects will require a number of Temporary Construction Easements will need to be
secured to allow for the proper construction of the proposed improvements. It is anticipated that
by extending the Mayor’s authorization to execute Temporary Construction Easements to the City
Engineer for these two projects will more efficiently utilize the time of the Governing Body and
city staff resources. Your support and approval of this resolution is respectfully requested.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE
2101 O’Neil Avenue
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001

RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE GOVERNING
BODY FOR THE CITY ENGINEER TO EXECUTE CERTAIN
TEMPORARY CONSTRUCTION EASEMENTS ON BEHALF OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE ASSOCIATED WITH THE STOREY BOULEVARD
AND SUMMIT DRIVE EXTENSION PROJECT AND THE EAST DELL
RANGE CONSTRUCTION PROJECT."

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne routinely engages in public infrastructure projects to
conduct necessary maintenance, repairs and improvements to the transportation elements of the
city, including city streets, sidewalks and traffic control devices;

WHEREAS, the scope of these projects varies but on many occasions the City Engineer's
Office must coordinate with real property owners adjacent to the projects' bounds and secure
Temporary Construction Easements (TCEs). These easements are necessary when the work of the
City cannot be completed within the public right-of-way, for example when conducting certain
earthwork or paving to ensure that the road surface follows the correct grade for proper drainage,
safety and usability.

WHEREAS, when a TCE is necessary as part of a city construction project, the governing
body customarily affirms and supports the Mayor's authority to execute the attendant easement
agreement;

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is currently engaged in administering the Storey
Boulevard and Summit Drive Extension Project, and the East Dell Range Construction Project,
with both projects anticipated to require TCEs under a number of circumstances;

WHEREAS, the objective of the Storey Boulevard and Summit Drive Extension Project is
to reconstruct Summit Drive beginning at the intersection with North College Drive and continuing
east to the intersection with Highland Road. The project would further improve and extend Storey
Boulevard beginning at the intersection with Whitney Road and continue west to intersect with
Highland Road. This project contemplates many improvements, not only to support the movement
of traffic but to address stormwater drainage within the corridor;

WHEREAS, the objective of the East Dell Range Construction Project is to reconstruct
and expand Dell Range Boulevard to a five (5) lane urban section, beginning at the intersection
with Griffith Avenue and continuing east to the intersection with Van Buren Avenue.

WHEREAS, The East Dell Range Construction Project is planned to a include on-street
bike lanes, a center lane to allow for vehicle access to businesses and adjoining streets and the
installation of a traffic signal at the intersection with Van Buren Avenue. This signal is intended
to support safer pedestrian crossings near Dildine Elementary School. This project also plans to
incorporate improvements to the storm sewer, water, and sanitary sewer systems;

WHEREAS, due to the large scope of the Storey Boulevard and Summit Drive Extension
and the East Dell Range Construction Projects, the City Engineer's Office will need to secure
numerous TCEs for each project and at different time intervals as each project progresses;

WHEREAS, in the interest of efficiency and to better utilize the time of the Governing
Body and city staff and resources, it is appropriate that the Mayor be able to execute the necessary
TCEs associated with each City project without additional advisement or consent by the Governing
Body; and

WHEREAS, it is also appropriate that the Mayor be able to empower the City Engineer as
his official designee to execute the necessary TCEs on behalf of the City in the interest of
efficiency in the management of these projects.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor has the consent and support to execute all
necessary temporary construction easements associated with the Storey Boulevard and Summit
Drive Extension Project, and the East Dell Range Construction Project, without further
consultation with the Governing Body; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Governing Body does not object to the Mayor
delegating his signature authority for the execution of the temporary construction easements
should he believe it would be appropriate and help expedite the completion of these projects.
