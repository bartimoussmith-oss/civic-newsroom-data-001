SpyCare Service Agreement

This agreement, effective as of the later of the dates of signature below (“Effective Date”), is between City of
Cheyenne, WY (“Company”), and The SpyGlass Group, LLC (“SpyGlass”).

1. SpyCare Services. Company hereby engages SpyGlass as an independent contractor to provide the following
SpyCare services:


HealthCheck – SpyGlass will perform an initial optimization of Company’s services immediately upon kickoff of
the engagement and an annual re-optimization of Company’s services which will commence on the one (1) year
anniversary of the Effective Date and the two (2) year anniversary of the Effective Date which will include the
following:
o
Analysis of Company billing provided to SpyGlass relating to services by local, long distance, wireless,
data, Internet, and cloud/datacenter providers using SpyGlass’ auditing processes;
o
Creation of complete telecommunications inventory of all services;
o
Identification of opportunities which exist in such billing for:

Recovery of past billing mistakes;

Elimination of all dormant and underused services; and

Reduction of overall cost;
o
Provision of access to recommendations and inventories in telecommunications expense management
software used by SpyGlass.


MobilityCare – Upon provision of the wireless billing and materials needed from Company, SpyGlass will
commence its ongoing MobilityCare services for the length of the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, which will
include the following:
o
Performance of onboarding meeting to setup mobility management software;

Company will provide SpyGlass full access to either their existing online access credentials to
mobile service billing accounts or create a login specific to SpyGlass for online access to
mobile service billing accounts.

In cases where Company does not have online access credentials already created, Company
authorizes SpyGlass and will assist in creating appropriate online access credentials.
o
Initial load of all mobile billing accounts into the software;
o
Monthly import of Company’s mobile billing information into the software;
o
Provision of access to the software that upon each successful monthly data load generates trend
analysis and cost saving recommendations, maintains an inventory of all current devices, and offers
management reporting;
o
Provision of monthly executive summary email report containing key mobile indicators.


InSights – As part of the SpyCare service, Company will also have access to InSights for the length of the Initial
Term and any Renewal Term, which will include the following:
o
Access to nationwide database of carrier pricing benchmark data;
o
Access to technology trend insights provided by SpyGlass;
o
Access to contract tracker which documents and provides visibility to all expiring service contracts
under review.


DedicatedSupport – As part of the SpyCare service, Company will also have access to DedicatedSupport for the
length of the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, which will include the following:
o
Provision of online dashboard of critical account service provider information;
o
Addition of any new provider accounts that Company provides into online master service inventories;
o
Provision of a billing helpdesk where Company can submit tickets for billing questions for service
accounts under maintenance;
o
Investigation of each helpdesk ticket and provision of feedback. Should any helpdesk ticket require
corrective action, SpyGlass will attempt to correct the problem and provide confirmation;
o
Provision of access to original set of recommendations and inventories from the initial Snapshot Audit
optimization and all HealthCheck reviews by way of software used by SpyGlass.

Company agrees that it will provide access to client’s service billing as directed by SpyGlass to facilitate the SpyCare
services described above.

2. Term. This agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and end three (3) years after the kickoff of the
engagement (the “Initial Term”). Unless either party provides written notice to the other within sixty (60) days of the
end of the Initial Term or any Renewal Term (as defined herein) of its desire not to renew, this agreement shall renew
