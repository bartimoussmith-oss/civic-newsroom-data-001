MEMORANDUM
TO:
City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:
November 21, 2023
SUBJECT:
Cost-of-living wage increase to be considered for approval
On November 27, 2023, the Governing Body will be referring the attached resolution to the Finance
Committee to authorize a cost-of-living salary increase for all permanent full and part-time employees of
the City of Cheyenne.
The latest cost-of-living index for southeast Wyoming is 4.3 percent year-over-year for the second quarter
of 2023.  Considering this, city administration is proposing that all permanent full and part-time employees,
excluding elected officials and employees of the Board of Public Utilities, receive a 3 percent cost-of-living
salary adjustment effective January 1, 2023.
Mayor Collins, facing the challenging task of balancing the City’s long-term financial stability with
the importance of providing a cost-of-living wage adjustment to the City’s valued workforce, has
decided to use a somewhat more conservative approach in this request compared with the cost-of-living
index.  This is due to leading economic indicators continuing to signal an impending recession in the
United States next year.  Mayor Collins is attempting to strike a delicate equilibrium between fiscal
prudence and maintaining a supportive work environment during these uncertain economic times.
The estimated six-month cost for this request is $526,094.31 from General Fund reserves and $167,409.83
for all other City funds.  A budget amendment will be completed in December to appropriate funds for
these increases.
As always, if you have any questions, please give me a call at 637-6336 or email at
rlockman@cheyennecity.org.

RESOLUTION NO. __________
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A THREE (3) PERCENT COST-OF-
LIVING SALARY ADJUSTMENT FOR ALL PERMANENT FULL AND
PART-TIME EMPLOYEES OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE."
WHEREAS, the City recognizes the rising cost-of-living affecting its employees;
WHEREAS, maintaining a competitive and fair compensation structure is essential to
attract and retain skilled and dedicated personnel;
WHEREAS, a cost-of-living increase is an effective way to address financial challenges
faced by employees;
WHEREAS, on October 31, 2023 the State of Wyoming, Economic Analysis Division,
released the Wyoming Cost-of-Living Index for the second quarter of 2023, which measures
Wyoming inflation rates compared to one year ago;
WHEREAS, the regional inflation rate for Southeast Wyoming, which covers Albany,
Carbon, Goshen, Laramie, Niobrara and Platte Counties, is 4.3 percent; and
NOW THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, effective January 1, 2024, that all eligible
permanent full and part-time City employees, excluding elected officials and employees of the
City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities, will receive a three (3) percent cost-of-living salary
increase,
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that employees with salaries exceeding their position's
pay grade in the City's compensation plan as a result of this cost-of-living adjustment will instead
receive a pro-rata one-time payment in lieu of a salary increase in the amounts approved for their
position,
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City Treasurer shall create a budget
reappropriation to allocate the necessary funds from reserves and grant funding sources to
accommodate this cost-of-living adjustment.
