ZONING, INTENT, APPLICABILITY&DESIGN OBJECTIVES.The ability of the application to support
the Intent and Applicability statements of the proposed zoning district, and achieve the Design
Objectives of any specific standards.
The MUR Districtis intended for a mix of Residential, Retail, Service, Employment, and Civic
uses where the predominant use will be Residential, with moderate or higher densities and a
range of housing types.Non-residential uses should be limited to those that primarily support
residential uses within the district.The district regulations are designed to allow a variety of
housing types, and establish the necessary amenities to support higher-density urban
neighborhoods. The MUR District is applicable to areas where more urban, higher-density and
walk able residential neighborhoods are desired and are appropriate with a highlevel of
pedestrian and civic aimenities, specifically areas designated Mixed-use Residential Emphasis in
the Comprehensive Plan.
This application proposes multi-family four-plexes, and “lofts”above pedestrian-scale retail and
service businesses.This proposal could contribute to service provision for students from the
nearby schools, as well as those citizens who are using the Greenway, either for transportation or
recreation.
ZONING, FUTURE DEVELOPMENT.The ability of all potential future development to meet all
applicable standards for development in the proposed zoning district, including other
development options in the event that any specific development proposed in association with the
application is not executed after approval.
Should the proposal not materialize, and the MUR zoning is in place, thislotwill still havemany
options.It could become an entirely residential project, or scale down the proposed commercial
component, depending on market demands.Futhermore, the design guidelines in a pedestrian
district likeMUR would assure reasonable development compatible with the surrounding area.
