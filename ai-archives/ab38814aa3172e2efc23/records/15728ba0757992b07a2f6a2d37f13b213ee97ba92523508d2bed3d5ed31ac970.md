Sam's West, Inc. ("Sam's Club") is pleased to submit its Application for the Transfer of a Retail

Wyoming Retail Liquor License No.35.Sam's Club's application and the required attachments
are provided with this letter, and Sam’s is seeking approval of the transfer of ownership as well
has the location of the Liquor License.
For background purposes, Sam's Club in Cheyenne is a membership only warehouse club, with
over 29,000 members in Cheyenne, Wyoming. As many of you know, our club offers
electronics, apparel, home furnishings, with a full line of groceries, including fresh produce,
meat and dairy products, and dry goods. Our location also provides photo, optical, and pharmacy
services. Significantly, the liquor license Sam’s Club is seeking to purchase is currently being
utilized in a building adjacent to the Sam's Club warehouse. Thus, if approval of the transfer is
obtained from the City Council, thelocation of its newplace of usewill beonly a fewhundred
yards from its current place of use.
I am the manager of the Cheyenne Sam's Club.My professional background includes more than
12 years in the field of operations and business management.My career began with Sam's Club
in 2003, and has expanded across the Midwest, including: Nebraska, South Dakota, Colorado,
promoted to an Assistant Manager and have held the roles of member service manager, fresh
produce manager, membership manager, hardlines manager, and grocery manager. I came to
Cheyenne in 2013 and lead a Sam's Club team of 140 associates, including 6 members of
management.I am thrilled to have this company as a lifelong employer which shares my belief
in the value of investing in my associates and helping them toreach their full potential.In my
spare time, I enjoy fishing, hiking and spending time with my growing family.My wife is a
Registered Nurse at theVA hospital and we recently returned from Africa where we adopted a 6
year old boy, who is our first child.
Sam's is committed to building strong communities and has been a part of Cheyenne since 1993,
investing as an employer, contributing to the economy, and serving the people of the community.
