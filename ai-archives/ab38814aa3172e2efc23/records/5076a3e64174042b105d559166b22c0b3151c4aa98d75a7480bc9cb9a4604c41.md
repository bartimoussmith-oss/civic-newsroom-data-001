WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Governing Body has amended Section 5.12.110 to
allowfor creation of special districts in which the possession of open containers is permitted; and
WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority (DDA) requests the boundaries of
their district be declareda special district for the“Cheyenne Days-Legendary Nights Event”at
the following dates and times:
1. Cheyenne Day - Wednesday, July 22, 2020, from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.
2. Fridays on the Plaza -Friday, July 24, 2020, from 5:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.
3.Saturday, July 25, 2020, from 7:00 a.m.to 10:00 p.m.; and
WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days is an economic engine for Laramie County that
generates approximately $36.2 million in business activity between visitor spending and the
creation of jobs, generating approximately $1.2 million in local and state sales tax revenue; and
WHEREAS, in theinterest of public health and safety, the2020 Cheyenne Frontier Days
has been cancelled due to the coronavirus pandemic; and
WHEREAS, Cheyenne Days - Legendary Nights Events will promote local shopping by
offering shopping specials, sidewalk sales, and entertainment including a street dance, concerts,
potentially a pancake breakfast, and the Thunderbirds Air Show; and
WHEREAS, shopping locally creates a multiplier effect through direct, indirect, and
induced spending which boosts the local economy and creates more local wealth and jobs; and
WHERAS, a number of studies illustrated on the website of American Independence
Business Alliance (AMIBA)have proven a 48% return to the community occurs when you spend
locally; and
WHEREAS, the event organizers will comply with any and all laws, rules, and
regulations to ensure thesafety of the community; and
