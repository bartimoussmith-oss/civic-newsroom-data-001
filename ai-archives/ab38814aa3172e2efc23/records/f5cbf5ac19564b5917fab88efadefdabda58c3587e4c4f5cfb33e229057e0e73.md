I APPROVED
LBY Michael R. O’Donnell at 8:30 am, Jun 16, 2021
CONTRACT NO.
AGREEMENT BETWEEN
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND
THE LARAMIE COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT NO. 1
THIS AGREEMENT is made and entered into by and between the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”),
whose address is 2101 0 ‘Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, acting by and through its
political subdivision, the Cheyenne Police Department (“Department”), whose address is 415
West
18rh Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the Laramie County School District No.
1
(“District”), whose address
is 2810 House Avenue,
Cheyenne, Wyoming
82001.
This
Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the District has identified the need for law enforcement services at various
schools and school-related events under its control and supervision; and
WHEREAS, the Department is a municipal police agency engaged in providing law
enforcement services to the City of Cheyenne; and
WHEREAS,
the
District desires
to
obtain
the
law
enforcement
services of the
Department, and the Department desires to provide said law enforcement services to the District.
NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual promises and covenants
hereinafter contained, the parties agree as follows:
1.
The Department agrees to provide routine law enforcement services for School
Resource Officers (SRO) to the District under the terms and conditions herein contained.
The
duties assumed by the Department under this Agreement are duties owed generally to the public,
and by entering this Agreement, the Department does not assume any other duty to the District,
nor does the Department assume any duty to any particular individual.
2.
This Agreement shall be in effect from August 1, 2021, through July 31, 2022.
The Department shall have no obligation under this Agreement to provide law enforcement
services to the District at any school location during any time that such school location is closed
by scheduled or short-term official action of the District. Except as provided in this Agreement,
the Department shall have no obligation to provide law enforcement services to the District, as
described in this Agreement, beyond the hours provided for in this Agreement.
a.
The District shall have authority to flex an SRO’s schedule to assist during
special events, short-term official closures, or other circumstances.
b.
Should any non-weather related event require long-term official closure of
school locations for more than five (5) consecutive school days, the District may request
limited law enforcement services with the balance of an SRO’s daily hours redirected to
supervise schools and/or release back to the Department as best represents the interests of
both the District and the Department. If such interests and duties cannot be agreed upon,
this Agreement shall be suspended for the remainder of the period of the official closure,
and payments vacated for duties not performed during such period.
3.
In return for law enforcement services to be provided by the Department pursuant
to this Agreement, the District agrees to pay to the City Treasurer for the 2021-2022 school year
the sum as set forth in paragraph 10.a. Said sum shall be invoiced to the District monthly and in
arrears.
The District shall reimburse the Department for additional costs incurred by the
Department in the performance of law enforcement services as set forth in paragraphs 10.b. and
10.c. at the rates prescribed, for which the District shall be invoiced monthly.

4.
All costs of salary and benefits incurred by the Department in the performance of
duties under this Agreement shall be paid by the Department, and the District shall reimburse the
Department for all such costs as set forth in paragraph 10 of this Agreement.
5.
The District and Department are aware that each party to this Agreement is
subject to the supervision of separately elected political entities.
Each party to this Agreement
obtains funding which is subject to annual budget approval.
Each party hereto shall notify the
other immediately if a party’s future fiscal year funding for this Agreement is curtailed or
eliminated.
6.
Law enforcement officers assigned to provide law enforcement services under this
Agreement shall, at all times, be employees of the City of Cheyenne Police Department.
The
Department shall comply with all federal, state, and local laws and ordinances regarding the
employment relationship between the City of Cheyenne and law enforcement officers assigned to
provide services under this Agreement. The Department shall exercise sole responsibility for the
supervision and evaluation of law enforcement officers assigned under this Agreement with input
from
District
Administration.
In
extenuating
circumstances,
the
District
may
request
reassignment/replacement of an SRO.
7.
The Department personnel providing law enforcement services pursuant to this
Agreement shall conduct themselves as law enforcement officers and shall at all times follow the
policies and procedures of the Department. All matters of officer conduct and performance shall
be referred to the Department for disposition and resolution.
Further, the Department will be
enforcing state laws and regulations, and will not be required to enforce the District’s policies
and procedures.
8.
The Department shall appoint a representative who shall be responsible for
managing and coordinating the Department’s performance under this Agreement and shall
identify this representative to the District by name and telephone number.
9.
The
Department personnel
provided
by
the
Department
pursuant
to
this
Agreement shall be directly supervised by the Department’s Patrol Commander. Such personnel
will coordinate their activities with the principal of each of the District’s schools identified
herein.
However, such personnel will be subject to other assignments in times of emergencies,
as determined by the Department.
In selecting personnel to provide services pursuant to this
Agreement, the Department shall consider each officer’s interest in the assignment, ability to
deal with the District’s students, and any special training which may have been received.
The
Department shall request a District representative in the selection of the law enforcement officers
assigned under this Agreement and consult with the District prior to any changes in SRO
assignments expected to occur within the normally-scheduled school year, with consideration
given to preserving the student-officer relationships critical to the success of this program. The
Department does not guarantee or commit to a specific number of hours, and law enforcement
needs of the Department are paramount to the services provided under this Agreement.
The
Department shall reimburse the District for any hours related to the diversion of law enforcement
officers from normal SRO duties for a half-day or more.
10.
The Department shall provide routine law enforcement services to the District at
schools located within Cheyenne city limits at the following locations and at the following times:
a.
For the 2021-2022 school year, seven (7) officers and one (1) sergeant
stationed variously at high schools, junior high schools, and elementary schools within
the District as may be determined by mutual agreement between the Department and the
District.
Officers assigned to these locations shall perform routine law enforcement
services during regular school hours.
The officers will also be involved with current
programs the District is promoting for drug/alcohol awareness and anti-bullying as may
be determined by mutual agreement between the Department and the District.
The base
contract period shall be for one hundred eight-five (185) days, for which the District shall
reimburse the Department seventy-five percent (75%) of the actual total annual salary
and benefits for seven (7) officers and one (1) sergeant for one (1) school year.

Department shall provide a replacement officer to cover the officer’s duty assignment at

Further, the District shall not be billed for
periods when individual officers are absent from their assigned duties/school(s), to
include periods of professional training for collateral duty.
The SRO is encouraged,
when possible, to take personal leave during scheduled school breaks.
e.
Except as provided for emergencies in Paragraph 11 of this Agreement, an
SRO assigned to provide services under this Agreement shall devote substantially all of
his or her working time to the District during regular school hours and at such other times
as may be determined by mutual agreement between the Department and the District.
11.

a.
The District shall provide an assigned SRO with keys and/or access cards

b.
When

or

warrant,
