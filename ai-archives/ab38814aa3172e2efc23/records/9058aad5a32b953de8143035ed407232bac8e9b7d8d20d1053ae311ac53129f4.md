Chairman Malm called the meeting to order at 1:00 pm. Laramie County Clerk Debra
Lee did the roll call for Laramie County. Platte County Clerk Malcolm Ervin did the roll
call for Platte County.
Chairman Malm explained the purpose of the meeting was to appoint a person to fill
the vacancy for Wyoming Senate District 6. He explained that each candidate would
be interviewed individually and the order of interview would be determined by a
manual drawing. He said candidates waiting to be interviewed would be sequestered
in another room and would surrender their cell phones. He stated that following the
interviews, the board would meet in executive session for deliberations, and the vote
would be done in public.
Candidates were invited to approach the dais and select a number for the order of
their interview. Taft Love was the first interviewee, Roy Birt second, and Jeff Barnes
third.

Board of County Commissioners
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
August 29, 2025
Each commissioner asked questions of the candidates. Topics include constituencies
they would represent, description of their budget experience, knowledge of property
taxes and position on further cuts, recommendations for keeping young people in
Wyoming, land use and land rights issues, support for voter-approved sales tax
options, and industries the counties should cultivate.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Hollingshead, to go into executive session for deliberation
for this position. The motion carried by a unanimous vote.
The boards went into executive session at 3:05 pm
The boards reconvened at 3:30 pm.
Clerk Lee distributed a ballot to each commissioner that contained the names of the
three candidates. Ballots were labeled by county in order to assign the weighted vote
as required in W.S. 22-18-111(a)(iii)D).
Ballots were returned to the Clerk who read each vote by county into the record. Clerk
Ervin entered the results on a publicly displayed spreadsheet that tabulated the
weighted vote.
A motion was made by Commissioner Hollingshead, seconded by
Commissioner Heath, to appoint Taft Love to fill the vacant Senate District
6 position.
Clerk Lee did the roll call for Laramie County. Clerk Ervin did the roll call for Platte
County. All members voted ‘aye’.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:
The meeting adjourned at 3:38 pm.
