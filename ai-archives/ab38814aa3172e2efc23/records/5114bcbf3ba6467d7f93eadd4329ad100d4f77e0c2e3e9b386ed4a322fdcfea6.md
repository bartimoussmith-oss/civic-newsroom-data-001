RESOLUTION NO________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION CERTIFYING THAT THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE IS IN SUPPORT OF THE TOWN OF ALBIN’S PROJECTS
TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT BOARD FOR THE
DECEMBER 2012 COUNTYWIDE CONSENSUS BLOCK GRANT FUNDING IN THE
TOTAL AMOUNT OF $115,000.”

Whereas, the rules of the State Loan and Investment Board require that application for funds
under the Countywide Consensus Block Grant Program be made by the Board of County
Commissioners and agreed to by municipalities representing at least 70% of the county’s
incorporated population; and

Whereas, the City of Cheyenne represents greater than 70% of the incorporated population
of Laramie County.

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING

That the City of Cheyenne supports the Town of Albin’s Consensus grant request to the State
Loan and Investment Board for the projects set forth in the FY 2013/2014 Countywide
Consensus Block Grant Application Form; and

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS 22nd DAY OF October, 2012.

                                                                  Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor

ATTEST:

Carol Inlekofer, City Clerk
