MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Marian Orr, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

June 2, 2020
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-23-20 for furnishing One (1) New 2019-2020 Daihatsu
Hi-Jet S510P Mini-Truck, for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Botanic Gardens
______________________________________________
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) bids were received from:

1. Woody’s Mini Trucks (Poplar Grove, IL)

2. Bogue, Inc. d/b/a America’s Mini Truck (Beresford, SD)

The bids were reviewed by Jason Sanchez (Community Recreation & Events), Tina Worthman
(Botanic Gardens), Jacob Mares (Botanic Gardens), and Matt Titus (Turf Maintenance Shop).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid, from Woody’s Mini Truck, Poplar
Grove, IL.  The bid award is for the purchase of One (1) New 2019-2020 Daihatsu Hi-Jet S510P
Mini-Truck ($23,784.00), and Acceptance of Trade-In Offer for Block #3576 (-$200.00).  The
total, less trade in, is $23,584.00.  The City is declining all additional options.

Funding:
022-26-2614-45-43102 (1% Sales Tax 15-18 Equipment)
$23,584.00
