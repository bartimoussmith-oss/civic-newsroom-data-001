Jaycee Park Tennis Courts Restoration
Recreation Mill Grant
Need
The city of Cheyenne has just twelve regulation-size tennis courts. Two ofthese tennis courts are in
Jaycee Park and are in is very poor condition, with irreparable crumbling court base, many fissures
throughout the play surface, and ponding water. In addition, the chain-link fence around the tennis
courts has become damaged and stretched out over the years, with the bottom fence fabric no longer
securely connecting with the ground.
These conditions make play on these courts less than a
positive experience.
With the current condition of these highly-visible tennis courts, serious tennis players will not use
them. Further, the cracks and dips on the surface of the courts create a potential tripping hazard.
We would like to make the repairs to this unusable tennis courts to put them back into use.
The fenced tennis courts in Jaycee Park, are in an older neighborhood with smaller, quaint homes.
The neighborhood park is 2.2 acres in size. It offers large shade trees, a playground, a basketball
court, a drinking fountain and irrigated grass. It has a very pleasant atmosphere.
Resolution to Need
We propose renovating the two tennis courts at Jaycee Park, in the Avenues neighborhood of
Cheyenne. The court resurfacing and the associated repairs will make these tennis courts useable
again.
The proposed project includes removing the chain-link fence and replacing it with new fence. The
surface of the tennis courts is to be scraped, with lose surface material removed and a paving
fabric is to be applied over the entire court. An overlay of two inches of hot-mix asphalt will then
follow. One coat of RSS acrylic resurfacer will then be applied, followed by two coats of RSS
Acrylic Color System. The court layout will be marked, and a two-inch white textured line will be
painted on the court. New tennis net posts will be installed, and new nets will be hung. Minor
traffic control will be provided.
Project equipment and cost
The estimated cost for the tennis courts includes the clean-up and removal ofmaterials from the two
existing tennis courts, new tennis court materials and installation, new tennis court posts and nets
and installation, and the application of a white textured painted line. Minor traffic control is also
included. A price quote of $112,500, from “renner” follows:
