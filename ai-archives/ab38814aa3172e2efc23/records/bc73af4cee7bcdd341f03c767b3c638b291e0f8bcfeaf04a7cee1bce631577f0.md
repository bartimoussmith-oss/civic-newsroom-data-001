OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
August 24, 2009
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning at
6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.
Absent: Mr. Pierson.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City
Attorney.
RECOGNITION OF POLICE CHIEF ROBERT D. FECHT ON HIS RETIREMENT. Carol
Intlekofer, City Clerk, read a proclamation into the record honoring Chief Fecht for his 19 years of
service with the Cheyenne Police Department and proclaiming Aug. 31, 2009 Robert D. Fecht Day.
Mayor Kaysen expressed appreciation to Chief Fecht and presented him with a plaque and the
proclamation.  Several members of the governing body expressed appreciation to Chief Fecht for his
years of service and support.  Chief Fecht thanked the Mayor, Council members, his officers, staff and
co-workers for the privilege of working for the City.

discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two other
members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its normal
sequence on the agenda.).  Mayor Kaysen advised Agenda Item #19 was being withdrawn from the City

Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing  to receive comments and input on the submission of
an application to the Wyoming Business Council, under Business Ready Communities: Community
Enhancement Program, for a grant to develop Saddle Ridge Park.  Todd Feezer, Assistant Director of
Parks and Recreation, provided information on the proposed park; explained proposed grant request
in the amount of $285,820.00 and the City’s proposed grant match mechanism, and noted funding has
also been sought and approved in the amount of $105,125.00 through the recreation mill levy,
administered through the school district, to assist with the project, which will serve as an urban quality
amenity located within Saddle Ridge subdivision in east Cheyenne.  He stated the developer has
donated 14 acres for the project of which seven acres are proposed for park development at this time
as part of the grant application.  Mr. Feezer advised the park will serve residents and workers in the
workforce housing and business area in the vicinity of the Cheyenne LEADS business park, and will
also be near the Greenway path system and new Saddle Ridge school.  He indicated the developer is
also donating assistance with site grading and utility stubs, and described uses of grant funds, if
approved.  Appreciation was expressed to staff for their creativity in seeking this type of Business
Council grant for park development.  There being no further comments or input and purpose of the
hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the public hearing.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive comments and input on the submission of an
application to the Wyoming Business Council, under the Business Ready Communities: Business
Readiness Project, for a grant to develop a manufacturing building in the Cheyenne Business Parkway.
Scott Sutherland, Vice President, Cheyenne LEADS, advised LEADS is seeking the City’s assistance
in applying for grant funding that, if received, will provide a manufacturing speculation building that
LEADS can promote to manufacturing companies interested in a Cheyenne location.  He explained a
community need for this type of shell building exists; that it will not be a building for warehouse
purposes, and that it will provide for potential economic development.  He stated the grant, if approved,
requires a match that would be provided by LEADS through property valuation.  Upon inquiry, Mr.
Sutherland indicated the facility would be approximately 40,000 sq. ft. in size and provided information
on the estimated number of employment opportunities that may be available based upon past inquiries.
There being no further comments or input and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen
closed the public meeting.

“AN ORDINANCE CREATING SECTION 10.24.170, DRIVING WHILE USING
CELLULAR TELEPHONE PROHIBITED; EXCEPTIONS, OF CHAPTER 10.24, RULES OF THE
ROAD GENERALLY, OF TITLE 10, VEHICLES AND TRAFFIC, OF THE CODE OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”  John Fafoutakis commented in support of violations constituting a
secondary offense instead of a primary one if the ordinance were approved, and cited concerns with
ordinance enforcement, other distractive driving scenarios, and governing body infringement of
individual rights and liberties.  Major Dorr spoke in favor of laws that assist with keeping people safe.
Pacomio Sandoval spoke in favor of drivers utilizing hands free devices when using cell phones while
driving, and commented on passage of laws due to individuals’ irresponsibility and enforcement
concerns.  Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr. Brown moved to approve on second reading,
seconded by Mr. Collins. Discussion followed on use of hands free devices, safety concerns with use
of cell phones and texting while driving, enforcement issues and concerns, proposed costs related to
community signage if signs were posted to provide notification to the public of ordinance restrictions,
and public education and awareness. Upon inquiry,  Jim Elias, Public Works Director, advised if the
ordinance is approved and signage was to be installed at entryways into the City, approximately 20
signs would be posted at an estimated cost of $120/sign.  It was advised public awareness and education
would be provided through media releases, public service announcements and City website postings.
Upon inquiry regarding enforcement, Captain Jeff Schulz, Police Dept., advised the ordinance would
be enforced on a priority basis as with other ordinances, and provided information on offenses
involving careless driving, explaining that citations written for careless driving involves the manner
of operation of a vehicle vs. what a driver may be doing within the vehicle.  Regarding local statistics,
Captain Schulz provided information pursuant to 2008 accident reports involving drivers who admitted
to using cell phones.  Discussion continued on safety issues; driving practices; hand held vs. hands free
cell phone usage by drivers; governing body authority to regulate driving, and protection of the public
and other drivers.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” –  all members of the governing body present with
the exception of Mr. Valdez voting “no”.

“AN ORDINANCE REPEALING SECTION 9.08.030, GAMBLING-PROHIBITED-
EXCEPTIONS, AND SECTION 9.08.040, VIOLATIONS BY OWNER OR OCCUPANT OF
PREMISES WHERE GAMBLING CONDUCTED, OF TITLE 9, PUBLIC PEACE AND WELFARE,
CHAPTER 9.08, OFFENSES AGAINST PUBLIC PEACE AND DECENCY, OF THE CHEYENNE
CITY CODE.”  Mr. Brown moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Mr. Collins. Upon
inquiry, Dan White, City Attorney, provided information on a recent amendment to state statute which
would allow certain types of gambling/gaming activities by patrons of businesses under certain
circumstances, and in which the business does not receive any direct profit from the activity and where
no person is participating directly or indirectly in professional gambling.  Captain Jeff Schulz, Police
Dept., provided information on past acceptable practices and changes due to an Attorney General’s
opinion that resulted in state law changes, and discussed enforcement concerns with the current city
ordinance as written.  Discussion followed and included: Repeal of the ordinance not reflecting that the
City is legalizing gambling activities; state law definition of gambling; what constitutes a bona fide
social relationship; whether advertising casino or poker events by bars was in violation of state law;
interpretation and enforcement of state law if the ordinance is approved, and different types of
gambling/gaming activities and scenarios.  Upon inquiry, Mr. White expressed his understanding,
pursuant to law enforcement practices, that, for instance, a bar can not advertise a gambling event, such
