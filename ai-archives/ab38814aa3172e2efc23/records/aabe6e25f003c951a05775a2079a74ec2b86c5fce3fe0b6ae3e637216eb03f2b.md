MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

June 9, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and CenturyLink,
Littleton, Colorado, to relocate CenturyLink owned fiber optic utilities in
preparation for an upcoming construction project
______________________________________________
This is a sole source purchase through CenturyLink, therefore no bids were requested.
This is a utility relocation in order to accommodate a future construction project.  The utility
alignment will need to be relocated outside the limits of deep footings and a pedestrian underpass.
Century Link is the utility owner / operator of the facility and has full control of operation and
maintenance.  Therefore, they require that they relocate their own facilities.
The City of Cheyenne Engineering Department is seeking funding authorization in an amount not
to exceed $72,000.00.

Funding:
(030-17-1754-45-43717 / Greenway Projects)

$72,000.00
