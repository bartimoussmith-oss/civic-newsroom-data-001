In general the higher densities encouraged with theNR-3zone district are more appropriate near
higher intensity uses, such as the commercial to the north. This is a small lot and would have
more limitations in the current CB district, as well as only being available for commercial use.
Locating higher densities near activity centers optimizes access to the activity center by
accommodating legitimate pedestrian access as well as vehicular access. This minimizes
impacts on streets and also produces a buffer to the lower density development to the south.
