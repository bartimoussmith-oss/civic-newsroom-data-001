EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
Final plat procedures are intended to create an accurate public record for the sale of lots, to
guarantee construction of all public improvements according to the standards in the subdivision
regulations, and to ensure that all lots are buildable lots according to the appropriate zoning prior
to recording. The final plat process is applicable to any division of land in the City or within 1
mile of the City that has been acknowledged and approved by the City Council through the
preliminary plat procedures

This Final Plat meets the required criteria outlined in 2.1.3.d, as follows:
1. The layout and design of the final plat is in substantial compliance with the approved
preliminary plat considering the number of lots or parcels, street and block layout, and
access, and any deviations in the final plat brings the application in further compliance
with the Comprehensive Plan.
2. The application meets any conditions or modifications of the approved preliminary plat.
3. The application meets the purposes, intent, and applicable standards of Article 4.

with staff conditions 1 – 4.  On January 28, 2013, the City Council acknowledged the

Park Fees:

Total = $1,470 payable in full at the time of the signing of the final plat.

Additionally, The Residential Enhancement Fee of $400 per Dwelling Unit (DU) is payable at
the issuance of a building permit.

This Final Plat is in substantial compliance with the acknowledged Preliminary Plat. It proposes
twelve (12) twinhome lots, two (2) multifamily lots, and an extension of Sapphire Street (52’
ROW) 300 feet to the east, connecting to Pierce Ave.  To satisfy Condition 4 from the
Preliminary Plat, the Final Plat replaces the single lot for stormwater detention “dedicated to the
City of Cheyenne”, with a drainage easement of the same dimensions, now contained within Lot
7, the eastern portion of which is intended for multifamily. This removes the City from any
maintenance responsibility for area. The 30’ access and drainage easement now runs along the
northern edge of the two multifamily lots as well.

A Zone Change Request, from HR-2 to MR-2, was submitted in conjunction with a portion of
the Preliminary Plat to accommodate the lot type.  Of greater significance, however, is that this
zone change also serves to enable aspects of the model design proposed for the twin-homes,
primarily with regards to driveway access widths. Specifically, the applicant proposes to
continue with the general pattern of access widths throughout the rest of Diamond Estates,
namely 36’ “ganged” driveways. The Zone Change request was approved by City Council on
February 11, 2013, but will only take effect upon the approval and recordation of this Final
Plat.

Block Perimeters are 1,700 on North, 1,900 on south – both meet requirements set forth in Table
4-6. Within the subdivision boundaries, neither Block 1 nor Block 2 exceed 60% of the
requirement, as given in Section 4.3.3.a., UDC (see attached aerial). Lots 1-6, Block 1 and Lots
1-6, Block 2, reflect the same use and pattern across right-of-way. Transition of twinhome to
multifamily occurs along the rear lot lines on Block 2 (ref. Section 4.3.3.b.5). The zone change
request reflects this principle.

While the proposed street types have not been explicitly called out, it is assumed that the most
likely and appropriate type for Pierce Street is a Residential Type A, which is 60’ ROW with a 7’
tree lawn and 5’ sidewalk. Sapphire Street, would be a Residential Type B, which is 56’ ROW
with a 7’ tree lawn and 5’ sidewalk.  It’s likely that the applicant will desire an attached sidewalk
