RESOLUTION NO. ______
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO ENTER INTO AN AGREEMENT FOR THE
PURCHASE OF REAL PROPERTY, DESCRIBED AS A PARCEL OF LAND
GENERALLY LOCATED EAST OF MOUNTAIN ROAD, SOUTH OF
PLAINVIEW ROAD AND WEST OF CHAPEL HILL, CONSISTING OF
APPROXIMATELY 121,669 SQUARE FEET (ESTIMATED VALUE OF
$400,000.00), FROM HIGHLAND UNITED PRESBYTERIAN CHURCH FOR
THE PURPOSE OF LAND ACQUISITION FOR A NEIGHBORHOOD
PARK.”
WHEREAS, the Highland United Presbyterian Church is the owner of a parcel of land which
is generally located East of Mountain Road, South of Plainview Road and West of Chapel Hill Road,
consisting of approximately 121,669 square feet; and
WHEREAS, the Highland United Presbyterian Church has stated its intention to sell said
land; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Parks & Recreation Department wishes to purchase said
land for the purpose of creating a City owned and operated neighborhood park in an underserved
neighborhood in Cheyenne, Wyoming; and
WHEREAS, a Phase I environmental site assessment has been completed by Terracon
Consultants, Inc., Cheyenne, Wyoming, on said land; and
WHEREAS, the environmental site assessment did not result in any findings regarding
recognized environmental conditions that would prohibit the purchase of said land; and
WHEREAS, an appraisal of said land has been completed by Christopher S. Brown, MAI,
Cheyenne, Wyoming, and it is his opinion that the market value of said land is $400,000.00.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to enter into an
agreement for the purchase of the above-described parcel of land owned by Highland United
Presbyterian Church, and to execute all further and additional documents as necessary to achieve
the purchase of said land as directed herein.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _______ DAY OF ___________________,
_________.
__________________________________________
Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
(Seal)
Attest:
____________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
