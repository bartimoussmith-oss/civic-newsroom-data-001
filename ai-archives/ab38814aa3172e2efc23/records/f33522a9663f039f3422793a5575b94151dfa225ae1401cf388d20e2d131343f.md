BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have) applied for a zoning map
amendment for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, of the Cheyenne
Unified Development Code ("UDC").

Section 2.  That on April 3, 2023, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted a public
hearing, reviewed the proposed zoning map amendment that is the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended approval of said zoning map amendment.

Section 3.  That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zoning map amendment
that is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.

Section 4.  That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2, Zoning
Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the aforementioned application
is hereby approved, and the zoning classification of the real property described as:
Tract 12, Dell Range Addition, a subdivision of part of the west one-half of Section 22, Township
14 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M., Laramie County Wyoming less that portion described
in warranty deed recorded in Book 1148 Page 1486 and less that portion being the north 120 feet
of the east half of Tract 12, Dell Range Addition, records of Laramie County, Wyoming
is hereby established as MR Medium-density Residential.

Section 5.  That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the Official
Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zoning map amendment as
described in Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

Section 6.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are severable. If
any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional, such
unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and
phrases.

BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from County MR Medium-density Residential to
City MR Medium-density Residential for 1.29 acres of land.  The land is currently occupied by a religious
facility.  The proposed MR zoning allows for a variety of residential uses in a more compact neighborhood
development pattern.

The proposed MR zoning allows for primary and secondary schools with a Conditional Use approval.  A
Conditional Use for a primary and secondary school was approved by the Board of Adjustment on March 16,
2023.

identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

UDC-23-00065-01

April 3, 2023
The Future Land Use Designation for this property and the surrounding area is Urban
Residential. According to comprehensive plan there are many optimal City zones for the subject
property’s future land use designation, including MR Medium-density Residential.

The policies of PlanCheyenne are supportive of this proposed zone change. The future land use
plan encourages a variety of residential development patterns and the MR zone allows a wide
variety of residential uses.  The future land use plan for the Urban Residential area encourages
complementary uses, such as schools.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

All adjacent property is currently within the City limits and zoned MR.  Property to the north and
east are existing single-family residential.  Property to the south and west are currently used for
a Church.  The site will be replated and combined with the existing lots to the south and west for
the proposed primary and secondary school.  MR is compatible with the area. The proposed
zoning is also compatible with the future land use designation of Urban Residential.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current zone is a County zone and a City zone is required to develop within City limits.  The
City zone requested is the same as the County zone.  The MR zone in both the City and the
County are intended to develop with a variety of residential uses.  Primary and secondary
schools are a permitted use by right in the County Zone but require a Conditional Use approval
in the City zone.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The zone change is proposed in association with a proposed redevelopment, a platting action
and annexation. The associated platting action will conform to Article 4 standards to the extent
possible.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

UDC-23-00065-01

The MR zone is intended to develop with a mix of density and dwelling types within a
neighborhood.  It is intended  for areas where more compact neighborhoods  development
patterns are desired and specifically the Urban Residential Category in the PlanCheyenne. This
area is designated as Urban Residential on the Future Land Use Plan.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

A primary and secondary school is proposed with this development.  A primary and secondary
school is allowed by Conditional Use in the MR zone district.  A Conditional Use has been
approved for a primary and secondary school on March 16, 2023.  The proposed school should
have no issues meeting standards for the MR zone district.  The site could be used for a wide
variety of housing options if it were not to develop into a school.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No reviewing agencies provided recommendations regarding the proposed zone change.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
