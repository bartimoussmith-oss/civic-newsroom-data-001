RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
August 22, 2011
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning at
6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
Mr. Allen, Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.
Absent: Mr. Collins. Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan
White, City Attorney. The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two other
members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its normal
sequence on the agenda.) Mr. Brown moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Spiker.
Motion carried. Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on August 8, 2011.  Motion (per Consent
Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest) due to a voucher payable to Ameri-
Tech HVAC Services and vacated Council Chambers. Mr. Allen moved to approve payment of
vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body present in Council Chambers. Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown
returned to Council Chambers.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to determine whether compliance of annexation
conditions required by Wyo. Stat. §15-1-402 exist for the property described as a parcel of land being
situated in portions of Sections 14 and 23, T.13N., R.67W., 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming
(generally located between Interstate 25 and Clear Creek Parkway, South of West College Drive).  Jim
Flesher, Urban Planning office, reviewed requirements and compliance of conditions contained in Wyo.
Stat. §15-1-402, including specific information indicating compliance with Wyo. Stat. §15-1-402(a)(i)
through (vi).  Donn Edmunds expressed concerns with City fire and police manpower coverage for the
proposed area to be annexed.  There being no further comments or input and purpose of the hearing
having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the public hearing.
Resolution determining that the sale of real property owned by the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming
would benefit the economic development of the City pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 15-1-112(b) and
authorizing the Mayor to sign a Purchase and Sale Agreement and a Development Agreement with
Menard, Inc. for the sale of 23.87 acres of City-owned land platted as Lot 2, Block 1, Cahill Park
Addition (located at the southeast corner of the intersection of Windmill Road and Dell Range
Boulevard, Cheyenne, Wyoming).  Frank Cole, Cole Shopping Center, spoke in opposition to the City
selling the vacant park land, noting uses in the area for recreational purposes and commenting that the
proposed purchasing offer amount was not high enough.  Barbara Stafford, Friends of Botanic Gardens

Foundation Board, referenced a document (on file with City Clerk’s office) submitted earlier in the day
to governing body members, and spoke about the assistance provided by the Foundation Board in
acquiring release of property use restrictions through the U.S. Dept. of Veterans Affairs, and in support

Menard’s desire to expand its operations to serve the Cheyenne area and the company’s development
plans and construction season timing issues.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Nuttelman provided information on
evaluation of other site location options they had researched within the community, and advised the
Windmill site provides for the necessary infrastructure for utilities and more general access to the
community with room for expansion of other retail businesses.  During discussion, Mr. Nuttelman
explained they did review the Swan Ranch development area but considered it more of an industrial site,
which might be viable for a potential manufacturing and distribution facility, than a commercial retail
site; reviewed the location of Menard stores in the region, and potential opportunity to expand into other
western states in the future.  In response to Mr. Nuttelman’s comments involving evaluation of other
property sites in the area, Mr. Cole advised that he was contacted by Menard’s but at the time of the
inquiry he did not have any property available for retail development in the acreage size Menard’s
desired.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Valdez moved to adopt, seconded by Ms. Ash.  Mr.
Valdez moved to postpone to Sept. 12, 2011, seconded by Mr. Pierson.  Mr. Nuttelman suggested
approving the sale with conditions to resolve any concerns the Council may have, and advised a
postponement may affect the company’s time frame to begin construction.  It was noted the
recommendation to postpone made at Finance Committee was to allow time for review and clarification
of confidential information provided by the City Attorney.  Upon inquiry, Dan White, City Attorney,
provided information on the  attorney-client confidential memorandum he had issued to governing body
members involving a potential property interest claim in the parcel involving the Laramie County
Commissioners.  He explained since providing the memorandum to Council members, the County
Commissioners have asserted a property interest claim and explained this issue needs to be resolved
prior to acting on the resolution.  Discussion included the purpose of postponement, which would allow
time to further review the City Attorney’s memorandum and to provide time for the County
Commissioners to meet to make a final decision on their proposed interest in the property.  Mayor
Kaysen advised, pursuant to communication with County Commission Chair Diane Humphrey, that
the Commissioners have not made a final decision yet on their claim of interest in the property and that
it is still under review, and stated the Commissioners plan to take this up at their Sept. 6th meeting.
Mayor Kaysen recognized County Commissioner Troy Thompson in attendance at the meeting, who
confirmed the information shared by the Mayor was correct.  Motion to postpone carried.  Voting “yes”
-- all members of the governing body present with the exception of Ms. Ash voting “no”.
