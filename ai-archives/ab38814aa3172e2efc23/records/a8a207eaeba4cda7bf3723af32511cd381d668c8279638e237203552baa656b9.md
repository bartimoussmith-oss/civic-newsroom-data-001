BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 2.04.020, Mayor—Election—Term of office, of Chapter 2.04,
Mayor and City Council, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, is hereby amended to read:

2.04.020 - Mayor;—Election—Term of office election; term of office; staffing.

A. The mayor of the city will shall be elected at large. The election shall be conducted in
accordance with Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 22-23-101, et seq. The mayor shall be elected for
a term of four (4) years.

B. All employees hired in the mayor's office are hired as at-will employees and serve at the
pleasure of the mayor. The will exempt mayoral employees from certain The mayor may
hire a chief of staff whose duties shall be specified by the mayor, whose position shall be
at will and who shall serve at the pleasure of the mayor. To the extent any provisions of
the City of Cheyenne Personnel Rules and Regulations conflict with the employment
classification of the chief of staff as an at will employee, or notwithstanding any other
provisions as further specified by the mayor, those provisions shall not apply. Any other
person hired in the mayor's office may only have their employment terminated for just
cause.

Section 2.  Any person employed in the mayor's office prior to the effective date of this
ordinance, other than someone employed as the chief of staff, shall not be deemed to have been
hired as an at will employee and that person's employment with the city of Cheyenne may only be
terminated for just cause.

Section 3.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional or otherwise unlawful, such declaration shall not affect any of the remaining
sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases.
