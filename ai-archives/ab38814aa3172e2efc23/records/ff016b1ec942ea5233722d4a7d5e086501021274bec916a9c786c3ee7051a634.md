Preliminary Plat/UDC-18-00067
3
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
This Final Plat proposes dividing a 4.25 acre parcel into 4 lots for residential development. The land is
currently developed with one residential unit and associated outbuildings. The existing residential
development is anticipated to remain on the proposed Lot 4.

A Preliminary Plat for this land was approved by the City Council on January 22, 2018. The Preliminary
Plat proposed two phases, the first phase (this Final Plat) creating Lots 1-3 out of the larger parcel, and
the second phase (a later final plat) would create Lots 4-9 as shown on the Preliminary Plat map. The
Preliminary Plat indicated drainage improvements would be provided with the first phase.

The submitted Final Plat creates Lots 1-3 out of the larger parcel and provides all drainage easements
found in the Preliminary Plat. This Final Plat fulfills 3 of the 4 conditions placed on the Preliminary Plat
approval. The condition which remains unfulfilled was to provide 10’ of right-of-way to York Avenue. York
Avenue acts as an access to the neighborhood and is a narrow right-of-way which should be wider to
effectively serve the community. However, as there is limited opportunity to obtain right-of-way from
other property owners along York Avenue, the County and the MPO have agreed that requiring
right-of-way as part of this platting action is not necessary. Staff recommends removing the additional
right-of-way condition, and therefore recommends approval of this Final Plat with no conditions.

Due to meeting the review criteria set forth in Section 2.1.3.d of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, staff recommends the Planning Commission approve this final plat.

right-of-way which should be wider to effectively serve the community. However, as there is limited

As the MPO was the primary organization requesting a widening of York Avenue through the
Preliminary Plat process, and as the MPO has agreed with the County that a widening is not
necessary as part of this platting action, Staff recommends approval of this Final Plat with revised
Plat map and no conditions, despite the Final Plat map not fulfilling condition 4 on the Preliminary
Plat approval.

3. SUBDIVISION STANDARDS - The application meets the purposes, intent, and applicable standards of
Article 4.

Transportation Network:
York Avenue was originally platted north of College Drive as a street with 80’ of right-of-way (as
part of Interior Heights, 2nd Filing in 1890). Capitol Tracts, platted in 1938, continued the York
Avenue right-of-way by dedicating 40’ (or half of the right-of-way), south of College Drive. When
Capitol Tracts was originally platted, it was likely expected that the neighboring property owner
to the east would plat another 40’ to continue the 80’ wide York Avenue. All other streets in the
Capitol Tracts plat are 50’ wide.
However, when the property to the east was platted in 1940 (as Orchard Valley) the 40’ east half
of York Avenue right-of-way was not dedicated, leaving York Avenue at 40’ wide, where it
remains to this day.
Unfortunately, a 40’ wide road is not really an acceptable right-of-way width, especially for a road
which acts as access into a neighborhood. To facilitate the transportation of the residents of this
neighborhood, the York Avenue travel way should be widened. Widening the right-of-way,
providing a public access easement, or other solution could be provided to resolve this issue. As
there is limited opportunity to obtain right-of-way from other property owners along York
Avenue, the County and the Metropolitan Planning Organization have agreed that requiring
right-of-way as part of this platting action is not necessary.

Community Facility Fees:
This land is not within City limits. Plat fees are not required unless this property annexes to the
City in the future.

Open Space:
This property is exempt from providing open space during this plat action.
