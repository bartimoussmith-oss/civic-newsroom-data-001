RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: “A
RESOLUTION
CREATING
A
SPECIAL
DISTRICT
FOR
‘CHEYENNE DAYS – LEGENDARY NIGHTS EVENTS’, JULY 17, 2020
TO JULY 26, 2020, IN WHICH THE POSSESSION OF OPEN
CONTAINERS IS PERMITTED WITHIN THE DOWNTOWN DISTRICT
BOUNDARIES.”

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Governing Body has amended Section 5.12.110 to
allow for creation of special districts in which the possession of open containers is permitted; and

WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority (DDA) requests the boundaries of
their district be declared a special district for the “Cheyenne Days – Legendary Nights Event” at
the following dates and times:

1. Paint Slingers 2020 – Saturday, July 18, 2020, from 12:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. (alcohol
serving time – actual event times will be from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.)

2. Cheyenne Day – Wednesday, July 22, 2020, from 10:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.

3. Fridays on the Plaza – Friday, July 24, 2020, from 5:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m.

4. Saturday, July 25, 2020, from 7:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m.; and

WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days is an economic engine for Laramie County that
generates approximately $36.2 million in business activity between visitor spending and the
creation of jobs, generating approximately $1.2 million in local and state sales tax revenue; and

WHEREAS, in the interest of public health and safety, the 2020 Cheyenne Frontier Days
has been cancelled due to the coronavirus pandemic; and

WHEREAS, Cheyenne Days – Legendary Nights Events will promote local shopping by
offering shopping specials, sidewalk sales, and entertainment including a street dance, concerts,
potentially a pancake breakfast, and the Thunderbirds Air Show; and

WHEREAS, shopping locally creates a multiplier effect through direct, indirect, and
induced spending which boosts the local economy and creates more local wealth and jobs; and

WHERAS, a number of studies illustrated on the website of American Independence
Business Alliance (AMIBA) have proven a 48% return to the community occurs when you spend
locally; and

WHEREAS, the event organizers will comply with any and all laws, rules, and
regulations to ensure the safety of the community; and

WHEREAS, adherence to the following requirements are required for participation by
alcohol vendors and consumers when open containers are permitted as designated herein:

1. Wristbands shall be awarded by the licensee only to a person 21 years of age or more and
must be worn and visible to purchase, possess, or consume liquor within the designated
special district downtown area.  The wristbands shall have an identifying mark, name, or
logo, as required by the City.

2. The only containers for alcoholic beverages in the special district downtown area’s public
spaces, for the day specified herein, are designated plastic cups.  The cups shall have an
identifying mark, name, or logo, as required by the City.

3. Beer and malt beverages (including malt beverage alternatives shall be served in 12 oz.
cups; servings of wine shall not exceed a 5 oz. serving; and mixed drinks, whose mixed
contents shall contain no more than 1.5 oz. of distilled spirit(s) per serving.
By Alessandra at 3:42 pm, Jun 18, 2020
