MEMORANDUM
TO:

Governing Body, City of Cheyenne
FROM:

Renee Smith, Grants Manager
DATE:

FEBRUARY 6, 2023
SUBJECT:
GRANT AGREEMENT FOR THE 18TH ST INTERCEPTOR PROJECT

The City of Cheyenne has been awarded a U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) Federal Emergency
Management Agency (FEMA) FY 2021 Hazard Mitigation Grants Program grant for project scoping of the
Cheyenne 18th Street Storm Sewer Interceptor Advance Assistance Project.
The federal award amount is $270,000 with a city match of $30,000 to be funded from the 1% Special Drainage
Account (5th Penny Tax).  The performance period for the project is from August 5, 2021 through July 31,
2025.
The main focus of the project area is the lower/downtown portion of the Capitol Basin and represents the final
phase required to provide critically needed flood protection and ensure the continued vitality of the
community’s core business district.  Grant funds will be used for hydrologic and hydraulic evaluations, data
collection, research, and storm sewer and hydraulic structure design that will be used to draft and submit an
implementation grant.
Please let me know if you have any questions or need clarification on this grant agreement.
Renee Smith
307-637-6285
rsmith@cheyennecity.org

cc Thomas Cobb, City Engineer
