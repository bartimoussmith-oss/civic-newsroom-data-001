On January7,2013, the Cheyenne Planning Commission recommended denialof the
Preliminary Plat and Zone Change for Scenic Subdivision.The Commission cited that the
transportation network plan submitted by the applicant is not consistent with the adopted
transportation network plan, which is a requirement under Section 4.3.2.The Commission also
described aconcern with thelack of pedestrian and street connections to the immediately
adjacent commercial activities.The Commission expressed concern about whether sufficient
open space would be provided.The Cheyenne Planning Commission also expressed concern
over the planning of the drainage features in the area beyond the plat boundaries without
coordinating streets and open space beyond the plat.
The applicant has requested the preliminary plat and zone change bebrought before the
governing body.
The application does attempt torectify the transportation networkwith arevised transportation
networkplan thatis consistent with the Plan Cheyenne Master Transportation Plan.The
following items were discussed with the applicant and need to be made consistent with the
proposed network plan.
1.Identify the street being platted as Melton Street.Melton Street is the local street at this
alignment west of Powder house.Naming the street Melton clarifies the intent reflected
in the network plan the applicant submitted, asopposed to the Carlson Street name which
is not consistent with the network plan submitted.
2.Classify the street as a local street. Similar reasoning as above, Melton Street is a local
street whereas Carlson Street is a collector street. This is an important distinction
because local streets serve a different purpose than collector streets and are constructed
differently aswell (UDC Table 4-5).
