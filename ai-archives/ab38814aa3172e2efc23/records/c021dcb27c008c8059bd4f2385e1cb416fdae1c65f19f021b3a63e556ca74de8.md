the protection, enhancement and perpetuation of areas, improvements and landscape features
having historic character or special historical or aesthetic interest or value is a public necessity;
and
WHEREAS, the City Code stipulates that the governing body must ratify the designation
of any area, building, structure or other feature as being worthy of preservation; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne originally nominated the properties located within the
Capitol North Historic District for consideration of listing to the National Register of Historic
Places in 1980; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne received a grant to survey the properties in the
existing district and updated the Contributing Structures List in 2015; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board received a grant and hired
a consultant during 2018 to conduct a survey of the properties within the expanded area,
generally from 29Lh Street north to Pershing Boulevard and Bent Avenue east to Warren Avenue;
and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board approved the recommended
updates to the Contributing Structures List which added one hundred nineteen (119) structures
within the expanded Capitol North Historic District, as shown in Exhibit A.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City ratifies the addition of one hundred nineteen
(119) structures within the expanded Capitol North Historic District and does officially update
the City’s Contributing Structure List to be protected in accordance with City Code.

Resolution No.
The governing body of the City of Cheyenne is required by City Code to approve by resolution
all properties to be added to and removed from the City’s Contributing Structures List.
Facts bearing on this request are as follows:
1.
The city originally nominated this district to the National Register of Historic Places in
1980; it is the oldest residential neighborhood listed on the National Register in
Cheyenne.
2.
The CHPB applied for a Certified Local Government Grant in November 2017 to expand
the existing district. The grant was approved by the State Historic Preservation Office in
December 2017 for the amount of $12,500.00.
The expanded area in general was from
291h Street north to Pershing Boulevard and Bent Avenue east to Warren Avenue.
3.
A Request For Proposal (RFP) was sent out in February 2018 to consultants; the project
was awarded in April 2018.
4.
The first public hearing was held on June 6, 2018 in Pando Park to inform the
neighborhood of the project work that was about to begin. The neighbors were notified
via letter through the USPS and a notice was published in the Wyoming Tribune-Eagle;
there were thirteen (13) neighbors in attendance.
