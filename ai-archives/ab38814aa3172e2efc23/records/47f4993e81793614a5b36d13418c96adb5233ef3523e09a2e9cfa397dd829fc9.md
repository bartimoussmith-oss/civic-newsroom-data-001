```text
Jim Flesher, Senior Planner

RECOMMENDATION:  The Urban Planning Office recommends acceptance of the grant.

The Wyoming State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) has approved an application for a
Certified Local Government (CLG) grant to fund a historic film contest that will involve local
youth in producing short films on Cheyenne’s history and historic built environment.

CLG grants are 60 percent federally funded with a 40 percent match; however, the value of staff
and volunteer (Board member) time is allowed to be counted for the required match, as is the
value of meeting space provided for public meetings. Members of the Cheyenne Historic
Preservation Board will manage this grant. The award amount is $7,500.
```

```text
RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
EXECUTE A STATE HISTORIC PRESERVATION OFFICE CERTIFIED
LOCAL GOVERNMENT (CLG) GRANT AGREEMENT IN ORDER TO
FUND A HISTORIC CHEYENNE FILM CONTEST PROJECT.”

WHEREAS, Section 2.64.020(A) of the Cheyenne City Code provides as follows: “The
governing body recognizes that many areas, improvements and landscape features have historic
character or special historical or aesthetic interest or value and many improvements representing
the finest architectural products or distinct periods in the history of Cheyenne. It is declared a
matter of public policy that the protection, enhancement and perpetuation of areas, improvements
and landscape features having historic character or special historical or aesthetic interest or value
is a public necessity and is required in the interest of the people.”; and

WHEREAS, according to the National Park Service, “the CLG Program integrates local
governments with the national historic preservation program through activities that strengthen
decision-making regarding historic places at the local level”; and

WHEREAS, according to the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO), “the Wyoming
SHPO strives to help local communities achieve their preservation goals, for it is at the local
level that preservation efforts are often the most effective”; and

WHEREAS, in 1999 the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board produced and distributed
a VHS video entitled “Cheyenne's Historic Homes,” which was well-received by the community;
and

WHEREAS, a local contest to produce a DVD about Cheyenne’s history and historic
built environment would serve to promote historic preservation and engage youth in preservation
activities through education, participation, and ownership of the community’s history; and

WHEREAS, SHPO has awarded the City of Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board a
grant in the amount of $7,500 in order to contract with a professional production company to
develop, promote and administer (with active Historic Preservation Board participation) a short
film/documentary contest for regional film-makers and engage teams of youth film-makers to
produce specific topical documentaries.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, Wyoming, that the City of Cheyenne is hereby authorized to accept the
said grant award in the amount of $7,500.00 from the SHPO to be used solely for the Historic
Cheyenne Film Contest project.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to
execute all documents relating to the grant upon approval of this resolution.
```
