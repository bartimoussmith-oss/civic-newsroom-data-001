A meeting of the Public Services Committee was held on Tuesday, April 2, 2024, in Committee
Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Scott Roybal, Chairman. Pete Layboum (via Zoom). Dr. Rinne.
and Tom Segrave.

— Medium Density for land located south of West 5’’

AVI P.C., was available for additional questions. Committee member Rinne commented on the
item.
