PUBLIC FACILITY AND CIRCULATION EASEMENT AGREEMENT

THIS ACCESS EASEMENT AGREEMENT ("Agreement") is made as of the

day of
, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, a duly-organized municipal corporation, having an address at 2101 O'Neil Avenue,
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 ("City"), and Magic City Enterprises, Inc., having an address at 1780
Westland Road, Cheyenne, WY 82001 ("Magic City," together with the City, the "Parties," and
each individually, a "Party").

RECITALS

A.
Magic City is the fee owner of that certain real property located at 1780 Westland
Road, Cheyenne, Wyoming, and more particularly described in Exhibit A attached
hereto, the northern portion of which, as more particularly described on Exhibit B
attached hereto, Magic City has agreed to subject to the terms of this Agreement
(such portion, the "Easement Area M-1");

B.
The City is the fee owner of that certain real property located at 1800 Westland
Road, Cheyenne, Wyoming, and more particularly described in Exhibit C attached
hereto, the southern portion of which, as more particularly described on Exhibit D
attached hereto, the City has agreed to subject to the terms of this Agreement (such
portion, the "Easement Area C-1," and together with Easement Area M-1, the
"Easement Areas");

C.
Each of the Parties affirms to the other that (i) it is the lawful owner of its real
property as described above, (ii) it has a good and lawful right and authority to
grant, sell, and convey its interest in its property, or any part thereof, described
above, and (iii) it warrants the title to its property described above;

D.
The Parties desire to mutually convey each to the other, and their respective
successors and assigns, the right to use and occupy their respective Easement Areas
for purposes of constructing public facilities for access and other ancillary
purposes, all on the terms and conditions more specifically set forth in this
Agreement; and

E.
The Parties acknowledge that, following execution hereof, they will have a mutual
obligation to maintain the facilities within the Easement Areas in accordance with
this Agreement.

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00), other good and
valuable consideration paid to or received by each of the Parties, and the mutual covenants, terms,
and conditions set forth herein, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the
Parties agree as follows:

located upon the Burdened Party’s real property (the "Burdened Property") for
the benefit of the Benefitted Property, for purposes of pedestrian and vehicular
access, parking, ingress and egress; maintenance and repairs to the Easement
Area; and any and all other ancillary purposes reasonably associated therewith.

3.
Duration. This Easement granted herein shall remain in full force and effect in
perpetuity, unless and until terminated by written agreement signed by the then-
current owners of both the Burdened Property and the Benefitted Property.

4.
Use of Easement Area. The Easement, rights, and privileges set forth herein shall
be for the benefit of the Benefitted Property Owners, each as an owner of a
Benefitted Property, and binding on their respective successors and assigns, each
as owner of a Burdened Property, and each of their respective tenants, licensees,
customers, employees, agents, and invitees. The Easement, rights, privileges,
restrictions, benefits, and obligations hereunder shall be covenants running with
title to each Benefitted Property.

5.
Access. The Parties, including their respective employees, contractors, and agents,
shall have the right of access to the Easement Area twenty-four (24) hours per day,
seven (7) days per week, subject to reasonable periods of temporary closure to
accommodate maintenance, repair and similar activities.  Each Party’s use of the
Easement Area shall be undertaken in a fashion so as not to unreasonably interfere
with the use thereof by the other Party hereto.

Both Parties agree that future modifications to their respective properties by a
change of use defined by the Cheyenne Unified Development Code may affect
traffic trip generation rates. If the actual trip generation rates increase or decrease
by twenty (20) percent or more, regardless of whether a change of use occurs, both
parties shall agree and sign an amendment to this Agreement to adjust their
respective maintenance obligations accordingly. Any costs incurred for the
modification shall be either (a) borne by the Party which changed the use of its
property or (b) split equally if there is no change in use. See paragraph 13.g.

9.
Damage and Destruction.

a.
Neither of the Parties shall have any responsibility to the other or their
respective agents, contractors, tenants, or other invitees in the event of any
damage to or theft or loss of any equipment or property of the other Party,
and the Party incurring such damage, theft, or loss shall look to its own
insurance coverage (and to any self-insured portion of the damage, theft,
or loss), if any, for recovery in the event of any such damage, theft, or loss,
except in each case if the loss is caused by the willful or negligent acts or
omissions of the other Party.

b.
If all, or a portion, of the Easement Areas is destroyed or damaged by fire
or other casualty, the Parties shall promptly proceed to repair such damage
and restore the Easement Areas to the condition existing prior to such
damage. The cost shall be equally shared by both parties, except in the
event the damage is caused by the willful or negligent ats or omissions of
a Party, in which case said Party shall be responsible for such costs.
