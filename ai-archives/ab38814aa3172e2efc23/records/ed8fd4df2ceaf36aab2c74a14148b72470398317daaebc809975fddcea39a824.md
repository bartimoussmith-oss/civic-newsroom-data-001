RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE GOVERNING
BODY TO CONDUCT A REVIEW OF DOWNTOWN PARKING ENFORCEMENT."

WHEREAS, two downtown parking discussions took place on August 15 and 16, 2022,
with stakeholders, elected representatives and traffic enforcement representatives in attendance to
review and analyze the necessity of parking in the downtown area;

WHEREAS, in the spring of 2016, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization
(MPO) engaged Kimley-Horn and Associates to develop a Strategic Parking Management Plan for
downtown Cheyenne. Unique from a more traditional supply/demand assessment, the parking
strategic plan is a higher-level planning and visioning document designed to guide the City and
other stakeholders as they work to improve parking access for all who live in, visit and enjoy
downtown Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, during the August 15 and 16, 2022 parking discussions, many assurances
were made in regard to expanded use of the parking application known as ParkCheyenne, owned
by the City of Cheyenne through the parent software company, Passport, Inc.

WHEREAS, the Governing Body requires additional information on this parking
application. Using examples on how it could be implemented with stakeholders Point of Sale
software, stakeholders invoicing capabilities, and the use of the most modern technology in regard
to scans and coding, ie QR codes.

WHEREAS, the Governing Body shall be informed on how this application could apply to
potential metering in downtown Cheyenne, using the peak time information provided in the
Strategic Parking Management Plan for Downtown Cheyenne, including the capabilities of
adjustable rates during peak times;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body would recommend a revenue assessment done of
downtown Cheyenne blocks determined to have a zero percent (0%) to fifty-five percent (55%)
parking occupancy. This would include citations issued and collected since the date the Cheyenne
Police Department was placed in charge of parking enforcement for the City to June 30, 2022. The
revenue assessment shall be provided by using the zero percent (0%) to fifty-five percent (55%)
parking occupancy in 2016 Strategic Parking Management Plan for Downtown Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes that once the revenue assessment is complete,
the data provided can assist the City Engineer or their designee, with proper guidance on areas of
downtown Cheyenne that did not exhibit parking occupancy issues according to the 2016 Strategic
Parking Management Plan for Downtown Cheyenne;

By Stefanie Boster at 4:24 pm, Sep 02, 2022
