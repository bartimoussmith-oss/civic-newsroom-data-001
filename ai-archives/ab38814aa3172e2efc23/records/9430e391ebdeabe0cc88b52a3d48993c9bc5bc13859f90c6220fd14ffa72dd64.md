TO:

Mayor Kaysen, City Council
FROM:
Matt Ashby, Planning Service Director
SUBJECT:
Updates to Development Review Fee Schedule
DATE:
May 5, 2014
Following discussion at the April 28, 2014 City Council meeting, the following items are presented
for consideration by the Finance Committee:
1.) Clarifications noted by Mr. Emmons at Council Meeting of April 28, 2014 have been reflected in
the attached document.
a. Development Agreement (boilerplate pages excluded), Financial Surety (Clarified), Site
Plan levels have more description, Subdivision Waiver clarifies several may be requested
under one request, and Residential Annexation pocket is defined.
b. The costs for Preliminary and Final Plat have been reversed to allow the preliminary plat
to be the lower cost, since there is a chance the project might not be approved, or the
developer may not choose to proceed with the development.  The higher cost comes
with the final plat, when a developer has more certainty that a project is viable.
2.) The concept of moderating costs for small developments could be addressed by using a flat rate
plus a per lot cost for platting activities.  If the Governing Body would like to consider this
approach, an alternative fee schedule that could be substituted for Preliminary, Expedited, and
Final Plat is listed below:
Plat – Final:
$250 + $15/Lot
Plat – Preliminary Expedited:
$600 + $15/Lot
Plat – Preliminary:
$350 + $15/Lot

In comparing the original “Flat Rate” proposal to the revised “Flat + Per Lot” proposal, the revised
approach successfully reduces the total cost for small developments.  However, it tends to increase the
cost of a larger development.  If the Governing Body wishes to address the scale on the top end, one
approach might be to establish a maximum fee.

3.) Discussion regarding the percentage increase of the fees has been reviewed, and questions have
been raised regarding a stepped implementation of any fee increase.  Should the Governing Body
wish to address this issue, the resolution could be modified to include the following: “Be It
Further Resolved that the schedule of fees, charges, and expenses attached to this Resolution
shall become effective on July 1, 2014 and phased-in over the next x-months/x-years in equal
steps.”  The Governing Body would need to determine what time period is appropriate to phase
in any desired increase.  Phasing possibilities could be 1/3 of proposed on 7/1/14, increasing to
2/3 of proposed 7/1/15, with total proposed implemented 7/1/16.
