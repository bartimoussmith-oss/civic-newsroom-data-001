Flooring Abatement
Based on site visit and information provided by the owner, Asbestos & Technical Services (ATS) has
prepared the following proposal to remove asbestos containing materials from the residential property
located at 2247 E 19th Street, Cheyenne, WY. The identified asbestos materials will be removed as
pad of a demolition project.
Prolect Scope
The base scope of work includes removing the following:
- Asbestos textured drywall from walls and/or ceilings in the Family Room, Kitchen, Dining Room, Hall
Bathroom, Hallway, Bedroom, Workout Room, Powder Bathroom, Back Entrance and Associated
Closets of the main level/lower level of the home (excludes drywall in staircase or basement level)
- Asbestos spray-applied texture and drywall from ceilings in the Family Room, Hallway, Bedroom,
Dining Room and Workout Room areas of the main level) lower level of the home
- Asbestos vinyl floor tile and mastic in the Living Room, Kitchen, Bedroom, Hallway, Laundry Room
and Storage Room in the basement level of the home
- Asbestos vinyl floor tile and mastic in the Bonus Room that is accessible from the back yard and
beneath the building addition/first floor of the building
- Asbestos glazing by component removal of associated windows that are on the first floor and
basement level of the building
- Asbestos cement siding (“transite”) that is on all for exterior sides of the home
The asbestos materials will be removed within a series of containments or regulated work areas that
will created using 6 ml. polyethylene sheeting. All containment structures will be kept under continuous
ne9ative air flow throughout the abatement process. All waste material will be disposed of at an EPA-
approved facility.
Project Costs
The cost to complete the Base Scope detailed above is $39,700.00 and includes the inspection,
abatement labor, materials, equipment, tools, travel costs needed to perform the abatement scope and
waste disposal at an EPA-approved site.
