SUBJECT:
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and GLM Design
Group for the for Dry Creek Master Plan Update and Drainage Code Review project
______________________________________________
Three (3) Responsive Proposals were submitted under Request for Proposal (RFP) #8-22, for the
Dry Creek Master Plan Update and Drainage Code Review project.

The proposals were received from:
1. Anderson Consulting Engineers, Fort Collins, CO.
2. GLM Design Group, Cheyenne, WY.
3. Y2 Consultants LLC, Cheyenne, WY.

Upon review of the three (3) proposals by the evaluation panel and subsequent in person
interviews, the group has recommended acceptance of the proposal submitted by GLM Design
Group, Cheyenne, Wyoming, for a project award amount of $524,234.00 (Design Process &
Construction Period Services).

Basis for award was based on a Qualifications based review and scoring of proposals.  GLM
Design Group submitted the highest scoring proposal, therefore their sealed pricing envelope was
opened & negotiated.
