RESOLUTION NO.
ENTITLED:"A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE BOARD
OF PUBLIC UTILITIES TO DISCHARGE UN COLLECTIBLE DEBTS
PURSUANT TO WYOMING STATUTE16-4-502."
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute16-4-502provides for discharge of un collectible debts upon
proper certification to the Governing Body;
WHEREAS, the City Treasurer has prepared the required certification for un collectible
debts onthe books of the Board of Public Utilities (Board), a copy of which is attached to and
incorporated aspart of this Resolution;
WHEREAS, the debts have been reviewed by the appropriate Board staff and collection
effortshave failed; and
WHEREAS, the Board desires to remove the un collectible debts from its books as
expediently as possible.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City Treasurer is authorized to discharge the un collectible
debts in the amount of fourteen thousand one hundred ninety-five dollars and twenty-eight cents
($14,195.28) from the books of the City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this resolution shall be effective upon passage and
execution by the mayor and city clerk.
