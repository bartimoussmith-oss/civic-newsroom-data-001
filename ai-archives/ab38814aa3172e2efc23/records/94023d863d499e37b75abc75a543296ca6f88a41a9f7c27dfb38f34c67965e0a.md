```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

April 20, 2022
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-18-22 for furnishing Twenty-Nine (29) Trucks, for the
City of Cheyenne, Community Recreation & Events Department, Fleet
Maintenance Division, and Animal Control Division
______________________________________________
Bid #E-18-22 was solicited for furnishing Twenty-Nine (29) Trucks, for the City of Cheyenne,
Community Recreation & Events Department, Fleet Maintenance Division, and Animal Control
Division.
A Mandatory Pre-Bid Meeting was held, and Three (3) Bidders (Ken Garff, Greiner Ford, Cowboy
Chrysler Dodge Jeep Ram), and One (1) Upfitter (Able Equipment) was in attendance.  Two (2)
bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Bids were received from:
1. Ken Garff Cheyenne, Cheyenne, Wyoming.
a. Submitted most responsive bid, not overall lowest cost bid.
i. Page 34, Item 14, Yes, but struck 3.73, and wrote 4.30 limited slip
ii. Page 52, Item 14, Yes, but struck 3.73, and wrote 4.30 limited slip

2. Greiner Ford.  Casper, Wyoming.  Bid Rejected, Multiple Bid Exceptions.
a. Submitted overall lowest cost bid, however bid was least responsive and contained
multiple Bid Exceptions.
i. Conditioned bid to request assistance on prepayment on body equipment,
and potential for cost increases.

“If bids are awarded, we would like assistance on prepaying for equipment
to hold the price.  We will be out over 300 days waiting for vehicles and
equipment.  If you decide to wait to order equipment, there would be
additional manufacturer imposed surcharges passed on.”

ii. Dump Body: Rugby Eliminator was not an approved equal, but Duraclass
Yardbird was.
iii. Wyoming Residency Not Selected, No Certificate Provided.
iv. Page 26, Item 14, Yes, but wrote Limited Slip Axle.
v. Page 44, Item 16, No. Limited Slip Only Available in 4.30 Ratio.
vi. Page 45, Item 31, No.  No Radio Installation.
vii. Page 48, Item 29, No.  No Radio Supply OR Installation.
```
