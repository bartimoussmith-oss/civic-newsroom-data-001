RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROPRIATING EXCESS FUNDS FROM THE
NOVEMBER 2, 2021, ONE PERCENT (1%) SPECIFIC PURPOSE SALES
AND USE TAX FOR THE ACQUISITION OF REAL PROPERTY.”

WHEREAS, at the special election held in Laramie County on November 2, 2021, voters
approved specific purposes to be funded by a one percent (1%) specific purpose sales and use tax;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 39-15-211, the Governing Body may appropriate its
proportionate share of excess funds from such tax for other specific purposes authorized by a
majority vote of the Governing Body, provided such appropriation does not fund the ordinary
operations of local government;

WHEREAS, final accounting and reconciliation of revenues from the November 2, 2021
tax are not yet complete, with completion anticipated to occur in May or June 2026;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body has identified the property commonly known as the
former Senior Center, located at 2101 Thomes Avenue and owned by the Cheyenne Housing
Authority, as suitable for municipal purposes;

WHEREAS, the property’s proximity to the City’s municipal building will provide
operational efficiencies, improved public access to services, and enhanced coordination of City
functions;

WHEREAS, acquisition and renovation of the existing structure is more cost-effective than
acquisition and construction of a new facility, and therefore represents a fiscally responsible use
of public funds;

WHEREAS, based on historical performance of comparable ballot measures, including
approximately Five Million Seven Hundred Twenty-Nine Thousand Four Hundred Fifty-Two
Dollars ($5,729,452.00) in excess funds generated from the 2017 specific purpose sales and use
tax, the Governing Body finds that sufficient excess revenues are expected to be available to fund
acquisition of the Senior Center; and

WHEREAS, the use of excess specific purpose sales and use tax revenues for the
acquisition of real property constitutes a capital expenditure and does not fund ordinary
governmental operations, in compliance with Wyo. Stat. § 39-15-203(a)(iii)(B).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that, to the extent excess funds are available following final
reconciliation, excess tax funds from the November 2, 2021 one percent (1%) specific purpose
sales and use tax in an amount not to exceed One Million One Hundred Seventy-Seven Thousand
Sixty-Two Dollars and Ninety-Seven Cents ($1,177,062.97) are hereby appropriated for the
purpose of acquiring the real property located at 2101 Thomes Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming, from
the Cheyenne Housing Authority.
8:29 am, Apr 21, 2026
