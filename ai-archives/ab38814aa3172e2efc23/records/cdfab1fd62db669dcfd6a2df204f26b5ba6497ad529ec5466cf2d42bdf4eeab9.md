TO:

Mayor Marian Orr, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

April 5, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-26-21 for furnishing Aggregates on an annual basis, for
the City of Cheyenne, Street & Alley Division
______________________________________________
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) bids were received from Knife River d/b/a JTL Group Inc. (Cheyenne, WY) and
Simon Contractors (Cheyenne, WY).

The bids were reviewed by Randy Hickman (Street & Alley), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the bid from Knife River d/b/a JTL Group Inc.

The bid award is for the purchase of the following:

1. Knife River d/b/a JTL Group Inc. (Cheyenne, WY)
a. 5,000 Tons of Aggregate at $8.00 Per Ton ($40,000.00)
b. Total Award:  $40,000.00
i. *Note* The Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) Requested 6,000 Tons of
Aggregate under this bid also.  They will be awarding theirs separately.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-32303 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Sand & Aggregates)

$40,000.00
