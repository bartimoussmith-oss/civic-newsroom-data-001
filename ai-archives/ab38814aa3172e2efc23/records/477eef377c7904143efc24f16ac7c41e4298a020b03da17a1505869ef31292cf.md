BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 15.08.031, Building permit fees–Exceptions for incentivization
program, of Chapter 15.08, International Building Code Adopted, of Title 15, Buildings and
Construction, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is amended as follows:

15.08.031
Building permit fees–Exceptions for incentivization program.

A.
The city of Cheyenne governing body along with the downtown development
authority desires to encourage and incentivize quality infill projects and the redevelopment and
reuse of existing buildings and property within the downtown core. Programs that reduce the total
amount of project fees paid have been found to successfully incentivize property development.

B.
A program, to be called the “City of Cheyenne Core Development Incentivization
Program,” is hereby established as of the effective date of ordinance no. 4241.

1.
To be eligible for the program, an applicant must fulfill the following:

a.
Upon a form to be provided, complete and submit an application to
the city planning and development department.

b.
A building permit application must be received by the city building
department prior to June 30, 2019 2025.

C.
The city shall waive building permit fees, as set forth in Section 15.08.030 of this
chapter, up to five thousand dollars ($5,000.00) per program project located within the boundaries
of the core development incentivization program project area identified in exhibit A (map) attached
to ordinance no. 4241. A map of the program project area is also available through the planning
and development department.

D.
A decision from the director of planning and development, or their designee, on
any matters of interpretation in this section shall be final.

E.
Planning and development staff shall provide reports prior fiscal year program
activity to the governing body not less than annually quarterly. The reports shall contain:

1.
The total number of projects utilizing the program.

2.
The total amount of fees waived.

3.
The total valuation of projects incentivized by the program.

F.
The total amount of building permit fees waived shall be capped at one hundred
thousand dollars ($100,000.00) for each fiscal year 2018-2019 as determined by the city treasurer.

G.
The provisions of Section 15.08.031 of this chapter shall be in effect until June 30,
2019 2025.

By Alessandra at 8:57 am, Nov 15, 2021
