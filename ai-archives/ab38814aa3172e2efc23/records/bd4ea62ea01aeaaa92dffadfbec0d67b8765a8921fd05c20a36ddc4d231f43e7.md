19th Street, Cheyenne Wyoming 82001("BOARD"), and the City of Cheyenne, Youth
Alternatives,1328Talbot Court, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001("CONTRACTOR").The
BOARDand CONTRACTOR have previously entered intoa contract for services in the
form of a Juvenile Diversion Coordinator to be provided by CONTRACTOR for the period
of time between January1,2025 and June 30,2026, alsoknown as City Contract #8111
("CONTRACT")
2.
Purpose of Amendment.This FIRST AMENDMENT shall constitute the first
amendment to the CONTRACT between the BOARD and the CONTRACTOR.The
purpose of this Amendment is to: a)increase the total potential CONTRACT dollar amount
by up to Seventy-Eight Thousand, One Hundred Seventy-Five dollars and Sixty-Five Cents
($78,175.65)fora total maximum contract amount of Two Hundred Thirty-Four Thousand,
Four Hundred Twenty-Six dollars and Ninety-Five cents ($234,426.95) to allow for the
BOARD to compensate the CONTRACTOR for services provided by a Diversion
Coordinator, and b)allow for CONTRACTOR to provide services and receive
compensation for those services by a Diversion Caseworker.
Theoriginal CONTRACT required the CONTRACTOR to provide services in the form of
a Juvenile Diversion Coordinator working directly with the Laramie County District
Attorney's Office for a total maximum payment amount of One Hundred and Fifty-Six
Thousand, Two Hundred Fifty-One dollars and Thirty cents with an expiration datefor the
CONTRACT of June 30,2026.Although the total maximum payment amount is being
increased by this Amendment, the term of the CONTRACT is not changed by the
Amendment and the end dateofthe CONTRACT remains June30,2026.
3.
Termof the Amendment.This Amendment shall commence upon the date the last
required signature is affixed hereto,(Effective Date), and shall remain in full force and
effect through the term of the CONTRACT unless terminated at an earlier date pursuant to
the provisions of the CONTRACT.
