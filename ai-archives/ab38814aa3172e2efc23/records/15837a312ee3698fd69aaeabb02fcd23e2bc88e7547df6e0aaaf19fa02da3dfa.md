MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

June 8, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-32-21 for furnishing One (1) New 2021-2022 Front End
Wheel Loader With Less Than 10 Hours (Compost Facility), for the City of
Cheyenne, Sanitation Division
______________________________________________
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) Bids were received from:
1. Honnen Equipment.  Cheyenne, Wyoming.
a. Submitted Two Bid Options (Bids In Compliance, One Bid with No Exceptions,
One Bid With Bid Exceptions Present).
2. Titan Machinery.  Windsor, Colorado.
a. Submitted Two Bid Option (Bids In Compliance, No Bid Exceptions).
3. Tri-State Truck and Equipment.  Casper, Wyoming.
a. Submitted One Bid Option (Bid In Compliance, Bid Exceptions Present)

The bids were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), Matt
Theriault (Landfill and Compost Manager), and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance).  Their
recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Honnen Equipment, for One (1) New
2021-2022 Front End Wheel Loader With Less Than 10 Hours (Compost Facility), Less Trade In,
for a bid award amount of $206,000.00.

Funding:
023-14-1423-43-43102 (Solid Waste Fund / Equipment)

Total $206,000.00
