The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

The proposed NR3 zone is to match the existing NR3 zone to the east to allow the continuation
of the existing multifamily development. This area is planned to be residential and a mix of
commercial according to the Comprehensive Plan. The currently vacant land surrounding the
development is anticipated to develop with a mix of commercial and higher-density residential
uses. The higher-density residential uses associated with the NR-3 zone would be compatible
with this anticipated development pattern.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property is being annexed into City Limits and being assigned the AG Agricultural zone. The
AG zone may be appropriate if the landowner wished to continue the current agricultural use,
but is not appropriate for urban development. The AG zone would not allow multifamily
development. The NR3 would match the existing multifamily development to the east and
allows multifamily as a use by right.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The proposed area is also being replatted and proposing to extend E Carlson St. Utilities and
infrastructure will be extended with the development. The proposed development is an
extension of the existing one to the east. The NR-3 zone is intended to have a more compact
development pattern than the current block allows. Connections to the street network to the
north, west, and south are needed, as are internal pedestrian connections if road connections
are infeasible. The street network issues will be addressed with the proposed platting action.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

the primarily residential use of land. The overall density of this district should be more than 15

achieve these objectives and match the existing development to the east.

of their existing multifamily development, which was recently constructed and managed to
meet current design standards.
