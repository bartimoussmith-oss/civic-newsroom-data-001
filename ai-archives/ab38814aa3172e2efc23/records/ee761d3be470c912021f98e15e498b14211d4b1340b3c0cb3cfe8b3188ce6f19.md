Zone Change | UDC-20-00038

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from County LI Light Industrial to P Public
District for four City-owned tracts adjacent to Crow Creek. The area is proposed to be annexed to the
City of Cheyenne and therefore requires a City zone. The subject tracts are adjacent to Crow Creek,
where the Crow Creek Revival effort is anticipating completing stream restoration. Additionally, future
Greenway is planned to go through or adjacent to the subject tracts.

The current County zoning designation of LI Light Industrial is not suitable for the planned Greenway
through the City-owned tracts. Additionally, most of the property is within the FEMA Floodway or the
FEMA 100-year Floodplain, making future light industrial development on the property unlikely. The
planned stream restoration project on Crow Creek through the tracts further conflicts with light industrial
development.

The City ownership, stream restoration, and future Greenway through the tracts are most compatible
with a recreation and open space designation, which is achieved through the P Public District. The P zone
is intended primarily for public sites, buildings and uses where activities conducted provide service to the
public, including educational facilities, recreational areas and community service facilities. The P zone is
compatible with the variety of existing development patterns, existing zoning, and future land use
designations found on the surrounding properties.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change.
