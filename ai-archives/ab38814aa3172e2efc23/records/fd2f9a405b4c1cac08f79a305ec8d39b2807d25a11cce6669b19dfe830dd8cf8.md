RE: Clear Creek Subdivision 1 and 2uid Filings
On December 14, 2017, the agent for the applicant for Clear Creek Subdivision 1st and 2 Filings
submitted revised maps to City Staff. The revised maps have fulfilled a majority of the conditions as listed
in the Staff Report.
Condition 3 on Clear Creek Subdivision, 1 Filing was to “Provide an access to Lot 2, Block 1”. This access
has been provided by suppling an access easement through Clear Creek Subdivision 2 Filing
— however,
the new access easement on the 2nd Filing plat map is lacking dimensional data, which will be needed on
the recorded document.
Staff recommends approval of Clear Creek Subdivision, 1 Filing with revised map and the following
condition:
1.
Include the Mayor’s and City Clerk’s names and titles in the signature block in the dedication
statement.
Staff recommends approval of Clear Creek Subdivision, 2nd Filing with revised map and the following
conditions:
1.
Include the Mayor’s and City Clerk’s names and titles in the signature block in the dedication
statement.
2.
Provide dimensional data for the 40’ access easement OR provide a reference book & page if this
access easement follows a pre-existing easement.
Regards,
Seth Lloyd
Planner
