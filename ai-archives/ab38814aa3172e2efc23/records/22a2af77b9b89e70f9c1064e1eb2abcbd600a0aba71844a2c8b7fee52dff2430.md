RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE ADOPTION OF A VISION ZERO
POLICY TO ELIMINATE FATALITIES AND SERIOUS INJURIES THAT
ARE A RESULT OF CRASHES ON CITY STREETS WITHIN THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE BY 2040."

WHEREAS, the life and health of all persons living and traveling within the city of
Cheyenne are the city's utmost priority, and no one should die or be seriously injured while
traveling on city streets;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization ("MPO") created a
complete streets policy in 2006 as part of Plan Cheyenne, intending to improve safety and
infrastructure for all road users;

WHEREAS, the city of Cheyenne’s transportation infrastructure serves an increasing
number of vulnerable road users such as pedestrians and bicyclists;

WHEREAS, twenty-five (25) people in the Cheyenne urban area lost their lives to traffic
deaths from 2019 through 2022, and traffic crashes are among the leading cause of deaths in the
United States;

WHEREAS, according to the Cheyenne MPO, in the period from 2019 through 2022,
seventy-five (75) crashes involved pedestrians and forty (40) crashes involved bicyclists, resulting
in eight (8) fatal crashes involving pedestrians and one (1) fatal crash involving a bicycle;

WHEREAS, the number of people dying and suffering serious injuries on streets within
the city of Cheyenne is a serious public health problem which necessitates public action;

WHEREAS, Vision Zero is the concept that traffic deaths and serious injuries on our
roadways are unacceptable and preventable;

WHEREAS, Vision Zero sets the goal of eliminating traffic deaths and serious injuries in
a set time frame with clear, measurable strategies;

WHEREAS, Vision Zero recognizes that people will sometimes make mistakes, so the road
system and related policies should be designed to ensure that those inevitable mistakes do not
result in severe injuries or fatalities; therefore, transportation planners and engineers
