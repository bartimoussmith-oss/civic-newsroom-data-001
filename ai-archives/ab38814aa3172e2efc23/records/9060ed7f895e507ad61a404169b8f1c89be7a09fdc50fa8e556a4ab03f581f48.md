WHEREAS, the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides
funding to communities to address housing and urban development problems; and

WHEREAS, HUD requires entitlement communities receiving Community Development
Block Grant (CDBG) funds to develop a comprehensive consolidated plan on a five-year basis;
and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Housing and Community Development Office
prepared the “City of Cheyenne Comprehensive Consolidated Plan 2015-2019,”  incorporating
the Annual Action Plan 2015-2016, for review and adoption by the Governing Body; and

WHEREAS, the “City of Cheyenne Comprehensive Consolidated Plan 2015-2019" was
developed with citizen participation and has completed a public comment period.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the “City of Cheyenne Comprehensive Consolidated
Plan 2015-2019" is adopted and the Cheyenne Housing and Community Development Office is
authorized to submit the Plan to the Rocky Mountain, Region 8, HUD Office.  A copy of the
“City of Cheyenne Comprehensive Consolidated Plan 2015-2019" is attached to this Resolution.
