 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-5-24 was solicited for furnishing One (1) New 2023-2024 Motorized Articulated Trash
Compactor for the City of Cheyenne Landfill.
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1. Wyoming Machinery Company

The bids were reviewed by Sam Quattrini (Landfill), Jeff Vassar (Landfill), Craig LaVoy (Public
Works) and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance) and their recommendation is to accept the lowest
responsive bid from Wyoming Machinery Company.

The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, for an amount
not-to-exceed Fifteen Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid.
The total Bid Award amount is $882,180.00 (New Unit, Less Trade-In) + Potential Future
Surcharge Contingency (15%) $158,577.00, for a grand total of $1,040,757.00.
The City does wish to accept the Trade-In Offer for Block #5922, but will not be selecting any of
the Additional Bid Items (ABI’s).

Funding:
