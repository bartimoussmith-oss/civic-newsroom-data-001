SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-22-23 for furnishing Bituminous Asphalt Hot
Mix, Tack Oil, and Cold Mix on an annual basis, for the City of Cheyenne,
Street & Alley Division
 ______________________________________________

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) bids were received from:

1.
JTL Group Inc. d/b/a Knife River (Cheyenne, WY)
2.
Simon Contractors (Cheyenne, WY)

The bids were reviewed by Bill Gonzalez (Street & Alley), Mike Schlabs (Street & Alley), Craig
LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive Bid from JTL Group Inc. d/b/a Knife
River for the purchase of Bituminous Asphalt Hot Mix ($384,625.00), Tack Oil ($16,000.00),
and Cold Mix ($39,000.00) on an annual basis, for a total cost of $439,625.00.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-32302 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Asphalt)

$439,625.00
