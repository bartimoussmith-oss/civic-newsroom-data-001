CITY CONTRACT # _________

AGREEMENT FOR THE PURCHASE AND SALE OF LANDS

This Agreement for the Purchase and Sale of Lands (“Agreement”) is made effective as
of the date of execution (“Effective Date”) of the last signing party below, and entered into by
and between the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized under the laws
of the State of Wyoming, having its principal offices at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and Sunflower Ridge L3C, a limited liability corporation organized
under the laws of the State of Wyoming, having its principal offices at 2900 Sunflower Road,
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009 (“Seller”) and sometimes individually referred to herein as “Party”
and collectively to as “Parties;”

WHEREAS, Seller owns real property situated at 2900 Sunflower Road, Laramie
County, Wyoming (“Real Property”), with an appraised value of four million five hundred
thousand dollars ($4,500,000.00), plus equipment, for a total value of five million dollars
($5,000,000.00), which real property is particularly described in “Exhibit A – Legal Description
and map of Real Property,” attached to this Agreement and incorporated herein by this reference;

WHEREAS, Seller operates the BEAST Foundation at the Real Property, which is a
single-level, special-use property that provides quality academic enrichment and sports programs
for youth, in addition to programs for adults;

WHEREAS, the Real Property contains building improvements consisting of two (2)
structures containing a total of twenty-eight thousand four hundred seventy-five (28,475) square
feet of gross building area;

WHEREAS, the Real Property was developed in 1960 and originally operated as a
religious facility consisting of three thousand one hundred eighty-three (3,183) square foot
structure. In 2016, the Seller purchased the religious facility and added a new gymnasium
building consisting of an additional twenty-five thousand two hundred ninety-two (25,292)
square feet. In 2018, the Seller constructed the current gymnasium building, and the religious
facility is now utilized as office space. The gymnasium portion of the Real Property includes a
lounge area, two restrooms, a concession area and four (4) basketball courts that can be
converted for other uses such as for volleyball and pickleball play. These improvements were
constructed upon a ninety-five thousand three hundred and nine (95,309) square foot (2.188 acre)
site;

WHEREAS, the Real Property is fully furnished with certain gym equipment which is
affixed to the Real Property;

WHEREAS, Seller desires to sell their Real Property to the City, to include all equipment
and improvements; and

WHEREAS, the City wishes to purchase Seller's Real Property.
