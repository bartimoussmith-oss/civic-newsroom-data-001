BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from City PUD (ORD 3989) to CB Community
Business for 36.4 acres of land.

The proposed CB zoning allows variety of commercial, customer-based uses.

The land is currently vacant (although some limited agricultural operations – such as grazing – may be
operating on the site). The impetus behind the zone change is an anticipated recreation use which will
include food service and potentially lodging uses at a later date. The City PUD also defaults to the pedestrian-
oriented design standards of the UDC for non-industrial uses, while the CB zone’s design standards are less
onerous for a vehicle-oriented development.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is very close to the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure.

The market opportunity associated with the freeway interchange would encourage the
development of automobile-oriented commercial uses in this area. PlanCheyenne encourages
the flexibility to be responsive to market demands.

This property is part of a designated gateway to Cheyenne. Investment has been put into the
Welcome Center and the I-25 and High Plains Road interchange to make a positive impact on
those entering not only Cheyenne, but the State of Wyoming. Gateways are encouraged to
provide an enhanced visual experience, including the use of landscaping.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-use Employment Campus.
Surrounding areas are designated Industrial, Mixed-use Employment Campus, and Mixed-use
Commercial. According to comprehensive plan the optimal City zone for the subject property’s
future land use designation MUE Mixed-use Employment, although LI Light Industrial or CB
Community Business zoning is often also applicable. The proposed zoning is CB. Mixed-use
Employment Campus areas are encouraged to develop with a primary focus on employment
designed in a business campus setting. Parks, plazas, pedestrian amenities, and commercial uses
are expected parts of development established under this designation. While the regional
development pattern and proposed zoning appear antithetical to these objectives, the plan
specifically uses Swan Ranch as an example of a ‘developing’ Mixed-Use Employment Campus
(and the plan was adopted after the original Swan Ranch PUD was adopted). It is evident that
the framers of PlanCheyenne considered the Swan Ranch Rail Park as an acceptable form of
Employment Campus, and designated this region as “Employment Campus” to reflect the
current Swan Ranch conditions.

This property is also designated as a Community/Regional Activity Center on the Future Land
Use Map. Activity Centers are intended for a mix of commercial and residential uses with access
to regional transportation. Regional-serving retail is expected to be a major component of a
Community/Regional Activity Center. The proposed CB zone is considered one of the optimal
zoning for activity centers of this type.

The policies of PlanCheyenne are supportive of this proposed zone change. The future land use
plan encourages more commercial-style zoning at this location.

The proposed zone change largely complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

All surrounding property within City are zoned with the Swan Ranch PUD, which is also the
current zoning of this parcel. All surrounding property outside of City limits west of I-25 are
zoned with the County PUD. Both the City and County PUDs allow a variety of industrial uses,
with some commercial, service, or office uses.

This proposed zone change would allow this parcel to develop with more of a commercial
nature than the surrounding property; however the property to the south is more of an office
building, and commercial uses at this location may be able to provide services otherwise
unavailable in the vicinity.

The proposed zoning is also compatible with the future land use designation of an activity
center.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property currently is zoned with an industrial-centric PUD, along with most of the
surrounding area. The UDC building design standards for PUD development enforce pedestrian-
scale design standards unless otherwise specifically indicated in the PUD, and this PUD only
otherwise indicates for industrial uses. The nature of the property makes the property much
more likely to develop with commercial uses if the property is zoned CB than if the property was
zoned with the Swan Ranch PUD zone. The location of the property close to an interchange
makes the development of automobile-oriented commercial uses expected and appropriate.

The current PUD basically defers to the HI zone for allowed uses. The HI zone prohibits a
number of classic highway interchange commercial uses – including retail, food service, and
lodging. The proposed CB zone allows these example uses as uses by right.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

Block sizes are not applicable in areas designated mixed-use employment or industrial. Access
may be limited based on street classification and proximity to the interstate, but specific access
would be reviewed with plat(s) or site plan(s). Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the
area.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.
