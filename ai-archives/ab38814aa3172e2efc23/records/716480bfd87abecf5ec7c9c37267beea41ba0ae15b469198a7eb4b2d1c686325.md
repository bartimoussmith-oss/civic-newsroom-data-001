Bid #E-24-23 was solicited for Four (4) New Trucks for the City of Cheyenne Landfill, Aquatics,
and Facilities Divisions.

One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1.  Ken Garff Cheyenne.  Cheyenne, Wyoming.

The bid was reviewed by Steve Gaer (Facilities), Samuele Quattrini (Landfill), Kyle Myers
(Aquatics), Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and Vicki Nemecek
(Public Works), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Ken Garff
Cheyenne.

The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, for an amount
not-to-exceed Fifteen Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid

total of $300,562.80.
