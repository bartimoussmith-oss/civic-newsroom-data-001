WHEREAS, the City ofCheyenne acknowledges its citizens’ rights to access public records,
and to obtain such records pursuant to Wyoming law; and
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Public Records Act, Wyoming Statutes § § 16-4-201 through 16-
4-205, provides authority and sets forth procedures for the City to follow with regard to responding
to requests for public records; and
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 164-204(c) requires any fees or charges assessed by a
custodian of public records to “first be authorized by duly enacted or adopted statute, rule,
resolution, ordinance, executive order or other like authority”;
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the following fee schedule be established for all requests for
public records; and
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that nothing in this resolution shall be construed as
authorizing a fee to be charged as a condition of making a public record available for public
inspection.
