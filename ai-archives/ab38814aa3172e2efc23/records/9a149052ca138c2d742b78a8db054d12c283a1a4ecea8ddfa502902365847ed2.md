MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Jeanie Shrednik, Parks and Greenway Planner
Subject: Grant of Utility Easement between Cheyenne Light, Fuel and Power Company DBA Black
Hills Energy located on a portion of Lot 8, Centennial Heritage, 2nd Filing on City owned
property
Date:
June 17, 2024

Background:
The item for consideration is a grant of 244+/- SF utility easement to Cheyenne Light, Fuel and Power
Company DBA Black Hills Energy for utility facilities on a portion of Lot 8, Block 1, Centennial Heritage,
2nd Filing.  The requested easement is located in the area of a future Greenway and language has been
added for required bury depth of utility facilities to accommodate future Greenway construction.  Both
the agreement and exhibit have been reviewed and approved by City Surveyor

The Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral 06/24/2024
•
Public Services Committee 07/02/2024

Final Reading 07/08/2024

UTILITY EASEMENT

THIS EASEMENT is made and entered into this _____ day of ____________, 2024, by
and between the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming (“City”), a Municipal Corporation, its
successors and assigns, (hereinafter “Grantor”), and Cheyenne Light, Fuel and Power
Company DBA Black Hills Energy; having an address of 1301 West 24th Street, Cheyenne,
WY 82001 (hereinafter “Grantee”).

Grantor, in consideration of the sum of Ten Dollars ($10.00) and other valuable
consideration, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, hereby grants, bargains, sells and
conveys to Grantee, its lessees, licensees, successors and assigns, a perpetual right-of-way and
utility easement for an UNDERGROUND ELECTRIC POWER DISTRIBUTION LINE(S),
collectively “Facilities”; and all appurtenances and appliances necessary in connection
therewith, including but not limited to any and all necessary attachments and appurtenances for
said Facilities;

TOGETHER WITH the power to extend to any communications company, or “dry”
utilities provider, the right to use any trench or conduit placed pursuant to the provisions hereof.

AND TOGETHER WITH the right, privilege and perpetual right-of-way to survey,
construct, operate, patrol, inspect, maintain, alter, repair, rebuild, upgrade, up-rate, enhance,
replace, and remove said facilities, under and over said lands, now or at any future time, and the
right of ingress and egress to and from the Facilities of Grantee. Said Easement being more
particularly described as follows:

An 10-foot-wide utility easement being more particularly described as:

All of the West 10 feet of Lot 8, Block 1, Centennial Heritage, 2nd Filing, City of
Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming.

Said Easement containing approximately 244 square feet more or less.

As a further consideration of this agreement, the parties hereto agree as follows:

1.
Grantee has selected the route for said Facilities over and through said premises,
and shall be responsible for all construction, reconstruction, operation, installation, use, repair,
replacement, upkeep, monitoring, and maintenance of the Facilities,

2.
That Grantee will bury all Facilities a minimum of 36 inches below existing
ground to allow for construction of future Greenway trail,

3.
That Grantee will replace or rebuild any and all unusual or excessive damage to
the easement property, and/or pay for any damage caused which shall be occasioned by the
construction, operation and maintenance of said Facilities under and through the above-described
premises,
