BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1. That the owner(s) of the property described herein have applied for a zoning
map amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map
Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).
Section 2. That on September 5, 2023, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission
conducted a public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this
ordinance, and recommended approval of said zone change.
Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change
that is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.
Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the
aforementioned application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property
described as Lots 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, & 8, Block 1, Hitching Post Plaza, 3rd Filing is hereby changed to
MUB Mixed-Use Business.
Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as
described in Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

publication, and upon the official filing of the affiliated Hitching Post Plaza, 3rd Filing final plat

PUDC-23-103

September 5, 2023
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-Use Commercial and Mixed-Use
Residential and the surrounding area is Mixed-Use Commercial and Mixed-Use Residential.
According to the comprehensive plan the optimal City zones for the subject property’s future
land use designations is MUB Mixed-Use Business and MUR Mixed-Use Residential, although a
variety of commercial, residential, and mixed-use zones could also be appropriate based on the
context of the site.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Nearby properties to the north are zoned MUB. This is the same zone that is proposed with this
zoning map amendment.

Nearby properties to the north, south, east, and west are zoned CB. Some of these properties
are a part of a commercial corridor along a principal arterial. The Comprehensive Plan
encourages commercial activities which serve neighborhoods, the community, and region. The
Comprehensive Plan encourages the blending of CB zones with nearby neighborhoods in terms
of scale, design, signage, and lighting. The MUB zone is intended for a diverse mix of residential,
retail, service, employment and civic uses integrated into a walkable compact development
pattern.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current CB zone allows a variety of commercial uses but prohibits residential uses and
requires large setbacks from streets. The proposed MUB zone allows a variety of commercial
uses, residential uses, and mixed uses. The MUB zone also allows buildings to be built to the
street. The MUB zone has a higher design standard than the CB zone; however, the subject
property should be able to meet the required the design standards.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The existing block is the type expected for the urban-style of development anticipated for MUB
zoning. CB zoning typically anticipates larger suburban-style blocks. The proposed pattern with
the associated plat is compatible with the proposed zoning and no further subdivision is
expected.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The MUB zone is intended for a diverse mix of residential, retail, service, employment and civic
uses integrated into a walkable, compact development pattern. The MUB District is applicable to
areas where more intense development and diverse mix of uses is appropriate with a high level
of pedestrian and civic amenities.

The existing development patterns, including existing uses, are reflective of the intent and
applicability of the MUB zone. Commercial and Civic uses are within a short distance of the
subject property.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The existing property meets the standards of the MUB zone. The MUB zone allows a building to
be brought closer to the street, while a new building on this property in the CB zone would
require 25’ setbacks from both streets.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No reviewing agencies provided recommendations regarding the proposed zone change.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
