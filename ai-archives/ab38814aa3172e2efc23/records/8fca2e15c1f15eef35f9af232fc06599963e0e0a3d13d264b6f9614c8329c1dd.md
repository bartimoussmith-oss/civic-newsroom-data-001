WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyo.Stat.Ann.§ 1-40-118, all grantees receiving V AWA and

deadlines established through the Office on Violence Against Women and the Family Violence
Prevention and Services Program; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance
Program will apply for funds to provide basic, direct services to victims of crime andbe in good
standing with the Wyoming Division of Victim Services Rules.Awarded applications are funded
with one, or a combination of, State Domestic Violence and Sexual Assault funds, State
Victim/Witness funds, State Child Advocacy funds, State Salary funds, Federal Violence Against
Women Act (VAWA) Services, Training, Law Enforcement Officers, and Prosecution (STOP)
funds, Sexual Assault Services Program (SASP) funds, federal Victim of Crime Act (VOCA)
funds and Federal Family Violence Prevention Services Act (FVPSA) funds; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance
Program will submit an application through the Wyo Grants System by Sunday, March 15, 2020
for State Fiscal Year2021and2022; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance will
be required to submit a DUNS number and register the DUNS number with the System for Award
Management(SAM) database; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will be required to meet a 20% cash or in-kind match.
Applicants will not be required to provide match information at the time of application.Upon
receipt of the award notice, applicants will be required to submit a final detailed budget, including
match, based on actual award amounts as part of their award stipulations and submit a match
certification form; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne, the City of Cheyenne,
Cheyenne Police Department Victims Assistance, understand the long-term obligations of the
Wyoming Division of Victim Services Funding Application State Fiscal Year2021 and 2022, July
1,2020 to June 30,2022.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body approves the funding application and
application process through the Wyoming Division of Victim Services State Fiscal Year 2021and
2022 (July 1, 2020 to June 30, 2022).
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to execute
all documents relating to the Wyoming Division of Victim Services State Fiscal Year 2021 and
2022 (July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022).
