MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Jeanie Vetter, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner
Subject: A resolution supporting the conversion of a portion of Dutcher Ball Fields, located at 4200
Converse Avenue from perpetual public outdoor recreation use and committing to
purchase and encumber one additional replacement property to be deed restricted for
perpetual outdoor recreation use pursuant to the requirements set forth in the Land and
Water Conservation Fund Act.

Date:
August 23, 2023

Background:

The item for consideration is a resolution of support required by the LWCF conversion process to request
conversion of a portion of Dutcher Ball Fields (Saddle Tramps Site), approximately 2.5 acres, to be
removed from perpetual public outdoor recreation use for a new city fire station.  The LWCF regulations
require that we provide other suitable properties that meet LWCF requirements such as being of at least
equal fair market value and of reasonably equivalent usefulness as trade.  In October of 2022, the
Governing Body, with Resolution #6278, previously approved three properties as replacement properties
with all three having a total value of $537,000.00.  The value did not meet the requirement of at least
equal fair market value as compared to the Dutcher Ball Field (Saddle Tramps) Fire Station site value of
$540,000.00, therefore, an additional property is needed for conversion. Appraisal has been completed
for the Lewark property and along with the previous 3 properties meets the requirement of equal fair
market value.  The Lewark property is intended to be purchased with 6th Penny Greenway Expansion
funding and will provide needed land for the Sun Valley Open Space to Kiwanis Park Greenway Connector.

Upon purchase, the City will be required to place deed restrictions on all four replacements properties
which will restrict their use to outdoor recreational uses in perpetuity.

WHEREAS, The City desires to convert approximately two point five (2.5) acres of the
Dutcher Ball Field ("Saddle Tramps") property to a non-recreational use to build a municipal fire
station;

WHEREAS, Section 6(f)(3) of the Land and Water Conservation Fund Act ("LWCF")
(now codified at 54 U.S.C. § 200305(f)(3)) and its implementing regulations at 36 C.F.R. Part 59
requires that no property acquired or developed with LWCF assistance shall be converted to other
than public outdoor recreation uses without the approval of the Secretary of the Interior, and only
if the Secretary finds it to be in accordance with the then existing Statewide Comprehensive
Outdoor Recreation Plans ("SCORP") and only upon such conditions as the Secretary deems
necessary to assure the substitution of other recreation properties of at least equal fair market value
and of reasonably equivalent usefulness and location. Parks or recreation areas acquired or
developed with LWCF assistance (in whole or part) are now referred to as "LWCF-assisted areas"
and/or "LWCF boundary areas.";

WHEREAS, the City has agreed previously and pursuant to Resolution #6278 to take
actions to purchase three (3) properties as replacement and has agreed to allow deed restriction for
perpetual outdoor recreation use;

WHEREAS, the City intends to take action to purchase an additional property as
replacement and agrees to allow deed restriction for perpetual outdoor recreation use (Greenway
corridor) as described in attached exhibit, at the time of purchase;
