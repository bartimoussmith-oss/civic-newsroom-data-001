TASK 2: Station Location Alternatives Evaluation
The Project Study Team will evaluate three potential station sites within the study area. Two sites include
the existing Union Pacific Railroad (UP) depot location (or vicinity) and the Reed Avenue Corridor (or
vicinity).  To identify the additional site beyond the identified two within the Cheyenne area, criteria for
site selection will be established during a site selection kickoff meeting.  The criteria will be used to score
the location alternatives.  The Project Study team will analyze the identified sites meeting most, or all, of
the established criteria and create an initial site matrix identifying potential additional sites.
The three sites will be vetted using the selection criteria at a ½-day workshop with the MPO and other
identified stakeholders. This workshop will provide a shortlist of no more than two sites that will be
evaluated in more detail as proposed below. The two finalist sites that will be vetted further in Task 5 for
selection of a single preferred alternative.

evaluate the size of site, potential transit interface, passenger rail rider accessibility, street access, parking,
fit within the community and neighborhood, planned uses for the adjacent areas, development,
redevelopment, and adaptive reuse opportunities, pedestrian and bicycle access; impacts on railroad
operations, proximity to other transportation modes and other relevant circumstances. The potential
station sites will be evaluated in detail through a second matrix process and will be ranked by the Project
