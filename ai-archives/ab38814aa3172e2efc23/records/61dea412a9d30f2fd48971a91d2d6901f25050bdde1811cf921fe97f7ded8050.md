Staff Report, Mark Escobedo, Traffic Engineer
August 19, 2014

Application for Critical Traffic and Parking Area

An application has been received requesting consideration by the Governing Body for establishment of a
Residential Critical Traffic and Parking Area consisting of the 200 through 500 blocks of East 22nd Street,
the 200 Block and the 400 through 500 Blocks of East 23rd Street, the 400 through 500 Blocks of East 24th
Street, the 2100 through 2400 Blocks of Evans Avenue and the 2200 through 2300 Blocks of Van Lennon
Avenue.  The zoning classification in the proposed area includes MR-1, MUR, MUB and PUD.

The application included a petition containing signatures of a majority of the residents of the dwellings
located within the proposed critical traffic and parking area, a map of the proposed area, and a listing of
all residents and/or property owners within the proposed area.

The area does not have sufficient off-street parking for the use and convenience of the residents in the
vicinity of their homes.  The area is detrimentally impacted by parking of vehicles by employees, patrons
and visitors of the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center, thereby placing an unacceptable hardship on the
residents of the area.

22nd Street between Warren Avenue and House Avenue

CURRENT REGULATION

Municipal Code 10.68.010 allows for designation of a critical traffic and parking area and the specific
traffic and parking restrictions within such critical traffic and parking area provided it is found that:

1.
The area is zoned as a residential district and is adjacent to an area zoned as a business,
manufacturing, or governmental district and is detrimentally impacted by parking of vehicles by
patrons of businesses or offices in the said business, manufacturing, or governmental district.
2.
The area does not have sufficient off-street parking for the use and convenience of the
residents.
3.
Vehicle congestion poses unacceptable hardships on the residents of the area if the present
parking is allowed to continue unregulated.
4.
The health and welfare of the residents of the area and the attractiveness and livability of the
neighborhood will be promoted.

The establishment of a critical traffic and parking area provides permit parking for residents and time
limited parking of two hours or less for service vehicles or any commuter vehicle or non residential
vehicles.  A parking permit however, shall not guarantee or reserve to the holder, a parking space within
the designated area.

CRITICAL TRAFFIC AND PARKING STUDY AREA

As part of the application process preliminary surveys of the traffic and parking conditions in the area
were conducted to evaluate the need for a critical traffic and parking area.  The on-street parking
occupied in the area from Warren Avenue to Maxwell Avenue between 21st Street and 25th Street was
surveyed and the number of cars recorded.  The field reconnaissance was conducted on July 25, July 31,
August 1, August 2, and August 5, 2014 during different hours of the day to get a representation of the
parking demand in the area throughout the week.  No attempt was made to identify parked vehicles as
related to specific establishments, i.e., hospital employees, visitors, residents, etc.  The field
reconnaissance revealed that parking was in significant demand in a majority of the blocks adjacent to
the hospital and into the surrounding residential neighborhood.
