MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

April 28, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Wyoming
Machinery Company, Cheyenne, Wyoming, to replace the Undercarriage, Track
Shoes, and related components on a Caterpillar D5K Dozer (City Block #5901)
______________________________________________
This is a sole source purchase through Wyoming Machinery Company, therefore no bids were
requested.
City Block #5901, a Caterpillar D5K Dozer, is in need of replacement of the Undercarriage, Track
Shoes, and related components.  Wyoming Machinery Company has provided a Sole Source
Letter, dated April 27, 2021, included for reference.  Per the provided letter, “Wyoming Machinery
Company is the authorized dealer serving Caterpillar customers in Wyoming with sales, parts and
service.”  Additionally, if the City of Cheyenne wished to install a different undercarriage,
“Machine modifications would have to be performed to run a conventional undercarriage with
added expense.”
Due to the City wishing to continue use of the Caterpillar System-one/Abrasion Undercarriage,
the City is requesting this repair be awarded to Wyoming Machinery Company.  This
undercarriage is a “Proprietary premium undercarriage and is manufactured and designed
specifically for Cat machines.” & the “System-one/Abrasion undercarriage is a Cat exclusive
undercarriage that can be only sourced from authorized Cat dealers.”
The replacement of the Undercarriage, Track Shoes, and related components is a complicated
repair.  Due to the environment that the D5K Dozer operates in (Landfill) the extent of damage
and repairs is not known, until the repairs commence.  Wyoming Machinery Company has
provided a potential cost range of $11,773.21 to $40,398.64.  An additional buffer of $5,000.00
may be required if during the repair damaged or broken bolts are encountered that were not
discoverable until the repair is underway.
The City of Cheyenne Fleet Maintenance Division & Sanitation Division is seeking funding
authorization in an amount not to exceed $45,398.64.

Funding:
(101-14-1415-40-21601 / Fleet Maintenance Fund / Maintenance)

$45,398.64
