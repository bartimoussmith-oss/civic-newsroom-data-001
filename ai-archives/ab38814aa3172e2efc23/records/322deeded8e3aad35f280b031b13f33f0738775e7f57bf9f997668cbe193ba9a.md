Board of County Commissioners

Report & Public Petitions
16. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a request for the vacation of the 40-foot
of the public right-of-way known as Astronaut Dr, as identified on
exhibit A.
24 - 588
Viewer's Report, Astronaut Dr, RP# 511
Draft Resolution, Astronaut Dr, RP# 511
Attachments:
Molly Bennett, Public Works, and Scott Larson, appointed viewer, reported to the
board that they recommended denying the petition because a parcel would be
landlocked.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Mike Urban, resident,
said he doubted the feasibility of the proposed road because telephone poles would
have to be relocated and property would have to taken from landowners. Cotton
Jones, agent for the applicant, said he did not see the area south and east of the
proposed vacation as a valid access point because the road will not be able to meet
county or city road requirements. He stated the vacation will benefit those immediately
adjacent to and within a subdivision development where multi-family housing is being
built. Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the hearing.
Commissioner Holmes asked County Attorney Mark Voss about the legality of
abandoning a public right of way and disposition when there are two landowners.
Voss referenced Title 34, noting that with a road vacation, the property underlying the
road goes to adjacent property owners. Voss said the caveat is in the event of a
reserve of right to the road where possible control could be noted on the deed or a
history of ownership could determine ownership.
Commissioner Malm questioned Larson about a 1995 vacation through a plat action.
Larson said he could not find McAllister Ln's partial vacation. Jones pointed out that
the exhibit has a book and page showing the vacation and the date.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Malm, seconded by Commissioner
Thompson, to accept the viewer's report and deny the vacation of the
alley.
Commissioner Malm said he could understand the petitioner's point of view based on
the maps. He said it was unfortunate that a former governing body had vacated two
other portions, and if that action had not been done, today's decision would have been
much easier. Commissioner Malm said he would hate to landlock a parcel.  He
acknowledged that even though a single owner currently owns both parcels,
landownership can change.
Chairman Lovett clarified that a yes vote on the motion is to deny the vacation.
The motion carried by the following vote:

24 - 577
Triple Dot Capital Investments, LLC, Board Approval
Vacation and Administrative Plat, Archer Estates, 11th
Filing
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of Triple Dot
Capital Investments, LLC, submitted an administrative plat application for Archer
Estates, 11th Filing. He said the Board of County Commissioners on Dec 3, 2024,
postponed the hearing until the entire board could be present.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the
administrative plat with the adoption of findings of fact a and b with no conditions.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Richard Wright and
Rod Guthrie, residents,  expressed concern about water. Hearing no further PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Malm, seconded by Commissioner
Thompson, to approve the administrative plat for Archer Estates, 11th
Filing, located in a portion of the NE1/4 of the SE1/4 of Sect 16, T14N,
R65W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY with no conditions and adopt
the findings of fact a and b of the staff report.
Commissioner Holmes said he opposes splitting larger tracts into 5-acre tracts and
considers it poor planning.
Commissioner Malm said he understands and appreciates concerns about water. He
said the county needs to follow science, and the county has relied on the AMEC
memo for an extended period of time. Commissioner Malm said the governing body
owes residents a clear set of rules and must abide by them.
The motion carried by the following vote:
