EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This Planned Unit Development (PUD) amendment application proposes changing the signage and buffering
requirements on a 1-block (2 acre) PUD adopted in 1997 and located in the Original City. This structure on
this property is a historic school which is currently being utilized by a church. The church approached the
City about placing new signage on the building and found the PUD limited signage to residential zone
signage allowances. The proper process to allow greater signage area is to modify the PUD. In investigating
the modification of the PUD, a requirement to provide a masonry wall was also found. The masonry wall
has not been built and the PUD amendment proposes removing this requirement and all screening of the
parking lot from the park to reflect the existing conditions.

The proposed PUD amendment would maintain residential-style signage standards along the sides of the
building facing the residential and park areas, but allow larger, more commercial-style signage on the side
of the building facing the highway. The signage allowance proposed would match the allowance of
properties on the other side of the highway. Larger signage has the potential to be more distracting to
motorists, but also has the potential to allow motorists to better identify their destination.

Staff recommends approval of the proposed PUD sign amendment with the following buffering amendment
to the PUD regulations shown in red underlined text:

Section F. BUFFERING AND LANDSCAPING:

Buffering and lLandscaping shall be provided as depicted on the PUD Site Plan Map, dated
March 134, 1997. A 6-foot high masonry screen fence will be erected on the east property
line, fronting Lincoln Park. A minimum four-foot tall vegetative screen shall be installed
between the church parking lot on the east side of the property and the adjacent City park.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.2.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, Staff recommends approval of this PUD amendment with the condition that the
buffering Section F be amended to require a vegetative screen on the east side of the property
to screen the parking lot from view from the park.
