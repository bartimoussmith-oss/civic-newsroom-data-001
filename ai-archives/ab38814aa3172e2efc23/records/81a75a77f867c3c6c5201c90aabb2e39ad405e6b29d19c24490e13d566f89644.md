AMENDMENT NO. TWO
TO KONE CONTRACT NO. 40118968/CITY CONTRACT NO. 5930
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND KONE, INC.
This agreement amends KONE Contract No. 40118968/City Contract No. 5930, dated
December 1, 2012, between the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and KONE Inc., in the following
manner:
OBJECT:
To add the Depot Plaza hydraulic elevator and the Fire Training Facility hydraulic
elevator to the master contract of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Each unit will be
billed at $198.00 per unit per month.
All other terms and conditions of the existing agreement shall remain in full force and effect.
This agreement will become effective upon execution by the parties following Governing
Body approval.
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING
