RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 16, 2026

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR – Patrick Collins; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Aldrich, Dr.
Emmons, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Moody, Mr. Segrave and Mr. White. Absent: Dr. Rinne
and Mr. Wolfe. Also present: Kylie Soden, City Clerk; John Brodie, City Attorney; Amber Ash,
Mayor’s Chief of Staff; Robin Lockman, City Treasurer and Chief Andrew Dykshorn, Deputy
Chief Don Wood and Deputy Chief of Administrative Services, Glenn Rodgers, Cheyenne Fire
Rescue.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Chris Thoms, President of Local #279, emphasized the Union’s priority of maintaining fair
market wages and noted that peer departments, including those along the Front Range, provide
relevant comparisons. Jason Lindsay, Vice President of Local #279, presented pension and salary
comparisons for firefighters in Wyoming, Colorado, and Montana. Discussion included specialty
pay, Cheyenne’s economic growth, and cost-of-living data sourced from bestplaces.net. Mr.
Thoms presented a counter proposal which included updates to Section 19 – Inclement Weather
and Section 21 – Classification of Employment and Basic Wages. Additional discussion included
recruitment efforts, attrition history, firefighter candidate pools, appropriate peer comparisons, the
importance of aligning recurring revenues with recurring expenses, as well as the City’s use of
reserves.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Segrave moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present. The following staff members were in attendance: Amber Ash, Mayor’s
Chief of Staff; John Brodie, City Attorney; Robin Lockman, City Treasurer; Kylie Soden, City
Clerk and Chief Andrew Dykshorn, Deputy Chief Don Wood and Deputy Chief of Administrative
Services, Glenn Rodgers, Cheyenne Fire Rescue. [City Clerk note: audio and video recording of
the special meeting was stopped; Executive Session convened at 6:41 p.m. in the City Council
office]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Collins reconvened the special meeting
at 7:06 p.m. and discussion continued regarding wage comparisons, specialty pay, and appropriate
peer groups, including how comparable communities structure and fund compensation. Mayor
Collins expressed the belief that the City’s firefighters are not underpaid. Discussion also included
cost-of-living comparisons among certain Wyoming communities, paramedic compensation and
training considerations, and ongoing challenges in reaching an agreement on wage comparisons
and the identification of appropriate peer groups. The City reiterated its standing offer related to
wage increases. The Union requested adjournment to review rank average amounts provided by
the City.

There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was
officially adjourned at 7:35 p.m.
