RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
June 14, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and
Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Mr. Collins.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mayor Kaysen announced that Agenda Item #19 contains a
clerical error reflecting the item had been referred to Public Services Committee; it should instead
be listed as the Finance Committee.  Upon request by Ms. Case, and pursuant to support received
by Mr. Spiker and Ms. Broyles, agenda item #19 was removed. Ms. Case requested removal of
Agenda Item #32(c) with Mayor Kaysen advising the item is a referral to Committee and was not
eligible to be removed from the Consent Agenda.  Upon request by Ms. Case, and pursuant to
support received by Mr. Spiker and Mr Pierson, agenda item #21 was removed from the Consent
Agenda.  Mr. Brown moved to approve remaining Consent Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Spiker.
Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on May 24, 2010.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
[CA]
Minutes from Vouchers Meeting of the Governing Body on June 7, 2010.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown and Mr. Valdez each declared a conflict of interest (business interest) due to
vouchers payable to Ameri-Tech HVAC Services and radio station KYOY respectively, and vacated
Council Chambers. Upon inquiry, City Treasurer Barb Dorr and City Engineer Doug Vetter
provided information available concerning a voucher payable to M&F Towing and Recovery LLC,
towing of vehicles in conjunction with the street overlay project.  Mr. Spiker moved to approve
payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Pierson. Upon inquiry, Dennis Pino, Solid
Waste Director, provided information concerning a voucher payable to Pioneer Printing for
residential recycling program informational brochures, and Vicki Nemecek, Assistant Public Works
Director, provided information affiliated with a voucher payable to the Wyoming Tribune Eagle for
5 Penny Sales Tax brochures.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
th
present in Council Chambers. Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown and Mr. Valdez

Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing for the purpose of receiving public comments on the
proposed budget for the City of Cheyenne and Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) for fiscal year 2011.
City Treasurer Barb Dorr provided a brief summary on the proposed budget explaining the total
appropriation is $119,528,474.00 of which $46,260,700.00 comprises the general fund with a total
City budget of $66,648,774.00.  The proposed BOPU budget amount is $52,879,700.00.  She
reported department heads met the goal of a 12% budget cut from the 2010 budget, and provided
other budget information relative to a suspended $1,000,000.00 BOPU payment and savings from
the employee volunteer early retirement offering.  Randy Hayes, BOPU Administration Manager,
provided a brief summary on the proposed BOPU budget explaining the water fund appropriation
is $43,163,700.00 and the sewer fund budget is $9,716,000.00.  He provided further information on
anticipated revenue amounts for each fund, potential grant assistance, and amounts to be funded out
of cash.  There being no further comments and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor
Kaysen closed the hearing.
“An Ordinance appropriating moneys for the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming for the various
purposes in conducting the municipal government of said city and fixing the amount of general and
special taxes as part of the revenue required to meet the said appropriation, all for the fiscal year
beginning July 1, 2010, and ending June 30, 2011.”  Ed Melko questioned whether proposed
sanitation fee increases were included in the ordinance with Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, responding
that the FY2011 budget includes a 1% increase and if there were any increases following budget
approval, a budget reappropriation could correct the amounts.  Reporting for Finance Committee,
Mr. Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Pierson. Dr. Rinne moved
to amend the ordinance to add funding for a child care assistant position for Parks and Recreation’s
Latch Key program in the amount of $41,877.72 to include benefits, and, in the affiliated revenue
line item, to add $42,000.00 to reflect funding to be received to cover the position expense with the
condition that if program revenue generated can not cover position expenses, funding will come out
of the Parks and Recreation budget, seconded by Mr. Spiker.  Rick Parish, Parks and Recreation
Director, explained there are two facilities that have the Latch Key and Summer Rec. Camp
programs (Pioneer Park and David R. Romero Youth Activity Center) and stated his belief that
enough revenue can be generated through registration fees to cover the position expense.  He
reviewed registration participation numbers, waiting lists for those interested in registering for the
programs, and program benefits to community youth.  Upon inquiry, Ms. Dorr and Mr. Parish
reviewed program registration fees. Discussion included program benefits and revenue generation,
and Parks and Recreation loan repayment schedule affiliated with construction of the Romero Youth
Activity Center.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present with the exception of Ms. Case voting “no”.  Upon inquiry regarding the possibility of filling
the position by former employees affected by the reduction in work force, Mr. Parish advised job
description criteria must be met with Mayor Kaysen requesting Mr. Parish communicate with the
Human Resources Director on the topic.  Comments followed on funding included in the budget for
the City’s annual membership dues to the Wyoming Association of Municipalities (WAM) and
membership services provided.  Ms. Ash moved to strike budget funding for the dues in the amount
of $40,485.00, seconded by Dr. Rinne.  Discussion followed and included support of WAM
membership, City’s representation on the WAM Board, and WAM’s budgetary assistance to its
membership by reducing next fiscal year’s dues amount by 10%.  Motion failed.  Voting “yes” – Ms.
Ash and Dr. Rinne.  Voting “no” – Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker, Mr.
Valdez and Mayor Kaysen.  Main motion as amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present with the exception of Ms. Ash voting “no”. (#3892)
