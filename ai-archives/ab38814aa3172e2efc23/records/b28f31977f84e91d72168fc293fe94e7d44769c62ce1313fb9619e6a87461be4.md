Februaiy 20, 2020
RE:
City Liquor License Renewals for 2020-2021
Attached is a listing ofthose applicants applying for renewal ofexisting City liquor licenses
(i.e.: retail, restaurant, limited retail, resort, bar & grill licenses, microbrewery, satellite distillery,
special malt beverage and winery permits) and Liquor Licensee Actions that occurred during this
past licensing year. Public hearings for the applications are scheduled during the February 24,2020
City Council meeting.
Due to the large volume of paperwork involved in renewing the liquor licenses, I didn’t
photocopy each application for your review. Instead, the attached listing provides the “basics” from
application information in a readable format. Ifyou would like to review an individual application
file, please contact me and I will make it available to you.
If you have any questions concerning applicant information or renewal requirements and
procedures, please do not hesitate to contact me at your convenience at Ext. 6346.
