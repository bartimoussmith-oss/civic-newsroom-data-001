CHEYENNE UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE(UDC), AMENDING
SECTION1.4.4AND5.1.4OF THE U DC TO CLARIFY DEFINITIONS FOR
ENTERTAINMENT AND RECREATION LAND USES; TOCREATEA
NEW ENTERTAINMENT AND RECREATION-OTHER LAND USE;
ADMINISTRATIVE, CONDITIONAL OR PRINCIPAL PERMITTED
USES."
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1.That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the text
amendments that are the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.4.1.d of
the UDC.
Section 2.That theintent of these text amendments is to clarify the types of activities and
venues or facilities that are included within theland use definitions of indoor and outdoor
entertainment and recreation activities; to establisha new“Entertainment, Amusement, Recreation
Services—Other”land use category and define the type of activities, venues and facilities included
therein; and to establish the zoning districts that thevarious entertainment and recreation land uses
would be permitted as Administrative, Conditional or Principal permitted uses.
Section 3.That the intent of these text amendments is to facilitate the reuse and
redevelopment of infill properties, to facilitate the establishment of entertainment and recreation
activities and venues that appeal to residents’modern cultural tastes; and to“legalize”existing
entertainment and recreation activities andvenues that are long-established in zoning districts that
currently may not allow them.
Section 4. That these text amendments are not regarding a specific parcel or a change in
boundary dueto a specific development proposal, changed circumstances for any specific property
or its surrounding area, or changed public policy with respect to long-range plans.
Section 5. That sub-sections 1.4.4.b and c and 5.1.4, Table 5-1 are amended to read as
