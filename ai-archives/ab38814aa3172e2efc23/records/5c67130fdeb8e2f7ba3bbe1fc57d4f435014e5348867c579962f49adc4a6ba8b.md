programs.  Also speaking in favor of the application were Jennifer Tollefson, Regional Director,

ORDINANCE – 2 READING – Amending the City of Cheyenne’s Solid Waste Fee
nd
Schedule.  Vicki Nemecek, Public Works Director, provided detailed information on the history
of the City’s solid waste and landfill operations, expansions and disposal practices; solid waste
rate study conducted in 2013, and rationale for the proposed rate increases.  Barbie Harrington;
Julie Gliehm, Wyoming Landlords Association; Edward Melko, Rick Fierro, Ram Trucking, and
Brad Harrington spoke in opposition of the proposed rate increases.  Topics discussed included:
Potential closures of some Wyoming landfills; waste disposal rates in rental units and difference
in rates compared to other Wyoming municipalities; sanitation collection practices; rate
structuring; effect of increases on individuals with limited budgets; purchase of the Belvoir
Ranch by the City for landfill purposes; landfill account; landfill cap and closure costs; landfill
DEQ permitting; concerns with re-introducing the ordinance after it previously failed, and
protecting people’s rights.  Mr. Luna moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Mr.
Roybal.  Discussion included:  DEQ requirements; waste management operations by the private
sector; landfill scenarios relative to privatization; consequences of not increasing rates, and
history of rate increases.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Fierro expressed his opinion on negotiable annual
contracts; operational inefficiencies; competition with private business and solid waste as a fuel
source.  Request was made that Ms. Nemecek provide information for the next Finance
Committee meeting regarding consequences of not increasing rates and affiliated landfill costs,
landlord rates, roll-off service expenses, and amount of revenue returned to the general fund.
Upon inquiry, Ms. Nemecek provided rate increase history since 2002.  Motion carried.  Voting
“yes” -- all members of the governing body present with the exception of Mr. Cook and Mr.
Luna voting “no”.
