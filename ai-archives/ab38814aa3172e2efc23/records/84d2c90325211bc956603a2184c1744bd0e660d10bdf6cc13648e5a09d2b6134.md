ORDINANCE NO. ____________________
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING THE OFFICIAL ZONING MAP OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE CHANGING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION
FROM CB COMMUNITY BUSINESS TO HI HEAVY INDUSTRIAL FOR
LAND LOCATED NORTH OF CAMPSTOOL ROAD, WEST OF
PROGRESS CIRCLE.”
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1. That the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have) applied for a
zoning map amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1,
Zoning Map Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).
Section 2. That on February 7, 2022, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission
conducted a public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this
ordinance, and recommended approval of said zone change.
Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change
that is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.
Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the
aforementioned application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property
described as Tracts 20-22, Rocky Mountain Industrial Park Addition Third Filing; and Lots 1-2,
Block 1, Rocky Mountain Industrial Park Fifth Filing, Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby changed to
HI Heavy Industrial.
Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as
described in Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.
Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional, such unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections,
paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases.
Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and
publication.

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from CB Community Business to HI Heavy

existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure. Plan Cheyenne also encourages infill development.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Industrial, and the zoning classification of
HI is one of the optimal zones for areas so designated.

UDC-22-00005
February 7, 2022
Policies within PlanCheyenne encourage industrial areas to be located outside of activity centers
and with ready access to regional transportation routes. This area supports these objectives.

Based on the policies and future land use designation of the subject property, HI zoning is
appropriate at this location.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Nearby property to the north and west are largely undeveloped and across a railroad track.
Property to the south is zoned HI and developed with a large-scale distribution facility. Property
to the east is zoned CB and contains both offices and warehousing-type facilities. The
development patterns in this area are compatible with the proposed HI zoning. While CB allows
more commercial-style development, the CB region to the east is surrounded by HI zoning. HI
zoned land is required to have buffers to areas zoned CB.

The comprehensive plan encourages industrial uses and zoning for all of the property between
the railroad and I-80 in this area of the community.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property is currently zoned CB Community Business. CB zoning requires building design
standards and is intended for regional-serving commercial and service uses. The proposed
zoning of HI has no design standards and allows more intense uses which are intended to be
buffered from the standard residential/commercial areas of the community. While the property
has the ability to develop according to the standards of either zone district, the subject property
is more likely to develop with industrial-style uses than with commercial service style uses. A HI
zone is required to provide a buffer to CB zoned areas, but the parcels in question should have
enough area to be able to provide the intended buffer.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The standards of Article 4 regarding block size and connections are not really applicable to
industrial areas; and due to the adjacent railroad and highway, north-south options for
connections are very limited. The area is already platted and no replatting actions are currently
proposed with this zone change. The existing subdivision pattern better matches HI zone
expectations than CB zone expectations.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The HI zone is intended for uses of very high intensity which should be able to be isolated and
buffered from other uses. This zoning is most appropriate in areas with access to major regional
transportation infrastructure and areas designated Industrial in the Comprehensive Plan.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

While HI zoning does not have design standards, other development standards are still
applicable. The property zoned HI should be able to provide the required landscape buffer to
the adjacent CB zoning, and certain HI uses are required to have buffers from streets. There
have been no official applications submitted in concert with this zone change application, and HI
zoning will allow a variety of more intense uses  should the potential development action that
prompted this zone change not be executed, the HI zone is appropriate at this location.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No recommendations were received through the review process.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 7.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 8.
