BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That various chapters and sections within Title 8, Health and Safety, of the
Cheyenne City  Code , relating to general provisions pertaining to fire protection and regulations,
and adoption of the International Fire Code, 2012 edition, and certain codes and practices
promulgated by the National Fire Protection Association are hereby amended, created, deleted
and re-created as set out in this ordinance.  Chapters and sections currently existing within Title
8, Health and Safety, not set out or clarified by an [Editing note:…] within this ordinance remain
unchanged.

Section 2.  That Chapter 8.12, International Fire Code Adopted, is amended as follows:

8.12.010
Purpose – Authority – General provisions.

The purpose of this chapter is to adopt the International Fire Code, 2006 2012 Edition, as an
enforceable regulation governing the safeguarding of life and property from fire and explosion
hazards arising from the storage, handling and use of hazardous substances, materials and
devices, and from conditions hazardous to life or property in the occupancy of buildings and
premises.

8.12.020
International Fire Code – Adopted.

A document, one copy of which is on file and open for inspection of the public in the office
of the city clerk, being marked and designated as:

International Fire Code, 2006 2012 Edition, as published by the International Code
Council, Inc., including Appendices A through G  A, B, C, D, E, F, G AND I;

is referred to, adopted and made a part hereof as if fully set out in this chapter.

8.12.030
Amendments.

A.  All persons who sell, or offer for sale, an unvented fuel-burning room heater within
the city shall attach the following written warning in a conspicuous place on all unvented fuel-
burning room heaters and their packaging, sold or offered for sale:

WARNING
The laws of the city of Cheyenne prohibit the installation or use of this appliance in any building,
whether new or as a replacement installation.

PENALTY
Maximum fine of seven hundred fifty dollars ($750.00) or six (6) months in jail, or both.

B.  For purposes of this section, "unvented fuel-burning room heater" means any heating
appliance, either wick, wickless or pot-burner type, which uses oil, gas or kerosene for fuel, is
either stationary or portable, and the products of combustion of which are not directly conducted
to the outside of the building via a chimney connector pipe.

C.  Exceptions. The warning prohibition in subsection A of this section does not apply to
the following:

1.  Listed or approved unvented overhead room heaters which meet the installation
requirements of Section 807(c) of the Uniform Mechanical Code, 1997 Edition; or

2.  Temporary use in buildings while under construction.
