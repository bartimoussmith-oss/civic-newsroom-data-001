for
Evers Boulevard Road Rehabilitation 35% Design Plan

THIS AGREEMENT made and entered into this _______day of July, 2014 by and between the
Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization, hereinafter referred to as the "MPO" and Ayres
Associates, hereinafter referred to as "Consultant."  The City of Cheyenne is acting as the agent of the

#6111.
RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by and between
the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the above mentioned project, in
accordance with the Scope of Work attached hereto as Exhibits "A1" and "A2", incorporated by
reference and expressly made a part hereof.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this scope of work will be completed in accordance with the project
schedule attached as Exhibit “B”.  Any extensions of the time limit set forth in the Scope of Work
shall be agreed upon in writing between the parties.

3.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
The Consultant shall be responsible for the professional quality, technical accuracy, timely
completion of drawings and other services rendered by the Consultant and its sub-consultants
and shall, without additional compensation, promptly remedy and correct any errors, omissions,
or other deficiencies, except to the extent that such errors, omissions or other deficiencies arise
from the digital data created by Cheyenne/Laramie County Cooperative Geographic
Information System (CLCCGIS)

4.
GIS MAPS AND DIGITAL DATA
If the consultant uses any maps, coverages, images, or other digital data created by the
CLCCGIS for the project specified in Exhibit A, the consultant agrees to return or destroy that
information once the project is complete. Consultant agrees not to reuse or sell the GIS maps or
data, which were provided as a professional courtesy and to minimize the cost of the project.

5.
GIS DATA LIMITATION AND DISCLAIMED LIABILITY
GIS data is collected primarily for use by the City of Cheyenne and Laramie County. Any
unauthorized use of the data is at the risk of the user. The CLCCGIS cannot vouch for any
unauthorized use.

EXHIBIT A1

EVERS BLVD. ROADWAY ANALYSIS

PROJECT OBJECTIVE:  The goal of this project is to provide a conceptual design for Evers
Boulevard.  The street is in generally poor condition and is in need of extensive rehabilitation.
This project provides an opportunity to enhance the multi-modal operations of the corridor as
well as provide a gateway to the well-established Western Hills neighborhood.

TASK 1 – PROJECT KICK-OFF AND RECONNAISSANCE

1.1
Scoping/Kick-Off Meeting.  Ayres and the City of Cheyenne will hold a scoping/kick-off
meeting to insure that the scope proposed by Ayres meets the goals of City staff.  Ayres
will prepare meeting minutes to document the discussion and distribute for comment and
feedback.

1.2
Observation of On-Site Conditions/Notes/Photos/Review Existing Documents.
Ayres will review all available existing documents for Evers Boulevard as well as conduct
a site visit to observe the existing conditions and take photos of the corridor.

1.3
Collection of Utility Information.  Ayres survey crews will obtain existing utility
information from Brittany Drive to Bishop Boulevard including water valve locations,
sanitary sewer manhole rim elevations, and sanitary sewer pipe inverts.

1.4
Project Administration.  This task includes the processing of all billings and completion
of tasks necessary for administration of the project.

TASK 2 – ANALYSIS

2.1
Speed Data Collection Intersection Count Set-Up/Take Down.  Speed information
will be collected using automatic traffic recorders placed at three locations between
Bishop Boulevard and Oakhurst Drive.  These counters will collect 48 hours of data and
be summarized to identify measures such as peak hours of traffic, ADT, and 85th

EVERS BLVD. STORM DRAINAGE ANALYSIS

PROJECT OBJECTIVE:  The goal of this analysis is to determine a storm sewer system design
that will remove storm water off of Evers Boulevard, west of I-25.  The accepted design should
increase the life of the pavement while providing some level of storm protection for the
surrounding area.   The level of protection provided is based on the design alternative chosen
for this project.  This scope includes an alternative analysis and final design on the selected
alternative.

The City’s drainage master plan calls for 100-year protection along Evers Boulevard.  The storm
sewer proposed is a 42-inch RCP at the upstream end that transitions ultimately to double 66-
inch RCP culverts under I-25.  For a variety of reasons including budgetary constraints, this
alternative may not be practical, therefore, alternative design concepts should to be developed.
