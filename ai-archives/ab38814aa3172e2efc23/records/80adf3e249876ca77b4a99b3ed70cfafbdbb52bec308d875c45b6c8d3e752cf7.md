RESOLUTION NO

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION CERTIFYING THAT THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE IS IN SUPPORT OF THE TOWN OF PINE BLUFFS
PROJECT TO BE SUBMITTED TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT BOARD
FOR THE DECEMBER 2012 COUNTYWIDE CONSENSUS BLOCK GRANT
FUNDING IN THE AMOUNT OF $26,000.”

Whereas, the rules of the State Loan and Investment Board require that application for funds
under this program be made by the Board of County Commissioners and agreed to by
municipalities representing at least 70% of the county’s incorporated population; and

Whereas, the City of Cheyenne represents greater than 70% of the incorporated population
of Laramie County.

THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING

That the City of Cheyenne supports the Town of Pine Bluffs Consensus grant request to the
State Loan and Investment Board for the project set forth in the FY 2013/2014 Countywide
Consensus Block Grant Application Form; and

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS 22nd DAY OF October, 2012.

                                                                  Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor

ATTEST:

Carol Inlekofer, City Clerk
