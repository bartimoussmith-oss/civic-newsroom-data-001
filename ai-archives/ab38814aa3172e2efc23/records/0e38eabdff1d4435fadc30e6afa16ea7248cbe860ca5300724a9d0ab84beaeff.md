RESOLUTION NO. _________
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE DOWNTOWN
DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
JULY 1, 2012, AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2013.”
WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority (“DDA”) was created pursuant to
Ordinance No. 2804, adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984; and
WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute § 15-9-218 provides that the governing body shall approved
the proposed budget of the DDA after administrative review by City staff; and
WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Section 2.76.050(C), provides that the DDA budget shall
be subject to alteration, modification and approval by the governing body in accordance with the
policies of the City; and
WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget for fiscal year ending June 30, 2013, of the
Downtown Development Authority, as set forth in the attached spreadsheet entitled “Cheyenne
Downtown Development Authority, Fiscal Year 2013 Budget,” in the total amount of $621,370.00,
is hereby approved.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS ______ DAY OF ______________, 2012.
__________________________________________
Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
(SEAL)
Attest:
____________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
