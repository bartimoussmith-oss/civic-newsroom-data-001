MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Patrick J. Collins, City of Cheyenne
Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Erin Gates, PE, Staff Engineer

DATE:
August 21, 2024

SUBJECT:
Drainage easement granted by Kenrich, LLC to the City of Cheyenne along the
Christensen Road corridor.

Please find attached documentation for a permanent drainage easement grated to the City of
Cheyenne from Kenrich, LLC. This drainage easement is necessary due to the encroachment of
drainage infrastructure associated with Christesen Road onto private property. An appraisal has
been completed for the 446 square feet of affected area. The diminution value of the parcel is
$424.00. Compensation for this easement will come from the 2017 Specific Purpose Option Tax
Fund, GL 030-20-2033-42-21301. Your approval of this drainage easement is respectfully
requested.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE
2101 O’Neil Avenue
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001

DRAINAGE E ASEMENT

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS that the undersigned, Kenrich, LLC, a
Wyoming Limited Liability Company, hereinafter referred to as "Grantor," in consideration of the
sum of Ten Dollars and other good and valuable consideration as provided in that certain Drainage
Easement Agreement of even date herewith, the receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, and the
further consideration of performance of the covenants and agreements by the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming ("City"), a Municipal Corporation, its successors and assigns, hereinafter referred to as
"Grantee," as hereinafter set out and expressed, hereby grants, bargains, sells and conveys to the
Grantee, a permanent drainage easement ("Easement") to construct, reconstruct, operate, maintain,
repair, remove and replace a drainageway and appurtenant features thereto, including ditches,
swales, catchments, riprap, catch basins, storm drains, storm sewers, culverts, detention facilities,
and retention facilities ("Facilities") on, over, through and across certain lands, situate in the
NE1/4NE1/4, Section 25, T.14N., R.66W., 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming, said easement
being more particularly described as follows:

An easement for a drainageway and appurtenant facilities located in the Northeast Quarter of
Section 25, Township 14 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming,
more particularly described as follows:

Beginning at a point on the East line of the Northeast Quarter of Section 25, from which the
Northeast Corner of said Section 25 bears N0°04’21”E, a distance of 727.22 feet; thence
N89°55’39”W, a distance of 50.00 feet to the True Point of Beginning, being on the West Right
of Way line of Christensen Road; thence S89°58’50”E, a distance of 17.86 feet; thence
S0°01’10”E, a distance of 25.00 feet; thence N89°58’50”E, a distance of 17.82 feet to a point on
the West Right of Way line of Christensen Road; N0°04’21”E, a distance of 25.00 feet to the
point of beginning.

Said Easement contains 0.01 acres more or less. See Exhibit A attached hereto.
TO HAVE AND HOLD unto said Grantee so long as the "Facilities" shall be maintained,
together with the right of ingress to and egress from said premises for the purpose of constructing,
reconstructing, operating, maintaining, repairing, removing, and replacing said Facilities, or the
removal thereof in whole or in part, at the will of the Grantee, it being the intention of the parties
hereto that the Grantor is hereby granting the uses herein specified without divesting the Grantor of
the right to enjoy the above-described premises, subject only to the rights of the Grantee to use the
same for the purposes herein expressed.

As further consideration of this agreement, the Grantor herein agrees as follows:

1.
The Grantor shall not construct or place any structure or building, on the Easement.
Grantor may otherwise use the Permanent Easement area as long as such use does not interfere with
Grantee's use.

2.
That Grantee will replace or rebuild any and all unusual or excessive damage to the
easement property which shall be occasioned by the operation and maintenance of said Facilities
under and through the above-described premises.
