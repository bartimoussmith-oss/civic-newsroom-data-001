SUBJECT:
Professional Services Agreement for Laramie County Roads Impact Fee Study
______________________________________________________________________________

A Request for Qualifications was put out on the PlanCheyenne website. We received one qualification
packet from TischlerBise and was selected base on qualifications.

The purpose of this Professional Services Agreement with TischlerBise is to develop an impact fee system
for Laramie County that: 1) equitably recovers the cost of County transportation/road impacts caused by
new development to include road signage and drainage; 2) is not an administrative burden to the County;
and 3) provides the timely and equitable financing of public services and improvements to mitigate and
prevent negative impacts from new development, residential, commercial or industrial.

This contract is not-to-exceed the amount of $97,840.

The Policy Committee is scheduled for this contract approval and WYDOT Local Government
office has approved the MPO to take this through City Council.

Please reach out to me directly if you have any questions regarding this contract.  I can be reached at 638-
4308 and cyaney@cheyennempo.org.

TASK 4: EVALUATE DIFFERENT ALLOCATION METHODOLOGIES
The purpose of this Task is to determine the methodology most appropriate for the road impact
fee. As noted previously, the three basic methodologies that can be applied in the calculation of
impact fees are the plan-based, incremental expansion, and cost-recovery approaches. Selection of
the particular methodology for each component of the impact fee category will depend on which
is most beneficial for the County. In some cases, we will prepare the impact fee using several
methodologies and will discuss the trade-offs with the County/MPO. This allows the utilization of
a combination of methodologies within one fee category. Policy discussions will then be held at
the staff level regarding the trade-offs associated with each allocation method prior to proceeding
to the next Task.

methodologies and relevant policy issues. One (1) meeting/presentation of the initial Road Impact
Fee Study findings with the Board of County Commissioners.

“Storyboard” presentation on fee options.
TASK 5:  DETERMINE NEED FOR “CREDITS” TO BE APPLIED AGAINST CAPITAL
COSTS
A consideration of “credits” is integral to the development of a legally valid impact fee
methodology. There are two types of “credits” that are included in the calculation of impact fees,
each with specific, distinct characteristics. The first is a credit due to possible double payment
situations. This could occur when a property owner will make future contributions toward the
capital costs of a public facility covered by an impact fee. The second is a credit toward the
payment of an impact fee for the required dedication of public sites and improvements provided
by the developer and for which the impact fee is imposed. Both types of credits will be considered
and addressed in the Road Impact Fee Study.
Deliverables:
Memoranda as appropriate. See Task 7.
TASK 6: CONDUCT FUNDING AND CASH FLOW ANALYSIS

gross revenues, but also the capital facility costs and any deficits. In this case, some consideration
should be given to anticipated funding sources. This calculation will allow the County/MPO to
better understand the various revenue sources possible and the amount that would be needed if the
impact fees were discounted.

funding strategy might need to be changed to have new growth pay its fair share of new
transportation infrastructure. This could also affect the total credits calculated in the previous Task.
