Hearing nor further comments the public hearing was closed and Chairman Caughey
called for a motion and discussion. Commissioner Stadel recused himself from any
discussion and vote.
Molly Bennett, Public Works Manager, said the developers have agreed to build the
roads to County Standards. The maintenance agreement will be between the
developer and residents to the south since Public Works is overburdened and  would
not assume the responsibility of any more road maintenance.Since traffic counts do
not meet the threshold requirement for paving these roads will not be required to be
paved.  Roads will be designed by an engineer, reviewed by Public Works,  and also
a third party engineer. A letter of compliance will be issued by Public Works after the
roads are constructed and before any building permits will be issued.
A motion was made by Member Watkins, seconded by Member Smith, to
approve The Badlands Preliminary Development Plan with four conditions
and adopt the findings of fact a of the staff report. Vote 3 ayes and 1
abstain.
