RE: Revised Plat Map for Arroyo Subdivision (dated 6/22/2017)
On June 22, 2017, the applicant submitted a revised plat map to the City for Arroyo Subdivision. This
revised map addresses some comments from reviewing agencies. The lots have been renumbered to
meet code requirements and the utility easements have been moved from the rear of the lots to the front
of the lots (adjacent to the street).
However, the revised map has the following deficiencies:
•
The proposed lots do not meet the LR zone standards
•
Dimensions for the drainage easement on Lot 1 are missing
•
It is the determination of the City Attorney’s Office that the new Note 10 provided with this plat is
unenforceable. Note 10 will be required to be removed prior to the City signing of the plat.
