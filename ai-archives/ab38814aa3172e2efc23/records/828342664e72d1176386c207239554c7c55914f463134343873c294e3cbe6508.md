RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

December 20, 2018

Due to the Christmas holiday schedule, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne
instead met in regular session on this date beginning at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present
were:  MAYOR – Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Rocky Case, Bryan Cook, Richard
Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Mike Luna, Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Dicky Shanor and Jeff White.
Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, and Sylvia Hackl, City Attorney.  The pledge of
allegiance was recited.

Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Upon request by Mr. Roybal, and pursuant to support received
by Mr. Cook and Mr. Johnson, Agenda Item #13 was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Upon
request by Mr. Case, and pursuant to support received by Mr. Cook and Dr. Rinne, Agenda Item
#20(a) was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Luna moved to approve remaining Consent
Agenda items, seconded by Mr. White.  Mr. Shanor advised he would be abstaining from voting
due to an employment-related conflict of interest on Agenda Item #18.  Motion carried.  Voting
“yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Shanor who did not vote.

chapters and sections within Title 15, Buildings and Construction, of the Cheyenne City Code

ORDINANCE – 2nd READING – Pursuant to Sections 1.1.6 and 2.4.1 of the Cheyenne
Unified Development Code (UDC), amending Section 6.2.7 of the UDC to remove the maximum
parking limitation and to allow applicants to request the Director to approve additional parking.
Mr. Roybal moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Upon inquiry, Susana
Montana, Planning and Development office, provided information on the proposed UDC
amendment, advising the change was brought forward by staff and the Planning Commission to
provide for administrative flexibility relating to UDC maximum parking requirements for
developments or redevelopments.  Discussion included raising the 10 percent threshold (contained
in Section 6.2.7) to 25 percent as discussed at the recent Public Services Committee (PSC) meeting.
Ms. Montana and Charles Bloom, Director of Planning and Department, indicated an amendment
to increase the threshold would be prepared for the next PSC meeting.  Motion to approve carried.
Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Johnson voting “no”.

RESOLUTION – Pursuant to Section 2.2.2 of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code
authorizing approval of a revised Cheyenne Business Parkway Corridor Plan as a Development
Master Plan for the Cheyenne Business Parkway Development (located on the north side of
Campstool Road between Burlington Trail and Christensen Road).  Mr. Roybal moved to adopt,
seconded by Mr. Case.  Mr. Roybal moved to amend by substitute Sheet L4.0 to Attachment A
which involves sketch adjustments to the right-of-way landscaping, seconded by Mr. Case.  Susana
Montana, Planning and Development office, explained the amendment is to correct an inadvertent
error involving inclusion of the south side of Campstool as part of the Parkway Corridor, and
clarified the amendment will delete the sketches of landscaping located within that right-of-way
due to the land not being located within the Parkway.  Discussion followed on Corridor greenway
path and sidewalk widths and locations; City maintenance and cost of same (ex: snow removal;
concrete repairs); maintenance responsibilities between departments and coordination of same;
greenway vs. public works maintenance budgets; management of City non-motorized
transportation systems, and involvement by the Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO)
relative to planning of future non-motorized transportation system growth in the vicinity.  During
discussion, Ms. Montana provided information in response to comments and noted the MPO has
been involved in looking at other future greenway/sidewalk connectivity in the area.  Motion to
amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr.
Johnson voting “no”.  Main motion as amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body with the exception of Mr. Johnson voting “no”. (#5964)

Under other business:

Jeff Dierksen, speaking on behalf of residents on Pinto Lane, discussed speed studies that
had been conducted and interpretation of speed measurement percentiles, and expressed his
continuing concern with high speeds by vehicle drivers traveling through the neighborhood
affecting safety.  During his comments Mr. Dierksen distributed information (on file with the
office of City Clerk) on speed data he had compiled through use of a personally owned speed gun.
Comments followed and included the following topics:  Speed gun calibration; neighborhood
safety awareness; governing body authority to determine speed limits set by state statute
provisions, and suggestions to Mr. Dierksen on seeking solution to his concerns through the
Wyoming Department of Transportation and State Transportation Commission.
