Zone Change Application/UDC-18-00146-01
3
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application proposes changing a land from MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis to CB
Community Business. This land totals about 1.03 acres. This land is currently vacant, and the applicant
proposes developing the property with an automobile service use (specifically, a car wash).

The Comprehensive Plan and the Unified Development Code support regional-serving commercial uses at
this site. PlanCheyenne specifically encourages MUB and CB zoning for the activity center located at
College and Pershing. MUB is the current zone and CB is the proposed zone.

MUB zoning is intended for areas with small blocks, a high degree of pedestrian amenities, and buildings
built near the street. CB zoning is intended for regional-serving commercial areas, automobile-oriented
development, and buildings setback from the street. The blocks in this area are larger and the current
commercial development in this area (between Cheyenne Street and Pershing) match the development
patterns anticipated by areas zoned CB. By zoning this parcel CB, the development on this site will better
match the existing development in the vicinity. The proposed CB zone is more suitable to this site than
the existing MUB zoning.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change.

ZONE CHANGE DATA:  In reviewing, providing comments, and making recommendations and decisions
on a proposed Zone Change Request, the Staff, Planning Commission and City Council shall use the
following criteria of Section 2.2.1.d:

1. PLANCHEYENNE - The application is in accordance with the
development policies and physical patterns identified in
the Comprehensive Plan.

PlanCheyenne is supportive of this zone change.

Principle 1.4 is supportive of this zone change, as it
encourages revitalization and infill development which
maximizes existing infrastructure. Policies within
PlanCheyenne encourage concentrating commercial uses
in activity centers, this property is in the Pershing/College
activity center and will keep commercial development
close to a major intersection.

The future land use map designates this area as Mixed-
use Commercial Emphasis. This mixed-use future land
use designation is intended to develop with primarily
commercial uses with secondary residential uses, with the
commercial uses ideally servicing the community. The current MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis
zone is the suggested zone for this future land use designation. This area is also designated as a
Mixed-use Commercial Activity Center. Mixed-use Commercial Activity Centers are intended to
develop with a mix of commercial uses with some housing. The CB and MUB zones are both
considered appropriate zones for Mixed-use Commercial Activity Centers.

With the intersection of College and Pershing anticipated to develop with commercial uses which
serve a larger area, the CB zone is an appropriate zone for this area.

2. SURROUNDING AREA - The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering
existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the
guidance of that plan for these areas.

The proposed zoning designation is compatible with nearby properties as it matches some of the
commercial zoning in the area and as nearby blocks include CB commercial-style development in the
MUB zone. Under modern standards, the MUB zone is intended to develop with commercial buildings
near the street and parking to the side or rear. CB zoning anticipates development with parking and
vehicle activities in the front with the building in the rear. The commercially developed properties to
the east, west, and south all match the anticipated development patterns of the current CB zone
better than the anticipated development patterns of the current MUB zone. While nearby properties
are zoned MUB, the development of this site in a CB style would be compatible with the surrounding
area.
