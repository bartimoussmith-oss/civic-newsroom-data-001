38. PUBLIC HEARING regarding the correction of the 2024 mill levies pursuant
to WS 39-13-102(g), (c)(iii), (j).
24 - 428
Mill Levy 2024, Revised
Attachments:
County Attorney Mark Voss explained that mill levies are set in August. He said that
after a review, it was determined that a clerical error caused the mill levy for a fire
district to be set too low. Voss said the district's voters approved repayment of a
bond, and without adjusting the mill levy, the bond payment would be insufficient. Voss
noted that the assessor has recalculated the amount that needs to be assessed and
to correct the tax roll.

PUBLIC COMMENT, Acting Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
