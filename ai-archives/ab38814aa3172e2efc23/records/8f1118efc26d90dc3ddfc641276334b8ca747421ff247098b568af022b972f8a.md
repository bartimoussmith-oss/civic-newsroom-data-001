MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

January 18, 2022
SUBJECT:
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and KONE, Inc., for the
repair of the South East Hydraulic Elevator located at the Jack Spiker Parking
Garage
______________________________________________
This is a single source purchase through KONE, Inc., therefore no bids were requested.
The City of Cheyenne is currently under Contract with KONE, Inc. to provide elevator
maintenance for the Jack Spiker Parking Garage, therefore the City is engaging KONE, Inc. to
provide the necessary repairs as they will have continued responsibility for the maintenance of the
elevator.
The primary scope of work for the repairs is to furnish and install a new hydraulic power unit for
the elevator.
The City of Cheyenne, Facilities Maintenance Division, has provided the following notes to
document the history of the elevator:
1. The Spiker Parking Garage structure was built in 2004 and the equipment is original to the
building.
2. The elevator is under contract with KONE and receives quarterly inspections.
3. All city elevators are under contract for maintenance with KONE except One (1) which is
under contract with Schindler.
4. The assumption is that the elevator has been abused by transients over the course of its life
and has become problematic recently.
The total request purchase amount is $42,665.00

Funding:
General Fund Reserves / $42,665.00

Description of Work: Power Unit and Oil
We propose to furnish and install the labor, materials, tools and supervision to perform the following work on the
South East Hydraulic Elevator located at the Jack Spiker Parking Garage in Cheyenne, WY.
We will furnish and install a new hydraulic power unit.  The motor and pump will be submersed under the oil
inside the tank in order to provide for sound isolation. A muffler, designed to reduce pulsation and noise, which
may be present in the flow of hydraulic oil, will be provided in the oil line at the top of the pump.
Control valves, including a safety check valve, an up direction valve with high pressure relief including up
leveling and soft stop features and a lowering valve including down leveling and manual leveling feature, will be
mounted in a compact unit assembly. A shut off valve, designed to shut off the flow of oil between the cylinder
and the Power Unit, will be provided in the oil line in the machine room. Automatic two-way leveling will be
provided to automatically stop and maintain the car approximately level with the landing, regardless of change
in load.
