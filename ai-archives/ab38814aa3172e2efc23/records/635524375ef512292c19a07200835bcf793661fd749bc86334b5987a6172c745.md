MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT
Between The
Federal Aviation Administration(FAA)
And

1.
This memorandum of agreement (MOA)provides guidance for emergency
response services totheFAA.This MOA is part of the FAA Facility Security
Management Program, whichis designed to protect personnel, property, and
sensitive information and mission capabilities.Thetype's of support service
being provided are fire protection, medical assistance, hazardous materials
assistance, annual building inspections, and fire extinguisher training.
2
This document is presented to theFAA and Cheyenne Fire Department for
ratification and subsequent implementation.
SectionI: References:
a.Current edition ofFAA Order 1600.69, FAA Facility Security
Management Program.
b.Current edition ofFAA Order1900.1, FAA Emergency Operations
SectionII: Purpose:
FAA facilities have an occupant emergency plan (OEP) which is tested at
least annually.The OEPprovides detailed information for evacuation
routes, individual emergency actions, and other individuals involved in the
OEPprocess.This MOA documents the special agreement between the
FAA and Cheyenne Fire Department to provide emergency response
service to assist personnel in dealing with the risks associated with natural,
human related, and or technological hazards.
Section III: Scope:
a.Emergency response will be provided in accordance with the
provisions established by both parties totheMOA.
b.The emergency responder will not be held liable for acting in good
faith when responding to a request for support.
c.FAA facilities identified for coverage with in this MOA:
