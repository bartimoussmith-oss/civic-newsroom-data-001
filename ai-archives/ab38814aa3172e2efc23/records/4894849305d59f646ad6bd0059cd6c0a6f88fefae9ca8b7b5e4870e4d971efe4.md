I. Description of Changes
The City of Cheyenne and Novo Benefits, LLC, entered into a professional services agreement
dated February 14, 2018 (City Contract no. 6786). The original agreement’s completion date is
December 31, 2023. The Parties both agree that an additional three (3) year term is necessary to
continue the successful long-term planning and stability in the City’s health coverage plan.
II. New Contract Items
The Parties agree that the fee for this service shall be no greater than: seventy-three thousand
seven hundred and thirty-three dollars ($73,733) beginning on January 1, 2023; seventy-five
thousand nine hundred and forty-five dollars ($75,945) beginning on January 1, 2024; and
seventy-eight thousand two hundred and twenty-three dollars ($78,223) beginning of January 1,
2026.  Novo benefits shall bill the City of Cheyenne monthly in 1/12th increments.
III. Schedule
The original contract is amended to extend the terms of the agreement from an additional three (3)
years period, beginning on January 1, 2024 through December 31, 2027. The Agreement shall
otherwise remain in full force and effect unless agreed to in writing by the parties hereto through
December 31, 2027.
