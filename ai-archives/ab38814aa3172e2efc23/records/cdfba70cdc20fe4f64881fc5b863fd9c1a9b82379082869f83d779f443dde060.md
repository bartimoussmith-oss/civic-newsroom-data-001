RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
May 29, 2012
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.
Absent: Mr. Collins and Mr. Pierson.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance, led by members of the Mayor’s
Youth Council, was recited.
Expression of Appreciation to Mayor’s Youth Council and State of the Youth report.  Mayor
Kaysen and Jan Spires, Mayor’s Youth Council Coordinator, commended members of the 2011-
2012 Mayor’s Youth Council and presented those members present with a certificate and gift of
appreciation. Those recognized were: Dean Jackson - Chair, Ian Dome - Vice-Chair, John Matsen -
Co-Secretary, Tyler Bedwell, Juliette Givhan, Joey Marchand, Robert McGarry, McKinzea Olsen,
Jacob Peterson and Gary Sayler.  Those members unable to attend were: Kadi Davis - Co-Secretary,
Addie Fleming and Margarita Medrano.  Mr. Jackson provided a State of the Youth report and
described various activities and issues the group was involved in during the past year.  He noted
members learned valuable lessons with regard to leadership and educational experiences that
included involvement with local government staff, tours of several City facilities and operations, and
participation in community efforts.  Mr. Jackson was presented with a plaque recognizing his service
during the past school year as chair of the Youth Council.  Mayor Kaysen expressed appreciation
to members for their time, commitment and dedication, and also to members’ families for their
support.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Upon request by Mr. Brown, and pursuant to support received by
Mr. Allen and Ms. Broyles,  agenda item #17 was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Brown
moved to approve remaining Consent Agenda items, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Motion carried.
Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.

Director, provided information on the amount of DDA budget reserves, explaining the level of
current reserves and carry-over amounts, and anticipated parking revenue.  During comments and

information received from the state and her calculations.  Inquiries included the following topics:
