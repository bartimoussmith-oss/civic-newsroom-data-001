Parties. This Maintenance Agreement ("MA") is made and entered into by and between
The Sweetgrass Owners Association ("SOA") and The Sweetgrass Commercial Owners
Association ("COA"), both c/o Sweetgrass Land Co. LLC., 1825 Campstool Road, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82007, herein collectively referred to as the Sweetgrass Associations (*SA"), and the
City of Cheyenne, a municipality organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming
Ownership Interests.The Cityis the owner of parcels of land consisting of approximately
4.33 acres combined, legally described as Tracts D, E, F, H, J, K, L, U, and T, Sweetgrass, 1st
Filing and 2nd Filing, City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming, and legally transferred to the
City pursuant to an instrument recorded in Book 2652, Page 1179, in the Office of the County
Clerk of Laramie County, Wyoming.See exhibit attached.
3.
Purpose. The purpose of this MA is to outline responsibilities for the maintenance of a ten
(10) foot concrete trail (Greater Cheyenne Greenway or “Greenway") and enhanced landscaping
and irrigation that will be constructed within the above-referenced tracts of land owned by the City
and for areas located within the public right-of-way located within Sweetgrass, 1s and 2nd Filings.
It is mutually understood and agreed that the Greenway, as well as enhanced landscaping and
irrigation, willbe constructed and installed at theexpense of the Sweetgrass Developers orentities
other than the City and shall be designed and constructed to City Greenway standards and other
specifications as found in the City of Cheyenne and Board of Public Utilities’ Construction
Specifications and Standard Drawings,2014.
ThisMA shall remain infull force and effect from the effective date of this MA until January 1,
2030, or unless sooner terminated in accordance with the provisions of Section7of this MA.This
MA may not be extended or renewed except pursuant to a written instrument executed by
authorized representative of the Parties.
4.
Definition. “Enhanced Landscaping” consists of any landscaping on the City owned
property other than seeded, dryland grass.

Remedies.In the event a party declares the other party in default hereto, said party
declaring default shall notify the defaulting party in writing and such defaulting
party shall be allowed a period of fifteen (15)days to cure said default. In the event
that the default remains uncorrected, the party not in default may elect to: (a)
terminate this MA and seek damages; (b) treat the MA as continuing and require
specific performance; or (c) avail itself of any other remedy at law or equity. In
addition, if the SAfails toperform in accordance with this MA, the City may elect
tomake good such deficiencies and seek reimbursement of its costs and expenses
from the SA.
