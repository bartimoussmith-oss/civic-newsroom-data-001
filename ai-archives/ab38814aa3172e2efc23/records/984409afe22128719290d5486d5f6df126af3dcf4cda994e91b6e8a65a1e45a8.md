PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Public Services Committee was held on Monday, May 19, 2025, in Council
Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Tom Segrave, Chairman; Dr. Michelle Aldrich: and Dr. Kathy
Emmons. Absent: Jeff White.
AGENDA ITEMS
9.
ORDINANCE —3 READING— Amending Sections 1.4.3, 1.4.4, and 5.7.2 of the Unified
Development Code to amend specific uses allowed as Home Occupations and add
clarifying definitions.
Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on 3I and final reading, seconded by Dr. Emmons.
Dr. Aldrich
moved to amend by substitute dated May 9, 2025, seconded by Dr. Emmons. Motion to amend
carried by unanimous voice vote.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend section 5.7.2cj)(5) to add after
“employing” to include “or apprenticing”, seconded by Dr. Emmons. Motion to amend, the
substitute, carried by unanimous voice vote. Main motion, as amended, carried by unanimous
voice vote.
Connor White, Planning & Development Department, provided a staff report and advised two
substitutes have been provided based on conversations from 2nd reading. Chairman Segrave and
committee member Aldrich commented on the item.
Ward III Councilman Mark Moody also
commented on the item.
12.
RESOLUTION
— Granting an exception to the ‘201 Agreement for the owner of the
property located south of High Plains Road and west of South Greeley Highway, between
Robert Avenue and Dayshia Lane. (POSTPONED FROM MAY 12, 2025)
Dr. Aldrich moved to adopt, seconded by Dr Emmons. Dr. Aldrich moved to amend by substitute
dated May 16, 2025, seconded by Dr. Emmons.
Motion to amend carried by unanimous voice
vote. Main motion, as amended, carried by unanimous voice vote.
Seth Lloyd,
Planning &
Development
Department, advised Charles
Bloom,
Planning &
Development Department Director, would be providing the staff report based on new information.
Director Bloom provided the staff report and advised there is a recommended substitute. Gunner
MaIm, agent for the applicant, provided additional information. The Chairman and all committee
members commented on the item.
19.
RESOLUTION
— Certifying compliance with Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402 for the City-
initiated annexation of various tracts of land completely surrounded by current city limits
situate in east Cheyenne generally located south of Dell Range Boulevard, east of Dry
Creek, west of Whitney Road, and north of Charles Street. (SPONSOR-- MR. SEGRAVE)
