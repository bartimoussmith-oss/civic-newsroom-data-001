BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:
Section 1. That the owner(s) of the property described herein have applied for a zoning map
amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment,
of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).
Section 2. That on June 7, 2021, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted a public
hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this ordinance, and recommended
approval of said zone change.
Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change that is the
subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.
Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2, Zoning
Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the aforementioned application
is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property described as Lot 1, Block 1,
Meadowlark Estates, 2nd Filing is hereby changed to CB Community Business; the zoning classification
of the real property described as Lots 2-7, Block 1 and all of Block 2, Meadowlark Estates, 2nd Filing is
hereby changed to LR Low-density Residential; and the zoning classification of the real property described
as all of Block 3, Meadowlark Estates, 2nd Filing is hereby changed to MR Medium-density Residential.
Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the Official
Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as described in
Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.
Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are severable. If
any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional, such
unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and
phrases.
Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and publication,
and upon the official filing of the affiliated Meadowlark Estates, 2nd Filing final plat map with the County
Clerk and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for Laramie County, Wyoming.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-use Commercial Emphasis and
Urban Transition Residential. The optimal City zoning for the future land use designation of
Mixed-use Commercial is MUB Mixed-use Business, but commercial or higher-intensity
residential zoning may also be appropriate. The optimal City zoning for the proposed future land
use designation of Urban Transition Residential is LR Low-density Residential or RR Rural
Residential, but slightly higher-intensity or lower-intensity residential zoning may also be
appropriate, especially on the edges of the area designated.

Based on the future land use designations, the proposed LR zone is the optimal zone for areas
proposed to be zoned LR. While MUB is the optimal zone for the area designated Mixed-use
Commercial, the CB zone is an acceptable alternative which would allow commercial
development at the intersection. The area proposed to be zoned MR serves as a transition
between the mixed-use area to the south and the lower-intensity residential areas to the north.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

To the north and east, the existing zoning is County LR and designated Urban Transition
Residential by the comprehensive plan. The area of this re-zoning adjacent to this land is
proposed to be zoned LR, which is the City zone most analogous to that County zone and
considered the most appropriate City zone for areas and designated Urban Transition
Residential.

The portion of this zone change proposed to be zoned MR is adjacent to areas zoned MU and NB
in the County and designated Mixed-use Commercial, across a major transportation route. The
MR zone is a lower-intensity zone than these adjacent zones, and would not have an undue
influence on the surrounding parcels to the south.

The area proposed to be zoned CB is at the intersection of two major transportation routes. The
property to the south is zoned MU in the County and designated Mixed-use Commercial. The
property to the west is zoned AG in the City and also designated Mixed-use Commercial. The AG
zone is a holding zone for future development  the area zoned AG adjacent to the area
proposed to be zoned CB is anticipated to be zoned commercial use in the near future (likely CB
zoning). While CB zoning is generally considered a higher-intensity zone than MU, the
commercial expectation of the future land use designation and the location at a major
intersection means CB zoning is compatible with nearby property.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current County LR zone allows lower-density, urban residential style uses, and the County
MU zone allows a variety of commercial and residential uses  but in order to develop with lots
any smaller than 5 acres, connections to a central sanitary sewer system is required. There is no

sanitary sewer system available in this area of the County outside of the Board of Public Utilities.
The BOPU generally only services land within City limits (special exceptions are required to
service unannexed land). The requirement for sanitary sewer or minimum lot sizes for septic
system provision limits development options.

As the property is proposed to be annexed, City zoning is required. The proposed City LR zone is
the zone most similar to the current County LR zone, and provides a similar range of
development options, but by being part of the City, BOPU sewer would be anticipated to be
extended to the parcel, allowing functional LR-style development.

The MR zone allows a broader mix of residential uses than the current County LR zone. The CB
zone allows a range of commercial uses, but limits residential development.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The property is constrained by topography, adjacent major transportation routes, and previous
development. The ability to create blocks and connections that meet the standards of Article 4
would be limited, no matter what City zoning was proposed. Also, due to the existing patterns,
the ability of this land to meet optimal intersection spacing or block arrangement may be
limited. The property is able to meet other standards of Article 4, but specific application of
these standards to this land is reviewed and analyzed with the platting action. A concurrent
Preliminary Plat is proposed with this zone change application.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The CB zone is intended for retail, employment, service and civic uses that may serve a number
of residential neighborhoods. Typically, areas zoned CB are expected to be more automobile-
oriented and serve the region. It is typically expected in areas designated as activity centers or
Community Business by the comprehensive plan. The future land use map designates the
intersection of Christensen and US 30 as an activity center, and the area around the intersection
of Whitney and US 30 as Community Business. Commercial development at this intersection is
anticipated by the properties mixed-use future land use designation, and any commercial
development at this intersection would likely have a regional service area (including the
surrounding rural and low-to-medium density residential developments).

The LR zone is intended for a mix of lower density residential development with access to public
infrastructure and city services. LR zoning is anticipated in areas designated Urban Transition
Residential and should be less than a mile from commercial development. This area is
designated Urban Transition Residential and is practically adjacent to current and future
commercial development.

The MR zone is intended for a mix of residential densities and dwelling types with access to
public infrastructure and city services. MR zoning is anticipated in areas designated Urban

The applicant is seeking the proposed zone change in concert with an annexation. The proposed
HI and LI zoning does not have zone-specific design standards beyond setback requirements,
and the parcels proposed to be zoned LI and HI are large enough to allow development to meet
the setback requirements. The proposed AG zoning is the zone most similar to the existing
zoning on the property and on surrounding County properties. Development on the property
zoned AG would require a future rezoning.

A specific development pattern is proposed on the land to be zoned, and the zoning amendment
is dependent on alignment with the associated plat. Should the specific plat proposed for the
land not be recorded after the zone change is approved, a separate zoning action should be
pursued. Should the plat be recorded, but subsequently not developed, the areas zoned LR and
MR could be replatted to accommodate different development patterns which would still be
appropriate at this location.

There is no specific development proposed for the parcel to be zoned CB, and the CB-zoned
parcel would have multiple development options.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

No reviewing agencies provided recommendations regarding the proposed zone change.
