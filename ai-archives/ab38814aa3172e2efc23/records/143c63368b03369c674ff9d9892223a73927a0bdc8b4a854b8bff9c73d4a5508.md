13. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a new retail liquor license submitted by
Riedel Ventures, LLC, dba Bit-O-Wyo Ranch Wedding & Event
Venue, located at 470 Happy Jack Rd.
25 - 484
Riedel Ventures, LLC, Retail Liquor License
Application
Attachments:
Laramie County Clerk Chief Deputy Dale Davis said the application is in order. He
noted the applicant modified the application to show that the land is leased. Davis
explained the modified application and lease have been forwarded to the WY Liquor
Division for review.
Tania Riedel, agent for Riedel Ventures, LLC, described their plans for the property.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.
John Ysebeart and Jason Caughey, residents, and Justin Hesser, counsel for Kelly
Zimmerman and Lisa Losee, expressed concerns about the liquor license. They cited:
access, traffic, intoxicated people, offset from the property line, emergency services,
need to go through the county planning process, building and fire codes, residential
access only easement, well-being of the residents, and adverse effect on the

Riedel explained they have had talks with Planning. She said the liquor license is

to the next meeting. County Attorney Mark Voss proposed postponing the hearing until
the liquor division has reviewed the modified application and lease.
