RESOLUTION NO. 2015- ________

Entitled:
“A
RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING
THE
SUBMITTAL
OF
AN
APPLICATION TO THE WYOMING STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT BOARD
FOR A LOAN FROM THE WYOMING CLEAN WATER STATE REVOLVING
FUND FOR AN AMOUNT NOT TO EXCEED $9.9 MILLION FOR
CONSTRUCTION COSTS FOR THE PHASE ONE SOUTHERN SEWER
INTERCEPTOR MAIN PROJECT AND AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR, THE
CITY CLERK AND THE DIRECTOR OF THE BOARD OF PUBLIC
UTILITIES TO EXECUTE THE APPLICATION AND ALL RELATED LOAN
DOCUMENTS.”

WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute 15-7-401 provides for a Board of
Public Utilities (BOARD) to manage, operate, maintain and
control a municipal waterworks, a sanitary sewer system and
sewage disposal plants; and

WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Section 2.68.010, established
such a BOARD to have exclusive authority to manage, operate,
maintain and control the Municipal Waterworks, Sanitary Sewer
System and Sewage Disposal Plants of the City and to make all
rules and regulations necessary for the safe, economical and
efficient operation and maintenance of the above facilities; and

WHEREAS, the 2003 and 2013 Water and Wastewater Master
Plans recommend development of the Phase One Southern Sewer
Interceptor Main to collect and transport sewage collected from
the southern portion of Cheyenne and deliver the sewage flows to
the Crow Creek Water Reclamation Facility to increase flows into
the treatment plant to increase the supply necessary for the
expansion of the recycle water treatment works; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne (CITY)
and the BOARD recognize the 2003 and 2013 Water and Wastewater
Master Plans as essential planning tools and the necessity for
the Phase One Southern Sewer Interceptor Main Project (PROJECT);
and

WHEREAS, the PROJECT includes, but is not limited to, the
construction
and
installation
of,
and
materials
for,
approximately 4.25 miles of sanitary sewer mains with diameters
of 10 inch to 24 inch, with manholes, diversion structures,
sewage lift stations, back-up power, easements and all other
necessary appurtenances to make the PROJECT functional; and

WHEREAS, the construction costs associated with the PROJECT
are estimated to be $9.9 million; and

WHEREAS, the CITY and the BOARD are committed to repaying
the loan from revenues collected from charges for wastewater
services with the adoption of:
City Ordinance No. 3975 on June 10, 2013 and BOPU
Resolution No. 2013-04 on May 1, 2013; and
City Ordinance No. 4025 on June 9, 2014 and BOPU Resolution
No. 2014-05 on May 5, 2014; and
City Ordinance No. 4073 on June 8, 2015 and BOPU Resolution
No. 2015-04 on May 4, 2015; and

WHEREAS, the CITY and the BOARD recognize that the annual
principal and interest payment on the proposed loan will be
about $635,056.57, assuming a loan term of 20 years at a 2.5%
interest rate; and

WHEREAS, the CITY and the BOARD recognize that the Project

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED THAT THE GOVERNING BODY OF

1)
The submittal of an application to the Wyoming State
Loan and Investment Board for a loan from the Wyoming Clean
Water State Revolving Fund for an amount not to exceed
$9,900,000 for PROJECT construction and related costs of the
Phase One Southern Sewer Interceptor Main Project for the City
of Cheyenne; and

2) That the Mayor, the City Clerk and the Director of the
Board
of
Public
Utilities
are
hereby

as
the
authorized representatives of the City of Cheyenne to act on
behalf of the City of Cheyenne in all matters related to this
application and related documents and are authorized to execute
the application and all related loan documents including, but
not limited to, the loan agreement, the assignment and pledge of
revenues, the promissory note and any amendments to those
