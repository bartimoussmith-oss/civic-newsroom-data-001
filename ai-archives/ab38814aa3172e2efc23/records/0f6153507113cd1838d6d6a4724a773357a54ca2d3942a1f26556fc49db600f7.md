SCHEDULEA-2
(As Amended Pursuant to Ordinance No.
Decreasing state speed limits in certain zones.
In accordance with Section 10.32.030 and when properly posted and when no special hazard
exists, the following maximum speeds are permitted:
(a)
Twenty-five miles per hour: No vehicle shall be driven at a rate of speed in excess
of twenty-five miles per hour upon all local streets, as shown on the Urban System
and Functional Classification Map, and not otherwise posted.
(b)
Twenty miles per hour:
(1)
Through parks.
(2)
Central business district, bound by Sixteenth Street on the south, Twenty-
Second Street on the north, Evans Avenue on the east, and Bent Avenue on
the west.
(3)
In school zones. Extreme care shall be exercised by every driver in
designated school zones.
(C)
Ten miles per hour: No vehicle shall be driven at a rate of speed in excess of ten
public.
