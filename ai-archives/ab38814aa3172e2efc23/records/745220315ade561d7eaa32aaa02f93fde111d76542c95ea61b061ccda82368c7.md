FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
Due to the Presidents Day holiday, a meeting of the Finance Committee was instead held on
Wednesday, Febniary 19, 2025, in Committee Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at
12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Pete Laybourn. Chairman; Dr. Kathy Emmons: and Ken Esquibel.
Absent: Mark Moody and Scott Roybal.
AGENDA ITEMS
13.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
b)
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and TDSi
- The
Design Studio, inc., for professional architecture and engineering services for
various projects at the historic Cheyenne Depot and Depot Plaza. (2021 Specific
Purpose Option Tax Fund)
Mr. Esquibel moved to approve in an amount not to exceed S167,539.O0, seconded by Dr.
Emmons. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Jason Sanchez, Community Recreation & Events Director, provided a staff report and Christy
McCarthy, Cheyenne Depot Museum Executive Director, provided additional information. Randy
Byers and Will Byers, TDSi, were available for questions. Chairman Laybourn and committee
member Esquibel commented on the item.
14.
CONTRACT MODIFICATIONS:
b)
Contract Modification #3 to Contract #753 1 between the City of Cheyenne and
Waste Management for Recycling Services through April 15, 2026. (Solid Waste
Fund)
Mr. Esquibel moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $250,000.00, seconded by Dr.
Emmons
Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Craig LaVoy, Public Works Deputy Director, provided a staff report. Chairman Layboum and
committee member Esquibel commented on the item.
c)
Contract Modification #2 to Contract #7447 between the City of Cheyenne and
IIDR Engineering, Inc., for the 5h Street Bridge over Crow Creek Design Project.
(20 19-2022 Optional 1% Sales Tax Fund)
Mr. Esquibel moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $224,793.00, seconded by Dr.
Emmons. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
