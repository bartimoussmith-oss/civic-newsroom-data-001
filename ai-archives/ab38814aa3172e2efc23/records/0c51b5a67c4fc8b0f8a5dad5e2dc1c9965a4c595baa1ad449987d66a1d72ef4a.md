The Cheyenne Regional Airport was founded on September8,1920duringa time when aviation
was still in its adolescence.Originally created as aresult of the needs of the Transcontinental
Airmail Route, the Airport has seen significant changes over the course of its92yearhistory.
The airmail routes of the1920s gradually paved the way for passenger service to and from
Cheyenne.As the home base for United Airlines in the1930's andearly1940s, Cheyenne was a
world hub for aviation, featuring over24daily arrivals and departures.The wantsof elite
passengers were trumped by needs of the US Military during World War ll when Cheyenne was
the home to a large, B-17modification center.Post war, the Airport incurred a period of
decline as aviation technology changed and planes received enhanced performance, nolonger
requiring the need to have a staging area prior to crossing the Rocky Mountains.Since that
time, the Cheyenne Regional Airport has continued to provide the residents of Southeastern
Wyoming and Northern Colorado with varying amountsof airservice.Additionally, inan effort
to foster the self sustaining financial goals mandated by FAA grant assurances, the Airport has
