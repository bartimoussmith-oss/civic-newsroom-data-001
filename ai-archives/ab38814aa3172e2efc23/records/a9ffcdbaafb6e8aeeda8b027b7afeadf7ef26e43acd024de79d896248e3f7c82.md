PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Public Services Committec was held on Monday, February 2, 2026, in City
Council Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as
follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Dr. Kathy Emmons, Chairwoman; Dr. Michelle Aldrich; Pete
Laybourn, and Dr. Mark Rinne.
AGENDA ITEMS
6.
ORDINANCE —3 READING
— Creating Chapter
1.28, Administrative Inspection
Warrants, establishing authority for their issuance, and amending the Municipal Code of
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, to authorize certain city officials, as specified, to apply
for administrative inspection warrants. (POSTPONED FROM JANUARY 26, 2026)
Dr. Rinne moved to approve on 3rd and Final Reading, seconded by Dr. Aldrich.
Dr. Rinne moved to amend by substitute dated January 30, 2026, seconded by Dr. Aldrich.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend to add Section 4 to read “An incident report will be provided to the
City Council following each executed Administrative Inspection Warrant,” seconded by Dr.
Rinne. Motion to amend carried by unanimous voice vote.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend section 1.28.020 (C) (3) to add the sentence “The warrant shall not
be issued unless the certified mailing has been received as indicated by the postal service or seven
(7) business days have elapsed,” seconded by Dr. Rinne. Motion to amend failed with Mr.
Laybourn and Dr. Rinne voting “NO” and Dr. Aldrich voting “YES.”
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend section 1.28.020 (B) to remove item #1 in its entirety. Motion to
amend died due to the lack of a second.
Dr. Aldrich moved amend section 1.28.020 to remove item (B) in its entirety. Motion to amend
died due to the lack of a second.
Dr. Rinne moved to amend section 1.28.020 (B) item #1 to read “Reasonable legislative or
administrative standards for conducting a routine, periodic, or area inspection of commercial
properties are satisfied with respect to the particular commercial property,” seconded by Dr.
Aldrich.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend the amendment to add to the section at the end of the
sentence “or subject to an open building permit.” seconded by Dr. Rinne. Motion to amend the
amendment carried by unanimous voice vote.
Motion to amend section 1.28.020 (B) item #1
carried by unanimous voice vote.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend section 1.28.020 (B) to add to item #2 to include before the “OR” the
following “that causes an immediate risk to public health and/or safety,” seconded by Dr. Rinne.
