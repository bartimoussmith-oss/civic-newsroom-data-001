```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

April 6, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-19-21 for furnishing Concealable Body Armor, for the
City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department
______________________________________________
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The two (2) bids were
received from:

1. Galls LLC.  Lexington, KY.
2. Skaggs Public Safety.  Centennial, CO.

The bids were reviewed by Lew Simpson (CPD Equipment Quartermaster).  His recommendation
is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Galls LLC.

Bid E-19-21 was solicited to provide Concealable Body Armor for a three (3) year period.  Galls
LLC provide the lowest bid price for all three (3) years, and did not provide any price increases
across the three (3) year term of this Bid Award.

Due to the nature of how the Concealable Body Armor is issued to officers and new hires, exact
quantities needed for a three (3) year period are not precisely known at this time.  Based on
calculated estimates, the Cheyenne Police Department is requesting approval for $79,610.00 for a
three (3) year period (Estimated Quantities Required, included for reference).

If unforeseen changes arise, additional purchases may be required.  Any additional unforeseen
purchases will be made in accordance with the individual line item pricing, submitted by Galls
LLC, in accordance with the Bidding Documents.

Funding:
(024-15-1530-41-85409 / Bulletproof Vest Program Grant)
(001-15-1511-41-21330 / Small Grant Match)
(001-15-1511-41-32115 / Clothing)

$79,610.00 / Federal Grants & General Fund
```
