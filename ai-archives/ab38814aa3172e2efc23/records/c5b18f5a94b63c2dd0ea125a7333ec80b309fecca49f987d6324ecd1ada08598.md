PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
Due to the Presidents Day holiday, a meeting of the Public Services Committee was instead held
on Tuesday, February 17, 2026, in City Council Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at
12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Tom Segrave, Acting Chairman; Dr. Michelle Aldrich; Pete
Laybourn, and Dr. Mark Rinne (via Zoom). Absent: Dr. Kathy Emmons, Chairwoman.
AGENDA ITEMS
12.
ORDINANCE
READING
— Creating Chapter 8,72, Private Displays on Public
Property, to prohibit the unauthorized use of public property for specified private displays.
(POSTPONED FROM FEBRUARY 9,2026)
Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on 2’ reading, seconded by Mr. Laybourn. Dr. Aldrich moved to
amend by substitute dated February 13, 2026, seconded by Mr. Laybourn.
Motion to amend
carried by unanimous voice vote.
Main motion, as amended, carried with Dr. Aldrich and Mr.
Laybourn voting “YES” and Dr. Rinne voting “NO.”
David Hopkinson, Deputy City Attorney, provided a staff report and provided an overview of a
proposed substitute.
Dr. Aldrich, sponsor of the item, provided background for the proposed
ordinance and provided information on the proposed substitute. Committee members Rinne and
Laybourn commented on the item.
