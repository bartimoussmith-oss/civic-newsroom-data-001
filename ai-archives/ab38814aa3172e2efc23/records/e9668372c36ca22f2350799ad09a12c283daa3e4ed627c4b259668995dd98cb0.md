BUDGET SUMMARY
Operating Revenues - are predicted for this budget cycle to be $4,289,508. **lt must be noted that
Operating Revenues will vary based on Airport Operations as the amount of fuel, de-icing fluid, line
service tasks, etc., vary by the number of aircraft received from private, military and commercial.
It should also be noted that Operating Revenues are anticipated to be down $270,650 now that Great
Lakes Airlines has ceased operations.
Non-Operating Revenue
— are projected at $12,174,916. This number reflects the majority of the costs
associated with the New Terminal Project and are carried over in this budget cycle to complete the
terminal. It should be noted that the majority of this funding is derived from 5th g 6th Penny Taxes,
Federal, State and Wyoming Business Council grants.
Compensation & Benefits
— are projected at $1,958, 230. The Airport currently employs 17 FTE’s and 3
PTE’s.
AIRPORT & FBO Supplies
— are projected at $1,652,656. It should be noted the largest expense in this
category is the bulk purchase of aviation fuels for resale.
Contract Service
— are projected at $884,793. These costs are primarily attributed to Airport legal
counsel, insurance, printing, telecommunications, web based applications, accounting services and
supplemental lawn and snow removal services.
Capital Outlays
— are projected at $7,748,338. It should be noted that the majority of these expenses
are offset with agency grants.
Debt Service — are projected at $355,288. This debt service pertains to the Federal Government building
the Airport built for the various agencies and are recouping the debt payments through existing leases
according to the agreements.
BUDGET PROFIT/LOSS
— Anticipated $598,129 will be returned to the Airport Reserve account for
consideration in the 2019/20 Operating Budget.
CONCLUSION
Since the Cheyenne Regional Airport Joint Powers Board was formed in 1980, the Airport has not been
an ongoing line item in either the City or County operating budgets. The Airport is completely self
sustaining and covers all expenses associated with the operations and maintenance of the facility.
For additional reference, we have attached City Ordinance 2426 which delineates the JPB agreement
between the City, County and Airport Board.
