CONTRACT EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT
THIS AGREEMENT, made and entered into this — day of August, 2019, by and between
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of
Wyoming, acting by and through its political subdivision, the Cheyenne Police Department,
hereinafter referred to as the “Department,” and Yolanda Ronnau, hereinafter referred to as
“Ronnau.”
W1TNESSETH:
WHEREAS, the Department is a law enforcement agency serving the City of Cheyenne, and
is a department thereof; and
WHEREAS, the Department has received a grant of funds from the Wyoming Division of
Victim Services for the purpose of providing crime victim assistance services; and
WHEREAS, in order to provide crime victim assistance services, the Department desires to
retain the services of a “Part Time Victim Assistance Advocate;” and
WHEREAS, because funding a Part Time Victim Assistance Advocate position is dependent
on the receipt of a grant, the Department wishes to create a contract employee rather than permanent
employee position; and
WHEREAS, Ronnau has training and experience in the field of assisting crime victims; and
WHEREAS, Ronnau desires to provide services to the Department under the terms and
conditions hereinafter contained.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises and mutual covenants herein, the
parties agree as follows:
1.
The term of this Agreement shall commence July 30, 2019, and terminate June 30,
2020, unless sooner terminated pursuant to Section 9 below or if grant funding is no longer available.
2.
Ronnau will be considered a contract employee of the Department. She will act as
Part Time Victim Assistance Advocate and assist the Department.
3.
Ronnau shall at all times coordinate her activities hereunder with the Department and
shall work under the direction of the Department.
4.
The Department agrees to pay Ronnau the sum of Two Thousand Nine Hundred
Three Dollars and Twenty-Six Cents ($1,200.00) per month. The total amount over the term of this
agreement will not, in any event, exceed the sum of Ten Thousand Eight Hundred Dollars and Zero
Cents ($10,800.00).
5.
The City of Cheyenne will withhold appropriate federal income and social security
taxes and remit them to the federal government.
6.
Tn the event that the governing body authorizes a bonus to be paid to city employees,
Ronnau would be eligible to receive a bonus similar to other city employees. Such bonus payment
shall first be made out of grant funds, if available, then out of Department funds, if grant funds are
not available.
7.
In the event that Ronnau is eligible to receive a pay increase based on her Annual
Performance Evaluation, such pay increases shall first be made out of grant funds, if available, then
out of Department funds if grant funds are not available.
