Background

Legislature of the State of Wyoming on March12,2018.House Bill0014,2018
Wyoming Session Laws Chapter83, relates to municipalities and modified the
extraterritorial jurisdiction for municipalities.This is the basis for the City'sreview of
County subdivisions within one-mileof City limits.
This law became effective January1,2019.County plat s submitted prior to January1,
2019have been processed in accordance with prior statutes and require approval by
the City Council.Thefull textof the legislation and amendments(House Bill0014) is
attached.
The legislation removed the requirement that plat s located within one-mile of city limits,
and in areas regulated by a county comprehensive plan, bejointly approved by the
cities and counties.It was replaced with a city'sability to"comment”onhow the
proposed plat effected the city's infrastructure or other development plans.l dent if i ed in

comments by the county commissioners, and a requirement for the county
commissioners to respond toitemsof disagreement.Specific review criteria and for
county approval was also incorporated in the legislation.The legislation does not
distinguish between preliminary, final or administrative plat.
