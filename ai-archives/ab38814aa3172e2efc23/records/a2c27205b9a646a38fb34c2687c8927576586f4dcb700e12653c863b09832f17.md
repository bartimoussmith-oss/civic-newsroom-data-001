```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

March 24, 2023
SUBJECT:
Re-Appropriation to be considered for approval

On April 10, 2023, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee to
re-appropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2023 City of Cheyenne Budget per W.S. § 16-4-112
and 16-4-113.  Below is more detailed information regarding this re-appropriation request.

GENERAL FUND – RESERVES REQUEST
The following General Fund reserve requests are included for your consideration:
Contract public defender fees.  The Mayor is requesting additional General Fund reserves in the
amount of $90,400 for contract public defender fees.  This will cover the shortage in this line item
for our contract public defenders.  The current budget is $110,400; and to date, we have spent a
total of $157,900 in contract public defender fees.  In addition to this amount, there are five more
months of expenses to pay (February and March will be $10,200 per month, and then for April,
May, and June, the cost will drop down to $7,500 per month).
Salary and benefits for new City Public Defender.  The City Attorney has received approval
from Mayor Collins to hire a new Public Defender who will assist with current caseloads and
reduce the costs of contracting public defender services. This position will be paid out of the
Mayor’s budget.  The City intends to employ a local attorney to fill this position; once hired, this
individual is scheduled to begin on May 1, 2023.  Therefore, the City Attorney is requesting
$18,955 from General Fund reserves to cover salary and benefits for May and June.
Salary and benefits for new Senior Accountant.  I am requesting $33,173 from General Fund
reserves to cover salary and benefits of a new Senior Accountant position for the remaining three
months of FY 2023.  The Mayor approved this position and I am pleased to announce the hiring
of Sarah Crone as the City’s new Senior Accountant.  She is a Certified Public Accountant and is
currently employed by MHP, LLP.  She serves as the in-charge supervisor of the City’s annual
audit and has considerable experience in governmental accounting.  She will start on April 3, 2023.
```

```text
Sarah will assist the City Treasurer’s Department with the OpenGov software conversion, as this
will be a massive undertaking.  As you will recall, the City Treasurer’s Department will implement
five modules, including Financials, Budgeting and Planning, Procurement, Cartegraph Work
Management and Financial Transparency, over a two-year period starting this fiscal year.
Additionally, in December 2022, Congress approved the “Financial Data Transparency Act”,
which is now an unfunded mandate that requires state and local governments to face time-
consuming and costly new requirements for sharing financial information with the public by
putting their financial information in a standardized, machine-readable, and searchable format.
This is going to be a significant undertaking for the City of Cheyenne and current staffing levels
in the City Treasurer’s Department do not support these additional responsibilities.
Salary and benefits for new DDA Administrator and Marketing Manager.  The Governing
Body approved a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) between the City and the Downtown
Development Authority (DDA) on March 13, 2023, and as a result, the Planning and Development
Director is requesting to hire an Administrator and Marketing Manager for the DDA.  The
Administrator is projected to start approximately on April 15, 2023, and the Marketing Manager
on June 1, 2023.  The City has already paid the DDA’s $250,000 annual allocation for Fiscal
Year 2023.  Therefore, per the stipulations of the MOU, the DDA’s account will reimburse the
City for these payroll costs in the amount up to $41,432.
Arts Cheyenne MOU.  Included in the General Fund reserves request is $50,000 as a result of the
new Arts Cheyenne MOU approved by the Governing Body on February 27, 2023.
```
