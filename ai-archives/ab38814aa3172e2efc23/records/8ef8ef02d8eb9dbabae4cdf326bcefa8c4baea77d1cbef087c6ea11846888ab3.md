RE: Greenway adjacent to the 409 W 4th Street Zone Change Application
At the City Council meeting on October 23, 2107, a member of City Council asked what the proposed
location of the Greenway was in this area of the City, and if the applicant or owner of the zone change
request was aware of the proposed route.
The attached map was created with input from the Greenway coordinator showing the possible Greenway
alignments in this neighborhood. This map was presented to the applicant, and to the Public Services
Committee at their regular meeting on November 7, 2017.
None of the proposed Greenway alternatives will use (or encroach upon) the applicant’s land that is part
of the proposed zone change. The Greenway will likely be used by pedestrians and cyclists which would
pass by the west side of the proposed zone change property.
