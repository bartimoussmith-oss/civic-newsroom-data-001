MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU), by and between the Carbon Asset Network
(CAN), located at 113 East 20th Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the City of Cheyenne
(City), a municipal corporation existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, located at 2101
O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, is entered into and becomes effective on the date of
the last signature affixed hereto.

WHEREAS, CAN was formed in 2020 as a resource for land managers through the help
of nonprofits that support soil health, in order to empower the land managers to be a better
steward of their land.

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1.
Purpose.  The purpose of this MOU is to set forth the terms and conditions agreed
to by the parties regarding the partnership to empower the managers to be a better steward of
their land.

2.
Term.  This MOU is effective until terminated by written agreement between the
parties.

3.
Process.  CAN recruits land managers into CAN.
a.
CAN and land manager will enter into a separate Memorandum of
Understanding which will require that the land manager will receive funds in exchange
for working their acreage in a more regenerative and sustainable manner for twelve (12)
months.
b.
CAN’s agronomist will conduct extensive soil testing of the acreage and
produce a report creating benchmarks for soil organic carbon and other soil elements.
This sampling is “boots on the ground” sampling and not based on satellite imagery or
