By Alessandra  a

ORDINANCE NO.

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 2.32, FIRE DEPARTMENT,
SECTION 2.32.020, DEPARTMENT PERSONNEL, AND CHAPTER 2.84,
MISCELLANEOUS BOARDS AND COMMISSIONS, SECTION 2.84.050,
INTERNATIONAL FIRE CODE BOARD OF APPEALS, OF TITLE 2,
ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL, OF THE CHEYENNE CITY
CODE PERTAINING TO THE FIRE AND RESCUE DEPARTMENT.”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1. That Chapter 2.32, Fire Department, Section 2.32.020, Department personnel, and
Chapter 2.84, Miscellaneous Boards and Commissions, Section 2.84.050, International Fire Code
board of appeals, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the Cheyenne City Code pertaining
to the Fire and Rescue Department is amended as set out in this ordinance.  Chapters and  sections
currently existing within Title 2, Administration and Personnel, not set out within this ordinance
remain unchanged.

2.32.20
Department personnel.

A. The fire department consists of the chief of the department, division chiefs, battalion
chiefs, lieutenants, inspectors, engineers and firefighters as determined by the mayor and city
council.

2.84.050
International Fire Code board of appeals.

Provisions relating to the powers and duties of the International Fire Code board of appeals are
located in the International Fire Code, 2015 2018 Edition, Appendix A, adopted by the city in
Chapter 8.12 of the city code. A copy of the board's rules and regulations are on file in the office
of the city clerk.

Section 2. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication
but not sooner than January 1, 2019,

FIRST READING:

SECOND READING:

THIRD AND FINAL READING:

MAYOR
(SEAL)
ATTEST:

CAROL INTLEKOFER, CITY CLERK

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle
