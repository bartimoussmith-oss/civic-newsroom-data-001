______________________________________________
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Spieth Gymnastics, for the purchase
of gymnastics equipment for the new Cheyenne Gymnastics Facility.

The total amount of the purchase is $200,518.00.

This is a single source purchase, therefore no bids were requested.  In conducting research for this
purchase, it was determined that purchasing directly from the manufacturer was the best
procurement option.  3rd party Spieth resellers are able to source some of the required gymnastics
equipment, but pursuing purchases from 3rd parties could result in needing to purchase from up to
five (5) different resellers.  In order to ensure that all of the required equipment is procured in an
expeditious and efficient manner, it is recommended to purchase directly from the manufacturer.

Information from Shannon Mitchell (City of Cheyenne - Gymnastics):

In the US there are 2 brands to choose from for competitive gymnastics equipment (vault table,
uneven bars, balance beams, and spring floor), Spieth America and American Athletic Inc (AAI).
It is my recommendation to conduct this purchase directly from Speith Gymnastics because it is
generally less expensive (compared to AAI) and Spieth is the brand of equipment used by other
competitions in our region.  There are other mat manufacturers that make some of the mats we
need, but to host competitions at the State Championship level or higher, you need to provide
equipment/mats from the same manufacturer.
