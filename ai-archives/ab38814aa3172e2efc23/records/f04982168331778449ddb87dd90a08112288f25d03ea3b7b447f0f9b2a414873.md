RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
May 10, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS: Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne, Mr.
Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and
Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
RECOGNITION OF FIRE AND RESCUE CHIEF GUY E. CAMERON ON HIS
RETIREMENT.  City Clerk Carol Intlekofer read a Mayoral proclamation into the record
recognizing Chief Cameron for his years of service to the residents of Cheyenne and proclaiming
May 19, 2010 Guy E. Cameron Day.  Mayor Kaysen presented Chief Cameron with the
proclamation, a plaque of appreciation for his 32 years of dedicated service to the City and a 100
year anniversary Fire Department commemorative medallion.  Appreciation was expressed by
Mayor Kaysen and members of the governing body on the Chief’s achievements benefitting the
department and community.  Chief Cameron commented on his positive employment experience
with the City, and expressed appreciation to past and present mayors and members of the governing
body for their trust and confidence in him and also to co-staff with whom he has worked over the
years.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Pierson moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded
by Mr. Spiker.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on April 26, 2010.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.

carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.
Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown returned to Council Chambers.

“An Ordinance amending Title 17, Zoning, Chapter 17.08, Zoning Districts Established and
Section 17.08.040 Cheyenne City map of the Cheyenne City Code, by changing the zoning
classification from PUD Planned Unit Development District to MR-2 Medium Density Residential-
Developing District for Lots 1 through 14 inclusive of Dry Creek Twin Homes Subdivision,
Cheyenne, Wyoming, to be known as a portion of Woodhaven Subdivision (located west of College
Drive, between the Dry Creek Channel and McCann Avenue).”   Due to any perception of conflict
involving distant family ties with the developer, Mr. Spiker declared a conflict of interest and
vacated Council Chambers.  Reporting for Public Services Committee, Ms. Ash moved to approve
on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Collins.  Ms. Ash moved to amend by substitute,
seconded by Mr. Collins.  It was noted, pursuant to discussion and amendments recommended by
the Public Services Committee, the substitute includes the addition of the word “conditional” in the
ordinance entitlement and in Section 1.; inserts a new section which reads as follows: “That the zone
change was conditioned by the Governing Body to allow only those uses by right and uses by
administrative approval.  No other uses shall be allowed by Board Approval.  All other requirements
for the MR-2 Medium Density Residential-Developing District will remain as outlined in Section
17.48 of the Cheyenne City Code.”, and includes the following staff recommendation: “A condition
be placed on the approval of the zone change that the applicant record a “Notice of Conditional
Zone” to provide public notification of specific restriction to prospective buyers”.  It was further
noted the intent of the changes is to protect the neighborhood by providing that any development
of apartment buildings/complexes would require the consent of the governing body.  Motion to
amend carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.
Main motion as amended by substitute carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Spiker returned to Council
Chambers. (#3890)

services within the existing building or #2. Church and related services within the existing building,

ordinance.  Motion to postpone carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
