RESOLUTION NO. _______________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE FILING OF AN APPLICATION
WITH THE FEDERAL TRANSIT ADMINISTRATION FOR FEDERAL
TRANSPORTATION ASSISTANCE AUTHORIZED BY TITLE 49, UNITED
STATES CODE, SECTION 5339.”

WHEREAS, the Federal Transportation Administrator has been delegated authority to
award Federal financial assistance for transportation projects pursuant to 49 U.S.C. §5339; and

WHEREAS, the grant or cooperative agreement for Federal financial assistance will
impose certain obligations upon the Applicant, and may require the Applicant to provide the
local share of the project cost; and

WHEREAS, the Applicant has or will provide all annual certifications and assurances
required for the project to the Federal Transit Administration; and

WHEREAS, the Applicant will apply to the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) for
$1,040,000.00 of FTA Section 5339 funds for the purchase and renovation of a transit operations
facility.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

1.  That the Mayor is authorized to execute and file an application for Federal financial
assistance in the amount of $1,040,000.00 on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne
Transit Program, with the Federal Transit Administration, as authorized by 49 U.S.C. §5339.

2.  That the Mayor is authorized to execute and file with the application the annual
certifications and assurances and other documents the Federal Transit Administration requires
before awarding a Federal assistance grant or cooperative agreement.

3.  That the mayor is authorized to execute grant and cooperative agreements with the
Federal Transit Administration on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit
Program.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Patrick Collins, Mayor, is designated as the
authorized representative of the City of Cheyenne to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all
matters relating to this grant application.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _______ DAY OF ______________
2021.

___________________________________________

Patrick Collins, Mayor
