BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from LI Light Industrial to CB Community Business

UDC-24-121

August 5, 2024
Mixed-use Employment designation is a transition from Community Business to Industrial in this
area, and a mix of lighter industrial and commercial uses are appropriate. According to the
comprehensive plan the optimal City zones for the subject property’s future land use
designation is Mixed-use Employment; however the Mixed-use Employment zone is set up for a
campus-like atmosphere and this area of the community does not have a campus-like industrial
park. Secondary zones that would be appropriate to the uses anticipated by the comprehensive
plan would include both CB and LI zoning.

Based on the policies and future land use map, CB zoning is appropriate at this location.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

The proposed CB zone would match commercial zoning in the area. A node of CB zoning is less
than 1000’ away to the west. The surrounding zoning is LI, which has similar setbacks, and
height allowances. The LI zone allows for limited commercial uses and prohibits the most
intense industrial uses. The CB zone would be compatible with surrounding zoning.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The LI zone allows more industrial-style uses and fewer commercial-style uses. The CB zone
allows more commercial-style uses, but is much more restrictive on industry. These zones have
some overlap, and some of the existing uses in the area could exist in either zone. These zones
are both already in the area.

The applicant is requesting a change to CB because they would like to add a dog washing facility
to their proposed development. All other uses for the development are permitted within the
existing LI zone.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The zone change is occurring in a pre-existing neighborhood. No changes to the street network
are proposed. Accesses will not be changed with the zone change. Infrastructure (streets and
utilities) exist in the area.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the

The CB zone is intended for larger commercial uses serving the community as a whole and is
applicable in activity centers or other areas where regional-serving commercial uses are
anticipated. Internal circulation between adjacent sites is encouraged. The CB zone applies
commercial design standards.

This property is near to other CB zoned parcels, and is in an area with ready access to regional
transportation routes. The proposed facility is a regional-serving commercial facility. The
proposal meets the intent and applicability of the CB zone.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The CB zone allows a variety of Commercial Uses and some Limited Industrial uses. Should the
proposed development not move forward, another commercial development could use the
property.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No major comments received in regard to the proposed zone.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
