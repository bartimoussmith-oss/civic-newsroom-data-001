ORDINANCE NO. __________
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE DELETING AND RECREATING SECTION 2.04.142(C),
CONSENT AGENDA, OF TITLE 2, ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL,
OF CHAPTER 2.04, MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL, OF THE CHEYENNE
CITY CODE, PERTAINING TO THE MEETING AGENDA FORMAT FOR
REGULAR MEETINGS OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE.”
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1.  That Section 2.04.142(C), entitled Consent agenda, of Title 2, Administration and
Personnel, of Chapter 2.04, Mayor and City Council, of the Cheyenne City Code, pertaining to the
meeting agenda format for regular meetings of the governing body of the City of Cheyenne, is hereby
deleted and recreated as follows:
2.04.142
Consent agenda.
C.
ALL ITEMS LISTED ON A CONSENT AGENDA ARE INTENDED TO BE
CONSIDERED BY THE GOVERNING BODY AS A SINGLE ITEM.  APPROVAL OF THE
CONSENT AGENDA REQUIRES A MOTION AND A SECOND.  THE MOTION SHALL BE
NON-DEBATABLE, UNLESS A MEMBER OF THE GOVERNING BODY REQUESTS A
PROCEDURAL CLARIFICATION. PASSAGE OF THE MOTION REQUIRES AN
AFFIRMATIVE MAJORITY VOTE OF GOVERNING BODY MEMBERS PRESENT AT THE
MEETING AND WILL HAVE THE EFFECT OF MOVING TO APPROVE ALL ITEMS
DESIGNATED AS BEING INCLUDED ON THE CONSENT AGENDA.   ANY CITY
COUNCIL MEMBER PRESENT AT THE MEETING SHALL HAVE ONE OPPORTUNITY
TO AMEND THE MOTION BY SEEKING THE REMOVAL OF ANY ELIGIBLE ITEM(S)
FROM THE CONSENT AGENDA.  THE MOTION TO AMEND REQUIRES A SECOND, IS
NON-DEBATABLE AND PASSES UPON AN AFFIRMATIVE MAJORITY VOTE.  IF AN
ELIGIBLE ITEM(S) IS REMOVED FROM THE CONSENT AGENDA, THE ITEM(S) SO
REMOVED WILL BE ACTED UPON IN THE NORMAL AGENDA SEQUENCE AS FOR
NON-CONSENT AGENDA ITEMS.  ITEMS ON THE CONSENT AGENDA FOR REFERRAL
TO COMMITTEE ARE EXEMPT FROM REMOVAL.
1.
APPROVAL OF A MOTION TO APPROVE THE CONSENT AGENDA
SHALL BE FULLY EQUIVALENT TO APPROVAL, ADOPTION OR ENACTMENT OF
EACH CONSENT AGENDA ITEM AS IF EACH ITEM HAD BEEN ACTED UPON
INDIVIDUALLY.
Section 2.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and
publication.
