RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
September 28, 2009
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning at
6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS:
Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Dr. Rinne and Mr. Spiker.  Absent: Ms. Case, Mr.
Pierson and Mr. Valdez.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City
Attorney.
Mayor Kaysen welcomed and introduced the following delegates representing Cheyenne’s
Sister City Voghera, Italy in attendance at the meeting: Dr. Professor Daniele Salerno, Dott. Carolo
Gianotti, Carlo Riccardi and Anna Riccardi.  He advised Voghera has been a Sister City since 2002.
Dr. Salerno reviewed the City of Voghera’s history, its location within Italy, its culture, terrain, notable
cuisine, and agricultural, industrial and transportation crossroad similarities shared between Voghera
and Cheyenne.  As a western similarity, he noted that Mr. Riccardi is the owner of the Cowboy Guest
Ranch.  In recognition of the Sister City relationship, Dr. Salerno presented plaques depicting the
insignia of Voghera to Mayor Kaysen and Council President Patrick Collins.  In return, on behalf of
the City of Cheyenne and its residents Mayor Kaysen presented the delegation with a rustic framed
rope depicting the word Cheyenne for placement at Voghera’s City building.
City of Cheyenne employees were recognized by Mayor Kaysen for receiving the following
awards at the 2009 Wyoming Planning Association Conference:  Cheyenne area Metropolitan Planning
Organization, Urban Planning Project of the Year for the Cheyenne Wayfinding Plan, project
coordinator Sreyoshi Chakraborty; Jeanie Anderson, New Planning Professional of the Year in the
State of Wyoming, and Western Planner Magazine’s Sheldon D. Gerber Merit Award for Excellence
recognizing the City of Cheyenne and Project Coordinator Matt Ashby for excellence in environmental
planning in the Project of the Year Category for the Belvoir Ranch Master Plan.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two other
members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its normal
sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Collins moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by Mr.
Brown.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present.

“AN ORDINANCE AMENDING VARIOUS SECTIONS OF TITLE 5, BUSINESS
LICENSES AND REGULATIONS, CHAPTER 5.68, BODY ART ESTABLISHMENTS AND
OPERATIONS, OF THE CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE.”  Reporting for Finance
Committee, Ms. Ash moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Upon inquiry,
Gary Hickman, Cheyenne-Laramie County Health Dept., Division of Environmental Health, advised
the ordinance amends health reporting requirements for body art establishments and provides for
licensing of body artists.  He explained National Environmental Health guidelines were reviewed and
business operators and artists support the proposed amendments and requirements, and indicated
Environmental Health will help guide the establishments in providing a bloodborne pathogen written
exposure control plan, which is also a requirement through OSHA.  Upon further inquiry, Mr. Hickman
explained how Environmental Health will permit current licensees and body artists at renewal and
City Clerk Carol Intlekofer clarified current operators will have until Jan. 1, 2010 to meet requirements
of the ordinance through the City Clerk’s office; new applicants for a license or permit would have to
meet the requirements of the ordinance upon its approval and publication.  Upon inquiry regarding
whether the term “pre-sterilized” contained in the definition of “body piercing” had the same meaning
as “sterilized”, Mr. Hickman clarified individuals can bring in their own studs which have to be
sterilized by the operator, or they can purchase pre-sterilized studs from the establishment.  Dr. Rinne
moved to delete “pre-“ from the term “pre-sterilized” in the three areas referenced within the
definition, seconded by Mr. Collins.  In response to query concerning whether the ordinance changes
conflict with OSHA requirements, Mr. Hickman advised he did not believe there were any conflicts
as OSHA requirements involve bloodborne pathogens, but explained he would need to research this
to know for sure.  Following comments, motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Main motion as amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.

CHEYENNE CITY CODE, BY CHANGING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION FROM LR-2 LOW
DENSITY RESIDENTIAL-DEVELOPING DISTRICT TO MR-1 MEDIUM DENSITY
RESIDENTIAL-ESTABLISHED DISTRICT FOR LOTS 1- 8, BLOCK ONE; LOTS 1- 8, BLOCK
TWO; LOTS 1- 4, BLOCK THREE; GOODWATER ADDITION, CHEYENNE, WYOMING
(LOCATED EAST OF AND ADJACENT TO VAN BUREN AVENUE, SOUTH OF DELL RANGE
BOULEVARD).” Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr. Brown moved to approve on second
reading, seconded by Mr. Collins.  Upon inquiry, Lisa Pafford, Asst. Director of Building and
Development, advised the applicant is requesting a zone change from LR-2 to MR-1 to allow for a
smaller lot size enabling more units on the property.  She advised MR-1 does allow for multi-family
units but would require Board of Adjustment approval if apartment units were proposed.  Comments
followed on concerns with zoning from LR-2 to MR-1 and potential of apartment buildings being built
rather than town homes or duplexes, resulting in increased density.  John Steil, Steil Surveying
Services, agent for the applicant, clarified the applicant is proposing to build town homes, and advised
the change in zoning classification will make the project more feasible, and noted MR-1 zoning is in
the nearby area.  He also noted there will be no problem with height requirements. He explained a MR-
1 zone will  allow smaller town homes to be built than what the LR-2 zone requires, allowing the
building of more town homes. Upon inquiry, Ms. Pafford provided information on density and parking
requirements relative to when a replatting action may be involved, minimum setback requirements and
distances between buildings.  Comments followed on property layout for the town homes, setbacks,
garages facing the street-side of the property, and PlanCheyenne relative to product materials utilized.
Mr. Steil advised the buildings will be staggered, not one long row of buildings.  Motion carried.
Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present with the exception of Ms. Broyles voting
“no”.
