Scope of Work Included in
Cheyenne Depot Renovation
Cheyenne, Wuoming
Scope of Project:
1. Renovation of the existing First Floor Public Restrooms located between Depot Lobby
and Accomplice Brewing. The scope of work includes replacement of restroom fixtures
(toilets, urinals, lavatories, countertops, and accessories), update of finishes, and
adjustments to provide proper handicap access and to address vandalism.
2. Repair and/or replacement of the exterior Depot Lobby doors (5 locations total). This
work will repair existing doors, and in two locations will replace the doors and sidelight
configuration to allow wider access to allow movement of items into and out of the
Depot Lobby.
3. Development of a Pedestrian Patio on the southwest side of the building. This work will
incorporate brick pavers to match those found elsewhere in the Depot Plaza, shade
structures, tables, benches and other amenities. The work will also revise landings at
doors to ensure proper handicap access. This patio will be accessible from the Depot
Lobby and will provide additional and highly desirable visitor space for train watching,
etc. and will be available for Depot or private events.
4. Installation of decorative metal fence along the UP Railroad right of way from the
parking area west of the Depot to the east end of the Depot property. This will further
enhance the Depot appearance and visitor experience. The development of the
Pedestrian Patio and the upgrade of the fence will be significant improvements to south
side of the Depot property.
5. Renovation of the kitchen area adjacent to the Depot Lobby. The renovation work will
update this area to allow use for catering and/or a concessionaire.
6. Additional power receptacles are being added at the exterior of building.
7. Repair of the in-floor Train Map display located in Depot Lobby. This unique feature has
been inoperable for some time and will be renovated to bring it back to life.
8. Replacement of exterior door lock with keypads/access control in selected location to
enhance security at the facility.
