SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-2-25 for Ten (10) New Four-Door 2024/2025 Model
Utility Police Interceptors for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department
 _____________________________________________
Bid #E-2-25 was solicited for Ten (10) New Four-Door 2024/2025 Model Utility Police
Interceptors for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department.

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bids were
received from:
1.
Dana Safety Supply (Livingston, Montana)
2.
Ken Garff Cheyenne (Cheyenne, Wyoming)

The bids were reviewed by Joel Hickerson (CPD), Shannon Keslar (CPD), and Bryan Bell
(Fleet Maintenance), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from
Dana Safety Supply.

The total requested bid award is $703,288.39.

Funding:
020-26-2613-41-43201 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Motor Vehicles)

021-26-2613-41-43201 (1% Sales Tax 23-26 / Motor Vehicles)
