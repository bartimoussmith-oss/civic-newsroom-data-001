RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION
TO THE WYOMING DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR A
WYOMING RURAL PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION PROGRAM GRANT
TO PROVIDE THE LOCAL MATCH FOR OPERATING FUNDS FOR THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR ITS TRANSIT PROGRAM."

WHEREAS, the United States Department of Transportation is authorized to make grants
to states through the Federal Transit Administration (FTA) to support operating, project
administration and maintenance assistance projects for non-urbanized area public transportation
systems under 49 U.S.C. § 5311 of the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law (BIL), Public Law No. 117-
58, November 15, 2021, as amended ("Section 5311");

WHEREAS, the Wyoming Department of Transportation (WYDOT) has been designated
by Governor Gordan to administer Section 5311;

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, acting through the Cheyenne Transit Program, wishes
to apply for financial assistance; and,

WHEREAS, the contract for financial assistance will impose certain obligations upon the
applicant, including possible provisions for the applicant to provide the local share of project costs.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE:

1.
That the Mayor is authorized to execute and file an application for financial
assistance on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program,
with the WYDOT to aid the financing of operating, project administration and
maintenance assistance for projects pursuant to Section 5311;

2.
That the Mayor is authorized to furnish such additional information as the WYDOT
may require in connection with the application of the project; and,

3.
That the Mayor is authorized to execute grant and cooperative agreements with the
WYDOT on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Mayor Patrick Collins, Mayor is designated as the
authorized representative of the City of Cheyenne to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all
matters relating to this grant application.

8:38 am, Mar 23 2023
