```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

City of Cheyenne Governing Body
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

July 21, 2021
SUBJECT:
Resolution Authorizing One-Time Payment for City Employees

On July 26, 2021, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee for
consideration of a one-time payment of up to $5,000 to be given to all City of Cheyenne full-time
and permanent part-time employees. Excluded from this are elected officials and employees of the
separate Board of Public Utilities.
As indicated on the included graph, the City will receive a total of $12,191,813 from the
Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Fund of the American Rescue Plan (ARP).  I have
also included on this graph the five eligible uses of the funds, including essential employee
premium pay.
Another eligible use of ARP funds is the replacement of lost public sector revenue.  Governments
have broad latitude to use this funding to ensure that fiscal austerity measures do not hamper
government services.  I have calculated the City’s revenue recovery and am estimating a total of
$3,595,102, 65% of which is for the Civic Center, Ice and Events Center and Recreation Programs
Fund.  The remainder, or $1,243,902, is available for any allowable use, including these one-time
payments.
I have worked with Department Directors to categorize all City employees, based on the U.S.
Treasury Interim Rules, as either essential or non-essential.  As you know, because of budget
shortages in Fiscal Years 2020, 2021 and 2022, City employees, for the most part, have not
received a salary increase for nearly three years.  Furthermore, in last year’s budget City employees
either lost their longevity pay or received a salary decrease.  The one-time payments will help
address these inequities as well as the City’s current struggle in hiring and retaining employees.
The one-time payment, including payroll taxes, will be disbursed as follows:
• $1,691,322 for employees who are defined as essential and will be paid for by the
“Providing Premium Pay for Essential Workers” section of the ARP.
• $832,872 for those employees considered non-essential to be paid for by the “Replacement
of Lost Public Sector Revenue” section of the ARP.
• $339,316 to be paid by other City funds (as indicated in the resolution).
As always, if you have any questions, please give me a call at 637-6336 or email at
rlockman@cheyennecity.org.
```

```text
RESOLUTION NO. ___________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING A ONE-TIME PAYMENT GIVEN TO
FULL-TIME AND PERMANENT PART-TIME EMPLOYEES OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE (EXCLUDING ELECTED OFFICIALS AND
EMPLOYEES OF THE CHEYENNE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES)
FROM FUNDS RECEIVED FROM THE CORONAVIRUS STATE AND
LOCAL
FISCAL
RECOVERY
FUND
ESTABLISHED
BY
THE
AMERICAN RESCUE PLAN ACT OF 2021.”

WHEREAS, the Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Fund, established by the
American Rescue Plan Act of 2021, was created to address the public health emergency that began
in January of 2020; and
WHEREAS, the Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds provide local
governments with a substantial infusion of resources to respond to the public health emergency,
replace lost public sector revenue, provide premium pay for essential workers, and invest in water,
sewer, and broadband infrastructure projects; and
WHEREAS, on May 18, 2021, the City of Cheyenne received fifty percent (50%) of its
total allocation, or $6,095,906.50, from the United States Department of Treasury, with the balance
to be delivered in May of 2022; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne’s employees have faced heightened risks because of the
public health emergency and have put their physical well-being in jeopardy to meet the daily needs
of their communities and to provide care for others; and
WHEREAS, employees of the City of Cheyenne have also faced negative economic
impacts caused by the public health emergency, including wage reductions and elimination of
longevity pay; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne’s Governing Body wishes to provide a one-time
payment of $5,000.00, subject to federal withholding requirements, to each current full-time
employee, excluding elected officials and those employed by the separate Board of Public Utilities,
who are employed by the City on June 30, 2021.  Full-time employees who were hired on or after
July 1, 2020 will receive a pro-rated amount of the payment, depending on which month their
employment began with the City; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne’s Governing Body also wishes to provide a one-time
payment to each permanent part-time employee, subject to federal withholding requirements.
Permanent part-time employees will receive a prorated amount of the one-time $5,000.00 payment,
based on a percentage of the total number of regular hours worked from July 1, 2020 to June 30,
2021; and
By Michael R. O'Donnell at 9:50 am, Jul 21, 2021
```

```text
WHEREAS, on June 30, 2021, the City of Cheyenne has 325 full-time essential employees
and 22 part-time essential employees, defined by the Interim Final Rule, 31 CFR Part 35, issued
by the United States Department of Treasury.  Employees meet this definition whose work
involves regular in-person interactions or regular physical handling of items that were handled by
others and include, but are not limited to, custodians, sanitation workers, public health and safety
staff, transit staff, childcare workers, and social services and human services staff; and
WHEREAS, these essential employees qualify to receive a premium payment from the
Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body also wishes to recognize the remaining 207 full-time and
146 permanent part-time employees who do not qualify as an essential employee as defined by the
Coronavirus State and Local Fiscal Recovery Funds Interim Final Rule, by providing the same
one-time payment as described above; and
WHEREAS, payment for the employees that do not qualify as essential employees will be
made from the replacement of lost public sector revenue portion of the Coronavirus State and
Local Fiscal Recovery Funds. Governments have broad latitude to use these revenue recovery
funds to support government services; and
WHEREAS, eight City funds do not qualify to receive revenue recovery funds, including
the Weed and Pest Fund, CDBG Fund, One Percent Tax Fund, Solid Waste Fund, Transportation
Planning Fund, Transit Fund, Special Purpose Option Tax Fund, and Fleet Maintenance Fund.
Therefore, these funds will cover the one-time payment for their own employees.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that all current full-time and permanent part-time employees, on
a pro-rata basis, who are employed on June 30, 2021, are eligible to receive the one-time $5,000.00
payment, subject to federal withholding requirements, excluding elected officials and Cheyenne
Board of Public Utilities employees.  To receive the one-time payment, qualifying employees must
be actively employed with the City of Cheyenne on the date these checks are issued.
```
