CITY CONTRACT NO. ________

ADULT SOFTBALL OFFICIATING AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made and entered into this _______ day of ________________, 2022,
by and between the City of Cheyenne, a municipal corporation of the State of Wyoming, 2101
O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, hereinafter referred to as “City,” and the Cheyenne
Umpires Association, 1505 East 16th Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, hereinafter referred to
as “Association.”
W I T N E S SE T H:

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and conditions contained
herein, it is hereby agreed as follows:
1.
Leagues and Role.  Association will umpire and score games and tournaments during
the City’s 2022 season for Adult Men’s and Women’s Softball Leagues, Co-Rec Softball
League, and tournaments scheduled by the City of Cheyenne Recreation Division.
2.
Game Fee to Umpires and Scorekeepers.  City shall pay umpires Thirty-Five Dollars
($35.00) per Adult Men’s and Women’s Softball games, Adult Co-Rec Softball games,
and Tournament games (one umpire required), and Fifteen Dollars ($15.00) per game to
the scorekeeper (one scorekeeper required).  Umpires in Training will receive Fifteen
Dollars ($15.00) per game up to ten (10) games total. Refer to 4. Payment for tracking
and payment of Umpires in Training.
3.
Additional Fees.  Association will charge City a one-time $600 annual administrative
service fee for the three leagues (Men’s Softball League, Women’s Softball League, and
Co-Rec Softball League) and tournaments.  In addition, a $.35 (thirty-five cents) per
scheduled game fee will be charged to the City.
By Stefanie Boster at 9:05 am, Mar 09, 2022

4.
Payment.
a. Association will invoice the City for the first 400 games.  Upon receipt of the invoice,
City will process immediately through the City Treasurer’s Office payable system.  The
City processes invoices on a schedule with payable cut-off dates, submits to City Council
approval process, and then checks are cut and mailed. Association may turn in the first
400 games invoice immediately following approval of this Agreement.
b. Association will invoice the City for the subsequent games to be umpired by the
Association for the balance of the initial league as scheduled by the City shall be
submitted prior to the mid-point of the season.
c. Within thirty (30) days after the conclusion of each league, the City will invoice
Association for all fines accumulated and credit any games not paid for.  Association
shall pay invoice within 45 days of receipt of the invoice.
d. Association will invoice City immediately following a tournament, and the City will
process the invoice through the normal City Treasurer’s Office payables system.
e. Association will invoice City for Umpires in Training separately.  Invoice will include
Umpire in Training names, dates, and number of games officiated.

(1) umpire and one (1) scorekeeper for the Adult Men’s and Women’s League, the Co-
Rec Softball League, and City tournaments to umpire and score games as referred to
above according to the schedule provided to the Association by the City.
6.
Schedule.  Association shall provide the City with a schedule of the umpires and
scorekeepers assigned to each game at least two (2) weeks in advance of each game.
7.
Contacts.  Association shall provide the City with a list of names and telephone

Inclement Weather.  Parties mutually agree that if inclement weather begins after the
umpires and scorekeeper have arrived for the first scheduled game and the game is
cancelled before being started, the umpires and scorekeepers will receive travel pay in the
amount of $5.00 each.  The umpires and scorekeeper must sign in.
a. The parties further agree the maximum delay due to inclement weather will be thirty
(30) minutes.
b. If games are cancelled due to inclement weather, all subsequent games are cancelled.

c. It is further agreed that on weekends (tournaments only), the decision to cancel one or

pay the Association the game fee to the umpire(s) and scorekeeper(s).  If a game is
cancelled or forfeited and the Association was notified by 3:30 p.m., the Association will
not be paid for the game if it is a first or last game.

14.
Scoring.  Association scorekeeper shall keep and maintain a score book, score report
form, and distribute lineup cards for each game.  The scorekeeper shall mark the official
starting time for each game in the scorebook and enter the first initial and last names of
each player into the score book. The scorekeeper shall also fill out the score report form
and have the umpire sign score book after each game to verify the score of each game
and then the scorekeeper shall turn in the score report form to the site supervisor.
a. Failure to turn in the score report form, the Association will be fined Ten
Dollars ($10.00) per game
b. Failure to report a score correctly, Association will be fined Ten Dollars
($10.00) per game.
c. Sanctioned League.  City shall provide a National Association sanctioned league
and maintain all requirements for sanctioning.
