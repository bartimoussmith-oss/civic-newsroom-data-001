MEMO

Subject: Downtown Development Authority Proposed Budget - Fiscal Year 2025
Date:
May 13, 2025

Background:

Attached is the proposed budget for fiscal year 2025 (July 1 to June 30). The proposed Governing Body
review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral: May 13, 2025
•
Public Services Committee: May 20, 2025
•
Final consideration: May 28, 2025

The budget represents a $76.649.16 increase in mill levy assessment collections ($463,649.16) and an
increase in interest earned. The interest earned estimate is based on an estimated $1 million dollars
being left in the Wyoming Government Investment Fund (WGIF) which as of April 12, 2024, has a seven-
day yield of 5.19%.

The proposed budget is a decrease in the budget from the prior year. The proposed budget of
$1,049,049 is $187,101 less than fiscal year 2024, brings in $500,000 from reserves, accounts for a
$20,000 grant from the Cheyenne-Laramie Co. Economic Development Joint Powers Board, and $5,000
in funding from Laramie County. The City contributes $209,929 covering all payroll, insurance, and
benefits for employees. Other City staff also support DDA employees at no cost to the DDA.

•
DDA Board Strategic Projects
This line item replaces the previous “Large Projects” line item and provides clarity that this is a
special fund to carry our unique Board projects.

•
Sponsorship
This line item represents sponsorships to other events and organizations that may make
requests throughout the year. Allocations will be made on a request basis and sponsorship
funds must face public facing activities and programs that have a direct public benefit.

•
Downtown Planter Program
This program has been increased based on discussions with potential providers.

•
Façade Improvement Program (FIP)
This is a preservation-based facade improvement program aimed at revitalizing historical
structures by restoring and enhancing their exterior appearance while fostering economic
growth through increased property values and tourism. The program has not been used in that
capacity recently and staff intends to further develop the program in the upcoming year. For
budgeting and accounting purposes, we recommend that this and the Capital Improvement
Grant program be independent line items.

•
Capital Improvement Grant (CIG) Program
This program has been commonly used in recent years and is comprised of a majority of the
grants awarded in recent years. In fiscal year 2024, $500,000 was budgeted and it is expected
that all grants will be allocated prior to the end of the fiscal year. For budgeting and accounting
purposes, we recommend that this and the Façade Improvement Program be independent line
items.

•
Sidewalk Match Program
This is a new line item to carry out the “Sidewalk Repair Assistance Program” which will be in
response to the City Engineering Department’s sidewalk repair initiative.

•
Logo Design Program
Staff recommends zeroing out this program because logo and brand development results in a
copyrighted product exclusive to a private party. Additionally, a similar program exists through
the Wyoming Small Business Development Center Network which offers logo design assistance.

The DDA Board may elect to adopt the budget as presented or make modifications. If the DDA Board
desires more discussion on this item, this can be postponed to a special meeting next week.

The DDA Board approved this budget at their April 18, 2024.

RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE CHEYENNE
DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR
BEGINNING JULY 1, 2024 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2025."

WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority ("DDA") was created pursuant to
Ordinance No. 2804 adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984;

WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute 15-9-208(a) requires the DDA to propose a budget, which
shall be administratively reviewed by the City prior to submission of the proposed budget to the
Governing Body for approval;

WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Chapter 2.76.050(C), provides that the DDA budget
shall be subject to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance
with the policies of the City;

WHEREAS, the DDA Board approved the proposed budget at their April 18, 2024,
meeting; and

WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA .

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1,
2024 and ending June 30, 2025 of the Downtown Development Authority, as set forth in the
attached information with this Resolution entitled "Downtown Development Authority Proposed
Budget - Fiscal Year 2025" in the total amount of $1,049,049.00, is hereby approved.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _______ day of _____________________,
2024.

Patrick Collins, Mayor
