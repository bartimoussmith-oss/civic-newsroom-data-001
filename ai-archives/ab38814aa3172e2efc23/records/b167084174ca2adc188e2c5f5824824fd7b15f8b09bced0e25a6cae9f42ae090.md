Move to approve the rezoning of Lots 1 and 2, Block 8, Whitney Ranch 4th Filing to MUB Mixed-Use

UDC Article 5

PlanCheyenne

BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from CB Community Business and
HR High-density Residential to MUB Mixed-Use Business for 11.6 acres of vacant land. The properties are
anticipated to have a multifamily housing and possible commercial uses in a mixed-use format. The
properties collectively were anticipated to develop with this mix of uses, just with a more defined separation.
The MUB zone on the entire block gives more flexibility to the location of these uses.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with planned
infrastructure improvements. Policies in PlanCheyenne encourage development with a balance
of uses, including higher-density housing along major streets.

PUDC-23-149
November 6, 2023
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-Use Commercial and Urban
Residential and the surrounding area is Mixed-Use Commercial and Urban Residential. The area
designated Mixed-use Commercial is diagonal and not based on any plats, as the designation
pre-dates the plats. The Mixed-use Commercial designation should therefore be seen as having
a flexible boundary with the Urban Residential areas to the north and west. The optimal City
zoning for the future land use designation of Mixed-use Commercial is MUB Mixed-use Business,
which is the proposed zone of this application (the existing CB zone was an acceptable
alternative).

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Nearby properties to the north and west are zoned MUB (the zone that is proposed with this
zoning map amendment). Property to the northwest is zoned MR. The MR zoned area was
already adjacent to MUB zoning and is currently undeveloped. Property to the east is zoned CB,
and MUB is considered a less intense zone, compatible with the commercial nature of that
zoning. Property to the south is zoned AR in the County. However, this land is adjacent to major
streets and the future land use designations in the area clearly anticipate more intense uses.
The MUB zone is considered a less intense zone than the existing CB zone.

The area proposed to be zoned MUB is adjacent to major roadways and is generally compatible
with anticipated development along major transportation corridors.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current CB zone allows a variety of commercial uses but prohibits single-unit residential
uses and requires multifamily to go through a conditional use process. The CB zone requires
large setbacks from streets. The proposed MUB zone allows a variety of commercial uses,
residential uses, and mixed uses. The MUB zone also allows buildings to be built to the street.
The MUB zone typically has a higher design standard than the CB zone.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The existing block is larger than expected for the urban-style of development anticipated for
MUB zoning. However, the block qualifies for exceptions due to the proximity to a major street
intersection.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.
