WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board ("Board")was created pursuant to
Ordinance No.2407adopted by the Governing Body on February 11,1980, as amended; and
WHEREAS, City Code Section 2.60.050(B) setsforth the requirement that the Board shall
submit on or before the first day of May of each year, to the governing bodies of the city and the
county, a proposed budget forthe ensuing fiscal year; and
WHEREAS, City Code Section 2.60.050(B) provides that the Board budget shall be subject
to alteration, modification, and approval by the Governing Body in accordance with the policies
of the City; and
