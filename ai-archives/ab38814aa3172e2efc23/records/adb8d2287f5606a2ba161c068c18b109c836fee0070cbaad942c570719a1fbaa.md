A Text Amendment amending the Unified Development Code of the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, repealing UDC 5.4.7. UU – Urban Use Overlay District and

PLN-22-00003

Mark Christensen, AICP, Planner II, Planning and Development

RECOMMENDED MOTION:

Move to recommend the Governing Body approve text amendments to the Unified Development Code
to repeal UDC 5.4.7. UU – Urban Use Overlay District and create UDC 5.6.9. UU – Urban Use Overlay
District, noting the request meets the review criteria of UDC 2.4.1.d.

BACKGROUND AND DISCUSSION:

The City’s Unified Development Code (UDC) was adopted by the Governing Body on January 23, 2012, by
Ordinance Number 3943 with an effective date of April 30, 2012.  The Unified Development Code is the
regulatory document carrying out the vision of Plan Cheyenne, and is reviewed annually pursuant to UDC
1.1.7 [Annual Review Requirement] to determine if any particular standard or regulation is timely,
relevant and adequately addresses current market conditions. In addition to the annual review, the
Planning and Development Director and City Engineer are authorized under UDC 2.4.1.b.3 to bring
forward text amendments on their own initiative.

The City of Cheyenne Plannning and Development Department drafted the Urban Use Overlay (UU)
District in Spring of 2021 as part of on-going efforts to revitalize the West Edge District. The overlay was
adopted into the Unified Development Code on June 28, 2021. Staff looked to mapping the UU Overlay
District in late 2021 but noted that the regulations have been placed in the incorrect location in the UDC.
Additionally, staff noted the verbiage in the Intent, Applicability, and Context limited the potential
implementation of the UU Overlay District. This current effort remedies both situations. The proposed
ordinance moves the UU Overlay District to 5.6.9. in the Special Purpose and Overlay Districts section of
the UDC and proposes new verbiage in the Intent, Applicability, and Context of the District to allow more
areas of the community to take advantage of the flexibility the UU Overlay District provides.

REVIEW CRITERIA:

Review of a UDC text amendment shall be measured by the following three (3) criteria of Section 2.4.1.d:

1. COMPREHENSIVE PLAN:
“The amendment is in accordance with general goals and policies of the Comprehensive Plan, or is
necessary to address an emerging issue not anticipated in the Comprehensive Plan and is not
inconsistent with the plan.”

This proposed text amendment is consistent with the principles and policies within PlanCheyenne.
Specifically, the proposed amendment addresses the following policies and principles:

•
Policy 1.1.B: Flexibility to Respond to Market Demands.
The UU Overlay text amendment will provide flexibility with zoning regulations to promote
infill development, adaptive reuse, and redevelopment of an important area in the City of
Cheyenne. The flexibility should incentivize the market to consider developing in the West
Edge.

•
Policy 1.4.A: Infill Opportunities
The Urban Use Overlay text amendment provides the opportunity for infill development
through regulations that promote flexibility, but also consider the character of the area in
which they are located.

•
Policy 1.4.C: Downtown Revitalization and Mix of Uses
The Urban Use Overlay can be applicable to the MUR, MUB, and P zone districts in or adjacent
to downtown. As the Urban Use Overlay is applied to the West Edge it is anticipated to
revitalize the area and further mix uses in the area. Staff anticipates the development of the
West Edge will greatly benefit the downtown as a whole.

•
Policy 1.4.D: Infill and Revitalization Incentives
The Urban Use overlay achieves several items that are mentioned in this policy; such as
reducing parking minimums, providing flexible dimensional standards, promoting higher
densities than currently permitted, and providing flexibility in design requirements for
utilizing existing buildings.

•
Policy 2.3.A: Mix of Housing Types
The Urban Use Overlay will lead to the West Edge being zoned MUB or MUR; each of which
provide ample opportunities for a variety of housing.

•
Policy 3.2.B: Active Uses in the Street Level
The Urban Use Overlay promotes active uses on the street level by permitting various street
furnishings and projections into the Right of Way.

2. STATE STATUTORY REQUIREMENTS:
The amendment is consistent with the Wyoming Statutes, and specifically the Municipal Planning
and Zoning statutes. The following Articles are all listed verbatim from Wyoming State Statute.

WYO 15-1-602 (a):
The governing body shall specify how regulations, restrictions and the
district boundaries are to be determined, established, enforced,
amended, supplemented or otherwise changed.
Assessment:
State statute states that the governing body shall specify how regulations
regarding zoning are established and enforced.  The governing body
possesses the authority to amend regulations and therefore can amend
the Unified Development Code to reflect this text amendment.

WYO 15-1-601 (a):
The governing body of any city or town, by ordinance, may:
(i) Regulate and restrict the:
(D) Density of the population
(E) Location and use of buildings, structures and land for trade,
industry, residence or other purposes.
Assessment:
This amendment proposes modifying the Unified Development Code for
the purposes of regulating the location of buildings and density of the
population; thus, fulfilling the requirements of this statute.

WYO 15-1-601(d):
All regulations shall be made:
(ii) With reasonable consideration, among other things, of the character
of the district and its peculiar suitability for particular uses;
(iii) With a view to conserving the value of buildings and encouraging the
most appropriate use of land throughout the city or town.
Assessment:
This amendment proposes modifying the Unified Development Code for
the purposes of encouraging redevelopment and infill development in
the City of Cheyenne with respect to the existing character of the districts
to which these regulations are applied. Furthermore, these regulations
contain provisions for height regulations to preserve views of existing
prominent structures. Regulations of this nature are allowable under
state statue and may be modified by the Governing Body.

3. HEALTH, SAFETY, AND PUBLIC WELFARE:
“The amendment promotes the general health, safety and public welfare.”

This amendment will promote general health, safety, and public welfare by reinvesting in the
community and promoting walkable, mixed-use development. Furthermore, it is anticipated that
this amendment will promote much needed housing development in the community.

CONCLUSION:

The Urban Use Overlay district provides an effective framework for redevelopment in near-downtown
neighborhoods in Cheyenne, specifically in the West Edge District. However, staff feels this flexibility
should also be available to more areas of the community. The proposed amendments allow for more
utilization of the UU Overlay District and relocate the UU Overlay District to the correct location in the
UDC. Staff is satisfied that this text amendment meets the review criteria of UDC 2.4.1.d.

STAFF RECOMMENDATION:

Staff recommends approval of text amendments to the Unified Development Code to repeal UDC 5.4.7.
UU – Urban Use Overlay District and create UDC 5.6.9. UU – Urban Use Overlay District, noting the
