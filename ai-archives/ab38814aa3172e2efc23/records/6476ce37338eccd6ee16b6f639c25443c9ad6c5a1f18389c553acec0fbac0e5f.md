MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Jeanie Vetter, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner

Subject: Public Hearing for Disposal of Real Property and Trade Agreement for land for Greater
Cheyenne Greenway, Sun Valley to Kiwanis Park Connector

Date:
April 5, 2023

1. The MOU outlining responsibilities for the proposed property exchange with Sun Valley Estates
LLC, Contract #7642, was approved by Council and signed by the Mayor on November 29, 2022.
Since this time, all responsibilities of both the city and the Developer have been met.
2. The plat for this project was approved by Council on February 27, 2023, and is awaiting
recordation based on Council’s approval of this agreement.
3. Pursuant to Wyoming Statute 15-1-112, staff has completed all steps associated with the
disposal of city-owned real property for the purposes of trading property for a future Greenway
corridor and trail.  City staff has reviewed and approved the associated property as outlined in
the City of Cheyenne checklist for disposal/trade of real property.
4. Pursuant to Wyoming Statute 15-1-112(b)(ii) An advertisement of the proposed trade has been
published for three (3) weeks in the local newspaper.  March 11th, 18th and 25th. An
announcement of a public hearing is included on this agenda for tonight’s City Council meeting,
for public comment.
5. The trade/sale agreement has been reviewed by all interested parties.  The item will be placed
on the Finance Committee agenda for Monday, April 17th for further recommendation and final
reading for approval by the governing body will take place on Monday, April 24th.  Once
approved by the governing body and signed by all interested parties, the process shall proceed
to closing for final execution.
Thank you in advance for your consideration.  If you have any question, please feel free to contact me.

Respectfully,
Jeanie Vetter
