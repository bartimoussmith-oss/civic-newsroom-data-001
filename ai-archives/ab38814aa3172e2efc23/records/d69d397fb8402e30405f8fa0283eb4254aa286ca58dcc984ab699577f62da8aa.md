RESOLUTION NO. ____________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE CHEYENNE
DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
JULY 1, 2022 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2023.”

WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority ("DDA") was created pursuant to
Ordinance No. 2804 adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984; and

WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat.§ 15-9-208(a) requires the DDA to propose a budget, which shall
be administratively reviewed by the City prior to submission of the proposed budget to the
Governing Body for approval; and

WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Chapter 2. 76.050 (C), provides that the DDA budget
shall be subject to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance
with the policies of the City; and

WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget for the Fiscal Year beginning
July 1, 2022 and ending June 30, 2023 of the Downtown Development Authority, as set forth in
the attached information with this Resolution entitled “Cheyenne Downtown Development
Authority Fiscal Year 2023 Proposed Budget” in the total amount of $1,431,500.00, is hereby
approved.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this ______ day of ________________, 2022.

__________________________________________

Patrick J. Collins, Mayor

(SEAL)
Attest:

__________________________________
Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk

By Stefanie Boster at 12:50 pm, May 11, 2022
