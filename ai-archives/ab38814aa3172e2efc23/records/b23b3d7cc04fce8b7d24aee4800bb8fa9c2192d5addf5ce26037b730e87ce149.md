RESOLUTION NO. ___________

ENTITLED: “RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION TO
THE WYOMING DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION FOR A WYOMING
RURAL PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION PROGRAM GRANT TO PROVIDE
THE LOCAL MATCH FOR OPERATING FUNDS FOR THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE FOR ITS TRANSIT PROGRAM, AS AUTHORIZED BY 49 U.S.C.
§ 5311.”

WHEREAS, the Director of the Wyoming Department of Transportation has been
delegated authority to award Federal financial assistance to qualified public transit providers; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, acting through the Cheyenne Transit Program, wishes
to apply for financial assistance; and

WHEREAS, the grant of financial assistance imposes certain obligations upon an applicant
and may require an applicant to provide the local share of the project cost; and

WHEREAS, an applicant must provide all required annual certifications and assurances to
the Federal Transit Administration.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE:

1.
That the Mayor is authorized to execute and file an application for financial
assistance on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program, with the
Wyoming Department of Transportation as authorized by 49 U.S.C. § 5311.

2.
That the Mayor is authorized to execute and file with the application the annual
certifications and assurances and other documents the Wyoming Department of Transportation
requires before awarding an assistance grant.

3.
That the Mayor is authorized to execute grant and cooperative agreements with the
Wyoming Department of Transportation on behalf of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne
Transit Program.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Patrick Collins, Mayor, is designated as the
authorized representative of the City of Cheyenne to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all
matters relating to this grant application.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _______ day of ______________, 2022.

__________________________________________

Patrick Collins, Mayor
