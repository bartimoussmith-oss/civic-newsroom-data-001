RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE CLEAN CROW
CREEK
INITIATIVE ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNING BODY FOR THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING."

WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the city of Cheyenne, WY recognizes the need for
clean water in our creeks to support a healthy community;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the city of Cheyenne, Wyoming acknowledges the
importance of protecting our creeks from stormwater pollution;

WHEREAS, the Clean Crow Creek Initiative aims to improve water quality through the
installation and maintenance of Wyoming made catch basin stormwater filters;

WHEREAS, it is understood that in the past the city of Cheyenne has accepted stormwater
filters donated by all the Rotary International Clubs of Cheyenne, the Boys and Girls Club, the
Laramie County Conservation District, multiple anonymous donors, and Microsoft Corporation;

WHEREAS, the city of Cheyenne encourages and will accept donations from sponsoring
businesses, organizations, or citizens who wish to contribute to the community effort of protecting
the creek;

WHEREAS, the Clean Crow Creek Initiative will support the Public Works Department
by reducing the amount of debris and pollutants entering the stormwater system, with the intent to
decrease maintenance costs and improve operational efficiency;

WHEREAS, the Clean Crow Creek Initiative aligns with the city's goals to protect the
environment for the betterment of the community, enhancing the quality of life for residents by
ensuring cleaner waterways, which can contribute to public health and recreational opportunities;

WHEREAS, the implementation of the Clean Crow Creek Initiative helps the city comply
with federal, state, and local environmental regulations, furthering the city’s efforts to meet or
exceed standards that protect the creek from stormwater pollution, and recognizing that these
efforts can contribute positively to the community; and

WHEREAS, the city of Cheyenne is committed to exploring, accepting, and not
unreasonably denying multiple methods of maintaining the stormwater filters, including city
maintenance, hiring third-party vendors, or allowing third parties to clean the stormwater filters
themselves with city approval (which will not be reasonably withheld).

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that staff of the city of Cheyenne will support the Clean Crow
