Resolution
A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING SUBMISSION OF A FEDERAL MINERAL ROYALTY
CAPITAL CONSTRUCTION ACCOUNT GRANT APPLICATION TO THE STATE LOAN
AND INVESTMENT BOARD ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNING BODY FOR THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE FOR THE PURPOSE OF FUNDING THE FINISH OF THE SECOND
FLOOR OF A NEW MUNICIPAL COURTHOUSE.
WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the Federal
Mineral Royalty Capital Construction Account Grant program to assist in fmancing this project;
and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body for the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for the project;
and
WHEREAS, the Federal Mineral Royalty Capital Construction Account Grant program requires
that certain criteria be met, as described in the State Loan and Investment Boards Rules and
Regulations governing the program, and to the best of our knowledge this application meets
those criteria; and
WHEREAS, the project cost is estimated at $1,991,200 and the Governing Body ofthe City of
Cheyenne plans to provide the 50% match ($995,600) required for the Federal Mineral Royalty
Capital Construction Account Grant from City Reserves and the status of these matching funds
will be approved with the approval of this resolution.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY FOR THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, that a grant application in the amount of $995,600 be submitted to the
State Loan and Investment Board for consideration at the June 20, 2019 meeting to assist in
funding the finish of the second floor of a new municipal courthouse, and that the City of
Cheyenne commit the same for this project.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that Cindy Schneider, Grants Manager, is hereby designated
as the authorized representative ofthe City of Cheyenne, to act on behalf of the Governing Body
on all matters related to this grant application.
PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS l’ day of February 2019.
Marian I. Off, Mayor
Attest:
Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk

Re: New Municipal Courthouse Second-Floor Budget
Dear Cindy,
Attached to this letter you will find a budget estimate for the completion of the second-floor area in the new
Municipal Courthouse. These budget numbers were based on information obtained from the Architect and the
General Contractor as we have developed project construction estimates during the ongoing design phase.
The additional cost to the project to finish the second-floor is estimated to be $1,991,200.
The total cost to complete second floor is around $2.6M but items that you have noted that cannot be included
in the grant application have been deleted out such as furniture and contingency reserve. This estimate is also
based on second-floor completion at the same time as the initial construction of the building, utilizing the same
design and construction teams that will be in place. This allows for efficiencies, potential cost savings, and
greater value to the City for this investment.
Please let me know if you should have any questions.
