RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE A
SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT WITH WJE, LLC TO RESOLVE THE
APRIL 6, 2023 NOTICE OF GOVERNMENTAL CLAIM REGARDING AN
ALLEGED TAKING OF REAL PROPERTY IN SADDLE RIDGE
ADJACENT TO CHRISTENSEN ROAD."

WHEREAS, on April 6, 2023, through legal counsel, WJE, LLC filed with the City of
Cheyenne’s City Clerk’s office a Notice of Governmental Claim ("Governmental Claim") pursuant
to the Wyoming Governmental Claims Act, Wyoming Statute § 1-39-101 et seq. alleging the City
of Cheyenne had taken real property from WJE, LLC during its construction of the Christensen
Road extension;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC’s Governmental Claim specifically alleged that the affected real
property included approximately forty (40) acres of property at the northwest corner of the
intersection of Pershing Boulevard and Christensen Road;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC’s approved plat allowed for the construction of nineteen (19) home
lots adjacent to the road in the Saddle Ridge addition, eighteenth (18th) filing;

WHEREAS, during the construction of the Christensen Road extension, WJE, LLC granted
the City of Cheyenne a fifty (50) foot permanent and exclusive easement adjacent to WJE, LLC’s
Saddle Ridge development;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC also granted the City of Cheyenne an additional thirty (30) foot
temporary easement for construction of the Christensen Road extension. WJE, LLC claimed that
at the conclusion of the construction of the road, the property subject to the temporary construction
easement would be returned to WJE, LLC for the development of the additional platted nineteen
(19) homes in the Saddle Ridge addition, eighteenth (18th) filing;

WHEREAS, when the City of Cheyenne completed the Christensen Road extension, WJE,
LLC claimed that the City of Cheyenne made dramatic and permanent changes to the land
including removing a significant quantity of dirt/soil, which was relocated to other places;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC alleged that the removal of the soil/dirt altered the grade in the
temporary easement area to approximately thirteen (13) feet lower then the rest of the WJE, LLC’s
land resulting in a large amount of missing dirt/soil in the area of lands platting for the nineteen
(19) lots;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC estimated that the missing soil/dirt represented approximately
forty two thousand, four hundred eighty-four point fifty four (42,484.54) cubic yards. Claimant
further estimated the value of the soil/dirt to be between three dollars and fifty cents ($3.50) to five
dollars ($5.00) per cubic yard, depending on the distance replacement soil/dirt would have to be
transported to a given site;

SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT AND RELEASE

This SETTLEMENT AGREEMENT (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into this
_____ day of ______________, 2023, by and between WJE, LLC, 2247 E. 19th St., Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82001 (“WJE, LLC”), and the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming a municipality organized
under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming, 82001 (the
“City”), sometimes individually referred to herein as “Party” and collectively as “Parties.”

WHEREAS, on April 6, 2023, through legal counsel, WJE, LLC filed with the City of
Cheyenne’s City Clerk’s office a Notice of Governmental Claim ("Governmental Claim") pursuant
to the Wyoming Governmental Claims Act, Wyoming Statute § 1-39-101 et seq. alleging the City
of Cheyenne had taken real property from WJE, LLC during its construction of the Christiansen
Road extension;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC’s Governmental Claim specifically alleged that the affected real
property included approximately forty (40) acres of property at the northwest corner of the
intersection of Pershing Boulevard and Christiansen Road;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC’s approved plat allowed for the construction of nineteen (19) home
lots adjacent to the road in the Saddle Ridge addition, eighteenth (18th) filing;

WHEREAS, during the construction of the Christiansen Road extension, WJE, LLC
granted the City of Cheyenne a fifty (50) foot permanent and exclusive easement adjacent to WJE,
LLC’s Saddle Ridge development;

WHEREAS, WJE, LLC also granted the City of Cheyenne an additional thirty (30) foot
temporary easement for construction of the Christiansen Road extension. WJE, LLC claimed that
at the conclusion of the construction of the road, the property subject to the temporary construction
easement would be returned to WJE, LLC for the development of the additional platted nineteen
(19) homes in the Saddle Ridge addition, eighteenth (18th) filing;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and promises set forth in
this Agreement, and for other good and valuable consideration, the adequacy and sufficiency of
which are herby acknowledged by the parties, on behalf of City of Cheyenne and WJE, LLC the
Settling Signatories agree as follows:

1.
EXECUTION DATE. As used in this Agreement, the “Execution Date” shall mean
the date as of which the Agreement is fully executed by the Settling Signatories.

2.
SETTLEMENT AMOUNT.  In consideration of the release and dismissal of WJE,
LLC’s claims against the City, including WJE, LLC's withdrawal of its Governmental Claim, the
City agrees to allowing WJE, LLC to donate to the City the relevant  soil/dirt that was removed
from WJE, LLC’s real property by the City and any diminution in value to real property owned by
WJE, LLC related to the removal of the earth type materials from the temporary easement area
referenced above, for an agreed value of two hundred fifty thousand dollars ($250,000.00) with
the City to execute such documents as are necessary for WJE, LLC to claim such donation as a
charitable donation with its filings with the Internal Revenue Service;

3.
The City and WJE, LLC agree that the temporary easement area is no longer in use
by the City, and any fencing put up by the City to protect re-landscaping efforts in the temporary
easement area or for other purposes, is not meant to change ownership of the real property so
fenced, or claim any ownership by the City of the temporary easement real property area owned
by WJE, LLC;

4.
WAIVER AND RELEASE OF LIABILITY.  In consideration for the settlement
of WJE’S claims against the City of Cheyenne, WJE, LLC agrees to forever release, settle and
discharge the City of Cheyenne from any and all claims, and liability of every nature, whether
known or unknown, that concern, arise out of or relate in any way to the above-described
Governmental Claim.
