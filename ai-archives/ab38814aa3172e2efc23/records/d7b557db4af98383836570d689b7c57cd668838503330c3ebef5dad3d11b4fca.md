LEASE
This Lease is made this ______ day of ___________________, 2011, between the City of
Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2101 O’Neil
Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and Little America Hotels & Resorts, Inc., a
corporation organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2800 West Lincolnway,
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“Lessee”).
SECTION ONE
DESCRIPTION OF PROPERTY; TERM
In consideration of the rent provided for in Section Two of this Lease, City leases to Lessee
real property belonging to City located in Laramie County, Wyoming, and more particularly
described as a site for the placement of a billboard advertising sign at a location near mile post 347.3
on the south side of Interstate 80, approximately 13 miles west of Cheyenne, Wyoming, for a term
of five (5) years beginning on the date that this Lease is fully executed by the parties.  That date shall
be hereinafter referred to as the “commencement date.”  The advertising sign site is hereinafter
referred to as the “leased property.”
SECTION TWO
RENT
Lessee shall pay City an annual rent of One Thousand ($1,000.00) payable within thirty (30)
days after the commencement date, and payable in the advance on the commencement date in each
year thereafter for the entire term of this Lease.
SECTION THREE
USE OF PROPERTY
Lessee shall use the leased property for the construction and maintenance of a billboard,
measuring no more than _________ feet in length by _________ feet in width, and for no other
purpose whatsoever.  Lessee shall not placed on the leased property any advertisement for alcohol
or tobacco products.
SECTION FOUR
COMPLIANCE WITH LAW
In its use of the leased property, Lessee shall fully comply with all applicable federal, state,
county and municipal statutes, rules, regulations, and ordinances.  In its use of the leased property,
Lessee shall secure from the proper authorities all required licenses and permits before commencing
any construction, re-construction, or significant repair of the billboard.
SECTION FIVE
REPAIRS AND MAINTENANCE
Lessee shall, at all times during the term of this Lease, keep all improvements placed by
Lessee on the leased property in good repair, and shall maintain them at all times in a clean and
attractive condition. Construction and maintenance activities shall be conducted in a manner that
is not hazardous to livestock, including the installation of fencing if necessary to prevent
unreasonable risks of harm to livestock.  Lessee shall be responsible for any and all livestock injuries
and deaths caused or in any way resulting from construction or maintenance activities.

SECTION SIX
INSURANCE
Lessee shall obtain, and keep in full force and effect at all times during the term of this Lease,
the following specified insurance coverages for the protection of City and Lessee against liability

SECTION SEVEN
OWNERSHIP OF IMPROVEMENTS
All improvements erected by Lessee on the leased property shall remain the property of
Lessee, and shall be removed by Lessee at the termination of this Lease or any renewal of the
Lease.
SECTION EIGHT
ACCESS TO LEASED PROPERTY
City grants Lessee the right to pass to and from the leased property over City’s private
roads located on the City’s Belvior Ranch for the purposes of construction, maintenance, and
repair of the billboard advertising sign located on the leased premises; provided, however, that
Lessee shall use said private roads in accordance with good management practices and shall take
reasonable precautions to prevent damage to said roads and to the ranch lands adjacent to said
roads and the leased property.
SECTION NINE
TERMINATION
A.
By City.
1.
If the City should make a bona fide contract for the sale of the leased
property, the City may, at City’s option, terminate this Lease by giving Lessee one
hundred and eighty (180) days notice in writing, and refunding to Lessee the rental paid in
advance pro rata for the unexpired term of this Lease.
2.
If Lessee defaults in the payment of rent, or breaches any other covenant or
condition of this Lease, the City may, in addition to any other remedy that the City may
have, declare a forfeiture of this Lease for the default, re-enter the leased property, and
remove all personal property from the premises.
B.
By Lessee.  During the term of this Lease, should the use of the leased property
for the purpose permitted by this Lease be prohibited, limited or restricted by the terms of any
restriction, covenant, regulation, ordinance, or zoning resolution affecting the property, Lessee
may, at Lessee’s option, terminate this Lease by giving the City thirty (30) days notice in writing
of its election to do so.
C.
Upon termination of this Lease, Lessee shall restore the leased property to its
natural state including, without limitation, filling all holes and removing all debris caused by the
removal of billboard signs and other items of personal property placed on the leased property by
Lessee.
SECTION TEN
EFFECT OF CONDEMNATION
If the leased property, or any part of the leased property, is taken by eminent domain, this
Lease shall terminate on the date when title vests pursuant to such taking.

SECTION ELEVEN
RENEWAL OPTION
The Lessee shall have the option to extend this Lease for one additional five (5) year
period, provided, however, that said option may be exercised only if (1) the Lessee is not in
default of any of the terms and conditions of this Lease at the time the option is exercised; (2) the
Lessee has practiced good stewardship of the leased property, access roads, and adjacent ranch
lands as determined by the City in the exercise of its reasonable discretion; and (3) the Lessee
provides written notice to the City of its intent to exercise its option to renew at least one hundred
and twenty-days (120) days prior to the scheduled expiration of the Lease.  Any such extension
shall be on the same terms and conditions as set forth in this Lease, unless such terms and
conditions are amended or renegotiated pursuant to mutual written agreement.
SECTION TWELVE
INDEMNITY
The Lessee agrees to defend, hold harmless, and indemnify the City of Cheyenne, its
officials, employees, agents, and authorized volunteers against any and all claims and costs,
including attorney's fees, arising during or resulting from the Lessee’s performance of its
obligations under this Lease.  The Lessee acknowledges its understanding of this paragraph and
realizes it may have a financial responsibility to the City.  The City does not waive any applicable
defenses and expressly reserves the right to invoke governmental immunity pursuant to the
Wyoming Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. 1-39-101, et seq. for any claim arising out of
this Lease.
It is expressly understood and agreed that although the City has the right to observe and
review the work and operations of the Lessee, this shall not relieve the Lessee from any of its
covenants and obligations hereunder, and the Lessee shall be responsible for and hold the City
and its officers, employees, agents and representatives harmless from all suits, actions or claims
of any character due to injuries or damages sustained by any person or property in consequence
of any breach or of default under this Lease, or of the failure to observe safety standards or
regulations, or otherwise, or through the use of unsafe or unacceptable practices or materials in
the performance of any operations under this Lease, or the Lessee’s failure to comply with any
law, ordinance or regulation.
The parties have executed this Lease the day and year first above written.
