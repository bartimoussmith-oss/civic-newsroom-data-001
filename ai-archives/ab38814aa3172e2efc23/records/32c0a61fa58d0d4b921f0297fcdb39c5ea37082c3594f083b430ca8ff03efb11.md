RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
EXECUTE A WYOMING CULTURAL TRUST FUND GRANT
AGREEMENT FOR RESTORATION OF THE CEMETERY VAULT AT
LAKEVIEW CEMETERY.”
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes “that the
protection, enhancement, and perpetuation of areas, improvements, and landscape features
having historic character or special historical or aesthetic interest or value, is a public necessity;”
and
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Legislature created the Wyoming Cultural Trust Fund
(WCTF) to “preserve and enhance Wyoming’s unique cultural heritage for the citizens of the
state and nation and for future generations to understand and appreciate the heritage that makes
Wyoming unique among the United States;” and
WHEREAS, the WCTF Board has selected the City of Cheyenne Historic Preservation
Board for a grant in the amount of $8,907.00 to assist in paying for restoration of the cemetery
vault building in Lakeview Cemetery and producing interpretive displays to be researched and
designed by the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, Wyoming, that the City of Cheyenne is hereby authorized to accept the
said grant award in the amount of $8,907.00 from the WCTF to be used solely for the restoration
of the cemetery vault in Lakeview Cemetery and producing interpretive displays to be researched
and designed by the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to
execute all documents relating to the grant upon approval of this resolution.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED ON THIS ________ DAY OF
_____________, 2011.

GRANT AWARD RESOLUTION SUMMARY REPORT

Summation

Cemetery Vault Restoration and Interpretation Grant

RESOLUTION:
First Reading, September 26, 2011; Finance Committee October 3, 2011;
Second Reading, October 10, 2011

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS:  All required documents have been submitted and reviewed by
staff.

RECOMMENDATION:  The Urban Planning Office recommends acceptance of the Grant.

The Wyoming Cultural Trust Fund (WCTF) has approved the Cheyenne Historic Preservation
Board’s application for a grant to fund restoration of the cemetery vault at Lakeview Cemetery
and interpretative panels to address both the cemetery’s better know residents and the life and
times of a typical cemetery sexton at the turn of the 20th Century.

The award amount is $8,907, of which $7,032 is for restoration/construction services. Cash
match from the City includes $3,000 from existing cemetery maintenance funds. In-kind match
includes $5,907 in staff time (cemetery maintenance personnel) and volunteer time (Historic
Preservation Board members). Board members will provide project management, architectural
services, and research and design for the interpretative display.

Enclosed:
Application and award letter
