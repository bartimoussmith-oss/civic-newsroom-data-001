RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
June 19, 2012
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in special session at 5:30 p.m. in
Council Chambers for the purpose of acting upon the Fiscal Year 2013 budget appropriation ordinance
on 3 and final reading.  Those present were:  MAYOR: Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
rd
Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.
Absent: Mr. Allen.  Also present were: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City Treasurer; Randy
Hays, Administrative Manager, Board of Public Utilities, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge
of allegiance was recited.
An Ordinance appropriating moneys for the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming for the various
purposes in conducting the municipal government of said City and fixing the amount of general and
special taxes as part of the revenue required to meet the said appropriation, all for the fiscal year
beginning July 1, 2012, and ending June 30, 2013.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Brown
moved to approve the ordinance on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Ms. Ash moved
to amend by removing the following amounts contained within each applicable departmental budget,
which had been included as an anticipated increase in employee health insurance costs during the
upcoming fiscal year, and placing the amount, which totals $279,413.00, in a special projects fund line
item to be identified for economic development, seconded by Ms. Broyles:  $6,460.00 - City Council;
$11,392.00 - Mayor; $12,347.00 - City Clerk; $66,951.00 - Public Works; $69,215.00 - Police;
$61,359.00 - Fire; $31,703.00 - Parks and Recreation; $2,253.00 - City Treasurer; $4,274.00 -
Engineering, and $13,459.00 - Planning Services.  Upon inquiry, Lois Huff, City Treasurer, advised the
funding amounts were included during preliminary preparation of the proposed budget and since the
time the budget was submitted to the governing body the City had received information that there will
be no increase in health insurance costs during the next budget year.  She explained budgeted amounts
for health insurance costs for the current fiscal year, noting an increase in premiums was not as high as
estimated during preparation of the Fiscal Year 2012 budget, and stated the special project line item that
would be created if the motion to amend were approved would be added to the budget book.  Motion
to amend carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.  Main motion as
amended carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.  Appreciation was
expressed to staff for their efforts on preparing the budget.  (#3950)
There being no further business previously noted for the special session, Mayor Kaysen
adjourned the meeting at 5:38 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
City Clerk
This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.
Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle
June 25, 2012
