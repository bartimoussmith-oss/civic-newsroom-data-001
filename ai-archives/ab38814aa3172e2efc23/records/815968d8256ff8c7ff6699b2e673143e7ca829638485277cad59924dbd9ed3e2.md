MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

January 3, 2023
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-9-24 for the Cheyenne Civic Center LED Lighting Retrofit
Equipment project
 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-9-24 was solicited for the Cheyenne Civic Center LED Lighting Retrofit Equipment
project.
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1. Consolidated Electrical Distributors, Cheyenne, Wyoming
The bid was reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Steve Gaer (Facilities), and Steve
Hastings (ES3 Consultants), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from
Consolidated Electrical Distributors.
The total requested bid award is for $331,500.00 for all items requested on the bid, and a 10 year
extended warranty.

Funding:
EECBG grant:
$125,720.00
(Energy Efficiency and Conservation Block Grant
Program)

One Time Funds:
$205,780.00 (approved by the Mayor)
(One-time distribution of the State received by the General
Fund – and is being transferred to the Civic Center fund)

5th Penny Funds:
May be utilized to fund a portion of this project, or the
subsequent installation costs for the equipment.
