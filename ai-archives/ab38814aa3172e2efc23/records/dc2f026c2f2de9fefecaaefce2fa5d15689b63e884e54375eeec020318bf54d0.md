WYOMING:

Section 1.
That the City of Cheyenne hereby finds as follows:

(a)
That WJE, LLC, owner of the parcel of land being situated in a portion of the E½
of Section 25, T. 14N., R. 66W., 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming, has
petitioned the governing body of the City of Cheyenne to annex the real property
described herein and to make it a part thereof; and
(b)
That an accurate annexation map of said land is attached to this ordinance as
Exhibit A, said map prepared by Jeffery B. Jones, a Professional Land Surveyor,
registered in the State of Wyoming, PLS Number 5910; and
(c)
That the annexation of said area is for the protection of health, safety, and welfare
of the persons residing in the area and the City of Cheyenne; and
(d)
That the urban development of the area sought to be annexed will constitute a
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the City of Cheyenne; and
(e)
That the area is a logical and feasible addition to the City and that the extension of
basic services such as water and sewer systems, police and fire protection and
other services customarily available in the City of Cheyenne can, within reason,
be furnished; and
(f)
That the area is adjacent to the City of Cheyenne and is currently zoned A-1
Agricultural and Rural Residential – by the Laramie County Commissioners; and
(g)
That the City of Cheyenne does not own or operate its own electrical utility, and
its governing body is prepared to designate a utility to serve the annexed area
pursuant to W.S. 15-1-410, and to authorize the designated utility to serve the
entire annexed area.

Section 2.
That all of the real property described in Exhibit A is hereby annexed to
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and shall become a part thereof.

Section 3.
That the City Clerk is directed to file an executed copy of this annexation
ordinance and map (Exhibit A) with the County Clerk and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for
Laramie County, Wyoming, and shall also notify the City Engineer's Office to add said area to
the official City Map of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.

Section 4.
Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any phrase, clause, sentence, paragraph or section of this ordinance is declared
illegal or unconstitutional, such illegality or unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the
remaining phrases, clauses, sentences, paragraphs and sections.

Section 5.
That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its publication.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This annexation application is for about sixty-five (65) acres of land north of and adjacent to E.
Pershing Boulevard, west of Christensen Road. This application was submitted along with a zone
change application from County A-1 Agricultural and Rural Residential to MR-2 Medium Density
Residential-Developing. Eventually, a final plat application will need to come before you before
construction can begin.

PlanCheyenne is supportive of this sort of development and annexing it into the City. This is
evidenced by it being designated Urban Residential in the future land use plan as well as it being
located within the Urban Service Boundary. The City has also been actively involved in the
potential development of Christiansen Road underscoring the expected City growth into this area.
PlanCheyenne is also supportive of its location near existing and proposed schools as well as the
proposed Greenway extension through the project.

As with any expansion at the edge of the City, this development will impact services within the
City. This can be seen with the Police Department’s comments relating to additional officers
needed to continue to provide the same level of service. Similar impacts are expected with fire
protection, sanitation services, streets, and parks maintenance. In general, there are a couple
options with City services. These options include maintaining the same levels of service by
making new investments as the City expands or to not make new, additional investments and to
reduce the levels of service across the City. With regard to making investments to maintain levels
of service, the options are for the tax base as a whole to make those additional investments for
the new development, for new development to contribute to the new investments, or some
combination of the two.

With this annexation and subsequent development, it is expected that the area will be developed
according to standards of the Unified Development Code (UDC). It is also expected that all
utilities will be stubbed to the edges of the project to allow future development on neighboring
properties to occur.

Due to meeting the review criteria found in Wyoming Statute 15-1-402, staff
recommends approval of this annexation with the following conditions:
1) All technical revisions stated by staff in the official comments are addressed
(City Surveyor & Development Department)
2) E. Pershing Boulevard from the current City Limits to the farthest point of this
annexation be included as part of this annexation.

PlanCheyenne:

This annexation and subsequent platting and zone change are consistent with PlanCheyenne. On
the future land use map found in PlanCheyenne, the subject area has been designated as Urban
Residential. The Urban Residential land use designation is for a broad variety of housing types
and densities. It also calls for development within the Urban Service Boundary, in which this plat
is located. Urban Residential also contemplates complimentary uses being integrated within the
neighborhoods to serve the community. This 8th Filing is near both an existing elementary school
and a proposed elementary school. It is also near parks and trails to serve the neighborhood.

ANNEXATION ELIGIBILITY DATA/ANALYSIS: Before any territory is eligible for annexation, the
governing body must find that the following criteria are met (W.S. § 15-1-402 (a)):

COMMON WELFARE – An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety, and welfare
of the persons residing in the area and in the city or town.

The annexation of this property will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the residents of
the development once it is built. By being in City Limits, the residential will receive access to City
Fire and Police services. The residents will also be hooked to City Water and Sanitary Sewer
instead of having wells and septic systems.

PART OF THE CITY – The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would be constituted a
natural, geographic, economic, and social part of the annexing city or town.

This subdivision, once developed, will constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part
of the city. The contiguity to the west makes the parcel a natural and geographic park of the
City. The residents will work likely work in the Cheyenne area and will be a part of the social and
economic fabric that is Cheyenne. Christiansen Road is planned to be a major north/south
connection that would tie this area into the employment center known as Cheyenne Business
Parkway, which is also in the City.

LOGICAL EXPANSION – The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the
annexing city or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of
the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed.

The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City
services normally available to residents will also be available to this location. As with any
expansion at the edge of the City, it will typically require the expansion of City services. This is
referenced by the Police Department’s comment. Similar impacts are expected for services such
as fire protection, sanitation services, street maintenance, and park maintenance.

CONTIGUOUS – The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or
town, or the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407.

The parcel proposed for annexation is about 31% contiguous, all along the western edge of the
proposed product. This meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407.

UTILITIES – If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to service the annexed area pursuant to W.S.
15-1-4102.

Utility franchises are in place to serve the parcel.
