RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 20, 2019

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:02 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Cook, Mr.
Laybourn, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, and Mr. White. Absent:  Mr. Case, Mr. Esquibel, and
Mr.  Shanor.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Alessandra McCoy Fakelman, Senior Assistant
City Attorney; Logan Sharpe, Assistant City Attorney; Ron Downey, Human Resources Deputy
Director, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.
The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook live.
Mayor Orr explained it is the governing body’s intention to go into executive session for the
purpose of evaluating proposed remaining sections.

Mayor Orr provided a review of previous meetings.

Bill Hinz, Union President, provided information and a review of the negotiation process
to date and noted details relating to retirement benefits, use of sick leave, E.O.D, I.O.D, training,
and overtime.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Hinz advised it is the Union’s belief that management is in
control of overtime.  Mr. Hinz advised the Union does not desire to go into arbitration and proposes
the following:  Section 4-agreement with a one or two year contract term, Section 15-add three
members from 88 to 91 in FY 2020 and increase shift levels from 26 to 27 for FY 2020 beginning
on July 1, 2019, Section 17-reoffer Kelly Day proposal reducing hours worked per week from 56-
52, Section 18-accept City’s language amendment and remaining Proposed Contract Language
(PCL), Section 20-PCL, Section 21-reduce work hours per week from 56 to 52 and 3.0% wage
increases for FY 2020 beginning July 1, 2019 and by adding that members would receive any
additional bonuses in the form of money or time off offered to non-uniformed employees, Section
22-accept City offer that no member shall be granted overtime while on vacation and 480 hours of
E.O.D compensatory time instead of 240 hours, Section 23-reoffer Union’s last proposal including
amendment to Letter F-changing the acting roster for Day Personnel positions to four years instead
of three, Section 27-add “Battalion Chief of the Shift” in Section G and remaining PCL, Section
28-reoffer Union’s last proposal and accept City’s language in Paragraphs 1, 2, and 3 remaining
PCL, and Section 43-reoffer Union’s original offer.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Roybal moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body
present, the City Clerk, Senior Assistant City Attorney, Assistant City Attorney, City Treasurer,
Human Resources Deputy Director, and Chief of the Fire and Rescue Department would also be
