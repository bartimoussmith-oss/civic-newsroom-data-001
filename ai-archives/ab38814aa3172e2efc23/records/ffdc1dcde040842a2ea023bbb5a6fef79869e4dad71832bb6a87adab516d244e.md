27. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a zone change from AR - Agricultural
Residential to LI - Light Industrial for the north 116 feet of Tracts 143
and 144, Sunnyside Addition, 5th Filing, Laramie County, WY.
24 - 284
WC Investments, Inc, Zone Change
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said AVI, PC, on behalf of WC Investments, Inc, submitted a
zone change for 4919 Cheyenne St. He said the application was submitted for zone
change from AR - Agricultural Residential to LI - Light Industrial to allow for an RV
Storage Facility.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of zone change
with the adoption of findings of fact a and b.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Malm, seconded by Commissioner
Thompson, to approve the zone change from AR – Agricultural Residential
to LI – Light Industrial for the North 116 feet of Tracts 143 and 144,
Sunnyside Addition, 5th Filing, with no conditions, and adopt the findings
of fact a and b of the staff report.  The motion carried by the following
vote:
Lovett, Malm, Heath, Holmes, Thompson
