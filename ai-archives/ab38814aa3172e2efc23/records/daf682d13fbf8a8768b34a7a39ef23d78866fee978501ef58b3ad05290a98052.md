SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND
HOLLYFRONTIER CHEYENNE RENEWABLE DIESEL COMPANY, LLC

1.
Parties. The parties to this Agreement are the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipality
organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming
82001 (“City”), and HollyFrontier Cheyenne Renewable Diesel Company, LLC, a limited liability
company duly organized under the laws of the State of Delaware, 300 Morrie Avenue, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82007 (“HollyFrontier”).

2.
Purpose of the Agreement.  The purpose of this Agreement is to designate HollyFrontier
as the Presenting Sponsor of the 2022 Superday, the 2022 Superday Tour de Prairie, and the 2022
Superday 5K Walk/Run, a Cheyenne Community Recreation and Events Summer Celebration
which is annually hosted in June to kick off Recreation month in July.  This Agreement designates
HollyFrontier as the Presenting Sponsor with no liability implied, expressed, or written at any
event.

3.
Term.  The term of this Agreement will be from the date of the last signature affixed until
June 30, 2022.

4.
Payment.  The City of Cheyenne will provide HollyFrontier an invoice to process
payment directly to the vendor.  HollyFrontier agrees to pay the City the amount of Twenty-Five
Thousand Dollars ($25,000.00) pursuant to the terms of this Agreement.

5.
City’s Responsibilities.

A.
As the Presenting Sponsor of the 2022 Superday, the 2022 Superday Tour de
Prairie, and the 2022 Superday Family Fun 5K Walk/Run, in exchange for Twenty-Five Thousand
Dollars ($25,000.00) of sponsorship revenue to the City, HollyFrontier will receive:

i.
Brand recognition as the presenting sponsor on all print (posters, brochures,
banners, registration forms, and newspaper advertising), radio, social media (Facebook,
www.CheyenneEvents.org, www.CheyenneRec.org), and television recognition as the
Presenting Sponsor of the 2022 Superday, the 2022 Superday Tour de Prairie, and the 2022
Superday Family 5K Walk/Run;

ii.
Two main entry locations with tents, tables, chairs, and banner recognizing
HollyFrontier as the Presenting Sponsor;

iii.
Superday brochures for HollyFrontier employees to distribute to and engage
with participants;

By Stefanie Boster at 1:28 pm, Feb 23, 2022

6.
General Provisions.

A.
Amendments.  Any changes, modifications, revisions, or amendments to this
Agreement, which are mutually agreed upon by the parties, will be incorporated by writing.

B.
Applicable Law.  The construction, interpretation, and enforcement of this
Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State of Wyoming
shall have jurisdiction over this Agreement and parties, and the venue shall be in the First Judicial
District, Laramie County, Wyoming.

C.
Compliance with Laws.  HollyFrontier agrees to keep informed of and comply
with all applicable federal, state, and local laws and regulations in the performance of this
Agreement.

D.
Notice.  All notices arising out of or from the provisions of this Agreement shall
be in writing and given to the parties either by regular mail or delivery in person.

E.
Termination.  Either party may termination this Agreement for cause without
liability in the event either party fails to perform.

F.
Governmental Immunity.  The City of Cheyenne, along with its officials and
employees, does not waive its governmental immunity by entering into this Agreement, except to
the extent necessary for the parties to pursue a contract action to clarify or enforce the written
terms of the Agreement.  Furthermore, the City specifically retain all immunities and defenses
available to it as a sovereign or governmental entity pursuant to Wyo. Stat. § 1-39-101, et seq.,
and all other relevant state and federal law. Designations of venue, choice of law, enforcement
actions, and similar provisions should not be constructed as a waiver of governmental immunity.
The parties agree that any ambiguity in this Agreement shall not be strictly construed, either against
or for either party, except that any ambiguity as to governmental or sovereign immunity shall be
construed in favor of governmental immunity.

G.
Indemnification.  Each party to this Agreement shall assume the risk of any
liability arising from its own conduct.  Neither party agrees to insure, defend, or indemnify the
other.
