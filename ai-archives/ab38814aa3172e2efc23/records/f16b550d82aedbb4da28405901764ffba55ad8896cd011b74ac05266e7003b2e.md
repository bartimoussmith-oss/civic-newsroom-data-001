TELECOMMUNICATIONS RIGHT-OF-WAY LICENSE AND USE AGREEMENT
BETWEEN AT&T CORP. AND THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

1.
Parties.  The parties to this Agreement are AT&T Corp., whose address is 3450 Riverwood
Parkway, Room 162, Atlanta, Georgia 30339 (“Company”), and the City of Cheyenne, a
municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, whose address
is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”).

2.
Purpose of Agreement.  To permit the Company’s limited use of the public right-of-way in
the Public Right-of-Way Area for the installation and operation of Facilities in accordance
with this Agreement.  For the purposes of this Agreement only, the terms below are defined
as follows:
a.
The term “City” means the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation
organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming.
b.
The term “Company” means the entity named above which is authorized to do
business in the State of Wyoming.
c.
The term “Communications Services” means terrestrial based transport and
delivery of electronic communications and includes telecommunication services
and internet services.
d.
The term “Existing Installations” means Facilities installed previously to the
Effective Date of this Agreement.  Existing Installations are identified in Exhibit A,
attached to this Agreement and incorporated herein by this reference.
e.
The term “Facilities” means the Company conduits, raceways, cables, fiber, hand
holes (vaults), pedestals, cabinets, and any and all other appurtenances which make
up a fiberoptic communication systems which are or will be installed at locations
specified in a permit application within the Public Right-of-Way Area.
f.
The term “New Installations” means Facilities installed after the Effective Date of
this Agreement.
g.
The term “Street” means the entire width between the boundary lines of every way
dedicated to the public use, and publicly maintained for the purposes of vehicular
travel, including alleys.
h.
The term “Public Right-of-Way Area” means the limited geographic areas where
the Company is authorized to use the City’s public right-of-way pursuant to permits
authorized by the City in accordance with Chapters 12.04 and 12.12 of the
Cheyenne City Code. The City Engineer may refuse to authorize a permit for any
reason.

Company’s Facilities:
a.
All standard permitting fees for plan review, permitting and construction as required

b.
An annual seventy-five cents ($.75) per linear foot of conduit or related Facility in
the Public Right-of-Way Area (“Use Fee”), due beginning January 1, 2020, for all
Existing Installations.  The Use Fee for New Installations shall be due once as-builts
are completed pursuant to Section 5(g) and shall thereafter be payable on an annual
basis of seventy-five cents ($.75) per linear foot of conduit or related Facility in the
Public Right of Right-of-Way Area commencing on the first day of January
following the initial installation.

The fees outlined in this Section 4 are the exclusive fees for the Company’s use of
the City’s public right-of-way for the installation, operation and use of Facilities

Company or are due from the Company for the Company’s use of the City’s right-
of-way. The fees outlined in this section are intended to be in lieu of the Company
obtaining a franchise and paying franchise fees.

Company does not waive it rights under state and federal law, both now and in the
future.

The Facilities shall be located, constructed and maintained so that none of the

upon completion, at its own expense, replace and restore the Street(s) in accordance
with standards for such work set by City Code and by the City Engineer. In the
event any Street(s) become uneven, unsettled or otherwise require repair due to
disturbance by the Company, as determined solely by the City Engineer, the
Company shall immediately repair or restore the Street(s) in compliance with the
ordinances and standards of the City at the Company’s expense.
