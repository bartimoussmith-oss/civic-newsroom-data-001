ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary. Development will
require infrastructure improvements and investment.

Policies in PlanCheyenne encourage industrial development near regional transportation routes
and on the edges of the community (where they can be buffered from the residential and
customer-based commercial uses).

While the current future land use map would not support LI zoning, the Comprehensive plan is
meant to be a guide and is encouraged to be responsive to market demands. It was originally
thought that this area would be optimal for largely residential development, but there had been
no true development proposals for the land. The land is within the urban service boundary and
would be expected to develop within City limits.

A concurrent PlanCheyenne amendment proposes changing the property to Mixed-use
Employment for future light industrial development.  The proposed zoning establishes LI for a
portion of the area for immediate development while zoning the remainder as AG as an interim
holding zone, and bringing forward the CB zone from the County areas currently zoned County
CB.

The proposed zoning allows for establishment of a new business park with regional connectivity
to I-25 and U.S. Highway 85.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1, with conditions.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

A majority of the surrounding property is largely vacant with limited use. The future land use
map and comprehensive plan speculated that this area would develop with largely residential
uses, but considered a mix of commercial uses possible. These speculations did not have any
true development proposals behind them. The large acreage of the site would allow for buffers
to adjacent property and surrounding areas could still develop with largely residential
development patterns. The proposed zoning of LI only allows limited industrial uses, which can
be compatible with nearby residential uses.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 2.

3)
The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of

The property currently has a County zone, but in order to develop within the City, a City zone is

zones. The proposed LI zone district requires screening and buffering along street rights-of-ways
and district boundaries. Typically, Cheyenne LEADS business parks attract large tenants and
develop in accordance with applicable landscaping and screening standards.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

Block sizes are not applicable in areas designated mixed-use employment or industrial. Access
may be limited based on street classification and proximity to the interstate, but specific access
would be reviewed with plat(s) or site plan(s). Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the
region, but will need to be extended to effectively service the subject property. A transportation
network plan submitted with the project demonstrates the ability for the project to meet
over-arching transportation goals.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The CB zone is intended for regional-serving commercial uses. Large acreage to allow larger
businesses is appropriate. The CB zone includes design standards. The area proposed to be
zoned CB by this zone change is close to regional transportation routes and development should
be able to meet all design standards.

The AG zone is intended to keep land in a natural state, but is also appropriate as a holding zone
until more specific development proposals are made. This zone district has no design standards.
The area proposed to be zoned AG is appropriate as a holding zone.

The LI district is intended for controlled manufacturing or intense employment uses
that may have accessory commercial, service or office uses, but which cannot readily fit into
other general business or mixed-use areas due to the nature or intensity of operations.
The uses and building and site designs in this district should not produce impacts beyond the
property lines. The LI zone should be in areas which have access to regional infrastructure and
space to provide buffers to neighboring non-industrial uses. There are no design standards in
the LI zone. The area proposed to be zoned LI meets these expectations.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 5.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC

The area proposed to be zoned LI does abut existing residential areas to the east and southeast.
However, there is anticipated to be roads between the subject property and the residential
areas, and the size of the property allows for buffering between the LI use of the land and these
existing residential areas. A Greenway is also located here on the future Greenway Map, and
may help provide a buffer. City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not
adversely affect the public health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 8.
