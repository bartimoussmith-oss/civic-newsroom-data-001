RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 January 28, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr.
Valdez and Ms. Williams.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and
Dan White, City Attorney.
Mayor Kaysen noted clerical corrections on the numbering of items (2) contained on the first
page of the meeting agenda.  Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA]
are considered to be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There
will be no separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and
support by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be
considered in its normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Brown moved to approve Consent Agenda
items, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on January 14, 2013.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest in voucher payable to Ameri-Tech
HVAC Services) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Valdez moved to approve payment of
vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Allen.  Upon inquiry, information was provided by Mayor
Kaysen and applicable staff on the following vouchers presented for payment:  Alsco ($1,388.79),
laundry services for floor mats, shirts, uniforms, etc.; Belfor Property Restoration ($2,468.20),
emergency boarding-up of an abandoned property located near an elementary school involving
unacceptable activities (City seeking restitution from the property owner); Brown’s Shoe Fit Co.
($249.98), steel-toed safety boots and foot ware (re: employee clothing allowance program approved
for certain departments); Employers Council Services Inc. ($164.63), expenses affiliated with work
place investigation involving a personnel matter; Gametime ($1,725.36), slide replacement in Sun
Valley Park due to vandalism; High West Energy ($25.20), utility (electric) bill for welcome to
Cheyenne sign at Archer Interchange; Mingles Lounge ($100.00), refund of business license
application fee due to non-approval of application; VISA/UMB ($462.66), credit card charge to Bird-
X, Inc. for decoys to discourage geese presence in parks.  Staff providing information on the
aforementioned vouchers were: Jim Elias, Public Works Director; Rick Parish, Parks and Recreation
Director; Nathan Beauheim, Acting City Engineer; Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and Carol Intlekofer,
City Clerk.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present in Council
Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown returned to Council Chambers.

to approve in an amount not to exceed $78,760.00, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Upon inquiry, Tom
Mason, MPO Director, explained the role of the MPO Policy Committee in budgeting, reviewing
and setting priorities of street projects, and explained that if a project isn’t funded for a specific
budget year, it may be reconsidered for possible inclusion on a future project list.  He noted the
Committee meets annually to discuss the MPO budget as well as on a quarterly basis.  In response
to query concerning the status of Parsley Blvd. being included on a project list relative to a past
study, Mr. Mason advised that a 2004 study looked at the street network generally for all of south
Cheyenne, including this area, and stated that Parsley Blvd. will be looked at in the future in more
detail.  He explained the contract will assist with traffic calibration information.  Motion carried.
Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Luna voting “no”.
