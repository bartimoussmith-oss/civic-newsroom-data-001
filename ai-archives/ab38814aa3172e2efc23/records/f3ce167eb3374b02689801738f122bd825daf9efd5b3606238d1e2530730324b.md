MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

May 4, 2022
SUBJECT:
Request to Purchase Used Brush Truck Style Vehicle, for the City of Cheyenne,
Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department
______________________________________________
BACKGROUND INFORMATION ABOUT PURPOSE OF PROPOSED PURCHASE:
From Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department:
In light of Boulder County and many other municipalities throughout the Western United States,
the need for quick response/attack is crucial during these wind driven events. We have seen a
dramatic increase in wind driven events and brush fires within municipalities and Cheyenne.
Increased development in areas that are otherwise prairie and open in nature are prime locations
for these type of events. Places like Whitney Ranch, Saddle Ridge, Sweetgrass, Swan Ranch, Bison
Business Park and many others to name a few. Time is crucial and if we are capable of a quick
response, we have a better opportunity to prevent major loss. We will still be reliant upon our
mutual aid partners for all of these types of events and already have agreements established.

An investment in alternate vehicles (i.e. brush trucks and/or mini pumpers) for these types of
events is quite worth the investment. Current loses within Boulder and Boulder County have
exceeded 513 million dollars. The use of the vehicle would be cross staffed when we are dispatched
to these events.

PROCUREMENT INFORMATION & PROCESS:
The City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department, is requesting spending authority in an
amount not-to-exceed $150,000.00 for the purchase of a used Brush Truck.  Due to the current
market for used vehicles and equipment, timing is of the essence when a used piece of equipment
becomes available on the used vehicle market.

In accordance with the City of Cheyenne Purchasing Policy, “Selection will be made only from
vehicles or equipment meeting or exceeding the minimum requirements set forth by the
department/division. If the amount exceeds $35,000.00 the expenditure must be approved by the
Governing Body.”

This is a preemptive request for approval by the Governing Body, to grant spending authority when
a used vehicle that meets all of the minimum specifications is located & identified.  In order to
ensure that the City can make an offer on a vehicle in a timely manner, without placing a
Consideration of Award on City Council Agenda (3-4 Week Process), the Cheyenne Fire Rescue
