Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the Whitney Ranch, 3rd Filing Final Plat, subject to

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

UDC 2.1.3 Final Plat

UDC Article 4 Subdivisions

Plan Cheyenne

BACKGROUND:

This is a request to final plat 107 single-unit residential lots, with associated open space and detention
lots, and public rights-of-way. The lots were preliminary platted as part of the Whitney Ranch, 3rd Filing
preliminary plat which was approved by the Governing Body on July 27, 2020. Pursuant to UDC
2.1.2.c.6.a preliminary plats remain active 18 months after their approval or since the last final plat was
approved within the preliminary plat.

Concurrent applications for an annexation and a zone change to MR Medium Density Residential have also
been submitted.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of final plat, the Commission must make
the following findings required in UDC 2.1.3.d a detailed analysis is below. The purpose of a final plat
review is to ensure compliance with the approved preliminary plat and its conditions of approval.

1) The layout and design of the final plat is in substantial compliance with the approved
preliminary plat considering the number of lots or parcels, street and block layout, and access,
and any deviations in the final plat brings the application in further compliance with the
Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.3.d.1)

The layout of the Final Plat is identical to the approved Preliminary Plat in dedicated rights-of-
way. The open space lots to the northwest and east have been reduced in size, and a detention
pond has been relocated. The relocation of the detention pond removed six lots from Block 2,
but added six lots to Block 4. The overall number of lots and sizes of the residential lots are
substantially the same as with the approved preliminary plat. The applicant has indicated that
the reason for moving the detention pond is to provide adequate detention capacity, which may
not have been possible in the original location.

Adequate detention areas are encouraged by the Comprehensive Plan.

The application complies with this review criterion.

Open Space Systems

The open space narrative submitted with the Preliminary Plat application indicated the
development proposed with this plat would provide trail corridors and natural areas. For
residential development, open space is required at 750 square feet per dwelling unit or 8%
of the gross plat area (whichever is less). This plat proposes 107 single-unit parcels and has a
gross acreage of 48.6 acres. The required open space is 80,250sqft. Although the size of the
natural areas have decreased with the final plat submittal, the natural areas provided with
the Final Plat still exceed the minimum open space area required.
Required Improvements and Engineering Specifications

This proposed subdivision appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the
properties within this development.
