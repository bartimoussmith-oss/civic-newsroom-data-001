MEMORANDUM
TO:

The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

August 7, 2023
SUBJECT:
Re-Appropriation to be considered for approval

On August 14, 2023, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee
to re-appropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2023 City of Cheyenne Budget per W.S. § 16-4-
112 and 16-4-113.  More information is detailed below regarding this final FY 2023 re-
appropriation request.

GENERAL FUND RESERVES REQUEST
The following General Fund reserve requests are included for your consideration:
City Council Health Insurance Coverage Changes
Members of the City Council made changes to their health insurance coverages during last fiscal
year that were not included in the budget.  I am asking for $20,745 from reserves to pay for the
increase to this line item.
Vacation Accrual Payouts for Terminated Employees
During FY 2023, the City has paid out several sick and vacation accrual leave balances once an
employee terminated their employment.  Generally, accrual payouts are not budgeted for unless a
retirement is anticipated.  If the position is replaced immediately, the budget is not enough to cover
the accrual payout.  I am requesting a total increase of $57,572 from reserves to cover these
expenses.
Increase in Natural Gas and Electricity and Fleet Fuel, Labor, and Parts Costs
My department estimates the natural gas and electricity costs for city buildings when the budget is
developed each year.   In December 2022 and January 2023, extremely frigid temperatures across
the nation forced homes and businesses to increase their heat during those two months, which sent
the price of natural gas soaring.  The average spot price for natural gas at the Opal Hub surpassed
$26 per thousand cubic feet for more than a month last winter.  Normal levels are $3.98 per
thousand cubic feet.  As a result, the City’s projections for natural gas costs were inadequate.
Therefore, I am requesting $93,700 from General Fund reserves to cover this shortage.

Additionally, the City’s expenditures for fuel, labor and parts have exceeded the approved FY
2023 budget for several General Fund departments.  I am requesting an increase of $131,323 from
General Fund reserves to cover this budget shortfall.
Vandalism Expenses
Throughout the fiscal year, the Facilities Maintenance, Forestry, Aquatics, Golf Courses, Parks,
Cemetery, and Clean and Safe Divisions have been forced to pay for unforeseen vandalism
expenses to City assets.   These expenses were not budgeted for, as it is difficult to estimate
these types of costs when the budget is initially prepared.  I am requesting an increase of
$45,639 from reserves to cover these costs.
Insurance Claims
Repair costs due to accidents are unpredictable; therefore, when the budget is initially prepared
each year, I do not forecast the City’s portion of loss claims. Instead, I choose to handle these
expenditures through a budget reappropriation at the end of the fiscal year.
