SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-26-20 for furnishing One (1) New 2020-2021 Motorized
Articulated Trash Compactor with straight blade, with heavy duty trash guarding,
for the City of Cheyenne, Landfill
______________________________________________
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The One (1) bid was received from:

1. Wyoming Machinery Company (Cheyenne, Wyoming)

The bid was reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), Matt
Theriault (Public Works), and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid, from Wyoming Machinery
Company.  The bid award is for the following:

1. Purchase of One (1) New 2020-2021 Motorized Articulated Trash Compactor with straight
blade, with heavy duty trash guarding ($495,000.00)
2. Optional Auto-Lube System ($10,461.64)
3. Acceptance of Trade-In Offer for Block #5752 (-$2,000.00)
4. Acceptance of Trade-In Offer for Block #5608 (-$20,000.00).

The total, plus option, less trade-ins, is $483,461.64.

Funding:
023-14-1424-43-43102 (Solid Waste Fund - Equipment)

$483,461.64
