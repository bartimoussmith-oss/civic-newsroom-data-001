Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

March 20, 2023
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-13-24 for Three (3) Motorcycles for the City of Cheyenne
Police Department
 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-13-24 was solicited for Motorcycles for the City of Cheyenne Police Department.

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1. PS CO1, LLC. D/B/A Northern Colorado Powersports
2. IOF Inc.

The bids were reviewed by Dan Long (Cheyenne Police Department), and Bryan Bell (Fleet
Maintenance), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from PS CO1,
LLC. D/B/A Northern Colorado Powersports.

The total requested bid award is for $76,493.34 for the purchase of Three (3) Motorcycles.

Funding:
020-26-2613-41-43201 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Motor Vehicles)
