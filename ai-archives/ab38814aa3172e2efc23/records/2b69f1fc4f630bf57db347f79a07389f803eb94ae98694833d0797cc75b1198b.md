RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

October 21, 2022

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session on this date beginning
at 12:00 p.m. at 2101 O’Neil Ave. Room 104 and via electronic conference meeting, for the
purpose of collective labor bargaining with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International
Association of Fire Fighters, Local No. 279.  Present were: MAYOR – Patrick Collins, COUNCIL
MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Bryan Cook, Ken Esquibel, Richard Johnson (via Zoom), Dr.
Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, and Jeff White.  Absent: Pete Laybourn and Tom Segrave.  Also
present: Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Collins advised the special session was for the purpose of re-negotiating the existing
contract labor agreement between the City of Cheyenne and the Fire Union to address issues
involving the Fair Labor Standards Act.  Chief John Kopper, Cheyenne Fire & Rescue Department,
provided details relating to the Ground Rules for the special session and advised the only Sections
being addressed were 4, 17, 22 and 26.   The proposed Ground Rules were agreed upon and signed
by the City and Union.  Vincent McQueeney, spokesperson for the Cheyenne Professional Fire
Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local No. 279, provided an offer to the city
which rectifies issues relating to the Fair Labor Standards Act.  The city announced acceptance of
the Union’s offer.  Mayor Collins advised the revised contract would come before the governing
body in November 2022 and would become effective January 7, 2023 if approved.

There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was
officially adjourned at 12:12 p.m.

Submitted by,

Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk

This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle

October 27, 2022
