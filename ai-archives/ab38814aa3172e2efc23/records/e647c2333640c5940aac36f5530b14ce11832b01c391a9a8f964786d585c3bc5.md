DEPARTMENT OF THE AIR FORCE
90TH MISSILE WING (AFGSC)

DETER • MODERNIZE • STRIKE

VIA FIRST CLASS MAIL, RRR & ELECTRONIC MAIL

Bryan Cook, Chairman
City of Cheyenne Public Services Committee
City of Chyenne, WY  82001

Dear Chairman Cook

The Air Force is in receipt of the attached September 25, 2023, Memo from the City
Planning and Development Department to Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City
Council (attached).  Thus, the Air Force is now aware that the City of Cheyenne has initiated
official annexation proceedings to annex Francis E. Warren Air Force Base (AFB).
When an Air Force installation becomes aware of a proposed annexation, certain
notifications and reporting requirements are triggered.  See Air Force Instruction 32-9002:
Management of Real Property, (18 Dec. 2020), accessible at:  https://static.e-
publishing.af.mil/production/1/saf_ie/publication/afi32-9002/afi32-9002.pdf.  The base and other
designated components across the country are required to prepare reports evaluating the
proposed annexation.  Those reports are then routed to the ultimate decision-maker on proposed
annexations, the Air Force’s Deputy Assistant Secretary, Environment, Safety and Infrastructure
(SAF/IEI).
The Air Force respectfully requests that the Public Services Commission, the Governing
Body, and all City departments defer consideration of the proposed annexation until such time as
the Air Force can properly consider and make a decision on the City’s proposal. Additionally, to
assist the Air Force’s review of the proposed annexation, I kindly request the City provide its
annexation report (such as the report required by Wyo Stat § 15-1-402(c)), an explanation why
the annexation is being proposed, the supporting information for the proposal and a point of
contact for the City to assist in the information exchange for the Air Force’s evaluation of the
potential impacts.  My point of contact for this matter is Kurt Mabis, in the base legal office, who
can be reached at kurt.mabis.2@us.af.mil or 307-773-2256.

Based on a current lack of necessary information to evaluate the proposed annexation, it
is presently unclear how long the Air Force may need to evaluate the proposed annexation.
Hopefully, we will be able to update you on the status of the Air Force’s review as we obtain
more information, are able to perform necessary analysis and proceed through the Air Force’s
process.
