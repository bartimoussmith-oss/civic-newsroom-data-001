RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

March 11, 2024

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers and via electronic conference meeting.  Present were: MAYOR
– Patrick Collins, COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Bryan Cook, Ken Esquibel,
Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Tom Segrave and Jeff White .
Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda).  Upon request by Mr. Cook, and pursuant to support received by
Mr. White and Mr. Johnson, items #17 & 18 were removed from the Consent Agenda. Upon
request by Dr. Aldrich, and pursuant to support received by Mr. Johnson and Mr. Cook, item
#24(a) was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Esquibel moved to approve remaining items,
seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.

[CA] Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on February 26, 2024.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.

 Mayor Collins opened a public hearing to determine whether compliance of annexation
conditions required by Wyoming Statute §15- 1-402 exist for about 923.93 acres of land located
south of I-80 adjacent to Roundtop Road.  Comment was made by the following member of the
governing body: Dr. Aldrich. There being no further comment or input and purpose of the hearing
having been met, Mayor Collins closed the public hearing.

 ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Amending certain sections of Chapter 44, Solid Waste,
and Chapter 56, Noise Control, of Title 8, Health and Safety, and further amending certain sections
of Chapter 8, Offenses Against Public Peace and Decency, and Chapter 12, Offenses Against
Property, of Title 9, Public Peace and Welfare, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, to eliminate the potential imposition of a jail sentence for violation of specified code
provisions. Reporting for Finance Committee, Dr. Aldrich advised there was no recommendation
from committee. Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. White.
Dr. Aldrich moved to amend by substitute dated February 27, 2024, seconded by Mr. Segrave.
Motion to amend by substitute carried .  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body. Dr.
Rinne moved to amend by striking “shall” and adding “should to the maximum extent practical”
to #6 on page 5, seconded by Dr. Aldrich. Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members
of the governing body.  Dr. Aldrich moved to amend by striking “and sanitary” in lines 2 & 3 in
#4 on page 4, seconded by Mr. Segrave.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members
of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Cook and Mr. Johnson voting “no”.  Main motion
as amended by substitute, as twice amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body.  Comments were made by the following members of the governing body:  Dr. Aldrich, Mr.
Johnson and Dr. Rinne. (#4553)

 ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Creating Section 2.40.100, Public Defender and
Representation of Needy Persons; Determination of Need, of Chapter 2.40, Municipal Court, of
Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming,
Specifying Public Defender Appointment Procedures and Standards for Representation in the
Municipal Court.  Dr. Aldrich advised there was no recommendation from Finance Committee.
Dr. Aldrich moved to approve on third reading, seconded by Mr . White. Dr. Aldrich move d to
amend by substitute dated February 27, 2024, seconded by Mr. White. Motion to amend by
substitute carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mr.
Johnson voting “no”.  Main motion as amended by substitute carried. Voting “yes” – all members
of the governing body with the exception of Mr. Johnson voting “no”.  Comments were made by
the following members of the governing body: Dr. Aldrich and Mr. Johnson. (#4554)

 ORDINANCE –2nd READING – Amending Section 10.12.160, Authority to Impound
Illegally Parked Vehicles, and Section 10.12.170, Immobilization, of Chapter 10.12,
Administration and Enforcement, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the Municipal Code of the
City of Cheyenne , Wyoming, modifying specified enforcement provisions regarding vehicle
impoundment.  Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr. Roybal moved to approve on second
reading, seconded by Mr. Segrave.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body.  Comments were made by the following members of the governing body: Mayor Collins
and Mr. Laybourn.

 ORDINANCE –1st READING – Amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of
Cheyenne changing the zoning classification from MR Medium -Density Residential to NR -2
Neighborhood Residential – Medium Density for land located south of West 5 th Street between
Patton Avenue and Snyder Avenue  – referred to Public Services Committee.   (Sponsor – Mr.
Roybal)

 ORDINANCE –1st READING – Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, land
located southwest of the I-25 and I-80 Interchange adjacent to Roundtop Rd. – referred to Public
Services Committee.  (Sponsor – Mr. Roybal)

 ORDINANCE –1st READING – Amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of
Cheyenne establishing the zoning classification of AG Agricultural, LR Low-Density Residential,
MR Medium -Density Residential, HI Heavy Industrial, and CB Community Business for land
annexed to the City of Cheyenne located southwest of the I -25 and I-80 Interchange adjacent to
Roundtop Road – referred to Public Services Committee.  (Sponsor – Mr. Roybal)

 ORDINANCE –1st READING – Amending the Official Zoning Map of the City of
Cheyenne changing the zoning classification of AG Agricultural, LR Low -Density Residential,
MR Medium -Density Residential, HI Heavy Industrial, and CB Community Business to AG
Agricultural and LI L ight Industrial for land located southwest of the I -25 and I -80 Interchange
adjacent to Roundtop Rd. – referred to Public Services Committee.  (Sponsor – Mr. Roybal)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Approving the Department of Energy Efficiency and Conservation Block
Grant Program Grant for lighting upgrades for the Cheyenne Civic Center . Motion (per Consent
Agenda) carried. (#6389)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Expressing the support of the Governing Body for City staff to review

in Downtown Cheyenne and directing City staff to submit the ordinance rewrite for Governi ng
Body consideration.  Motion (per Consent Agenda) carried. (#6390)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Expressing the support of the Governing Body for an Action Plan to
address deficiencies regarding sidewalks in the Downtown Development District .  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried. (#6391)

RESOLUTION – Supporting the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board in the application for
and acceptance of Federal Assistance from the Federal Aviation Administration under the Airport
Improvement Program to assist in the purchase of snow removal equipment.  Dr. Aldrich moved
to adopt, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body.  Public comment was made by: Tim Bradshaw, Director of Aviation, Cheyenne Regional
Airport.  Comments were made by the following members of the governing body: Dr. Aldrich and
Mr. Johnson. (#6392)

  RESOLUTION – Supporting the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board in the application for

members of the governing body. (#6393)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Re-appropriating the Fiscal Year 2024 Budget of the City of Cheyenne

[CA] RESOLUTION – Authorizing the Mayor to execute an amendment to the Development

 Renewal applications for existing City liquor licenses (retail, restaurant, limited retail,

[CA] Designation of Official Voting Delegate and Alternate Delegate for the City of Cheyenne

 No comments were made on the Voucher Report as presented.

Announcement was made of a Public Hearing to be held March 25, 2024 at 6:00 p.m. in
City Council Chambers, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY, and via electronic conference
meeting, for a restaurant liquor license application filed with the City Clerk’s Office for Rebecca
Andrew Ventures, Inc. d/b/a Mr. Jim’s Pizza, 4001 Laramie St., Unit A, Cheyenne, WY.

Under other business:

Mr. Esquibel noted the motion to adopt the following Resolution - Requesting that the
Wyoming State Legislature consider legislation authorizing the sale of alcoholic beverages with
an alcohol by volume of 5% at grocery stores and convenience stores throughout the state , failed
at the February 26, 2024 City Council meeting.  Having voted on the prevailing side and pursuant
to Chapter 2.04 of City Code, Mr. Esquibel moved to reconsider the vote taken on the resolution,
seconded by Mr. Johnson.  Motion to reconsider carried.  Voting “yes” – Mr. Cook, Mr. Esquibel,
Mr. Johnson, Dr. Rinne and Mr. Roybal.  Voting “no” – Dr. Aldrich, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Segrave
and Mr. White.  Dr. Rinne moved to postpone to March 25 , 2024, seconded by Mr. Johnson.
Motion to postpone carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception
of Dr. Aldrich, Mr. Laybourn and Mr. White  voting “no”.  During public comment, Dr. Rinne
called a point of order related to comments not being made by the public.  Public comment was
made by: Mike Moser, Wyoming State Liquor Association.  Comments were made by the
following members of the governing body: Dr. Aldrich, Mr. Cook, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Johnson, Dr.
Rinne and Mr. Roybal.

The following members of the governing body made comments under other business:  Dr.
Aldrich, Mr. Esquibel and Mr. Johnson.

 There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was
officially adjourned at 7:13 p.m.

        Submitted by,

         Kristina F. Jones,
