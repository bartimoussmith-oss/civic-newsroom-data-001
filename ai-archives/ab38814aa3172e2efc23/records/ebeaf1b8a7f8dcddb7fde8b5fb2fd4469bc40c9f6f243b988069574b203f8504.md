RESOLUTION NO. ________________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND THE CITY
CLERK TO SIGN A FINAL PLAT FOR PLAINS INDUSTRIAL
PARK, A REPLAT OF A PORTION OF HR RANCH ROAD
RIGHT-OF-WAY WITHIN GDC SUBDIVISION AND PORTIONS
OF SECTION 6, T.13N., R.65W.; SECTION 1, T.13N., R.66W.;
SECTION 31, T.14N., R.65W.; AND SECTION 36, T.14N., R.66W.,
6TH P.M., CHEYENNE, WYOMING (LOCATED SOUTH OF I-80,
WEST OF CAMPSTOOL ROAD).”

WHEREAS, the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have)
subdivided said land in accordance with the statutes in such cases made and provided;
and

WHEREAS, the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have) caused a
subdivision plat of said land to be made, acknowledged, and certified, particularly
describing the lots, blocks, easements and right-of-way; and

WHEREAS, the above described subdivision plat has been presented to the City
of Cheyenne Planning Commission for consideration and has been recommended for
approval to the governing body and the plat has been duly executed by the Development
Office.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, THAT the subdivision described as
Plains Industrial Park, a replat of a portion of HR Ranch Road right-of-way within GDC
Subdivision and portions of Section 6, T.13N., R.65W.; Section 1, T.13N., R.66W.;
Section 31, T.14N., R.65W.; and Section 36, T.14N., R.66W., 6th P.M., Cheyenne,
Wyoming, be and the same hereby is approved and confirmed as presented, and that the
Mayor and the City Clerk be and are hereby authorized, empowered, and directed to
execute said plat when Community Facility Fees have been paid in full, $12,950 of which
will be reimbursed from the City to B and L Land Co. LLC for dedication of Lot 3, Block
2, of the above described plat within 90 days of the recording of said plat.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _____________ DAY OF

__________________________, 2013.

_____________________________________

RICHARD L. KAYSEN, MAYOR

(SEAL)
ATTEST:

___________________________________
CAROL INTLEKOFER, CITY CLERK
