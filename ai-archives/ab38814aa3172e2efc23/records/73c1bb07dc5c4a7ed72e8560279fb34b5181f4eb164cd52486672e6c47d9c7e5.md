RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION BY THE GOVERNING BODY DESIGNATING A PAID
ON-STREET PARKING DISTRICT FOR DOWNTOWN CHEYENNE IN
ACCORDANCE WITH SECTION 10.60.015 OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING."

WHEREAS, on July 22, 2024, the Governing Body adopted Ordinance No. 4582, which created
Section 10.60.015 of the city code and established the Paid On-Street Parking Districts Program for the
City of Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, in accordance with Section 10.60.015, the Governing Body is responsible for
designating any paid on-street parking district in the city;

WHEREAS, upon designation of any paid on-street parking district, the Parking Division within
the Cheyenne Police Department must establish appropriate on-street parking zones and reasonable on-
street parking rates with the district; and

WHEREAS, as required by the Section 10.60.015 of the city code, the Governing Body has
determined that a paid on-street parking district should be created for the downtown area of Cheyenne
as provided herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that in accordance with Section 10.60.015 of the city code, the geographic
area for the downtown paid on-street parking district is hereby designated as provided by the map
appended to this Resolution, which map is incorporated herein by reference;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the downtown paid on-street parking district designated
herein shall include both sides of the roadways forming the boundaries of the district; and

BE IT FINALLY RESOLVED, that the designation of the downtown paid on-street parking
district shall not become effective until the on-street parking zones and on-street parking rates have been
established by the Parking Division within the Cheyenne Police Department in accordance with Section
10.60.015 of the city code.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _______ day of _____________________, 2024.

__________________________________________
Patrick Collins, Mayor
