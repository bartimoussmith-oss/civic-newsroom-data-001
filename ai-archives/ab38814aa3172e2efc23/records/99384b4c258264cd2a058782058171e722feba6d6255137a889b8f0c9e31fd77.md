Background:
The item for consideration is a Zoning Map Amendment.   This is approved by Ordinance.

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning of the West Edge area from MUB, MUR, LI,
and CB to MUB with the UU Overlay. Planning efforts in the West Edge District have been underway for
nearly a decade in the City of Cheyenne. One of the most consistent and critical components of these
planning efforts has been the recommendation to rezone the area. The West Edge plans of both 2014
and 2016 note that the area should consider the Mixed-Use Business Emphasis or Mixed-Use Residential
Emphasis zones with special characteristics. Through extensive outreach and comprehensive internal
efforts, the Planning and Development Department has crafted the Urban Use overlay district to fulfill
the West Edge vision. Rezoning the properties included in the West Edge district to MUB with the UU
Overlay will permit a wide variety of uses with flexible development standards to promote
redevelopment and reinvestment in the area. This is a City-initiated zoning map amendment initiated
pursuant to UDC 2.2.1.b.2.

Future Land Use Categories

Mixed-Use Commercial

The Mixed-Use Commercial Emphasis category encompasses existing and future developments,
many of which align with activity centers. This category includes a wide variety of land uses and
activities, focused primarily on commercial activities, but also including other employment,
public, and residential uses. While many existing Mixed-Use Commercial Emphasis areas do not
currently integrate residential uses, as they redevelop and change over time, residential units
may be incorporated to add more activity to these areas.

Mixed-Use Residential

The Mixed-Use Residential Emphasis category promotes self-supporting neighborhoods
primarily containing a mix of housing densities with complementary Neighborhood Business
Centers. This category includes existing and future neighborhoods. Many of the newer Mixed-
Use Residential Emphasis areas in the Cheyenne Area have focused on development of the
residential components first, with development the non-residential and other amenities lagging
somewhat behind due to market demand. These non-residential elements and other amenities
are important next-steps for enhancing these newer neighborhoods as they become more
established.

Mixed-Use Employment

The Mixed-Use Employment Campus category is intended to include a variety of uses, with a
primary focus on employment designed in a business campus setting. Many of the Mixed-Use
Employment Campuses in the community (e.g. portions of Swan Ranch) are in the early stages
of development or are established (e.g. North Range Business Park, and Cheyenne Business
Parkway), but still have many sites available.

Open Space and Parks

The Parks and Open Space category includes existing and planned future parks and open space
areas.

Public

The Public and Quasi-Public category encompasses areas related to governmental and other
community-based services and activities, like educational facilities and other institutions. Many of these
areas are already developed, and future Public and Quasi-Public areas will need to be coordinated with
the City, County, and their partner organizations and service providers as necessary.

The proposed zoning map amendment is consistent with the principles and policies within
PlanCheyenne. Specifically, the proposed amendment addresses the following policies and principles:

•
Policy 1.1.B: Flexibility to Respond to Market Demands.
The MUB District with the UU Overlay will provide flexible zoning regulations to promote infill
development, adaptive reuse, and redevelopment of an important area in the City of
Cheyenne. The flexibility should incentivize the market to consider developing in the West
Edge.

•
Policy 1.4.A: Infill Opportunities
The MUB District with the UU Overlay provides the opportunity for infill development through
regulations that promote flexibility, but also consider the character of the area in which they
are located.

•
Policy 1.4.C: Downtown Revitalization and Mix of Uses
As the Urban Use Overlay is applied to the West Edge, it is anticipated to revitalize the area
and further mix uses in the area. Staff anticipates the development of the West Edge will
greatly benefit the downtown as a whole.

•
Policy 1.4.D: Infill and Revitalization Incentives
A rezoning to MUB with the UU Overlay achieves several items that are mentioned in this
policy; such as reducing parking minimums, providing flexible dimensional standards,
promoting higher densities than currently permitted, and providing flexibility in design
requirements for utilizing existing buildings.

•
Policy 2.3.A: Mix of Housing Types
Much of the West Edge District is currently zoned LI, which does not permit residential uses.
The rezoning to the MUB District will allow a variety of housing types to be located within the
district.

•
Policy 3.2.B: Active Uses in the Street Level
The Urban Use Overlay promotes active uses on the street level by permitting various street
furnishings and projections into the Right of Way.

•
Principle 4.3: The Cheyenne Area will build a safe, multimodal transportation system that
consists of streets, sidewalks, bicycle facilities, and transit
The Urban Use Overlay promotes bicycle infrastructure and pedestrian amenities through
increased bicycle parking and an active pedestrian zone.

•
Policy 5.1.A: Historic Building Restoration
The Urban Use Overlay promotes building reuse and restoration with flexible site design and
building placement requirements for utilizing existing buildings.

UDC-22-00017

Furthermore, the West Edge has been subject to several Planning efforts (2014, 2016, and 2018)
that have recommended the area be transformed into a mixed-use, pedestrian-oriented District
that celebrates the industrial history of the district. The MUB zone with the UU overlay allows
this style of development.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Again, the entire area of the West Edge has been identified as an area for redevelopment and
infill development to create an urban, pedestrian, and mixed-use atmosphere. The plans have
led several properties to rezone individually in anticipation of future redevelopment. The
legislative zoning initiated by the City of Cheyenne will ensure the entirety of the district is
rezoned at the same time, delivering complementary development patterns. Furthermore, the
Urban Use Overlay will provide a harmonious step down from the Central Business District into
the surrounding neighborhoods zoned MUB and MR.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

Much of the West Edge District west of Snyder Avenue contains detached single-family housing
units, which are not permitted in the existing LI Zone District. This has led the uses to be
considered non-conforming uses, requiring a conditional use for expansion. A rezoning to MUB
with the UU Overlay will be more conducive to residential expansion and additions with the
existing homes. In addition to this, much of the redevelopment interest falls east of Snyder
Avenue. The UU overlay will allow new development opportunities to utilize existing buildings
for new development. The UU will also not require parking minimums. The existing site and
block layout is not conducive towards individual sites meeting parking minimums; therefore, the
flexibility provided by the UU District will allow creative redevelopment options without having
to consider parking. Many of the existing businesses in the West Edge area are considered
limited industrial, which is a permitted use in the MUB District, or moderate industrial, which is
a conditional use in the MUB District, meaning the rezoning is not anticipated to have a
significant impact on the existing businesses.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The West Edge District is within the Original City Plat, meaning it adheres to a strict grid system.
With this, many of the subdivisions in the area often result in lot consolidation or clarification.
The subdivision standards applicable to these sites often pertain to building detached sidewalks
and other minor improvements to the right-of-way. Staff does not anticipate redeveloping
properties having difficulties in meeting these standards.
