5.44.010 - Purpose—Authority—General and authority; general provisions.

A. The purpose of this chapter is to protect the public health, safety and welfare by
regulating and controlling the practice of certain building and professional trades within
the city by requiring that only contractors, licensed by the contractor licensing board, and
persons actively supervised by licensed contractors may engage in those certain building
trades.

B. Authority is granted cities and towns by Wyo. Stat. Section W.S. 15-1-103(a)(xiii) to
license and regulate activities affiliated with building contractors and certain building
and professional trades. Pursuant to Ordinance No. 3335, this This chapter may also be
referred to as the "Cheyenne contractor licensing ordinance."

"Hose station equipment" means hoses, nozzles, hangers and connectors to a standpipe
system.

"Hydrostatic testing" means pressure testing by hydrostatic methods.

"Portable fire extinguisher" means a device that contains chemicals, fluids or gases for
extinguishing fires, the method for application of its contents, and which is capable of being readily
moved from place to place.

"Service and servicing of hose station equipment and portable fire extinguishers" means
servicing of hose station equipment and portable fire extinguishers including any or all of the
following: Charging, filling, maintaining, recharging, refilling, repairing and testing.

8.28.020 - Business license required.

Every person who services and tests portable fire extinguishers and hose station equipment
is required to obtain a business license. The license must state specifically whether it is issued for
portable fire extinguishers or hose station equipment, or both. Applications, with nonrefundable
annual licensing fee of ninety dollars ($90.00), must be submitted to the office of city clerk. Upon
approval by the fire chief of the fire department, the city clerk will issue a license which will be
effective for one year from date of issuance. General licensing provisions set forth in city code
Chapter 5.04 apply to this type of license.

8.28.030 - Servicing and testing.

All persons licensed must service and test all portable fire extinguishers and hose station
equipment in accordance with the International Fire Code, as adopted and referenced in
Chapter 8.12.

8.28.040 - Hydrostatic testing.

Every person performing hydrostatic testing of fire extinguishers manufactured in
accordance with the specifications of the U.S. Department of Transportation shall do so in
accordance with the procedures specified by that department for compressed gas cylinders. Persons
qualified to do this work shall be given such authority on their licenses.

8.28.050 - Examination.

A.
Applicants for a portable fire extinguisher and hose station equipment
license are required to pass a written examination administered by the fire department or
submit proof of recognized certification to be approved by the fire prevention bureau of
the fire department. The examination may be supplemented by practical tests or
demonstrations deemed necessary to determine the applicant's knowledge and ability to
service portable fire extinguishers and hose station equipment. The results of the
examination shall be forwarded to the office of city clerk to accompany the license
application.

Chapter 8.29 - HOOD SYSTEM CLEANING COMPANIES

8.29.010 - Definitions.

The following definitions shall apply in the interpretation and enforcement of the following
provisions relating to hood system cleaning and equipment.

"Cleaning and returning to service hood system" means servicing of the hood system,
including any or all of the following: washing, maintaining, inspecting, returning to service, and
providing documentation of the work performed.

"Commercial cooking appliances" means appliances used in commercial food service
establishments for heating and cooking food and which produce grease laden vapors, steam, fumes,
smoke, or odors that are required to be removed through a local exhaust ventilation system. Such
appliances include deep fat fryers, upright broilers, griddles, broilers, steam-jacketed kettles, hot-
top ranges, underfired broilers (charbroilers), ovens, barbecues, rotisseries, and similar appliances.
For the purpose of this definition, a food service establishment shall include any building or portion
thereof used for the preparation and serving of food.

"Hood system" means an air-intake device used to capture by entrapment, impingement,
adhesion, or similar means, grease and similar contaminants before they enter the duct system. The
types of hood systems are:

Type I. A kitchen hood system for collection and removing grease vapors and
smoke.

Type II. A general kitchen hood system for collecting and removing steam
vapor, heat, odors, and products of combustion.

Every company who cleans hood systems as defined by [Section] 8.29.010 is required to

All persons licensed must clean and inspect all hood systems and equipment in accordance

8.29.040 - Examination.

Applicants for a hood system cleaning license shall have successfully passed a written
examination administered by a recognized association or group meeting the standard of NFPA 96.
The applicant must submit proof of recognized certification to be approved by the city. The
examination may be supplemented by practical tests or demonstrations deemed necessary to
determine the applicant's knowledge and ability to clean hood systems and related equipment. The
results of the examination shall be forwarded to the office of city clerk to accompany the license
application.

8.29.050 - Insurance.

All persons licensed are required to maintain current general liability insurance equal to
the minimum amount required of all other contractors pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 5.44
of the city code.

8.29.060 - Tagging of cleaned hood systems and related equipment.

When a licensee cleans and returns the hood system and its related equipment back into
service, a tag shall be affixed that at least states the month and year the service is performed, the
type of work completed, and the name and license number of the licensee. Further documentation
of the reported work is required to be sent to the fire prevention division of the fire department
within seven days of completed work. Only pre-approved forms meeting the requirements of NFPA
96 and the fire prevention division of the fire department will be accepted as documentation of
work performed.

MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Patrick J. Collins, City of Cheyenne

Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Bob Mason, BC of Prevention, Cheyenne Fire Rescue Dept.

DATE:
October 3, 2024

SUBJECT:
An ordinance repealing Chapters 8.28 and 8.29 of The Code of the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming.

Please find attached a proposed ordinance change that repeals Chapter 8.28 Portable Fire
Extinguishers and Hose Station Equipment, and Chapter 8.29 Hood System Cleaning
Companies, of the Cheyenne City Code.   Currently the responsibility for licensing companies
that service fire extinguishers, and companies that provide restaurant hood cleaning services falls
on the City Clerk’s Office.  Due to the nature of these licenses, it is appropriate that the approval
and licensing of these entities rests with the Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department and the
Cheyenne Contractor Licensing Board (CLB).  Over the past several months I have amended the
requirements for fire licensing in the CLB’s City of Cheyenne Contractor Licensing Regulations,
which includes new licensing requirements for fire extinguisher service companies, and hood
system cleaning companies.  These changes are ready to be put before the CLB for consideration
and adoption, so I respectfully request your support and approval of this ordinance change.
