RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
ACCEPT AND EXECUTE A STATE HISTORIC PRESERVATION
OFFICE CERTIFIED LOCAL GOVERNMENT (CLG) GRANT
AGREEMENT FOR THE ARCHITECTURAL ASSISTANCE PLAN FOR
THE 2017 HISTORIC CHEYENNE FAÇADE IMPROVEMENT
PROGRAM.”
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is a Certified Local Government (CLG), as
determined by the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) and assumes the duties and
responsibilities thereof; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 2.64.010, the City of Cheyenne has created the
Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board (CHPB) to further the public policy of historic
preservation; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 2.64.020 of the Cheyenne City Code, the governing
body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes “that the protection, enhancement, and perpetuation of
areas, improvements, and landscape features having historic character or special historical or
aesthetic interest or value is a public necessity”; and
WHEREAS, the SHPO has awarded the CHPB a grant in the amount of $13,500 to
contribute to the Architectural Assistance Program for the 2017 Historic Cheyenne Façade
Improvement Program; and
WHEREAS, the purpose of the Architectural Assistance Program is to provide
assistance to the applicants of the 2017 Historic Façade Improvement Program to visualize,
understand and identify historic and appropriate improvements for their buildings.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, Wyoming, that the City is hereby authorized to accept, and for the
Mayor and the City Clerk to execute, the grant award in the amount of $13,500 from the SHPO
to be used solely for the Architectural Assistance Plan for the 2017 Historic Cheyenne Façade
Improvement Program.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _____________ DAY OF

__________________________, 2017.

_____________________________________

MARIAN J. ORR, MAYOR

(SEAL)
ATTEST:

___________________________________
CAROL INTLEKOFER, CITY CLERK

GRANT AWARD RESOLUTION SUMMARY REPORT

Revised Summation

2017 CLG Grant for Architectural Assistance Plan for the
2017 Historic Cheyenne Façade Improvement Program

RESOLUTION:
First Reading, January 23, 2017; Finance Committee, February 6,
2017; Final Reading, February 13, 2017

PREPARED BY:
Jan Spires, Urban Planner

RECOMMENDATION:  The Urban Planning Office recommends acceptance of this grant

The Wyoming State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) has approved the Cheyenne
Historic Preservation Board’s (CHPB) application for this grant as described herein. The
attached resolution allows the City to accept this grant and proceed with the project.

The SHPO has awarded the City $13,500 to hire an architect to help with the third year of the
Façade Improvement Program. Applications for façade improvements from property owners
in the project area will be reviewed by the Downtown Development Authority/Main Street
(DDA/MS) Design Committee.  The SHPO originally awarded the City $9,500 in late
December 2016, then increased the award to $13,500 in mid-January. The selected projects
will have the opportunity to participate in a Group Design Effort and have the architect
provide professional assistance to help applicants with appropriate designs for their work
projects. This may include architectural renderings of remodeled façades, detail drawings,
and cost projections that the property owner can use in hiring a contractor and having the
work completed. A representative from the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) serves
on the Design Committee and projects selected for the Group Design Effort must comply
with the Secretary of Interior Standards. The City and Economic Joint Powers Board have
each allocated $20,000 annually, along with $80,000 from the DDA/MS toward helping to
pay for the project. The required match will include in-kind match from staff and volunteer
time, as well as cash match from the funds the DDA and the City are contributing.
