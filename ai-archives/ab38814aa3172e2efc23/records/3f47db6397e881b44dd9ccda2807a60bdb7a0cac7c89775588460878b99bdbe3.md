RESOLUTION NO. ________________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND THE CITY
CLERK TO SIGN A FINAL PLAT AND DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT FOR TERRE PONT SUBDIVISION, A PORTION
OF THE S1/2 OF SECTION 20, T.14N., R.66W., 6TH P.M.,
CHEYENNE, WYOMING (LOCATED AT THE NORTHEAST
CORNER OF THE INTERSECTION OF PRAIRIE AVENUE AND
FRONTIER MALL DRIVE).”

WHEREAS, the owners of the property described herein have subdivided said
land in accordance with the statutes in such cases made and provided; and

WHEREAS, the owners of the property described herein have caused a
subdivision plat of said land to be made, acknowledged, and certified, particularly
describing the lots, blocks, easements and right-of-way; and

WHEREAS, the above described subdivision plat has been presented to the City
of Cheyenne Planning Commission for consideration and the Planning Commission has
recommended that the Governing Body approve the subdivision plat; and

WHEREAS, the above-described plat has been duly executed by the
Development Office; and

WHEREAS, the owners of the property described herein and the City wish to
enter into the attached development agreement addressing the connection of Rue Terre to
the southeast.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, THAT the subdivision described as
Terre Pont Subdivision, a portion of the S1/2 of Section 20, T.14N., R.66W., 6th P.M.,
Cheyenne, Wyoming, be and the same hereby is approved and confirmed as presented,
and that the Mayor and the City Clerk be and are hereby authorized, empowered, and
directed to execute said plat when Community Facility Fees have been paid in full, and
after the attached development agreement has been executed and recorded with the
Laramie County Clerk.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _____________ DAY OF

__________________________, 2013.

_____________________________________

RICHARD L. KAYSEN, MAYOR

(SEAL)
ATTEST:

___________________________________
CAROL INTLEKOFER, CITY CLERK
