MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Marian J. Orr, City of Cheyenne
Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Doug Klahn, Construction Manager

DATE:
June 10, 2020

SUBJECT:
Contract Modification #3 to Simon Contractors, for the 2018 Mountain Road and
20th Street Pavement Rehabilitation Project.

Please find the attached proposed contract modification for the above referenced city project.
Construction and Engineering Staff have discussed the unforeseen condition of the underlying road
surface of 20th Street from Evans Avenue to Bent Avenue and determined that it will require
additional preparatory work to achieve a quality project. The additional cost associated with these
repairs is estimated at $119,428.80. The project is carrying an approximate balance of unused
funds in the amount of $70,000 therefore, the requested amount for this modification is $65,000.
In addition, this will require an extension of the completion date to July 3, 2020. Staff respectfully
requests your approval of this contract modification.

.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE
2101 O’Neil Avenue
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
