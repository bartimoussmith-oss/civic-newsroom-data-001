RESOLUTION NO._______
ENTITILED: “A
RESOLUTION
FOR
GOVERNING
BODY
SUPPORT
OF
COMMUNITY FORUM(S) AND OTHER APPROPRIATE INITIATIVES
TO ENCOURAGE MORE KINDNESS, CIVILITY, COOPERATION AND
RESPECTFUL DIALOGUE.”
WHEREAS; We want everyone in our community to feel safe and assured that their
equal rights to life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness are honored; and
WHEREAS; Every individual has great value and potential; and
WHEREAS; Diversity makes us stronger and more competitive as a community; and
WHEREAS; Cheyenne is a caring community with opportunities for business and
industry looking to relocate or establish a presence in Wyoming; and
WHEREAS; As a community we are experiencing growth and have seen some
challenges and some behavior by individuals and that we are not proud of and are not reflective
of our community; and
WHEREAS; We value the presence of all our military in Cheyenne and the rich diversity
and economic value they bring to our community; and
WHEREAS; We recognize that modeling civility and kindness are important for
everyone, especially young people in our community; and
WHEREAS; We encourage all citizens, organizations, groups, and businesses to unite for
more kindness, civility, cooperation, and respectful dialogue; and
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body supports community forum(s)
and other appropriate initiatives to encourage more kindness, civility, cooperation, and respectful
dialogue.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this resolution shall be
effective upon passage and execution by the Mayor and City Clerk.

By Stefanie Boster at 11:25 am, Mar 23, 2022
