PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
for
AC FOAM FOR THE CITY OF CHEYENNE HAPPY JACK LANDFILL

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and
between the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
(“City”), and Atmos Technologies, located at 17 Campus Boulevard, Newton Square,
Pennsylvania 19073 (“Consultant”).  This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of
the last signature affixed hereto.

RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by
and between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide daily cover on the active working face of the City of
Cheyenne Happy Jack Landfill every day of operation.

a.
Equipment Installation. Consultant shall provide, at its own expense and at a
mutually agreeable location at the Landfill, one Morooka Pneumatic Foam
Application Unit (PFU).  Consultant shall provide the foam concentrate as
needed to the City when it becomes apparent that the current supply will run out
within one week and upon notification of the City.  Due to the climatic conditions
that will affect the foam concentrate supply requirements, Consultant shall
provide 48 totes of foam concentrate per year to the City, and will make
reasonable efforts to ship them in a quantity of 4 totes per shipment to minimize
variable freight costs and unnecessary shipments of foam concentrate. The City
shall provide a heated building to enclose the Morooka Pneumatic Foam Unit and
the foam concentrate from the outdoor elements to prevent damage from freezing
and other climatic conditions and unforeseen events.  In addition to the heated
building, the City shall provide the water needed to mix with the foam
concentrate.

b.
Ownership. All equipment provided by Consultant for the use of AC 667 Foam
shall remain the property of Consultant and the City agrees that it will not assert
any interest therein.  The totes that the foam concentrate is shipped in will belong
to the City upon arrival to the Happy Jack Landfill to dispose, reuse, sell, gift, or
use at the landfill as they reasonably desire.

c.
Operation and Maintenance.  Consultant shall be solely responsible for the
operation, maintenance, and repair of the Morooka PFU.  Consultant shall keep

TIME OF PERFORMANCE
This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall terminate on the first

3.
COMPENSATION.
Following the Commencement Date, the City shall pay Consultant a flat monthly rate of
$22,500 billed on the first of every month and due by the 30th of every month. This
monthly fee includes freight that shall be covered by the Consultant. The City shall pay
$0.72 a pound of foam concentrate that they use above and beyond the contractual 48
totes per year. There will be times during the year where the City will use more or less
foam in a given month due to climatic conditions of rain, snow, and wind. Both parties
will have a “true up” period during the 4th quarter of the current calendar year to
determine if any additional charges on extra foam are needed above and beyond the
agreed upon amount.

4.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the

not meeting such a standard.

b.
Treat the Agreement as continuing and require specific performance.

15.
TERM AND TERMINATION
The term of this Agreement shall be from date of execution and shall continue for one
year.  The parties may by mutual written agreement renew or extend this Agreement.

terminate this Agreement in whole or in part at any time, for the City’s convenience.
