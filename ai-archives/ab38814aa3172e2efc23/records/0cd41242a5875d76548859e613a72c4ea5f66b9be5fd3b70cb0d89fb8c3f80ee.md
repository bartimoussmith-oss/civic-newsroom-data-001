Background:
Attached is a substitute resolution with a modified attachment A.   The changes incorporated are:
1. A requirement for staff review of the fee schedule every five years (Councilman Johnson request).
2. Removal of the appraisal cost fee from the street/alley vacation by ordinance application.
3. A $2,500 limit has been established for preliminary and final plat applications.
4. A $5,200 limit has been established for expedited plat applications.  This is the sum of the preliminary and
final plat applications plus a $200 fee for staff prioritization and the convenience of a quicker review.
5.
Vacation Appraisal Fee Removed:
After hearing public comments at the February 28th City Council meeting, staff agrees that it could cause undue
and unpredictable delays associated with a street/alley vacation application.  Additionally, receipt of this fee has
not been included in the annual revenue projections since applications occur infrequently.  Typically, no more than
one application is filed per year, if at all.

RESOLUTION NO.

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION ADOPTING A FEE SCHEDULE FOR PLANNING AND
DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS."

WHEREAS, on January 23, 2012, the Governing Body approved Ordinance 3943 creating
the Unified Development Code (UDC); and

WHEREAS, on October 25, 1993, the Governing Body, jointly with the County
Commissioners, adopted Resolution 3451 creating a schedule of fees related to development and
subdivision applications; and

WHEREAS, the costs of conducting business has increased and the established fees do not
reflect costs associated with development actions or the services offered by the City; and

WHEREAS, pursuant to UDC Section 1.3.7 the City may charge fees to defray some or all
of the cost of processing land development applications; and

WHEREAS, adoption of a fee schedule is necessary to defray the costs associated with
processing land development applications; and

WHEREAS, staff has conducted internal research and surveys to measure costs associated
with development, including but not limited to, costs associated with staff review time, legal notice,
ordinance publication, data storage, development related software, and data storage; and

WHEREAS, staff recognizes that some services of the department are a public service in the
interest of the public safety, health and welfare of the Cheyenne community and fees to defray total
costs may not be necessary and appropriate and may be nominal to encourage compliance; and

WHEREAS, a fee schedule has been created and in attached to this resolution as Attachment
A consisting of one (1) page which is attached hereto and incorporated herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, the fee schedule shown in Attachment A of this
resolution is hereby adopted and all previous development fees are hereby replaced.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this resolution shall become effective March 31,
2022, and shall apply to all applicable applications filed on or after the date.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the attached fee schedule shall, at minimum, be
reviewed by Planning and Development Department staff every five years hereafter and
amendments to the fee schedule shall be brought forward to the Governing Body as necessary.
