MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Jeanie Vetter, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner

Subject: Contract Modification #4, East Cheyenne Community Park Master Site
Development Plan

Date:
April 18, 2023

1. Parks and Greenway Planning has been working on plans to begin construction efforts at
the Kiwanis Community Park, located at the SW corner of E. Pershing and Whitney Road.
These plans include a detention pond/constructed wetlands/open water area that will
provide all detention needs for future improvements to be made at the park as well as
improved wildlife habitat and water quality within Dry Creek.  The project will include
gravel walking paths surrounding the wetlands/detention area, two blinds for observing
birds, and educational signage describing the Kiwanis Park Master Plan,
wetland/riparian area wildlife, water quality and park history.
2. The contract modification before you is for additional consultant fees relative to design
revisions that were necessitated by permitting requirements of the Wyoming
Department of Environmental Quality (WYDEQ), United States Army Corp of Engineers
(USACE) and State of Wyoming Engineers Office.  The increased fees include
environmental assessment to be performed by HDR for existing wetlands. The fees also
include bidding and construction administration for construction of the project.
3. With approval of this contract modification, it is expected that final construction
documents can be completed, and approvals can be obtained from WYDEQ, USACE and
the State Water Engineers office by the end of this year or early next year and
construction can begin in the spring of 2024.
4. WYDEQ has suggested that this project would be a good option for a 319 grant for water
quality, available through their office. This grant would be a 60/40 match, with funding
from $20,000.00 to $300,000.00 potentially available.  Kiwanis Club of Cheyenne will
support this project with $80,000.00 in park sponsorship funds.  Cheyenne Audubon
Society and Cheyenne Greenway Foundation have committed $5,500.00 to support the
bird blind portion of the project.
