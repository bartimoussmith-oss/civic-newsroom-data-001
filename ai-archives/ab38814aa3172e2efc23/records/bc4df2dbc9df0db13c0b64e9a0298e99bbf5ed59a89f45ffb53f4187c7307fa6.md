RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE GOVERNING
BODY FOR THE SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION BY THE
CHEYENNE TRANSIT PROGRAM TO THE WYOMING DEPARTMENT
OF TRANSPORTATION FOR GRANT FUNDING UNDER THE
WYOMING RURAL PUBLIC TRANSIT PROGRAM."

WHEREAS, in accordance with the Formula Grants for Rural Areas Program, codified at
49 U.S.C. 5311 (the "Section 5311 program"), the Secretary of the federal Department of
Transportation may make grants to assist states and local governmental authorities in financing
capital, operating, planning, and job access and reverse commute projects, associated with
providing public transportation in rural areas;

WHEREAS, the Wyoming Department of Transportation (WYDOT) administers the
Section 5311 program for the State through its Rural Public Transit Program, which program
provides state funding and federal formula grants to support development, maintenance and
improvement of public transportation in rural and small urban areas;

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has previously received grant funding from WYDOT
under the Rural Public Transit Program, most recently for Fiscal Year 2024 in the amount of three
hundred thousand dollars ($300,000.00);

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, acting through the Cheyenne Transit Program, wishes
to again apply to WYDOT for financial assistance under the Rural Public Transit Program; and

WHEREAS, if the grant application were approved, the Cheyenne Transit Program intends
to use the grant funding to cover operational, administrative, and maintenance costs associated
with providing public transportation in the City's service area in Laramie County.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the Governing Body of the City of
Cheyenne supports the Mayor executing and filing an application for financial assistance on behalf
of the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program, with the Wyoming Department of
Transportation to aid the financing of operating, project administration and maintenance assistance
for projects under the Rural Public Transit Program;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor is authorized to furnish such additional
information as the Wyoming Department of Transportation may require in connection with the
grant application;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor is authorized to execute any necessary
grant and cooperative agreements with the Wyoming Department of Transportation on behalf of
the City of Cheyenne and the Cheyenne Transit Program; AND

1:42 pm, May 01, 2024
