SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-14-26 for furnishing Residential Automated
Refuse Carts - 95/65 Gallons, for the Sanitation Division

Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Four (4) bids were received from:
1. Formex Holdings, LLC
2. IPL North America Inc. (Rejected)
3. Rehrig Pacific Company
4. Schaefer Plastics North America, LLC (Rejected)

The bids were reviewed by Austin Reiner (Sanitation), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

The bids submitted by Schaefer Plastics North America, LLC & IPL North America Inc.,
have been rejected, as their Refuse Carts did not meet all of the required specifications.

Specifically:

1. Their containers for the 65 gallon cart option did not meet the ‘actual volume’
requirement of 65 gallons.  Schaefer Plastics submitted a 64.5 gallon capacity
cart, and IPL North America Inc. submitted a 64 gallon capacity cart.  Due to
the bid requiring a 65 gallon volume capacity, these two bids have been
rejected.

Rehrig Pacific Company was the next lowest responsive bidder.

The total requested bid award for Rehrig Pacific Company is $204,884.00.

Funding:
Solid Waste Fund / Small Equipment

$204,884.00
