FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Monday, July 15, 2024, in Committee Room
104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Chairwoman; Bryan Cook; Richard Johnson
(via Zoom); and Jeff White.
AGENDA ITEMS
13.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
c)
Minimum Revenue Guaranteed Payment Agreement between the City of Cheyenne
and the Cheyenne Regional Air Service Focus Team (CRAFT) for Fiscal Year
Ending June 30, 2025. (2021 Specific Purpose Option Tax Fund)
Mr. White moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $400,000.00, seconded by Mr. Cook.
Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided a staff report. Wendy yolk, Cheyenne Regional Air
Service Focas Team (CRAFT) President, provided a brief history of CRAFT. Committee members
Johnson and White commented on the item.
d)
Juvenile Housing Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Laramie County
for the housing of juvenile detainees in the Laramie County Juvenile Services
Center located at the Archer Complex. (General Fund)
Mr. Cook moved to approve, seconded by Mr. White, Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Captain David Janes, Police Department, provided a staff report and committee member Cook
commented on the item.
e)
Contract between the City of Cheyenne, Laramie County Sheriffs Department and
Laramie County for housing prisoners at the Laramie County Detention Center.
(General Fund)
Mr. White moved to approve, seconded by Mr. Cook. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Captain David Janes, Police Department, provided a staff report and committee member Johnson
commented on the item.
f)
Improvement Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Brakes Plus, LLC for
Pedestrian
Connectivity
Improvements
Cash-In-Lieu
of
Right-Of-Way
Improvements for development of a Brakes Plus.
