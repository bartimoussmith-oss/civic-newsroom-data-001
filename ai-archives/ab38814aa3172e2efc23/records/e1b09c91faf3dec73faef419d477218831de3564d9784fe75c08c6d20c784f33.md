UDC-22-00117

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is very close to the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure. Policies in PlanCheyenne encourage industrial development near
regional transportation routes and on the edges of the community (where they can be buffered
from the residential and customer-based commercial uses).

The market opportunity associated with the rail line would encourage the development of more
industrial uses in the of the Swan Ranch PUD. PlanCheyenne encourages the flexibility to be
responsive to market demands.

This property is part of a designated gateway to Cheyenne. Investment has been put into the
Welcome Center and the I-25 and High Plains Road interchange to make a positive impact on
those entering not only Cheyenne, but the State of Wyoming. Gateways are encouraged to
provide an enhanced visual experience, including the use of landscaping. The proposed PUD
reduces the amount of required landscaping; however, this is not necessarily a challenge with
the proposed rezoning, as there would be potential to coordinate landscaping on this parcel to
present attractive and quality landscaping closest to the interstate and interchange entrance
into the facility.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Mixed-use Employment Campus.
Surrounding areas are designated Industrial, Mixed-use Employment Campus, and Mixed-use
Commercial.
future land use designation MUE Mixed-use Employment. The proposed zoning is closer to HI
zoning than Mixed-use Employment. Mixed-use Employment Campus areas are encouraged to
develop with a primary focus on employment designed in a business campus setting. Parks,
plazas, pedestrian amenities, and commercial uses are expected parts of development
established under this designation. While the regional development pattern and proposed
zoning appear antithetical to these objectives, the plan specifically uses Swan Ranch as an
-Use Employment Campus (and the plan was adopted after the
original Swan Ranch PUD was adopted). It is evident that the framers of PlanCheyenne
considered the Swan Ranch Rail Park as an acceptable form of Employment Campus, and

This property is also designated as a Community/Regional Activity Center on the Future Land
Use Map. Activity Centers are intended for a mix of commercial and residential uses with access
to regional transportation. Regional-serving retail is expected to be a major component of a
Community/Regional Activity Center. The proposed zone would not allow the retail and
residential components typically expected in activity centers. However, there is an allowance for
varied activity center formats which respect the surrounding neighborhood; and there is
potential for future development around the interchange to be more reflective of the kind of
regional center anticipated by the future land use plan designation.

The policies of PlanCheyenne are supportive of this proposed zone assignment. The future land
use plan would encourage more commercial-style zoning at this location, but the current Swan
Ranch area is a unique style of employment campus activity center clearly anticipated by the
plan on the whole.

The proposed zone change largely complies with review criterion 1.
