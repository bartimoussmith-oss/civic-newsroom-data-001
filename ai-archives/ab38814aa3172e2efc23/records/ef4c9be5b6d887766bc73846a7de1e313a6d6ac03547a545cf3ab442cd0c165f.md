development plans for single family homes to be located on adjacent property, and a community
plan to blend multi-family developments with existing neighborhoods.  Mr. Roybal moved to
approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Case.  Upon inquiry, Ms. Montana provided
information on reviews and input that will occur during the site plan review process; land density
calculations used to determine the number of units per acre, and concerns she has received from
individuals regarding the proposed development project.  Discussion followed and included:
Infrastructure limitations; effects of low income housing on the community; project location;
misperception related to City Council’s involvement in the project; neighborhood homeowners’
concerns regarding property values, and positive aspects of the project and benefit to the
community.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.
