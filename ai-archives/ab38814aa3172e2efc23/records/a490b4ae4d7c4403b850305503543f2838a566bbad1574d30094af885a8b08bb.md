ORDINANCE NO.__________

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE ESTABLISHING ENFORCEMENT PROVISIONS FOR
THE WYOMING STATEWIDE PUBLIC HEALTH ORDERS RELATED
TO THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC.”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That this Ordinance hereby creates enforcement provisions within the Cheyenne City
Code in response to the current Wyoming Public Health Orders, the “SECOND CONTINUATION
OF STATEWIDE PUBLIC HEALTH ORDER CLOSING BARS, RESTAURANTS,
THEATERS, GYMNASIUMS, CHILD CARE FACILITIES, K-12 SCHOOLS, COLLEGES,
UNIVERSITIES, AND TRADE SCHOOLS STATEWIDE” issued by Alexia Harrist on April 3,
2020, and “SECOND CONTINUATION OF STATEWIDE PUBLIC HEALTH ORDER #2:
FORBIDDING GATHERINGS OF TEN (10) PEOPLE OR MORE” issued by Alexia Harrist on
April 3, 2020, and any further Statewide Public Health Orders issued by the State Health Officer
and Countywide Public Health Orders issued by the Laramie County Health Officer.

Section 2.  Violation of any Order referenced herein above, Statewide Order of the State Health
Officer, or any Countywide Order of the Laramie County Health Officer, entered in response to
the COVID-19 pandemic, including any continuation of those Orders, shall constitute a civil
violation punishable by a fine established herein.  Notice of a civil violation shall be issued by a
civil citation.  It is the intent of this Ordinance that no citation shall be issued unless the individual
or entity refuses to disperse, or otherwise comply with the Statewide Orders, upon lawful
command to do so.
A. First violation:  $50.00 fine.  A person who commits a first violation of this section shall
be fined fifty dollars ($50.00).
B. Second violation: $100.00 fine.  A person who commits a second violation of this section
shall be fined one hundred dollars ($100.00).
C. Any subsequent violation: A person who commits a third or subsequent violation of this
section shall be fined two hundred dollars ($200.00).

Section 3.  Business establishments and venues where gatherings of people occur in numbers
greater than ten (10) are required to impose conditions that ensure compliance with the Statewide
Orders. Failure to ensure compliance is a civil violation subject to the same fines noted above.
Each violation of an Statewide Order, measured by the number of people in violation, is a separate
offense.  Managers of business establishments subject to this requirement, as well as any
individual in any organization who is responsible for ensuring compliance with this requirement,
are individually subject to citation, as well as the entity itself.  This requirement shall not apply
to businesses whose essential personnel are required to work closer to one another, or in greater
numbers, than the social distancing requirements.  In those instances, it shall be the responsibility

Section 4.  Any person, business establishment, or venue issued a civil citation pursuant to
Section 2 of this Chapter shall have fifteen (15) days from the day following issuance of the civil
citation to request a hearing before a municipal court judge.  A request for a hearing shall be
made in writing and provided to the municipal court clerk.  A request for a hearing may be made
by email.

Section 5.  A hearing under this Chapter shall be conducted pursuant to the Wyoming
Administrative Procedure Act.  The City shall bear the burden of proof that a given person,
business establishment, or venue did violate this Chapter by a preponderance of the evidence.

Section 6.  Any person, business establishment or venue aggrieved by a decision in a civil hearing
under this Chapter may seek judicial review in the manner provided by the Wyoming
Administrative Procedure Act and the Wyoming Rules of Appellate Procedure.

Section 7.  If a person, business establishment or venue who requested a hearing in accordance
with Section 4 of this Chapter fails to appear at their scheduled hearing, the municipal court clerk
shall enter default judgment for the City and impose a fine in the amount stated on the issued
citation.

Section 8.  The municipal court clerk may utilize a third-party collection agency to collect
defaulted fines pursuant to Section 7 of this Chapter.

Section 9.  This ordinance shall sunset on September 1, 2020, or and cease to be effective upon
any aspect of any of the Statewide or Countywide Orders when that aspect ceases to be in effect.

Section 10.  This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon the passage.
