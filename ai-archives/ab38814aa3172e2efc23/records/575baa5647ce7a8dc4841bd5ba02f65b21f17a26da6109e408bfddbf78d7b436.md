RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
EXECUTE AN AMENDED AND RESTATED DEVELOPMENT
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY AND GRANITE PEAK
DEVELOPMENT, LLC, CLEAR CREEK LAND COMPANY, LLC, AND
SWAN RANCH, LLC, CONCERNING SWAN RANCH RAIL PARK.”
WHEREAS, City Contract #5648, recorded May 27, 2011 at Book 2221, Page 1156 of
the books and records of the Laramie County Clerk is a development agreement (“Original
Development Agreement”) between the City of Cheyenne and Granite Peak Development, LLC,
Clear Creek Land Company, LLC, and Swan Ranch, LLC, concerning Swan Ranch Rail Park
(the latter three entities collectively referred to as “Developer/Owner”); and
WHEREAS, certain provisions of said Original Development Agreement require
clarification; and
WHEREAS, certain new issues relating to development in Swan Ranch Rail Park have
arisen since the time of said Original Development Agreement; and
WHEREAS, the City and Developer/Owner wish to amend and restate said Original
Development Agreement.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, Wyoming, that the Mayor and City Clerk are hereby authorized and
directed to execute an Amended and Restated Development Agreement between the City and
Granite Peak Development, LLC, Clear Creek Land Company, LLC, and Swan Ranch, LLC,
concerning Swan Ranch Rail Park.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED ON THIS ________ DAY OF
_____________, 2013.

__________________________________

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
SEAL:
ATTEST:

__________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
