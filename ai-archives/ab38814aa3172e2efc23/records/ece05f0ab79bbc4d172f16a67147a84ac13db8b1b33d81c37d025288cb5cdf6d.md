MEMORANDUM
TO:
Mayor Richard L. Kaysen and City Council
FROM:
Rudy T. Apodaca, Director of Purchasing
DATE:
March 6, 2013
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid No. M-11-13 for an annual purchase
for furnishing AGGREGATES
________________________________________________________________
Three bids were received for an annual purchase for furnishing
untreated base and sub-base aggregates.  The City Street & Alley
Division estimates that it will use 6,000 tons of gravel in the
next year. We also included the BOPU’s requirement for 11,000 tons
in our bid. BOPU will approve their portion separately.  Bids were
as follows:
Knife River
Street & Alley $4.70/Ton
$28,200.00
Cheyenne, WY.
BOPU
$4.70/Ton
$51,700.00
Simon Contractors
Street & Alley $4.85/Ton
$29,100.00
Cheyenne, WY
BOPU
$4.85/Ton
$53,350.00
Don Kehn Construction
Street & Alley $6.50/Ton
$39,000.00
Ft. Collins, CO.
BOPU
$6.50/Ton
$71,500.00
It is recommended the bid be awarded to the low bidder, Knife
River,for $4.70 per ton. The Street & Alley Division anticipates
spending $28,200.00 during the term of the contract,  although
annual expenditure will depend upon actual quantities used through
April 15, 2014.  Funding is available in the 2011/14 1% sales tax
account #021-26-2616-42-32303.
