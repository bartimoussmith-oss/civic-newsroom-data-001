Background:
The item for consideration is a Resolution overruling a Planning Commission disapproval of a proposed
Future Land Use Map Amendment (or Master Plan Amendment).  This is needed to fulfil Wyoming State
Statute 15-1-506 and allow the Future Land Use Map Amendment to be considered by the Governing
Body.

The Planning Commission heard a future land use map amendment at their April 6, 2026, meeting and
voted 3-2 in favor of the amendment. State Statute requires 4 ‘yes’ votes from the Planning
Commission. Thus, the request was disapproved according to Wyoming State Statute (15-1-505).
Wyoming State Statute allows the Governing Body to overrule the Planning Commission and consider
plans or plan amendments the Planning Commission disapproves.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:

overrule the Planning Commission’s disapproval.

OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body hereby formally overrules the Planning
