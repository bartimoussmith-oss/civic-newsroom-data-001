BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of a replat of 13.17 acres of land. The subject area is currently vacant.
The proposed development is additional single-family homes. Proposed Lot 9, Block 16 was proposed to be a
lot for a future school when originally platted but the school district has decided to look at locations further
to the east and no longer plans to develop this lot.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is zoned as part of the Sweetgrass PUD.  The Sweetgrass PUD is generally described as “a
±2,349.5-acre community envisioned as a distinctive mixed-use master planned village, integrating
retail, entertainment, lodging, employment opportunities, and variety of intimately scaled residential
neighborhoods.”  The district generally allows a broad range of uses. The MDR is intended to provide for
residential development allowing for a variety of single-family detached and attached dwelling units,
located nearby to schools and commercial centers. An extensive open space network should be
provided with within each MDR parcel. To promote greater diversity, lower density development, such
as that permitted within LDR districts, is permitted within MDR districts.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d  – a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

This development is in conformance with the principles and future land uses identified in
PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.
The future land use map within PlanCheyenne identifies this area as developing with residential.
PlanCheyenne encourages a mix of residential types. The applicant is proposing lots to be
developed with single-family.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2)

This plat is one of the MDR zones of the Sweetgrass PUD. The MDR is intended to provide for
residential development allowing for a variety of single-family detached and attached dwelling
units, located nearby to schools and commercial centers. An extensive open space network
should be provided within each MDR parcel. To promote greater diversity, lower density
development, such as that permitted within LDR districts, is permitted within MDR districts.

The proposed plat does not change the street layout that has already been platted. It does
propose reducing the width of Sirocco Drive to a local road width. A future extension of Ledoux
Drive is proposed to create another connection at a later date.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
2.1.2.d.3)

Community Facility Fees (4.2):
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre. The
required fees for the 8.4 acres of non-exempt land in this plat are as follows:

$2,940.00 ($350 per acre)

$5,880.00

the time of the signing of the final plat.

Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a
building permit for each structure.
Residential Enhancement Fee

Community Facility Fees were only calculated for Block 16 because when it was originally
platted it was designated as a lot for a school and did not have any fees at the time. Block 15 has
already been paid for with the previous plat.

Transportation Network (4.3):
This is a replat of an area that has previously been approved. The preliminary plat does propose
extending Ledoux Drive to connect into Railspike Road. Sirocco Drive is proposed to be
narrowed and reduced to a local right-of-way width. The original network is not being changed
with the final plat.

Open Space (4.4):
An open space parcel is provided on this plat. There are also open space easements proposed.
This track and easements are part of Sweetgrass PUD trail system.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this development.

Public Safety Fees (4.6):
Public Safety Fees are required at $949.37 per dwelling unit. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public
Safety Fees are payable at the issuance of a building permit for each structure.

The application complies with this review criterion.

4) Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and demonstrates a logical and
well-planned development pattern. This may include consideration of amenities and public
facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to the anticipated timing or the
likelihood of future phases being constructed as planned. (UDC 2.1.2.d.4)

No specific phasing is proposed with this project.

Review criterion not applicable.

5) The impacts identified in specific studies or technical reports that were prepared or requested
to be prepared in association with the application, and whether the findings and
recommendations of these reports, or any modifications or conditions on approval of the

Drainage:
A Final Stormwater Management Plan for Phase 1 of Sweetgrass was submitted and reviewed.
There were no concerns with drainage at the plat level for the project as presented.

Traffic:
A Transportation Impact Study was submitted and reviewed. There were no concerns with
traffic at the plat level for the project as presented.

The application complies with this review criterion.
