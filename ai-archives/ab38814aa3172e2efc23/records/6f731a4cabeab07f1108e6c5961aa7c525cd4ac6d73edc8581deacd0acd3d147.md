RECOMMENDED MOTION:

Move to approve the rezoning of Lot 2, Block 1, Q-B Subdivision to CB Community Business, noting that
the project meets the review criteria for approval.

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

•
UDC 2.2.1 Zoning Map Amendment
•

•

BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from MUB Mixed-use Business to CB Community
Business. The land is currently an outdoor storage area for a utility company. The purpose of the proposed
zone change is to allow for automobile service use as a use-by-right with more appropriate design standards.

Shortly after the adoption of the UDC, Staff identified various areas of the community as “mis-zoned”. The
UDC kept the same zone names and zone locations as Title 17, but the intent and applicability had changed
significantly, and some areas of the community carried forward zones for their properties which were not
optimal based on the new UDC language. One of the two areas specifically identified for change in the 2014
UDC annual review was the King Soopers shopping center. It was stated then that the entire area should be
zoned CB, not MUB. Unfortunately, Staff never had the time or resources to do a comprehensive rezoning of
the entire community, electing to go with potential ‘spot zoning’ in the interim to address properties where
issues arise.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

The Future Land Use designation for this property is Mixed-use Commercial. The Mixed‐Use
Commercial category is intended to promote a range of land uses, with primarily retail, office,
light industrial, and live/work designed as an “activity center”. Parks, plazas and/or open space
should also be part of the core of Mixed‐Use Commercial areas.

Policies in the comprehensive plan encourage commercial development near activity centers,
and the intersection of Dell Range and Ridge is considered an activity center by the plan.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

There is CB zoning to the southeast of this property. The surrounding area consists of a mix of
different residential and service uses. The CB zone would be compatible with the nearby
properties as it is a commercial zone with slightly different standards than the MUB zone.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The surrounding area better matches the expected development patterns of CB zoning. The
MUB zone requires buildings to be set closer to the streets and have a certain amount of
windows and other building design elements, whereas the CB zone pushes buildings further
from the street and requires fewer building design elements. The CB zone is also meant for
more vehicle-oriented areas which would be the case for Dell Range, Ridge, and College.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

This is in an area with an existing street network. The CB zone generally anticipates larger blocks
and is better suited to the likely development patterns in this situation.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The CB District is intended for Retail, Employment, Service and Civic uses that may serve a
number of residential neighborhoods or other districts in meeting daily needs, discretionary
needs, or other business functions. The district regulations are designed to encourage these
uses, while also protecting the abutting and nearby areas.

The CB District is applicable in areas where moderate- or large-scale business uses are
appropriate, specifically larger Mixed-use Commercial Activity Centers, Community/Regional
Activity Centers, or Community Business areas of the Comprehensive Plan.

This property is close to the Ridge Road and Dell Range intersection and the existing
development is more low-density, spread-out – better meeting the Intent and design objectives
of the CB zone, than those of the MUB zone.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The specific proposal for this project is a automobile repair facility. However, if that project is
not executed after approval, the CB zone offers a wide variety of potential auto-oriented uses at
this site (including apartments).

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No major concerns were raised by other revieing bodies.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.
