Expedited Preliminary Plat/UDC-15-00252
3
EXPEDITED REVIEW:

This plat is being pursued as an expedited application.  An expedited review process is a
concurrent preliminary and final plat. A project may be eligible for an expedited review when the
proposal contains no significant planning, design and engineering issues.  In other words
proposals that meet all applicable regulations and generally meet the review criteria for
preliminary and final plat may be eligible for the concurrent preliminary and final plat process.

Staff recommends allowing the plat to proceed as an expedited application.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

The expedited preliminary plat before you is for about 22 acres of land at the southeast corner of
the intersection of Interstate 80 and Southwest Drive. This application was submitted with a zone
change application to change the zone district to MUB Mixed-Use Business Emphasis and is
following an annexation application that was recently approved by the Governing Body. The
purpose of this plat application is to plat the land that was recently annexed land to prepare the
land for sale.

There are a few noteworthy items about this property. Across the northwest portion of the
subject property, Clear Creek enters the property. The applicant is proposing to put the current
floodplain for Clear Creek on its own lot and to dedicate said lot to the City. This will create a lot,
Lot 1, that is out of the current floodplain and able to be developed. Another note with this
property is that there is an existing drainage easement on the property that the applicant would
like to vacate with this plat. Any future drainage needs will be analyzed when a site plan is
submitted with future development. Finally, while there is sanitary sewer currently in Southwest
Drive, there is not an water nearby.

Due to meeting the review criteria found in Section 2.1.2.d of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code (UDC), staff recommends approval of this expedited preliminary
plat, advancing the final plat to the Governing Body.

PLAT DATA:  In reviewing, providing comments, and making recommendations and decisions on a
proposed preliminary plat, the Staff, Planning Commission and City Council shall use the following
criteria:

PLANCHEYENNE - The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the PlanCheyenne.

Mixed-Use Residential is the designation given to the subject area within ShapeCheyenne, which
has the future land use map within PlanCheyenne. Mixed-Use Residential areas are intended for a
variety of housing options, diversifying both density and type of housing units. As a secondary
use, Mixed-Use Residential also should include a mix of retail, offices, and light trade that are
related to the neighborhood. The LI Light Industrial zone district can work within the Mixed-Use
Residential area. LI Light Industrial has more restrictions on the uses allowed compared to HI
Heavy Industrial. The restrictions include not allowing some uses as well as certain uses requiring
more conditional use permits.

PLANNING - The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future streets,
open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities.

Clear Creek runs across the northwest corner of the property. Within this plat, the applicant is
proposing to plat off the existing 100 year floodplain and dedicating the lot containing the
existing floodplain to the City for ownership and maintenance. There are not any new rights-of-
way being proposed with this development.

STANDARDS - Division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4.

Transportation Network:
There are not any new rights-of-way proposed with plat. All lots should have access to Southwest
Drive. The area is constrained from a transportation perspective with a busy Union Pacific
railroad crossing to the north and the intersection of Southwest Drive and College Drive close to
the interchange. Future development may be limited due to these constraints. The site plan
process for future development will address traffic volumes on Southwest Drive.
Community Facility Fees:
Park Fees:
As outlined in Section 4.2.5.c.2&3 and Table 4-1, UDC, Park fees are calculated as follows:

Total Plat Area: 22.38 acres
(less) ROW Area: 0 acres
(equals) Park Fee Subject Area: 22.38 acres

Land Acquisition Fee = $350 x 22.38 or $7,833
Infrastructure Fee = $350 x 22.38 or $7,833
