THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1.
That Chapter 10.56, Handicapped Parking Spaces, is amended as follows:
10.56.050
Restriction on residential handicap parking space use.
All applications for a residential handicapped parking space and all requests for renewal
shall be submitted to the parking administration manager city clerk on forms provided.
10.56.080
Permit renewal
Applications for renewal shall be made during November and December of each year. If
a request for renewal has not been received by the parking administration manager city clerk
by December 31, the city traffic engineer shall immediately proceed to remove the handicapped
parking space signs in question.
Section 2.
This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and publication.
