BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1. That the owners of the property described herein have applied for a zoning map
amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map
Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).

Section 2. That on February 4, 2019, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted a
public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended approval of said zone change.

Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change that is
the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.

Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the aforementioned
application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property described as Lot 4,
Block 2, Swan Ranch Rail Park Third Filing, Laramie County, Wyoming is hereby changed to City
Swan Ranch PUD Planned Unit Development (extending the original Swan Ranch PUD Planned Unit
Development established by Ordinance No. 3989 to the subject property), the Regulating Plan for which
is attached hereto and incorporated herein as Exhibit A.

Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as described in
Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are severable.
If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared unconstitutional, such
unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and
phrases.

Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval, publication,
and the recording of the annexation encompassing the area described in Section 4 with the County Clerk
and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for Laramie County, Wyoming.

Zone Change Application/UDC-19-00007

P a g e | 3

This zone change application proposes changing one lot (concurrently proposed to be annexed into the
City) from County Swan Ranch PUD and County HI Heavy Industrial to City Swan Ranch PUD.  The
proposed City Swan Ranch PUD is an established PUD for the Swan Ranch Rail Park area and is very
similar to the standard City HI Heavy Industrial zone district with the exception that industry requiring rail
service can be accommodated therein.  This property is currently vacant.  The owner has proposed to
develop the site with a facility that packages and distributes cat litter.  The owner petitioned to annex to
the City to connect to City water and sewer service.

PlanCheyenne generally considers HI zoning an appropriate zone in areas designated Industrial by the
Future Land Use Map and this PUD is essentially HI zoning with a few modifications such as reduced lot
setbacks and alternate landscaping standards.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change.
