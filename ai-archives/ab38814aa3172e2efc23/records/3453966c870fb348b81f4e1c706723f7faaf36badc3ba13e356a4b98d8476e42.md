For Animal Control Services:
i.
Operate an animal control department, including a physical plant and
necessary equipment for effective animal control;
ii.
Enforce City Code relating to animal control including, but not limited to, the
issuance of warnings, summons, citations and rabies quarantine orders;
iii.
Provide vehicles and equipment necessary to perform the duties of animal
control in a humane fashion including, but not limited to, two-way radios and devices
necessary to capture, hold and transport animals;
iv.
Use professionally-trained personnel authorized by the City in the exercise
of animal control functions and provide uniforms clearly identifying personnel as animal
control officers;
v.
Employ and schedule sufficient personnel to perform animal control duties
within the City, including the provision of emergency animal control services twenty-four
(24) hours per day and seven (7) days per week. Such duties include, but are not limited to,
the control of vicious animals, the prevention and investigation ofanimal bites, the removal
of dead animals creating traffic hazards, the keeping of animals belonging to detainees of
the Cheyenne Police Department, and the capture of stray animals constituting a traffic
hazard or interfering with commerce;
vi.
Establish and maintain records documenting calls and investigations
conducted by animal control officers including, but not limited to, records of complaints
received and the resolution of each complaint, citations and warnings issued, quarantine
orders issued, records of animal bite cases reported and current rabies vaccination records;
vii.
Provide humane traps to all residents of the City on a first-in-time priority
basis;
viii.
Pick up and dispose of deceased, small animals (Le. not larger than a house
pet) and upon request ofthe State of Wyoming Game and Fish Department andJor Wyoming
veterinarians, assist with small animal management, care of sick or injured animals and
relocation of small and wild animals; and
ix.
Contractor shall not allow animal control officers to carry fire arms while
serving in that capacity.
