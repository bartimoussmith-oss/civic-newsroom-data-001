Memorandum of Understanding between
Laramie County School District #1
And
The City of Cheyenne

THIS AGREEMENT, made and entered into this  24th day of  August 2020, by and
between the City of Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized under the laws
of the State of Wyoming, acting by and through its political subdivision, the
Cheyenne Police Department, hereinafter referred to as the “Department,”
whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming  82001, and the
Laramie County School District No. 1, hereinafter referred to as the "District," whose
address is 2810 House Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the District has identified the need for law enforcement services at
various schools and school-related events under its control and supervision; and

WHEREAS, the Department is a municipal police agency engaged in providing
services to the City of Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, the District desires to obtain the law enforcement services of the
Department, and the Department desires to provide said services.

NOW, THEREFORE, IN CONSIDERATION of the mutual promises and
covenants hereinafter contained, the parties agree as follows:

1. This Agreement shall be in effect from August 1, 2020, through July 31, 2021.
The Department shall have no obligation under this Agreement to provide law enforcement
services to the District at any school location during any time that such school location
is closed by scheduled or short-term official action of the District.  Except as provided in
this Agreement, the Department shall have no obligation to provide law enforcement
services to the District, as described in this agreement, beyond the hours provided for in
this Agreement.

a.  The District shall have authority to flex an officer’s schedule to assist during
special events, short-term official closures, or other circumstances.

b.  Should any non-weather related event require long-term official closure of
school locations for more than five (5) consecutive school days, the District may request
limited law enforcement presence with the balance of officers’ daily hours redirected to
supervise schools and/or released back to the Department as best represents the interests
of both the District and the Department.  If such interests and duties cannot be agreed,
this agreement shall be suspended for the remainder of the period of official closure, and
payments vacated for duties not performed during such period.

2.    The Department shall appoint a representative who shall be responsible for
managing and coordinating the Department's performance under this Agreement, and
shall identify this representative to the District by name and telephone number.

3.      The Department agrees to provide routine law enforcement services to the
District under the terms and conditions   herein contained. The duties assumed by the
Department   under this Agreement are duties owed generally to the public, and by entering
this Agreement, the Department does not assume any other duty to the District, nor does
the Department assume any duty to any particular individual.

4. The parties agree that this Agreement does not create an agency relationship
between the Department and the District, and no representations of any kind, type or
