to query by Mr. Basom regarding frequency and cost of training, Chief Kozak provided
information on the frequency of Traffic Incident Management System (TIMS) training and who
would be trained, stating the Police Department will provide training at no cost to participants and
that owners/participants attending can then train others.  Jodi Shea, attorney with Pence and
MacMillan, representing I-80 Towing and its affiliate towing companies, requested the ordinance
be tabled for further discussion.  She commented on day to day effects on towing companies the
ordinance will have, including financial concerns; safe, effective and efficient services; fees for
services, and common goals for improvements.  Ms. Shea proposed alternative solutions involving
tabling of the ordinance to allow towers to address issues and enable additional communication
efforts, and developing a committee to evaluate complaints, identify issues and propose solutions
to concerns that have been raised.  Ms. Shea further discussed issues she felt need to be re-
addressed, including fee schedules; requirement of being open four hours on weekends; forms of
payment for services; vehicle “hold” time; salvage license requirement, and personal property
contents within towed vehicles.  Randy LeBeaumont, Randy’s Towing; Bob Cairns, Rocky

storage lot zoning and compliance; current police rotation list requirements and regulating
companies through rotation list enforcement; allowing towing companies to work together to
address issues and concerns; support for postponement and formation of a committee to study and
offer solutions to issues of concern, and upcoming meeting (invitation extended to governing
body, law enforcement and others interested) of local towing companies to revamp the towers’
group and further discuss the proposed ordinance and call list rate resolution.  Speaking in favor
