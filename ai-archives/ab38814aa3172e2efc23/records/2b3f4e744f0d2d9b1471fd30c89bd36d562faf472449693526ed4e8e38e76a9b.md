MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT #_________________
BETWEEN CITY OF CHEYENNE, A MUNICIPAL CORPORATION
And
Arts Cheyenne, a 501(c)3 Non-Profit Organization
THIS MEMORANDUM OF AGREEMENT made this ______ day of ___________, 2022, by and
between Arts Cheyenne (ARTS CHEYENNE) and City of Cheyenne (the City), provides that:

WHEREAS, Arts Cheyenne was formed as a non-profit entity in 2008 for the purpose of promoting
Cheyenne as a vibrant arts community; and

WHEREAS, Resolution No. 5954 designates Arts Cheyenne as the Local Arts agency for the City
of Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, Arts Cheyenne plans to host events in the West Edge in the summer of 2022 to further
community involvement and education on West Edge development; and

WHEREAS, the City, has adopted plans in 2014, 2016, and 2018 stating their commitment to the
redevelopment of the West Edge; and,

WHEREAS, the City, as implementation steps to the adopted plans, has created the Urban Use
Overlay in the Unified Development Code and plans to apply it to the West Edge to foster
redevelopment; and,

WHEREAS, the City has determined that the summer events hosted by Arts Cheyenne are
important implementation steps of the West Edge vision and has determined that this project is
deserving of public support.

NOW, THEREFORE, the City and Arts Cheyenne in consideration of the mutual covenants and
promises set forth herein hereby agree, covenant and represent as follows:

Terms

1.
The City grants permission to Arts Cheyenne to paint, construct, or install public
art in the City Right-of-Way. The public art may include, but is not limited to painted crosswalks,
street murals, kinetic art, or QR Codes directing citizens to information on future development.

2.
Prior to the painting, construction, or installation of said public art, Arts Cheyenne
shall obtain a Right-of-Way Encroachment Permit from the City Engineer. The application for the
permit will contain a traffic control plan created by a licensed contractor that will specify any road
closures or rerouting necessary to paint, construct, or install public art.

3.
Arts Cheyenne shall only paint, construct, or install public art as approved by the
City Engineer by the Engineer Plan Review Process. This will be a plan sheet at minimum showing
proposed locations, layout, pavement markings, parking, and drive lanes.

4.
Arts Cheyenne and their artists shall complete all painting, construction, or
installation of public arts in accordance with the current edition of the City of Cheyenne and Board
of Public Utilities Construction Specifications and Standard Drawings.

5.
Arts Cheyenne may make any necessary repairs to the public art, including, but not
limited to, restoring a damaged portion of the public art. Any said repairs may require an additional
Right-of-Way Encroachment Permit or Engineering Plan Review. City is not responsible for any
maintenance of public art installed by Arts Cheyenne.

6.
The City reserves the right to inspect public art installations during construction
and at the time of completion. Should the City find the installations do not meet the approved plans
or specifications, the City may remove said art at Arts Cheyenne’s expense.

7.
The City reserves the right to remove any public art installed in the right-of-way
for maintenance or other actions the City deems necessary. Removal due to maintenance or
aforementioned actions shall be at the City’s expense. The City will not be held responsible for
replacement of public art.

8.
The Artist(s) warrants and represents that the renderings and the Public Art have
never heretofore been published or copied and that the Artist(s) is the sole owner of all rights
therein, including but not limited to the right to prevent the making and dissemination of copies,
and the right to obtain statutory copyright extending to him/her the exclusive right to manufacture
and sell the above items and copies for a full statutory term and renewal thereof.

9.
Arts Cheyenne shall not assign or attempt to assign, directly or indirectly, any of
its rights under this Agreement or under any instrument referred to herein without the prior written
consent of the City.

10.
Neither this Agreement nor any provision hereof may be changed, waived,
discharged, or terminated orally and may only be modified or amended by an instrument in writing,
signed by the parties hereto.

11.
All rights, powers, and remedies herein given the City are cumulative and not
alternative, and are in addition to all other statutes or rules of law. Any forbearance or delay by the
City in exercising the same shall not be deemed to be a waiver thereof and the exercise of any right
or partial exercise thereof shall not preclude the further exercise thereof and the same shall
continue in full force and effect until specifically waived by an instrument in writing executed by
the City. All representations, warranties, covenants and the provisions hereof shall be binding upon
Arts Cheyenne, its successors and assigns and inure to the benefit of the City, its successors and
assigns.
