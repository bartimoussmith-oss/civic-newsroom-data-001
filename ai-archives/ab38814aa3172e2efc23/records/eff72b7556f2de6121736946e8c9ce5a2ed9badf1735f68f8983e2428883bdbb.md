2.29.010
Purpose-Authority-General provisions.
A.
The purpose of this chapter is to recognize and approve the existing community
service officers(CsO) program implemented by the Cheyenne police department and set forth the
qualifications for residents interested in serving as community service officers.
B.The chief of police may appoint residents of Laramie County as a CSO to serve under
the rules, orders and regulations of the Cheyenne police department. CSO's may be volunteers or,
at the discretion of the police chief, paid employees.
C.The CsO program supports the functions of the police department by assisting police
are specifically community care-taking functions.CSO's may enforce city ordinances involving
municipal violations following an investigation ofa traffic crash, parking violations, animal control
violations and nuisance control violations, at the direction of the police chieforhis or her designee.
D.
A subset of the CSO program is further created toprovide security servicesfor the
protection of life, limb and property at municipal facilities and surrounding premises. This subset
shall be known as Municipal Security Officers (MSO's).
2.29.020
Qualifications and training.
A.
The following criteria must be met by CSO/MSO applicants to be considered for
appointment:
1.
CSO's must be Aat least eighteen (18) years of age; MSO's must be at least
twenty-one (21) years of age;
2.
Highschool diploma or recognized equivalent;
3.
Physically able to perform CSO/M SO functions as directed;
4.
Nofelony convictions; and
5.
Valid Wyoming driver's license.
The chief of police, ordesignee, will identify, develop and implement an appropriate
training program to ensure aCSO/M SO can perform required duties in a professional and competent
manner.
C.
MSO's must complete a required forty (40) hour training on the use of firearms and
completea minimum ofsix(6)firearm training days each year.
