UDC-22-00096
May 9, 2022
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

UDC 2.4.2 Annexations

PlanCheyenne

BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting annexing of 1.2 acres of land to place the entire property under one jurisdiction.
Currently, the property is divided between City and County jurisdiction. Despite this, the County Assessor
presently shows both the City and County areas as one combined parcel. The land is currently developed in
both jurisdictions with an industrial use. The user of the property desired to expand the business by placing a
new building. A zone change is proposed concurrently with this annexation. Both applications are anticipated
to come to final consideration before the Governing Body at the same meeting.

Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. The petition to annex has been accompanied by a submission
to change the zoning from County LI Light Industrial to the City LI Light Industrial zone.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned LI Light Industrial in the County. The property is concurrently proposed
to be annexed and rezoned to the City LI Light Industrial zone. The City LI zone is intended for
environmentally controlled manufacturing or intensive employment uses that may have accessory
commercial, service or office uses, but which cannot readily fit into other general business or
mixed-use areas due to the nature or intensity of operations.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

To accept the petition to annex and approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make
the following findings required in WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below:

1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of the
persons residing in the area and in the city or town.  [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)]

The annexation of this property will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the occupants. By
being in City limits, the property will receive access to City Fire and Police and sanitation

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a natural,
geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (ii)]

This property, once developed, will constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part
of the city. The property is largely surrounded by the City (the non-annexed area adjacent is
mostly WYDOT right-of-way). The contiguity, the fact the ownership parcel is already partially in
the City, and the proximity to surrounding urban-style development makes the parcel a natural
geographic part of the City. The development of this property will benefit those who live within
the City and will be a part of the social and economic fabric that is Cheyenne.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city or town
and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of the city or
town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a)
(iii)]

The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection
will be extended upon annexation. Utilities already exist in the area.

The application complies with this review criterion.

4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or town, or
the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iv)]

The area proposed for annexation is about 54% contiguous, along the eastern edge.

The application complies with this review criterion.

5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the annexed area pursuant
to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)]

Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.

The application complies with this review criterion.
