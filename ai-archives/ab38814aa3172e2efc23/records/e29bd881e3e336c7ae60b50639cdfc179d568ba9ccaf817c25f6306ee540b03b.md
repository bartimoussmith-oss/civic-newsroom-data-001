ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION CERTIFYING THE EASTDELL RANGE
BOULEVARD/U.S. 30 CORRIDOR STUDY.”
WHEREAS, East Dell Range Boulevard, between College Drive and U.S. Highway 30, is
classified as a Principal Arterial and jurisdictional responsibilities fall with both the City of
Cheyenne and Laramie County; and
WHEREAS, U.S. Highway 30 between College Drive and the U.P.R.R. overpass near the
Archer Interchange is a Principal Arterial with jurisdictional responsibility by the Wyoming
Department of Transportation; and
WHEREAS, this 2019 East Dell Range Boulevard/US.
30 Corridor Study is
a
reevaluation and update to a similar 2008 study; and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) retained Kimley
Horn and Associates, Inc. on June 13, 2018 to develop the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30
Corridor Study; and
WHEREAS, the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study identified and
addressed roadway deficiencies, traffic safety problems, traffic volume growth, multimodal needs,
environmental constraints, and aligned roadway functionality and character with planned land uses
and desired character; and
WHEREAS, the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 corridor Study and resulting
preliminary design provides the partner agencies (city, county and state) and the public with an
implementation strategy of investments to meet the transportation need throughout the study area
into the future; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has 5th Penny Sales Tax funds programmed for the
reconstruction of East Dell Range between College Drive and James Street; and
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Department of Transportation has funds programmed through
the Cheyenne MPO Policy Committee to reconstruct the Dell Range and U.S. Highway 30
intersection and the Dell Range Boulevard and Whitney Road intersection; and
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Department of Transportation has funds programmed to
reconstruct U.S. Highway 30 between East Pershing and the U.P.R.R. Overpass; and
WHEREAS, these infrastructure project investments will enhance safety, multimodal
operation, and accessibility, while the recommended improvements support the larger economic,
environmental, and livability goals of the community; and

WHEREAS, public involvement for the project consisted of three well attended public
open houses, (August 21, 2018, December 4, 2018 and April 16, 2019) and on-line surveys using
SurveyMonkey© to gather feedback on issues and recommendations; and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne MPO Technical and Citizens’ Advisory Committees reviewed
the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study and recommended adoption by the MPO
Policy Committee; and
WHEREAS, the Lararnie County Planning Commission held a public hearing on October
10, 2019, accepted public comments, and recommended that the Board of Commissioners approve
the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study; and
WHEREAS, the City ofCheyenne Planning Commission held a public hearing on October
21, 2019, accepted public comments, and recommended that the City of Cheyenne Governing
Body certify the East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:
THAT, the City of Cheyenne Governing Body hereby certifies the “East Dell Range
Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study” dated September 2019; and
THAT, the “East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30 Corridor Study” amends the Cheyenne
Area Master Transportation Plan.
FURTHERMORE, BE IT RESOLVED, that the “East Dell Range Boulevard/US. 30
Corridor Study” will be used as guidance for the future design and reconstruction of East Dell
Range and U.S. 30.
