RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE EFFORTS OF THE CHEYENNE
SOCCER
CLUB
TO
RAISE
FUNDS
TOWARD
DESIGN,
CONSTRUCTION, MANAGEMENT, AND ON-GOING MAINTENANCE
OF AN INDOOR SOCCER COMPLEX TO BE LOCATED ON A PORTION
OF THE CITY OWNED NORTH CHEYENNE COMMUNITY PARK."

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club has been in existence since November 1, 1978;
Cheyenne Soccer Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club currently has a membership in Laramie County of
about two thousand (2,000) players;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club practices on several fields owned by the City of
Cheyene including North Cheyenne Community Park;

WHEREAS, weather plays a significant factor in the ability of the Cheyenne Soccer Club
to hold practices and games for its members.  For example, since the beginning of the spring season
2022 through the summer season of 2023, the Cheyenne Soccer Club experienced the cancellation
of approximately one hundred and eighty-two (182) games out of a scheduled one thousand two
hundred thirty-four (1,234) games, which represents a cancellation rate of nearly fifteen percent
(15%);

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club has identified a demonstratable need to offer year-
around opportunities for indoor recreation for the community for a variety of sports including
football, baseball, lacrosse, and soccer;

WHEREAS, outside groups such as youth organizations, Laramie County School District
Number One, and Laramie County Community College have expressed a need and a desire for an
indoor facility for practices and games;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club currently utilizes several fields throughout the City
of Cheyenne which requires that parents transport children to various locations throughout the
community.  One main, indoor facility could combine most of these facilities into one location;

WHEREAS, North Cheyenne Community Park currently experiences a great deal of use
resulting in wear and tear to the field;

WHEREAS, there are very few options for indoor facilities in the state of Wyoming and
Northern Colorado, especially during winter months;

WHEREAS, nothing like the facility proposed by the Cheyenne Soccer Club is currently
being built in Southeast Wyoming;

WHEREAS, North Cheyenne Community Park consists of one hundred thirty-four (134)
acres. Of the total amount, thirty-six (36) acres have been designated for outdoor recreation.  North
Cheyenne Community Park has enough room for an indoor facility in addition to outdoor space.
The City’s parks master plan would create a synergy between the outdoor fields and the indoor
fields, safety, convenience, and room;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club has created a rendering of a proposed indoor
facility (which is attached and incorporated herein);

WHEREAS, full build out of this project would consist of two (2) full size indoor fields
for a total project expense of seventy million dollars ($70,000,000) depending on design; the initial
goal is to have this facility built in three (3) years;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Soccer Club has a long-standing relationship with the city of
Cheyenne and the development of recreational activities for the Cheyenne community;

WHEREAS, in the past, the Cheyenne Soccer Club has raised funds for programmatic and
operational areas of their organization, including car wash fundraisers for their scholarship
program, and gala fundraisers for programs and operations;

WHEREAS; in support of this project, the Cheyenne Soccer Club intends to raise funds
through a variety of opportunities such as foundation grants, government grants and funding,
corporate sponsorships, naming rights for the indoor complex, individual contributions, and rental
fees, to provide for design, construction, management, and on-going maintenance of the proposed
facility;

WHEREAS, outdoor recreation has a significant positive economic impact for the City of
Cheyenne in the amount of eight million two hundred thousand dollars ($8,200,000) a year;

WHEREAS; once the facility is built, Cheyenne Soccer Club will put together a
Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with the City of Cheyenne and will enter into separate
agreements with existing users of North Cheyenne Community Park with the intention of not
removing any existing uses, but reorganizing some of the current uses to fully utilize the property
for the benefit of all users; and

WHEREAS; The Cheyenne Soccer Club is committed and understands that the design and
construction costs as well as future management and on-going maintenance costs of the future
indoor soccer facility will be the responsibility of the Cheyenne Soccer Club. The role of the City
of Cheyenne will be to provide land for the facility.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor and members of City Council support the idea of
utilizing a portion of the North Cheyenne Community Park to accommodate a proposed indoor
soccer complex as well as efforts towards fundraising for the design, construction, management,
and on-going maintenance by the Cheyenne Soccer Club.
