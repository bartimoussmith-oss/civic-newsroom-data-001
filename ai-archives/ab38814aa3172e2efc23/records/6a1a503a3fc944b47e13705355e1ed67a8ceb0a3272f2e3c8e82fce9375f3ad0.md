COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Committee of the Whole was held on Wednesday, December 4, 2024. in Council
Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at 6:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
President Ken Esquibel, Chairman: Dr. Michelle Aldrich (via
Zoom); Bryan Cook; Richard Johnson; Pete Laybourn; Scott Roybal: Tom Segrave; and Jeff
White. Absent: Dr. Mark Rinne.
AGENDA ITEMS
14.
ORDINANCE —3 READING
— Amending Section 8.60.080, Non-Compliant Vehicles as
a Nuisance, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyeime. Wyoming, declaring oversized
vehicles as a nuisance, providing for their regulation, clarifying provisions and making
conforming amendments.
Mr. Johnson moved to approve on 3”’ and final reading, seconded by Dr. Aldrich.
Mr. Johnson
moved to amend by substitute dated December 4, 2024, seconded by Mr. White. Dr. Aldrich
moved to amend, the amendment, to remove number (1) in section 8.60.080(8), seconded by Mr.
Johnson. Upon discussion, a friendly amendment by Mr. Johnson was made to add removing the
“Labor Day and Memorial Day” language in section 8.60.080(8) to the previous motion to align
with the intent to remove the allowance of parking oversized vehicles during the period of”5 days
before the Memorial Day holiday to 5 days following the Labor Day holiday.” Motion to amend,
the amendment, failed with all members voting “NO” with the exception of Dr. Aldrich voting
“YES.” Motion to amend carried with President Esquibel and committee members Cook, Johnson,
Roybal. and White voting “YES” and Aldrich and Laybourn voting “NO.” Mr. Roybal moved to
postpone until the December 23, 2024, meeting of the governing body, seconded by Mr. Cook.
Motion to postpone failed with President Esquibel and committee members Aldrich, Johnson,
Laybourn, and Segrave voting “NO” and Cook. Roybal, and White voting “YES.” Main motion,
as amended, carried with President Esquibel and committee members Johnson, Roybal. Segrave,
and White voting “YES” and Aldrich, Cook, and Laybourn, voting “NO.”
Ward Ill Councilman, Richard Johnson, and co-sponsor of the proposed ordinance provided a brief
review of the progress of the proposed ordinance and provided information on an amendment.
John Brodie, Deputy City Attorney, and Chief Mark Francisco, Police Department, provided
information related to the proposed amendment which included the addition of enforcement and
permitting guidelines. President Esquibel and committee members Aldrich, Cook, Johnson,
Layboui-n, Roybal, Segrave, and White commented on the item. Public comments were made by
the following: Lany Atwell. Abigail Boudewyns, Georgia Broyles, Lori Chestnut, David Cook,
Cathy Deistar. Maeke Ermarth, Paul Howard. Matthew Ilumphery, Lee Filer, Ryan Lacey, Steve
Manning, Joe McCord, Jon Puls, Alan Sheldon, Bill Strickland. Catelyn Wilson, and Mike
Woodworth.
