PLANNING.The application reflects sound planningand urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future streets,
openspaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities.
With an elevated track to the east and the Crow Creek Floodplain to the north, this site will rely entirely
on Westland and Old Happy Jack Roads for access. Westland Road is classified as a collector, while Old
Happy Jack Road is classified as local. No new ROW is proposed. However, its close proximity to I-25
will provide an excellent location for those businesses that need immediate access to the interstate.At
over5 acres, the project requires the construction of an internal access way to provide for adequate
circulation.These access ways will look and function like a street.
STANDARDs.Division of land meets thepurposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article4.
Community Facility Fees in the amount of $7,480.00 were paid in 2010 when the area was platted as
Classic Subdivision. As the fees have not changed in the last 3 years, no payment is required as it would
be duplicative (ref.4.2.5.a.1, UDC).
No new Right-of-Way is being proposed with this application.However, any single block, lot or
development site larger than 5 acres shall provide a system of internal access ways to establish
connectivity and mobility within the site and coordinate with streets and blocks adjacent to the site(ref.
Article 6, Section 6.2.3.b., UDC). The Internal access ways will be built to resemble the
Commercial/Industrial street design described in section 4.3.4, UDC (page 4-18).
While Plan Cheyenne certainly recommends some open space inthe context of a Mixed-Use Employment
Campus, there are no Open Space requirements for a site in the LI district (ref., Table 4-15, UDC).
