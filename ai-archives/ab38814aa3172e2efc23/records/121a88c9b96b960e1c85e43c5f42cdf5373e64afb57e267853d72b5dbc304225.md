RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

February 23, 2017

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:06 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Rocky Case, Brian
Cook, Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Mike Luna, Dr. Mark Rinne and Dicky Shanor.  Absent:
Scott Roybal and Jeff White.  Also present were:  Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Sylvia Hackl, City
Attorney; Alessandra McCoy, Assistant City Attorney; Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and Tom
Schingle, Interim Chief, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded, and stated that Wyoming State
Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings Act allows the governing body to meet in executive
session to consider or tender offers regarding salaries, wages or other terms and conditions of
employment during negotiations with the fire fighters union and that any motion approved by the
governing body to move into executive session during the special meetings would be pursuant to
this section of statute.  Mayor Orr further advised if the governing body met in executive session,
they would be meeting in the City Council Administrative office, and that Fire Union
representatives were welcome to use Room 104 for the purpose of meeting to caucus.

It was noted that spokespersons for the City during negotiations would be Mayor Orr and
Councilman Shanor and Union spokespersons would be Jonathan Bertsch, President, and Andrew
Dykshorn, Local Union #279.  Mayor Orr confirmed that any agreement reached between the City
and the Union must be approved by the governing body with the agreement placed on a regular
City Council meeting agenda and approved through the Council’s usual procedures.  Mayor Orr
further confirmed that proposed Ground Rules had previously been shared between the parties.
Mr. Bertsch requested consideration that, per wording in Section 2 of the City’s proposed Rules,
the Union’s negotiation team be increased from not more than five members to up to eight
members, and requested clarification regarding wording in Section 9 with regard to whether
interaction with City Council members would be entertained in addition to the City’s
spokespersons.  Mr. Bertsch expressed the Union’s preference to approve proposed agreement
provisions on a section-by-section basis, which differs from proposed wording in Section 13.

Mr. Shanor moved to meet in executive session, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Motion carried.
Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present.  Mayor Orr identified City staff
requested to be present at the executive session in addition to members of the governing body.
[City Clerk note: Audio recording of the meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 6:19
p.m. in the City Council Administrative office]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:03 p.m.  Mr. Shanor reported the governing body’s position on the following sections of the
proposed Ground Rules:

Section 2 - The City agrees to increasing the number of members on the Union’s
negotiating team from five to not more than eight members, and requests the Union agree to
increasing the number of members on the City’s negotiating team from three to not more than six
members.

Section 9 – The City prefers the language as written, however, informally if members of
either negotiating team wish to submit written questions to their respective spokespersons, the
spokespersons can determine whether to pose the question(s) or not, but there would be no
obligation for response by either side.

Section 13 – The City prefers the language as written which states “ … the collective labor
agreement shall be agreed to and signed off as a single document, not on a section-by-section
basis”.

Upon inquiry by Mr. Bertsch, it was explained the purpose of requesting an increase to the
number of members for the City’s negotiating team was to include, if needed, additional staff
members.  Discussion followed between the spokespersons regarding Sections 9 and 13.  Upon
request by Mr. Bertsch, Mayor Orr recessed the special meeting at 7:08 p.m. to allow Union
representatives to caucus on the City’s offer regarding the Ground Rules.  [City Clerk note:  Audio
recording of the meeting was stopped]

Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at 7:15 p.m.  Mr. Bertsch reported the Union’s
position was to accept the City’s proposed Ground Rules (as amended), and commented on the
Union’s reasons for previously requesting consideration of changes to Sections 9 and 13.  Mr.
Shanor moved to approve the Ground Rules as amended, seconded by Mr. Case.  Motion carried.
Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.

Exchange of proposed Collective Labor Agreements by the Union and the City was
conducted followed by opening statements by Mr. Bertsch and Mr. Dykshorn relative to the
Union’s proposal.  Mayor Orr recessed the meeting at 7:32 p.m. to allow the Union representatives
to set up equipment to use in conjunction with a power point presentation affiliated with the
Union’s proposed agreement.  Mayor Orr reconvened the meeting at 7:55 p.m. and clarified the
City’s proposed agreement is not “as is” and that it contains a 1% salary increase in the base wage
with a contract term of one year.  Mr. Bertsch and Mr. Dykshorn presented and reviewed their
power point slides.  Following conclusion of the presentation and upon inquiry, Mr. Bertsch
provided additional information pertaining to data sources used relative to the information in the
presentation involving personnel response standards.

It was agreed that two originals of the Ground Rules would be signed at the current
meeting, one for each party, and that the next special City Council meetings for negotiation
purposes would be held Tuesday, February 28, 2017 and Thursday, March 2, 2017 each beginning
