27. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a subdivision permit and plat for Bison
Crossing, 11th Filing, situated in the SW1/4 of Sect 30 and the NW1/4
of Sect 31, T13N, R66W of the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY.
24 - 137
Bison Crossing, 11th Filing, Subdivision Permit and
Plat
Attachments:
Bryce Hamilton, Planning, said Lovas Engineering, on behalf of TFS II, submitted a
subdivision permit and plat application for property located generally in the SW1/4 of
Sect 30 and NW1/4 of Sect 31, T13N, R66W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY.
The application was submitted to subdivide roughly 34 acres into 12 residential lots of
approximately three (2.84) acres each.
Hamilton said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the
subdivision permit and plat with the adoption of findings of fact a and b with one
condition: A nonadverse Chapter 23 recommendation is provided by DEQ prior to
recordation.
Chris Lovas, Lovas Engineering and the applicant's agent, said the subdivision will tie
into a privately maintained public water supply, and sewer will be provided by onsite
septic and leach fields.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Malm, seconded by Commissioner
Holmes, to adopt findings of fact a and b of the staff report and to approve
the subdivision permit and plat for Bison Crossing,11th Filing, with one
condition.
Commissioner Malm clarified the AMEC memo was not applied to this subdivision,
as it will be on a private water system. Justin Arnold, Planning, confirmed that the
water system, Winchester Hills Utility Co, was permitted as a public water system in
1979, and regulations state that it is a grandfathered water system that can continue
and expand its service.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Lovett, Malm, Heath, Holmes, Thompson
