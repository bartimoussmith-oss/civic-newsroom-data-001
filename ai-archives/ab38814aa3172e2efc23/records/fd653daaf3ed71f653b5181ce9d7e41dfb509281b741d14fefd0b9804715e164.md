COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Committee of the Whole was held on Wednesday, January 14, 2026, in Council
Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at 6:00 p.m. This meeting was held a week early
due to council member availability. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Tom Segrave, Council President and Chairman; Dr. Michelle
Aldrich; Dr. Kathy Emmons; Ken Esquibel; Pete Layboum; Mark Moody; Dr. Mark Rinne;
Lawrence Wolfe; and Jeff White.

17.
RESOLUTION
Establishing the City of Cheyenne’s prioritized list of projects proposed
for potential inclusion on the One Percent (1%) Specific Purpose Sales and Use Tax Ballot
proposition to be submitted to the qualified electors of Laramic County at the Primary
Election on August 18, 2026. (SPONSOR

Mr. Moody moved to amend by substitute dated January 13, 2026, which included increasing “City
Street Maintenance Project” from nine million dollars ($9,000,000.00) to twenty million dollars
($20,000,000.00) and replacing (#10) “Municipal Building Remodel Project” at twenty-two
million dollars ($22,000,000.00) with “Fire Station #6 Replacement and Relocation Project” at
eleven million dollars ($11,000,000.00), seconded by Dr. Aldrich. Mr. Moody moved to amend
the substitute to remove “Fire Station #6 Replacement and Relocation Project” and re-insert
“Municipal Building Remodel Project” at eleven million dollars ($11,000,000.00), seconded by
Mr. Esquibel. Motion to amend the substitute failed with all members voting “NO” except for Mr.
Moody who voted “YES.” Motion to amend by substitute failed with all members voting “NO.”
