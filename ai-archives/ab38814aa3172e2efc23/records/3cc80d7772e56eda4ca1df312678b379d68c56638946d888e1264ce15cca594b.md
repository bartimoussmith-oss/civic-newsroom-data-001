Please explain your interest in serving on this
As of young adult with a disability, I feel that each and
Board/Committee/Commission
every one of us deserves to live the best life possible.
To me, living the best life possible is dependent on
community involvement and memorable experiences.
In my experience, these memorable experiences
come from attending a Symphony concert at the
Cheyenne CMc Center, being able to take a walk in
your neighborhood, and being able to dine at local
restaurants. As an individual with a disability, there
have been several times where the walk in my
neighborhood was not enjoyable because there was
no curb cut out for me to access with my wheelchair.
Some restaurants I have been unable to access due to
lack of accessibility. Limited transportation makes
participating in community events difficult for those
who can’t drive, I have experienced this many times.
Cheyenne is a great community, however for those of
us with a disability sometimes it’s great attributes go
unseen. I feel that every Cheyenne resident should
have the opportunity to embrace our community to
the fullest by living and working among community
members. No resident with a disability should be
denied the opportunities the Cheyenne Community
presents. I believe that with my experience with living
with a disability in Cheyenne, I can bring valuable
knowledge to the improvements that need to be made
in order for Cheyenne residents to enjoy Cheyenne.
No resident in any Community should have to struggle
to enjoy their community and by serving on the
mayor’s Council for people with disabilities, I feel that
I can help in that struggle for Cheyenne community
members.
