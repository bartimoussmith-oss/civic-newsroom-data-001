ORDINANCE NO. ____________________

ENTITLED:
“AN ORDINANCE PURSUANT TO SECTION 2.2.1 ZONING MAP
AMENDMENTS, SECTION 5.1.2 ZONING DISTRICTS ESTABLISHED,
AND SECTION 5.1.3 OFFICIAL ZONING MAP, OF THE UNIFIED
DEVELOPMENT CODE, CHANGING THE ZONING CLASSIFICATION
FROM COUNTY MR MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL TO MR-2
MEDIUM DENSITY RESIDENTIAL-DEVELOPING FOR A PORTION OF
LOT 6, ALL OF LOTS 7 AND 8, AND THE WEST 12 FEET OF LOT 9,
BLOCK 2, PLUS THAT ADJACENT 8 FEET OF VACATED ALLEY,
COULSON ADDITION, CHEYENNE, WYOMING (LOCATED NORTH OF
AND ADJACENT TO RAWLINS STREET, WEST OF MCCANN
AVENUE).”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1. That the owner of the property described herein, has applied for a zoning map
amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map
Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).

Section 2. That on August 3, 2015, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted a
public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended approval of said zone change.

Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change that
is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.

Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the aforementioned
application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property described as a portion
of Lot 6, all of Lots 7 and 8, and the west 12 feet of Lot 9, Block 2, plus that adjacent 8 feet of vacated
alley, Coulson Addition, Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby changed to MR-2 Medium Density
Residential-Developing (See attached Exhibit A).

Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as described in
Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional, such unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections, paragraphs,
sentences, clauses, and phrases.

Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and
publication.

Zone Change Request/UDC-15-00441
3
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application is for a property located at 3714 Rawlins Street that is being
annexed into the City. This zone change application will change the zone district from County MR
Medium Density Residential to MR-2 Medium Density Residential-Developing. The owner of the
property is requesting annexation so that he can connect to City sanitary sewer. A zone district of
MR-2 would be consistent with the other parcels in the area that are within City limits.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.1.2.d of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change, advancing it to
the Governing Body.

Zone Change Request/UDC-15-00441
4
ZONE CHANGE DATA:  In reviewing, providing comments, and making recommendations and decisions
on a proposed Zone Change Request, the Staff, Planning Commission and City Council shall use the
following criteria:

PLANCHEYENNE - The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.

PlanCheyenne has designated this property as Urban Residential on the future land use map and
is found within the Urban Service Boundary. Within the Urban Residential land use, a desired
characteristic according to PlanCheyenne is to connect to City water and sanitary sewer. With the
purpose of this annexation being that the property can be connected to City sanitary sewer, this
application is in accordance with PlanCheyenne.

SURROUNDING AREA - The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing
zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance of
that plan for these areas.

The application is consistent with the uses recommended in PlanCheyenne. The application to
change to MR-2 is compatible to the surrounding area as it would be changing the zone match
the properties within the area that are already within the City.

ZONING: BASIC STANDARDS & USES - The suitability of the subject property for development according to
the standards and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property
for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district;

There are not any plans for redevelopment. The purpose of this zone change is that since
property is also being annexed, it must be given a City zone district. The property, already being
used as a residential property, is suitable for the MR-2 district should there be any redevelopment
in the future.

STREETS & OPEN SPACE - The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed
according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning;

As stated, there are no plans for development other than connecting to City sanitary sewer.
Should the property redevelop in the in future, it should be able to be developed according to the
standards found in Article 4.

ZONING: INTENT, APPLICABILITY & DESIGN OBJECTIVES - The ability of the application to support the
Intent and Applicability statements of the proposed zoning district, and achieve the Design Objectives of
any specific standards.

Since the property is already being used as a residential property, it has the ability to meet the
Intent and Applicability statements found in the MR-2 district. There are no design requirements
for single family homes found in the MR-2 district.
