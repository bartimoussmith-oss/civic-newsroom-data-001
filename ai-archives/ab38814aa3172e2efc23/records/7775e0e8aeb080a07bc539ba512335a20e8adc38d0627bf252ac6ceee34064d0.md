RESOLUTION NO. _________
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION DISSOLVING THE LARAMIE COUNTY EARLY
CHILDHOOD EDUCATION AND DEVELOPMENT JOINT POWERS
BOARD.”
WHEREAS, on February 9, 2009 the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming,
approved an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne, the County of Laramie and the Laramie
County Community College creating the Laramie County Early Childhood Education and
Development Joint Powers Board; and
WHEREAS, Section 11. of the Agreement authorizes the dissolution of the Joint Powers
Board “ ... by a resolution adopted by at least two-thirds (2/3rds) of the governing bodies of the
participating agencies”; and
WHEREAS, by letter dated March 19, 2013, the secretary of the Joint Powers Board has
asked participating agencies to dissolve the Board, indicating that: the Board failed to receive
anticipated grant funding from the State Land and Investment Board; the participating agencies
provided no funds to the Board at its inception; the Board has ceased to meet; and, the Board has no
assets and no outstanding financial obligations.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Laramie County Early Childhood Education and
Development Joint Powers Board is dissolved, effective upon the adoption of like Resolutions by
a total of two-thirds (2/3rds) of the participating agencies to the Agreement.
PRESENTED AND ADOPTED this __________ day of ______________, 2013.
(S E A L)
____________________________________
RICHARD L. KAYSEN, MAYOR
ATTEST:
__________________________________
CAROL INTLEKOFER, CITY CLERK
