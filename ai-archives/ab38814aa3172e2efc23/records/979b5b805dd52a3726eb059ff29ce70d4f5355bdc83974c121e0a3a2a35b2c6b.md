Section 1. That the owner of the property described herein, has applied for a zoning map

recommended approval of said zone change.

Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change that
is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.

of Lots 3 and 4; and Lot 2, Block 252, Original City, Cheyenne, Wyoming is hereby changed to MUB
Mixed-Use Business Emphasis.

Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as described in
Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.

publication.

Zone Change Request/UDC-15-00704
3
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application is for two properties located at 1315 E. 21st Street and 2019
Alexander Avenue. Both properties are owned by the Wyoming Eye Institute, LLC, who also owns
the adjacent property to the south where their main facility resides. This zone change application
will reassign the zone district from MR-1 Medium-Density Residential-Established to MUB Mixed-
Use Business Emphasis. The owner of these properties is proposing a new use for the existing
structure at 1315 E. 21st Street as a billing office and continuing the use of 2019 Alexander
Avenue as a private residence.

The subject properties and the surrounding parcels have been classified as Urban Residential
within PlanCheyenne. A zone district of MUB would be consistent with secondary uses described
by the Urban Residential classification for use as neighborhood business. This zone change will
allow for future redevelopment at the site as a majority of the parcels will be of the same zone
and under the same ownership.

PlanCheyenne further supports this zone change by promoting the expansion of an existing
business thus promoting economic growth, allowing possible future redevelopment in an older
neighborhood, and allowing an expanded set of permitted uses that can be flexible to market
demands.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change, advancing it to
the Governing Body.

ZONING: BASIC STANDARDS & USES - The suitability of the subject property for development according to
the standards and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property
for development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district;

The primary purpose of this zone change is to allow for a billing office operate at one of the
existing structures. The secondary purpose of this change is to bring all the properties owned by
the institute under the same zoning district as to allow for possible future redevelopment. The
second structure is to remain a residence for the foreseeable future. Both a residential detached
dwelling and an office employment use are permitted by right in the MUB zone.

The property, currently zoned MR-1, is suitable for
the MUB District. A rezoning is necessary to allow
for most employment and commercial uses to occur
on this parcel. In the future a number of uses could
occur if rezoned. These include numerous
residential types by permitted right, civic uses by
administrative review, and most service and
commercial uses by permitted right or conditional
review. Automotive, kennels, agricultural, industrial
(except Limited Industrial), and storage yards or
parks are not allowed. Public Services as
government facilities are still allowed. This rezoning
gives the parcel flexibility to meet the needs of the
business on the adjoining property while expanding
possible future commercial uses. Additionally the
change retains the parcel’s ability to be used for
residential purposes.

The zone district includes design standards which provide reasonable assurance that any
redevelopment will be integrated into the neighborhood consistent with PlanCheyenne.

STREETS & OPEN SPACE - The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed

in the site plan review process. This neighborhood has a robust street network which supports a
mix of uses in a compact neighborhood.

standards for development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options in the
event that any specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after

This zone change is being submitted with uses in mind for existing structures, but the change will
allow for future development to meet standards for possible commercial redevelopment. Should
this development not proceed, the parcel would be available to numerous uses. Any future
substantial redevelopment will have to be reviewed administratively during the Site Review,
Engineering Plan Review, and the Building Permit processes to ensure all standards are met.
