```text
RESOLUTION NO.

ENTITLED: “A
RESOLUTION
ACKNOWLEDGING
RECEIPT
OF
‘THE
CHEYENNE CEMETERY ARBORETUM MASTER PLAN REPORT
AND APPENDICES, DATED SEPTEMBER 15, 2014, PREPARED BY
ROBERT PECCIA AND ASSOCIATES.”

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne engaged Robert Peccia and Associates (“RPA”), as Prime
Consultant, to develop a Master Plan for the Expansion of the Cheyenne Municipal Cemetery
System; and

WHEREAS, “RPA” has completed the scope of services pertaining to the Master Plan in
accordance with City Contract No. 6097, approved by the Governing Body of the City of
Cheyenne on November 25, 2013, and has submitted the Cheyenne Cemetery Arboretum Master
Plan Report and Appendices (“Master Plan”) to the City; and

WHEREAS, the Master Plan provides  direction for the future development of
approximately twenty-five (25) acres of land within the City-owned grounds of the High Plains
Arboretum and the USDA High Plains Grasslands Research Station, located off Round Top
Road, Laramie County, Wyoming, as a municipal cemetery; and

WHEREAS, the Master Plan further provides a site for the future development of a
National Burial Grounds anticipated to be owned and administered by the U.S. Veterans
Administration; and

WHEREAS, the Master Plan further provides opportunities for shared use of multi-
purpose facilities to serve the future municipal cemetery, the National Burial Grounds, the
remaining areas of the High Plains Arboretum, and a portion of land the City currently leases to
the USDA High Plains Grasslands Research Station, while preserving open space, and natural and
cultural resources in the area; and

WHEREAS, the Master Plan further provides guidelines for future phased expansions of the
municipal cemetery and enhancement of vehicle and pedestrian access to the surrounding
arboretum.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body acknowledges receipt of the “Cheyenne
Cemetery Arboretum Master Plan Report and Appendices,” dated September 15, 2014; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that a complete copy of the Cheyenne Cemetery
Arboretum Master Plan Report and Appendices, dated September 15, 2014, shall be on file for
public inspection in the office of the City Clerk of the City of Cheyenne; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Cheyenne Cemetery Arboretum Master Plan
Report and Appendices, dated September 15, 2014, will serve as a guideline for the future design,
construction and affiliated operations of the new municipal cemetery area at the aforementioned
location.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS
DAY OF
, 2014.

(SEAL)
Attest:
Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor

Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
```
