MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Jeanie Vetter, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner
Subject: MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND SUN VALLEY
ESTATES LLC OUTLINING RESPONSIBILIIES IN PURSUIT OF A LAND EXCHANGE OF
APPROXIMATELY 15,375 SQUARE FEET TO FACILITATE BETTER ALIGNMENT OF THE PUBLIC
GREENWAY SYSTEM AND BETTER SITE LAYOUT FOR PRIVATE MULTI-FAMILY DEVELOPMENT

Date:
November 7, 2022

Background:
The purpose of this MOU is to outline responsibilities of both the City and the Developer to
pursue a land exchange to facilitate a better alignment of the future Sun Valley Greenway
Connector and better site layout for the Developer of future multi-family apartments.  Once the
MOU is approved by Council and responsibilities of the MOU are met by both parties, the parties will
enter into a Purchase, Sale and Exchange Agreement for exchange of lands.  No money will be
exchanged.  The City has begun the processes of annexation and zoning for the City owned parcel as
described in the MOU and the items will go in front of Planning Commission on December 5th and will be
at City Council for 1st reading on December 12th.

WHEREAS, the purpose of this agreement is to outline responsibilities of the City and
the Developer;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1.
Purpose.  The purpose of this MOU is to exchange land that would benefit both
parties.  No money will change hands.

2.
Term. This MOU shall commence on the last date executed by the duly
authorized representatives of the Parties to this MOU and shall remain in full force and effect for
one calendar year from the date this document is executed by both Parties unless modified or
renewed in writing and signed by both Parties.

3.
Payments.  Neither Party shall have any obligation to make any payment of any
kind to the other Party under this MOU.

4.
Responsibilities of the Developer.  The Developer shall:
a. Seek approval of all necessary applications to divide the subject lands to facilitate
their Development and the conveyance of land.

b. Seek approval of a conditional use permit application based on the revised project
layout and land areas; along with any and all required development entitlements;
including but not limited to Site and Landscape Plan(s), Engineering Plan
Review(s), Subdivision Plat(s) or Replat(s), and Permits or Licenses.

c. Bear all cost associated with the above-mentioned applications.

5.
Responsibilities of the City. The City shall:
a. Initiate annexation and zoning of the current City owned property to Medium-
density Residential (MR) zoning district and shall bear all costs associated with
these processes.

b. Design and construct all Greenway elements within the future City owned
property.

c. Upon completion of both City and Developer responsibilities noted herein, initiate
an Agreement for the Purchase, Sale and Exchange of Lands and bear all costs
