NOTICE OF PROPOSED SALE OF REAL PROPERTY

COMES NOW the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and pursuant to the provisions of Wyoming
Statute Section 15-1-112(b)(i)(D), provides public notice of a proposed sale of undeveloped
public property described below with an appraised value of $293,000.00 to John J. Koehler,
without calling for bids on the property for the reasons that such sale will provide development
of a private enterprise within a Community/Regional Center as identified by the 2014 update to
the Cheyenne Comprehensive Plan, and will serve as a catalyst for economic development within
a Community/Regional Center.  The property is legally described as:  A 1.46 acre portion of Lot
1, Block 1, Cheyenne Ice & Events Center, 1st Filing, City of Cheyenne, Laramie County,
Wyoming.  The proposed price will be $293,000.00.  A public hearing on this proposed sale will
be held by the Governing Body in City Council Chambers at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne,
WY 82001, on Monday, August 26, 2019 at 6:00 P.M.  The public is invited to offer comments
on the proposed sale of real property either in person at the meeting or by mailing comments to
the City Clerk, 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY 82001.

Publish:
August 9, 16 and 23, 2019.
