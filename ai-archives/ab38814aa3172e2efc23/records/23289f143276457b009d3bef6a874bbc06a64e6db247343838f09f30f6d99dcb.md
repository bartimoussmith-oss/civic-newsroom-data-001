RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 June 10, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr.
Valdez and Ms. Williams.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and
Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA]  are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Allen  moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by
Ms. Broyles.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
[CA]

[CA]

Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest in voucher payable to Ameri-Tech
HVAC Services) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Valdez  moved to approve payment of vouchers
as presented, seconded by Mr. Allen.  Upon inquiry, Mayor Kaysen advised a voucher payable to AM
Signal Inc. ($7,665.80 for a solar powered school zone flashing beacon) is a cooperative effort and
shared expense with the school district.  Upon inquiry regarding a voucher payable to Labor Ready
Center, Inc. ($7,292.13) relative to amounts paid for temporary laborers for the Transfer Station, Jim
Elias, Public Works Director, provided information on the number of individuals involved in the
billing; hours worked; tasks assigned, and reasons for and benefits of utilizing temporary laborers
through the agency.  Upon further inquiry, Mr. Elias stated the individuals are not permanent or
seasonal City employees and receive no benefits.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown
returned to Council Chambers.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive comments and input for a restaurant liquor
license application filed with the City Clerk’s Office for Anong Larsen and Brett Larsen d/b/a Anong’s
Thai Cuisine, 620 Central Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Upon inquiry, Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk,
confirmed the application is for a new restaurant liquor license at the site.  There being no further
comments and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the hearing and  referred
the application to Finance Committee.
