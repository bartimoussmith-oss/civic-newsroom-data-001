RESOLUTION  _________
ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION CERTIFYING THAT THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE IS IN AGREEMENT ON A PROJECT TO BE SUBMITTED
BY THE TOWN OF PINE BLUFFS TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT
BOARD FOR FUNDING THE MAIN STREET LIGHTING PROJECT THROUGH THE
2015-2016 COUNTYWIDE CONSENSUS BLOCK GRANT.”

Whereas,  the Laramie County Board of Commissioners support the Pine Bluffs Main Street
Lighting Project, and Pine Bluffs desires to participate in the State Loan and Investment Board’s
Countywide Consensus Block Grant program; and
Whereas, the rules of the State Loan and Investment Board require that application for funds
under this program be made by the Board of County Commissioners and agreed to by
municipalities representing at least 70% of the county’s incorporated population; and
Whereas, the Governing Body of Cheyenne represents greater than 70% of the incorporated
population of Laramie County;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING
That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne is in agreement with the submission of a
request by Laramie County, to the State Loan and Investment board for Countywide Consensus
Block Grant funding 2015-2016, for Pine Bluffs Main Street Lighting Project.
PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 27th DAY OF APRIL, 2015

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
Attest:
________________________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
Consens 15-16 Co  RES PB.wpd
