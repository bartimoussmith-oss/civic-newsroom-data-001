BUSINESS READY COMMUNITY GRANT AND LOAN PROGRAM
GRANT AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE WYOMING BUSINESS COUNCIL AND
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Preamble: This Agreement is for a grant from the Wyoming Business Council to the City of
Cheyenne, as described below and in the identified attachments thereto. This Grant Agreement
and the attachments hereto will not create or constitute a legal or moral obligation or debt of any
kind of the City of Cheyenne and will not be encumber or be chargeable against the City’s assets,
general reserves, general funds, or any other revenues or properties whatsoever.

1.
Parties.
The parties to this Grant Agreement are the State of Wyoming, by and
through the Wyoming Business Council (“WBC”), whose address is 214 West 15th Street,
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82002, and the City of Cheyenne, a political subdivision of the State of
Wyoming (“Grantee”), whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.
2.
Purpose of Grant Agreement.  The WBC shall provide Business Ready Community
Grant and Loan Program (“BRC”) Large Business Infrastructure Business Committed funds to
Grantee in the amount set forth in Section 4, and Grantee shall undertake and complete materials,
projects and/or services (collectively, the “Project”) described in Attachments A, B, and C
attached hereto.  Performance by Grantee of the requirements of this Grant Agreement and
compliance with all BRC program rules and regulations is a condition to Grantee’s receipt of
monies hereunder.
3.     Term of Grant Agreement and Required Approvals.  This Grant Agreement shall
commence upon the date the last signature is affixed hereto. All construction services shall be
completed by June 30, 2016, unless an extension is approved by WBC.  This Grant Agreement
shall terminate on June 30, 2019, unless otherwise amended or terminated in accordance with the
terms and conditions specifically provided herein.  This Grant Agreement may be extended
when, in the sole discretion of the WBC, circumstances require an extension.  Any extension
shall be done by written amendment.
4.
Payment.  WBC agrees to grant monies to Grantee for performance of the Project, as
invoices are submitted for work done in connection with the Project, completed in accordance
with the requirements of this Agreement and the BRC program.  The total payment to Grantee
under this Grant Agreement shall not exceed two million dollars ($2,000,000) (“Grant”).
Payment will be made following Grantee’s delivery to WBC of invoices detailing services
performed in connection with the Project in a form satisfactory to WBC.  Payment shall be made
from WBC’s BRC budget pursuant to the schedule shown on Attachment B hereto.  No payment
shall be made for any services performed in connection with the Project prior to the date upon
which the last required signature is affixed to this Grant Agreement.

5.
Responsibilities of Grantee Regarding the Project.  The Project to be undertaken is
described in Attachment A and C which are attached and made a part of this Grant Agreement.

INTEREST AND REVENUE RECAPTURE PLAN

1. The City of Cheyenne will receive a loan of $3,000,000 and a grant of $2,000,000 from
the Wyoming Business Council (WBC”) to facilitate the Swan Ranch Rail Project.

2. The City of Cheyenne will, in turn, and pursuant to various agreements between the State
of Wyoming, the City of Cheyenne, and Granite Peak Development, and its related entities,
transfer the $3,000,000 loan proceeds and the $2,000,000 grant proceeds to Granite Peak
Development to construct the Swan Ranch Rail Project.

3. The project is more particularly described in the BRC application received by the WBC
on December 2, 2013.

4. Commencing on the one (1) year anniversary of Substantial Completion of the Swan
Ranch Rail Project as that term is defined in the Amended and Restated Project Development
and Administrative Agreement executed between the State of Wyoming and the City of
Cheyenne, Granite Peak shall, in full and complete satisfaction of all duties and obligations
imposed on Granite Peak with regard to the $3,000,000 loan and the $2,000,000 grant involved
with the Swan Ranch Rail Project, begin making payments to the City of Cheyenne pursuant to a
Non-Negotiable Promissory Note executed between the City of Cheyenne and Granite Peak.

5. For the first three (3) years of this Note, Granite Peak shall not make any principal
payments on this Note and shall pay a total of $50,000 interest per year on the Note payable in
equal annual installments. Commencing in year four (4) of the Note, Granite Peak shall pay
yearly installments of $743,141.00. All payments under this Note shall be due by the fifth (5th)
day of any month that a payment is due and shall be considered delinquent if not paid in full by
the fifteenth (15th) day of any given month.

6. The City of Cheyenne shall retain a portion of all amounts paid by Granite Peak in any
year as Revenue Recapture. The City of Cheyenne shall transfer the remainder of all funds
received from Granite Peak in any payment year to the WBC to repay the $3,000,000 loan. The
amount of funds to be retained by the City as Revenue Recapture in any given payment year is
set forth in Table 1 to this document. The City of Cheyenne is eligible to receive a total of
$175,994 in economic development revenue recapture on this project. All funds retained by the
City of Cheyenne as Revenue Recapture shall be transferred to Cheyenne Leads to be used by
Cheyenne Leads and the City of Cheyenne for appropriate economic development initiatives
including the purchase of land or buildings, business park enhancement including landscaping or
signage, and investment in infrastructure such as roads, water, sewer, fiber, optics, and power.
