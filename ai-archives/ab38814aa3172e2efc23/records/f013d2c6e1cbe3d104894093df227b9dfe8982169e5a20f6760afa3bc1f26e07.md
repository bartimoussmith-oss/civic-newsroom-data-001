KNOW ALL PERSONS BY THESE PRESENTS that, Jeff T.Trefren("Grantor"),

the DELL RANGE/VAN BUREN AVENUE IMPROVEMENTS PROJECT, including, butnot
work force, over, upon and across the described easement, together with the right of ingress and
egress;

any part thereof, and that itwarrants the title of said property;

Grantee on and subject to the terms and conditions of this agreement; and
WHEREAS the Grantee agrees to surrender possession of the premises upon completion
of theproject, and to leave the premises in substantially the same or better condition than it was
on the date this easement was executed. However, it is understood that grading changes may
exist upon completion of the project.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing premises and the mutual
agreement of the parties hereto, the parties hereby agree asfollows:
1.Temporary Construction Agreement. Grantor executes this in favor of City, its
successors and assigns, the right and privilege to a temporary construction easement in the
Easement Area for the purposes of locating, constructing, improving, and completing other
necessary work as part of the DELL RANGE/VAN BUREN AVENUE IMPROVEMENTS
PROJECT, including, but not limited to, the space necessary for the operation and storage of
equipment and the movement of a work force, over, upon and across the described easement.
Such grant includes all rights and privileges necessary or convenient for thefull enjoyment and
use thereoffor the above-mentioned purposes including, butnot limited to, theright of ingress
and egress over Grantor's adjoining property to exercise the explicit and implicit rights granted
herein by this agreement.
