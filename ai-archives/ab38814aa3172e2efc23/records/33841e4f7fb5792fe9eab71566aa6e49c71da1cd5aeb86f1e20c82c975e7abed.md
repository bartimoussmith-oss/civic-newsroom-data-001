WHEREAS, W.S.§ 39-15-204(a)(ii) allows counties in Wyoming to impose an optional
sales tax for specific purposes subject to the limitations specified therein and subject to the approval
of qualified electors in accordance with W.S.  39-15-203(a)(i)(B); and
WHEREAS, the1%Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee, composed of representatives
of the City of Cheyenne, the County of Laramie, and the Towns of Burns, Pine Bluffs and Albin,
has proposed that an optional 1% sales tax beimposed for specific purposes authorized by the
qualified electors of Laramie County, Wyoming; and
WHEREAS, the1%Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee has proposed that One Hundred
Five Million Dollars ($105,000,000.00) of specific purpose projects be placed on the 1% specific
purpose ballot proposition for consideration by qualified electors during the August 21,2012
Primary Election; and
WHEREAS, specific purpose projects proposed by the City of Cheyenne have historically
averaged approximately57% of the total amounts proposed by Laramie County governmental
entities in prior1%specific purpose elections; and
WHEREAS, the specific projects itemized in this resolution will enhance the ability of the
Cityof Cheyenne to discharge core functions including police protection, fire protection, drainage
control, safe and efficient streets, sanitation and recreation; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne has adopted the following
prioritized list of 1% specific purpose projects for consideration by the 1% Specific Purpose Sales
Tax Committee.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OFCHEYENNE, that the following prioritized list of specific projects be adopted and presented to
the1%Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee for its consideration and potential inclusion on the
1%Specific Purpose ballot proposition to be submitted to the qualified electors of Laramie County
during the August 21,2012Primary Election.
