“AN ORDINANCE CREATING SECTION 10.24.170, DRIVING WHILE USING
CELLULAR TELEPHONE PROHIBITED; EXCEPTIONS, OF CHAPTER 10.24, RULES OF THE
ROAD GENERALLY, OF TITLE 10, VEHICLES AND TRAFFIC, OF THE CODE OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”  John Fafoutakis, Eric Miller and John Palmer spoke generally in
opposition to the proposed ordinance, and expressed concerns on use of cell phones for text messaging
while driving, local governmental control, ordinance enforcement, distractive driving scenarios,
violations constituting a primary vs. secondary offense, and current traffic laws in place that might be
utilized instead of the proposed ordinance.  Upon inquiry, Capt. Jeff Schulz, Interim Police Chief,
provided information on careless driving violations resulting in citations.  Reporting for Public
Services Committee, Mr. Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr.
Collins. Mr. Pierson moved to amend by indicating that violations of the ordinance be enforced as a
secondary offense, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Mr. Fafoutakis, Mr. Palmer, Mr. Miller and David
Hallam spoke in support of the amendment.  Reverend Michaela Pena spoke in opposition to the
amendment.  Discussion included the purpose of violations constituting a primary offense, enforcement
of secondary vs. primary offenses, concern with passing laws due to irresponsible drivers, other
distractions affecting safe driving practices, notification to the public and community visitors of
ordinance restrictions, and balancing of rights and freedoms for safety purposes.  Motion to amend
failed.  Voting “yes” – Mr. Pierson and Mr. Valdez. Voting “no” – Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr.
Collins, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and Mayor Kaysen.  Comments  followed regarding enforcement as a
primary offense and effect on local residents, statistics supporting cell phone usage as the cause of
many accidents, protection of drivers and passengers, community signage, and ordinance provision
allowing use of cell phones with hands free devices while driving.  Upon inquiry, Captain Schulz
advised the ordinance, if approved, will be enforced like every other traffic ordinance on the books,
and commented on probable cause scenarios as noted by officers involving violations.  Mayor Kaysen
clarified signs will be posted around the City and entry ways into Cheyenne alerting residents and
those visiting of the cell phone ordinance, and stated exact sites have not yet been determined.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present with the exception of Mr. Pierson
voting “no”. (#3870)
