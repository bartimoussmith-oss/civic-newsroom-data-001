BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 2.32.020, Department Personnel, of Title 2, of Chapter 2.32, Fire
Department, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, is hereby amended as set out in this
Ordinance.

2.32.020 - Department personnel.

A. The fire department consists of the chief of the department, division chiefs, battalion
chiefs, lieutenants, engineers and firefighters as determined by the mayor and city
council.

B. The chief of the fire department will shall be appointed by the mayor, in accordance
with state law and Chapter 2.08 of this code. All other officers and members of the
department will shall be appointed by the mayor chief of the fire department from
applicants certified by the fire department civil service commission. Their
compensation will be fixed by the governing body.

C. In case of an emergency, the mayor may, with approval of city council, appoint
additional personnel necessary for the efficient operation of the department. If no
applicants are available from the eligibility list, the mayor will employ, temporarily,
such other persons necessary to meet the needs of the emergency.

Section 2.  Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional, such unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections,
paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases.

Section 3.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and
publication.
