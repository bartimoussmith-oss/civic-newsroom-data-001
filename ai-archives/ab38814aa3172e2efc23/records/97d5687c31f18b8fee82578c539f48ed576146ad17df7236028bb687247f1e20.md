RESOLUTION NO. ___________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION ESTABLISHING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE’S
PREFERRED PROJECTS TO BE PROPOSED FOR POTENTIAL
INCLUSION ON THE ONE PERCENT SPECIFIC PURPOSE SALES AND
USE
TAX
BALLOT
PROPOSITION
TO
BE
PRESENTED
TO
QUALIFIED ELECTORS OF LARAMIE COUNTY AT A SPECIAL
ELECTION TO BE HELD ON NOVEMBER 2, 2021.”

WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 39-15-204(a)(iii) allows counties in Wyoming to impose
an optional sales tax for specific purposes subject to the limitations specified herein and subject
to the approval of qualified electors in accordance with Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 39-15-203(a)(ii)(3);
and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has proposed that an optional one percent sales and
use tax be imposed for specific purposes authorized by the qualified electors of Laramie County,
Wyoming; and

WHEREAS, the specific projects itemized in this resolution will enhance the ability of
the City of Cheyenne to discharge core functions including, fire protection, drainage control, safe
and efficient streets, recreation and economic development; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne has adopted the following
prioritized list of preferred one percent specific purpose sales and use tax projects for
consideration.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the following prioritized list of specific projects be
adopted and presented to the Laramie County Attorney for its consideration and potential
inclusion on the One Percent Specific Purpose Sales and Use Tax ballot proposition to be
submitted to the qualified electors of Laramie County at a special election to be held on
November 2, 2021:

1.
Three (3) Fire Stations Project:  to relocate and replace Fire Station No. 3 and Fire
Station No. 5, and to construct an additional Fire Station in the amount of Fifteen Million Seven
Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Dollars ($15,762,000.00).

2.
Fire Truck Fleet Replacement Project:  to replace outdated and unreliable fire
trucks in the amount of Four Million Two Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Dollars
($4,262,000.00).

3.
Police Portable Radios Replacement Project:  to replace outdated and unsupported
portable radios in the amount of Nine Hundred Six Thousand Nine Hundred Sixty-Eight Dollars
($906,968.00).

By Michael R. O'Donnell at 3:35 pm, Apr 22, 2021
