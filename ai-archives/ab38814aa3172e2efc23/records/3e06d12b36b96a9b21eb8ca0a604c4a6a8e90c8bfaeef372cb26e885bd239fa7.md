RE: Application for Bar and Grill Entertainment carve out
Honorable Mayor Collins and Members of the Cheyenne City Council,
Ace’s Range is a new virtual simulation where popular outdoor activities are done inside with
virtual simulators. In our case, we propose utilizing both golfing and shooting state of the art
simulators. A similar venue, XGolf, has been open in Fort Collins since 2019 and brings in
visitors, sales tax and property tax. Equally important Ace’s Range fills the empty warehouse
space at 1 5th Street and Pioneer Avenue with another quality venue in downtown Cheyenne.
We respectfully request your consideration in granting our business the newly available bar and
grill entertainment carve out liquor license. It is imperative to this type of business that we have
the ability to serve alcohol to the extent only allowed with a bar and grill entertainment carve out
license.
We know you will be considering a lot of requests and need as much information as possible to
make an informed decision. The most salient topics we would like to briefly touch on in this
letter are as follows:
15th Street Renaissance: We share the Governing Body’s passion for revitalizing downtown and
believe in the importance of the 151h Street Renaissance to that mission. Ace’s Range will be
located at the corner of 5 Street and Pioneer in a 1940’s warehouse that is vacant. We will
completely rehabilitate the interior and exterior of the building and create a vibrant property at
this important corner. We intend to partner with local businesses in the vicinity as well as local
organizations in the city to maximize the use of our simulators and facility.
Economic Activity: Partnerships with downtown businesses, property tax, sales tax,
partnerships with golf organizations and shooting clubs. Ace’s Range will offer leagues and

Simulator Technology: Ace’s Range is truly a new and unique experience for Cheyenne. We
will feature six Trackman golf bays and two Smokeless Range shooting simulator bays. Two of
the six Trackman bays will be in a privately enclosed area with their own ambiance separate
from the rest of the building. Trackman is a state-of-the-art golf simulator featuring premiere
courses from all over the world. Trackman is the best medium to teach the game of golf via the
numbers provided for every swing. Smokeless Range is a software that utilizes a laser bullet to
simulate discharging a firearm over various applications. These applications include fast draw,
hostage scenarios, zombies, moving targets and of course standard shooting ranges. Ace’s
Range will host leagues for golf.
Competition: Lastly, with any license, we know you have to consider the potential for

Ace’s Range Business Concepts and Menu
*Our menu and league schedule is a rough draft and a work in progress. We will most likely be limiting the
ingredients listed below depending on our storage and warming kitchens abilities. Due to our limited kitchen space we
want to offer a convenient and customizable style of various ready to eat and frozen foods that are heated up to
prepare.
Ace’s Range is proud to offer a fully customizable menu that offers unique fare. We have a few set menu items,
however please feel free to create your own culinary delight! We have listed below six categories that you can choose
from to make up your basket. We offer 3 sizes of baskets, The Caddy, Golfer and Greenskeeper. The Caddy is a smaller
portion proportionate to an app or kids serving. The Golfer is a full portion while The Greenskeeper is two baskets.
Due to limited space in our warming kitchen we are proud to offer fare from other Downtown Cheyenne
restaurants for those seeking different options, or those with specific food allergies that conflict with any ingredient on
our list. You may order food from these restaurants while on premise or upon booking a tee time with us, we’ll pick it
up and bring it to you at Ace’s. Please consider a longer wait time for items ordered from other establishments when
ordered while at Ace’s Range, we strongly recommend ordering from other restaurants when you book your tee time to
reduce waiting time. We believe in our local businesses and we’re proud to support them. Thank you for your
patronage, we appreciate your support of local small business!
We will have QR codes in each bay, bar area and at front desk. Patrons will use these QR code to sign into our
system, once you create a login you can create a profile, from which you may book tee times, order food from
Downtown Cheyenne restaurants, register for leagues and tournaments, spend your Ace Bucks and so much more! Our
Ace’s Range membership is starting at $300. This membership includes discounted league and tournament fees, early
booking, event booking, access to recorded lessons anywhere on your mobile device, enrollment in Ace’s Bucks in house
credit system which will include promotions from Cheyenne businesses.
Our normal business hours are subject to change in order meet Cheyenne’s needs, please call or text, (307) 757-
7299 or email us at Acesrangewygmail.com if you’d like to book a private event with catered food service, private
parties including multiple bays, sections of the building, or book a completely private party at Ace’s. We’re open to
personal requests to make your experience special. We do have a set schedule of events we’re excited to offer;
From opening date until leagues start we will have open play with a few tournaments to gauge Cheyenne’s
needs. We encourage the community to sign up for an Ace’s Range membership and establish a handicap (recording
score for 9 holes) used for league and tournament play.
During this opening period we’re excited to offer a unique culinary expertise challenge for patrons who sign up
for an Ace’s Range membership. From opening day for our first month of operation, Our fully certified chef/food
handlers will sample each customers custom Ace’s Range dish entered into the competition. We will record each Ace of
the day with the best recipe. Each Ace will be entered into a private tasting team building event for Ace’s Range
employees. Each Ace will be evaluated by our staff grazing on munchies while enjoying our unique simulator
experience. One winner with the best recipe chosen by Ace’s Range employees will be awarded Ace Bucks. *must sign
up for Ace’s Range membership to qualify.
League Play
12 teams of consisting of 3 players play each other once over 11 weeks. Points are awarded for handicapped
adjusted lvi match play for each team. The Top 4 teams after the group stage make the cut to playoffs, each playoff
contender plays one another for a 13 week season. Prizes from local vendors are to be awarded each week for skins
challenges. Playing the time schedule is encouraged, however players may play the round in advance to accommodate
schedule conflicts. Please notify us and we’ll book you a tee time to record your round for the week.
