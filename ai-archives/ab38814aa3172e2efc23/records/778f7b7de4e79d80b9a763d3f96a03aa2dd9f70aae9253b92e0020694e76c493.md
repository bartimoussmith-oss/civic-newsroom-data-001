WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board desires to participate in the Federal
Aviation Administration grant program under the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law ("BIL") to assist
in funding costs related to the rehabilitation of the Air Traffic Control Tower ("ATCT");

WHEREAS, maintaining the ATCT is crucial for preserving air service at Cheyenne
Regional Airport ("CYS"). The air service plays a key role in improving the transportation to and
from the city of Chyenne, which increases tourism and positively impacts the local economy.  In
addition, by having the ATCT, it encourages other potential air carriers to consider the airport
when expanding air service;

WHEREAS, the ATCT is staffed with Air Force Air Traffic Controllers (Squadron 243rd)
and is used for training controllers. The rehabilitation ensures that ATCT personnel can continue
to use the facility, preserving their current good-paying positions and generating additional
employment opportunities as trained personnel transition to other ATCTs for long-term careers.
Additionally, the project will include good-paying jobs for construction;

WHEREAS, The existing tower is part of the national airspace system and supports
commercial, general aviation and military aircraft.  This rehabilitation is necessary to ensure that
the ATCT remains operational. Without an investment into the facility, the tower will approach
end of life condition and will struggle to support the national airspace.  The tower also plays a vital
role for the Air National Guard (ANG). This facility is used for training controllers for the Air
Force. The required maintenance for this tower is necessary for the continued support of the ANG
and the surrounding community;

WHEREAS, the building assessment that was conducted in 2021 determined that the
ATCT building requires rehabilitation that includes sealing for water intrusion, replacing doors
and windows, and other general building repairs to improve personnel safety and overall life
expectance of the building. Additionally, HVAC and electrical improvements are required to keep
the existing equipment operational and to improve energy efficiency;

WHEREAS, minor maintenance needs to be conducted on the elevator and the associated
electrical and mechanical components to ensure that the building maintains ADA compliance. The
design will also look at ways to improve ADA accessibility;

WHEREAS, the existing facility has poor energy performance, specifically in HVAC and
Electrical infrastructure.  Building renovations will adhere to LEED standards wherever feasible,

WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes that the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board is

WHEREAS, Once the final amount has been established, then the airport will submit the

(September 30, 2024); and,

WHEREAS, the grant is 93.75% FAA funded with the remaining funding provided by the
WYDOT Aeronautics Commission at 3.75%, and the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board at 2.5%.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne supports the grant application to be
submitted by the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board to the Federal Aviation Administration under
the BIL in the amount of up to eight hundred fifty thousand dollars ($850,000) over a 5-year period
to assist in funding costs related to the PBB.
