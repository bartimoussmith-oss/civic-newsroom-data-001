CITY CONTRACT # _______

NON-HAZARDOUS SOLID WASTE
DISPOSAL AGREEMENT

This Non-Hazardous Solid Waste Disposal Agreement (the “Agreement”) is entered into
this __ day of ___________, 2015 by and between Waste Management Disposal
Services of Colorado, Inc. (“WM”) and the City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming
(“City”).

W I T N E S S E T H

WHEREAS, WM owns and operates the North Weld Landfill, located at 40,000 Weld
County Road 25, Ault, Colorado (the “Landfill”); and

WHEREAS, City owns and operates a solid waste transfer station within the City (the
“Transfer Station”); and

WHEREAS, City desires to enter into this Agreement permitting City to dispose of
Permitted Solid Waste collected by its collection vehicles and/or received at its Transfer
Station at the Landfill at the herein stated Contract Rates for the contract period of July 1,
2015 through June 30, 2017.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises contained herein, the
parties to this Agreement agree as follows:

1.
PERMITTED SOLID WASTE.  “Permitted Solid Waste” is defined for the purpose
of this Agreement as all non-hazardous commercial, residential and construction
debris wastes collected by City by its collection vehicles and/or received at its
Transfer Station. All regulated hazardous, PCB and radioactive wastes are
specifically excluded from the definition, as are any other wastes for which the
Landfill is not permitted to accept (collectively, “Excluded Wastes”). Additionally,
whole tires, high-density demolition debris, and industrial wastes (collectively,
“Special Wastes”) are specifically excluded from the definition of Permitted Solid
Waste. However, Special Wastes may be accepted at the Landfill upon written
agreement of the parties as to legal status of the waste, price and conditions of
disposal.

A. DRIVER'S KNOWLEDGE AND AUTHORITY. City warrants that its drivers who
deliver waste to the Landfill have been advised by City of WM's prohibition of
deliveries of Excluded and Special Wastes.

B. RIGHTS OF REFUSAL/REJECTION OF WASTES.  WM has the right to reject
or revoke after acceptance any load or portion of any load of waste delivered to

3.
TERM OF AGREEMENT.  This Agreement shall be for a term commencing July 1,
2015 and ending June 30, 2017.  For this Agreement, a “Contract Year” shall be

shall be according to the provisions of Paragraph 4(b) for each additional year
during the extension period.  This Agreement supersedes the current agreement,
contract number 5580, between the City and WM, dated August 9, 2010.

4.
PRICE. The price to be paid for each ton of Permitted Solid Waste delivered to the
Landfill shall be the Contract Rate stated below (subject to the Annual Minimum
Quantity requirement being met).  Each load will be weighed at the Landfill to
determine weight.  All Special Waste shall be priced at the time WM agrees in
writing to accept such waste, as provided for herein.

If City fails to deliver the required Annual Minimum Quantity of Permitted Solid
Wastes during a Contract Year, City shall, within 30 days following such Contract
Year, pay WM an amount equal to the following:

(30,000 * published gate rate at Landfill) – Amounts paid and owed by City
for Permitted Solid Waste delivered during the Contract Year based on the
Contract Rate

Such payment shall be in addition to amounts otherwise due based on the
Contract Rate for the relevant Contract Year. For example, assuming City only
delivered 25,000 tons of Permitted Solid Waste during the Contract Year ending
June 30, 2016, and the assumed published gate rate as of June 30, 2016 is
$38.00, the following would be due from City by July 30, 2016:

 Contract Rate for all Permitted Solid Waste delivered during the
Contract Year (which had not yet been paid), plus
 (30,000 * $38.00) – (25,000 * $23.90) = $542,500

The Contract Rate(s) include Weld County landfill environmental fees/surcharges

WM's written notice of approval of Profiled Special Waste. Rates for Profiled
Special Wastes will be based on mutual agreement between City and WM prior to
the approval of the waste.

7.
CREDIT TERMS.  City agrees to pay all WM invoices within 30 days of the invoice

D.
ENTIRE AGREEMENT.  This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement
between the parties hereto with respect to the subject matter hereof.  No
modification, amendment or waiver of any provision or obligation hereof
shall be binding upon any party hereto, or in the case of a waiver, by the

Agreement.

F.
LIMITED LICENSE TO ENTER.  During the term of this Agreement, the
City shall have a license to enter the Landfill for the limited purpose of, and
only to the extent necessary for the off-loading Permitted Solid Wastes at
the location and in the manner directed by WM. Except in an emergency, or
at the express direction of WM, City personnel shall promptly leave the
Landfill facility. Under no circumstances shall City or its personnel engage in
scavenging of waste at the Landfill.  WM may refuse to accept waste from,
and shall deny an entrance license to, any of City's personnel who WM
believes is under the influence of alcohol or other chemical substance.

G.
INSURANCE.   City has liability coverage through Wyoming Association of
Risk Management (WARM), a joint powers liability pool that provides risk
financing and claims administration to the City, in the amounts and
coverages represented by the WARM Certificate of Liability Coverage
(Attachment A).  The City is under the Wyoming State Workers
Compensation Program.

WM will provide the City with proof of commercial general and auto liability
insurance coverage in the form of an insurance certificate throughout the
entire term of the contract as a certificate holder.  All insurance certificates
provided by WM must include a clause stating that the commercial
general and auto liability insurance may not be canceled without at least
thirty (30) days advanced written notice to the City.  Ten (10) days notice
is acceptable for cancellation due to non-payment of premium.

H.
WAIVER.   The waiver by any party hereto of any breach, default,
