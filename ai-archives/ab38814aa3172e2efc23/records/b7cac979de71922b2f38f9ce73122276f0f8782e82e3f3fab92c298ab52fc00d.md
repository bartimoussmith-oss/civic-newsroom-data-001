MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Patrick J. Collins, City of Cheyenne
Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Wesley D. Bay, P.E., Deputy City Engineer

DATE:
October 10, 2022

SUBJECT:
Professional Service Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and AVI P.C., for
the design of the Powderhouse Road and Carlson Street Intersection Project.

Please find the attached Professional Service Agreement between the City and AVI P.C. for
engineering services related to the design of the Powderhouse Road and Carlson Street Intersection
Project. The design will provide a vertical realignment of Powderhouse Road and Carlson Street
to better accommodate ADA facilities and include the addition of signals warranted by the opening
of a 5-6 elementary school east of the intersection.

Engineering Staff evaluates fee proposals for engineering services based on time and material
estimates for the specific project.  Those costs are readily tracked and verified during design
operations and the City only pays for the actual time expended. We believe this agreement will
provide the required services to complete the design portion of the project.

The fees include services for project management, intersection re-design, topographical surveying,
geotechnical investigations, utility investigation, traffic signal design, and plan creation. Funding
for this project will come from the 2018 1% Specific Purpose Tax Fund (5th Penny). The total
professional services will be a cost not-to-exceed $189,932.50. We respectfully request your
approval of this professional services agreement.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE
2101 O’Neil Avenue
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001

Dear Mr. Bay,
AVI is pleased to submit the following proposal for engineering services for the above
referenced project.  This proposal follows the scope of services provided in the “Professional
Services Agreement” dated August 26th, 2022, with revisions or alternations based on the site
visit with yourself, Ben Weaver (Stantec) and Tristan Cordier (AVI) on September 28th, 2022.

As you are aware, AVI and Stantec are currently working together to provide professional
services for the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study. We believe we are well-positioned to
complete this scope of work efficiently.

We understand the City of Cheyenne is in need of professional services to complete the design
work associated with the proposed reconstruction of the Carlson Street and Powderhouse Road
intersection. The proposed Project, including construction, is required to be completed by
opening day of the new Coyote Ridge Elementary School.  We understand this timeframe to be
at the beginning of January 2024.

The existing topography of the intersection does not currently provide for adequate ADA
crossings and accessibility. This is in part due to the profile elevation of Powderhouse Road.  To
mitigate this issue, AVI proposes to broadly assess four differing scenarios, and then work with
the City of Cheyenne to select the most optimal scenario with which to continue. The four
different scenarios are as follows:

1. Facilitate ADA ramps, landings, and connections to existing sidewalks without major
roadway construction.
a. This scenario will assess the use of retaining walls to elevate or lower sidewalks
and connections.
2. Adjust (lower) the grades of Powderhouse Road at the Carlson Street intersection.
a. Utility elevations will be assessed to verify any required minimum cover for both
public and private utilities.
