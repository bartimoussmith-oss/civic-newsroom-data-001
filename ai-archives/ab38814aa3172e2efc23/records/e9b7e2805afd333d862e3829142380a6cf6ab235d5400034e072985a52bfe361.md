YOUTH ALTERNATIVES FOSTER GRANDPARENT PROGRAM."
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Office of Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent
Program is a not-for-profit organization that depends on grants, donations, and local support; and
WHEREAS, the Foster Grandparent Program provides opportunities for senior service to
meet unmet needs and assign individuals, 55 and older, to schools and human service agencies
that serve youthunder the age of 21 having special or exceptional needs or with conditions or
circumstances identified as limiting their academic, social, or economic development; and
WHEREAS, the Corporation for National and Community Service is a federal
program as authorized by the Domestic and Volunteer Service Act of 1973, as amended (42
U.S.C.Chapter 22); and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Office of Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent
Program has applied for and been awarded a grant in the amount of Three Hundred Eighteen
Thousand Three Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($318,375.00) from the Corporation for National
and Community Service; and
WHEREAS, the statutory match for the grant is waived and the Office of Youth
Alternatives shall provide therequired local match in the amount of One Hundred One Thousand
Seven Hundred Ninety-Two Dollars ($101,792.00) through state, local, and other funding.
NOW, THEREFORE, BEIT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OFTHE
CITY OF CHYE NNE, WYOMING:
1.That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne is in support of the Office of
Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent Program and the acceptance of the grant from the
Corporation for National and Community Service to receive funding in the amount of Three
Hundred Eighteen Thousand Three Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars (S318,375.00).
2.That the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is hereby designated as the authorized
representative of the City to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all matters relating to this
grant and is authorized to execute thegrant agreement and associated documents.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED this
