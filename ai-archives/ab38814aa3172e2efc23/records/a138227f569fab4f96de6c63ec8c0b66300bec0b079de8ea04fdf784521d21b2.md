RESOLUTION NO. ____________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE CHEYENNE
REGIONAL AIRPORT BOARD FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING
JULY 1, 2022 AND ENDING JUNE 30, 2023.”

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board (“Board”) was created pursuant to
Ordinance No. 2407 adopted by the Governing Body on February 11, 1980, as amended; and

WHEREAS, City Code Section 2.60.050(B) sets forth the requirement that the Board shall
submit on or before the first day of May of each year, to the governing bodies of the city and the
county, a proposed budget for the ensuing fiscal year; and

WHEREAS, City Code Section 2.60.050(B) provides that the Board budget shall be subject
to alteration, modification and approval by the Governing Body in accordance with the policies of
the City; and

WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the Board.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the proposed budget for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1,
2022 and ending June 30, 2023 of the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board, as set forth in the attached
information with this Resolution entitled “Cheyenne Regional Airport Operating Budget for the
Fiscal Year Ending June 30, 2023” in the total amount of $30,367,848.57, is hereby approved.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this ______ day of ________________, 2022.

__________________________________________

Patrick J. Collins, Mayor
(SEAL)
Attest:

__________________________________
Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk
By Stefanie Boster at 10:31 am, May 18, 2022
