Section 1.That Chapter 2.29, Community Service Officers, of Title 2, Administration

2.29.010
Purpose-Authority-General provisions.
A. The purpose of this chapter is to recognize and approve the existing community
service officers(CsO) program implemented by the Cheyenne police department and set forth the
qualifications for residents interested in serving as community service officers.
The chiefof policemay appoint residents of Laramie County as a CSOto serve under
the rules, orders and regulations of the Cheyenne police department.CsO's may be volunteers or,
at the discretion of the police chief, paid employees.
C.The CsO program supports the functions ofthe police department by assisting police
officers in the performance of their duties that do not require peace officer statutory authority and
are specifically community care-taking functions.CSO's may enforce city ordinances involving
municipal violations following an investigation of a traffic crash, parking violations, animal control
violations and nuisance control violations, at the direction of the police chief or his or her designee.
D. A subset of the CSO program is further created to provide security services for the
protection of life, limb and property at municipal facilities and surrounding premises. This subset
shall be known as Municipal Security Officers (MSO's).
2.29.020
Qualifications and training.
A.
The following criteria must bemet by CSO/MSO applicants tobe considered for
appointment:
1.
CSO's must be Aat least eighteen(18)years of age; MSO's must be at least
twenty-one (21) years of age;
2.
Highschool diploma or recognized equivalent;
3.
Physically able to perform CSO/M SO functions asdirected;
4.
No felony convictions; and
5.
Valid Wyoming driver'slicense.
B.
The chief of police, resignee, will identify, develop and mpl em nt an appropriate
training program to ensure a CSO/MSO can perform required duties ina professional and competent
manner.
C.
MSO's must complete a required forty (40) hour training on the use of firearms and
complete a minimum of six(6)firearm training days eachyear.
D. MSO's may, at the direction of the chief of police, be armed with firearms after
completion of all MSO basic training as prescribed by the chief of police.

2.29.030
Appointment.
Applicants for the CSO program will be selectedfor appointment, and retained within the
program, at the sole discretion of the chief of police.The chief may establish policies outlining
criteria for selection that may include a written test, psychological and medical screening, polygraph
assessment and oral interviews.
2.29.040
Oath of office.
Every person appointed by the chief of police as a Community Service Officer shall first be
administered an oath acknowledging their intent tofulfil the performance of their duties as defined
by the chief of police.The oathmay be administered by the mayor, city clerk or deputy city clerk
and shall be on file with the office of the city clerk.
2.29.050
Additional responsibilities of Municipal Security Officers.
A.
At the Municipal Court Building, MSO's may engage in the following additional
functions:
1.
Confiscate property including contraband and prohibited items as described
and itemized by the standing order of the senior municipal court judge.
2.
Transport inmates and prisoners within the court facility.
3.
 MSO's may transport inmates and prisoners to and from detention facilities
and to and from the Cheyenne Municipal Court.
B.
 In order to protect life, limb and property at municipal facilities and surrounding
premises, MSO's have the authority to:
1.
Detain an individual, only to the extent necessary, to prevent injury to persons
or damage to property.
2.
Act in the defense of others to prevent injury.
