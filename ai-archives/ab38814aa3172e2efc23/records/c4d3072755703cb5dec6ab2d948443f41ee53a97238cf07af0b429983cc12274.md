CONTRACT NO. __________

CONTRACT EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT, made and entered into this ___ day of _______, 2013, by and between
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of
Wyoming, acting by and through its political subdivision, the Cheyenne Police Department,
hereinafter referred to as the “Department,” and Ashley McInelly, hereinafter referred to as
“McInelly.”

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Department is a law enforcement agency serving the City of Cheyenne, and
is a department thereof; and

WHEREAS, the Department has received a grant of funds from the Wyoming Division of
Victim Services for the purpose of providing crime victim assistance services; and

WHEREAS, in order to provide crime victim assistance services, the Department desires to
retain the services of a “Victim Assistance Advocate;” and

WHEREAS, because funding a Victim Assistance Advocate position is dependent on the
receipt of a grant, the Department wishes to create a contract employee rather than permanent
employee position; and

WHEREAS, McInelly has training and experience in the field of assisting crime victims; and

WHEREAS, McInelly desires to provide services to the Department under the terms and
conditions hereinafter contained.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises and mutual covenants herein, the
parties agree as follows:

1.
The term of this Agreement shall commence July 1, 2013, and terminate June 30,
2014, unless sooner terminated pursuant to Section 9 below or if grant funding is no longer available.

2.
McInelly will be considered a contract employee of the Department.  She will act as a
Victim Assistance Advocate and assist the Department.

3.
McInelly shall at all times coordinate her activities hereunder with the Department
and shall work under the direction of the Department.

4.
The Department agrees to pay McInelly the sum of Two Thousand Two Hundred
Fifteen Dollars and Forty-Three Cents ($2,215.43) per month.  The total amount over the term of this
agreement will not, in any event, exceed the sum of Twenty-Six  Thousand Five Hundred Eighty-
Five Dollars and Sixteen ($26,585.16).

5.
The Department shall provide McInelly the benefits provided to full-time employees
of the City of Cheyenne.  The City of Cheyenne will withhold appropriate federal income and social
security taxes and remit them to the federal government.

6.
In addition to the compensation set forth above, the Department agrees to pay
McInelly for hours worked in excess of forty hours in a work week at time and one-half rate.

7.
In the event that the governing body authorizes a bonus to be paid to city employees,
McInelly would be eligible to receive a bonus similar to other city employees. Such bonus payment
shall first be made out of grant funds, if available, then out of Department funds, if grant funds are
not available.
