RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
April 13, 2015
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Brown, Mr. Cook (arriving at 6:02 p.m.), Mr. Johnson, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr.
Roybal, Mr. Shanor, Mr Valdez and Ms. Williams.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk, and
Dan White, City Attorney.  Mayor Kaysen recognized members of the Mighty Eagles 4-H Club
present at the meeting who then led recitation of the pledge of allegiance.
Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed  from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Luna moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by
Ms. Williams.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on March 23, 2015.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown and Mr. Roybal each declared a conflict of interest (business interest in vouchers
payable to Ameri-Tech HVAC Services and Tyrrell-Doyle Chevrolet respectively) and vacated
Council Chambers.  Mr. Shanor moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by
Mr. Valdez .  Upon inquiry, Matt Ashby, Planning Services Director; Kari Kivisto, Leisure Services
Director and Mayor Kaysen provided information available on vouchers payable to Environmental
Health (fee collected for reviewing plats); refunds to several individuals for deposits for “Learn to
Play” hockey equipment rentals, and The Nature Conservancy (cost of easement share for property
located next to the Belvoir Ranch per recent approval by resolution).  Motion carried.  Voting “yes”
-- all members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the
vote, Mr. Brown and Mr. Roybal returned to Council Chambers.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive public comment regarding the intent of the
City of Cheyenne to submit an application for funding to the Wyoming Business Council’s Business
Ready Community, Community Enhancement Grant Program, to assist in funding the West Edge
Streetscape and Civic Center Commons.  Cindy Schneider, Grants Manager, advised the grant
request amount is $500,000.00 with a 50% City match.  She noted elements of the project for which
the funding will be used and reviewed the grant approval process and time frames.  Gene
MacDonald,  Ayres Associates, project consultant, provided information on the project and advised
the grant will assist with streetscape design and implementation throughout the West Edge District
and Civic Center Commons area.  Upon inquiry, Mr. MacDonald reviewed the geographic area of
the Civic Center Commons portion of the project.  Frank Cole expressed concern with the proposed
removal of the parking lot west of the municipal building used by the public during numerous
activities held at the Civic Center.  Mayor Kaysen advised as part of the grant process involving
planning and streetscape design, the parking issues will be addressed.  There being no further
comments and  purpose of the public hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the hearing.
