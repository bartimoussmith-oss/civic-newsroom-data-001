The above referenced contract is amended or modified as set forth in the following and these
amendments and modifications of the plans and specifications are agreed to by the parties. All other
aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

I. Description of Changes

Per the Single Audit Act, governmental recipients of $750,000 or more per year in federal financial
assistance must undergo an organization-wide financial and compliance audit.  When MHP provided
the cost proposal for the City’s annual single audit for Fiscal Year 2022, the total amount of federal
expenditures from July 1, 2021 to June 30, 2022 was not known.  Once the City Treasurer prepared
the Schedule of Expenditures for Federal Awards (SEFA) and provided it to MHP, they determined
that a total of seven federal programs were required to be audited instead of the four that were done
the previous fiscal year. In Fiscal Year 2021, the City had $8,273,134 in federal expenditures and 36
federal grant awards.  In comparison, in Fiscal Year 2022 there were 43 awards, an increase of
seven.  Additionally, federal expenditures increased by $3,445,558 to $11,718,692 in Fiscal Year
2022.  Therefore, MHP is requesting a contract modification in the amount of $10,500, which is
$3,500 for each additional federal program.

II. New Contract Items

Audit of three additional federal programs that were not included in the original scope of the project.
This Contract Modification #1 also includes Federal Provisions and Clauses, as required by the
funding source (American Rescue Plan Federal Funds).

III. Schedule

This Contract Modification #1 will not have an impact to the project schedule.  The annual audit
will be conducted as previously scheduled.
