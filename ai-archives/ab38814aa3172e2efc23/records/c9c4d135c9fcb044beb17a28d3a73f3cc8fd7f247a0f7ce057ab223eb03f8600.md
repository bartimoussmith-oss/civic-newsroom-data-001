Laramie County Road Impact Fee_County Commission

Colin McAweeney of TischlerBose consulting firm, presented an overview of what a
transportation impact fee would look like for unincorporated areas of Laramie County.
He explained an impact fee would be a one-time payment for growth-related
infrastructure that is generally collected at the time building permits are issued.  He
said the fee is similar to a contractual arrangement to build infrastructure with fee
revenues.
The report was received and filed.
