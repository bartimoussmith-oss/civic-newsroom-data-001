```text
CONTRACT NO. __________
AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND
THE CHEYENNE SYMPHONY ORCHESTRA
1.
Parties.  The parties to this Agreement are the City of Cheyenne, a municipality duly
organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, hereafter “City”, whose address is
2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the Cheyenne Symphony Orchestra, a
Wyoming non-profit corporation, hereafter “CSO”, whose address is 1904 Thomes Avenue,
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.
2.
Purpose of Agreement.  The purpose of this Agreement is to authorize the CSO to use the
Cheyenne Civic Center, subject to the terms and conditions in this Agreement, on the dates contained
in Attachment A, attached hereto, incorporated by reference and expressly made a part of this
Agreement.
3.
Term.  This Agreement is effective when all parties have executed it and all approvals have
been granted.  The term of this Agreement is from July 1, 2013, through June 30, 2014.  By mutual
written agreement, this Agreement may be extended for two additional one year terms.
4.
Payment.  The CSO agrees to pay the City the sum of $3,200.00 per performance.  This
amount will constitute the total amount to be paid by the CSO for use of the Civic Center for each
performance, which shall include all labor, utilities, rental, and all other fees.  The term
“Performance” includes each public performance and all rehearsals, which shall not exceed three per
performance.  Use of the Civic Center for Saturday rehearsals is part of the Saturday performance
and are included as part of the performance charge.
5.
City’s Responsibilities.  The City will:
a.
Provide storage for the CSO refrigerator, shelves, signs, floral arrangements,
programs, decorations and related materials, and storage in one Green Room cupboard;
b.
Display the CSO Foundation logo and related materials;
c.
Provide concessions during concert intermissions;
d.
Furnish unsecured storage for musical instruments and related equipment during the
week of rehearsals.  The CSO acknowledges its understanding that the risk of loss for instruments
and equipment while in storage or use at the Civic Center will remain exclusively with the CSO.
The CSO agrees to hold the City of Cheyenne and the Civic Center harmless from and indemnify
them from any loss or damage to any instrument or equipment which may occur;
```

```text
CSO’s Responsibilities.  The CSO shall:
a.
Conduct all performances and rehearsals in accordance with the rules and regulations

b.
Clean up and maintain all areas used following rehearsals and performances.  CSO

c.
Furnish and commit to performance and rehearsal dates no later than 45 days prior
to each;
d.
CSO shall provide commercial general liability insurance for claims arising out of
bodily injury, illness and death, or from damage to or destruction of property of others, including loss
or use thereof, with minimum limits of $1,000,000.00 per occurrence and $2,000,000.00 aggregate
for the entire term of this Agreement.
i.
The City of Cheyenne shall be named as an additional insured on CSO’s
general liability insurance policy and CSO shall provide the City with a copy of the
endorsement.
ii.
CSO shall provide the City with certificates of insurance acknowledging the
above-stated coverage prior to beginning any work under this Agreement.
```

```text
7.
Special Provisions.
a.
Rehearsals.  During rehearsal periods, CSO will prevent its personnel from entering
areas of the Civic Center other than the specific contracted rehearsal space and related performer
support areas: i.e., the stage, dressing rooms, Green Room and restrooms.  Performers are to avoid
the front-of-house areas.
b.
Rehearsal Sites.  It is the intent of the parties that rehearsals be held in the Civic
Center when it is available.  For the 2013-2014 season, the performance and rehearsal dates are set
forth in Attachment A, attached hereto.  The Civic Center reserves the right to preempt any
scheduled  rehearsal date identified in Attachment A by scheduling another activity or event in the
Civic Center on said date, so long as the other activity or event is scheduled at least 45 days prior
to the scheduled rehearsal date.  The Civic Center may not preempt a scheduled rehearsal in the 44
day period prior to the scheduled rehearsal date.
i.
If the Civic Center commits to another activity or event at least 45 days before
a scheduled rehearsal date identified in Attachment A, the Director will hand deliver notice
of the commitment to the CSO at least 45 days prior to the scheduled rehearsal.  Upon proper
delivery of notice, the CSO may choose one of the alternate rehearsal sites listed in
subparagraph c.  The CSO must hand deliver notice of its intent to use an alternate site within
two weeks after receipt of notice from the Director.
```

```text
In the event a rehearsal is relocated, the Civic Center will furnish a crew to
load CSO instruments onto any means of transportation arranged by the CSO for that

e.

8.
General Provisions.
a.
Amendments.  Any changes, modifications, revisions or amendments to this
Agreement which are mutually agreed upon by the parties to this Agreement must be incorporated
by written instrument, executed and signed by all parties.
b.
Applicable Law.  The construction, interpretation and enforcement of this Agreement
are governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming.  In the event of a lawsuit, the courts of the State
of Wyoming will have jurisdiction and venue will be in the First Judicial District, Laramie County,
Wyoming.
c.
Compliance with Laws.  The CSO agrees to comply with all applicable federal, state
and local laws and regulations in the performance of this Agreement.
d.
Entirety of Agreement.  This Agreement and Attachment A represent the entire and
integrated agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations and
agreements, whether written or oral.
e.
Indemnification.  The CSO agrees to indemnify, hold harmless and defend the City
from and against any and all liabilities, claims, penalties, forfeitures and suits, and the cost and
expenses incident thereto including reasonable attorney’s fees, which may hereafter arise as a result
of death or bodily injury to any person, destruction or damage to any property, contamination of or
adverse effects on the environment, or any violation of governmental laws, regulations or orders to
```

```text
5
the extent caused by (1) CSO’s breach of any term or provision of this Agreement, or (2) any
negligent or wrongful act, error or omission by CSO, or its employees or subcontractors in the
performance of this Agreement.  CSO acknowledges that it may incur a financial obligation to the
City pursuant to the terms of this paragraph.
f.
Notice.  All notices arising out of or from the provisions of this Agreement will be
in writing and given to the parties either by regular mail or delivery in person.
g.
Prior Approval.  This Agreement will not be binding upon either party, no services
will be performed under the terms of this Agreement nor payment made until this Agreement has
been approved by all necessary authorities.
h.
Governmental Immunity.  The City of Cheyenne expressly reserves its right to invoke
governmental immunity for any claim arising out of this Agreement, pursuant to the Wyoming
Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. § 1-39-101, et seq.
i.
Termination.  Either party may terminate this Agreement for cause after providing
written notice and a 30 day opportunity to cure any breach of this Agreement.  Either party may,
upon 30 days written notice, terminate this Agreement without cause.
j.
Force Majeure.  The parties agree that any delay or failure of the other to perform its
obligations under this Agreement, except for the payment of money for services rendered, shall be
excused due to acts of God, strikes, action of regulatory agencies, fire, flood, windstorm, explosion,
riot, war, sabotage, or other cause or causes beyond the reasonable control of the non-performing
party, provided that timely notice of such delay is given by the party to the other and each of the
parties shall be diligent in attempting to remove the cause of non-performance.
9.
Signatures.  In witness thereof, the parties to this Agreement, through their duly authorized
representatives, have executed this Agreement on the days and dates set out below and certify that
they have read, understood and agreed to the terms and conditions of this Agreement.
```
