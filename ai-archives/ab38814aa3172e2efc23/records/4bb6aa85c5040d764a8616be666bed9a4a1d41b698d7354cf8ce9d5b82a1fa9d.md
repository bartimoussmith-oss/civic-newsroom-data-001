UDC-23-98

September 5, 2023
The Future Land Use Designation for this property and most surrounding properties is Mixed
Use Residential. Surrounding the area is also the Mixed Use Commercial designation. These
designations encourage a mix of residential and commercial uses with associated zone districts.
According to comprehensive plan the optimal City zone for the subject property’s future land
use designation is Mixed-use Residential, but a variety of commercial or residential zones may
be applicable to meet the intended development pattern. While the future land use map of
PlanCheyenne would suggest a commercial or residential zoning for the property, public uses
such as parks, schools, and recreation facilities can be appropriate in nearly any zoning district,
serving residents and businesses either nearby or regionally. It is hard to predict where public
uses will be needed or where such public-use development is feasible. Although there is a
‘public’ designation on the future land use map, it is largely only used for existing public (or
quasi-public) facilities.

PlanCheyenne encourages the measured growth of public facilities to keep up with the needs
and demands of a growing community. Public investment is encouraged along the west
Lincolnway corridor to encourage private investment. The creation of public facilities is one form
of public investment that can assist in this objective. Quality of life amenities (including
recreation-center-like facilities) are encouraged to be competitive with front range facilities.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Nearby properties are zoned LI, CB and MUB. Due to the nature of the P zone, it is frequently
found in areas all by itself for public facilities that are surrounded by private developments.
Generally speaking, the P zone is appropriate anywhere in the community where a public land
use is present, as such, the P zone is largely compatible with all zone districts and
comprehensive plan designations.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The existing zone district allows for a variety of residential and commercial uses. The P district
allows for a variety of public uses. While the requested P zone has significantly fewer allowed
uses, the property is owned by the City, and public uses are anticipated for the property.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

Although the underlying comprehensive plan designations would suggest a smaller block size,
due to the varied public uses Article 4 has a specific exemption for civic uses in regards to block
size. The property has multiple possible access points and could also share access with

The project is located in an area covered by the following plans:

Conformance with PlanCheyenne is covered by review criterion 1.

The Missile Drive Corridor Plan (2010) examine the potential redevelopment of the area. It suggested

focused on the Ice and Events Center. The Back 40 Subdivision 3rd Filing Plat helped establish street
network connectivity within the current constraints. The proposed gymnastics facility is considered an
entertainment venue and would expand the entertainment district at this location in conformance with
this plans’ recommendations.
