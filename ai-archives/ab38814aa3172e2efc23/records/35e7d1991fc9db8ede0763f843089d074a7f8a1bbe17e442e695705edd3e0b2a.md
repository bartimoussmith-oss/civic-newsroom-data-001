Note 1: The freon removal fee must be paid in addition to disposal fee for all refrigerators, freezers, and
air conditioners unless freon removal has been certified by a licensed repairman.
Note 2: A “building,” for the purposes of Section 8.44.010 D of City Code, is defined as a non-residential
premises using a water meter.  Buildings will be assessed at least a “Basic Rate Fee” for each collection
point (i.e., container or set of containers) or charges based on the type and quantity of containers and
frequency of pickups as outlined in this Solid Waste Fee Schedule.  Multiple collocated, non-residential
buildings owned by a single property owner will be assessed at least a “Basic Rate Fee” equal to the
number of collection points.  If 1.5 Cubic Yard or larger city dumpsters are used, the Basic Rate Fee is
included in the fees for those containers.  Charges are normally included in water and sewer bills issued
to property owners or agents of such premises by the Board of Public Utilities.

Note 3: If material is left outside of containers (dumpsters) requiring manual pickup, the owner of the
property may be assessed an additional cleanup fee for manpower and equipment time.

Note 4: City residents must show identification (driver’s license, etc.) and certify that material is their
personal household waste to receive reduced Transfer Station rates.

Note 5: Electronic Components: Charges to commercial entities for recycling of electronic components
are based on the City’s cost for recycling plus 10% for handling and processing. Contact the Solid Waste
Division for current prices.  Electronic components (computers, televisions, cell phones, etc.) contain
hazardous materials and must not be disposed of in the Landfill. Recycling of these components is free
to city/county residents at the Transfer Station.

Note 6: Tires larger than 20” will be charged by weight.

Note 7: Residents must present documentation from a doctor to qualify for “Special Attention” services.
Documentation must be updated on an annual basis to continue in the program.

Note 8: Residential recycle containers will be equal to or larger than residential trash containers.  To
qualify for the smaller 60-gallon recycle container, residents must also have a 60-gallon trash container.
Residents may choose not to accept a recycle container; however, there will be no reduction in the
residential automated collection fee.

Note 9: Overweight or over-packed Roll-Off Containers will be charged double the disposal fee.
Overloaded cardboard containers will be charged based on time required to empty the container.

Note 10: Residential trash and recycle service may be suspended for full months only. Full month
suspension dates are based on billing cycles as defined by the Board of Public Utilities.

Note 11: The optional yard waste collection program may not be suspended, only discontinued.
Residents who pay for the optional yard waste container receive pickup of up to one cubic yard of limbs,
tree trimmings, hedge clippings and similar material per pickup.  Residents must cut items to less than
four-foot lengths and contact the Sanitation Division to schedule pickup.  The Sanitation Division does
not provide yard waste removal services to those who do not subscribe to the optional yard waste
collection program.

Note 12: Place automated trash, recycle and yard waste containers a minimum of two feet from any
object, i.e., vehicles, trailers, fences, poles, trees, other containers, etc., and be sure the area in front of
the container (toward the street) is clear of obstructions. A Container Placement Fee is charged each
time a driver must exit the vehicle to move a container so it may be safely dumped without striking a
nearby object. The fee will not apply if a container is blown over by the wind or other act of nature.

Note 13:  These charges apply to customers who do not get a water bill.  Billing must be done manually
by Solid Waste Staff.

Note 14:  The Landfill Manager may request to inspect the origin of the soil and require testing to verify
the soil is uncontaminated.  The customer will pay for any testing.

Note 15:  The Public Works Director or Deputy Director may negotiate costs and terms for unusual
requests to dispose of material at the Landfill.  Examples include but are not limited to:  material from
large commercial demolition or remodeling projects; material such as dirt that may be needed by the
City; and material from accidents or natural disasters.  Testing for hazardous materials may be required
in these instances.
