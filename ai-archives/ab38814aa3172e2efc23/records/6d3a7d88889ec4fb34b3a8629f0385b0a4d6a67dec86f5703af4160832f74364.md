UDC-22-00425-01
January 9, 2023
APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

UDC 2.4.2 Annexations

PlanCheyenne

BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting annexing 10.7 acres of land to develop the property with apartments. The land is
currently developed with one rural-residential style dwelling. A zone change and expedited preliminary plat is
proposed concurrently with this annexation. All applications are anticipated to come to final consideration
before the Governing Body at the same meeting.

Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. The petition to annex has been accompanied by a submission
to change the zoning from County A-1 to City NR-3.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned with an agricultural zone in the County. The property is concurrently
proposed to be annexed and rezoned to the City zone of NR-3. The City NR-3 zone is intended for high-
density residential development in a walkable format.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

To accept the petition to annex and approve the annexation Ordinance, the Governing Body must make
the following findings required in WS 15-1-402; a detailed analysis of which is below:

1) An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety and welfare of the
persons residing in the area and in the city or town.  [WS 15-1-402 (a) (i)]

The annexation of this property will increase the health, safety, and welfare of the future
occupants. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to City Fire and Police and
sanitation services. The property, if occupied, will also be required to be connected to City water
and sanitary sewer instead of having wells and septic systems.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would constitute a natural,
geographical, economical and social part of the annexing city or town. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (ii)]

This property, once developed, will constitute a natural, geographic, economic, and social part
of the city. The property is adjacent to the City to the west, north, and east and is part of a
County pocket. The contiguity to the City and the proximity to surrounding urban-style
development makes the parcel a natural geographic part of the City. The development of this
property will benefit those who live within the City and will be a part of the social and economic
fabric that is Cheyenne.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city or town
and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of the city or
town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed. [WS 15-1-402 (a)
(iii)]

The area is a logical and feasible addition to the City due to the reasons previously listed. All City
services normally available to residents will also be made available. Police and fire protection
will be extended upon annexation. Utilities exist in the area.

The application complies with this review criterion.

4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or town, or
the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (iv)]

The area proposed for annexation is about 77% contiguous, along all but the southern edge.

The application complies with this review criterion.

5) If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the annexed area pursuant
to W.S. 15-1-410. [WS 15-1-402 (a) (v)]

Utility franchises are in place to serve the annexed area.

The application complies with this review criterion.

6) The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the public hearing
required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all landowners and affected public
utilities within the territory a summary of the proposed annexation report as required under
subsection (c) of this section and notice of the time, date and location of the public hearing
required by W.S. 15-1-405(a). [WS 15-1-402 (a) (vi)]

A certified mailing was made to the landowners and public utilities on December 6, 2022. The
public hearing for this annexation is scheduled for January 9, 2023. Twenty (20) business days
 prior to the announced public hearing is December 6, 2022. The earliest
confirmation of delivery was dated December 7, 2022.

CONFORMANCE WITH APPLICABLE PLANS:

The project is located in an area covered by the following plans:

Plan Cheyenne

Policies within PlanCheyenne encourage the annexation of County pockets. This is part of a County
Pocket.

The Future Land Use Map of PlanCheyenne, as updated by Connect 2045, anticipates this area of the
community to develop with urban residential uses. These areas are expected to develop with full access
to urban services  such areas are best served by being within City limits. The area is within the Urban
Service Boundary.

STAFF REVIEW:

Through the review process, no comments were received.

CONCLUSION:

Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria and therefore recommends approval of
the annexation.

The proposed annexation is a natural extension of the City, is contiguous to the current City limits, and
most services typically available to urban areas are available to be extended to this property.

Staff recommends approval of the annexation of the property.
