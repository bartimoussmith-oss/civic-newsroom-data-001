PROPOSED ALCOHOL TRAINING PROGRAM FOR WESTBY EDGE
Introductory statement:
We take this issue very seriously, we will provide consistent and substantive training for
all employees (and volunteers if any) serving alcohol in our establishment.
Initial orientation:
We will provide an initial orientation to all employees (and volunteers if any) when they
begin work about our commitment to a safe and secure establishment where alcohol will be served.
We will explain the required training including requirements of successfully completing the
training and updating and maintaining certifications.
Substantive training:
We will require that all employees (and volunteers if any) who sell, serve or deliver
alcoholic or malt beverages or provide on-site security are TIPS trained in a program approved by
the Wyoming Liquor Division by a certified trainer. Completion of the training will be a
requirement before the employee with be allowed to sell, serve or deliver alcohol. We will maintain
TIPS training and certification as part of our business records and make sure that every person
selling, serving, or delivering alcohol or involved in on-site security is current on his/her TIPS
training. This documentation will be available to law enforcement personnel for inspection.
Alcohol Related Policies:
No alcohol will be sold or served to persons under the legal drinking age within the licensed
or permitted building or authorized areas of service.
All incidents requiring law enforcement responsive services will be immediately reported
by the licensee, permittee, employees, volunteers, on-site managers, agents or any persons
providing on-site security services. Immediate notice will be provided to the Police Department of
any criminal or illegal activity observed on the licensee’s or permittee’ s property or adjoining
property affiliated with the sale or consumption of alcoholic liquor and malt beverages, including
the reporting of any assault, aggravated assault, or assault and battery.
