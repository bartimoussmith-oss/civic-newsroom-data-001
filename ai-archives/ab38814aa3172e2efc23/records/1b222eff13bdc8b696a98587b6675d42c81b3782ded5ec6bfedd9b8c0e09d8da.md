Background:

The item for consideration is a resolution of support required by the LWCF conversion process to request
conversion of a portion of Dutcher Ball Fields, approximately 2.5 acres, to be removed from perpetual
public outdoor recreation use for the new city fire station.  The LWCF regulations require that we provide
other suitable properties that meet LWCF requirements such as being of at least equal fair market value
and of reasonably equivalent usefulness as trade.  The city has already agreed to restrict the Keizer, Lewark
and Winkler properties for this purpose but, unfortunately, we have decided that we need further
environmental investigations on the Winkler property due to its previous use as an auto salvage yard.     A
federal standards appraisal and review have been completed for the Rotary Park property and it shows,
combined with the appraised value of both the Lewark and Keizer purchases, an equal and/or greater fair
market value.  The Rotary Park property will provide land for park amenities as well as improvements to
the Dry Creek corridor for water quality, storage, and public enjoyment.

WHEREAS, the City desires to convert approximately two point five (2.5) acres of the
Dutcher Ball Field property to a non-recreational use to build a municipal fire station;

WHEREAS, the Land and Water Conservation Fund (LWCF) Act of 1965 was enacted to
help preserve, develop, and ensure access to outdoor recreation facilities to strengthen the health
of U.S. citizens. The law created the LWCF in the U.S. Treasury as a funding source to implement
its outdoor recreation goals. Parks or recreation areas acquired or developed with LWCF assistance
(in whole or part) are now referred to as "LWCF-assisted areas" and/or "LWCF boundary areas.";

WHEREAS, the LWCF requires that no property acquired or developed with LWCF
assistance areas shall be converted to other than public outdoor recreation uses without the approval
of the Secretary of the Interior. If the Secretary finds the property to be in accordance with the then
existing SCORP and only upon such conditions as the Secretary deems necessary to assure the
substitution of other recreation properties of at least equal fair market value and of reasonably
equivalent usefulness and location;

WHEREAS, the City intends to take actions to accept donation of the twelve point six
(12.06) acre Rotary Park property and agrees to allow deed restriction for perpetual outdoor
recreation use as described in attached exhibits, at the time of purchase;
