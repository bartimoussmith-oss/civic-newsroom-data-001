The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing

The proposed plat re-arranges lots for multifamily development. The existing lots have received

plat allows a Greenway to run through the area and the multifamily to be more logically laid out.
The size and location of lots is similar to those found in the area. The plat appears to be able to

Community Facilities and Fees (4.2):
The proposed subdivision is in an area with existing facilities. The City owns land within the
plat boundary which can be developed with a public facility (the plan is to utilize the land for
a Greenway). Park Fees are not charged with the signing of the plat if the plat does not
increase area, number of lots or development area. As the developable area is being
swapped with the City. No fees are due at plat signing.

Transportation Network (4.3):
This plat is occurring within an area of the community which has largely been previously
developed. The plat is limited by the railroad to the south. An exception for block sizes must
be granted.

Open Space (4.4):
The Greenway is part of this plat, and multifamily units may provide open space at the site
plan level, should the Greenway not meet requirements.

Required Improvements (4.5):
The streets in the area are existing, but improvements and utilities may need to be
developed with the plat.

Public Safety Fees (4.6):
Public Safety Fees will be due with building permits of new construction on the site.

The application complies with this review criterion, with an exception.

4) Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and demonstrates a logical and
well-planned development pattern. This may include consideration of amenities and public
facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to the anticipated timing or the
likelihood of future phases being constructed as planned. (UDC 2.1.2.d.4)

No phasing is specifically proposed with this project.

narrow frontages to the east and west abut largely vacant land. The land has already received
conditional use approval for multifamily development and could develop in that manner without
this platting action. The plat allows for a more logical alignment of the project and interface with
a Greenway.

STAFF REVIEW:
Through the review process, comments were received regarding the following issues:
1. Correct ownership.
2. Technical corrections.
3. Drainage requirements needing to be addressed.
4. Including the entire City-owned parcel in the plat.
5. Putting all parking in a common lot.
6. Note language concerns.
Most issues have been addressed through a revised submittal. Technical corrections still need to
be addressed and Note 6 should be removed from the Plat. The utility easements should be a
general utility easement and not granted to a specific utility company. The easement on the
original plat was not granted to a specific utility company and should not be changed with this
Plat.

The applicant has indicated that an easement will be placed on the common parking lot at a
later date because the parking lot design/location could change during the site plan process.
Staff is satisfied with this.

CONCLUSION:
Staff analysis is that the application meets the review criteria and therefore recommends approval of
the expedited plat application as submitted by the applicant, with conditions and the granting of an
exception to block size requirements. The area this plat is proposed in an area with existing street
network. The property is constrained by the railroad.

Recommendation)
3. Recommend approval of the preliminary plat as submitted by the applicant and forward the
