RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
April 26, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS: Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker
and Mr. Valdez.  Absent:  Ms. Case.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Upon request by Mr. Brown, and pursuant to support received by
Mr. Collins and Ms. Ash, agenda item #16 was removed from the Consent Agenda. Mr. Spiker
moved to approve remaining Consent Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Valdez. Motion carried.

Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing for the purpose of receiving comments and input for
the City of Cheyenne Comprehensive Consolidated Plan for 2010-2014 and the Annual Action Plan
for Fiscal Year 2010-2011 for the City of Cheyenne Community Development Block Grant (CDBG)
entitlement.  Deanne Widauf, Housing and Community Development Office, provided an overview
of the CDBG entitlement program and the Comprehensive Consolidated Plan, and cited some of the
identified Plan goals and objectives.  She noted the Consolidated Plan also incorporates the Annual
Action Plan for fiscal year 2010-2011 for which the City’s estimated entitlement allocation is
$580,253.00 with an anticipated additional $5,000 in program income.  Ms. Widauf described the
grant application process; public notifications and opportunities for input; oversight of the grant
process by the Advisory Council, and the Advisory Council’s review and recommendations
involving eligible applications.  She identified proposed recommended projects and program
administration, and estimated funding allocation amount for each.  Upon inquiry regarding proposed
funding for a Parks and Recreation structural safety and restoration project, Rick Parish, Parks and
Recreation Director, clarified the project involves re-siding and energy efficient window
replacements for the Pioneer Park Activity Center.  There being no further comments or input,
Mayor Kaysen advised the purpose of the hearing had been met and closed the hearing.

Laramie County Commissioner, spoke in opposition of the proposed annexation.  Among the topics
presented and discussed were:  City/County zoning regulations; health, safety and welfare concerns
involving the volume of train and vehicular traffic and affiliated traffic congestion and delays;
unknown property development plans by the developer; annexation criteria pursuant to Wyo. Stat.
§15-1-402; contiguity concerns relative to annexation of Swan Ranch area; county island creation;
mental and financial stress to area residents and concerns involving future monetary liability
involving infrastructure costs; request to eliminate Woodenshoe Drive from the annexation area;
timeliness of emergency services response, and suggestion that a “sketch plan” be required to review
developer’s proposed  infrastructure, transportation and drainage solutions. Mike Basom, Dray,
Thomson and Dyekman law firm, attorney representing the developer, spoke in favor of the
annexation to enable the platting process to move forward to address issues raised involving safety
and traffic concerns.  He stated no changes to the area after annexation will be made until a plat is
prepared and approved by the governing body.  Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr.
Collins moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Ms. Ash.  Discussion followed
and included: Area access; traffic and transportation issues; property owner’s (developer) request
for annexation; contiguity; area residents’ concerns that have been raised, and train traffic at the
crossing on Southwest Drive.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present with the exception of Mr. Valdez voting “no”. (#3889)
