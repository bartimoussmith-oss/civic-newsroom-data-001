RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
August 2, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in regular session beginning at 12:00
p.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of acting upon vouchers for payment as presented.
Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms.
Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Mr. Collins and Dr. Rinne.
Also present:  Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City
Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Mr. Valdez moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Ms. Broyles.
Discussion was held concerning use of temporary personnel for coverage due to regular employee
vacation, sick leave, etc. absences; payroll (per hour) costs of same; qualifications, and comparison
of costs if services were utilized through private temporary employment agencies.  Mayor Kaysen
and Ms. Broyles, City Council President, provided information pertaining to specific recent
temporary staff position coverages involving the Mayor and City Council offices.  Motion to
approve carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present with the exception of
Ms. Case voting “no”.
Purpose of the meeting having been met, Mayor Kaysen adjourned the meeting at 12:09 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
City Clerk
This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request
Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle
August 5, 2010
