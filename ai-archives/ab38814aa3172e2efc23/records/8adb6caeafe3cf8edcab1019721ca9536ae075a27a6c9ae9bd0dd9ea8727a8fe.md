WYOMING:
Section 1. That various sections of Chapter 5.12, Alcoholic Liquor and Malt Beverages,
of Title 5, Business Licenses and Regulations, of the Code ofthe City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are
hereby amended as set out in this ordinance.
5.12.020
Definitions.
“Delivery service” means the taking of orders and providing the transporting and delivery
of alcoholic liquor and malt beverage products to patrons at a residence, organization or business
for a fee, in violation of the provisions of the chapter. and a liquor licensee’s process by which
their employee or contractor provides alcohol to a purchaser.
1.
“Delivery” means the act of providing alcohol to a purchaser.
2.
“Delivery Employee” means the licensee’s employee or contractor responsible for
delivering alcohol to the purchaser.
3.
“Sealed” means in its original package and unopened-factory sealed (bottle of wine or
spirits/case of beer), in a plastic bag, heat sealed closed, or in a container that has a
breakable seal incorporated in the top.
4.
“Serve” means the act of delivering alcohol to a purchaser or delivery employee or
contractor on the licensed premises.
5.12.070
Restrictions upon license or permit applicants and holders.
B.
Retail Liquor Licensees.
3.
A licensee shall not may provide any alcoholic liquor or malt beverage delivery
services in conjunction with the licensee’s business operations pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Section 12-
5-601.
a.
Retail liquor licensees, microbrewery permittees, winery permittees,
satellite winery permittees, and satellite manufacturing permittees, may be permitted to
provide delivery service for the delivery of alcohol to customers.
Microbreweries and
wineries shall only be permitted to provide delivery of their manufactured product.
Delivery services shall be subject to the following:
i.
No order shall be received from nor delivery made to a person under twenty-
one (21) years of age.
Each purchaser shall be required to provide a valid
driver’s license or valid government issued identification upon delivery.
ii.
No part of a publicly owned sidewalk, highway, street, or alley shall be used
for taking orders or conducting sales.
No sales shall be made to intoxicated
individuals pursuant to Section 5.12.160(C) of this chapter.
iii. Alcohol shall only be delivered to customers in a sealed package.
iv. Delivery shall not be made to or on public property and shall not be delivered
outside the city of Cheyenne boundaries unless otherwise allowed by Wyoming
Statute, resolution of the Laramie County Commissioners, or order of the
Governor of Wyoming.
