MEMORANDUM OF
UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
LARAMIE COUNTY SCHOOL DISTRICT NUMBER
ONE AND THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

1.
Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") is made and entered into by and
between Laramie County School District Number One ("District"), whose address is
2810 House Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001, and the City of Cheyenne, a
municipality organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, acting
through its Recreation Division ("City"), whose address is 1317 Parsley Blvd, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82001.

2.
Purpose. The purpose of this MOU is to provide transportation service for the Cheyenne
Parks and Recreation “REC Camp Program” by leasing school buses from the Laramie
County School District Number One.

3.
Term of MOU. This MOU shall, when approved and executed by the parties hereto,
remain in full force and effect from June 11, 2023, through August 11, 2023. The
payment and indemnification provisions of this MOU shall survive the termination
hereof.

4.
Payment. Upon receipt of an itemized invoice from the District and on or before
September 30, 2023, the City agrees to pay the District for the costs of transportation for
the duration of this MOU. Such costs shall include, but not necessarily be limited to
maintenance and depreciation, drivers’ salary and benefits, and fuel. Billing will include
the following:

A. $2.27/mile to cover maintenance, depreciation, and administrative costs.

B. Driver salary and benefits will be actual costs. The City will be responsible
hourly wages and benefits for the drivers’ personal break time, as long as said break
times do not exceed legal limits.

C. Fuel cost will be the average price for the month of fuel sold to the District
Transportation Department.

5.
Responsibilities of District.   The District shall furnish a maximum of four (4) buses with
a minimum capacity of seventy-two (72) passengers and a maximum of four (4) drivers to
operate the buses. In case of emergency, bus drivers shall remain with the buses at all times.

6.
Qualification of Drivers. For the term of this MOU, the District shall ensure the buses
under this MOU are driven by qualified District personnel who possess valid commercial
driver's licenses for the type of vehicle operated and have passed a Wyoming Division of
Criminal Investigation background verification.

7.
Special Provisions.

A.
The City shall add the designated buses to the City’s existing automobile
insurance policies and will cover the buses for liability and property damage. It is
expressly agreed and understood that the City’s insurance policy is primary, not
contributory. The City shall name the District as an additional insured/loss payee on the
City’s automobile insurance policy and furnish the District with a certificate of insurance
evidencing such coverage.

B.
It is expressly agreed and understood that the City will fully release,
indemnify, and hold harmless the district and its officers, agents, employees, successors
and assigns from any and all causes of action, claims, liabilities, demands, or expenses
arising out of the use of the district school buses under this MOU.

C.
It is expressly agreed and understood that the City will be responsible for
the conduct of students being transported to City programs and will assume disciplinary
responsibility in the event of misconduct.

8.
General Provisions.

A.
Amendments. Any party may request changes in this MOU. Any changes,
modifications, revisions, or amendments to this MOU which are mutually agreed upon
by and between parties to this MOU shall be incorporated by written instrument,
executed, and signed by all parties to this MOU.

B.
Applicable Law. The construction, interpretation and enforcement of this
MOU shall be governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming. The courts of the State of
Wyoming shall have jurisdiction over any action arising out this MOU and over the
parties, and the venue shall be the First Judicial District Court, Laramie County,
Wyoming.
C.
Assignments. This MOU shall be binding upon and inure to the benefit of
the parties hereto and their respective heirs, successors, transferees, and permitted
assignees. No party shall have the right to assign any rights or obligations under this
MOU without the express written consent of both parties.

D.
Entirety of Agreement. This MOU, consisting of five (5) pages, represents
the entire and integrated agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior
negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

E.
Force Majeure. No party shall be liable to perform under the MOU if such
failure arises out of causes beyond control and without the fault or negligence of said
party. Such causes may include, but are not limited to, acts of God or the public enemy,
fires, floods, epidemics, quarantine restrictions, freight embargoes, and unusually severe
weather.
