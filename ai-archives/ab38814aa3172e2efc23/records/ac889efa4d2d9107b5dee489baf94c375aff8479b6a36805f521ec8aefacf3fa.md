ORDINANCE NO. ________________
ENTITLED:
“AN ORDINANCE, PURSUANT TO SECTIONS 1.1.6 & 2.4.1 OF THE
CHEYENNE UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE, AMENDING THE
LANDSCAPING REQUIREMENTS UNDER UNIFIED DEVELOPMENT CODE
SECTIONS 6.3.4a, 6.3.4b, AND 6.3.7e.”
BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1.  That on December 5, 2011, the Cheyenne Planning Commission unanimously
recommended approval of the “Cheyenne Unified Development Code.”
Section 2.  That on January 23, 2012, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne
unanimously approved the Unified Development Code (Ordinance No. 3943) with an effective date
of April 30, 2012.
Section 3.  That, in accordance with the Unified Development Code (UDC) Section 2.4.lb(1),
the sponsors of this ordinance are, by their own initiative, proposing amendments to the UDC.
Section 4.  That the sponsors of this Ordinance find that the current landscaping requirements
contained in Sections 6.3.4a, 6.3.4b, and 6.3.7e of the Unified Development Code (“UDC”) need
to be amended.
Section 5.  That on August 19, 2013, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted
a public hearing, reviewed the proposed UDC amendments that are the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended denial of the amendments.
Section 6.  That UDC Section 6.3.4a is amended to read:
Trees.  When a landscape plan is required by UDC Section 6.3.2a, the sum of all
Tree points, as defined in Table 6-8 below, from trees used in the landscape must
exceed the required landscape area measured in square feet divided by 500.  When
a landscape plan is required by UDC Section 6.3.2b or Section 6.3.2c, the sum of all
Tree points, as defined in Table 6-8 below, from trees used in the landscape must
exceed the required landscape measured in square feet area divided by 1,000.  For
the purpose of calculating the Tree Point requirement, utility easements within
internal landscape areas, athletic fields, and playgrounds may be subtracted from the
required internal landscape area.
Section 7.  That UDC Section 6.3.4b is amended to read:
Ground Cover and Non-tree Landscape Features.  When a landscape plan is required
by UDC Section 6.3.2a, the sum of all Ground Cover and Non-tree Landscape
Feature points, as defined in Table 6-9 below, from ground cover and non-tree
landscaping features used in the landscape must exceed the square footage of the
required internal landscape area divided by 500.  When a landscape plan is required
by UDC Sections 6.3.2b or Section 6.3.2c, the sum of all Ground Cover and Non-tree
Landscape Feature points, as defined in Table 6-9 below, from ground cover and
non-tree landscaping features used in the landscape must exceed the square footage
of the required internal landscape area divided by 1,000.
Section 8.  That UDC 6.3.7e is amended to read:
Unless otherwise specified, when a landscape plan is required by UDC Section
6.3.2a, any required landscape area, including the internal landscaping area,
screening and buffering areas, landscaped setback and parking strips, shall consist
of a minimum of 75% ground cover by living grass or other plant materials based on
mature size of vegetation.  Unless otherwise specified, when a landscape plan is
