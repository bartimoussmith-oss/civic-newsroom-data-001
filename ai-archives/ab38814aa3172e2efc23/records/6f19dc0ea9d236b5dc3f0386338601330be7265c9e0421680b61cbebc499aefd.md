RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 19, 2019

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Luna, Mr.
Laybourn, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, and Mr. White.  Absent:  Mr. Case, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Cook,
and Mr. Shanor.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Alessandra Fakelman, Senior Assistant City
Attorney; Ron Downey, Deputy Human Resources Director, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, and
Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook live.
Mayor Orr explained it is the governing body’s intention to go into executive session for the
purpose of evaluating proposed remaining sections.

Mayor Orr provided a handout containing information involving the remaining open
Sections: #4, 15, 17, 18, 20, 21, 22, 23, 27, 28, and 43.

Bill Hinz, Union President, advised the Union proposes the following:  Section 4-leave
open as working document, Section 15-Present Contract Language (PCL), Section 17-PCL,
Section 18-accept City’s language “the Battalion Chief or designee with remaining as PCL,
Section 20-PCL, Section 21-leave as previously proposed-reducing work hours per week from 56
to 52, and 3.5% wage increases for both FY 2020 and 2021 beginning July 1st of each year, Section
22-accept strikeout under  letter A (b), remove strike through on 1st page, remove strike through
on 2nd page, remaining PCL, Section 23-PCL, Section 27-add “Battalion Chief of the Shift” in
Section G, remaining PCL, Section 28-new proposal pertaining to use, accrual and compensation
of sick leave, and Section 43-PCL.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Luna moved to meet in executive
session, seconded by Mr. Roybal.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body present, the
City Clerk, Senior Assistant City Attorney, Deputy Human Resources Director, City Treasurer,
and Chief of the Fire and Rescue Department would also be present at the executive session.  [City
Clerk note: Audio recording of the special meeting was stopped; executive Session convened at
6:17 p.m. in the Mayor’s Conference Room]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:11p.m.

Mayor Orr advised the City proposes the following:  Section 4-leave open as a working
document, Section 15-Present Contract Language (PCL), Section 18-counter Union’s offer with
