WYOMING:
Section1.That Cheyenne City Ordinance No.4503, adopted by the governing body on
May 8,2023, authorized thefee schedule currently applicable to solid waste services under Title 8,
Chapter8.44, of the Cheyenne City Code.

repealed, replaced and otherwise superseded by the schedule appended to this ordinance, whichis
incorporated here in by reference.
Section3.That Cheyenne City Ordinance No.4503isrepealed effective July1,2024.
Section 4. This ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication.
