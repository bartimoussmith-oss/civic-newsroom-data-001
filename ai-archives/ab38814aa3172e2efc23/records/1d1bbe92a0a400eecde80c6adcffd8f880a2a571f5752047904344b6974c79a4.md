BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of a plat for 1.1 acres of land. The subject property contains 8 formerly
platted lots, the alley between the lots, and a previously vacated public street. The plat would consolidate all
of this land into 1 parcel for multifamily development. The land has already received conditional use approval
for multifamily development on the land (UDC-21-00348  approval attached for reference).

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Preliminary Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d   a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

The future land use map within PlanCheyenne identifies this area as developing with urban
residential development, and the proposed plat in this zoning district would be compatible with
this objective. PlanCheyenne supports the use of existing infrastructure and timely provision of
services (including emergency and education services). This development is proposed in an area
which has largely existing infrastructure and no expansion of infrastructure is necessary.
This development is in conformance with the principles and future land uses identified in
PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.
