ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d a detailed analysis is below:
1)
The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)
The Future Land Use designation for the subject property is Public/Quasi-Public. The most
appropriate zone for the Public/Quasi-Public designation is P, Public District. Many religious
institutions within the City carry the Public/Quasi-Public Future Land Use designation. However,
the Public/Quasi-Public designation does not account for the redevelopment and reuse of such
properties. A Future Land Use Map amendment is accompanying this zone change to change the
designation to Community Business which will result in the zone change conforming to the
Future Land Use Map. Furthermore, the property to the southeast is designated Community
Business in the future land use map. Although the proposed Zoning Map Amendment does not
currently conform to the Future Land Use Designation within PlanCheyenne, it does accomplish
several policies and components contained within the Plan. The Zoning Map Amendment fulfills
the following development policies and physical patterns in the Comprehensive Plan:
•
Policy 1.1.B. Flexibility to Market Demands: This goal notes that the Future Land Use
Plan does not predetermine all uses on individual parcels and is meant to provide
flexibility. The future land use map amendment to CB is to accommodate a new use
other than a place of worship that was not foreseen at the time the Future Land Use
Map was created.
•
Policy 1.4.A. lnfill Opportunities: The future land use map amendment will facilitate the
reuse of an existing building and provide an infill opportunity. There is the potential this
use may locate on the periphery of the City if this infill opportunity does not come to
fruition.
•
Policy 1.4.E. Building Reuse: The applicant is proposing to reuse the existing building for
a child care center. This future land use map amendment will facilitate the reuse of an
existing building in a manner that is not possible under its current zoning designation.
Through the several policies addressed in PlanCheyenne and the accompanying Future Land Use
Map Amendment, the proposed zone change for the subject property from HR to CB complies
with review criteria 1.
2)
The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)
The application is compatible with nearby property zoning and future land use designations. The
subject property is situated in a moderate activity area in close proximity to Central High School
and other commercial properties. The surrounding properties to the south and east of the
subject property are zoned CB (Community Business) District and carry a Community Business
Future Land Use Designation. The proposed use is permitted by right in the CB District, meaning
it could locate on the surrounding properties if a development opportunity presented itself. This
illustrates the compatibility of this zone change with the surrounding area and land uses.

UDC-20-00380
October 5, 2020
The application conforms to PlanCheyenne per the discussion with Review Criterion 1.
STAFF REVIEW:
The application is consistent with the surrounding CB zone. The proposed child care use would
be compatible with the residential properties to the north by providing a place for residents to
have childcare in close proximity to their home. Furthermore, if the proposed use does not
come to fruition, staff believes the CB zone would still be appropriate for the subject property.
The application accomplishes several goals contained in PlanCheyenne without having a
negative impact on the health, safety, and welfare of the community.
CONCLUSION:
Staff is satisfied that the application meets the review criteria set forth in UDC 2.2.1.(d) The
application facilitates the reuse of an existing building, is complimentary to the surrounding
neighborhood by providing a place for child care, and is compatible with the existing CB zone
district to the east and south. The zone change meets the review criteria of UDC 2.2.1.(d);
therefore, staff recommends approval of the zoning map amendment as submitted by the
applicant.
ALTERNATIVES:
1.
Recommend approval of the zoning amendment as submitted by the applicant (Staff
Recommendation).
2.
Recommend approval of the zoning amendment subject to conditions or modifications.
3.
Recommend denial of the zoning amendment based on findings of denial. The Planning
Commission must conclude that the project does not meet all the review criteria. As part of the
motion, findings for denial must be stated.
4.
Postpone the zoning amendment until issues identified during the meeting can be resolved.
General Information Regarding Alternatives:
In the event that the Commission acts contrary to staff’s recommendation or modifies conditions of approval,
