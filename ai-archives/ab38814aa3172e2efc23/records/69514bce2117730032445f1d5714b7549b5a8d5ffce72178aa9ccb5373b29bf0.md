RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
September 13, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date
beginning at 6:02 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen;
COUNCIL MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson,
Dr. Rinne (arriving at 6:04 pm), Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City
Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was
recited.
PRESENTATION OF DRUG RECOGNITION EXPERT CENTENNIAL AWARD TO
POLICE CHIEF BRIAN KOZAK FOR HIS CONTRIBUTION TOWARD COMBATING
DRUG IMPAIRED DRIVING, AND COMMITMENT TO HIGHWAY SAFETY.  Mayor
Kaysen advised the individual who would be presenting the award had not yet arrived (traveling
from out of state) and, following query with no objection raised by City Council members, stated
the presentation would be deferred to a later time in the meeting.  Following the next
presentation on the agenda, Robin Roth, Colorado Department of Transportation, State
Coordinator for Colorado’s Drug Recognition Expert (DRE) program, awarded Chief Kozak
with the 2009 Drug Recognition Expert Centennial Award.  She briefly explained the award and
noted Chief Kozak was receiving it for his significant contribution to the DRE program during
his tenure as Avon Police Chief, Eagle County, Colorado.  Also commenting on Chief Kozak’s
contributions to the program were Lt. Jon Anderle, Laramie Police Department, State
Coordinator for the DRE program and chair of the Governor’s Leadership Team to Prevent
Impaired Driving, and Dee West Peterson, State of Wyoming Department of Transportation,
Highway Safety Office. Chief Kozak stated he was honored to receive the award and briefly
mentioned future programs he is working on for continued highway safety efforts.
Congratulations were expressed to Chief Kozak.
PRESENTATION OF CERTIFICATES TO CITY OF CHEYENNE LANDFILL AND
TRANSFER STATION BY THE WYOMING SOLID WASTE & RECYCLING
ASSOCIATION FOR OUTSTANDING MANAGEMENT AND EXEMPLARY HANDLING
OF SOLID WASTE MATERIAL. Jim Elias, Public Works Director, presented certificates of
recognition for outstanding management and for demonstrating daily exemplary handling of
solid waste materials at the City Landfill and Transfer Station to Monty Landers, Landfill
Manager, and Denis Pino, Solid Waste Director and Transfer Station Operations Manager.  Mr.
Elias stated Mr. Landers and Mr. Pino are recognized state-wide as experts in the solid waste
field and are frequently asked to provide their opinions and suggestions on operations.  Mayor
Kaysen expressed appreciation to the recipients for their service and commitment to the City of
Cheyenne.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and

“An Ordinance repealing Chapter 5.72, Public Transport, in its entirety and re-creating
Chapter 5.72, Public Transportation, of Title 5, Business Licenses and Regulations, of the
Cheyenne City Code.” Joe Elkins, owner of B.C. Cab, provided written comments (on file with
office of City Clerk) on the proposed ordinance and reviewed areas of concern, including the
lack of an option for an unique company color and lettering scheme for taxicabs, as opposed to
just a logo, and enforcement of violation of laws.  He cited examples of taxicab vehicle
violations involving non-transparency of side and rear windows due to lettering which impairs
driver visibility, and cell phone usage contrary to city ordinance.  Reporting for Finance
Committee, Mr. Brown moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Ms. Broyles.
Mr. Brown moved to amend by substitute ordinance, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Upon inquiry,
City Attorney Dan White explained the changes contained in the proposed substitute (dated
9/9/2010 by the attorney), stating changes were based upon discussion and comments made at
previous Council and Finance Committee meetings. Upon inquiry, City Clerk Carol Intlekofer
stated there are currently less than 12 public transportation companies licensed with the City,
which does not include horse drawn carriages or pedicab businesses.  Comments followed
regarding cab company’s identification of fleet vehicles with a specific logo vs. a color scheme,
or both.  Upon inquiry, Mr. White advised if a cab company uses a taximeter it must be visible
and properly calibrated but the ordinance does not require use of a taximeter; that fares for
services must be posted somewhere in the cab where it is visible, and that the ordinance does not
regulate rates that may be assessed for services.  Further comments followed regarding the
purpose of the Police Chief’s approval of a company’s logo for vehicles with Police Chief Brian
