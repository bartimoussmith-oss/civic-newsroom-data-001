if Sam's Club is fortunate enough to obtain a retail liquor license, it intends to isolate the
package sales areain a liquor box constructed to the City of Cheyenne's standards on the West
side of the store.No person under the age of twenty-one will (21) will be permitted to enter
unless accompanied by a parent or guardian.
C.Alcohol Management:
Sam's will sell package liquor toits members.Currently, Sam's Cluband Wal-Mart
locations in Cody, Riverton, Laramie and Casper all sell package liquor to the public. Sam's and
Wal-Mart maintain a "zero tolerance" policy on all age restricted products. To ensure compliance
with the policy, the sale of any age-restricted products (including alcohol) automatically triggers
a lock on the cash register until a valid birth date is entered.Furthermore, all sales associates are
trained to check identification and any associate who sells to a customer that does not appear to
be over 40 years of age without checking identification is subject to termination.Attached is a
flow diagram which demonstrates a typical transaction involving the sale of an adult beverage
and the safeguards that are in place at Wal-Mart stores to ensure a responsible sale. Sam's Club
will utilize the same process.
In addition to the safeguards in place at Walmart locations, Sam's Club provides
additional security for liquor sales.First, on membership, an individual must be at least eighteen
years old to become a member.Members must provide a date of birth with their application
which adds additional security measures on purchased adult beverages. Second, identification is
checked on age-restricted product purchases.Additionally, every member is required to swipe
their membership card for every purchase.
