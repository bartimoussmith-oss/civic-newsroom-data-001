II.AGREEMENT
NOW, THEREFORE, for Ten and no/100Dollars($10.00)and other valuable consideration,
the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows:
1.
Conveyance of Public Trail Easement.Grantor hereby grants to Grantee a non-
exclusive easement (the “Easement") for non-motorized hiking and biking uses, as is more
particularly depicted on Exhibit B attached hereto and incorporated here in by this reference, subject
to the terms, conditions and limitations set forth here in.The Easement will consistofa corridor one
hundred feet(10o') in width and shall run from point to point as identified in Exhibit B.The trail
corridor will be further identified by GIS coordinates of the point at which an access gatewill be
installed near a parking lot located on the Grantor's property and the points at which the trail
corridor will intersect the boundary between the Grantor's and Grantee's properties.It is mutually
understood and agreed that prior to construction, the alignment of the initial trail corridor may be
altered if necessary to accommodate areas that are sensitive due to archeological, endangered
species, and other findings that may affect the conservation values of the Conservation Easement.
Afterthe trailis constructed, the Easement will be fifty feet(50')in width centered onthe centerline
of the constructed trail. All ingress and egress points to the trail easement will be controlled by "V""
gates to prevent motorized vehicular access to the trail ease ment.
2.
Conservation Easement.The parties acknowledge and agree that the Ease ment is
subordinate and subject to the terms and conditions of the Conservation Ease ment.To the extent
thereexists a conflict in terms or conditions between the Conservation Ease ment and this Ease ment,
the Conservation Ease ment shall control.

represents that Grantor does not:

licensee towhom a duty or care is owed by Grant or; or

or for the death of any person caused by the use of the Easement.
