Resolution, Emergency 2026 Fair Rules
Attachments:
County Attorney Mark Voss said animal tagging for the county fair has begun. He said
the board will provide notice of intent to open the 45-day public comment period on
rules and modifications to the existing fair book before their final adoption.

11. Consideration of a resolution to suspend the retail liquor license
issued to Bullseye Operations, LLC, dba Outlaw Saloon.
26 - 059
Resolution, Outlaw Saloon, Suspend Retail Liquor
License
Attachments:
County Attorney Mark Voss explained that the corporation where the license was
being used had been foreclosed, and the separate entity holding the liquor license
was delinquent in paying sales tax. Voss said the county received notice from the WY
Liquor Division of the suspension of liquor sales to the licensee. He said there is no
dispensing area because the licensee no longer has possession of the facilities.
Voss stated the licensee violates license conditions for failure to pay sales tax and for
not having a place to dispense alcohol. He said the licensing authority has the ability
under Title 12 of the Wyoming State Statutes to suspend the license.

12. Consideration of a resolution to change the speed limit for one
segment of Rd 130, Christensen Rd, from mile post 0.0 to 2.25.
26 - 064
Resolution, Speed Limit, Rd 130, Christensen Rd, MP
0.0 to 2.25
Attachments:
John Poelma, Public Works, said this item is a speed limit change based on a study
conducted by Inberg-Miller Engineers.
Commissioner Thompson said he did not see the studies attached to the resolutions
and asked if the studies are available to the public. Poelma said the studies can be
made available to the public.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.
Randy Byers, Michael White and Mick Finn, residents, spoke against the resolution to
increase the speed limit on Rd129, Whitney Rd. Comments included the need for
safety and public input, concern about obstructed intersections, the need to see the
studies and the need for common sense.
Chairman Malm said that when speed studies are conducted, they are based on road
conditions, traffic, and crash information. He said the recommendations were
data-driven.
Hearing no PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
Commissioner Thompson referred to a WY Dept of Transportation US30 speed study
which concluded the speed limit should remain the same and cautioned against
lowering the limit. Commissioner Thompson said he would like to make the studies
publicly available, as well as to postpone the resolutions and to have Inberg-Miller
Engineers available to answer questions.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Hollingshead, to postpone this item until March 3, 2026.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:
