under the City’s cap and replace program, NextMedia currently has 8 credits and, if the ordinance
is approved, NextMedia is prepared to waive those credits.  Mr. Crank  provided information on the
number of proposed digital billboards that may be constructed, removal of other billboards, and an
ordinance provision stating that the support column of a new or relocated billboard will not be
constructed within 150 linear feet of the property line of a single family dwelling located in a zoned
district in which billboards are prohibited.  He further discussed community benefits of digital
billboards involving the ability to timely broadcast public safety messages involving law
enforcement announcements and weather alerts, advising that Police Chief Bob Fecht has agreed
to be the “entry point” person for all public safety and law enforcement messages.  Troy Hammond,
general manager, NextMedia, provided information on the reduction percentage of billboard signs
within the community with implementation of the ordinance, and commented on modern technology
enabling businesses to disseminate advertising messages in a timely manner.  Jeff Everson,
Daktronics, Inc., provided a presentation regarding digital billboards; LED display and lighting
