RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
May 9, 2011
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne,
and Mr. Spiker. Absent:  Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Collins moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded
by Mr. Brown.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on April 25, 2011.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Allen.
Comments were made regarding a voucher payable to Scott Gromer, refund request relative to lack
of a recreation center in Cheyenne; funding upgrades to the municipal pool, and proposed projects
for the next 6 penny sales tax ballot question as reported in a recent newspaper article.  Upon
th
inquiry, Rick Parish, Parks and Recreation Director, reviewed a voucher payable to Colorado
Analytical Lab for Airport Golf Course soil testing.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members
of the governing body present.  Under other City business later in the meeting, Mr. Parish provided
further explanation regarding the voucher payable to Mr. Gromer, stating the funds had been
previously received by the City from the Air National Guard for recreational use by the Gromer
family, who wish to utilize the funds toward recreational opportunities in another community.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive comments and input for a restaurant liquor
license application filed with the City Clerk's Office for 888 Mr. Hibachi, LLC d/b/a Mr. Hibachi,
1439 Stillwater Avenue, Suites 1 and 2, Cheyenne, Wyoming.  There being no comments and the
purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the hearing and referred the item to
Finance Committee.
Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive comments and input on the proposed
amendment to the Major Street Plan Official Map 2003 by updating the Official Map with newly
platted and built roads, and by adding U.S. Highway 30 and Dell Range Boulevard intersection, East
Pershing Boulevard and Christensen Road intersection, and U.S. Highway 30 and Christensen Road
intersection details. Tom Mason, Director, Metropolitan Planning Organization, explained the
purpose of the public hearing; reviewed the proposed amendments to the official map based on

Mayor Kaysen opened a public hearing to receive comments and input on the City’s intent

Business Committed Grant, for pedestrian improvements to West Lincolnway, and streetscape
improvements on West 17 Street, Thomes and Pioneer Avenues adjacent to Block 392, Original
th
City, Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Cindy Schneider, Grants Manager, clarified the primary intent of this
type of grant is to build community infrastructure involving a specific business who has committed
to relocate or expand in the community.  She noted the local business entity is Western EcoSystems
Technology, Inc., who plans to move its current local office, due to business growth, to the historic
Dinneen’s building on West Lincolnway, a downtown redevelopment project by Dinco
Development, LLC.  Ms. Schneider discussed the grant funding amount being sought ($770,625.00)
and required 10% match if the grant is approved.  She stated the 10% match would be provided by
Dinco Development (cash plus in-kind land donation) and Downtown Development Authority
(DDA) funding in the amount of $15,000.00, and that the grant funding, if approved, is just a portion
of the overall redevelopment project being accomplished through private investment.  Randy Byers,
The Design Studio, Inc., architect for Dinco Development, submitted schematic drawings of the
proposed redevelopment project (on file with City Clerk’s office) and reviewed major components
of the plan involving widening of the sidewalk, raised landscaping, buffering, streetscape
improvements and enhanced lighting on 17 Street.  Mr. Byers verified there is no request for project
th
funding assistance from the City nor surrounding landowners, and explained the in-kind land
donation based on the setback area of the Dinneen Bldg. as recognized through the Wyoming
Business Council.  Upon inquiry, Mr. Byers provided information on trees that will be planted as
part of the landscaping; possible irrigation plans after trees mature; project compatibility with DDA’s
established streetscaping design, and street lighting plans for W. 17 Street and proposed placement
th
of a statue through private funding by the Dinneen family.  Appreciation was expressed to the
Dinneen family for the downtown redevelopment project involving the area and for making project
information available to the public through the use of social media.  There being no further
comments and purpose of the hearing having been met, Mayor Kaysen closed the hearing.
