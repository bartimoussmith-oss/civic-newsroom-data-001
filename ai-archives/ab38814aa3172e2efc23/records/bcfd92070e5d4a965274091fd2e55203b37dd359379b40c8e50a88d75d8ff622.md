PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is very close to the Urban Service Boundary. Infrastructure
will need to be extended to service the property.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban Residential. Surrounding areas to the
east, west, and south are all designated Urban Residential. According to comprehensive plan,
the optimal City zones
include most of the
residential zones, including MR.

Areas designated Urban Residential are anticipated to develop with a broad range of housing
types, and in newly developing areas of the community, some commercial uses are appropriate.
Development is anticipated to be at an urban or suburban character with full access to urban
services.

The policies and future land use map of PlanCheyenne are supportive of this proposed zone
assignment.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Surrounding property in the County is zoned various levels of agricultural. The land outside of
City limits to the south and east are also separated from the subject property by major
transportation corridors. The property to the north is occupied with a church. The adjacent
properties within City limits are MR and P. The P zone is anticipated to develop with a school,
and the MR zoned areas are anticipated in in a way similar to the proposed use of the subject
property.

This proposed zone change would allow this parcel to develop in a way similar to adjacent City-
jurisdiction property. The proposed zoning is also compatible with the future land use
designations on nearby property.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property currently has a County zone, but in order to develop within the City, a City zone is
required. The proposed MR zone would allow a variety of residential uses.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)
