sales taxes, charges for services, and intergovernmental funds.  Property taxes
provide the largest source of revenue for the county at 41 percent, followed by
intergovernmental revenue at 32 percent.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.  Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
Commissioners applauded the work of the Clerk's finance office, the department
heads, elected officials and employees.  Commissioner Thompson noted that this
year, commissioners held meeting with each department head and elected official to
go through their draft budgets. He expressed heartfelt appreciation to the 460
employees of Laramie County as good stewards of taxpayer funds.
Commissioners Heath and Zwonitzer echoed Commissioner Thompson's comments.
Commissioner Zwonitzer singled out Sandra Bay, Grants, for her work in securing
funding for Laramie County projects and programs.
Chairman Malm stated the board held 17 public meetings on the budget that anyone
could have attended. He said he was extremely proud of the work done.  He said
Laramie County has a fiscally conservative budget that has set the county up for a long
and prosperous future.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Heath, that this agenda item be approved. The motion
carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Zwonitzer

Hollingshead

3.
PUBLIC HEARING on the FY26 annual budget for the Laramie County
Library.
25 - 290
FY26 Library Budget Presentation
FY26 Library Proposed Budget
Attachments:
Laramie County Library System Executive Director Antonia Gaona presented the
FY26 balanced budget for the Laramie County Library Board.  Gaona gave an
overview of the budget approved by the Library Board, accomplishments from the
previous year, and what residents can expect from the library in the coming year.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.  Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
The budget for the Laramie County Library System was received and
filed.
