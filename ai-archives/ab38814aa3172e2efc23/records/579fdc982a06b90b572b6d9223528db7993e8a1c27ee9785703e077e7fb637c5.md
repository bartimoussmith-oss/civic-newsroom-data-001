July 11, 2016

ORDINANCE - 3 READING - Amending Section 8.56.050, Motor Vehicle Mufflers, of
rd
Chapter 8.56, Noise Control, of Title 8, Health and Safety, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.
Mayor Kaysen clarified the purpose of the ordinance involves exhaust and smoke emissions and not
the level of noise generated by any type of vehicle.   Commenting on the proposed ordinance were:
Joshua Shuck, Rick Coppinger, and Andy Vehar and Jeannie Spraker, co-owners, Big Al’s Auto &
Exhaust.  Topics presented included the definition and measurement of excessive smoke; EPA
guidelines; “chipping” of engines and fuel management, and suggestion of amending Title 10 of City
Code instead of the proposed ordinance.  Upon inquiry, Chief Brian Kozak, Police Department,
advised the amendment dealing with excessive smoke is the same as state statute, and explained
generally what scenarios might be interpreted as excessive smoke.   Dr. Rinne moved to approve on
third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Johnson. Comments followed on voting the ordinance down
to allow additional time to review the topic further.  Motion failed.  Voting “no” -- all members of
the governing body.
