Cate Cundall, Associate Planner, said that Rocky Mountain Tower, on behalf of
Hayco, LLC, has submitted applications for a Class B Conditional Use Permit and a
Site Plan Permit for a 199 foot lattice tower and ground mounted telecommunications
equipment within a 60' x 60' lease area located at 2817 State highway 215, Albin,
WY. The tower is designed to support up to four carriers.
The site will be located in the NW corner of a 400 acre parcel with several pivots. The
Site Plan will be approved administratively and the Planning Commission will approve
the Conditional Use Permit.
The tower will be set back 200 feet from the property lines meeting the requirements
of the 2025 LCLUR. No markings or lighting will be required at the site, no increase in
traffic, and the tower will not emit any noise or odor. Agency reviews are being
completed with the WY Game and Fish requesting construction activities not occurring
between March 15 and June 30 since the site is located in a sharp-tailed grouse
range and habitat.
Sarah Neace, applicants agent, showed a PowerPoint presentation on the project.
Chairman Caughey called the Public Hearing to order. Shelley Neace, owner of Rocky
Mountain Tower, said that talks are ongoing with Verizon to use the tower.
There being no further comments the hearing was closed and a motion and
discussion called for.
