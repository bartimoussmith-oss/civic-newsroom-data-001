RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
November 22, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen;
COUNCIL MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Dr. Rinne,
Mr. Pierson, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr,
City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and
support by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be
considered in its normal sequence on the agenda.) Mr. Collins moved to approve Consent
Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Brown.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body.
[CA]

(per Consent Agenda) carried.

Director, provided information available pertaining to a voucher payable to Fort Towing &
Service ($295.00) involving towing service for a City transit bus.  Motion carried. Voting “yes” –
all members of the governing body.  Mayor Kaysen noted that Mr. Elias had recently submitted
an analysis for his review with regard to Fleet Maintenance and Sanitation budgets, and
explained some mechanical outsourcing of fleet vehicles is due to major repair work that can not
be done by in-house mechanics.

Consideration of a proposal for an agreement, recommended by the Art In Public Places
Committee,  to acquire artwork to be known as “Hare Raising” from Tim Cherry, Branson, MO,
for placement at the entrance area of the David R. Romero South Cheyenne Community Park.
John Goodier, Chairman, Art In Public Places Committee (AIPPC), reviewed remaining budget
funding (approximately $20,000.00) for artwork acquisition, the Committee’s purpose and
directive to seek artwork for the community pursuant to City Code, AIPPC’s efforts in reviewing
and recommending an appropriate art piece for the new park, and the artist’s offer to provide the
sculpture at a reduced cost ($20,000.00).  Upon inquiry, Mr. Goodier discussed the AIPPC’s
understanding of funding availability due to budgetary concerns; the number of sculptures that
have been placed in the community under the program; status of the Committee (active vs.
inactive) if funding is not available; sculpture proposals and selection process utilized by the
Committee; artists’ willingness to offer their sculptures at a reduced cost in order to have the
artwork publicly displayed; private donations that have been provided for some pieces, and
Committee members’ and City staff assistance, at times, in physical installation of artwork.
Upon further inquiry, he reviewed personal costs that Mr. Cherry incurred during the proposal
process regarding the sculpture for the park. Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Brown stated
there was no recommendation on the item from the Committee.  Mr. Collins moved to approve,
seconded by Ms. Case. Ms. Broyles distributed a brochure (on file with office of City Clerk) on
sculptures that have been placed throughout the City.  Discussion included the following topics:
Support for volunteer members serving on the City’s various boards and committees; timing of
the expenditure due to budgetary concerns, economic situation, reductions in staffing levels
incurred earlier in the year, and increasing costs to employees without compensation adjustment;
past and current status of budget funding for artwork; current fiscal year budget review affiliated

Announcement was made of a Public Hearing to be held December 13, 2010 at 6:00 p.m.
in City Council Chambers for a transfer of ownership and transfer of location for a retail liquor
license application filed with the City Clerk's Office for W.E. Dinneen, Inc. d/b/a [to be

Under other business, discussion was held on a proposed “write-off” listing that had been
submitted to members of the governing body by the City Treasurer for uncollectible debts due the
City totaling $85,579.00 for years 2003-2008.  Topics discussed included: Collection procedures;
recognition that many on the debt listing are active businesses in the community; suggestion of
initiating small claims court actions to seek payments due through assistance of City legal staff
and staff time that may be involved; whether City services could be restricted to those owing
money and setting a limit of indebtedness for same; certain billings issued through Board of
Public Utilities, and the dollar amount involving non-sufficient funds checks that constitutes a
felony offense.  During discussion, Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, explained past and current debt
collection procedures and communication and collection efforts by staff; that some amounts due
are unpaid accrued interest; collection agency services, and different types of debt areas, and
noted the listing was provided for review prior to a resolution being submitted to write-off the
debts.
