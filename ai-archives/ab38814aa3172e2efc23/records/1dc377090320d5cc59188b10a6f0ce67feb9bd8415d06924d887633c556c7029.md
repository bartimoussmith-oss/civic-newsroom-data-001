EXPEDITED REVIEW:
This plat is being pursued as an expedited application.  An expedited review process is a concurrent
preliminary and final plat. A project may be eligible for an expedited review when the proposal contains
no significant planning, design or engineering issues.  In other words, proposals that meet all applicable
regulations and generally meet the review criteria for preliminary and final plat may be eligible for the
concurrent preliminary and final plat process.

Due to meeting the expedited plat criteria set forth in Section 2.1.2.c(3) of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, this plat has proceeded as an expedited application.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This plat proposes platting nearly 10 acres of previously unplatted land into 2 lots. The subject property
has concurrently applied for a zone change to MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis. The specific
development proposed with the plat is a multifamily (senior housing) facility on about 2.8 acres. The
remaining acreage is reserved for unknown future development, but could become residential,
commercial, or a mix of uses under the proposed MUB zone.

PlanCheyenne is supportive of this plat. The comprehensive plan encourages infill development which
maximizes the use of existing infrastructure. The plat also contributes a potential mix of new commercial
and residential uses to an activity center.

Surrounding properties are largely already developed with commercial and residential uses. There is
limited opportunity for this plat to tie into the properties to the west and east, but this plat can provide a
north-south connection linking Lincolnway and Nationway for vehicles and pedestrians. The proposed plat
would not have an undue influence on surrounding development.

The applicant has provided an access easement to connect Lincolnway and Nationway, but the location
and width of the access easement still may need modification to provide an effective connection. The
drainage area and discharge proposed by this development is along Henderson Ditch, and further
information is required to determine if the proposed drainage mitigation measures would be effective.
Utilities need to be extended to service this development, and the applicant has indicated the intent to do
so, but final alignments and extensions need clarification.

Due to meeting the review criteria set forth in Section 2.1.2.d and 2.1.3.d, staff recommends
the approval of the Preliminary Plat.
