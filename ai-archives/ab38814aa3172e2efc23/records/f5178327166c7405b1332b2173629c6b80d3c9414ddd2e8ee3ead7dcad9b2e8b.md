Background:
The item for consideration is an Annexation. This is approved by ordinance.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral June 28, 2021
•
Public Services Committee: July 7, 2021
•
Second Reading: July 12, 2021
•
Public Services Committee: July 20, 2021
•
Third and Final Reading: July 25, 2021

This proposed City-initiated annexation is for approximately 75 acres of land southwest of the I-25 and
Missile Drive interchange. A portion of these 75 acres historically has been utilized for parking during
Cheyenne Frontier Days. This land has been designated by F. E. Warren Air Force Base as the “Enhanced
Use Lease” (EUL) area. The enhanced use Lease program is intended to allow revenue generating special
projects for the base by utilizing public/private partnerships.

This City-initiated annexation will not require an owner’s signature. To initiate an annexation, the City
must adopt a Resolution certifying that the annexation complies with Wyoming State Statute 15-1-402.
After the City adopts this Resolution, the annexation follows the typical schedule, including a public
hearing and the approval of an Ordinance which annexes the land. This resolution was adopted by the
Governing Body on December 19, 2019.

As with any annexation, City services will be extended to service the land contained in the annexation.
City water mains are already located in the vicinity. Sewer mains will need to be extended to the
property. Police, fire, and sanitation services will be coordinated with F. E. Warren Air Force Base. As the
land contained in this annexation is vacant, infrastructure improvements required related to the
annexation are minimal. Future development on the site will require on- and off-site infrastructure
improvements.

The future land use map of PlanCheyenne designates the area as Military/Federal within the Urban
Service Boundary. While the plan is silent on recommended uses or development patterns for the
Military/Federal designation, land within the Urban Service Boundary is expected to develop with urban
uses and served by urban services.
