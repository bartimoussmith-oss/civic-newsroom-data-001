SUMMARY:
Staff recommends approval of this annexation resolution of compliance.  This resolution resolves
that the minimum statutory criteria can be met with the proposed annexation.  The criteria are
specifically addressed later in this report.  This proposal is for a multifamily project expected to
be over 200 units.  This sort of land use is consistent with future land use plan and begins a
logical transition from a larger commercial center to more neighborhood scale residential and
commercial development.  The transportation network described in conjunction with this pro-
posal (see Attachment A) does propose deviations from the master transportation plan.  In
general the transportation plan addresses primarily collector and arterial streets which are the
larger streets in the community.  The plan helps to establish predicable expectations for both
landowners and for the City in terms of anticipating where improvements to the transportation
system will be necessary in order to efficiently and safely serve the community.  The transporta-
tion network will be specifically evaluated during the platting stage of the process.  A zoning
process will also be brought before the City Council in conjunction with this proposal.

DESCRIPTION OF SITE AND SURROUNDINGS:  The area being annexed is part of a
larger area referred to as Section 20 (1 square mile or 640 acres) which is loosely bounded by
Powderhouse, Storey, Dell Range and Converse. Development in this section has predominantly
occurred incrementally, working its way north from Dell Range. Over 300 acres remain unde-
veloped in this section of land.  This annexation proposes to annex just under 20 acres for
development as multifamily residential.
Immediately to the south of this annexation are the back portions of Wal-Mart and Sam’s Club.
To the east the annexation area is bounded by Converse Avenue and the back of two single
family residential units on Grandview Court. The proposed annexation is about ¼ mile east of
the existing Rue Terre and over ½ mile from Storey Blvd.
The proposed street access onto Converse is precluded from a direct connection east into Buffalo
Ridge, due to an existing stormwater detention pond on the east side of Converse. The applicant
landowner is also developing additional stormwater facilities in the County to the north and east
of this site near the existing Anderson School.

DEVELOPMENT PROCESS:  In general, future development on the proposed annexation
area will need to go through both preliminary and final plat processes as well as an appropriate
zone change. During the preliminary plat phase the transportation and drainage systems will be
evaluated, including the previously mentioned transportation network plan. The proposed annex-
ation includes an approximately four-acre detention pond that will serve areas outside of the
annexation and plat boundaries. This is unusual because the design of future drainage facilities
will also dictate the future transportation and land use patterns. This complicates the matter
further because the landowner in the un-annexed area may also pursue the development of
stormwater facilities without City review in the County, similar to those developed near Ander-
son School.
In addition to the platting and zoning process, the development of multifamily will also require a
site plan, construction design, and associated design elements consistent with City Code.
ANNEXATION ELIGIBILITY DATA/ANALYSIS:  Before any territory is eligible for an-
nexation, the governing body of any city or town, at a hearing as provided in W.S. 15-1-405,
shall find that:
1) The annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety, and welfare of the
persons residing in the area and in the city or town. Findings:  The City of Cheyenne has
adopted zoning and other City codes to assure new and existing development will provide
for the health, safety and welfare of those future residents and users of the annexed area
and the community as a whole.  By annexing, future landowners have agreed to comply
with said regulations.  In addition the City has the ability to adopt, by ordinance, rules
and regulations that provide assurances the City will continue to provide for the health,
safety and welfare of the community into the future.
2) The urban development of the area sought to be annexed constitutes a natural, geograph-
ical, economical, and social part of the annexing city or town. Findings:  The proposed
annexation area is part of a larger area that is 100% contiguous to the City.  Adjacent
roadways are under City jurisdiction and are maintained by the City.  Additionally the
parcel is served by existing greenway also maintained by the City.  This area is included
in PlanCheyenne for urban development.  The parcel is located adjacent to major com-
mercial and residential centers which include a variety of neighborhoods, schools,
commercial centers and various services including medical.  City-run transit system is
nearby as well.
3) The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city or
town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to the residents

cluding water and sewer, provision of City services and expansion of the transportation
system.  To the degree that future development will be required to develop according to
City regulations and consistent with adopted City planning documents, the annexed area
is a feasible addition to the City
4) The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with the annexing city or town, or the area
meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. Findings:  The property is approximately 55%
contiguous with existing City limits and part of a larger area that is 100% contiguous.
The parcel shares approximately 1,000 feet with the existing City boundary and is located
adjacent to an arterial roadway in the City.
