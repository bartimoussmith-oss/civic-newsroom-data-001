October 18, 2016

City Council
City of Cheyenne, WY

RE: Waterford Square 2nd Filing

After holding a round-table meeting on Wednesday, October 12, the applicant and staff decided to move
forward with the plat option which vacates a portion of Waterford Blvd. and provides a 40’ right-of-way
between the north lots and the south lots. After holding follow-up meetings with staff to address staff’s
remaining concerns, the applicant has submitted a revised map to council (dated October 17, 2016). This
map is a compromise solution which addresses the concerns regarding a private accessway and
maintenance thereof.

Staff recommends approval of the revised map dated 10/17/2016 with the following conditions:
1. Name all small north-south connector streets.
2. Include the entire right-of-way of Waterford Blvd in the plat (to the south right-of-way line).
3. Add a drainage easement over the detention pond.
4. Include Lot 1, Block 6 of Waterford Square and add the City of Cheyenne as an owner on the
plat.
5. Remove General Note 4, as it does not apply to this plat.
6. Replat the portion of Traymore Boulevard south of Waterford to align with the intersection
created by this plat.
7. Adjust the lot line between Lots 2 and 3, Block 11 to allow the proposed duplex to meet
setbacks.
8. Work with the City Parks department to provide all park facilities required by the MOU.
9. Re-align, remove, or adjust the utility easements to reflect the current plat’s dedication of
Right-of-way.
10. Adjust the technical aspects of the plat map to meet the recommendations of the City surveyor.
11. Provide a revised map to City (prior to submitting for recording) for agency review - to meet
the request of the County Clerk and to allow review of the fulfillment of other map conditions.

Regards,

Seth Lloyd
Planner

Encl:
Revised Map dated October 17, 2016

UDC-16-00539-01
Final Plat/Replat

REVIEWER COMMENTS

Black Hills Corp: Robert Mestas
Black Hills is opposed to General Note (6)(iii) which would require us to provide proof of utility easement
to the BOPU

Board of Public Utilities: Linda Gunter
1. If the 40’ Right of Way is approved by the City, then the BOPU will not require an easement.
2. If the 40’ Right of Way is not approved by the City, then appropriate BOPU easements for water and
sanitary sewer utilities that are not located or constructed within the roadway or Public Right of Way
must be provided. The easement width shall be for no less than 20 feet for one utility main located in the
center of the easement and shall be no less than 30 feet wide when two utility lines are constructed in
parallel and are centered within the easement.

Laramie County Assessor: Clarice Blanton
The Land Description does not describe all that is being re-platted; only a strip along Waterford Blvd. is
described. Thefirst portion of the Description is not shown on the map nor are the curves contained in
the Curve Table. The Description begins with ‘all of Lot 1, Block 1’, however, it appears that all of the lots
north of Waterford Blvd are ‘touched’ by this legal. The calls within the Description and on the map
should follow one another and not have to be ‘reversed’. Not all of the calls in the Description are shown
on the map.

The City of Cheyenne is the owner of Lot 1, Block 6 by Special Warranty Deed, Bk 2504 Pg 285. Their
name should appear in the Dedication also.

The distance between Lot 20, Block 6, Waterford Square and Lot 11, Block 12, Waterford Square, 2nd
Filing is not shown. If a 20’ alley is being proposed over Lot 20, it should also be called out in the Title
Block and Dedication as being re-platted.

The Match lines should be noted as the calls from one detail to another are not shown.

Change the road names of Waterford Loop, Waterford Place and Waterford Lane; too confusing with
Waterford Blvd. already established.

Laramie County Clerk: Vicki Swanson
All (1st Filing) places should be taken out. The first plat does not have 1st Filing on it. The Land
description references all of Lot 1 Block 1 Waterford Square. This is not part of the plat according to title
section on bottom right.
Plat shows Block 14 that is located in Lot 1 Block 6 that is also not included in this plat.
I would like to review again please once plat is corrected since it is so complicated.

Development: Susana Montana
I just want to express thanks for submitting a revised plat that includes a public street so the northern
buildings can be addressed for emergency response purposes, metering, and convenience for the
residents in the northern units. We still need to find a way to alert prospective buyers of each of the lots
that will not have garbage pick-up in front of their unit that they will need to bring their trash container
to the main street, quite some distance, especially through snow. Can we place a note on the lot deed to
advise buyers of this fact?

Note: No change from previous comments made on 5-1-15 and 8-12-16.
This development falls within the South Triad -- student’s home attendance boundaries are South High
School, Johnson Junior High School and Arp Elementary Schools.
Please note that this development indicates a significant number of homes and expectations are that they
will produce an influx of students into the boundaries for elementary and secondary schools. Also note
that due to high volume streets identified by the LCSD1 as a hazard condition, students living in this

school.

The current attendance for these schools is as follows:
Arp Elementary -- near or at capacity with little to no space available all grades.
Johnson Junior High - below capacity with space available in both 7th and 8th grades.
South High - below capacity with space available in all grades 9-12.

neighbors (UDC 4.3.6).
