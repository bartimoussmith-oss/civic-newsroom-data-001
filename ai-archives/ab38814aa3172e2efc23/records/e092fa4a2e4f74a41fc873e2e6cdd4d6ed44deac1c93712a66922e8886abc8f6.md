SUMMARY:
Theowners ofthis8.45-acre area at the northeast corner of Prairie Avenue and Frontier Mall Drive
have petitioned the City to annex the land into the City in order to sell the proposed Lot 1 to an
extended-stay hotel developer.The remaining proposed lot would remain undeveloped for the time
being.A concurrent preliminary plat and zone change application has been submitted as well.
Wyoming State Statutes require that prior to annexing territory to the City certain stepsmust be
followed, including the governing body conducting a public hearing and finding that the territory
is eligible for annexation.The statutes list six criteria that must be met for the territory to be eligible
for annexation. The attached resolution finds compliance with these six criteria. The attached
ordinance would approve the annexation, allowing the annexation map to be recorded and the
property brought into the City. Staff recommends adoption of the resolution and ordinance.
DESCRIPTION OF SITE AND SURROUNDINGS:
The subject property is approximately 8.45 acres of vacant land northeast of and adjacent to
Frontier Malli Drive and Prairie Avenue. On the east side of the property is the platted but unbuilt
Cutoff Road.The land raises several feet from Prairie Avenue, then slopes back down towhere
Rue Terre aligns.Frontier Mall Drive isfully constructed including the sidewalk.Prairie Avenue
is built, but the sidewalk will need to be constructed as the property develops.Rue Terre and Cutoff
Road are not constructed.Prairieis considereda minor arterial roadway.Frontier Mall Drive and
Rue Terre are considered collectors.
Looking northwest at subject property from southside of Prairie Avenue.
