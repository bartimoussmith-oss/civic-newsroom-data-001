EXHIBIT A
SCHEDULE A-2
(As Amended Pursuant to Ordinance No. ________)
Decreasing state speed limits in certain zones.
In accordance with Section 10.32.030 and when no special hazard exists, the following
maximum speeds are permitted:
(a)
Twenty-five miles per hour:  No vehicle shall be driven at a rate of speed in excess
of twenty-five miles per hour upon all locals streets, as shown on the urban System
and functional Classification Map, and not otherwise posted.
(b) (a) Twenty miles per hour:
(1)
Through parks.
(2)
Central business district, bound by Sixteenth Street on the south, Twenty-
Second Street on the north, Evans Avenue on the east, and Bent Avenue on
the west.
(3)
In school zones.  Extreme care shall be exercised by every driver in
designated school zones.
(c) (b) Ten miles per hour:  No vehicle shall be driven at a rate of speed in excess of ten
miles per hour upon all alleyways and parking areas open to and used by the general
public.
