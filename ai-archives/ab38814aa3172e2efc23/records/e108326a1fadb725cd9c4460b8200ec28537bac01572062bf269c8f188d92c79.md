Consideration of Bid #E-26-22 for furnishing a Digital Storage Hardware
Expansion for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department
______________________________________________
Bid #E-26-22 was solicited for furnishing a Digital Storage Hardware Expansion for the City of
Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department.

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Bids were received from:
1. Saras America Inc. DBA SarasITO.  Farmington, MI.
2. Lewan Technology.  Greenwood Village, CO.

The bids were reviewed by Joel Hickerson (Cheyenne Police Department), and Tyler Nelson
(Information Technology).  Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from
Lewan Technology, for the purchase of the Digital Storage Hardware Expansion.  The total cost
is $43,766.00.

Funding:

(SPOT 2021 Ballot) $26,376.00
