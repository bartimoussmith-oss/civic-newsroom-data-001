RELOCATION OF STATE LEGISLATIVE BRANCH
Negotiated Contract,  occupied A|R, Temporary Relocation

CLIENT: State of Wyoming
CONTACT REFERENCE
Wendy Madsen or Anthony Sara
Legislative Service Office
(307) 777-7881
YEAR COMPLETED: 2015
PROJECT SIZE: 45,000 sf renovation
PROJECT BUDGET: $750,000
Completed within budget.
CONTRACTOR: S&S Builders

PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The State of Wyoming commenced a complete renovation of the State Capitol Building, which required all oc-
cupants to be temporarily relocated during the multi-year project.  NGA was hired to provide design services to
relocate the entire legislative branch to the Jonah Financial Building.  The design and construction had a tight
time frame, with a defined move-out date at the Capitol Building.  A fast-track construction schedule was uti-
lized, with demolition and renovations beginning before final design drawings were complete.
The project was a complex undertaking, given site constraints and the State’s unique requirements.  Facilities
were needed for many different occupants and functions:  House and Senate chambers, galleries, lobbies, staff
spaces, attorney spaces, and lounge and break rooms; committee rooms; hearing rooms; House and Senate
leadership offices; substantial office and file spaces for the Wyoming Legislative Service Office; IT areas; media
space; and state security accommodations.  Intense coordination of IT, security, and communications was re-
quired.  In addition, NGA also assisted in utilizing existing furniture provided by the Jonah Building ownership,
which required substantial design consideration.  The project was completed within budget, and on time, so as
not to delay commencement of the Capitol renovations.
