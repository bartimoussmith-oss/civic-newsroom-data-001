RESOLUTION NO. ________
ENTITLED:
"A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE’S
APPLICATION FOR A LAND AND WATER CONSERVATION
FUND GRANT FOR THE MARTIN LUTHER KING JR. PARK
QUICKSTART TENNIS COURT PROJECT."
WHEREAS, currently there are no tennis court facilities in Martin Luther King Jr. Park; and
WHEREAS, the area residents have strongly expressed their desire to see tennis court facilities in
this park; and
WHEREAS, if all this grant funding requests are awarded,  the Parks & Recreation Department, will
proceed with this project to build QuickStart  tennis courts on the west side of Martin Luther King
Jr. Park; and
WHEREAS, this project will include ADA accessible walkways to the tennis facility, as well as
ADA accessible gate entries and related amenities; and
WHEREAS, the estimated cost of the tennis project is $129,000; and
WHEREAS, a grant in the amount of $64,500 is being requested from the Land And Water
Conservation Fund; and
WHEREAS, the Martin Luther King Jr. Park QuickStart Tennis Court Project is an eligible project
to receive a Land And Water Conservation Fund Grant and the required  match will come from “in-
kind”, the Cheyenne Parks & Recreation budget, earmarked Ward I contingency funds, a grant from
the United States Tennis Association, contributions from the Wyoming District United States Tennis
Association, and the Laramie County Recreation and Planning Advisory Board; all totaling $64,500.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING:
1. That the City of Cheyenne Governing Body supports a Land & Water Conservation Fund
(L&WCF) grant application for the Martin Luther King Jr. Park QuickStart Tennis Court Project and
that the match will be will be provide as described above.
2. That the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne, or other City official designated by the Mayor, is
authorized to sign an application for a L&WCF grant for a tennis court project in Martin Luther King
Jr. Park.  Further, that the Mayor is authorized to execute a grant agreement, in the event grant funds
are awarded to the City of Cheyenne for the Martin Luther King Jr. Park QuickStart Tennis Court
Project.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS_________ DAY OF November, 2012.

Martin Luther King Jr. Park QuickStart Tennis Court Project
The Land & Water Conservation Fund Grant guidelines require that a resolution of support from
the Governing Body be provided, along with our grant request to Wyoming Department of Parks &
Cultural Resources.   We are requesting $64,500.
The L&WCF is Federal money that come through the State and is awarded once each year on a
competitive basis, with very specific criteria.  The projects to be considered must be “outdoor
recreation”.
The grant requires a 50/50 match.  The estimated cost of the project is $129,000.  We are
proposing that our local match of $64,500 be supplied through the Parks & Recreation Department
funds and “in-kind” in the amount of $30,000, City of Cheyenne Ward One Contingency Funds of
$8,197, a grant from the USTA of $23,240, and other grant funding of $3,063; totaling $64,500.
Our project will include four QuickStart tennis courts.  These tennis courts are smaller and are
perfect for children learning to play tennis.  This approach is a new play format for kids ages ten
and under, to introduce them to the sport of tennis. These courts are also popular with seniors,
those with bad knees, obese people, and the mobility-challenged.  Many people will benefit from
this new format of play.  Four QuickStart courts fit on the same size space as one full-size adult
tennis court.  The United States Tennis Association is strongly advocating this court style.
A resolution of support for this grant request is required for consideration
