RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 February 11, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning at
6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez and Ms.

Attorney.

routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no separate

members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its normal
sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Allen moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by Dr. Rinne.
Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
