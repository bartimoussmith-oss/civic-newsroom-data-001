LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
AND UNION TELEPHONE COMPANY

1.
Parties.  This Lease is made and entered into this 1st day of December, 2022, by
and between the City of Cheyenne ("City"), a municipal corporation organized and existing
under the laws of Wyoming, whose address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming
82001, hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor," and Union Telephone Company, a Wyoming
corporation, whose address is P.O. Box 160, Mountain View, Wyoming 82939, hereinafter
referred to as "Lessee."

2.
Purpose.  The Purpose of this Lease is to allow Lessee to lease a portion of the
real estate described in Attachment A, for the purpose of maintaining a communications tower,
as described in Attachment B, to be used by Lessee to conduct telecommunications operations.
The exact location of the leased real estate for the communications tower and
telecommunications operations, along with an easement for access purposes, is described in
Attachment C. Attachments A, B and C are attached hereto, incorporated by reference and
expressly made a part of this Lease.

3.
Term.  The term of the Lease shall run from May 1, 2023, through November 30,
2027. This Lease may be renewed for up to four (4) successive five (5) year terms, contingent
upon Lessee's agreement to increase the annual rent in accordance with Payment of Rent. Lessee
shall furnish to Lessor, no later than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the lease term,
written notice of its intent to renew or terminate the Lease.

4.
Payment of Rent.  Lessee shall pay to Lessor the sum of Fifteen Thousand
Dollars ($15,000.00) on or before the first day of May, 2023, and in advance of the anniversary
date of this Lease each year thereafter.  Rent shall be adjusted each Anniversary Date of this
Lease in accordance with the change in the Consumer Price Index for All Urban Consumers
(CPI-U) reported by the United States Department of Labor Bureau of Labor Statistics using data
from the last month reported prior to the Anniversary Date, except that in no event will the rent
be decreased from the previous year’s amount.

5.
Responsibilities of the Lessor.  Lessor agrees that it will allow the Lessee use of
the premises for the limited purposes set forth in this lease.

6.
Responsibilities of the Lessee.  Lessee agrees that it shall:

a.
Use the property only for the express purpose of maintaining and
operating a communications site in strict compliance with the terms set forth in this
Agreement.  All other uses are expressly prohibited.
b.
Operate and maintain the site on the premises in a condition satisfactory to
the Lessor.
c.
Maintain the premises in a condition satisfactory to the Lessor.
