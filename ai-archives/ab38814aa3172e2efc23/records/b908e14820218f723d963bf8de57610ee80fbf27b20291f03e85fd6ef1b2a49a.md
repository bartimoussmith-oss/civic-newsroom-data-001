RESOLUTION NO. _   ___

ENTITLED: “A
RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING
SUBMISSION
OF
THE
AMERICORPS FOSTER GRANDPARENT PROGRAM APPLICATION
TO AMERICORPS ON BEHALF OF THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE TO SUBMIT CONTINUATION APPLICATION
FOR THE NEXT THREE YEARS OF FUNDING FOR THE YOUTH
ALTERNATIVES FOSTER GRANDPARENT PROGRAM NECESSARY
SUPPORT ITEMS.”

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne (“City”) requests assistance in
funding AmeriCorps Foster Grandparent Program and enrichment program and necessary
support items (“Project”);

WHEREAS, the City recognizes the need for this Project;

WHEREAS, the City desires to participate in the AmeriCorps Foster Grandparent
program grant;

WHEREAS, the AmeriCorps Foster Grandparent program has certain criteria to be met,
as described by AmeriCorps governing the program, and to the best of the City’s knowledge, this
application meets those criteria;

WHEREAS, grant funding for this Project will be considered by AmeriCorps after
submission on March 9, 2023;

WHEREAS, the total estimated programming budget received is Three Hundred,
Twenty-Six Thousand, Sixty-Eight Dollars ($326,068), and the City requests a grant of the
amount or Three Hundred, Twenty-Six Thousand, Sixty-Eight Dollars ($326,068); and

WHEREAS, the City will not need to have a match for the requested grant.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

1.
That the City supports the AmeriCorps Senior Foster Grandparent Grant
continuation application for funding AmeriCorps Foster Grandparent Program mentoring and
enrichment program and necessary support.

2.
That the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is authorized to sign an application for a
AmeriCorps Seniors Foster Grandparent Program Grant in the amount of Three Hundred,
Twenty-six Thousand, Sixty-Eight Dollars ($326,068) to be submitted to the AmeriCorps
Seniors for renewal for 3 years beginning July 1, 2023 – June 20, 2026, for assistance in funding
the Project.

3.
That the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne, is hereby designated as the authorized
