RE:

2014 Amendment to the City of Cheyenne & Board of Public Utilities
Construction Specifications and Standard Drawings

DATE:
February 19, 2014

After several years of work, the City Engineer’s Office in conjunction with the Board of Public
Utilities proposes to amend the City of Cheyenne & Board of Public Utilities Construction
Specifications and Standard Drawings – 2007.  This document sets construction requirements
for construction occurring within the City rights-of-way as well as for the construction of City-
owned utilities.  A summary of the proposed changes by specification number is attached.

The proposed amendments have been the subject of much discussion.  The City Engineer’s
Office and the BOPU held the final of a series of meetings in December of 2013 with local
contractors, engineering firms, and utility companies to discuss this amendment.  Changes were
made to address the feedback received at those meetings.  Subsequently, a formal 45 day public
comment period was held and advertised.  No formal public comments were received.

Several issues remain that were identified during the meetings that could not be addressed
without further delaying this amendment.  In general, the unaddressed issues fall into one or
more of the following categories: (1) issues where there is not wide consensus in the construction
community about how to handle; (2) issues that require extensive amounts of research and testing
before a specification can be written; or (3) issues that require work be done beyond these
specifications, such as the writing and passage of ordinance changes.  It is the intent of the City
Engineer’s Office to continue to work on these issues for possible inclusion in future
specification amendments and ordinances.

Presuming this Specification amendment is approved, the City Engineer’s Office is proposing
that the effective date will be Monday, March 31, 2014.  All projects approved on or after that
date will be required to conform to this amendment whereas all projects approved prior to that
date will continue to be constructed under the current specifications.
