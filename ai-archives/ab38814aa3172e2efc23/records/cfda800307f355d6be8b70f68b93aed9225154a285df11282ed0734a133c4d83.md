EXPEDITED REVIEW:
This plat is being pursued as an expedited application.  An expedited review process is a concurrent
preliminary and final plat. A project may be eligible for an expedited review when the proposal contains
no significant planning, design or engineering issues.  In other words, proposals that meet all applicable
regulations and generally meet the review criteria for preliminary and final plat may be eligible for the
concurrent preliminary and final plat process.

Due to meeting the expedited plat criteria set forth in Section 2.1.2.c(3) of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, Staff recommends allowing the plat to proceed as an expedited application.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This plat proposes replatting one large single-family home lot into 3 smaller lots intended for single-family
home development. The property is currently occupied by a single-family home on the eastern portion of
the land. The proposed platting action would allow the existing home and all its associated utility
connections to remain on one of the 3 lots.

PlanCheyenne is supportive of residential development at this location. While the Comprehensive Plan
encourages infill development and a mix of housing types, it is not the intent of the Comprehensive Plan
to change existing stable neighborhoods. The proposed plat is able to meet the intent and applicability of
the current MR zoning, and all lots are buildable lots in this zone district.

The proposed Lots 2 and 3 are significantly smaller than existing surrounding lots in terms of square
footage. However, both of these lots have frontage similar to surrounding lots and would be required to
have a front setback similar to that which already exists in the neighborhood. With similar front setbacks
and lot widths, homes constructed on these parcels can be similar in form to the existing homes and
therefore, this proposed platting action would not have an undue influence on potential redevelopment in
the area.

Due to meeting the review criteria set forth in Section 2.1.2.d and 2.1.3.d of the Cheyenne
Unified Development Code, staff recommends the Planning Commission approves this
expedited plat, advancing the final plat to the Governing Body.

PRELIMINARY PLAT DATA:  In reviewing, providing comments, and making recommendations and
decisions on a proposed preliminary plat, the Staff, Planning Commission and City Council shall use the
following 8 criteria, as found in Section 2.1.2.d:

1. PLANCHEYENNE - The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in PlanCheyenne.

PlanCheyenne is supportive of this proposed plat.

PlanCheyenne has multiple policies which encourage
infill development which maximizes the use of
infrastructure investments. The property is within the
Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.

This property, and all the surrounding area, is
designated as Urban Residential by the Future Land
Use Map in PlanCheyenne. This future land use
designation is intended to develop with a variety of
residential types and densities; however, proposed
development should take into consideration adjacent
development, and the Comprehensive Plan does state
that it is not the intent of the Plan to change existing
stable neighborhoods. While the plat does propose lots
that are smaller than those in the surrounding
neighborhood, the plat is generally proposing lots of
similar widths and the applicant has indicated Lots 2 and
3 would likely develop with single-family homes,
matching the pattern already found in the neighborhood.

2. PLANNING - The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to
existing and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities.

This plat proposes creating three residential lots in the MR Medium-density Residential zone district.
All surrounding properties are previously developed and are currently zoned MR. This platting action
would not have an undue influence on the potential redevelopment of surrounding properties. The
lots proposed with this platting action are significantly smaller in square footage than the surrounding
parcels; however, the proposed lots are similar in street frontage to surrounding parcels. The impact
of two new single-family homes would likely be minor, as the new development would have similar
front and side setbacks to the existing homes in the neighborhood.

As Hilltop Avenue is designated as a collector road, access to Lots 2 and 3 would be limited. Lot 2
would be required to access off of the alley, and Lot 3 would be required to access off of Bluff Place.
Exact access location would be addressed at the time of building permit.

3. SUBDIVISION STANDARDS - Division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in

Transportation Network:
The plat proposes dividing land within an established street network – no additions or
subtractions from the transportation network are suggested. This plat is part of a block which has
a perimeter of about 5,300 linear feet. The standard for maximum perimeter for newly
developing residential areas is 2,200 linear feet. However, there is no opportunity for this plat to
create a meaningful break in the existing block, and Staff’s judgement is that the plat qualifies for
the infill exception from maximum block sizes (UDC 4.3.3.c.6).

The proposed lot sizes are within the range allowed in
the MR zone. The new lot 1 has the frontage and area
to be considered the DD5 lot type. The new lots 2 and 3
have the frontage and area to be considered the DD7 or
SD1 lot type. The MR zone in which this property is
located “is intended for a mix of density and dwelling
types within a neighborhood [and] applicable to areas
where more compact neighborhood development
patterns are desired.” (ref UDC 5.3.2) The district’s
regulations are designed to encourage these uses, while
also protecting nearby areas. The proposed plat meets
these objectives.

7. SURROUNDING DEVELOPMENT - The application will not create any undue influence on existing or
potential development for adjacent property considering the existing zoning and Comprehensive Plan
policies for that property.

The typical single-family lots east of Hilltop (near the proposed plat) are about 75’ wide and
12,000sqft. The typical single-family lots west of Hilltop (near the proposed plat) are about 70’ wide
and 9,000sqft. Lot 1 is proposed at about 83’ wide and 12,000sqft. Lot 2 is proposed at about 83’
and 6,000sqft. Lot 3 is proposed at about 69’ wide and 6,000sqft. The size of Lots 2 and 3 are
significantly smaller than the typical lots in the area, but the frontage of all three lots is similar to the
surrounding neighborhood. Front setbacks would remain similar throughout the neighborhood and
the lack of depth to the proposed parcels should not unduly influence surrounding properties.

8. PUBLIC HEALTH, SAFETY, AND WELFARE – Whether the application will harm the public health, safety or
general welfare.

Staff is satisfied the proposed plat would not adversely affect the public health, safety, or general
welfare.
