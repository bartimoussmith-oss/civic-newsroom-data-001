RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION REQUIRING THE STUDY AND SUBMITTAL OF A
REPORT TO THE GOVERNING BODY REGARDING VEHICLES
VIOLATING POSTED SPEED LIMITS ON THE CITY'S ROADWAYS
AND VEHICLES DISREGARDING TRAFFIC SIGNALS AND RUNNING
RED LIGHTS."

WHEREAS, according to the 2020 Census from the U.S. Census Bureau, the population
of the city of Cheyenne is 65,132, making it the most populated city in Wyoming;

WHEREAS, being the most populated city, Cheyenne has an increased amount of vehicle
traffic and congestion, which in turn, causes an increased amount of traffic enforcement issues;

WHEREAS, these traffic enforcement issues vary but of particular concern are issues
relating to vehicles violating posted speed limits on the city's roadways and vehicles disregarding
traffic signals and running red lights;

WHEREAS, speeding vehicles in Cheyenne can create inordinate noise and significantly
endanger the people of Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, vehicles disregarding traffic signals and running red lights also significantly
endanger the people of Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, according to the Insurance Institute for Highway Safety, there were 12,330
speeding-related fatalities in the United States in 2021, with speeding-related fatalities accounting
for twenty-nine percent (29%) of traffic deaths that year;

WHEREAS, according to the Governors Highway Safety Association, an estimated
116,000 people were injured and 928 people were killed in crashes that involved red light running
in the United States for the year 2020—half of those killed were pedestrians, bicyclists and people
in other vehicles who were hit by the red light runners;

WHEREAS, under Wyoming Statute § 15-1-103(a)(xi), cities and towns have the authority
to "[t]ake all necessary action to plan, construct or otherwise improve, modify, repair, maintain
and regulate the use of streets[.]"

WHEREAS, under Section 10.12.130 of the city code, the city traffic engineer, under the
supervision and direction of the city engineer, is responsible for the installation and maintenance
of traffic control devices, and for maintaining the plan for the operation of traffic on the streets
and highways of the city;

WHEREAS, in accordance with the authority vested by state statute and under the city
code, the Cheyenne Police Department is responsible for enforcing the traffic laws within
Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, due to the size of Cheyenne, staffing challenges and other logistical
constraints, the Cheyenne Police Department simply cannot be everywhere at all times to monitor
and apprehend those who choose to violate posted speed limits or run red lights;

WHEREAS, because of the problems and dangers presented by this illegal conduct, the
Governing Body believes it is necessary for the Office of City Engineer in conjunction with the
Cheyenne Police Department to identify, study and analyze those areas within Cheyenne where
occurrences of unlawfully speeding vehicles and vehicles running red lights are most acute and
serious;

WHEREAS, based on their analysis and relying upon each agency's individual expertise,
the Governing Body seeks that a report be submitted articulating the analysis conducted and any
recommendations the agencies may have for better addressing and enhancing the enforcement of
the city's traffic laws pertaining to speeding vehicles and vehicles running red lights, which
recommendations may include implementing additional traffic control devices or the installation
and use of automated enforcement systems such as speed and red light cameras; and

WHEREAS, if the recommendations include the installation and use of automated
enforcement systems, an identification of the most appropriate devices and most qualified vendors
and service providers are to be included in the report, along with estimates on any associated costs.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Office of City Engineer in conjunction with the Cheyenne
Police Department shall conduct the study and analysis and submit the report to the Governing
Body on or before December 31, 2023, detailing the study's results and any recommendations
consistent with the provisions of this resolution; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that upon submittal of the report, the Governing Body
shall schedule a work session during which appropriate staff from the Office of City Engineer and
the Cheyenne Police Department shall present the results of the study, the substance of the report
and associated recommendations for better addressing and enhancing the enforcement of the city's
traffic laws relative to unlawfully speeding vehicles and vehicles running red lights.
