WHEREAS, Laramie County ("the Applicant") submitted an application to the City of
Cheyenne and the Board of Public Utilities ("BOPU") for water and/or sanitary sewer services
outside of the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne for the above-referenced properties;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 3577, BOPU was instructed to present to the
Governing Body for its review any application for the provision of water and/or sanitary sewer
services to any individual or entity outside the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, BOPU has reviewed the application submitted by the Applicant, and BOPU
and the Applicant will approve and execute applicable Outside User Agreement(s) upon approval
by the Governing Body pursuant to Resolution No. 3577; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body has reviewed the applications for sanitary sewer services
submitted by the Applicant and has determined that approval is in the best interest of the City of
Cheyenne.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, THAT the application submitted by the Applicant to BOPU for
sanitary sewer services outside of the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne for the property
known as the Archer Complex and more specifically described above, is hereby approved.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that BOPU and the Applicant are authorized to approve
and execute Outside User Agreement(s).
