  READING - Pursuant to Section 2.2.1 Zoning Map Amendments,

Reporting for Public Services Committee, Dr. Rinne moved to approve on third and final reading,

ORDINANCE - 2
 READING - Amending Section 9.12.090, Skateboards and Similar
ND
Devices Prohibited, of Chapter 9.12., Offenses Against Property, of Title 9, Public Peace and
Welfare, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Dr. Rinne moved to approve on second
reading, seconded by Mr. Johnson.  Discussion included: Difference between this ordinance and
another recent ordinance on the topic that was considered but not approved; proposed ordinance
allowing skateboard riding on sidewalks, streets and public areas in the downtown area for
transportation purposes unless signage indicates it is prohibited; skateboard riding on private
property being prohibited unless written permission is received from the property owner, and
enforcement of violations.  Ms. Williams moved to amend by adding “that a skateboarder is
responsible for any injuries he or she causes to themselves and unauthorized properties and to obey
all rules of the road and use of the sidewalks”, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Jacob Freeman suggested
adding language to the amendment that would include responsibility for any injuries skateboarders
cause to themselves and others.  Discussion on the proposed amendment included the following
