24 - 064
LCPC STAFF REPORT PKG 2024 LCLUR UPDATES
2.8.24
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning Manager, introduced the changes that are proposed to the
2022 Laramie County Land Use Regulations. Although the LCLUR is going through a
complete overhaul these few changes will cleanup a few inconsistencies prior to the
adoption of the larger rewrite. The changes will eliminate the concurrent filing of a
Preliminary Development Plan application with a Subdivision Permit and Plat
application, to increase the PDP requirement for actions over nineteen (19) lots, and
to clarify when Community Facility Fees and Public Safety Fees are assessed.
Chairman Caughey opened the Public Hearing for comments on the 2024 LCLUR
