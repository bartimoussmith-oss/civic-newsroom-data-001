RESOLUTION NO. ___________
ENTITLED:
“A
RESOLUTION
DESIGNATING
THE
CHEYENNE
DOWNTOWN
DEVELOPMENT AUTHORITY (“DDA”) AS THE LOCAL MAIN STREET
ORGANIZATION FOR THE IMPLEMENTATION OF THE WYOMING MAIN
STREET PROGRAM; AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
JOIN WITH THE DDA IN THE SUBMISSION OF A WYOMING MAIN
STREET–CERTIFIED TRACK APPLICATION TO THE WYOMING BUSINESS
COUNCIL; AND DESIGNATING THE EXECUTIVE DIRECTOR OF THE DDA
AS THE PROGRAM DIRECTOR FOR THE WYOMING MAIN STREET
PROGRAM IN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE.”
WHEREAS, the Wyoming Main Street program has been created by the Wyoming Business
Council to assist communities in the development of public-private partnerships designed to
revitalize downtown “Main Street” areas; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne and the DDA wish to pursue designation as a Certified
Wyoming Main Street Program which requires that the City and the DDA meet certain criteria
established by the National Trust for Historic Preservation Main Street Center (NTMSC) and the
Wyoming Business Council as set forth in the Wyoming Main Street program guidelines attached
hereto as Exhibit A; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne and the DDA desire to submit a joint Wyoming Main
Street Certified Track Application to the Wyoming Business Council in the form attached to this
Resolution as Exhibit B.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne:
1.
Authorizes the Mayor and the City Clerk to join with the DDA in the submission of
a Wyoming Main Street Certified Track Application to the Wyoming Business Council and to
execute such other and further documents as may be necessary to complete the application;
2.
Designates the DDA as the local Main Street organization which shall be responsible
for the implementation of Wyoming Main Street goals and objectives;
3.
Designates the Executive Director of the DDA as the local Program Director for the
Wyoming Main Street Program in the City of Cheyenne;
4.
Agrees to participate in the financial support of the DDA through tax increment
financing currently being provided to the DDA pursuant to W.S. § 15-9-207(c).
5.
Endorses the goal of economic revitalization of those portions of downtown
Cheyenne which are within the DDA District through the preservation and rehabilitation of its
historic buildings and through such other and further measures as determined to be appropriate by
the Wyoming Main Street Program.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _______ day of _________________, 2012.
__________________________________________
Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
(SEAL)
Attest:
____________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
