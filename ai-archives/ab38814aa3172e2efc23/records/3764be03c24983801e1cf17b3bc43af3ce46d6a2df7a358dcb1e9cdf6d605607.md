Section 6. HEALTH. MEDICAL. DENTAL AND LIFE INSURANCE.
The City will negotiate with or seek bids from qualified health insurance providers and will
enter into a contract with a qualified carrier or carriers to provide City employees with a medical
insurance plan, which is on file in the Human Resources Department, for fiscal year 2017 2018
2018-2019. Each non-uniformed City employee eligible for health insurance coverage (including
members of the Governing Body) shall contribute clcvcn ten percent (14 10%) of the monthly
health insurance premium attributable to the level of coverage selected by that employee. This
payment will be a payroll deduction.
The City will maintain dental insurance on City employees pursuant to the dental plan on
file in the Human Resources Department.
The City will pay premiums for employees.
If an
employee wishes to maintain dental insurance under this plan for eligible dependents as defined
in Appendix B of the dental plan, he or she may do so by paying the premium for the additional
person(s) insured.
The City will pay premiums for term life insurance on all employees in accordance with
the term life insurance plan on file in the Human Resources Department and City Clerk’s Office.
Section 7. TERM.
The terms and conditions of employment set forth in this ordinance shall apply for fiscal
year 2017 2018 2018-2019 and shall be approved as part of the budget process. This ordinance
will terminate at the beginning of fiscal year 2018 2019 2019-2020.
SectionS. REPEAL OF OTHER ORDNANCES.
All other ordinances establishing specific terms and conditions of employment are
repealed.
Section 9. SEVERABILITY.
If any clause, sentence, paragraph or part of this ordinance or application to any person or
circumstance shall for any reason be adjudged by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid
such judgment shall not affect, impair or invalidate the remainder of this agreement.
