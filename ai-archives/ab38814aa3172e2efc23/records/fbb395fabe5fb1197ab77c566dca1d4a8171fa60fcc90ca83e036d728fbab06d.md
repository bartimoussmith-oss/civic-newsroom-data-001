The Cheyenne Railcar Renovation Project includes three main railroad components.
Component 1 is Union Pacific Steam Locomotive 1242, a 1890s steam locomotive which has been static
displayed in a Cheyenne park for many years. The project involves lifting, loading, and moving the
locomotive from Lions Park, Cheyenne to 15th Street in Cheyenne where it will be stripped down, asbestos
abated, rebuilt and repainted for static display on rails.
Component 2 is Southern Pacific business/pas-
senger car 7077. This car will be moved from the
Cheyenne’s Union Pacific rail yard to 15th Street
to be displayed with Locomotive 1242. This project
involves stripping the car down for exterior and
interior repainting, repairing and replacing any rotten
wood and steel, and making the roof watertight.
Component 3 is Union Pacific 1959 Caboose 812.
The project involves moving this car from Cheyenne’s
Union Pacific rail yard to 15th Street. Restoration will
include exterior paint stripping, patch welding where
required, and new interior and exterior paint.
Project Overview
Project Overview
The Cheyenne Railcar Renovation Project entails the renovation of a historic train engine,
caboose, and passenger car, as well as transportation of the three cars to a downtown
attraction site. The three cars will be the start of an expanded railroad attraction near
Cheyenne’s historic train depot along the Union Pacific railroad tracks. The engine will
remain as a historic artifact for viewing, while the other two cars may be displays or
specialty retail or dining shops.
