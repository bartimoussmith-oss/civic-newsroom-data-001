Community Facility Fees (4.2):
For fees based on acreage, the area of the plat is pro-rated to the nearest tenth of an acre.
Lands reserved as unbuildable (for right-of-way, detention, parks, remainder lots, etc), are
exempt from fees. Park fees are calculated at $700 per acre. All fees are due at the time of
building permit.
Per UDC Section 4.2.5, Table 4-2, Enhancement Fees are payable at the issuance of a building
permit for each structure. The Park Enhancement Fee for commercial is $1,100.00 per acre
(pro-rated to the nearest one-tenth of an acre).
Transportation Network (4.3):
This plat proposes dedication of a new street named Florence Way. The proposed street
would connect into the adjacent lot to the west in the future and continue the right-of-way.
When the connection happens it would create a loop similar to what is to the north. Until that
connection happens, a proposed temporary emergency turnaround is proposed at the end of
the road.
Open Space (4.4):
Open space is not required as the parcel is zoned HI Heavy Industrial.
Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this
development. Easements are provided for BOPU utilities and for other utilities.
