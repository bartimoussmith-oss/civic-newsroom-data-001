BETWEEN
CHEYENNE FRONTIER DAYS AND MILLER/COORS FOR 2022.

WHEREAS, Cheyenne Frontier Days relies heavily on sponsors/partners to help produce
their event; and

WHEREAS, Wyoming State Statute § 12-5-402 allows a licensing authority to approve
malt beverage industry representatives to furnish, give or lend money or other things of value to
malt beverage license to support annual community events that are open to the public if certain
requirements are met; and

WHEREAS, those statutory requirements are met in this instance as Cheyenne Frontier
Days is a nonprofit corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, is qualified as
a tax-exempt organization under the Internal Revenue Code and has been in continuous operation
for a period of not less than two years.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body approves a one-year Bronze
Boot Sponsor relationship between Cheyenne Frontier Days and Miller/Coors for 2022.

  NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this approval is specific
only to the event known as Cheyenne Frontier Days and would apply to the following time frame:
July 22-31, 2022.

  NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this Resolution shall be
effective upon passage and execution by the Mayor and City Clerk.
