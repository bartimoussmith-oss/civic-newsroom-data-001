City Contract # ____________

City of Cheyenne Owned Ice and Events Center
1530 W. Lincolnway
Cheyenne, WY  82001

CITY OF CHEYENNE PARKS AND RECREATION DEPARTMENT
ICE AND EVENTS CENTER USER AGREEMENT

This Agreement (“Agreement”), dated ____________, 2013 is by and between the City of
Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the state of Wyoming ("City"), and
Cheyenne Stampede Junior “A” Hockey LLC (the “Cheyenne Stampede”), a for profit organization,
having its principal office at 3303 Forest Dr, Cheyenne, WY, 82001.  This Agreement pertains to the
City of Cheyenne Owned Ice and Events Center ("Events Center"), located at 1530 W. Lincolnway,
Cheyenne, Wyoming.  The Events Center is administered by the City of Cheyenne Parks and
Recreation Department.

WHEREAS, the City owns, manages, and operates the Events Center and has the power and
authority to lease the use thereof to others for the purpose of conducting certain events and other
activities; and

WHEREAS, Cheyenne Stampede desires to use certain portions of the Events Center as
indicated on Appendix A to this Agreement and the City desires to license the use thereof to Cheyenne
Stampede, for the purpose of holding and presenting Junior “A” Hockey practices and games
(collectively referred to herein as the "Season"), all subject to the terms and conditions set forth in this
Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants and agreements contained herein, and
intending to be legally bound hereby, the parties agree as follows:

1.      Premises.  City hereby grants to Cheyenne Stampede permission to use those portions of
the Events Center set forth on Appendix A, attached hereto and incorporated herein (the
“Premises”), upon the terms and conditions contained herein.  Only Cheyenne Stampede
members, prospective members, designated representatives and invitees are permitted by
this Agreement to use the Premises.

2.      Term.  The period of Cheyenne Stampede use of the Premises shall be from August 16,
2013 through March 23,  2014 (the “Term”).   Before the commencement of the term of
this Agreement as set forth in Appendix A, the City shall make the Events Center
available to Cheyenne Stampede, upon the payment of separate and additional charges,
for (i) preparation for the Season and delivery, move-in and set-up of Cheyenne
Stampede property and equipment; and (ii) load-out immediately following the
conclusion of the Season, ending no later than the time set forth on Appendix A.  Unless
expressly stated herein, or otherwise agreed to in writing, all deliveries and items
required for presentation of the Season shall be provided at the sole cost and expense of
Cheyenne Stampede.
