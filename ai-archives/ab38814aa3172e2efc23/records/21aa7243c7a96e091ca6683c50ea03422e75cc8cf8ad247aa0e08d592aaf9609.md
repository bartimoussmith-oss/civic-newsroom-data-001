PURCHASE AGREEMENT
THIS PURCHASE AGREEMENT (“PSA”) is made effective as of November 18, 2013
by and between City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, (“Seller”) and U.S. Bank National Association
(“Buyer”).
This PSA is made with respect to the following mutually agreed upon facts:
(i)
Buyer would like to acquire from Seller the Land and all existing
improvements located upon the Land, on which the Project will be completed in accordance
with the terms and conditions described in this PSA;
(ii)
Following the acquisition of the Project by Buyer, Buyer will lease the
Project back to Seller in accordance with the terms and conditions contained in that certain
Lease with an Option to Purchase (“Lease”), of even date herewith, by and between Buyer as
Lessor and Seller as Lessee;
(iii)
Buyer has agreed to pay the Acquisition Price over time, as described in
this PSA, so long as the proceeds of the Acquisition Price are used by Seller to reimburse the
Seller for payments made for the Project in accordance with the terms and provisions of this
PSA;
(iv)
Seller has agreed to pay the Rental Payments described in the Lease in
order to repay the sums advanced by Buyer to Seller for the Acquisition Price plus such other
accrued interest, fees and costs in accordance with the terms provided in the Lease, with an
option provided at the end of the Lease Term (as defined in the Lease) for Lessee to purchase
the Project, upon the terms and conditions set forth in the Lease; and
(v)
Seller finds that it is in the best interest of Seller to enter into this PSA,
to sell the Project to Buyer upon the terms and conditions hereinafter set forth; and
(vi)
Any capitalized term used herein which is not otherwise defined herein
shall have the meaning ascribed in Article I of the Lease.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the covenants and conditions, representations
and warranties, and agreements contained herein, and other good and valuable consideration, the
receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged by all parties hereto, Seller and Buyer
hereby promise and agree as follows:
