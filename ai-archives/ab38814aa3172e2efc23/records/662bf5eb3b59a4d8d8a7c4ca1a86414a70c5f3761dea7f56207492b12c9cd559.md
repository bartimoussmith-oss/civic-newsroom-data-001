ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION ACKNOWLEDGING RECEIPT OF THE PARSLEY
BOULEVARD CORRIDOR STUDY.”
WHEREAS, Parsley Boulevard is a collector roadway on the southeast edge of Cheyenne,
Wyoming; and
WhEREAS, the Parsley Boulevard bridge over 1-80 is scheduled to be reconstructed by
the Wyoming Department of Transportation (WYDOT) in 2022; and
WhEREAS, Parsley Boulevard needs a complete street design to enhance safety for all
transportation users; and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) retained A.V.I.
p.c. on July 29, 2018 to develop the Parsley Boulevard Corridor Study (Study), and
WHEREAS, the Study was guided by a steering committee comprised of the following
agencies and organizations: WYDOT, the Cheyenne MPO, the City of Cheyenne Public Works,
City of Cheyenne Planning and Engineering Departments, Laramie County Public Works, and the
City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities; and
WHEREAS, the Study provides design criteria and recommendations for vehicular travel
lanes, bicycle travel lanes and pedestrian facility improvements; and
WHEREAS, the Study provides a conceptual alignment for Parsley Boulevard from West
College Drive south to the Wallick Road section line; and
WHEREAS, public involvement for the Study consisted of two public open houses on
October 24, 201$ and May 7, 2019, and on-line surveys using SurveyMonkey© which gathered
feedback on issues and recommendations; and
WHEREAS, the Cheyenne MPO Technical and Citizens’ Advisory Committees reviewed
the Study and recommended adoption by the MPO Policy Committee; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission held a public hearing on June
17, 2019, accepted public comments and recommended that the City of Cheyenne Governing Body
approve the Study.
