This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and between
the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”),
and GSG Architecture, Inc., located at 1042 S. David St., Casper, WY 82601 (“Consultant”).
This Agreement shall become effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto.

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by
and between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the Municipal Building
Spatial Analysis Project, in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal
(RFP) #2-24, incorporated herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted
proposal, incorporated herein by reference.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by August 20, 2024.

3.
COMPENSATION
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the
Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed amount

Exhibit A – Price Proposal.

in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.
