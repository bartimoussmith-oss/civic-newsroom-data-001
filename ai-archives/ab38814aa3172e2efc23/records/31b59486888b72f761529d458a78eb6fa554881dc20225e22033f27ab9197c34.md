This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from NB Neighborhood Business to CB

UDC-23-189
January 2, 2024
The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

There is CB zoning to all but the south of this property. The surrounding area consists of a mix of
different restaurant, retail, and residential uses. The CB zone would be compatible with the
nearby properties as it is the commercial zone of all neighboring non-residential properties.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The surrounding area better matches the expected development patterns of CB zoning. The NB
zone requires buildings to be set closer to the streets and have a certain amount of windows
and other building design elements, whereas the CB zone pushes buildings further from the
street and requires fewer building design elements. The CB zone is also meant for more vehicle-
oriented areas which aptly describes development along Dell Range. The NB zone is intended to
integrate with residential neighborhoods and has much fewer allowed commercial uses than the
CB zone. Due to surrounding CB zoning, there
 need to integrate with a residential
neighborhood and limit uses at this location.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

This is in an area with an existing street network, and no changes to the network are
anticipated. The CB zone generally anticipates larger blocks and the existing block meets block
size standards.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with this review criterion.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The CB District is intended for Retail, Employment, Service and Civic uses that may serve a
number of residential neighborhoods or other districts in meeting daily needs, discretionary
needs, or other business functions. The district regulations are designed to encourage these
uses, while also protecting the abutting and nearby areas.

The CB District is applicable in areas where moderate- or large-scale business uses are
appropriate, specifically larger Mixed-use Commercial Activity Centers, Community/Regional
Activity Centers, or Community Business areas of the Comprehensive Plan.
