MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
STATE CONSTRUCTION DEPARTMENT
AND
CITY OF CHEYENNE

1.
Parties. The parties to this Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) are the State
Construction Department (SCD), whose address is: 700 W. 21st Street, Cheyenne, WY
82002, and the City of Cheyenne, whose address is:2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, WY
82001.

2.
Purpose. The purpose of this MOU is to designate the City of Cheyenne, Municipal
Building as the primary emergency evacuation assembly area for the SCD.

3.
Term of MOU. This MOU shall commence upon the day and date last signed and executed
by the duly authorized representatives of the parties to this MOU and shall remain in full
force and effect until terminated.  This MOU may be terminated, without cause, by either
party upon thirty (30) days written notice, which notice shall be delivered by hand or by
certified mail.

4.
Payment. No payment shall be made to either party by the other party as a result of this
MOU.

5.
Responsibilities of SCD.

A.
During an emergency at the SCD Building that requires evacuation, a member of
the SCD leadership team will notify the City of Cheyenne as soon as practicable
that an evacuation is in progress at the SCD Building, and that employees will be
proceeding to the Municipal Building for accountability.
a. Primary City of Cheyenne phone number: 307-637-6263
b. Secondary City of Cheyenne phone number: 307-638-4394

B.
SCD employees will meet at the Civic Commons Park during nice weather, and
will only shelter inside the Municipal Building during inclement weather or
hazardous conditions.  Employees will be permitted to use the restrooms as
necessary.

C.
Upon notification of the City of Cheyenne or designee that the Municipal Building
is not a suitable assembly area for all SCD employees, SCD employees will be
instructed to relocate to their alternative assembly area (Civic Commons Park).
City of Cheyenne employees will have precedence in using the Municipal Building
during emergencies.

D.
All SCD employees will comply with the code of conduct of the Municipal
Building and with instructions from City of Cheyenne staff, including instructions
to relocate, so as to minimize any disruption to the Municipal Building.
