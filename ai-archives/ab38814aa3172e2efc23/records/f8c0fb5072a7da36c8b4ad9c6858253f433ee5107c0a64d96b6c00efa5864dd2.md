Revised Maps for the Sweetgrass Annexation (UDC-17-00542)
City Councilmembers and Madam Mayor,
The City of Cheyenne Planning and Development Department has received a revised annexation map for
the Sweetgrass Annexation, which is Item #7 on the Public Services Committee meeting agenda for
Tuesday, November 7th, 2017. In the staff report accompanying the application, staff recommended the
following conditions to the Governing Body:
1.
Correct or remove the ‘Wote to Assessor” section per comments made by the County
Assessor and Planning and Development staff
2.
Correct the boundaiy measurements, per comments by the City Surveyor.
3.
Correct the legal descriotion to show a full metes and bounds descriotion, per comments by
the City Surveyor.
4.
Provide the boundary data for the W1/2 ofSection 14 and the NEJ/4NE1/4 ofSection 15.
5.
Add “a poition of Section 9” to the title block to match the entitlement of the associated
ordinance.
This new map fulfills all five conditions that were recommended by staff. Therefore, staff has changed
our recommendation to approval without conditions.
Due to the submittal of a revised map, staff is also submitting an updated annexation ordinance to the
Governing Body in order for the title block of the revised map to match the annexation ordinance. The
revised map and updated ordinance have been provided to Kris Jones, Executive Assistant to the City
Council, for distribution and are also attached to this memorandum. If you have any questions regarding
this memorandum or the application described above, please contact me by either of the methods below.
Regards,
