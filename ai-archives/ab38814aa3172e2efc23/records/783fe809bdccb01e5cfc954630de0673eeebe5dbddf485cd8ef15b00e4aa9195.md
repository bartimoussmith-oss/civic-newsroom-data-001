CONSENT TO LIEN AND EFFECT OF
RAILROAD TRACK CONSTRUCTION AND FINANCING

This CONSENT TO LIEN AND EFFECT OF RAILROAD TRACK CONSTRUCTION AND FI-
NANCING (this “Assignment”) is made as of the _____ day of _______________, 2014, by the
SWAN RANCH LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“Swan”), and CLEAR CREEK
LAND COMPANY LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company (“Clear Creek”; collectively, the
“Developers”).
A. Pursuant to a Rail Road Right of Way Easement made as of even date herewith and rec-
orded on May _____, 2014, in Book _____ at Page _____ among the Real Estate Records
of the County Clerk for Laramie County, Wyoming (the “Rail Easement Agreement”),
the Developers granted to City of Cheyenne, a Wyoming municipal corporation (the
“City”), a 10-year easement (the “Rail Easement”) for a rail road line and corridor within
the lands described therein.
B. The City has agreed to loan to Granite Peak Development Limited Partnership, a Wyo-
ming limited partnership formerly known as Granite Peak Development, LLC, a Wyo-
ming limited liability company (“Granite”), the maximum principal sum of $3,000,000
(the “Granite Loan”).
C. The City, Cheyenne-Laramie County Corporation for Economic Development (Cheyenne
Leads), a Wyoming non-profit corporation and statutorily-authorized community devel-
opment organization, and Granite have entered into an Amended and Restated Project
Development and Administration Agreement dated May 2, 2014 (the “Development
Agreement”), to construct railways, spurs, and switching and related facilities to serve the
Swan Ranch Rail Park (the “Park”) in Cheyenne, Wyoming (the “Project”).
D. As provided in the Development Agreement and an Assignment of Ownership of Rail,
Ties, and all Related Rail Equipment dated May _____, 2014, made by Granite in favor
of the City, Granite will construct and complete the Project.
E. During construction and afterward during the pendency of the Granite Loan, the City will
own the Project improvements installed.
F. The City has entered into a Track Lease Agreement for Rail Facilities Within Swan
Ranch Rail Park, Cheyenne, Wyoming (the “Track Lease”), with CLH, LLC, a Wyoming
limited liability company (“CLH”), and Granite, under which CLH will lease the City's
rail facilities within the Park to serve various business enterprises now or to be located
within the Park.
G. The Developers have executed a Mortgage (the “Mortgage”) dated May ____, 2014, in
favor of the City to secure, in part, the Granite Loan, which Mortgage was recorded on
May _____, 2014, in Book _____ at Page _____ among the Real Estate Records of the
County Clerk for Laramie County, Wyoming.
H. The City has applied for, and been approved by the Wyoming Business Council (the
“Council”), a body corporate organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming and op-
erating as a state instrumentality operated solely for the public benefit, for, a Wyoming
Business Ready Community-Business Committed Loan in the maximum principal sum of
Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000) (the “Loan”).

Consent to Lien and Effect of Railroad Track Construction and Financing, Page 2
I. To secure the City’s obligations under the Loan, it has made the following instruments
(the “City Collateral”) in favor of the Council:
1) a Collateral Assignment of Note and Mortgage, wherein the City has assigned all of
its interest in and to the Granite Note and the Mortgage to the Council;
2) a Collateral Assignment of Railroad Easement and Track Lease, wherein the City has
assigned all of its interest in and to the Rail Easement Agreement and the Track Lease
Agreement to the Council; and
3) a Business Ready Community – Business Committed Loan and Security Agreement
(the “Loan Agreement”), wherein the City has granted in favor of the Council a secu-
rity interest in and to the Project improvements; and
4) Financing Statements to be recorded among the Real Estate Records of the County
Clerk for Laramie County, Wyoming, and among the UCC Records of the Wyoming
Secretary of State.

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and adequacy of
which is hereby acknowledged, the Developers hereby consent to the lien, operation and effect of
the Project and of all of the documents described above.  The Developers shall have no liability
under such documents, other than their obligations, if any, under the Rail Easement Agreement
and the Mortgage.  In particular this Consent is made to evidence their consent to the Project and
(i) to the "clouding" of their land title caused by the Project and the Granite and the City’s fi-
nancing arrangements; (ii) to the effects on their abstract or title arising from the same, including
the liens created under the document described above; and (iii) to parties other than the City hav-
ing rights and interests in the lands described in the Mortgage and/or the Rail Easement Agree-
ment.
IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Developers have caused this Consent to be executed by
their duly authorized agents as of the effective dates of the Mortgage and the Rail Easement
Agreement, repsectively.
SWAN RANCH LLC
CLEAR CREEK LAND COMPANY LLC

By:

Susan Samuelson,

Managing Member

By:

Susan Samuelson,

Managing Member

Approved as to Form Only:

Assistant Attorney General
