MEMO
TO: Mayor Orr and City Council Members
FROM: Ron Downey, City Treasurer
RE: Budget Amendments
There were three amendments to the budget on Monday evening.The amendments consisted of the
following:
Amendment1:
Removal of the proposed3%pay for performance increase from allfunds.This decreased the General
Fund budget by$525,293(notthe$771,818asstated in the amendment)and reclassified expenditures
in the other funds to either Professional Services or Fleet Parts and Fuel.
Removal of the proposed$6,534,851from the General Fund"Supplemental Requests.”This was offset
by reducing one-time revenue from the sale of the land at Swan Ranch and reducing the revenue from
reserves line items to zero.
