Affordable Housing Task Force Recommendation:
The fourth Affordable Housing Task Force recommendation from 2022 was that the City create a fee
waiver for building permits for projects producing affordable housing. The recommendation was part of
the Affordable Housing Task Force Work Session and Staff was directed to generally work on all items
presented. The recommendation is attached.

Background:
The current City code has a section which gives building permits in the downtown area an exception to
building permit fees. This code section includes a sunset date and sets up the program as more of a re-
imbursement than a waiver. As opposed to creating a new section of code, Staff proposes fulfilling the
Affordable Housing Task Force’s recommendation by amending the current section.

The proposed code amendment has the following features:
•
The inclusion of a fee exception for building permits for the creation of income restricted
housing (per the recommendation of the Affordable Housing Task Force)
•
The inclusion of a fee exception for building permits for the maintenance of historic housing
(requested as part of the change to this section of code)
•
Defining the limit of fees eligible to be reimbursed for the new exception types
•
Increasing the limit of fees eligible to be reimbursed for all exception types
•
Extending the sunset of the program generally
The monetary limits were set based on an approximation based on the original downtown program.

Although the Ordinance is proposing modifying the text of the existing section of code, a redline
ordinance was deemed less clear than cleanly replacing the section. The redline is also attached for
reference.

The item for consideration is an amendment to City Code. This is approved by Ordinance. The Governing
Body review schedule is anticipated as follows:

Honorable Mayor Collins and Esteemed Cheyenne City Council
2101 O’Neil Ave.
Cheyenne, WY  82001
Recommendation Number Four, Fee Waiver Program
Dear Mayor Collins and Members of City Council,
We the members of the Affordable Housing Task Force recommend the Mayor’s Office and City
Council/City Staff immediately start creating a Fee Waiver/Reimbursement Program for
developers/builders to incentivize the building of affordable housing. The program would waive the
impact and/or building permit fee for affordable housing developments that are income restricted to
those who earn at or below 80% of AMI.  The waiver/reimbursement program will also be available for
those seeking to build shelters or other projects that benefit people experiencing homelessness.
We anticipate this program being very similar to the fee waiver/reimbursement program that is
currently in place for downtown development. The Fee Waiver application would be due at the time of
submission of the development to the planning commission for their review and verification that the
development will be income restricted.

Respectfully,

Brenda L. Birkle, Chair
