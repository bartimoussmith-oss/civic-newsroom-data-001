Board of County Commissioners
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
December 16, 2025
13. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a vacation of Vada Rae Ct and
renaming it as Canyon Ridge in Lone Tree Ridge Estates, 2nd Filing,
Laramie County, WY.
25 - 583
G & G Enterprises, Board Approval Vacation,
Renaming Vada Rae Ct to Canyon Ridge
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of G & G
Enterprises, submitted a road-naming application to rename Vada Rae Ct to Canyon
Ridge. He said the road is within the recorded Lone Tree Ridge Estates, 2nd Filing,
and the name change is requested by David Smith and G & G Enterprises,
landowners.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval with the
adoption of findings of fact a with no conditions.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Hollingshead, seconded by
Commissioner Zwonitzer, to grant the road naming from Vada Rae Ct to
Canyon Ridge and adopt the findings of fact a of the staff report. The
motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:
The meeting adjourned at 3:56 pm.
