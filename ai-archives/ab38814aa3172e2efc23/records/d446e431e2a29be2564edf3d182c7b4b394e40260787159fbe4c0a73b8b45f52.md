CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT FOR NON-UNIFORMED EMPLOYEES OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE.”  Eric Hardgrave, Cheyenne Public Employees Association (CPEA) President,
discussed  job loyalty by City employees, cost savings ideas that had been submitted to the Mayor
for consideration, opposition to reduction in workforce, and concern with proposed employee loss
of benefits, particularly longevity pay.  The following individuals expressed concerns regarding the
proposed ordinance amendments:  Nathan Maine, Parks Division; Mark Pollard, Golf Division; John
Palmer, Sanitation Dept.; Kelly Kenworthy, 1% Construction Engineering; Dave Sports, 1%
Construction Engineering; Steve Pennock, Sanitation; Donn Edmunds, citizen; Ron Conard, Golf
Division, and Dick Mason, Building Dept.  Topics discussed included: Dividing the proposed
ordinance amendments to allow for separate discussion; researching and reviewing various areas
for cost savings, including energy savings options, solar/wind energy, energy grants, land sales, fuel
usage, and cell phone usage and plans; voluntary time off by higher paid employees; hiring freeze,
furlough days; opposition to removal of longevity pay program; review areas involving revenue;
employee survey to be generated by the 2009 Leadership class with the support of CPEA to acquire
input and suggestions from employees on cost savings or revenue generation; support for furlough
days before implementing a reduction in workforce or other options involving wages; suggestion
of bringing BOPU “into the fold”; concern with recent softball field agreement with VFW relative
to costs; restoration time frame for any benefits which may be lost; employees’ concerns with
communication issues regarding their input, suggestions and the anticipated budget shortfall;
allowing time for areas involving cost reductions/efficiencies by departments to determine
effectiveness; review of police vehicle policy;  reducing construction and materials costs; statement
that furloughs and reductions in workforce doesn’t reduce employee workloads, and suggestion of
possible reduction in pay option predicated on reassurance monies would be restored when the
financial situation improves.  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Brown stated there was no
recommendation on the item from the Committee. Mr. Collins moved to approve on second reading,
seconded by Mr. Brown.  Mr. Collins moved to amend Section 4 and Exhibit B to restore longevity
pay provisions, seconded by Ms. Broyles.  Upon inquiry, City Treasurer Barb Dorr reviewed
anticipated budget shortfall amounts for the current fiscal year and potential shortfall for next fiscal
year, stating the amount will depend on upcoming state legislative decisions.  She also provided
information on the City’s use of 5th penny sales tax dollars.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes”

Mayor); energy efficiency efforts and lack of past due diligence in researching and conserving
savings; past employee pay increases; City reserves; cell phone plan review to ascertain savings;
energy savings and wind generation options, staff overtime costs, potential early retirement offering,
and review of all budget areas in conjunction with employee ideas for cost savings.  During
discussion City Attorney Dan White confirmed there presently is no single cell phone contract plan
