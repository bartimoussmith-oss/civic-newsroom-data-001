Steil
Surveying
Services
Professional Land Surveyors & Development Specialists
November 20, 2017
TO:
Eric Hammer, City of Cheyenne Development Office
InRe: POSTPONEMENT REQUEST for SWEETGRASS ANNEXATION #UDC-17-00542
Dear Mr. Hammer,
Steil Surveying Services, on behalf of the owner, hereby requests that the above-described
Annexation (3rd Reading) be postponed from the scheduled November 27 City Council meeting
(Postpone at November 21 Public Services Committee) in order to allow for the owner to continue
work with the City in finalizing Annexation Agreement(s) for a project of this unique size and scope.
PLEASE NOTE: I have not seen the Draft Ordinance, but based on the Public Service Comittee Agenda,
it appears that the Title Description is still incorrect on the City Paperwork:
ORDINANCE - 3rd READING -
Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a portion of Section 9, all of the WV2 Section 14, all of Section
15, a portion of Section 16, and all of Section 21; Township 13 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M.,
Laramie County, Wyoming (located to the southeast of the intersection of E. College Drive and Avenue C).
I think it should read:
ORDINANCE - 3rd READING -
Annexing to the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a portion of Sections 8 and 9, all of the WY2 Section
14, a portion of the E1/2E1/2 of Section 17, and all of Sections 15, 16, and 21; Township
13 North, Range 66 West of the 6th P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming (located to the southeast
of the intersection of E. College Drive and Avenue C).
We anticipate that following this request, the Annexation request will be placed on the Public Services
Committee agenda for December 5, 2017 and, barring any further request for postponement, will be
heard on 3 & final reading by the full City Council on December 11.
Please contact me with any questions or concerns.
Thank you and best regards,
Casey L. Palina, AICP
