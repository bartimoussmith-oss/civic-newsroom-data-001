The proposed application vacating a street and combining properties to create one larger lot.
The street could connect northward across the creek, but the street was platted in 1958 and
was never built to cross the creek. It is unlikely to be deemed necessary enough to the street
network to warrant the construction needed to cross the creek. One larger lot presents more
options for infill development (whether commercial or residential).

The surrounding area is largely developed with commercial uses along Dell Range, although
there is a small stretch of residential uses to the east of the subject property.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
2.1.2.d.3)

Community Facility Fees (4.2):
Subdivisions which add land to the development are not exempt from fees, and as this
project is taking land previously dedicated of right-of-way, fees are due. Fees previously paid
may be credited, but fees were not charged when the original plat occurred in 1958, so no
credits can be applied. Estimated fees due at plat signing: $910.

Transportation Network (4.3):
This plat is occurring within an area of the community which has been previously developed.
The creek creates a natural barrier to development of the network in this area, and despite
being platted for a street connection for over 60 years, no action has been taken to actually
build a street crossing the creek at this location. It appears logical to vacate this street to
allow more versatility in development options.

Open Space (4.4):
Open space is required at 750sqft per unit or 8% of the gross development area, whichever
is less. However, due to the nature of this application, plat-level open space is not required.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lot within this
development; although some utilities may have to be modified to allow for more
consolidated development.

Public Safety Fees (4.6):
Public Safety Fees will be due with building permits of new construction on the site.
