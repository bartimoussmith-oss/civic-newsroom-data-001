UDC-13-00272 – Terre Pont Zone Change – Page 3
ZONE CHANGE ANALYSIS SUMMARY:
The area is designated Community Business on the PlanCheyenne Future Land Use Plan map and the
proposed zone change appears to comply with criteria outlined in Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne UDC;
therefore staff recommends approval of the zone change.
DESCRIPTION OF SITE AND SURROUNDINGS:
The subject property is approximately 8.45 acres of vacant land northeast of and adjacent to Frontier
Mall Drive and Prairie Avenue. On the east side of the property is the platted but unbuilt Cutoff Road.
The land raises several feet from Prairie Avenue, then slopes back down to where Rue Terre aligns.
Frontier Mall Drive is fully constructed including the sidewalk. Prairie Avenue is built, but the sidewalk
will need to be constructed as the property develops. Rue Terre and Cutoff Road are not constructed.

Looking northwest at subject property from south side of Prairie Avenue.
PROJECT DATA:
The property is currently zoned A-1, Agriculture & Rural Residential, and MU, Mixed Use, in the County.
The proposed City zoning is CB, Community Business. Uses permitted by right in the CB zone include
hotels and motels, medical clinics, office and retail uses, automotive service stations, and restaurants,
among others.
A concurrent preliminary plat application proposes to subdivide the property into two lots. The west lot is
proposed for development of an extended-stay hotel. No development on Lot 2 is proposed at this time.

Annexation is in process as well. Platting and zoning will not take effect prior to the annexation. Site plans

ZONE CHANGE ANALYSIS:
Zoning map amendments are reviewed in accordance with the criteria listed in 2.2.1.d of the UDC:
1 || Policies & Physical Patterns of PlanCheyenne - The application is in accordance with the
development policies and physical patterns identified in the Comprehensive Plan.
Generally the proposal appears to be consistent with PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban
Service Boundary. The Future Land Use Plan map in PlanCheyenne indicates Community Business for
this property, which is primarily appropriate for general retail and office uses. Secondary uses may
include multifamily housing (apartments). Hotels and motels are a permitted use in the CB zone district.
2 || Neighboring Property - The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically
considering existing zoning, the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created
under the guidance of that plan for these areas.
The properties to the south and west are already similarly developed. Across Frontier Mall Drive is a
Buffalo Wild Wings restaurant and a retail center that includes Natural Grocers. To the south across
Prairie Avenue is Petco and other big box retailers. The land to the north is unincorporated vacant land
that will likely be developed as commercial or multifamily residential in the future after it is annexed.
The proposed development should have a positive effect on adjacent vacant land by extending water and
sewer lines and street improvements.
3 || Development Suitability - The suitability of the subject property for development according to the
standards and uses of the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district.
If the property is annexed into the City it will need City zoning prior to development. The subject
property and proposed hotel are appropriate for the proposed CB zoning, which allows a variety of uses
that are appropriate for the area.
4 || Subdivision Standards - The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be
developed according to the Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning.
The zone change application was submitted concurrently with the plat and the proposal appears to be
able to be developed according to the applicable standards in Article 4.
5 || Intent and Applicability - The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability
statements of the proposed zoning district, and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards.
According to Section 5.4.2.a of the UDC: “The CB District is intended for Retail, Employment, Service and
Civic uses that may serve a number of residential neighborhoods or other districts in meeting daily needs,
discretionary needs, or other business functions. The district regulations are designed to encourage these uses,
while also protecting the abutting and nearby areas.” The development will need to conform to Chapter 6.9,
General Commercial Design Standards, which will be reviewed on the subsequent site plans, and is intended
to encourage quality site and building design and construction.
6 || Future Development - The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable
standards for development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options in the
event that any specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval.
