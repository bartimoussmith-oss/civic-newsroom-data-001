TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

June 9, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #S-19-21 for a Contract between the City of Cheyenne and
Ameri-Tech HVAC Services, Inc., for the HVAC and Mechanical Systems
Maintenance project
______________________________________________
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The One (1) bid was received from:

1. Ameri-Tech HVAC Services, Inc.  Cheyenne, Wyoming.

The bid was reviewed by Steve Gaer (Facilities) and Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Ameri-Tech HVAC Services,
Inc. for $365,799.00 (Two Years of Maintenance Services, and Building Controller Upgrades at
Six (6) Facilities).

Funding:
Civic Center Fund, Fleet Maintenance Fund, General Fund, Solid Waste Fund, Ice
and Events Center Fund, Special Purpose Option Tax, 1% Sales Tax 19-22
