ORDINANCE NO. __________

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 9.12.080, ELECTRIC FENCES
PROHIBITED, OF CHAPTER 9.12, OFFENSES AGAINST PROPERTY,
OF TITLE 9, PUBLIC PEACE AND WELFARE, OF THE CODE OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”

BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 9.12.080, Electric fences prohibited, of Chapter 9.12, Offenses
Against Property, of Title 9, Public Peace and Welfare, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, is amended as set out in this ordinance.

9.12.080 Electric fences prohibited.

No person shall place or permit to be placed or allow to remain a fence containing
uninsulated electric conductors that may be exposed to human contact anywhere within the city
except as noted below.

A.
Low voltage (12 Volts) may be utilized for goat control and safety for vegetation
management in project specific locations identified and approved by the City of Cheyenne.

1.
Electric fencing adjacent to pedestrian areas (i.e., defined pedestrian paths
or trails) shall only electrified at night and not during any daylight hours.

Section 2.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage and
publication.
