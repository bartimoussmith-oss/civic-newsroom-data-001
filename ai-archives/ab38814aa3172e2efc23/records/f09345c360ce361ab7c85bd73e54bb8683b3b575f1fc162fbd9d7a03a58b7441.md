BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting connection to BOPU water and sanitary sewer services for property outside City
limits at an estimated amount of 5000 gallons per month. This property is in a County pocket, which already
has BOPU water and sanitary sewer infrastructure and multiple pre-existing outside user agreements.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned MR Medium-density Residential in the County.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

The request for an outside user agreement has no formal review criteria; however, the following
elements should be considered when reviewing outside user agreements:
-
BOPU capacity
-
Needed infrastructure improvements
-
Population served
-
Considerations on the effect on future annexation(s)
-
Fiscal cost/benefit to the City and the BOPU
-
Conformance with the Comprehensive Plan

The requested outside user agreement has minimal impact on BOPU’s system capacity. The BOPU
already has infrastructure adjacent to this site. The proposed use of the property is residential, and the
request is to allow a new home to be built. The area has many outside user agreements already and the
size, shape, and use of this parcel would not prevent or hinder future annexations of either this parcel or
surrounding land. This property is within a county pocket which the City will annex in the coming years.

Property tax would increase, but the City would not have any income from the property tax in the area.
The BOPU may charge a higher rate for outside users and the BOPU infrastructure is already in place –
so this agreement would likely be a net benefit to BOPU. City police, fire, and sanitation services are
already within this area as this is a county pocket, so if this parcel were to come into City limits by itself,
it would likely result in a net zero.

The property is within a County pocket and is anticipated to be annexed within the next few
