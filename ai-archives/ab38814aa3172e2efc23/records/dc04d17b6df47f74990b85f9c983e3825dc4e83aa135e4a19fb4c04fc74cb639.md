Section 1.That Chapter 2.32, Fire Department, Section 2.32.020, Department personnel, and
Chapter2.84, Miscellaneous Boards and Commissions, Section 2.84.050, International Fire Code
board of appeals, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the Cheyenne City Code pertaining

sections currently existing within Title 2, Administration and Personnel, not set out within this
ordinance remain unchanged.
2.32.020
Department personnel.
A.The fire department consists of the chief of the department, assistant-DIVISION chiefs,
BATTALION CHIEFS, lieutenants, inspeetors, ENGINEERS and firefighters as determined
by the mayor and citycouncil.
2.84.050
International Fire Code board of appeals.
Provisions relating to the powers and duties of the International Fire Code board of appeals
arelocated in the International Fire Code,2003 2012 Edition, Appendix A, adopted by the city in
Chapter 8.12 of the city code.A copy of the board's rules and regulations are on file in the office
of the city clerk.
Section 2.That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon approval and publication
but not sooner than November 1, 2013.
