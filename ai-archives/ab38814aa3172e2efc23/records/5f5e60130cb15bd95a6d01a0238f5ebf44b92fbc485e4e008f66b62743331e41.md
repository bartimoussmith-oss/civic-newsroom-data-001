RESOLUTION NO. ______
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO GRANT
AN EASEMENT TO THE UNITED STATES OF AMERICA, DEPARTMENT
OF ENERGY, WESTERN AREA POWER ADMINISTRATION (WESTERN
AREA POWER ADMINISTRATION), FOR THE INSTALLATION OF
BURIED FIBER OPTIC AND OTHER COMMUNICATIONS CABLES
WITHIN WESTERN AREA POWER ADMINISTRATION’S EXISTING
ELECTRIC TRANSMISSION LINE EASEMENT OVER A PARCEL OF
LAND OWNED BY THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”
WHEREAS, a Contract and Grant of Electric Transmission Line Easement exists between
the United States of America and Warren Livestock Company, recorded on September 6, 1962, in
Book 735, Page 239, of the official records of the Laramie County, Wyoming, attached hereto as
Exhibit A; and
WHEREAS, a portion of the land in the existing easement was recently purchased by the
City of Cheyenne from Swan Ranch, LLC, the legal description of which is attached hereto as
Exhibit A; and
WHEREAS, the United States of America, Department of Energy, Western Area Power
Administration, wishes to redefine the terms and conditions of the existing easement as it is
presently located on the City’s parcel of land and to allow for buried fiber optic and other
communications cables, in addition to the uses currently allowed in the existing easement, the terms
and conditions of which are set forth in the Contract and Grant of Easement, attached hereto as
Exhibit A; and
WHEREAS, for consideration of the Contract and Grant of Easement, Western Area Power
Administration agrees to pay the City the sum of Five Thousand Seven Hundred Dollars
($5,700.00).
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to enter into a Contract
and Grant of Easement with the United States of America, Department of Energy, Western Area
Power Administration, for the parcel of land owned by the City of Cheyenne and described in the
attached Exhibit A, and to execute all further and additional documents as necessary.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS _______ DAY OF ___________________,
_________.
__________________________________________
Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
(Seal)
Attest:
____________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
