SECOND AMENDMENT TO MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND SADDLE RIDGE, LLC

1.
Parties.  This Second Amendment to Memorandum of Understanding (“Second
Amendment”) is made and entered into by and between Saddle Ridge, LLC (“Saddle Ridge”),
whose address is 506 Shoshoni Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009, and the City of Cheyenne, a
municipality organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”), whose
address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.

2.
Recitals.  The purpose of this Amendment is to modify certain obligations under the
Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”), executed by and between Saddle Ridge and the City
dated July 10, 2006 and as amended August 3, 2011, so that certain additions to the Saddle Ridge
greenway system may be extended in a logical and chronological progression.  Unless otherwise
indicated, references to Blocks and Lots in this Second Amendment are intended to refer to
Blocks and Lots in Saddle Ridge Subdivision, Cheyenne, Wyoming.

WHEREAS, Saddle Ridge has requested that the MOU be amended to permit Saddle
Ridge to construct a portion of the Saddle Ridge greenway system, at its expense, in exchange
for the assumption by the City of certain other greenway and sidewalk development duties and
obligations.

WHEREAS, the portion of the Saddle Ridge greenway system to be constructed by
Saddle Ridge, pursuant to this amendment, is specifically described and identified as the
pathway for the Saddle Ridge Greenway Development Area (“SRGDA”) running through and
across Block 25, Lot 15, which portion is shaded in red on Exhibit “I” attached to this Second
Amendment and incorporated herein.

WHEREAS, under the MOU, Saddle Ridge is currently obligated to build a ten foot (10')
greenway path within the Whitney Road Greenway Development Area (“WRGDA”), which
pathway would be constructed within a utility easement along the eastern ROW of Whitney
Road running from the south ROW of U.S. Highway 30 to the northern boundary of Lot 1, Block
1, Saddle Ridge Fourth Filing, and running south from the connection of the pathways in the
WRGDA and SRGDA to the north ROW of Pershing Boulevard.  These portions of the Saddle
Ridge greenway system are highlighted in orange on Exhibit “I” attached hereto.

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties agree to amend the MOU so that the City shall be
responsible for design and construction of the WRGDA pathway from the south ROW of U.S.
Highway 30 running south to the northern border of Lot 1, Block 1, Saddle Ridge Fourth Filing,
as shown in orange highlighting on Exhibit “I”.  City shall also be responsible for the design and
construction of the sidewalk running south from the connection of the SRGDA and WRGDA to
the north ROW of Pershing Boulevard, as shown in orange highlighting on Exhibit “I”.  It is
further agreed that Saddle Ridge shall now be responsible for construction of a portion of the
SRGDA pathway running through and across Block 25, Lot 15, as shown in red highlighting on
Exhibit “I”.

“Pay for the construction cost of the 10’ greenway path from the northern
border of Lot 1, Block 1, Saddle Ridge Fourth Filing, running south to connect
with the SRGDA pathway.”

C.
Paragraph 6B shall be rewritten to include subsection “iv.” so that the MOU shall
now include and incorporate the following language:
“iv.  Shall construct a 10’ wide greenway path through and across Block 25, Lot 15 (as
identified in Exhibit “I”) with the exception of the already completed pathway.

Responsibilities of City.

D.
Paragraph 7A shall be rewritten to include subsection “v.” so that the MOU shall
now include and incorporate the following language:
“v.  Install and complete the 10’ greenway path from the south ROW of U.S. Highway 30
running south to the northern border of Lot 1, Block 1, Saddle Ridge Fourth Filing.
Design of the path through and across Block 1, Lot 1 shall be negotiated between the
developer and commercial buyer and the Parks and Recreation Department to make
reasonable accommodations for the steep topography, which may include construction of
the greenway path outside of the existing easement not to exceed what is reasonably
necessary.  The City shall make its best efforts to design and build the greenway path
within the existing easement.”

E.
Paragraph 7A shall be rewritten to include subsection “vi.” so that the MOU shall
now include and incorporate the following language:
“vi.  Pay for the construction cost of the 10’ greenway path from the south ROW of U.S.
Highway 30 running south to the northern border of Lot 1, Block 1, Saddle Ridge Fourth
Filing.

vii.  The City shall, at its expense, design and construct a sidewalk running from the
connection of the pathways of the WRGDA and SRGDA to the northern ROW of
Pershing Boulevard.”
