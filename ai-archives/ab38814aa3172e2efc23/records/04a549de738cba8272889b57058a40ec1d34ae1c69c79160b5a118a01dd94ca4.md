Background:
The item for consideration is a Development Agreement related to distribution of tax increment
financing (TIF) revenue for overages related to remediation, demolition, and cleanup of environmental
hazards at a site located at 1600 West Lincolnway and commonly known as the Hitching Post Inn.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Public Hearing
04/25/2022
•
Public Services Committee

05/03/2022
•
Second and Final Reading

05/09/2022

Summary:

The attached Development Agreement is between the City of Cheyenne, Swagger Construction, LLC, and
Banner Capital Bank for reimbursement of overage costs for environmental remediation and cleanup
associated with the redevelopment of the Hitching Post property. Reimbursement will come from the
TIF revenue generated by the property. The new approved TIF amount is not to exceed $1.66 million as
the applicant is requesting an additional $460,000 in TIF assistance. Based on staff projections, the
Hitching Post Plaza project is expected to generate a TIF amount between $2.7 and $3 million (and
potentially up to $3.7 million) over a 25-year period. The TIF was enabled by the adoption of the
Hitching Post Urban Renewal Plan and Project on August 23, 2021.

The Urban Renewal Authority (URA) approved Swagger Construction, LLC’s application for the additional
TIF on April 7, 2022. City Council approval of the Development Agreement is required due to the
Hitching Post Urban Renewal Authority special fund being held by the City. The terms of the agreement
stipulate that the applicant will be reimbursed from the URA special fund as revenue is generated from
the specific project and the applicant will be responsible for any costs beyond the amount of revenue
generated should the TIF increment underperform. The agreement is currently under review by Banner
Capital Bank and any changes on their behalf will be discussed at Public Services Committee on May 3,
2022.
