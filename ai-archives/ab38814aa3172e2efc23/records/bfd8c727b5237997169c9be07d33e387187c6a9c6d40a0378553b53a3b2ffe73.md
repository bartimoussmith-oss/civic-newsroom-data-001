12. Consideration of written approval of the Laramie County Board of
Commissioners to amend resolution 240416-12 to increase terminal
approval from 125 to 170 terminals.
26 - 042
Written Approval, 307 Horse Racing, Inc, 3310 Ridge
Rd
Resolution 240416-12, 307 Horse Racing, Inc, 3310
Ridge Rd
Attachments:
County Attorney Mark Voss said the agenda item authorizes additional terminals at
one 307 Horse Racing location.
Traci Lacock, general counsel for 307 Horse Racing, explained her client had a joint
venture with Wyoming Downs.  Wyoming Downs closed its Outlaw location as the
result of a foreclosure, and her client seeks to transfer 45 of those terminals to its
Ridge Rd location.  Written approval from the board is required to increase the
number of terminals at that location. She confirmed approval of this item does not
increase the number of gaming machines in Laramie County. Lacock added the WY
Gaming Commission has final approval of the additional terminals.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Hollingshead, that this agenda item be approved. The
motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
