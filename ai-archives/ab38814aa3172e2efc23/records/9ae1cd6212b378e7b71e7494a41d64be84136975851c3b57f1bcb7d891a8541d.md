```text
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:
Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:
1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure. Plan Cheyenne also encourages infill development.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property is currently Community Business; however, it
is anticipated that the designation for this area of the community will be changed to Mixed-use
Commercial. This is for two reasons – first, the existing development patterns and uses are more
reflective of Mixed-use Commercial – and second, it is likely any redevelopment in this area
would be of an urban, mixed-use nature.

The MUB zone is considered the optimal zone for areas designated Mixed-use Commercial.

Based on the policies and proposed future land use designation of the subject property, MUB
Mixed-use Business Emphasis zoning is appropriate at this location.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1, with conditions.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

This property, and most of the surrounding property, is currently zoned CB with the future land
use designation of Community Business. The CB zone allows a variety of commercial uses;
however, development within the CB zone is expected to be on larger parcels with more of an
automobile orientation. The MUB zone allows a mix of residential and commercial uses and is
expected to develop with more of an urban development pattern with higher densities and
buildings built to the street. Existing development in the area includes a variety of residential
uses and more urban-style commercial development.

The CB zone and MUB zones are compatible zoning districts, and the proposed MUB zone is
largely compatible with CB zoning; however, when developing (or redeveloping), a CB-zoned
parcel adjacent to a MUB-zoned parcel would be required to provide a landscape buffer.

The existing development patterns in this area of the community is more reflective of MUB-style
development and future redevelopment in the area is likely to be better facilitated by an urban-
style mixed-use zone than a suburban-style commercial zone.

Staff is processing an amendment to the Comprehensive Plan for the entire region to reflect the
existing development patterns and probable redevelopment patterns.
```

```text
UDC-21-00434
January 4, 2022
Although all adjacent properties are zoned CB, a nearby property to the north is already zoned
MUB.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current CB zone allows a variety of commercial uses, but prohibits residential uses and
requires large setbacks from streets. The proposed MUB zone allows a variety of commercial
uses, residential uses, and mixed uses. The MUB zone also allows buildings to be built to the
street. The MUB zone has a higher design standard than the CB zone; however, the subject
property should be able to meet the required the design standards.

While surrounding properties currently zoned CB, there are multiple properties that are
currently used for residential purposes and other properties which have commercial buildings
built to the street.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The existing block is the type expected for the urban-style of development anticipated for MUB
zoning. CB zoning typically anticipates larger suburban-style blocks. The existing pattern is
compatible with the proposed zoning and no further subdivision is expected.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The MUB zone is intended for a diverse mix of residential, retail, service, employment and civic
uses integrated into a walkable, compact development pattern. The MUB District is applicable to
areas where more intense development and diverse mix of uses is appropriate with a high level
of pedestrian and civic amenities.

The existing development patterns, including existing uses, are reflective of the intent and
applicability of the MUB zone. Residential and a variety of commercial uses are within a short
distance of the subject property. Although a regional transportation route is nearby, open areas
and a Greenway are also close by, providing pedestrian and civic amenities. However, the
neighborhood is lacking in sidewalks, which likely will be required with future redevelopment in
the area.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.
```

```text
6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The existing property meets the standards of the MUB zone. The MUB zone allows a building to
be brought closer to the street, while a new building on this property in the CB zone would
require 25’ setbacks from both streets. If the specific development proposed in association with
this application is not executed, the MUB zone would allow the existing structure to return to
residential use, be repurposed to mixed-use, or remain as a commercial use. The rezoning to
MUB would also allow more flexibility for new construction on the property.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

Staff noted that a change from CB to MUB zoning not supported by the current Future Land Use
Map designation; however, an amendment to the Future Land Use Map designation for the area
appears appropriate based on both current and probable future development patterns.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7, with
conditions.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
```
