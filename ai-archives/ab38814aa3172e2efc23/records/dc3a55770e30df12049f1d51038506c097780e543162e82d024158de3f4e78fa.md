SUBJECT:

Federal Project No. B251A02

Various Locations, Thermoplastic Striping, Laramie County

Please find attached the proposed Cooperative Agreement for Federal Project No. B251A02,
Various Locations, Thermoplastic Striping in Laramie County provided by WYDOT to the City
of Cheyenne for your approval and execution.  The agreement is summarized below for your
information.

Purpose of Project:
In conjunction with intersections planned to be restriped with thermoplastic markings by
WYDOT, the city determined it would be in its best interest to upgrade the markings on the
city roadways associated with the shared WYDOY/City intersections for improved
consistency and a best approach to providing improved safety to the public.  Markings
along Lincolnway, College Drive and at the intersection of Logan & Nationway were
identified to be in need of upgraded thermoplastic markings as part of this project.

Purpose of Agreement:
The primary purpose of the agreement is to set forth the terms and conditions by the City
will reimburse WYDOT for costs associated with thermoplastic restriping of crosswalks
and other street markings at various locations throughout the City.  The locations are
shown on Exhibit A, which is attached and in the agreement.  This project is associated with
WYDOT’s main project B251010.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE

Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
