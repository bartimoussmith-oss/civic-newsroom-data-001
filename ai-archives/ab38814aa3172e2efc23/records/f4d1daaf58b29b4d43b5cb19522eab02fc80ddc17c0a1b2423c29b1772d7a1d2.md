MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND THE POINTE HOMEOWNERS’ ASSOCIATION

PREAMBLE

This Memorandum of Understanding (“MOU”) is made and entered into by and between
The Pointe Homeowners Association (“PHOA”) c/o Touchstone Property Management, 2850
McClelland Dr., Suite #1000, Fort Collins, Colorado 80525, or other Property Manager designated
in writing by PHOA, and the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized and
existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”), 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne,
Wyoming 82001 (individually, “Party” or collectively, “Parties”).  This MOU is effective on the
date of the signature last affixed to the signature page.

RECITALS

WHEREAS, the PHOA desires to install, at its own expense, enhanced landscaping along
a portion of the Greater Cheyenne Greenway;

WHEREAS, the City ordinarily provides maintenance services to landscaping along the
Greater Cheyenne Greenway;

WHEREAS, the City does not intend to provide maintenance services to such enhanced
landscaping subject to this MOU, and the PHOA does intend to provide maintenance services to
such enhanced landscaping; and

WHEREAS, the PHOA and the City desire to enter into this MOU regarding the division
of maintenance responsibilities for any such enhanced landscaping the PHOA installs, or has
installed, along a portion of the Greater Cheyenne Greenway.

AGREEMENT

NOW THEREFORE, the Parties agree that:

1. PURPOSE

The purpose of this MOU is to outline maintenance responsibilities for the enhanced
landscaping along a portion of the Greater Cheyenne Greenway, shown on attached Exhibit A, if
such enhanced landscaping is provided and installed at the expense of persons or entities other than
the City.

2. TERM

This MOU shall have no force or effect until: (1) enhanced landscaping is installed at the

3. RESPONSIBILITIES OF PHOA

The PHOA shall be responsible for the maintenance of the enhanced landscaping in
accordance with the following standards and specifications.

A.
Planting Bed Care: The PHOA will check all landscaped bed areas at least once a
month during and between April and October for mulch coverage.  PHOA will
place additional mulch or rock as needed in bed areas. All beds shall be checked for
weeds monthly, and if such beds have weeds, the PHOA shall remove such weeds
by mechanical cultivation, hand weeding, and chemical control, as necessary, and
in compliance with environmental laws and regulations, industry standards, and
manufacturers’ recommendations. The PHOA shall make reasonable efforts to
control grasses and weeds growing directly in shrubs and groundcover.

B.
Tree and Shrub Care: The PHOA will check the health of all plant material at least
once a month, and shall use best practices as to fertilization, irrigation, insect
control, and disease control.  The PHOA shall be responsible for pruning all
shrubs and the lower fifteen (15) feet of all trees in accordance with regularly
accepted industrial standards.  All plants shall be pruned as needed in accordance
with regularly accepted industry standards.  All shrubs and trees are to be pruned
upon completion of annual growth seasons to maintain proper shape and uniformity
of appearance.  To prevent mower and trimmer damage to trees and shrubs planted
in turf areas, a cultivated, grass-free area or guard shall be provided and maintained
by the PHOA.

C.
Pond and Fountain Care: The PHOA will provide required maintenance to all
detention and/or retention ponds within the PHOA-responsible portion of Exhibit
A, according to relevant industry standards and best practices.  The PHOA shall be
responsible for installing and maintaining any water features, e.g., water
fountain(s), that it may choose to install as an element of the enhanced landscaping
which is the subject of this MOU.  The PHOA shall be responsible for any and all
costs and expenses related to maintenance and operation of any such fountain.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE CITY

The City shall provide all maintenance of the Greenway path within such area as depicted
on Exhibit A, and as determined necessary in the City’s sole discretion, including snow removal in
accordance with the City’s normal standards, practices, and procedures.  The City shall remove
snow from the Greenway path when such snowfall exceeds approximately one inch within any
given twenty-four (24) hour period, but shall only remove such snow on portions of the path
identified as the City’s responsibility on Exhibit A.

This MOU does not require the City to maintain the shared use path through the area
depicted on Exhibit A as being subject to the PHOA’s responsibility.  Until such time as the
Parties have executed an agreement reflecting the allocation of maintenance responsibilities for
such shared use path segment, the City shall not be responsible for maintaining the shared use path
segment through the area depicted on Exhibit A as being subject to the PHOA’s responsibility.

5. MODIFICATIONS

This MOU may not be modified, revised, or amended, except pursuant to written
instrument, duly executed by the Parties hereto.

6. DEFAULT

Each and every term and condition herein shall be deemed a material element of this MOU.
In the event either Party shall fail or refuse to perform according to the terms of this MOU, such
Party may be declared in default.

7. REMEDIES

In the event a Party declares the other Party in default hereof, said Party declaring default
shall notify the defaulting Party in writing and such defaulting Party shall be allowed a period of
fifteen (15) days to cure said default.  In the event that the default remains uncorrected, the Party
not in default may elect to: (a) terminate the MOU; or (b) treat the MOU as continuing and require
specific performance.  If the City remains in default after notice, the PHOA may treat the MOU as
continuing and seek specific performance, but the PHOA shall not be entitled to seek damages or
any other form of monetary relief.  In addition, if the PHOA remains in default, after notice, the
City may elect to cure the PHOA’s default and seek reimbursement of its costs and expenses from
the PHOA.

8. GOVERNING LAW, JURISDICTION, AND VENUE

The construction, interpretation and enforcement of this MOU is governed by the laws of
the State of Wyoming.  The courts of the State of Wyoming shall have jurisdiction over any action
arising out of this MOU and over the Parties.  The venue shall be the First Judicial District,
Laramie County, Wyoming.

9. GOVERNMENTAL IMMUNITY

Except with respect to an action for specific performance of this MOU as between the
Parties, the City does not waive its governmental immunity by entering into this MOU and the City
fully retains all immunities and defenses provided by law with respect to any monetary claims,
demands, causes of action, injuries or damages, whatsoever, arising in any way from this MOU.
The City fully retains all immunities and defenses with respect to claims for attorney’s fees.

10. ASSIGNMENTS

This MOU shall be binding upon and will inure to the benefit of the Parties hereto and their
respective heirs, successors, transferees, and permitted assignees. Neither Party shall assign this
MOU without the express written consent of the other Party.

11. FORCE MAJEURE

Neither party shall be liable to perform under the MOU if such failure arises out of causes
beyond control and without the fault or negligence of said party.  Such causes may include, but
are not limited to, acts of God or the public enemy, fires, floods, epidemics, quarantine restrictions,
freight embargoes, and unusually severe weather.
