MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

May 3, 2023
SUBJECT:
Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Power Equipment
Company for the repair of a Paving Machine for the Street and Alley Department
______________________________________________
This is a Sole Source purchase through Power Equipment Company, therefore no bids were
requested.
Power Equipment Company is the Sole Source authorized dealer for sales and service for Cedar
Rapids paving machines, therefore repairs must be made directly through them.
Repairs to the paving machine include replacing strike offs, screed plates, flange bearing, hour
meter, tachometer, and full machine check over to ensure unit is prepared for paving season.
The current repair estimate is $44,579.18.  The City recommends approval of a 15% Contingency
($6,686.88) in the event unexpected items need to be addressed during the course of repairs.  Total
requested amount for repairs, is a not-to-exceed amount of $51,266.06.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-43102 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Equipment Over $5,000)
