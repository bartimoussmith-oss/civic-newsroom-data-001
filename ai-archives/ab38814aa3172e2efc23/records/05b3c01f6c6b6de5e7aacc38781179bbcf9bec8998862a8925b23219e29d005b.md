RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE EPA CLIMATE POLLUTION REDUCTION
PLANNING GRANT

The City of Cheyenne has been awarded a U.S. Environmental Protection Agency Climate Pollution Reduction
Grant for planning in the amount of $1,000,000. Grant funds will support staffing and consultant services to
develop a Priority Climate Action Plan (PCAP), a Greenhouse Gas Inventory, and a Comprehensive Climate
Action Plan to support emission reductions, renewable energy initiatives, and other energy efficiencies for
Cheyenne consumers. The CPRG Planning Grant and published PCAP allows the City of Cheyenne to pursue
an EPA CPRG Implementation Grant for eligible projects, such as community solar.
This is formula grant through the Inflation Reduction Act of 2022 and the performance period of the grant
award is 01/01/2024 to 06/30/2027.
Please let me know if you have specific questions about the CPRG program and this grant opportunity.

WHEREAS, the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)'s Climate Pollution Reduction
Grants (CPRG) program provides a total of five billion dollars ($5,000,000,000) in grant funding
to states, local governments, tribes, and territories to develop and implement ambitious plans for
reducing greenhouse gas emissions and other harmful air pollution;

WHEREAS, Phase 1 of the CPRG program provides flexible support to states, local
governments, tribes, and territories regardless of the status of their climate planning and
implementation process;

WHEREAS, CPRG planning grant recipients can utilize funding to design climate action
plans that incorporate a variety of measures to reduce Priority Climate Action Plan (PCAP) and
Greenhouse (GHG) emissions from across six key sectors including electricity generation,
industry, transportation, buildings, agriculture/natural and working lands, and waste
management;

WHEREAS, as a condition of the one-million-dollar ($1,000,000) Phase 1 CPRG
program planning grant, the City of Cheyenne must submit a PCAP to the EPA by March 1,
2024;

WHEREAS, within two (2) years after receiving a Phase 1 CPRG planning grant, the
City of Cheyenne must submit a Comprehensive Climate Action Plan (CCAP) and within four
(4) years after receipt, the City must submit a Status Report;

WHEREAS, upon submitting a CPRG planning grant to develop its PCAP, the City of
Cheyenne will be eligible to compete for a CPRG implementation grant in an amount between
two million dollars ($2,000,000) and five hundred million dollars ($500,000,000) per individual
grant;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne has identified investigating the
feasibility of incorporating renewable energy systems, including a community solar project, as a
City Council goal; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is interested in better understanding its GHG
emissions and wishes to support efforts to stimulate innovative technologies and practices that
create cost saving and more sustainable efficiencies for Cheyenne residents to meet growing
utility needs.

Attachment 1 - Project Description
The agreement provides funding under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) to the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming to develop a comprehensive, economy-wide climate mitigation plan or update an existing plan
in collaboration with air pollution control districts, large and small municipalities statewide, and tribal
governments that will support actions to reduce greenhouse gases (GHG) and harmful air pollutants and
to conduct meaningful engagement with low-income and disadvantaged communities. The City of
Cheyenne is leading the development and implementation of innovative strategies to reduce GHG
emissions. The Cheyenne Metropolitan Statistical Area (MSA) has experienced an increase in flooding
and has been affected by forest fires, which are both attributed to shifting weather patterns. In 2020, over
176,000 acres were burned in the Mullen Fire, threatening the city’s watershed, releasing large quantities
of GHG, and causing significant harm to numerous ecosystems. To address these challenges, the City of
Cheyenne will lead the efforts to address the MSA’s most pressing environmental difficulties, while also
fostering positive economic development through innovative solutions and workforce development
opportunities. Power generation, transportation, manufacturing, industrial, and renewables are the
primary sectors to be addressed in the mitigation plans.In general, activities include the development,
updating, and evaluation of plans to reduce climate pollution (i.e., to reduce GHG emissions and/or
enhance carbon sinks). Specifically, the City of Cheyenne will create a comprehensive GHG emission
inventory to develop GHG reduction measures, identify strategies to implement energy efficiency, and
conduct outreach programs to enhance public awareness and participation.Three key deliverables will be
produced and submitted over the course of the four-year program period, including: a Priority Climate
Action Plan (PCAP), due March 1, 2024; a Comprehensive Climate Action Plan (CCAP), due two years
from the date of the award; and a Status Report, due at the close of the grant period.
Expected outcomes include a PCAP and CCAP that identifies: tons of pollution (GHGs and co-pollutants)
reduced over the lifetime of the measures; tons of pollution (GHGs and co-pollutants) reduced annually;
and tons of pollution (GHGs and co-pollutants) reduced with respect to low-income and disadvantaged
communities. Other expected outcomes include: improved ambient air quality and public health;
increased public awareness; improved staff capacity; development of a City of Cheyenne Standing
Committee on Sustainability; implementation of energy efficiency and other improvements to reduce
operating costs; and positive economic development impacts.
The intended beneficiaries include all residents and visitors of the Cheyenne MSA, with a specific focus
on low income and disadvantaged communities to promote environmental equity. The City of Cheyenne
will proactively seek public engagement with the low income and Hispanic populations to strengthen
relationships that invite dialogue and spark innovation. The City of Cheyenne will take active measures to
engage these communities to increase involvement and create a comprehensive benefits analysis.City of
Cheyenne anticipates $15,000 in subawards, $5,000 per year for years 1-3. Subawards will support and
enhance project goals and deliverables and facilitate public outreach efforts. Cheyenne anticipates 1-2
subawards per year.
