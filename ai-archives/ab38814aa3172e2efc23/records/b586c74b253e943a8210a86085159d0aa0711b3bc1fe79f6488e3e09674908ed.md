RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR AND CITY CLERK TO
EXECUTE A STATE HISTORIC PRESERVATION OFFICE CERTIFIED
LOCAL GOVERNMENT GRANT AGREEMENT FOR ATTENDANCE
AT THE 2014 SAVING PLACES CONFERENCE.”
WHEREAS, according to Section 2.64.020 of the Cheyenne City Code, the governing
body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes “that the protection, enhancement, and perpetuation of
areas, improvements, and landscape features having historic character or special historical or
aesthetic interest or value is a public necessity”; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne is a Certified Local Government (CLG), assuming the
duties and responsibilities thereof; and
WHEREAS, a requirement of CLG status is to provide regular training opportunities for
an appointed Historic Preservation Board; and
WHEREAS, the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) has awarded the City of
Cheyenne a grant in the amount of $1,542.00 to assist in paying for the cost of attendance at the
2014 Saving Places Conference.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, Wyoming, that the City of Cheyenne is hereby authorized to accept the
said grant award in the amount of $1,542.00 from the SHPO to be used solely for the 2014
Saving Places Conference.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to
execute all documents relating to the grant upon approval of this resolution.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED ON THIS ________ DAY OF
_____________, 2014.

__________________________________

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
SEAL:
ATTEST:

__________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
