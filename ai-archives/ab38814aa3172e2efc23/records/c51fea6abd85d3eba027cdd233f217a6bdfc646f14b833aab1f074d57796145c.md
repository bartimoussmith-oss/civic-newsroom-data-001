An Ordinance amending the City of Cheyenne’s Sanitation Fee Schedule.  Jim Elias, Public
Works Director, advised City ordinance requires an annual review of the fee schedule with rate
increases based upon the Southeast Wyoming Consumer Price Index (CPI), which is currently 4.1%,
and that the proposed fee increases are in the amount of 4.1% for commercial and residential solid
waste collection and disposal services.  Mr. Elias reported that a 1% increase was approved for
Fiscal Year 2011 (CPI was 3.4%) and no increase was approved for Fiscal Year 2012 (CPI was
3.2%).  He noted increased costs in City operations involving expanded areas of service due to

Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Mercer Group, Inc., for a Personnel
Organizational, Classification and Compensation Study and Analysis.  Rich Wiederspahn, Human
Resources Director, reviewed the Request for Proposal (RFP) process that was utilized resulting in
a recommendation from the City’s Employment Strategy Group (ESG) committee to enter into the
agreement with the Mercer Group.  He provided information on criteria used to evaluate and rank
proposals received; scope of services that will be provided under the agreement, and benefits to the
City involving an independent study and analysis.  Phillip Robertson, representative of the Mercer
Group, reviewed components that will be included in the study and analysis with regard to the areas
of compensation and classification, stating the classification portion will include review of positions
through use of employee questionnaires and interviews, overall point score based on 10 factors,  and
qualifications and skills needed to fulfill job descriptions, with the goal of providing information on
equal pay for equal work scenarios.  He noted that the compensation element will include a regional
salary survey and comparison with other similar market pay plans.  He advised that if the study and
analysis results reflect that certain salary areas should be adjusted, implementation of any
adjustments could be structured to occur over whatever time frame the City decides.  Upon inquiry,
Mr. Robertson stated the classification study would be conducted prior to the organizational
(staffing) study.  Commenting in support of the agreement were:  Vicki Nemecek, Assistant
Director, Public Works Department; Officer John Gay, representative for the Cheyenne Police
Protective Association; Sara Vasquez, President, Cheyenne Public Employees Association (CPEA);
Todd Feezer, Assistant Director, Parks and Recreation Department, and member of the ESG; Claus
Johnson, City employee, former CPEA president and member of the ESG, and Robin Lockman,
Deputy City Treasurer and member of the ESG.  Comments included the following topics: Benefits
of the study assisting with establishing the worth of particular jobs relative to pay scales;
information from the study helping the City with future planning as well as having a long term plan
in place for employees; having an employee master plan as a mechanism to provide administrative
guidance on staffing and compensation levels; study and analysis as a good investment when
compared to the percentage of budget attributed to employee costs (i.e. 74% of general fund budget
