ACCESS LICENSE AGREEMENT

THIS ACCESS LICENSE AGREEMENT (hereinafter “this Agreement”) is hereby
entered into by City of Cheyenne, GRANTOR, and Retail Properties C, LLC, GRANTEE.  For
$10.00 and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby
acknowledged, GRANTOR grants to GRANTEE an access license and the parties agree as
follows:
1.
The access license is for purposes of ingress and egress by GRANTEE with a width
of Thirty (30) feet along the length of the south boundary of Lot 6 and with a width of thirty (30)
feet along the length of the west boundary of Lot 6.  A copy of a map and legal description for the
access license is attached hereto as Exhibit “A.”
2.
The legal description of the parcel to be benefitted by the license is as follows:
Lot 5, Block 10, of the Fire Station Addition, an Administrative
RePlat of All of Lot 4, Block 9, Grier-Leech Addition 4th Filing,
City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming

3.
The legal description of the parcel to be burdened by the license is as follows:
Lot 6, Block 10, of the Fire Station Addition, an Administrative
RePlat of All of Lot 4, Block 9, Grier-Leech Addition 4th Filing,
City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming

4.
The parties agree that the access license is subject to the following terms and
conditions:
a.
The land within the access license shall be paved by GRANTOR.
By Stefanie Boster at 6:30 pm, Sep 08, 2022
