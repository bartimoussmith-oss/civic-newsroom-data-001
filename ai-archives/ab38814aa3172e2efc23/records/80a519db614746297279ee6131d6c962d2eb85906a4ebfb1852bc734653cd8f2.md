```text
RESOLUTION NO. ___________

FUNDING FOR THE CITY’S MOBILE D.U.I. COMMAND VEHICLE.”

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Police Department wishes to apply for grant funds for
reimbursement of expenses related to the Cheyenne Police Department’s Mobile D.U.I. Command
Vehicle.  The related expenses include overtime for operation of the Mobile D.U.I. Command
Vehicle, training expenses, travel expenses, and expenses for media campaigns related to D.U.I.
enforcement; and

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Police Department will submit an application for grant funding
through the Wyoming Department of Transportation, Highway Safety Program, 5300 Bishop
Blvd., Cheyenne, Wyoming, no later than March 31, 2022; and

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Police Department will apply for the grant using DUNS #
075758169; and

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Police Department will be required to provide an in-kind match
to be eligible for grant funding and the Cheyenne Police Department agrees to provide the in-kind
match by providing a secure location for the storage of the Mobile D.U.I. Command Vehicle,
regularly scheduled maintenance of the vehicle, the vehicle generator, and vehicle equipment.
The estimated value of this in-kind match is Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($2,500.00);
and

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Police Department acknowledges that it will assume certain
long-term obligations with respect to the Mobile D.U.I. Command Vehicle pursuant to grant
requirements imposed by the Wyoming Department of Transportation, Highway Safety Program.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Cheyenne Police Department is hereby authorized to
apply for grant funding under the Wyoming Department of Transportation, Highway Safety
Program, for Fiscal Year 2023 (October 1, 2022 to September 30, 2023), for reimbursement of
expenses related to the Cheyenne Police Department’s Mobile D.U.I. Command Vehicle.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to execute
any and all documents, including a grant agreement, to secure grant funding under the Wyoming
Department of Transportation, Highway Safety Program, for the Cheyenne Police Department’s
Mobile D.U.I. Command Vehicle to the extent that such documents are consistent with this
Resolution.

By Stefanie Boster at 10:18 am, Mar 23, 2022
```
