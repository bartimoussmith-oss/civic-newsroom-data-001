ORDINANCE NO.____________

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE ANNEXING TO THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING, A PORTION OF LOT 6, ALL OF LOTS 7 AND 8, AND THE
WEST 12 FEET OF LOT 9, BLOCK 2, PLUS THAT ADJACENT 8 FEET
OF VACATED ALLEY, COULSON ADDITION, LARAMIE COUNTY,
WYOMING (LOCATED NORTH OF AND ADJACENT TO RAWLINS
STREET, WEST OF MCCANN AVENUE).”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.
That the City of Cheyenne hereby finds as follows:

(a)
That Leonard Golding, owner of the parcel of land being situated in a portion of
Lot 6, all of Lots 7 and 8, and the west 12 feet of Lot 9, Block 2, plus that
adjacent 8 feet of vacated alley, Coulson Addition, Laramie County, Wyoming,
has petitioned the governing body of the City of Cheyenne to annex the real
property described herein and to make it a part thereof; and
(b)
That an accurate annexation map of said land is attached to this ordinance as
Exhibit A, said map prepared by Jeffery B. Jones, a Professional Land Surveyor,
registered in the State of Wyoming, PLS Number 5910; and
(c)
That the annexation of said area is for the protection of health, safety, and welfare
of the persons residing in the area and the City of Cheyenne; and
(d)
That the urban development of the area sought to be annexed will constitute a
natural, geographical, economical and social part of the City of Cheyenne; and
(e)
That the area is a logical and feasible addition to the City and that the extension of
basic services such as water and sewer systems, police and fire protection and
other services customarily available in the City of Cheyenne can, within reason,
be furnished; and
(f)
That the area is adjacent to the City of Cheyenne and is currently zoned MR
Medium Density Residential – by the Laramie County Commissioners; and
(g)
That the City of Cheyenne does not own or operate its own electrical utility, and
its governing body is prepared to designate a utility to serve the annexed area
pursuant to W.S. 15-1-410, and to authorize the designated utility to serve the
entire annexed area.

Section 2.
That all of the real property described in Exhibit A is hereby annexed to
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, and shall become a part thereof.

Section 3.
That the City Clerk is directed to file an executed copy of this annexation
ordinance and map (Exhibit A) with the County Clerk and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for
Laramie County, Wyoming, and shall also notify the City Engineer's Office to add said area to
the official City Map of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.

Section 4.
Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any phrase, clause, sentence, paragraph or section of this ordinance is declared
illegal or unconstitutional, such illegality or unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the
remaining phrases, clauses, sentences, paragraphs and sections.

Section 5.
That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its publication.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This annexation application was submitted for a parcel of about 20,000 SF of land on Rawlins
Street, west of McCann Avenue. This application was submitted along with a zone change
application so that the property can have a City zone district. The purpose of this annexation is
the owner would like to connect to City sanitary sewer. The owner has already hooked to City
water, which occurred in 1994.

PlanCheyenne supports this annexation. This annexation helps to address a county pocket in
Cheyenne. Further development beyond connecting to City sanitary sewer is not planned at this
time.

Due to meeting the review criteria found in Wyoming State 15-1-402, staff
recommends approval of this annexation.

Coulson Addition Annexation

PlanCheyenne:

PlanCheyenne has designated this property as Urban Residential on the future land use map and
is found within the Urban Service Boundary. Within the Urban Residential land use, a desired
characteristic according to PlanCheyenne is to connect to City water and sanitary sewer. The
purpose of this annexation is to connect to sanitary sewer. This annexation is consistent with the
goals of PlanCheyenne.

ANNEXATION ELIGIBILITY DATA/ANALYSIS: Before any territory is eligible for annexation, the
governing body must find that the following criteria are met (W.S. § 15-1-402 (a)):

COMMON WELFARE – An annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety, and welfare
of the persons residing in the area and in the city or town.

Annexing this property will allow the owners to connect to City sanitary sewer instead of using a
septic system. It will also allow them to have Cheyenne police and fire services available to their
property in the event of an emergency.

PART OF THE CITY – The urban development of the area sought to be annexed would be constituted a
natural, geographic, economic, and social part of the annexing city or town.

This property is already a part of the City naturally, geographically, economically, and socially.
This area is already developed and would be consistent with the surrounding area.

LOGICAL EXPANSION – The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the
annexing city or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to residents of
the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to be annexed.

With the property being part of a county pocket, it is logical that the property is annexed.

CONTIGUOUS – The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with or adjacent to the annexing city or
town, or the area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407.

This property has contiguity on both its northern and southern borders, for a total of about 53%
contiguous. This contiguity fulfills the requirements by Wyoming statute.

UTILITIES – If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body is
prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to service the annexed area pursuant to W.S.
15-1-4102.

A utilities franchise is in place to service this property.

CERTIFIED MAILING – The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the
public hearing required by W.S. 15-1-405(a), has sent by certified mail to all landowners and affected
public utilities within the territory a summary of the proposed annexation report as required under
subsection (c) of this section and notice of the time, date, and location of the public hearing required by
W.S. 15-1-405(a).

A certified mailing was sent on July 10, 2015.
