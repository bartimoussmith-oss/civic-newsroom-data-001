ATTACHMENT A
SERVICESTO BE PROVIDED BY YOUTH ALTERNATIVES TO THE LARAMIE COUNTY SHERIFF'S
DEPARTMENT AT THE JUVENILE SERVICE CENTER
The proposed agreement between Laramie County and the Office of Youth Alternatives for the provision of
screening, counseling and supportive services toyouth referred to the Laramie County Juvenile Services Center -
Youth Crisis Center (JSC-YCC).
The services would include:
1.
24/7/365 availability for crisis support
2.
Phone assessment
3.
Crisis meeting assessment
4.
Support servicesfor youth and families redirected from the JSC-YCC
5.
Family counseling and support for families in crisis
6.
Individual and family goal development for admitted youth
7.
Discharge planning
8.
Conduct discharge meetings
9.
Provide follow-up services for discharged youth, including referral to service providers if necessary
10.
Provide on-going family counseling or referral to appropriate service providers
To provide these services, Youth Alternatives will use its current on-call system.Coverage would be provided by a
family counselor hired to support these services and by other Youth Alternatives’ staff.
Youth Alternatives will be responsible for assuring that all services are provided by practitioners with appropriate
licensing or under licensed supervision.
When Youth Alternatives receives a call for services, the on-call staff will contact the JSC-Youth Crisis Center to
determine eligibility and bed space availability.Those situations involving physical altercations may not meet
eligibility requirements for the Youth Crisis Center, but should still be discussed, as there may be mitigating factors.
Parent or youth referrals in which there is a clear and imminent danger of physical altercation law enforcement
should be contacted; if not already present.
The procedures to be used to refer juveniles to the JSC -Youth Crisis Center and to other community services will
be jointly agreed upon by Youth Alternatives and the JSC-Youth Crisis Center.The procedures are broadly
described in“Admission Flow for Referrals to Juvenile Services Crisis Shelter” drafted by Youth Alternatives and
approved by the Laramie County Juvenile Services Center.
Both Youth Alternatives and the Laramie County Juvenile Services Center will keep the Laramie County Board of
County Commissioners informed of changes in services to be provided under the proposed agreement or any
conflict over the terms or requirements of the agreement.
