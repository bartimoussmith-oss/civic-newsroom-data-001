Dear City Councilmembers and Madam Mayor,
The City of Cheyenne Development Department has received revised final plat maps for Water-ford
Square 3rd Filing and Water-ford Square 4th Filing, which were Items #19 and #20 on the last City Council
agenda for the meeting held on 4/10/2017. The application will be considered by the Public Services
Committee at the regularly scheduled meeting on 4/18/2017.
Previously, Development Department Staff and the Planning Commission recommended the application
be conditionally approved with several conditions. Both revised final plat maps adequately address these
conditions and therefore staff recommends that amendments by substitute be made to include these
maps with their respective resolutions.
The updated final plat maps have been provided to Kris Jones, Executive Assistant to the City Council, for
distribution and are also attached to this memorandum. Seth Lloyd is the case planner for both projects.
If you have any questions regarding this memorandum, Water-ford Square 3td Filing, or Water-ford Square
4th Filing, please contact Seth or I by either phone or email. Seth is available at 307-637-6286 or
