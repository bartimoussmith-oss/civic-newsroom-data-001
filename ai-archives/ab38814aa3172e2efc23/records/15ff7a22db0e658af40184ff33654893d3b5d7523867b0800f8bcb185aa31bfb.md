DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT

THIS DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT (the "Agreement") is made this _____ day of
October, 2013, by and between the City of Cheyenne, a political subdivision of the State of
Wyoming, whose address is 2101 O'Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming, 82001 (the "City"), and
Edward George Cole, Casey Lee Cole, Christy Kay Cole, now known as Christy Marziano,
Kevin Louis Cole, Lester Lee Cole, Ann M. Cole, and their spouses, guardians, conservators,
trustees, and other representatives and assigns, (hereinafter collectively referred to as “the
Coles”), 15469 Harvest Mile Road, Brighton, Colorado 80603, and is intended to facilitate the
development of 8.45 acres of land (containing approximately 368,082 square feet), which land is
legally described in Exhibit A attached hereto.

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the Coles are the owners of  certain real property located within the municipal
boundaries of the City of Cheyenne City, more particularly described in Exhibit A, and
hereinafter referred to as the “Development Area;” and

WHEREAS, the Coles have proposed a final plat to divide the Development Area into certain
lots and blocks to be known as the “Terre Pont Subdivision” which are described and depicted in
the proposed final plat map, attached hereto as Exhibit B, and fully incorporated herein by
reference; and

WHEREAS, the City has determined that it is in the best interests of the residents of the City that
certain roadway improvements adjacent to and near the Development Area be constructed by and
at the expense of the Coles in connection with the platting and future development of the
Development Area and that the terms and conditions of this Development Agreement are entered
into for the purpose of establishing those development requirements as a condition of the
approval of the final plat for the Terre Pont Subdivision; and

WHEREAS, it is to the mutual benefit of the parties to enter into this Agreement.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, covenants and agreements
hereinafter contained and other good and valuable consideration, the sufficiency and adequacy of
which are hereby acknowledged by the parties, the parties hereto agree as follows.

1.
Development Area. This Agreement covers all of the development area (the
"Development Area") set out in Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated by this reference, all
of which is owned as of the date of this Agreement by the Coles.
2.
Intent of the Parties. It is the intent of the parties to this Agreement to impose certain
duties and responsibilities on the Coles to construct certain street and related infrastructure
improvements in connection with the present and future development of the Terre Pont
Subdivision. The duties and obligations of the Coles as specified herein are intended to constitute
a covenant which shall run with the land to all successors and assigns of the Coles and for that
purpose this Development Agreement shall be recorded, at the expense of the Coles, in the books

and records of the Laramie County Clerk and ex officio register of deeds to provide public notice

Duration of Agreement. This Agreement shall remain in full force and effect until such
time as the Coles, and their successors in interest, have complied with all of the duties and
obligations specified in this Development Agreement with regard to the construction of street
and related infrastructure improvements as provided for herein, or until such time as the parties
hereto, or their successors and assigns,  revoke, cancel or amend this Agreement through  a
written  instrument  executed  with  the same  formalities  as  this Agreement,  whichever occurs
first.  The parties hereto shall cause any amendment to this Agreement to be recorded in the
books and records of the Laramie County Clerk.
4.
Street Improvements.

(a) The Coles shall, at their expense, and in accordance with City and BOPU specifications,
construct the south half of the standard street section for Rue Terre, within its platted right-of-
way, from the west edge of Lot 1, Block 1, to the east edge of Lot 2, Block 1 of Terre Pont
Subdivision. This construction shall be completed prior to the issuance of any Certificate of
Occupancy for any improvement to be constructed on Lot 1, Block 1 of Terre Pont Subdivision.
The construction of the street section governed by this paragraph is graphically illustrated in
Exhibit C attached hereto.

(b) The Coles shall, at their expense and in accordance with City and BOPU specifications,
construct the south half of the standard street section for Rue Terre, within its platted right-of-
way, from the east edge of the Terre Pont Subdivision to reach and be connected to the existing
section of Rue Terre which has already been constructed. This construction shall be completed
prior to the issuance of any Certificate of Occupancy for any improvement to be constructed on
Lot 2, Block 1 of Terre Pont Subdivision. In the event the City pays for the construction of this
section of Rue Terre before development of Lot 2, Block 1 of Terre Pont Subdivision, the entire
costs of said construction shall be allocated, as a development cost, to Lot 2, Block 1 of Terre
Pont Subdivision and said costs shall be paid by the Coles, or their successors in interest to said
Lot and Block, prior to the issuance of any building permit for any improvement on said Lot and
Block. In the event a developer other than the City pays to construct this section of Rue Terre,
the provisions of Chapter 1.16 of the Cheyenne City Code shall apply. The construction of the
street section governed by this paragraph is graphically illustrated in Exhibit C attached hereto.
5.
Other Improvements. The terms and conditions of this Development Agreement are not
intended and shall not be construed to constitute a full and complete itemization of
improvements which may be required by the City and/or the City of Cheyenne Board of Public
Utilities ("BOPU") in connection with the development of Lot 1, Block 1, Lot 2, Block 1, Terre
Pont Subdivision, or any adjoining property owned or controlled by the Coles or their successors
and assigns. Extensions of water, sanitary sewer, storm sewer, road improvements, and other
items identified by the City and/or BOPU may be required in addition to the terms and
conditions of this Development Agreement. The Coles, and their successors and assigns, shall
comply with all standard policies, procedures, and requirements of the City and BOPU regarding
on-and off-site improvements.

6.
Cooperation. The parties agree that they will cooperate with one another in
accomplishing the terms, conditions and provisions of this Agreement and will execute such
additional documents as are reasonably necessary to effectuate the same.
7.
Amendments. Any changes, modifications, revisions or amendments to this Agreement
that are mutually agreed upon in writing by the parties hereto shall be incorporated by written
instrument, signed by all parties to this Agreement.
8.
Notices. All notices arising out of, or from, the provisions of this Agreement shall be in
writing and given to the parties at the addresses set out above by certified mail, return receipt
requested, with copy to: Edward George Cole, et al., 15469 Harvest Mile Road, Brighton,
Colorado 80603.
9.
Remedies and Enforcement. In the event the Coles or the City defaults or is deficient in
the performance of any term of this Agreement, the Coles or the City shall have the right to
exercise all remedies provided by law or in equity.
10.
Third Party Beneficiaries. It is expressly understood and agreed that enforcement of the
terms and conditions of this Agreement, and all rights of action relating to such enforcement,
shall be strictly reserved to the City and the Coles, and nothing contained in this Agreement shall
give or allow any such claim or right of action by any other third party on this Agreement. It is
the expressed intention of the City and the Coles that any person other than the City and the
Coles receiving services or benefits under this Agreement shall be deemed to be an incidental
beneficiary only.
11.
Waiver. A waiver by any party to this Agreement or the breach of any term or provision
of this Agreement shall not operate or be construed as a waiver of any subsequent breach by
either party.
12.
Successors and Assigns. As used in this Agreement, the term "the Coles” and "City" shall
include all successors, transferees, and assigns of such parties, and all such parties  shall  have
the  right to enforce this Agreement and shall be subject to the terms of this Agreement as if they
were the original parties hereto.
13.
Severability. Should any portion of this Agreement be judicially determined to be illegal
or unenforceable, the remainder of the Agreement shall continue in full force and effect as if the
illegal or unenforceable term was omitted, except that the breach of a condition precedent shall
void this Agreement through lack of formation.
14.
Governmental Immunity. The City does not waive governmental immunity or any other
defenses provided by law and specifically retains all immunities and defenses available pursuant
to W.S. § l- 39-101 et seq. and all other State laws.
15.
Kickbacks. The Coles warrant that no gratuities, kickbacks, or contingency fees were
paid in connection with this Agreement, nor were any fees, commissions, gifts, or other
considerations made contingent upon the award of this Agreement.
