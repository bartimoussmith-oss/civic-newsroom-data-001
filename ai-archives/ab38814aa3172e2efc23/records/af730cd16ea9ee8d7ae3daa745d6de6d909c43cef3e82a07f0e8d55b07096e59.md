RESOLUTION NO.

ENTITLED:"A RESOLUTION DIRECTING CITY STAFF TO RESEARCH AND
DEVELOP A PLAN TO INITIATE THE PROCESS TO PAVE THE ROAD
ON RIDGE ROAD BETWEEN HILLCREST ROAD AND STEVENS
DRIVE AND INSTALL CURB, GUTTER AND SIDEWALKS."

WHEREAS, the road on Ridge Road between Hillcrest Road and Stevens Drive is
currently unpaved and this portion of the road lacks curb, gutter and sidewalks;

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for paved
roads and the installation of curbs, gutters and sidewalks in all residential areas within its corporate
limits;

WHEREAS, this area of the road on Ridge Road between Hillcrest Road and Stevens Drive
is one of the only roads in this area that is not paved and as a result, it is in disrepair, dangerous to
the public, and is causing damage to private property;

WHEREAS, a paved road and the installation of curb, gutter and sidewalks are necessary
to properly repair the road in this area; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes that, pursuant to
Wyoming state law and the Cheyenne Municipal Code, it is the financial responsibility of the
property owners adjacent to the road to pay for the installation of curb, gutter and sidewalks.

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that City Staff is instructed to research why the road Ridge Road
between Hillcrest Road and Stevens Drive is not paved and does not have curb, gutter and
sidewalks, and to develop a plan to initiate the process for paving the road and installing curb,
gutter and sidewalks.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this _______ day of _____________________,
2024.
