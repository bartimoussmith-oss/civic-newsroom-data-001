Land Use:Variances/Board App./Plats
25. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a subdivision permit & plat for
Ironhorse, 3rd Filing, situated at: Tract 4, Ironhorse, 2nd Filing; a
portion of Tract 11, Sunset Tracts, 3rd Filing; and two unplatted
parcels located in the W1/2 of the NE1/4 of Sect 12, T14N, R67W of
the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY.
24 - 056
Ironhorse, 3rd Filing, Subdivision Permit and Plat
Attachments:
Bryce Hamilton, Planning, Palma Land Planning, LLC, on behalf of Ironhorse Capital
Limited Partnership, submitted a subdivision permit and plat application for
properties located generally in the NE1/4 of Sect 12, T14N, R67W of the 6th PM,
Laramie County, WY. He said the application submitted subdivides roughly 71 acres
into 16 residential lots of approximately 5.25 acres each.
Mr. Hamilton said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the
subdivision permit and plat with the adoption of findings of fact a and b with two
conditions: 1) The plat is revised prior to recordation to make Lot 9 compliant with
AMEC density requirements and 2) Plat note added stating: No county maintenance
of the right-of-way dedicated by the plat. The second condition was added by Mr.
Hamilton after the staff report was submitted.
Casey Palma, Palma Land Planning, LLC, and agent, spoke about the project and
how its scope has changed because the city of Cheyenne denied the outside user
agreement to provide water to the area.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Mike Cahoon, Dennis
Brown and Terry Miller, residents, spoke on this item. Their concerns were property
taxation, property line adjustment into the right-of-way, the aquifer and sustainable
water for wells, and need for open space.
Justin Arnold, Planning, explained gross and net acreage within the county's land use
regulations. He said the acreage is not related to tax purposes, only for resource
purposes, and the AMEC memo allows for additional impervious surfaces to be used
for the gross acreage calculation.
Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Malm, seconded by Commissioner
Thompson, to adopt findings of fact a and b of the staff report and
approve the subdivision permit and plat for Ironhorse, 3rd Filing, with two
conditions: 1) The plat is revised prior to recordation to make Lot 9
compliant with AMEC density requirements and 2) A note to be added to
the plat that no county maintenance of any right-of-way dedicated by the
plat will be undertaken.
