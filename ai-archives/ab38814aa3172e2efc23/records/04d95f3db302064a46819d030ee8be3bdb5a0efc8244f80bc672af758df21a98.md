Drainage Evaluation: Y2 Consultants staff are very familiar with the area’s history of flooding and has
worked with FEMA on Letters of Map Revision (LOMR) along this drainage area.
The existing Dry Creek drain beneath East Pershing Blvd. consists of five parallel 6-foot by 12-foot box
culverts. FEMA floodway mapping shows a rapid, ten-foot drop in the 100-year flood level immediately above and
below the culverts, suggesting some difference in the analysis years.
Y2’s approach to analysis of the Dry Creek crossing will combine our LiDAR expertise and city LiDAR
database with our experience with the City of Cheyenne’s Stormwater Management Model (SWMM) to evaluate
these culverts. Based on current development patterns, Y2 will model drainage for full build out to estimate future
runoff patterns and amounts, to determine if current capacity is adequate or ifupgrades need to be made. If upgrades
are needed, they will be sized to allow for sidewalks or Greenway along Pershing Blvd. Additionally, ceilings in the
current culverts on East Pershing Blvd. are too low for use as a pedestrian underpass. If drainage improvements are
indicated, another parallel culvert might be built tall enough for pedestrian use. In this way, this corridor plan may
offer an opportunity to incorporate a Greenway underpass into the drainage design. Y2 will anticipate this possibility
and evaluate the alternative if it arises.
