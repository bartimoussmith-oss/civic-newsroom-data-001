ENTITLED:
“A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE CONTINUATION OF THE OPTIONAL ONE
PERCENT SALES TAX FOR CALENDAR YEARS 2019 THROUGH 2022 AND
PLEDGING THAT ALL REVENUES DERIVED FROM THIS TAX WILL BE USED
FOR PROJECTS DESCRIBED IN THIS RESOLUTION.”
WHEREAS, Wyoming statutes allow cities and counties in Wyoming to impose an Optional One
Percent Sales Tax; and
WHEREAS, the Optional One Percent Sales Tax has been in effect in the City of Cheyenne and
Laramie County since 197$ and has been used to finance many improvements in the county and its
communities, primarily for the rebuilding, upgrading and maintenance of county roads, city streets and
traffic safety systems; and
WHEREAS, the efficiency and response capabilities of the City Fire & Rescue and Police
Departments have been dramatically upgraded and the tax continues to be necessary to allow these
Departments to continue to provide vehicles and equipment for public safety projects and services; and
WHEREAS, the health and safety of the community and federal rules dictate that storm water
drainage systems be maintained, developed, and improved; and
WHEREAS, the Public Works Department, Community Recreation & Events Department,
the City County Health Department, the Cheyenne Animal Shelter, Human Service Agencies, the
Cheyenne Airport Board, and the Cheyenne- Laramie County Economic Development Joint Powers
Board need the funding available through the continuation of this Optional One Percent Sales Tax to
provide services and upgrade facilities; and
WHEREAS, this funding has been instrumental to economic development which brings jobs to
Cheyenne; and
WHEREAS, the cost of the necessary improvements, facilities, equipment and services is well
beyond the capabilities of the City of Cheyenne without the continuation of the Optional One Percent
Sales Tax; and
WHEREAS, it has been the practice of the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne to define the
purposes for which these tax revenues would be used and to recognize that anticipated improvements and
expenditures are subject to availability of funds; and
WHEREAS, the elected officials are accountable for the use of these revenues; and
WHEREAS, accountability is dependent on verification of optional sales tax expenditures and
interpretation of this resolution.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that it supports the passage of the Optional One Percent Sales Tax for
calendar years 2019 through 2022.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that if the Optional One Percent Sales Tax is renewed, the revenues
received are to be placed into a special fund to be used solely for one percent sales tax projects.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that interest earned from Optional One Percent Sales Tax funds
will be used to pay administration and management costs of one percent sales tax projects and pay
$10,500 of the costs of United Way administering Human Services Agency funding. Designated project
funding saved by performing in-house engineering design or construction management may be used to
offset administration and management costs of one percent sales tax projects.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that all funds realized from the 2019-2022 Optional One Percent
Sales Tax shall be spent to complete one percent sales tax projects. Funds collected from the Optional
One Percent Sales Tax which exceed the estimate of $42,500,000 are for the purposes of this resolution
described as "overage funds." Overage funds shall first be used to pay administration and management
costs of one percent sales tax projects, if any, not covered by other funds allocated in this resolution.
Remaining overage funds will be distributed as follows: 80% shall be allocated to Street and Road
Maintenance, Drainage, and Construction projects listed on page 2 of this resolution; 5% shall be
allocated to the Public Works Department for City Operations and Maintenance Projects; and 15% shall
be allocated to Other Projects and Agencies in the following percentages for use as described on page 3 of
this resolution: Fire and Rescue Department 17.46%; Police Department 30.67%; Community Recreation
& Events Department 22.28%; Equipment and Facilities for Various City Agencies 10.16%; City County
Health Department 3.99%; Animal Shelter 1.28%; and Human Services 14.16%.
