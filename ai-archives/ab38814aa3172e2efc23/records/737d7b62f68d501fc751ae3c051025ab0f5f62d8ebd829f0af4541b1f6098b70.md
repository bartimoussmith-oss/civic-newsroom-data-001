BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:
Section 1.  That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, by Charter Ordinance
Number 1, approved on August 10, 2015, has directed the holding of a special referendum election,
as provided by Article 13, Section 1. (c) of the Wyoming Constitution, to submit to the qualified
electors, voting in said special referendum election, the question whether Charter Ordinance Number
1, establishing the position of City Administrator and exempting the City of Cheyenne, as a first
class city, from certain sections of the Wyoming Statutes, should take effect.
Section 2.  That the ballot question to be submitted to the qualified electors at the special referendum
election to be conducted on November 10, 2015 shall read as follows:
SHALL CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 1 ESTABLISHING THE POSITION OF
CITY ADMINISTRATOR AND EXEMPTING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, AS
A FIRST CLASS CITY, FROM CERTAIN SECTIONS OF THE WYOMING
STATUTES, TAKE EFFECT?
FOR THE CHARTER ORDINANCE TAKING EFFECT
_____
AGAINST THE CHARTER ORDINANCE TAKING EFFECT

_____
Section 3.  That, pursuant to Article 13, Section 1. (c) of the Wyoming Constitution, Charter
Ordinance Number 1 shall take effect only if approved by a majority of the qualified electors voting
in the special referendum election to be conducted on November 10, 2015.
Section 4.
(a)
That the polling (voting) places for the special referendum election are established
as follows:

Storey Gym, 2811 House Ave.

PUBLIC NOTICE OF ELECTOR QUALIFICATIONS
FOR CITY OF CHEYENNE SPECIAL REFERENDUM ELECTION
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming
PUBLIC NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the list of qualified electors, who are eligible
to cast their ballots at the special referendum election to be held in Cheyenne, Wyoming on Tuesday,
November 10, 2015, will be prepared and maintained by the Laramie County Clerk, in electronic
format.
A “qualified elector” is defined as a person who is a  citizen of the United States, who is a
bona fide resident of the State of Wyoming, who is a bona fide resident of the City of Cheyenne, who
has registered to vote and who will be at least eighteen (18) years of age on the day of the special
referendum election to be conducted on November 10, 2015.  No person is a qualified elector who
is a currently adjudicated mentally incompetent person, or who has been convicted of a felony and
his or her civil or voting rights have not been restored.

An applicant may register to vote in person at a polling place on the date of the special
referendum election and may cast a vote if the applicant is a “qualified elector” as defined in this
ordinance.
On the date of the special referendum election, an applicant attempting to register who lacks
proof of identification as required by W.S. § 22-3-104, shall be offered a provisional ballot in
accordance with W.S. § 22-3-105 and permitted, until the close of business on the day following the
special referendum election, to present documentation to the Laramie County Clerk establishing his
or her eligibility to register, to vote in the special referendum election as a “qualified elector” as
defined in this ordinance, and to have his or her ballot counted.
Section 9.
(a)
That immediately following closure of the polling places established by this
ordinance, the Laramie County Clerk will proceed to tabulate the ballots and issue unofficial results
of the election.  Members of the Laramie County Canvassing Board will meet no later then three (3)
days following the closure of the polling places to officially certify the results of the election.  The
Laramie County Clerk shall provide written certification of the official election results to the
Cheyenne City Clerk.

(b)
If the majority of votes cast on the ballot question is in favor of Charter Ordinance
No. 1 taking effect,  the question will have carried and the governing body will then proceed to
declare the results of the special election effective as of January 3, 2017.
