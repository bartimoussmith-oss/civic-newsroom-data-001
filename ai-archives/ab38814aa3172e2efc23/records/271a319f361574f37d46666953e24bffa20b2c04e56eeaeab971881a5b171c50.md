CONTRACT MODIFICATION

Contract Modification:
#1

Project Number:

Bid #E-4-25
Contractor:

Front Range Fire Apparatus
Date Prepared:

April 17, 2026

The above-mentioned contract is amended or modified as set forth in the following and these
amendments and modifications of the plans and specifications are agreed to by the parties. All
other aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

I. Description of Changes:
Consideration of Bid #E-4-25 for the purchase (3) Fire Apparatus for the City of Cheyenne,
Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department (CFR), funded by Future Ballot Specific Purpose Option Tax
Fund, was approved by the Governing Body on November 25, 2024. At the time of placing the
order, the anticipated delivery timeframe was three to four years out.

CFR has since been informed by the awarded Contractor, Front Range Fire Apparatus, that one
(1) stock fire apparatus is nearing production completion by Pierce Manufacturing and is
available for assignment to CFR
 Although this unit does not fully conform to the
specifications set forth in Bid #E-4-25, it remains within the same apparatus classification (Pump
Under Cab  PUC, Type 1 Pumper) and is considered operationally equivalent for the purposes
intended under the bid.

After review, CFR has determined that the opportunity to take delivery of a substantially similar
apparatus on an accelerated timeline provides a significant operational benefit. The ability to
place this unit into service well in advance of the originally anticipated delivery schedule is
deemed to outweigh the impacts associated with the specification differences. Acceptance of this
unit is further determined to be in the best interests of the City, as it enhances service readiness
without materially undermining the intent of the original procurement or resulting in unfair
advantage or prejudice to other potential bidders.

II. New Contract Items:
The specifications of the stock unit differ slightly from the specifications listed in Bid #E-4-25,
These differences have been reviewed and approved by CFR, City Fleet Maintenance, and City
Purchasing. The stock unit and related specifications, including the Proposal for Furnishing Fire
Apparatus and Option List, are attached hereto and incorporated herein as Exhibit A. Notable
differences include a higher gallon-per-minute rated water pump, a larger engine, and a larger
transmission. The stock unit also includes some differences in terms of cabinet placements,
storage configurations, equipment inclusions, and overall fit and finish. The overall intent and
function of the specifications are achieved through acceptance of the stock unit; however, there
are variances between the originally specified build and the stock unit.

Accepting the stock unit at this time presents the potential for cost savings to the City. At an
original bid price of $1,472,877.06, there is the potential to save $44,035.06 by accepting the
stock unit at a cost of $1,428,842.00. CFR will coordinate with the manufacturer to make select
adjustments to the stock unit to bring it closer to the original bid specification and requirements
of CFR, including the use of some allowances provided by the manufacturer. The final price of
the stock unit will not exceed the original bid price, and any remaining funds not utilized for
modifications will revert to General Fund Reserves.
