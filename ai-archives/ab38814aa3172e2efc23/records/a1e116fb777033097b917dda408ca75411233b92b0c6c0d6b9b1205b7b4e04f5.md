BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis to CB
Community Business for 2.0 acres of land.

The proposed CB zoning allows variety of commercial uses in an automobile-centric format.

The land is currently partially vacant and partially occupied by an existing senior center. The expansion of the
senior center on this site is an approved 6th penny ballot measure. The current MUB zone would allow this
development, but the MUB zone requires buildings to be brought closer to the street, occupy a certain
percentage of the lot frontage, and limits the parking to the side of the building. These design standards
make developing the site with a senior center physically linked to the existing center difficult. The CB zoning
would also allow the proposed use but would be less onerous in terms of site layout.

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure.

The location associated with three major streets (College, Pershing, and Lincolnway) would
encourage the development of automobile-oriented commercial uses in this area.
PlanCheyenne encourages the flexibility to be responsive to market demands.

The Future Land Use Designation for this property and the surrounding area is Mixed-use
Commercial. According to comprehensive plan the optimal City zone for the subject property’s
future land use designation is MUB Mixed-use Business Emphasis, although a variety of
commercial, residential, and mixed-use zones could also be appropriate based on the context of
the site. The proposed zoning is CB, a commercial-emphasis zone. Mixed-use Commercial areas
are encouraged to develop with primarily commercial uses, but public, residential, and other
employment uses are also encouraged.

This property is also designated as a Mixed-use Commercial Activity Center on the Future Land
Use Map. Activity Centers are intended for a mix of commercial and residential uses with access
to regional transportation. Regional-serving civic uses is an expected component of an Activity
Center. The proposed CB zone is considered one of the optimal zones for activity centers of this
type.

The policies of PlanCheyenne are supportive of this proposed zone change. The future land use
plan encourages more commercial-style zoning at this location.

The proposed zone change largely complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Surrounding property are zoned with the MUB and CB zoning. MUB is the current zoning of this
parcel and CB is the proposed zoning. The proposed zoning is also compatible with the future
land use designation of Mixed-use Commercial and an activity center.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The MUB zone encourages buildings to be built close to the street with limited side parking and
primarily rear parking areas. Pershing Plaza to the southwest of this proposal exemplifies the
type of development pattern. CB zoning is expected to develop with buildings further from the
street and side or front parking lots are acceptable. The existing development to the west and
east exemplify this development pattern. The CB zone also has less-specific requirements when

Written Statement
The construction of a new Senior Activity Center, to
be located on East Pershing Blvd. east of North
College Drive, was recently approved by the Voters of
Laramie County. The project consists of renovating
the existing Senior Center located on Cox Court and
tying it into the planned new Senior Activity Center.
The subject property is presently zoned MUB and is
located within the City of Cheyenne. The requested
Zone Change to CB Zone, Community Business,
would result in an extension of the present CB Zone to
the west of the Senior Activity Center site.
A primary reason for requesting a CB Zone in lieu of
the existing MUB Zone is: The Minimum Building
Facade Width as a Percentage of East Pershing
Boulevard's Lot Frontage Width. The desired CB Zone
has no minimum building facade width in relationship
to the East Pershing Blvd. lot frontage width while the
present MUB Zone has a minimum building width of
60% of the East Pershing Blvd. lot frontage. This MUB
Zone required building width minimum would
significantly reduce the planned parking area and
would alter the ideal connection proposed from the
new building addition to the existing building along
the eastern property line. The planned building facade
width on East Pershing Boulevard, within the
requested CB Zone, is approximately 25% of the lot
frontage, much less than the required minimum 60%
width in the existing MUB Zone. Additionally, it is not
practical to locate the primary building entrance on
East Pershing Blvd., which is also a requirement in the
present MUB Zone. The site layout and the somewhat
steep grades of the site preclude this main building
entry location at the lot frontage.
Rande Pouppirt, aia
Pouppirt Architects & Construction
