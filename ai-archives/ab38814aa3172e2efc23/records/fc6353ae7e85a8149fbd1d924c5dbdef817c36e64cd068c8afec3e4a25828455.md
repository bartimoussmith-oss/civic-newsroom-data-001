The owner is requesting a waiver for the transportation network as the lot adjoins Thomas

developed area of Cheyenne that has existing connections to other roads and no space for
additional roads. A previous plat for this location received an exception previously to allow
the larger than standard block at this location. This exception should be renewed with this
platting action.

Open Space (4.4):
The owner is requesting a waiver for the requirement of an open space narrative due to
meeting criteria 4.4.2.b.3.

Required Improvements (4.5):
This plat appears to be able to provide all required utilities to the lots within this
development.

Public Safety Fees (4.6):
Public Safety Fees are required at the time of construction of any commercial building or
residential building on any of the proposed lots. Per UDC Section 4.6.3.b, Public Safety Fees
