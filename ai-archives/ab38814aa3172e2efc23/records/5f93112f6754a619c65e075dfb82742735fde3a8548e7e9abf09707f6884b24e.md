Planning Commission
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
May 22, 2025
Sonny Keen, Associate Planner, reported that Section 20, LLC, submitted a Zone
Change application to change from A-1 to CB a portion of Section 20, T14N, R66W
for the purpose of changing to a zone district that conforms to the local area prior to
annexation by the City of Cheyenne. The property is 1.83 acres.
Jack Stadel moved, seconded by Natacha Gaspar to recommend
approval of a Zone Change from A-1 - Agricultural and Rural Residential
to CB - Community Business to the Laramie County Board of
Commissioners for the SE1/4 of Section 20, T14N, R66W, of the 6th P.M.,
Laramie County, WY, with no conditions. Vote 4-0
Caughey, Stadel, Gaspar, Tafoya II
Aye:
Watkins
Excused:
Adjournment
There being no further business the meeting was adjourned at 3:38 p.m.
