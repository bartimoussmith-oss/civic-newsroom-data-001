Fox Properties 440S, LLC, Vacation and Administrative
Plat and Zone Change for York Subdivision, 2nd Filing
Attachments:
Sonny Pourchot, Planning, said Jones Land Surveying, Inc, on behalf of Fox
Properties 440S, LLC, submitted applications for an administrative plat and a zone
change. She said the applications are to vacate Lots 1, 2 and 3, York Subdivision, 1st
Filing, to create a two-tract subdivision to be known as York Subdivision, 2nd Filing,
and to change the zone district from MR - Medium Density Residential to MU - Mixed
Use. Pourchot said the subject property is at 418, 420 and 426 Ave D. She said both
applications were combined into one staff report for ease of presentation and
discussion, with separate motions required for the two actions.
Pourchot said based on the evidence provided, the staff recommends approval of the
vacation and the administrative plat, with the adoption of findings of fact a, b and c
and approval of the zone change, with the adoption of findings of fact c and d.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no PUBLIC
COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Hollingshead, seconded by Vice
Chairman Thompson, to approve the administrative plat for York
Subdivision, 2nd Filing, with no conditions and adopt the findings of fact a,
b and c of the staff report. The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Thompson, Heath, Hollingshead,
Zwonitzer
Aye:
A motion was made by Commissioner Hollingshead, seconded by Vice
Chairman Thompson, to approve the zone change from MR to MU for
York Subdivision, 2nd Filing, Laramie County, and adopt the findings of
fact c and d of the staff report with one condition: Zone change is
contingent upon the approval of York Subdivision, 2nd Filing. The motion
carried by the following vote:

Zwonitzer

Scott Prestidge and Adam Marsano, Tallgrass, gave an overview of the company and

economic impact to the county. Whisker also explained the company will drill its own
well and will utilize closed-loop water resources.
Pourchot said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval with the
adoption of findings of fact a and b with no conditions.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.
Tucker Fagan, resident, and Renny MacKay, WY Business Alliance, spoke in favor of
the project, noting the positive economic effect on the county, advancement of AI for
national security, creating opportunities to keep young people in the state, attracting
innovative companies, and fostering economic diversification.
Thomas White, resident, spoke against the project, expressing concerns about traffic,
population increase and water.
Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Malm closed the hearing.

Use to PUD – Planned Unit Development for a portion of Sects 5, 6, 7,

findings of fact a and b of the staff report with no conditions. The motion
