```text
UDC-21-00256
August 2, 2021
The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban Residential. According to
comprehensive plan the optimal City zones for the future land use designation of Urban
Residential are LR, MR, HR, NR-1, NR-2, and NR-3.

Based on the policies and future land use designation of the subject property, NR-2
Neighborhood Residential  Medium Density zoning is appropriate at this location.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

This property, and most of the surrounding property, is currently zoned MR with the future land
use designation of Urban Residential. The MR zone allows a variety of residential development,
including apartments with conditional use approval, but the NR-2 zone allows smaller lot sizes
and smaller multifamily without conditional use approval. The NR-2 zone also allows cottage lot
style development. The Urban Residential future land use designation encourages a variety of
housing types and densities. Existing development in the area includes apartment buildings and
duplexes. The NR-2 zone is compatible with the surrounding area considering existing zoning
and development patterns.

The property adjacent to the north is zoned MUB and only partially developed. The MUB zone
allows a variety of residential and commercial uses and is considered a higher-intensity zone
than either the current MR zone or the proposed NR-2 zone. The rezoning of this property to
NR-2 would not have an undue influence on the property to the north.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current MR zone allows apartments as a conditional use, larger townhomes, duplexes, and
all but the smallest single-family home lots. The NR-2 zone allows larger apartments as a
conditional use, smaller apartments, townhomes, duplexes, and the smallest style of single-
family lots, including Cottage Lots.

While the surrounding area is all zoned MR, there are lots in the neighborhood which are too
small to be established under the current regulations. The NR-2 zone would allow smaller lots
which are compatible with existing development patterns.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.
```

```text
UDC-21-00256
August 2, 2021
4)
The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The existing block is larger than anticipated for the urban-style of development anticipated for
MUB or NR-2 zoning. However, the subject property is in an area previously developed and
limited options are available to create meaningful connections to the street network. No
additions to the street network are proposed.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The NR-2 zone is intended for a medium residential density and mix of dwelling types in a
compact development pattern (with smaller, walkable blocks).

While any residential development within the City is expected to meet the general residential
design standards, the NR-2 zone has additional compact and mixed-density neighborhood
design standards found in UDC 6.6.4. The MR zone allows wide driveways, provided they are
setback from the side lot line, and allows the front façade to be dominated by the garage. The
NR-2 zone requires more discrete access (narrower driveways or alley access) but allows
driveways to be built to the side property line. Garages are prohibited from dominating the
front façade. Existing development in the area is more reflective of the design standards for the
MR zone than the design of the proposed NR-2 zone. A change to NR-2 may necessitate
replatting of the subdivision for the best development capabilities.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The existing lots do not meet frontage requirements to have front access, a new alley or access
easement would be required to be dedicated. Lots would become less non-conforming from a
size and frontage aspect, but more nonconforming from an access standpoint. A rezone almost
requires a replat. A replat has been proposed.

While the NR-2 zone would give more flexibility in potential development (or redevelopment) of
this parcel should the specific development proposed not be executed; rezoning to NR-2 creates
difficulty in developing the existing lots, hence a replat likely being necessary to develop to the
maximum extent practical.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.
```
