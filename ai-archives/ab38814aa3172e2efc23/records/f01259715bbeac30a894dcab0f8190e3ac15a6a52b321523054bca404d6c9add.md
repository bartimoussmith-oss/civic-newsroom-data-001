RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

February 28, 2022

 The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. at Storey Gym, 2811 House Ave.,  and via electronic conference meeting.  Present
were: MAYOR – Patrick Collins, COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Bryan Cook,
Ken Esquibel, Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Tom Segrave and
Jeff White.  Also present: Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

 Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support
by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered
in its normal sequence on the agenda. Upon request by Mr. White, and pursuant to support received
by Mr. Johnson and Dr. Aldrich, agenda item #21 was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Upon
request by Mr. White, and pursuant to support received by Mr. Cook and Dr. Aldrich, agenda item
#26(b) was removed from the Consent Agenda.  Mr. Roybal moved to approve remaining items,
seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.

[CA] Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on February 14, 2022.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.

 Mayor Collins opened a public  hearing for renewal applications for existing City liquor
licenses (retail, restaurant, limited retail, resort, bar and grill licenses, microbrewery, satellite
distillery, special malt beverage and winery permits) filed with the City Clerk’s Office.  There
being no comments or input and purpose of the hearing having been met , Mayor Collins closed
the public hearing and referred the applications to Finance Committee.

 Mayor Collins opened a public hearing for a transfer of ownership and location of retail
liquor license application filed with the City Clerk’s Office for Dillon Companies, LLC, a Kansas
limited liability company d/b/a King Soopers, 3702 Dell Range Blvd., Cheyenne, Wyoming
