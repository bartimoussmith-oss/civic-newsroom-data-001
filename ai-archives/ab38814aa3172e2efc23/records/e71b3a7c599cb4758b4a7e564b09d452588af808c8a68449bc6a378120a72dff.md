ORDINANCE NO. ____________________

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:
Section 1. That the owner(s) of the property described herein has (have) applied for a
zoning map amendment (“zone change”) for said property in accordance with Section 2.2.1,
Zoning Map Amendment, of the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (“UDC”).
Section 2. That on August 1, 2022, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission conducted
a public hearing, reviewed the proposed zone change that is the subject of this ordinance, and
recommended approval of said zone change.
Section 3. That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the zone change
that is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the UDC.
Section 4. That, in accordance with Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2,
Zoning Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the UDC, the
aforementioned application is hereby approved and the zoning classification of the real property
described as Lots 3 and 4, Block 17 and Lots 1 and 2, Block 16 and all of Moran Avenue adjacent
thereto, Frontier Gardens 3rd Filing, Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby changed to CB Community
Business.
Section 5. That the City Engineer is hereby authorized and directed to alter and amend the
Official Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the UDC to comply with the zone change as
described in Section 4 above when this ordinance takes effect in accordance with Section 7 below.
Section 6. Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any section, paragraph, sentence, clause, or phrase of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional, such unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining sections,
paragraphs, sentences, clauses, and phrases.
Section 7. That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and
publication.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,

Some surrounding property is zoned residential (to the east and north). To the west the
property is zoned MUB, and to the south there is CB and P zoning. No adjacent zoning is CB, but
the commercial development patterns and commercial-allowed uses make CB zoning
compatible with surrounding zoning.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The property is currently zoned HR, which is intended for higher-density residential
development with a mix of almost exclusively residential uses. The proposed CB zone is
intended for auto-oriented commercial-centric development. The location along a major
vehicular corridor separated by nearby residential neighborhoods by a creek, makes the auto-
oriented commercial-centric development pattern appropriate at this location. The variety of
allowed uses (including returning to multifamily use by conditional review) makes this site much
more suited to the proposed zone district than to the existing district.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The zone change is occurring in a pre-existing neighborhood. The block is larger than expected
for commercial development, but the creek in the vicinity limits options to create meaningful
street network connections. No additions to the street network are proposed, in fact, an
associated platting proposal asks to vacate the street - as it has been platted for over 60 years
and in that time there has never been the impetus to construct the connection between Dell
Range and Sheridan along Moran over the creek at this location.

Access to Dell Range will be required to be at the signalized intersection, but specifics will be
reviewed with the site plan.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC

The CB zone is intended for Retail, Employment, Service and Civic uses that serve a number of
residential neighborhoods. CB zoning exists nearby to the southwest. Properties along Dell
Range have largely developed with the type of development anticipated in the CB zone. Some of
these were developed prior to the current regulations in the MUB zone (and are nonconforming
in the MUB zone, and some have developed in the CB zone after a rezoning to CB. A few have
pursued variances to develop with a CB style in the MUB zone. Despite the lack of current CB
zoning in the area, the corridor on the whole is more conducive to CB zoning than to any of the
other existing zoning districts.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The CB zone requires buffers to residential zones and setbacks from the streets. It appears the
subject property is able to provide both. While there is a specific development considered with
the proposed zone change, The CB zoning would allow a variety of use options should the
current development proposal not be executed. This even includes potential redevelopment
with multifamily as the CB does allow multifamily as a conditional use.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

No reviewing agencies provided recommendations regarding the proposed zone change.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.

8) Whether the application will harm the public health, safety, or general welfare. (UDC
2.2.1.d.8)

City staff is satisfied that this proposed zone change would not adversely affect the public
health, safety, or general welfare.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 8.
