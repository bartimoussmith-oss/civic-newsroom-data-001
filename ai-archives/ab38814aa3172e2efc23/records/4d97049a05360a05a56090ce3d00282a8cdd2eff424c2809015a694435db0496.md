```text
RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION TO AUTHORIZE THE APPLICATION FOR THE
CONTINUATION GRANT IN THE AMOUNT OF $318,375.00 FROM THE
CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE FOR
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE OFFICE OF YOUTH ALTERNATIVES
FOSTER GRANDPARENT PROGRAM.”

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Office of Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent
Program is a not-for-profit organization that depends on grants, donations, and local support; and

WHEREAS, the Foster Grandparent Program provides opportunities for senior service to
meet unmet needs and assign individuals, 55 and older, to schools and human service agencies
that serve youth under the age of 21 having special or exceptional needs or with conditions or
circumstances identified as limiting their academic, social, or economic development; and

WHEREAS, the Corporation for National and Community Service is a Federal
Governmental agency that awards grants to assist grantees in carrying out a national service
program as authorized by the Domestic and Volunteer Service Act of 1973, as amended (42
U.S.C. Chapter 22); and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Office of Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent
Program has will apply for the continuation grant in the amount of Three Hundred Eighteen
Thousand Three Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($318,375.00) from the Corporation for National
and Community Service; and

WHEREAS, the statutory match for the grant is waived and the Office of Youth
Alternatives shall provide the required local match in the amount of Thirty-One Thousand,
Eight-Hundred Thirty-Eight Dollars ($31,838.00) through State, Local and other funding.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHYENNE, WYOMING:

1.
That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne is in support of the Office of
Youth Alternatives Foster Grandparent Program and the application for the continuation grant
from the Corporation for National and Community Service in the amount of Three Hundred
Eighteen Thousand Three Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($318,375.00).

2.
That the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne is hereby designated as the authorized
representative of the City to act on behalf of the Governing Body on all matters relating to this
grant and is authorized to execute the grant agreement and associated documents.

By Stefanie Boster at 10:21 am, Feb 18, 2022
```
