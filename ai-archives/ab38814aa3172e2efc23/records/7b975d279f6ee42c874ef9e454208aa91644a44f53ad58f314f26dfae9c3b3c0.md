MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

March 23, 2022
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-19-22 for furnishing 95 Gallon Automated Refuse Carts
for the City of Cheyenne, Sanitation Division
______________________________________________
Three (3) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Three (3) bids were
received from:

1. Cascade Engineering, Inc.  Grand Rapids, Michigan.
a. Multiple Bid Exceptions, Bid Rejected
i. Page 9, Item 2, Composite Catch Bar, instead of Metal Catch Bar.
ii. Page 9, Item 4, a, I, Height 46”, instead of maximum of 44”.
iii. Page 10, Item 12, Non-Acceptance of City’s Mandatory Warranty
Requirements.
iv. Page 10, Item 15, a, b, c, d, e, f, In Mold Label, instead of Hot Stamped
Instructions for Use.
v. Page 12, Item 23, Non-Acceptance of City’s Mandatory Warranty
Requirements.
vi. Page 12, Item 24, Non-Acceptance of City’s Mandatory Warranty
Requirements.
vii. General, Material Adjustment Clause, the City did not request or provide
provisions to utilize a Material Adjustment Clause.
2. Otto Environmental Systems.  Charlotte, North Carolina. (Bid Options 1 & 2)
a. Submitted Two Bid Options:
i. Option 1 is fully compliant, Lowest Responsive Bid.
ii. Option 2 contains Bid Exceptions.
3. Able Equipment.  Casper, Wyoming.
a. Bid Exceptions, Not Lowest Responsive Bidder.

The bids were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), and Kaitlyn Garey (Sanitation).  Their
recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Otto Environmental Systems, Bid
Option 1, for the purchase of 95 Gallon Automated Refuse Carts, for a total cost of $38,539.20 for
One (1) 53’ Semi-Trailer Load of Carts (560 Carts).

Funding:
023-14-1423-43-21301 (Solid Waste Fund / Professional Services)
