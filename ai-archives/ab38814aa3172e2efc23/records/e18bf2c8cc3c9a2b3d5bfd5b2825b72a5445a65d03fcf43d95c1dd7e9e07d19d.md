The current block is excessively sized compared to regulations for the area. Even if a street

point to the south, the block perimeter would be over 2 miles in length, more than 3 times
the maximum allowed by the UDC. Due to these existing conditions, an exception to block
size is recommended.

There is a proposal by this developer and Sam’s Club to the south to create a pedestrian
connection from an earlier phase of this multifamily residential subdivision to the
commercial properties to the south. If built, the connection would greatly reduce the
perimeter for pedestrians and cyclists.

Open space is required at 750sqft per unit or 8% of the gross development area, whichever
is less. Due to the nature of the proposed development pattern, open space will be taken
