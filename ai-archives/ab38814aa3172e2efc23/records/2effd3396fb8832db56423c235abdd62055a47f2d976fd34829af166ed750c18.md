PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
Due to the Martin Luther King Jr. holiday, a meeting of the Public Services Committee was instead
held on Wednesday, January 17, 2024, in Committee Room 104 and via electronic meeting,
starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Scott Roybal, Chairman, Pete Laybourn, Dr. Rinne, and Torn
Segrave.
AGENDA ITEMS
11.
ORDINANCE 3”’ READfNG
— Amending Title 6, Animals, of the Municipal Code of
the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, regarding the registration, control, impoundment and
general care of animals and birds within the city.
Mr. Laybourn joined the meeting at 12:04 p.m. during discussion of item #11.
Dr. Rinne moved to approve on 3”’ and final reading, seconded by Mr. Segrave. Dr. Rinne moved
to amend by substitute dated January 11, 2024, seconded by Mr. Segrave.
Dr. Rinne moved to
amend the substitute to strike “animals” on both page 7, Section 6.08.020 (B) and Page 15, Section
6.08.150 and replace it with “livestock, birds, or nonhuman primates”, seconded by Mr. Segrave.
Motion to amend the substitute carried by unanimous voice vote. Motion to amend by substitute,
as amended, carried by unanimous voice vote. Main motion, as amended, carried by unanimous
voice vote.
John Brodie, Deputy City Attorney, provided a staff report and reviewed a proposed substitute.
Committee member Rinne commented on the item. Public comments were made by Amber Ash,
Cheyenne Animal Shelter Director of Strategic Initiatives, and Janet Stephens, Cheyenne Kennel
Club.
