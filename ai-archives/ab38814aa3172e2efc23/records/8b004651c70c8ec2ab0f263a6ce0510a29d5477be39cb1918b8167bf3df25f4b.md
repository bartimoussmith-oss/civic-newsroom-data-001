Board of County Commissioners
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
October 15, 2024
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of Sundance
Kid Properties, LLC, submitted zone change and administrative plat applications for
Tract 2, The Horse Creek Ranch, 2nd Filing, situated in a portion of Sect 34, T15N,
R67W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY. He said the subject property lies at 3817
Butch Cassidy Trl and the 12-acre parcel will be divided into two tracts, to be known
as The Horse Creek Ranch, 4th Filing, and Tract 1 will be a six-acre tract that includes
the existing residence and Tract 2 will be six acres for a future residence.
Arnold said both applications were combined into one staff report for ease of
presentation and discussion, with separate motions required.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the zone
change and administrative plat with the adoption of findings of fact a, b and c, with the
condition that the zone change will become effective only with approval of the
administrative plat for The Horse Creek Ranch, 4th Filing.
Commissioner Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Robert Hoard,
resident, expressed concerns about the zone change. Hearing no further PUBLIC
COMMENT, Commissioner Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Holmes, to approve the Zone Change from A-1 to AR for
Tract 2, The Horse Creek Ranch, 2nd Filing, with one condition, and
adopt the findings of fact a and c of the staff report.
Commissioner Thompson questioned the reason for the zone change. Arnold spoke
about the land use regulations and the current subdivision. He said the AR is the
proper zoning designation.
The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Heath, Holmes, Thompson
Aye:
Lovett
Excused:
A motion was made by Commissioner Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Holmes, to approve a vacation of Tract 2, The Horse Creek
Ranch, 2nd Filing, located in a portion of Sect 34, T15N, R67W, of the 6th
PM, Laramie County, WY, in order to create the administrative plat known
as “The Horse Creek Ranch, 4th Filing”, with no conditions, and adopt the
findings of fact b of the staff report. The motion carried by the following
vote:
Malm, Heath, Holmes, Thompson
Aye:
Lovett

13. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a rehearing on a revision for a
subdivision permit & plat for Open View Ranch, situated at the N1/2 of
Sect 7, T14N, R63W, of the 6th PM, Laramie County, WY.
24 - 497
Open View Ranch, Subdivision Permit and Plat,
Revision
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Steil Surveying, on behalf of Whiskey Hill LLC, had the
plat for Open View Ranch approved by this body on Jan 2, 2024, on the condition that
a nonadverse Chapter 23 recommendation was received by Planning prior to
recordation. He said the nonadverse recommendation was received by Planning on
or about May 15, and the plat has yet to be recorded due to an issue that arose in the
road construction permitting process.
Arnold said during the road construction permitting process, the engineer of record
recommended that the plat be modified to include two drainage easements to ensure
that specific low points of natural drainage on portions of the plat could not be
disturbed by home construction in the area. He said staff and the applicant request to
modify the previously approved plat to place the recommended drainage easements
on the final plat prior to recordation.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval with the
adoption of findings of fact a, b and c.
Commissioner Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Hearing no
PUBLIC COMMENT, Commissioner Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Holmes, to approve the revised subdivision permit and plat
for Open View Ranch with no conditions and adopt findings of fact a, b,
and c of the staff report. The motion carried by the following vote:
Malm, Heath, Holmes, Thompson
Aye:
Lovett
Excused:
