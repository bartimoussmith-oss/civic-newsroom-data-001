and representative for the applicant, spoke about the permit denial last year and the
court's injunction. Asay said the applicant has abided by all the conditions placed by
the court and requested that the permit be approved. Asay referred to Peters’ memo
and noted that the doors have been used for the last three years without an issue.
Commissioner Thompson pointed out that a sprinkler system was not previously
required because the square footage was below the requirement.  However, the
buildings are now connected because of enclosed walkways. Commissioner
Thompson asked the applicant’s attorney for an update on the court case. Asay said it
is ongoing, and  stated he would like a resolution. He noted two other cases are still
open for the petition for review. Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman
Malm closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Vice Chairman Thompson, seconded by
Commissioner Zwonitzer, that this agenda item be approved with the
addition of the language read into the record for the previous permits.
Commissioner Zwonitzer proposed postponement of the item for a month since the
case is under active litigation.
