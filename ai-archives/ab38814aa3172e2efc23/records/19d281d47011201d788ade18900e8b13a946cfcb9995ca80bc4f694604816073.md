 ______________________________________________
Bid #E-21-24 was solicited for One (1) New 2024 To 2026 Low Ground Pressure/Waste Handling
Dozer (42,000 LB Min. Operating Weight) for the Landfill Division.

One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1.
Wyoming Machinery Company, Cheyenne, Wyoming

The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Samule Quattrini (Landfill), Vicki
Nemecek (Public Works), and Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and their recommendation is to accept
the lowest responsive bid from Wyoming Machinery Company.

The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for the
review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model year
escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid.  Calculated on the base cost of
trailer, the Surcharge amount to be included with this bid is $77,586.00.

The total requested bid award is for $593,506.00.

$517,240.00 Base Bid, Less (-$32,500.00) Trade In, Plus GPS Base Station $31,180.00, Plus
Surcharge Contingency $77,586.00.

Funding:
023-14-1424-43-43102
(Solid Waste Fund / Equipment (over $5,000))

023-00-0000-35-06113
(Gain (Loss) on Disposal of Assets)
