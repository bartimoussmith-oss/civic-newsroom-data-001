LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease is made this _____ day of ________________, 2022, between the City of
Cheyenne, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming, 2101
O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and the Lamar Advertising of South Dakota,
LLC dba Lamar Advertising of Casper, tradename: The Lamar Companies, a limited liability
company organized and existing under the laws of the State of South Dakota, 5321 Corporate
Blvd., Baton Rouge, Louisiana 70808 (“Lessee”).

1.
Description of Property; Term.

In consideration of the rent provided for in Section Two of this Lease, City leases to Lessee
real property belonging to City located in Laramie County, Wyoming, and more particularly
described as a site for the placement of three billboard advertising signs described as billboard
238-7566 measuring not more than 40 feet in length by 10 feet in height located approximately
15.5 miles West of Junction I-25 and I-80 on the South side of I-80; billboard 238-7567 measuring
not more than 28 feet in length by 10 feet in height located approximately 16 miles West of
Junction I-25 and I-80 on the South side of I-80; and billboard 238-7568 measuring not more than
30 feet in length and 10 feet in height located approximately 2.7 miles West of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, for a term of five (5) years beginning on February 26, 2023.  That date shall be
hereinafter referred to as the “commencement date.”  The advertising sign site is hereinafter
referred to as the “leased property.”

2.
Rent

Lessee shall pay City an annual rent of One Thousand Eight Hundred Fifty Dollars
($1850.00), including $750 for billboard 238-7566, $500 for billboard 238-7567 and $600 for
billboard 238-7568, payable within thirty (30) days after the commencement date, and payable in
advance on the commencement date in each year thereafter for the entire term of the Lease.

3.
Use of Property

Lessee shall use the leased property for the construction and maintenance of three
billboards as described in Section One above, and for no other purpose whatsoever.  Lessee shall
not place on the leased property any advertisement for alcohol or tobacco products.

4.
Compliance with the Law

In its use of the leased property, Lessee shall fully comply with all applicable federal, state,
county and municipal statues, rules, regulations, and ordinances.  In its use of the leased property,
Lessee shall secure from the proper authorities all required licenses and permits before
commencing any construction, re-construction, or significant repair of the billboard.

5.
Repairs and Maintenance

Lessee shall, at all times during the term of this Lease, keep all improvements placed by
Lessee on the leased property in good repair, and shall maintain them at all times in a clean and
attractive condition.  Construction and maintenance activities shall be conducted in a manner that
is not hazardous to livestock, including the installation of fencing if necessary to prevent
unreasonable risks of harm to livestock.  Lessee shall be responsible for all livestock injuries and
deaths caused or in any way resulting from construction or maintenance activities.

6.
Insurance Requirement

Lessee shall obtain and keep in full force and effect at all times during the term of this
Lease, the following specified insurance coverages for the protection of City and Lessee against
liability arising out of the condition of the leased property, the improvements erected on the
property, or any operations of Lessee conducted pursuant to this Lease.  In the event Lessee retains
independent contractors to perform any operations on City land pursuant to this Lease, the
insurance requirements stated in this Section shall also apply to each and every such contractor.

Commercial General Liability Insurance
For claims arising out of bodily injury, illness, or death, or from damage to or destruction
of property of others, including loss or use thereof, with minimum limits of
$1,000,000.00 per occurrence, $2,000,000.00 aggregate for the entire term of this Lease.

Business Automobile Insurance
Including owned, non-owned and hired vehicles with minimum limits for bodily injury
and property damage of $1,000,000.00 per accident for the entire term of this Lease.

Workers’ Compensation
Workers’ Compensation coverage shall be in effect for the entire term of the Lease, as
required by Wyoming law, for all employees or agents providing services under this
Lease.  Lessee shall provide the City with proof of workers’ compensation or employer’s
liability insurance coverage.

Additional Insurance Information

Lessee shall provide the City copies of certificates of insurance acknowledging the
above-stated coverages prior to entering upon City’s land for the purpose of conducting
any operations pursuant to this Lease.

It is understood and agreed that these policies are primary and not contributory.  All
policies required under this Lease shall be in effect for the duration of this Lease.  It shall
be an affirmative obligation upon Lessee to immediately notify the City’s Risk Manager,
City Clerk, and City Attorney of any fact, circumstances, or occurrence that has resulted
in or may result in the cancellation or substantive change of any insurance coverage
required by this Lease, and failure to do so shall be construed to be a breach of this Lease.

In addition, Lessee shall provide the City with copies of insurance policies and policy
endorsements listing the City of Cheyenne as an additional insured.  The City’s failure to
request or review such policies, endorsements, or certificates shall not affect the City’s
rights or Lessee’s obligations hereunder.

Any insurance company providing coverage under this Lease shall have a minimum A.
M. Best rating of A- (excellent).

7.
Ownership Improvements

All improvements erected by Lessee on the leased property shall remain the property of
Lessee and shall be removed by Lessee at the termination of this Lease or any renewal of the Lease.

8.
Access to Leased Property

City grants Lessee the right to pass to and from the leased property over City’s private
roads located on the City’s Belvoir Ranch for the purposes of construction, maintenance and repair
of the billboard advertising sign located on the leased premises; provided, however that Lessee
shall use said private roads in accordance with good management practices and shall take
reasonable precautions to prevent damage to said roads and to the ranch lands adjacent to said
roads and the leased property.

9.
Termination

A.
By City.

1.
If the City should make a bona fide contract for the sale of the leased
property, the City may, at City’s option, terminate this Lease by
giving Lessee one hundred and eighty (180) days’ notice in writing,
and refunding to Lessee the rental paid in advance pro rata for the
unexpired term of this Lease.

2.
If the Lessee defaults in the payment of rent or breaches any other
covenant or condition of this Lease, the City may, in addition to any
other remedy that the City may have, declare forfeiture of this Lease
for the default, re-enter the leased property, and remove all personal
property from the premises.

B.
By Lessee.  During the term of this Lease, should the use of the leased
property for the purpose permitted by this Lease be prohibited, limited, or
restricted by the terms of any restriction, covenant, regulation, ordinance,
or zoning resolution affecting the property, Lessee may, at Lessee’s option
terminate this Lease by giving the City thirty (30) days’ notice in writing of
its election to do so.

C.
Upon termination of this Lease, Lessee shall restore the leased property to
its natural state including, without limitation, filling all holes and removing
all debris caused by the removal of billboard signs and other items of
personal property placed on the leased property by Lessee.

10.
Effect of Condemnation

If the leased property, or any part of the leased property, is taken by eminent domain, this
Lease shall terminate on the date when title vests pursuant to such taking.

11.
Renewal Option

The Lessee shall have the option to extend this Lease for one additional five (5) year period,
provided, however, that said option may be exercised only if (1) the Lessee is not in default of any
of the terms and conditions of this Lease at the time the option is exercised; (2) the Lessee has
practiced good stewardship of the leased property, access roads, and adjacent ranch lands as
determined by the City in the exercise of its reasonable discretion; and (3) the Lessee provides
written notice to the City of its intent to exercise its option to renew at least one hundred and twenty
(120) days prior to the scheduled expiration of this Lease, unless such terms and conditions are
amended or renegotiated pursuant to mutual written agreement.

12.
Indemnification

The Lessee agrees to defend, hold harmless, and indemnify the City of Cheyenne, its
officials, employees, agents, and authorized volunteers against all claims and costs, including
attorney’s fees, arising during, or resulting from the Lessee’s performance of its obligations under
this Lease.  The Lessee acknowledges its understanding of this paragraph and realizes it may have
a financial responsibility to the City.  The City does not waive any applicable defenses and
expressly reserves the right to invoke governmental immunity pursuant to the Wyoming
Governmental Claims Act, Wyo. Stat. 1-39-101, et seq. for any claim arising out of this Lease.

It is expressly understood and agreed that although the City has the right to observe and
review the work and operations of the Lessee, this shall not relieve the Lessee from any of its
covenants and obligations hereunder, and the Lessee shall be responsible for and hold the City and
its officers, employees, agents and representatives harmless from all suits, actions or claims of any
character due to injuries or damages sustained by any person or property in consequence of any
breach or of default under this Lease, or of the failure to observe safety standards or regulations,
or otherwise, or through the use of unsafe or unacceptable practices or materials in the performance
of any operations under this Lease, or the Lessee’s failure to comply with any law, ordinance or
regulation.

The parties have executed this Lease the day and year first above written.
