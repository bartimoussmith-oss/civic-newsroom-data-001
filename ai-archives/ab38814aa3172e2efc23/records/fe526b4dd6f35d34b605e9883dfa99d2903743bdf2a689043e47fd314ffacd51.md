CONTRACT NO. __________

CONTRACT EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT, made and entered into this _____ day of _______, 2013, by and
between the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, a municipal corporation organized under the laws of the
State of Wyoming, acting by and through its political subdivision, the Cheyenne Police Department,
hereinafter referred to as the “Department,” and Leslie Burch, hereinafter referred to as “Burch.”

WITNESSETH:

WHEREAS, the Department is a law enforcement agency serving the City of Cheyenne, and
is a department thereof; and

WHEREAS, the Department has received a grant of funds from the Wyoming Division of
Victim Services for the purpose of providing crime victim assistance services; and

WHEREAS, in order to provide crime victim assistance services, the Department desires to
retain the services of a “Victim Witness Coordinator;” and

WHEREAS, because funding a Victim Witness Coordinator position is dependent on the
receipt of a grant, the Department wishes to create a contract employee rather than permanent
employee position; and

WHEREAS, Burch has training and experience in the field of assisting crime victims; and

WHEREAS, Burch desires to provide services to the Department under the terms and
conditions hereinafter contained.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the promises and mutual covenants herein, the
parties agree as follows:

1.
The term of this Agreement shall commence July 1, 2013, and terminate June 30,
2014, unless sooner terminated pursuant to Section 9 below or if grant funding is no longer available.

2.
Burch will be considered a contract employee of the Department.  She will act as
Victim Witness Coordinator and assist the Department.

3.
Burch shall at all times coordinate her activities hereunder with the Department and
shall work under the direction of the Department.

4.
The Department agrees to pay Burch the sum of Three Thousand Seven Hundred
Forty-Nine Dollars and Forty-one Cents ($3749.41) per month.  The total amount over the term of
this agreement will not, in any event, exceed the sum of Forty-Four Thousand, Nine Hundred Ninety-
Two Dollars and Ninety-Two Cents ($44,992.92).

5.
The Department shall provide Burch the benefits provided to full-time employees of
the City of Cheyenne.  The City of Cheyenne will withhold appropriate federal income and social
security taxes and remit them to the federal government.

6.
In addition to the compensation set forth above, the Department agrees to pay Burch
for hours worked in excess of forty hours in a work week at time and one-half rate.

7.
In the event that the governing body authorizes a bonus to be paid to city employees,
Burch would be eligible to receive a bonus similar to other city employees. Such bonus payment
shall first be made out of grant funds, if available, then out of Department funds, if grant funds are
not available.
