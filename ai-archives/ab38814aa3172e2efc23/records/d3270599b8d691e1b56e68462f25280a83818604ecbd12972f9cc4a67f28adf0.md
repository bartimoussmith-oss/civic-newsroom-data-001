RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 September 23, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez
and Ms. Williams.  Absent: Mr. Allen.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City
Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA]  are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support
by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be
considered in its normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Brown moved to approve, seconded by Mr.
Luna.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.

date.  He stated, pursuant to Uniform Development Code (UDC) requirements, the signatures
must be verified and recommended postponement of the item to the October 14 City Council
th

approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Mr. Shanor moved to postpone to
October 14, 2013, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Ms. Williams noted that she had submitted the protest
petition to the City Clerk, and expressed appreciation to the developer and agent for
communication efforts with the neighborhood.  Upon inquiry regarding the petition process, Dan
White, City Attorney, recited wording contained in Wyo. Stat. Section 15-1-603 and stated it
would need to be determined whether the petition met statutory requirements and, if so, then it

ORDINANCE - Pursuant to Sections 1.1.6 and 2.4.1 of the Cheyenne Unified
Development Code, amending the landscaping requirements under Unified Development Code
Sections 6.3.4a, 6.3.4b, and 6.3.7e.  Commenting on the proposed ordinance were: Lea Bain,
John Volk, Dave Teubner, Nancy Loomis, Mark Ellison and Jan Nelson-Schroll.  Topics
discussed included the following:  Comments in support as well as non-support of the ordinance;
growing and maintaining liveable trees in Cheyenne’s climate; current landscaping requirements
and modification of same which would benefit various types of property owners/developers;
financial and maintenance concerns involving landscape installation and grounds care, including
City-owned parks, greenway paths and detention pond areas; support for community
beautification efforts; community appearance as a factor in attracting and retaining businesses
and employees; taking a progressive stance regarding landscaping relative to developments and
re-developments; primary purpose of the proposed ordinance; landscaping assisting with run-off
surface water and conservation/retention measures; providing for flexibility regarding
requirements for small businesses or determining on a case-by-case basis under certain
conditions; wise and efficient water use; water/pollutant discharges into storm sewers and storm
water management; trees providing economic, environmental and energy efficient benefits;
remaining competitive with other area communities’ landscaping requirements; annual UDC
review process; xeriscaping, and water re-use.  In response to comments involving maintenance
of City parks, greenways and detention ponds, Rick Parish, Parks and Recreation Director,
provided information on public areas maintained by Parks and Recreation; tree survival rate, and
water re-use and use of raw (untreated) water in certain areas.  Mr. Shanor moved to approve on
third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Discussion followed regarding landscaping costs
associated with development and re-development of properties, and landscaping requirements
and effect on large business vs. small business developments.  Mr. Shanor  moved to amend by

ORDINANCE - Amending the Cheyenne Unified Development Code (UDC), to allow,
after conditional discretionary review, Service Uses, Animal Care - Hospital/Clinic in the MUB
Mixed-Use Business Emphasis and the NB Neighborhood Business zone districts and to allow,
as a use permitted by right, Service Uses, Animal Care - Kennel in the LI Light Industrial zone
district.  Luke Meyer, representative for Black Dog Animal Rescue; Troy Thompson, local
veterinarian; Brittany Steinman, owner of local dog day care and pet hotel business, and Dr.
Tiffany Healey, local veterinarian, offered comments in support of the ordinance.  Topics
presented included: Accessible veterinary care services; proposed zone changes being appropriate
and providing for location options for animal care and kennel services, and minimum noise levels
generated by animal care facilities.  Mr. Shanor moved to approve on second reading, seconded
by Mr. Cook.  Comments followed in favor of the ordinance.  Upon inquiry concerning
conditional discretionary review, Brandon Cammarata, Planning and Development office,
provided information on approvals required through the Board of Adjustment for certain types
of requested uses and clarified the ordinance addresses two uses, one being veterinary clinics and
the other pertaining to uses involving kennels, which would change kennel uses now allowed
only in CB and Agricultural zones to include Light Industrial zones.  Motion carried.  Voting
“yes” -- all members of the governing body present.
