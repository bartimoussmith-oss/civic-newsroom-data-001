EMPLOYEE  ASSISTANCE  PROGRAM  AGREEMENT

This Employee Assistance Program Agreement (AGREEMENT) is made and entered into
by and between CITY OF CHEYENNE, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming
(CUSTOMER) and CAREBRIDGE CORPORATION, a Pennsylvania Corporation located at 855
Springdale Drive, Exton, Pennsylvania (CONTRACTOR).
Whereas, CONTRACTOR makes available an employee assistance program (“EAP”) in-
cluding work-life assistance services (“WLAS”); and,
Whereas, CUSTOMER desires to make an EAP available to its employees; and,
Whereas, CUSTOMER desires to contract with CONTRACTOR to provide an EAP for
CUSTOMER’s employees and CONTRACTOR desires to provide such EAP to CUSTOMER;
and,
Whereas, the Parties desire to enter into this Agreement in order to provide a full statement
of their respective responsibilities in connection with the provision of an EAP during the term of
this Agreement.
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises and covenants contained
herein, and intending to be legally bound hereby, it is understood and agreed by the parties as
follows:
1. DEFINITIONS:
1.1 COVERED EMPLOYEE shall mean any employee of the CUSTOMER included
in an eligibility file provided to CONTRACTOR by CUSTOMER. Employees on
Leave of Absence for FMLA, Short-term Disability, Military Leave and Workers’
Compensation are also recognized as COVERED EMPLOYEES if included on the
CUSTOMER-provided eligibility file.
1.2 COVERED PERSON shall mean any COVERED EMPLOYEE and any related
individual residing within the household of the COVERED EMPLOYEE as well
as children living outside the household to age twenty-six (26). A COVERED
PERSON may have the option to continue the EAP benefit through COBRA, if the
option is made available by CUSTOMER.
1.3 PROVIDER shall mean a third party designated by the CONTRACTOR to provide
EAP services.
1.4 COVERED SERVICES shall mean all related employee assistance services ren-
dered to a COVERED PERSON to the extent that such services are for the evalua-
tion of, and counseling for, personal and psychological problems.  The services
described herein are available, at no cost, to COVERED PERSONS.

1.5 CISD shall mean critical incident stress debriefing. A debriefing is a proactive in-
tervention involving a group meeting or discussion about a particularly distressing
critical incident. It is designed to mitigate the impact of a critical incident and assist
in recovery from the stress associated with the event. CISD services are ideally
conducted between 24 and 72 hours after the incident.
1.5.1 Standard Response: Any next day response that is not considered
immediate or same day.
1.5.2 Same Day Response: Requests that are over three hours and less
than 24 hours from scheduled response.
1.5.3 Immediate Response: Requests that are within three hours of sched-
uled response. Response scheduled after 11pm or before 9am the
next day in the service area time zone.
