38. PUBLIC HEARING regarding the Laramie Range Wind Project site
plan and high power transmission line, Laramie County, WY.
25 - 452
Laramie Range Wind Project Site Plan and Public
Hearing High Power Transmission Line
Presentation, Laramie Range Wind Project
Attachments:
Justin Arnold, Planning, said Charlie Banke, Project Manager, on behalf of
ConnectGen Laramie County, LLC, submitted applications for approval of the
Laramie Range Wind Project and a Public Hearing for high-power transmission lines
associated with the wind project for the locations in the northwest area of the county,
approximately 20 miles from Cheyenne. He said the applications were submitted for
the purpose of developing 56,150 acres into a wind energy facility.
Charlie Banke, Allison Poe and Kelley Pearson, representatives of ConnectGen,
presented an overview of the project. The project is anticipated to begin in early 2027
with two phases. Phase I is a 400 MW commercial operation to be completed in
2028, and Phase II is a 250 MW commercial operation to be completed in 2029.
Acting Chairman Thompson referenced a letter from WY Game and Fish. He asked if
there was a final agreement regarding the specific entities. Poe said the Industrial
Siting Council requires a mitigation agreement with Game and Fish prior to the
issuance of a permit.
Arnold said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval with the
adoption of findings of fact a, b and c with two conditions: 1) An approved Industrial
Siting Division permit from the WY Dept of Environmental Quality must be received
prior to issuance of a Large Wind Energy Systems permit and 2) All agency
comments are to be addressed accordingly.
Acting Chairman Thompson opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT.
