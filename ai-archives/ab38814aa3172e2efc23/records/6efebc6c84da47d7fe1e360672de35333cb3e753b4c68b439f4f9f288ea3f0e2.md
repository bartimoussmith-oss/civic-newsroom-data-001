Laramie County Government has been awarded the Department of Justice, Office of Justice Programs 2023
BYRNE JUSTICE ASSISTANCE(JAG)grant.Laramie County and the City of Cheyenne will useJAGfunds
for duty gear equipment, NIBRS compliance, and community policing overtime. Specifically, the Laramie
County Sheriff's Office will use JAG funds to purchase duty belt gear for patrol officers along with using a
portion of the funds for NIBRS compliance.The City of Cheyenne Police Department will use JAG funds to
support community policing programs/overtime expenses.
The Project Period is from 10/1/2023 to 9/30/2026.The federal award amount is $77,820, of which the City
willreceive$57,998.
For your review and approval, attached is the 2023 Byrne Justice Assistance Grant Subgrantee Agreement
between Laramie County and the City of Cheyenne and the required Certifications and Assurances bythe Chief
Executiveof the Applicant Government form.
If you would like additional information about the JAG grant application and award packet, pleasereach out
