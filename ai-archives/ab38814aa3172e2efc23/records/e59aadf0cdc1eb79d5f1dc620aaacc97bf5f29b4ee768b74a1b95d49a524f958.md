ORDINANCE NO.__________

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 8.40.010, PURPOSE – AUTHORITY –
GENERAL PROVISIONS, OF CHAPTER 8.40, FOOD AND FOOD SERVICE
ESTABLISHMENTS, OF TITLE 8, HEALTH AND SAFETY, OF THE CODE OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, BY CREATING A NEW SUBSECTION
8.40.010(D) EXEMPTING BEVERAGE STANDS SELLING LEMONADE, ICED TEA
OR SIMILAR BEVERAGES, OPERATED BY MINORS UNDER THE AGE OF
EIGHTEEN (18) YEARS, FROM THE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER.”

BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 8.40.010, Purpose – Authority – General Provisions, of Chapter 8.40, Food
and Food Service Establishments, of Title 8, Health and Safety, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, be amended by creating a new subsection (D) as set out in this ordinance.

8.40.010 Purpose – Authority – General provisions.

D.
MINORS UNDER THE AGE OF EIGHTEEN (18) YEARS OPERATING A
BEVERAGE STAND SELLING LEMONADE, ICED TEA OR SIMILAR BEVERAGES ON
PROPERTY OWNED, LEASED OR RENTED BY THEIR PARENTS OR LEGAL GUARDIANS
ARE EXEMPT FROM THE PROVISIONS OF THIS CHAPTER.

Section 2.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its passage and publication.
