```text
FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Monday. July 1,2024, in Committee Room 104
and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Chairwoman; Bryan Cook; and Jeff White
(via Zoom). Absent: Richard Johnson.
AGENDA ITEMS
16.
RESOLUTION
— Authorizing the submission of an application by the City of Cheyenne
for federal funding through the Transportation Alternatives Program administered by the
Wyoming Department of Transportation for the purpose of construction for a Greenway
path for pedestrians and bicycles from the Sun Valley Open Space into Kiwanis
Community Park. (SPONSOR
— MR. LAYBOURN)
Mr. Cook moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. White. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Jean Anderson—Shrednik, Parks & Greenway Planner, provided a staff report. Chairwoman
Aldrich and committee member White commented on the item. Ward I Councilman and sponsor
of the item, Pete Laybourn, commented on the item.
17.
RESOLUTION
— Supporting the Cheyenne Regional Airport Board in the application for
and acceptance of federal assistance from the Federal Aviation Administration under the
Bipartisan Infrastructure Law to assist in the rehabilitation of the air traffic control tower.
(SPONSOR - DR. ALDRICH)
Mr. Cook moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. White, Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Jennifer Nelson. Cheyenne Regional Airport Director of Finance, provided an overview of the
proposed grant and committee member Cook commented on the item.
18.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
h)
Grant of Utility Easement between the City of Cheyenne and Cheyenne Light. Fuel
and Power Company d/b/a Black Hills Energy located on a portion of Lot 8. Block
1. Centennial Heritage 2”” Filing, City owned property.
Mr. Cook moved to approve, seconded by Mr. White. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Jean Anderson—Shrednik. Parks & Greenway Planner, provided a staff report and Chairwoman
Aldrich commented on the item.
```
