BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from County X (Exempt) to SS
(Support Services) zoning district for the area commonly referred to as the ‘Enhanced Use
Lease Area (EUL).’ The area is proposed to be annexed to the City of Cheyenne and
therefore requires a City zone. The EUL is anticipated to be open to the public and to
include a mix of commercial, office, and multifamily development.

A UDC text amendment creating the SS zoning district was reviewed by the Planning
Commission at their October 5, 2020 Planning Commission meeting and was approved by
the Governing Body at their November 12th meeting.  The purpose of the new zone district
was to a create a new district that recognizes needed flexibility for government owned
lands associated with Francis E. Warren Air Force Base that may develop as civilian-
accessible property within the City limits.  The standard zone districts in the City do not
necessarily contemplate the unique land ownership structure (leases and other
arrangements) that may accompany development.

On February 3, 2020 the Planning Commission reviewed a proposed zone change to MUB
(Mixed Use Business) zoning district and recommended the Governing Body approve the
change.  At the request of the landowner, this was not forwarded onto the Governing Body
for consideration.

On November 2, 2020 the Planning Commission accepted withdrawal of a proposed change
UDC-20-00443.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This area is within the Urban Service Boundary and there are existing
infrastructure lines in the area; however, the development of the site would require
infrastructure improvements and investment.

This area, and the area to the north of this property is designated as Military/Federal on the
Future Land Use Map of PlanCheyenne. The Military/Federal designation is not discussed by the
Plan and is devoid of recommendations.

The triangle of land south of this property is designated Urban Residential, the rest of the area
directly to the south is designated Mixed-use Residential, and areas to the east (across I-25) are
designated Mixed-use Commercial and Mixed-use Employment. Mixed-use zoning is anticipated
for all of the mixed-use future land use designations and MUB is the principally suggested zone
for areas designated Mixed-use Commercial. While there is a triangle of land designated Urban
Residential, that triangle is currently zoned County MU and residential development is an
anticipated component of areas zoned MUB.

PlanCheyenne designates the area east of I-25 and south of Missile Drive as the center of a
Regional Activity Center. Regional Activity Centers are intended to develop with large-scale
commercial uses which service the entire region, with a mix of office, restaurants, and
residential as secondary uses. The CB and MUB zones are encouraged in these activity centers,
and MUB zoning is proposed with this zone change. This property is not located in the central
portion of the activity center but is within ½ mile and is adjacent to a freeway interchange.

The SS zone district supports mixed-use development and regional activity center aspects of the
area.   The proposed zone change is in accordance with the plans and policies outlined in
PlanCheyenne and complies with review criteria 1.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

The subject property and areas to the north are currently zoned X (Exempt) by Laramie County.
This zoning designation does not appear in, and is not subject to, the Laramie County Land Use
Regulations (2019). As the X zone is not subject to local regulation it is not possible to determine
exactly how compatible the proposed SS zoning is with the X zone district. However, the area
zoned X adjacent to the proposed zone change is developed with single-family housing, a school,
and vacant land. The general development pattern is reflective of City LR or MR zoning. SS
zoning can act as a transition zone from intense commercial zones to residential zones, so this
property being zoned SS could act as a transition zone from the CB and LI east of I-25 to the
residential development northwest of Happy Jack Road.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

This property is currently zoned X in the County, which designation does not appear in, and is
not subject to, the Laramie County Land Use Regulations (2019). Annexation to the City requires
a City zone. The City zone most analogous to the County X zone is the M Military District zone.
The M district is intended for property owned and controlled by the United States Government
(further known as F.E. Warren AFB), and specifically states that areas zoned M are not subject to
local regulation. The Air Force has indicated they will follow City development regulations for
development of portions of lands owned by them making the M zone district not appropriate.

The proposed zone for this property is the SS zone which is intended to recognize needed
flexibility for government owned lands associated with major F. E Warren Air Force Base that
may develop as civilian-accessible property within the City limits.   This zone encourages a mix of
commercial, office, and residential uses.

The proposed zone change complies with review criteria 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)
