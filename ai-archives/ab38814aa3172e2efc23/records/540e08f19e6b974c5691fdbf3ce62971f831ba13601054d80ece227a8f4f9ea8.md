The purpose of this Agreement is to jointly create a Human Services Advisory Council,
here in after referred to as“Council,”to coordinate and refine human services funding and delivery
systems now in effect and those initiated as outlined in the Powers and Duties section of this
Agreement.The Council will work to initiate a common application process, to conduct joint
hearings on funding requests, and to establish policy recommendations concerning future funding
and delivery of human services for review by the appropriate governing bodies.
The Council will develop and propose both long- and short-term improvements that
increase the efficiency in human service program delivery as well as maximize the effectiveness
of the funding available for human services.The Council will also work to establish and
