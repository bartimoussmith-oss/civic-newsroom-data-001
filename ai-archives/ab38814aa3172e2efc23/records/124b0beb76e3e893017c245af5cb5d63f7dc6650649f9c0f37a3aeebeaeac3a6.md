ORDINANCE NO. _________
ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SECTION 2.76.020, MEMBERS–TERM–
VACANCIES, OF CHAPTER 2.76, DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT
AUTHORITY, OF TITLE 2, ADMINISTRATION AND PERSONNEL, OF
THE CODE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING.”
BE IT HEREBY ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE WYOMING:
Section 1.  That Section 2.76.020, Members–Terms–Vacancies, of Chapter 2.76, Downtown
Development Authority, of Title 2, Administration and Personnel, of the code of the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby amended as set out in this ordinance.  Provisions currently existing
in Chapter 2.76 not set out within this ordinance remain unchanged.
Section 2.76.020
Members–Terms–Vacancies.
A.
The reorganized authority shall consist of newly appointed board members
numbering not less than five nor more than eleven (11) members appointed initially by the Mayor
and subsequently by the governing body.  The affairs of the authority shall be under the direct
supervision and control of a the board consisting of ten (10) members appointed by the mayor with
the advice and consent of the governing body.  A majority of the members, except any member from
the governing body, shall reside, be a lessee or own property in the downtown development district.
B.
The board shall be constituted as follows:
1.
At least one member shall be a member of the governing body appointed to
serve at the pleasure of the governing body;
2.
Nine members shall be appointed by the mayor in accordance with the by-law
of the downtown development authority.  The remaining members shall be appointed to
staggered terms in accordance with Wyo. Stat. Section 15-9-205(b).
C.
A member shall hold office until his or her successor has been appointed and
qualified.  After the terms of the initial members have expired, the terms of all members, except the
appointed member of the governing body, shall expire four years from the expiration date of the
terms of their predecessors.  Appointments to fill vacancies shall be for the unexpired term, and shall
be made within thirty (30) days after the occurrence of the vacancy.
D.
Board members shall serve without compensation, but may be reimbursed for actual
and necessary expenses.
E.
If the board enters into a cooperative agreement with any other entity to carry out all,
or any part, of its duties, a positive vote of a majority of the members in attendance at any meeting
of the cooperative shall be required on all matters affecting the statutory authority of the board.
Section 2.  That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its approval and
publication.
