Section 1.  That Chapter 8.68, Limiting the Use of Single Use Plastic Bags, of Title 8,
Health and Safety, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby created as set out in this
ordinance.

Chapter 8.68 - LIMITING THE USE OF SINGLE USE PLASTIC BAGS

8.68.010 – Purpose.

The City of Cheyenne has the responsibility to protect the economy, natural environment and
health of its citizens and guests and single use plastic bags create waste, pollute the city’s
waterways, roadways, landscapes and harm wildlife populations. Accordingly, the purpose of this
chapter is to abate the nuisance caused by the proliferation of single use plastic bags by eliminating
the use of these bags by all retail establishments within the jurisdiction of the City of Cheyenne.

8.68.020 – Definitions; applicability.

A. As used in this chapter:

1. "City" means the city of Cheyenne as defined by section 1.04.020 of the city code;

2. "Department" means the compliance department for the city of Cheyenne;

3. "Paper Grocery bag means a paper bag that contains a minimum of forty percent
(40%) post consumer recycled content and is one hundred percent (100%)
recyclable;

4. "Retail establishment" means any retail business in a commercial setting that sells
consumer goods of any kind directly to a customer, including and without
limitation, clothing, household goods or personal items;

5. "Reusable bag" means a bag with stitched handles that is specifically designed and
manufactured for multiple reuse and is either:

a. Made of cloth or other machine washable fabric; or

5. Bags used by a consumer inside a retail establishment to:

b. Contain or wrap frozen foods, meat or fish;

c. Contain or wrap flowers, potted plants or other items in order to prevent
moisture damage to other purchases; or

d. Contain unwrapped prepared foods or bakery goods.

8.68.030 - Single use plastic bags prohibited; environmental service fee; penalty.

A. No retail establishment shall provide or distribute a single use plastic bag to any
customer. Retail establishments may only provide reusable bags, paper grocery bags or
cardboard boxes at checkout to a customer. To further promote the use of reusable bags
and reduce the quantity of single use carryout bags entering the city's waste stream,
retail establishments are encouraged to make reusable bags free or for sale at checkout
counters and to provide boxes for use at checkout counters free of charge.

B. For any paper grocery bag provided to a customer by a retail establishment, the
establishment shall charge the customer an environmental service fee of ten cents ($.10)
per bag. Retail establishments shall specify on the customer transaction receipt the
number of paper grocery bags provided and the total amount of the environmental
service fees charged. It shall be a violation of this section for any retail establishment

8.68.040 - Enforcement, penalties and administrative remedies.

A. The compliance department is assigned administrative responsibility of this chapter.
The department shall be authorized to take any and all other actions reasonably
necessary to enforce this chapter, including but not limited to investigating alleged
violations of this chapter, including entering the premises of any retail establishment
during business hours.

B. If the director of the department determines that a violation of this chapter has occurred,
the department shall issue to the retail establishment a written warning advising the
establishment of the violation and that any subsequent and substantially similar
violation may subject the retail establishment to the criminal penalties specified under
subsection (C) of this section.

C. A retail establishment that violates any of the requirements imposed under this chapter,
after having received a written warning issued in accordance with subsection (B) of
this section, is guilty of a misdemeanor and punishable by fine of one hundred dollars
($100.00), for which the operator of the retail establishment shall be liable. Each day
of a continuing violation shall constitute a distinct and separate offense.
