______________________________________________
Bid #E-18-23 was solicited for Mosquito Site Testing and Mosquito Larvicide Application for a
Two (2) Year Term.

One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Bid was received from:
1. Vector Disease Control International (VDCI).  Little Rock, AR.

The bid was reviewed by Steven Trowbridge (Weed & Pest), and Jason Sanchez (Community
Recreation & Events), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from
Vector Disease Control International (VDCI).

The Bid Award amount is requested for $111,334.00 per year, for a Two (2) Year total of
$222,668.00.

This project was bid cooperatively so that Laramie County can participate in the Mosquito Site
Testing and Mosquito Larvicide Application as well.  They will Contract & Award their portion
separately, but the same vendor will be treating both City & County Sites.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
for
MOSQUITO SITE TESTING AND MOSQUITO LARVICIDE
APPLICATION

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and
between the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
(“City”), and Vector Disease Control International (VDCI), located at 1320 Brookwood Drive,
Suite H, Little Rock, AR 72202 (“Consultant”).  This Agreement shall become effective upon
the date of the last signature affixed hereto.

RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by and
between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services for the Mosquito Site Testing and Mosquito
Larvicide Application Project, in accordance with City of Cheyenne Invitation to Bid
(ITB) #E-18-23, incorporated herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted bid
proposal, incorporated herein by reference.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by December 31, 2024.

3.
COMPENSATION
In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement, the
Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed amount
of One Hundred Eleven Thousand, Three Hundred Thirty-Four Dollars ($111,334.00) per
year, for a two (2) year term total not to exceed Two Hundred Twenty-Two Thousand,
Six Hundred Sixty-Eight Dollars ($222,668.00).

4.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services
not meeting such a standard.

5.
COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.
