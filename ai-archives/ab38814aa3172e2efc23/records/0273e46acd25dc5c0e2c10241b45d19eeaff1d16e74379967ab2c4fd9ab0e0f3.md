RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

May 16, 2018

A special meeting of the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne was held on this date
beginning at 6:35 p.m. (immediately following a special City Council Finance Committee
meeting) in City Council Chambers for the purpose of acting upon the following:  Consideration
of Bid #S-14-18 for a contract between the City of Cheyenne, Engineering Department, and
Reiman Corp, for the Civic Center Commons Park Project (1% Specific Purpose Sales Tax;
FEMA, SLIB; Wyoming Business Council grants).  Present were:  MAYOR – Marian J. Orr;
COUNCIL MEMBERS – Rocky Case, Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn, Mike Luna, Dr. Mark
Rinne, Scott Roybal, Dicky Shanor and Jeff White.  Absent:  Bryan Cook.  Also present:  Carol
Intlekofer, City Clerk, and Sylvia Hackl, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. White moved to approve the bid submitted by
Reiman Corp, including Alternates A and C, in an amount not to exceed $6,833,524.21, seconded
by Mr. Roybal.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present with
the exception of Mr. Johnson voting “no”. (#6839)

Mayor Orr stated the purpose of the special meeting had been met, and there being no
business previously noted for the special meeting, adjourned the meeting at 6:38 p.m.

Submitted by,

Carol Intlekofer, MMC

City Clerk

This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle

May 29, 2018
