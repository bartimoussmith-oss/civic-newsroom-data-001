```text
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
February 17, 2009
Due to the President’s Day holiday on February 16, 2009, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne
instead convened in regular session on this date at 12:00 p.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of acting upon
vouchers for payment as presented. Those present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS -
Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Absent: Mr.
Collins. The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Also present were Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.
 Mr. Brown moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Pierson.  Mr. Valdez
declared a conflict of interest due to a voucher payable to KYOY ($250.00), advertising, included in the voucher
listing, and vacated Council Chambers.  Upon inquiry, City Treasurer Barb Dorr provided information available
for a voucher payable to Physio Control, Inc. ($1,030.00), heart monitors for use by Fire and Rescue Department
EMTs/paramedics, noting funding was provided through a Homeland Security grant.  Motion carried.  Voting
“yes” – all members of the governing body present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote,
Mr. Valdez returned to Council Chambers.
Purpose of the meeting having been met, Mayor Kaysen adjourned the meeting at 12:04 p.m.
Submitted by,
Carol Intlekofer, MMC
City Clerk
This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request
Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle
February 21, 2009
```
