City Engineer Concurrence of Selection
Upon review of the information provided by Mr. Domenic Bravo, CEO Visit Cheyenne, as well as the
current Statement of Qualifications on file, I would concur with the selection of the Martin/ Martin
Wyoming, Inc.  team to provide the above referenced professional services.  Furthermore, I would
state that the basis for award generally followed our Qualification based selection process.

Description of Project
The project consists of a new pedestrian bridge over the Union Pacific Railroad (UPRR) with the
purpose of providing access to existing trails and recreation areas currently owned by the City of
Cheyenne.  The proposed bridge is to be in Laramie County and east of railroad mile post 539.41.
The railroad right of way in this location is approximately 400 feet in total width.  The bridge
auxiliary components will include gravel pedestrian and bike paths from existing trails located
within 300 feet of the crossing, bridge abutments, elevated south abutment, and accompany
superstructure and/ or grading to provide pedestrian trail access to the existing trail system.  The
bridge structure is anticipated to be an approximately comprised of one to two (1 to 2) 120 foot
single-span, single-unit prefabricated steel truss superstructures, which will be developed as a
performance specification by Martin/Martin Wyoming, Inc.

Funding and Cost
The funding for this project shall be in the amount of up to One Hundred Thousand (100,000)
dollars from the Annexation Fund to support economic development in Cheyenne, pursuant to City
Council Resolution No. 5240 approved on September 13, 2021, for Visit Cheyenne to implement the
plan and engineering.  Supplemental funding for the remainder of the contract of $85,700 shall be
allocated from revenue from the Belvoir Ranch Recreation Special Revenue funding.   The contract
totals $185,700.
