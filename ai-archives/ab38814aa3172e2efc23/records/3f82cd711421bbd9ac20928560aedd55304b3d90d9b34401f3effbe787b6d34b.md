```text
RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

November 25, 2019

A special meeting of the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne was held on this date
beginning at 5:45 p.m. in Council Chambers for the purpose of reviewing the [Proposed] Findings
of Facts, Conclusions of Law, and Order in the matter of China Spa vs. the City of Cheyenne,
relative to the emergent suspension/revocation of a Massage Business Establishment License.
(Case #GB-01-19) Present were: COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Case, Council President, Mr.
Cook, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Luna, Mr. Roybal, and Mr. White.   Absent: MAYOR:  Marian J. Orr,
Mr. Esquibel, Dr. Rinne, and Mr. Shanor.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Also present were: Kris Jones, City Clerk; and Joe Chenchar, Assistant City Attorney,
respondent, providing legal representation on behalf of the City’s administration; and Robert C.
Jarosh, hearing officer and legal counsel to City Council members.

 Robert C. Jarosh, hearing officer, provided information relating to the [Proposed] Findings
of Facts, Conclusions of Law, and Order and complemented the council members who were
involved with the hearing process and noted the sensitive nature of the hearing and time involved.
Mr. Case expressed appreciation to Mr. Jarosh for his professionalism and time commitment to
the case.  Mr. Roybal moved to accept the Findings of Facts, Conclusions of Law, and Order as
written, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” all members of the governing body
present.

 Purpose of the special meeting having been met, Council President Case adjourned the
meeting at 5:48 p.m.

Submitted by,

Kristina F. Jones,

City Clerk

This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle

December 4, 2019
```
