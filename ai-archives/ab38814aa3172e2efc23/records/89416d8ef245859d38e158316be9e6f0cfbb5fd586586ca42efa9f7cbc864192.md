BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for a 15.38-acre parcel of land to create 89 lots for
Cluster Home (Attached) Development. There will be 12 open space lots with two of them being existing City
owned Greenway. The land is currently vacant.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is zoned as part of the Sweetgrass PUD.  The Sweetgrass PUD is generally described as “a
±2,349.5-acre community envisioned as a distinctive mixed-use master planned village, integrating
retail, entertainment, lodging, employment opportunities, and variety of intimately scaled residential
neighborhoods.”  The district generally allows a broad range of uses. The HDR (High Density Residential)
land use category is located in close proximity to Laramie County Community College and planned retail
/ commercial development along College Drive. These parcels are intended to provide for residential
development to support the student and employee housing needs associated with the community
college and surrounding community in the form of apartments, townhomes, and more affordable single
family detached dwellings. To promote greater diversity, lower density development, such as that
permitted within MDR and LDR districts, is permitted within HDR districts.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Expedited Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d and UDC 2.1.3.d a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

This development is in conformance with the principles and future land uses identified in
PlanCheyenne. The property is within the Urban Service Boundary and has access to all utilities.

The future land use map within PlanCheyenne identifies this area as developing with a
residential use. This plat proposes a replat of an existing lot to allow for townhouse
development.

Foundation 1 of PlanCheyenne encourages community growth. This development is in response
to market demands and is within the Urban Service Boundary. The development patterns
proposed by this plat are compatible with the designations found on the future land use map.

Foundation 2 of PlanCheyenne encourages new residential development to be compatible and
connected with existing residential neighborhoods. The proposed plat is a replat of an existing
lot that is adjacent to other residential developments and was planned to be a townhouse
development.

Foundation 6 of PlanCheyenne encourages the creation of open space and trails and the
preservation of significant natural resources. The plat proposes dedicated open space lots that
will be a part of an integrated trail system throughout Sweetgrass along with internal open
space areas for the residents of the development.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2)

This is a replat of an existing lot with in Sweetgrass, 6th Filing. Utilities and facilities are already
within the area due to the existing developments in the area. Additional Public right of way is
being proposed with this plan. Accesses are spaced properly along Sweetgrass Drive and East
Murray Road. This parcel is within the HDR zone in Sweetgrass.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
