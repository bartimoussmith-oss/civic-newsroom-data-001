GRANTOR further grants to GRANTEE the right to ingress and to egress from the above
described land and covenant that they shall, in no way, hinder or prevent the proper and
reasonable use and enjoyment of said property by GRANTEE.

GRANTEE and GRANTOR have executed a Driveway, Drainage, and Board of Public
Utilities (BOPU) Utility Easement Maintenance Agreement stating maintenance responsibilities
of the parties.

In the event of an excavation in the above-described easement by the GRANTOR, the
GRANTOR shall be responsible to backfill and grade such right-of-way to the specifications as
defined and adopted by City, and any costs for said restoration shall be borne by City, their
successors and assigns. However, City shall not be required to restore any improvements, such as
pavement or vegetation. Any costs for restoration beyond backfill and grading shall be borne by
GRANTEE, their successors and assigns.

The rights granted herein shall forever be subject to the rights of the City of Cheyenne, its
assigns or lessees to explore for, develop, and extract any and all minerals or other subsurface
resources beneath this easement. If required for mineral exploration, development or extraction,
the GRANTEE shall, upon written notice from the GRANTOR, remove or relocate at its own
expense the above described assets.

Upon abandonment or discontinuance of use of this easement for the purposes specified
above, all of GRANTEE’S rights under this grant of easement shall revert to the City of
Cheyenne or its assigns, the same as if this grant had never been made. Should this easement be
abandoned by the GRANTEE, GRANTEE shall remove said assets and the above described tract
of land shall be returned to a condition satisfactory to the landowner.

The easement may be transferred; however, no transfer may increase the burden on the
servient estate in any manner.

Any transfer of ownership of this easement, or any change of name or mailing address of
the owner of this easement, shall be reported to the GRANTOR in writing within thirty (30) days
of the transfer or change.

The GRANTEE releases and agrees to save and hold harmless GRANTOR, its officers,
agents and employees, from any causes of action or claims or demands arising out of this grant
of easement, except to the extent any such cause of action or claims or demands are the result of
the negligence or willful misconduct of the GRANTOR, its agents, or employees.

Included in this grant shall be the right to lay, construct, maintain, operate, alter, repair,
remove, change the size of, and replace at any time the assets laid by GRANTEE.
