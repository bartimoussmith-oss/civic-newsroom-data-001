MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Sam Crowley, Planner I
Subject: A Resolution authorizing the expansion of the Capitol North Historic District and the
addition of one hundred nineteen (119) structures to the contributing structures list
maintained by the City’s Planning Office.
Date:
June 23, 2021

Background:
The item for consideration is a Resolution.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral

6/28/2021
•
Public Services Committee

7/07/2021
•
Second and final reading

7/12/2021

Summary:

In 2019, the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board decided to pursue expanding the Capitol North
Historic District. A consultant, Terracon Surveying, was hired to evaluate the potential for expansion of
the existing Capitol North Historic District listed in the National Register of Historic Places. The
boundaries that were surveyed for consideration of the district expansion are from W. 29th Street north
to Pershing Blvd and Capitol Avenue west to O’Neil and Bent Avenues. The consultant completed the
survey, identifying 119 Wyoming Cultural Properties and updating the National Register Nomination for
the district with those properties. The project was not completed as proposed due to an error in
administrative procedures as the nomination was not recorded with the County Clerk within 90 days of
the ratification. In 2021, the legal process was reopened by the Planning and Development Department
and the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board. This resolution serves to ratify the nomination to expand
the district. The previous survey and list of Wyoming Cultural Properties will be applied. All legal
procedures referred to in Cheyenne Municipal Code Chapter 2.64.070 have been followed. These
procedures include the initiation of a petition by the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board, mailed
notice of the proposed designation, a neighborhood meeting, approval of 50% of all property owners in
the district, a public hearing, and a second mailed notice. The original survey and map are included in
this packet.

Attachments:

2. Exhibit A: Terracon Consulting Survey

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board is authorized by Section 2.64.070

WHEREAS, it is the stated public policy of the City of Cheyenne’s governing body that
the protection, enhancement and perpetuation of areas, improvements and landscape features
having historic character or special historical or aesthetic interest or value is a public necessity;
and

WHEREAS, the City Code stipulates that the governing body must ratify the designation
of any area, building, structure or other feature as being worthy of preservation; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne originally nominated the properties located within the
Capitol North Historic District for consideration of listing to the National Register of Historic
Places in 1980; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne received a grant to survey the properties in the
existing district and updated the Contributing Structures List in 2015; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board received a grant and hired
a consultant during 2018 to conduct a survey of the properties within the expanded area,
generally from 29th Street north to Pershing Boulevard and Bent Avenue east to Warren Avenue;
and

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Historic Preservation Board approved the recommended
updates to the Contributing Structures List which added one hundred nineteen (119) structures
within the expanded Capitol North Historic District, as shown in Exhibit A.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City ratifies the addition of one hundred nineteen
(119) structures within the expanded Capitol North Historic District and does officially update
the City’s Contributing Structure List to be protected in accordance with City Code.

The governing body of the City of Cheyenne is required by City Code to approve by resolution
all properties to be added to and removed from the City’s Contributing Structures List.

Facts bearing on this request are as follows:

1.
The city originally nominated this district to the National Register of Historic Places in
1980; it is the oldest residential neighborhood listed on the National Register in
Cheyenne.

2.
The CHPB applied for a Certified Local Government Grant in November 2017 to expand
the existing district. The grant was approved by the State Historic Preservation Office in
December 2017 for the amount of $12,500.00.  The expanded area in general was from
29th Street north to Pershing Boulevard and Bent Avenue east to Warren Avenue.

3.
A Request For Proposal (RFP) was sent out in February 2018 to consultants; the project
was awarded in April 2018.

4.
The first public hearing was held on June 6, 2018 in Pando Park to inform the
neighborhood of the project work that was about to begin. The neighbors were notified
via letter through the USPS and a notice was published in the Wyoming Tribune-Eagle;
there were thirteen (13) neighbors in attendance.

5.
The consultant performed the necessary work over the summer.  A second public
hearing was held September 11, 2018 at the Municipal Building to update the
neighborhood on the status of the survey and the draft report.  The neighbors were again
notified via letter through the USPS and a notice was published in the Wyoming
Tribune-Eagle; there were four (4) neighbors in attendance.

6.
Further refining of the survey forms and the draft National Register of Historic Places
nomination was needed.  A third public meeting was held on April 9, 2019 at the
Municipal Building to inform the neighbors of the contributing/noncontributing status of
the properties. The neighbors were notified via letter through the USPS; there were five
(5) neighbors in attendance.

7.
A fourth public meeting was held on May 14, 2019 at the Municipal Building for any
final comments from the Board or the neighbors regarding the boundary,
contributing/noncontributing list or the draft nomination.  The neighbors were notified
