Section 1.
That a new Chapter 12.28, Downtown Graffiti Abatement Pilot Program,
of Title 12, Streets, Sidewalks and Public Places, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, is hereby created as set out in this ordinance.
Chapter 12.28
DOWNTOWN GRAFFITI ABATEMENT PILOT PROGRAM
12.28.010
Creation.
There is hereby created the “Downtown Graffiti Abatement Pilot Program” (Pilot
Program).
12.28.020
Definitions.
The following definitions shall apply in the interpretation and enforcement of this
chapter:
A.
“Downtown Development Authority” (DDA) means the body corporate created
by the City of Cheyenne pursuant to Chapter 2.76 of the Municipal Code of the City of
Cheyenne.
B.
“Graffiti” means any unauthorized inscription, word, figure, painting or other
defacement that is written, marked, etched, scratched, sprayed, drawn, painted or engraved on or
otherwise affixed to any surface or public or private property, to the extent that the graffiti was
not given written authorization in advance by the property owner, or, despite written advance
authorization, is otherwise deemed a public nuisance by the Downtown Development Authority.
C.
“Property” means any real property, including structures and fixtures.
D.
“Property Owner” means a person, real or corporate, partnership or other business
venture owning any property within the boundaries of the downtown development district as set
forth in Section 2.76.040.
E.
“Structure” means any improvement to real property and shall include, but not be
limited to, buildings, walls, doors, utility boxes, sidewalks, windows, fixtures, sheds, storage
units, semi-tractor trailers, mobile storage and masonry.
12.28.030
Prohibited Acts.
It shall be unlawful for any person to apply graffiti to any natural or man-made surface on
any city-owned property or, without the permission of the property owner on any non-city
owned property.
12.28.040
Program Creation and Opt-Outln.
A.
The DDA shall develop and implement the Pilot Program inside the boundaries of
the downtown development district designated in Section 2.76.040.

12.28.050
Removal of Graffiti by DDA.
A.
Whenever the DDA determines that graffiti exists within the boundaries of the
downtown development district on property belonging to property owners that opted in to the
Pilot Program which is visible to the public, the DDA may remove such graffiti to the extent
allowed within their annual line item budget.
B.
Graffiti may be removed from publicly owned property and privately owned
property belonging to property owners that opted in to the Pilot Program pursuant to the
following provisions:
1.
Use of Public funds.
Whenever the DDA becomes aware or is notified
and determines that graffiti is located on publicly or privately owned property viewable
from a public or quasi-public place, the DDA shall be authorized to use DDA funds for
the removal of the graffiti, or for the painting over, power cleaning, or repairing of the
graffiti, but is not authorized to provide for the painting or repair of any more extensive
an area than where the graffiti is located.
2.
Right of Entry on Private Property.
All property located within the
boundaries of the downtown development district owned by property owners who fail to
opt-out of in to the Pilot Program as set forth in Section 12.28.0340 will be subject to
graffiti abatement by the DDA.
3.
The DDA may only abate graffiti existing on property within the
downtown development district owned by property owners who did not opt-out of in to
the Pilot Program.
4.
No graffiti determined to exist on property owned by property owners who
elect to opt-out of the Pilot Program or who fail to respond to the DDA’s notice as set
forth in Section 12.28.040 shall be abated by the DDA.
