BACKGROUND:

With the original plat in 1995, the area within this plat was designated specifically for drainage.
However, with the actual development of the property, the drainage plan was reconfigured. Due to
these changes, only a small portion of the property was transferred to the City for drainage purposes
(such transfer being common in that era) and the remainder of the property was sold off for
development. The property was developed with one house, and the owner is now requesting
subdivision of the remainder. The development requested is to final plat 11 single-unit residential lots
on this site. The plat and the development of the property must meet all current applicable
requirements, including site-specific drainage.

The preliminary plat was denied by the planning commission due to failure to meet review criteria 2 and
8. The applicant appealed the denial, and the City Council overturned the Planning Commission denial
and approved the preliminary plat (with conditions). Approval of the preliminary plat allows the
applicant to submit a final plat subject to final plat review criteria.

This application has submitted an Engineering Plan Review (EPR) to develop the property. In review of
the engineering for the property, there are some concerns about drainage. These concerns will be
addressed through the EPR process. The Engineering Department will not allow any development
without the final approval of the EPR. Should any minor modification need to be made to the plat they
can be address with an Administrative Plat. Such modifications are common on subdivisions through out
the community.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of final plat, the Commission must make
the following findings required in UDC 2.1.3.d a detailed analysis is below. The purpose of a final plat
review is to ensure compliance with the approved preliminary plat and its conditions of approval.

1) The layout and design of the final plat is in substantial compliance with the approved
preliminary plat considering the number of lots or parcels, street and block layout, and access,
and any deviations in the final plat brings the application in further compliance with the
Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.3.d.1)
