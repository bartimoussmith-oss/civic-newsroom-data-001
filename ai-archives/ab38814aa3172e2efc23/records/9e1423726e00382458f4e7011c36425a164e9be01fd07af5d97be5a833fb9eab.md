RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
June 15, 2020
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. via WebEx Electronic Conference Meeting for the purpose of collective
labor bargaining with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire
Fighters, Local No. 279. Present were: MAYOR
— Marian J. On; COUNCIL MEMBERS
— Mr.
Case, Mr. Cook, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, Mr. Shanor and
Mr. White. Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Michael O’Donnell, City Attorney; Alessandra
McCoy Fakelman, Deputy City Attorney; Robin Lockman, City Treasurer and Chief Greg
Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.
Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings Act allows,
Mayor On advised the governing body would be going into executive session to consider or tender
offers regarding salaries, wages or other terms and conditions of employment during negotiations
with the fire fighters union and that any motion approved by the governing body to move into
executive session during the special meetings would be pursuant to this section of statute. Mayor
On further explained when the governing body met in executive session, they would be meeting
in a private electronic conference, and that Fire Union representatives will meet privately off-line
for the purpose of meeting to caucus.
Michael O’Donnell, City Attorney, provided a review and the results as negotiated from
the June
1 1th Special Meeting of the Governing Body for the purpose of Collective Labor
Bargaining with Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters,
Local No. 279. David Evans, Attorney representing the Union, confirmed the last offer made by
the Union is as follows:
add back in 3 Firefighters-$266,727.00, add back in Longevity pay-
$139,610.00, subtract administrative savings per Chief Hoggatt-$30,000.00,
subtract Fire
Administration and Suppression Uniform line items-$48,650.00, subtract savings due to EOD
change 4 not 3 slots (with sunset date)-$62,000.00, subtract base salaries by 1 .5%-$1 11,058.00,
subtract reduction in force by attrition-$91,615.00, use reserves (approved by Governing Body on
June 10, 2020)-$57,687.00, and additional needed from reserves-$5,3 17.00.
Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute § 1 6-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Roybal moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Mr. White. Motion carried. Voting “yes”
— all members of the
governing body. Mayor On advised that in addition to members of the governing body present,
the City Clerk, City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer and Chief of the Fire and
Rescue Department would also be present at the executive session.
[City Clerk note: Audio
recording of the special meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 6:07 p.m. via
electronic conference meeting]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
6:23 p.m. Mayor Orr reported the City proposes to accept the modified Union offer with the
exception that the EOD slots be reduced from 4 to 3.
Mr. Evans requested a recess for the purpose of going in caucus at 6:26 p.m. Following
the Union requested caucus at 7:02 p.m., Mr. Evans advised the Union counter offer is for a
proposed 1% pay cut and going to 3 EOD slots from July 1, 2020 to January 1, 2021 and then
returning to 4 EOD slots for the remainder of fiscal year 2021.
Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute § 1 6-4-405(a)(x), Mr. White moved to meet in executive
session, seconded by Mr. Case. Motion carried. Voting “yes”
— all members of the governing
body. It was clarified that in addition to members of the governing body present, the City Clerk,
City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer and Chief ofthe Fire and Rescue Department
would also be present at the executive session. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the special
meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 7:06 p.m. via electronic conference meeting]
Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:39 p.m., Mayor Orr advised the City counter offers with a 1.25% pay cut and 4 EOD slots from
July 1, 2020 to January 1, 2021 and then reducing to 3 EOD slots for the remainder of fiscal year
2021.
Mr. Evans requested a recess for purpose of going in caucus at 7:40 p.m. Following the
Union requested caucus at 8:07 p.m., Mr. Evans advised the Union’s counter offer is for a 1% pay
cut and agrees to the 4 EOD slots from July 1, 2020 to January 1, 2021 and then reducing to 3
EOD slots for the remainder of fiscal year 2021. Mr. Evans reported this would be the Union’s
final offer.
Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute § 1 6-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Luna moved to meet in executive
session, seconded by Mr. Case. Motion carried. Voting “yes”
— all members of the governing
body. It was clarified that in addition to members of the governing body present, the City Clerk,
City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer and Chief ofthe Fire and Rescue Department
would also be present at the executive session. [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the special
meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 8:11 p.m. via electronic conference meeting]
Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
8:22 p.m. Mayor Orr advised the City accepts the Union’s final counter offer as follows: add back
in 3 Firefighters, add back in Longevity pay, subtract administrative savings per Chief Hoggatt,
subtract Fire Administration and Suppression Uniform line items, subtract savings due to EOD
change of allowing for 4 slots from July 1, 2020 to January 1, 2021 and then reducing to 3 slots
for the remainder of fiscal year 2021 (with sunset date) subtract base salaries by 1%, subtract
reduction in force by 3 through attrition, use reserves (approved by Governing Body on June 10,
2020), and the additional needed from reserves. Discussion involved consensus between the City
and Union attorneys to craft the appropriate language to reflect the agreed upon changes for the
Contract Labor Agreement.
