Chairman Malm explained agenda items #27 through #31 were related and would
be read into the record first, followed by an overview of speed studies by Inberg
Miller Engineers.  Public comment would then be taken on each item, followed by a
vote of the board.
John Poelma, Public Works, introduced Gary Steele and Brooke Bulgrin of Ingberg
Miller Engineers, who conducted county road speed studies using the WY Dept of
Transportation Traffic Studies Manual.
Steele explained that a 2011 WY legislative action set default speed requirements
for rural roads and mandated a formal, legislatively compliant speed study to justify
lowering speeds below the defaults. He said the process involved placing traffic
counters on roads to measure traffic volume and drivers’ speeds. Steele described
the key metrics: 85th-percentile speed (85% of vehicles travel at or below this
speed), used as an upper reference for setting limits, and 50th-percentile speed
(median; half above/half below), used as a lower reference.
Steele said research indicates that setting speed limits too low can reduce
compliance and potentially create safety issues (e.g., driver frustration, unsafe
passing, speed differentials).
