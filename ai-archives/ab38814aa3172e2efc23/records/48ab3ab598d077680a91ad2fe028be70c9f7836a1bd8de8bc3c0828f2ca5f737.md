Regular Cab, Full Size Pickup Truck & One(1) New 2019-2020% ton Ext.Cab,
Full Size Pickup Truck, for the City of Cheyenne, Parks Division
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.
The two (2) bids were received by Fremont Motor Company, (Lander, WY), and Spradley Barr
Motors, (Cheyenne, WY).
The initial lowbidwas submitted by Fremont Motor Company; however, theirbid submission did
not comply with the requirements of the Bidding Documents, specifically the Five Percent (5%)
Bid Guarantee.Additionally, Fremont Motor Company took abid exception to the specifications.
Due to the bid guarantee not meeting the requirements of the Bidding Documents, and the
submitted bid exception, Fremont Motor Company's bid has been rejected from consideration.
The next responsive bidder is Spradley Barr Motors.Spradley Barr Motors submitted a bid in
compliance with the Bidding Documents, provided a Five Percent (5%) Bid Guarantee, and did
not take exceptions tothe specifications.
The bids were reviewed by Sam Payne (Parks), Brandon Peterson (Parks), Jason Sanchez
(Community Recreation&Events)and Bryan Bell(Fleet Maintenance).Their recommendation
is to accept the bid from Spradley Barr Motors, for a bid award amount of $67,703.00. The bid
award is for the purchase of two (2) trucks, and declining acceptance of the trade in offers.
