RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

June 16, 2020

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. via WebEx Electronic Conference Meeting.  Attending electronic
conference meeting were:  MAYOR – Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Case, Mr.
Cook, Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, Mr. Shanor and Mr. White.
Also attending: Kris Jones, City Clerk.

[Clerk’s note:  Dr. Rinne was able to hear the proceedings but was unable to communicate due to
audio difficulty. Dr. Rinne cast affirmative votes for all agenda items and amendments presented
through alternative technology methods]

ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Amending Section 5.12.110, Open Container-Sales and
Consumption-Exceptions, and Section 5.12.120, Special Use Areas-Special Event Permit of
Chapter 5.12, Alcoholic Liquor and Malt Beverages, of Title 4, Business Licenses and
Regulations, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming. Mr. Luna moved to
approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body. (#4312)

ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Amending Section 8.40.063, Food Service
Establishments-Sidewalk Cafes, of Chapter 8.40, Food and Food Service Establishments, of Title
8, Health and Safety, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming.  Mr. Luna moved
to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body. (#4313)

RESOLUTION – Creating a Special District for the ‘Summertime on the Streets Event’ to
be held June 20, 2020, in which the possession of open containers is permitted between the hours
of 11:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m.  Alessandra McCoy Fakelman, Deputy City Attorney, provided
information on the resolution and affiliated ordinance stating the items allow special areas or
districts in which the possession of open containers is permitted and certain liquor licensees can
participate. The periods of time and boundaries are established by resolution adopted by the
governing body. This particular resolution is for a special event to occur on Saturday, June 20th
from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.  Ms. McCoy-Fakelman advised that upon feedback from the Police
Chief, a proposed amendment contains specificity for designated certain types of cups, wristbands
and doesn’t allow for any outside liquor.  Amber Ash, Downtown Development Authority
Executive Director, provided event details and advised the event will be in compliance with the
governor’s health orders.  She further confirmed that compliance will be followed and
implemented in efforts to limit COVID-19 exposure.  Mr. Luna moved to adopt, seconded by Mr.
Case.  Mr. Roybal moved to amend by substitute dated June 16, 2020, seconded by Mr. White.
Mr. Roybal explained the substitute provides additional safeguards including easy to identify

ORDINANCE – 3rd READING – Appropriating moneys for the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming for various purposes in conducting the municipal government of said City and fixing
the amount of general and special taxes as part of the revenue required to meet the said

by adding footnotes #1-13 and adding language that the City Treasurer has the authority as part of
this amendment to make any technical amendments to these footnotes and the budget ordinance to
reflect the intent of these footnotes, seconded by Mr. Roybal.  Mayor Orr clarified the footnotes
are from the June 10, 2020 Committee of the Whole meeting.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting
“yes” – all members of the governing body.  Mr. White moved to amend Footnote #2 to include:
This footnote also excludes seasonal workers and does not prohibit an employee form being
promoted into a pre-existing higher position with a pre-existing higher salary, seconded by Mr.
Luna.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body. Upon inquiry,
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, confirmed there were no additional amendments needed as a
result of recent discussions with the Fire Union relating to the Fire Department’s budget.  Main
motion as twice amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  (#4314)

Mr. Luna expressed appreciation to City Treasurer Robin Lockman, City Council and City
staff members for their dedicated work on the budget.  Mayor Orr echoed Mr. Luna’s comments
and advised that due to the unprecedented times, the budget may need to be reviewed through out
