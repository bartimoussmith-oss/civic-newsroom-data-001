RESOLUTION NO.

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION DIRECTING CITY STAFF TO REPAIR THE TREE
LAWN AREA ON VANDEHEI AVENUE BETWEEN YELLOWSTONE
ROAD AND STOCKMAN STREET.”

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need to repair
the public sidewalk on Vandehei Avenue between Yellowstone Road and Stockman Street; and
WHEREAS, the area is in disrepair, dangerous to the public, and is causing damage to
private property; and
WHEREAS, and an updated irrigation system, removal of necessary trees, and
replacement of concrete are necessary to properly repair the area; and
WHEREAS, funds, up to $40,000.00  have been secured for repairs withthrough the
Community Recreation and Events One Percent Sales Tax FundsCommunity Recreation and
Events Department; and
WHERAS, additional funding, if necessary, approved by the governing body; and
WHEREAS, time is of the essence.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne directs
City Staff to initiate, develop, and execute the repairs to the tree lawn area on Vandehei Avenue
between Yellowstone Road and Stockman Street.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED this ____ day of

, 2022.

CITY OF CHEYENNE
