FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Monday, October 7, 2024, in Committee Room
104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 pm. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Dr. Michelle Aldrich. Chairwoman;
Bryan Cook; Richard
Johnson; and Jeff White (via Zoom).
AGENDA ITEMS
10.
RESOLUTION
— Re-appropriating the Fiscal Year 2024 Budget of the City of Cheyenne
according to the provisions of ‘A’S.
§*
16-4-112 &
16-4-113. (SPONSOR
— DR.
ALDRICH)
Mr. Johnson declared a conflict prior to discussion of item #10 and excused himself from the room
for the discussion and vote on the item.
Mr. Cook moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. White. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided a staff report.
11.
RESOLUTION
— Re-appropriating the Fiscal Year 2025 Budget of the City of Cheyenne
according to the provisions of W.S. §
16-4-112 &
16-4-113. (SPONSOR
- DR.
ALDRICh)
Mr. Cook moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Johnson, Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided a staff report.
14.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
a)
Memorandum of Understanding between the City of Cheyenne and the Downtown
Development Authority to coordinate repair of sidewalks identified as trip hazards.
(2022-2026 Optional 1% Sales Tax Fund)
Mr. Johnson moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $100,000.00, seconded by Mr. White.
Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Irene Parsons. Downtown Development Authority/Main Street Manager. provided a staff report
and Tom Cobb, City Engineer, provided additional information. Chairwoman Aldrich and
committee members Cook and Johnson commented on the item.

g)
Consideration of Bid #E-2-25 for ten (10) new four-door 2024/2025 Model Utility
Police Interceptors for the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department. (20 19-

Mr. Johnson moved to accept the bid from Dana Supply, Livingston, Montana, in an amount not

h)
Consideration of Bid #S-5-25 for an Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and
S & S Builders, LLC for the Pronghorn Crossing Site Infrastructure Project.
(American Rescue Plan Federal Recovery Funds & other funding to be determined)
Mr. White excused himself from the meeting at 12:44 p.m., during discussion of item #16(h) and
did not vote on the item, due to a previously scheduled work engagement.
Mr. Johnson moved to accept the bid in an amount not to exceed $2,039,808.00 with $1,742,318.00
from ARPA Funds and $297,490.00 from the 2021 6th Penny Large Project Fund, seconded by Mr.
Cook. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Paul Bellotti, City Project Manager, provided a staff report and Doug Klahn, Construction
Manager, provided additional information related to the expenses of the project.
Mayor Patrick
Collins; Amber Ash, Mayor’s Chiefof Staff; TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager; Tom Cobb, City
Engineer; and Renee Smith, Economic Resource Administrator, provided information related to
the additional funding mechanism to be used for the project noting that ultimately habitat for
Humanity of Laramie County would be responsible for any expenses beyond the grant funding.
Dan Dorsch, Habitat for Humanity Executive Director, provided additional information and
advised a memorandum of understanding would be executed regarding the grant funding and
expenses beyond that of the grant funding.
There being no further items for the agenda to come before the Finance Committee, the meeting
was adjourned at 12:58 p.m.

Jennifer McClelland
