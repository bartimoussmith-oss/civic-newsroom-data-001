ORDINANCE NO.

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE INCREASING THE COMPENSATION FOR THE
MAYOR OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, COMMENCING JANUARY 1,
2025.”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.
That Ordinance No. 3736 is repealed effective January 1, 2025.

Section 2.
Compensation.

A.
The Mayor of the City of Cheyenne shall receive compensation in the amount
One-Hundred Ten Thousand Dollars ($110,000.00) per year commencing on January 1, 2025.

B.
This Ordinance shall be repealed if the City of Cheyenne changes its form of
government to the city manager or commission form of government.

Section 3.
This Ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon publication, and the
compensation herein shall be reviewed approximately every four years from the effective date
hereof.
