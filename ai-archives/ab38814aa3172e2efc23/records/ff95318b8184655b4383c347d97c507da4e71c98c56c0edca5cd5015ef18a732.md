RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
August 23, 2010
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date
beginning at 6:02 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen;
COUNCIL MEMBERS - Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Ms. Case, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson,
Dr. Rinne, Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Barb Dorr,
City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda. (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to
be routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion.  There will be no
separate discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and
support by two other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be
considered in its normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Brown  moved to approve Consent
Agenda items, seconded by Mr. Pierson. Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on August 9, 2010.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
[CA]
Minutes from Vouchers Meeting of the Governing Body on August 16, 2010.  Motion
(per Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Valdez moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr.
Spiker.  Discussion followed concerning a voucher payable to Cheyenne Regional Airport
($200,000.00 for infrastructure improvements) and issues regarding American Eagle Airline’s
flight schedule and lack of notification to the public regarding reduced flights in/out of
Cheyenne, pre-sold tickets for discontinued flights, and whether the airline was in the process of
being sold.  Mayor Kaysen advised several community officials are communicating with
American Eagle about concerns.  During comments Dr. Rinne stated, as a point of information,
that although he is the City Council representative to the Airport Board, he does not review the
Airport’s contracts they may enter into with other entities.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body.
“An Ordinance repealing Chapter 5.72, Public Transport, in its entirety and re-creating
Chapter 5.72, Public Transportation, of Title 5, Business Licenses and Regulations, of the
Cheyenne City Code” -- referred to Finance Committee (due to postponement from 7/26/2010).
“An Ordinance amending Section 10.08.010, State Law adopted, of Chapter 10.08, State
Traffic Laws Adopted, of Title 10, Vehicle and Traffic, of Cheyenne City Code pertaining to
Ignition Interlock Licenses” -- referred to Finance Committee (sponsor - Mr. Brown).
“An Ordinance adopting water rates to be charged to users of the Cheyenne water system for the
purpose of mineral exploration outside of the City in Laramie County and other portions of
southeast Wyoming” -- referred to Finance Committee (sponsor - Mr. Collins).

“A Resolution authorizing submission of a Mineral Royalty Capital Construction
Account Grant (Chapter 3) application to the State Loan and Investment Board on behalf of the
Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne to install storm sewer on Snyder Avenue, between
Pershing Boulevard and Randall Avenue.”  Reporting for Finance Committee, Mr. Brown
moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Mr. Brown moved to amend the date contained in the
4th Whereas clause from Sept. 16, 2010 to Jan. 20, 2011, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Motion to
amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  Main motion as amended
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  (#5234)

“A Resolution authorizing submission of a Mineral Royalty Capital Construction account
grant (Chapter 3) application to the State Loan and Investment Board on behalf of the Governing
Body of the City of Cheyenne for the Cox Parking Garage induction lighting retrofit project.”
Mr. Brown moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Mr. Brown moved to amend the date
contained in the 5th Whereas clause from Sept. 16, 2010 to Jan. 20, 2011, seconded by Mr.
Valdez.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  Upon
inquiry, Vicki Nemecek, Asst. Public Works Director, clarified induction lighting is a form of
fluorescent lighting that results in energy savings.  She reviewed estimated annual energy cost
savings, and advised Long Building Technologies is putting the project together and that the
project would be put out to bid.  Upon inquiry, City Treasurer Barb Dorr verified the City owns
the parking garage.  Comments followed on the City’s agreement with the County regarding use
of the garage and maintenance costs.  Main motion as amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body with the exception of Ms. Ash voting “no”.  (#5235)
“A Resolution authorizing submission of a Mineral Royalty Capital Construction account
grant (Chapter 3) application to the State Loan and Investment Board on behalf of the Governing
Body of the City of Cheyenne for an early warning system for Cheyenne’s drinking water.”  Mr.
Brown moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Mr. Brown moved to amend the date
contained in the 5th Whereas clause from Sept. 16, 2010 to Jan. 20, 2011, seconded by Ms.
Broyles.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.  Upon
inquiry, Tim Wilson, BOPU Director, explained the system will provide monitoring of
Cheyenne’s drinking water while in the distribution system, and would alert BOPU if any
chemical was introduced accidentally or intentionally into the drinking water system.  He
clarified the funding request is for 5 units to be placed throughout the city, and provided
information on BOPU’s funding participation, and system software, installation, communication
equipment and maintenance involved.  Main motion as amended carried.  Voting “yes” – all
members of the governing body.  (#5236)
“A Resolution prioritizing the City of Cheyenne’s three applications to the State Loan
and Investment Board on behalf of the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne for Mineral
Royalty Capital Construction account (Chapter 3) funds.”  Mr. Brown moved to adopt, seconded
by Mr. Valdez.  Mr. Brown moved to amend the date contained in the 3rd Whereas clause from
Sept. 16, 2010 to Jan. 20, 2011, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Upon inquiry, Cindy Schneider,
Grants Manager, clarified the amendment request is due to an oversight regarding the date of the
SLIB meeting.  Motion to amend carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body.
Upon inquiry, Ms. Schneider and Mayor Kaysen reviewed the administrative process utilized in
proposing projects for funding requests and determination of the prioritization of projects.
