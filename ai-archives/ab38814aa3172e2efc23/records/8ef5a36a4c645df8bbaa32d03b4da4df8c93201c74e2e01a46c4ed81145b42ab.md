RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION EXPRESSING THE WILL OF THE GOVERNING
BODY TO PRIORITIZE AND ADEQUATELY FUND SPECIFIED
DOWNTOWN 6TH PENNY TAX MAINTENANCE AND IMPROVEMENT
PROJECTS."

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has certain funds available for downtown Cheyenne
maintenance and improvement projects through the voluntary one percent (1%) specific purpose
sales and use tax ("6th penny tax") approved by the affected citizenry;

WHEREAS, of the downtown 6th penny tax projects authorized to receive funding, the
Governing Body has determined that certain projects, as specified herein, should be prioritized for
funding in an amount not to exceed one million four hundred sixteen thousand nine hundred
twenty-two dollars ($1,416,922.00);

WHEREAS, the specified projects, and their estimated funding requirements, are as
follows and are prioritized in the following order of calendar date:

A. Projects to be completed by December 31, 2022, at an estimated cost of one
hundred ninety-one thousand nine hundred twenty-two dollars ($191,922.00);

1. Purchase of commercial Christmas lights for the Downtown Depot
Plaza at an estimated cost of ninety-four thousand three hundred two
dollars ($94,302.00);

2. Purchase eighty-eight (88) trash receptacles for the downtown
Cheyenne area at an estimated cost of fifty thousand dollars
($50,000.00); and

3. Purchase of one (1) Bobcat articulating loader for snow removal at
an estimated cost of forty-seven thousand six hundred twenty dollars
($47,620.00).

B. Projects to be completed by June 30, 2023, at an estimated cost of eight hundred
eighty thousand dollars ($880,000.00):

1. Removal of existing security fencing and installation of 15th Street
ornamental security fencing cordoning off the Union Pacific railway
beginning approximately at the intersection of 15th Street and
O'Neil Avenue, running east and ending at the southeast corner of
the Cheyenne Depot building at an estimated cost of five hundred
thousand dollars ($500,000.00);

By John Brodie at 2:18 pm, Sep 07, 2022

WHEREAS, the Director of the Community Recreation and Events Department shall have
the authority to reprioritize any project specified herein or substitute a different downtown 6th
penny tax maintenance project not specified herein, upon determining that the reprioritization or
substitution is in the best interests of the maintenance needs of the downtown area or is necessary
to protect the health and safety of the citizens of the city of Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, the authority provided to the Director of the Community Recreation and
Events Department to reprioritize any project or substitute a different downtown projects shall not
apply to the removal of existing security fencing and installation of 15th Street ornamental security
fencing as specified herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body through issuance of this Resolution
appropriates authorized 6th penny tax funding in an amount not to exceed one million four hundred
sixteen thousand nine hundred twenty-two dollars ($1,416,922.00) for the purposes specified
herein;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that should the Director of the Community Recreation and
Events Department exercise his authority to reprioritize or substitute any downtown 6th penny tax
maintenance project specified herein, the Director shall submit a written report of this action to the
Mayor and the City Council within thirty (30) days of the exercise of such authority; and

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that this resolution shall be effective upon passage and
execution by the mayor and city clerk.
