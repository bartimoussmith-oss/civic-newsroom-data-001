GUIDELINES FOR AMOUNTS TO BE MAINTAINED IN THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE GENERAL FUND RESERVES AND ESTABLISHING REVISED
GUIDELINES FOR AMOUNTS TO BE MAINTAINED IN THE
UNRESTRICTED FUND BALANCE OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
GENERAL FUND."
WHEREAS, the Governing body of the Cityof Cheyenne recognizes its obligations to
provide community services and infrastructure to provide for the safety, health and welfare of its
citizens; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the Cityof Cheyenne recognizes that, inorderto
providefor community services and infrastructure, it is necessary toinvest certain amounts of
funds into specified budget areas of the General Fund; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body also recognizes the importance of being prepared for any
emergent fiscal needs which might arise; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body recognizes the importance of maintaining adequate
levels of fund balancein the General Fund tomitigate current and future risks such asrevenue
shortfalls and emergency expenditures; and
WHEREAS, in determining an appropriate amount for adequate levels of fund balance in
the General Fund, the Governing Body has considered the predictability of revenue sources,
volatility of expenditures and exposure to significant one-time expenditures; and
WHEREAS, the fund balanceis separated into five categories, including Non spend able
Fund Balance, Restricted Fund Balance, Committed Fund Balance, Assigned Fund Balance and
Unassigned Fund Balance, as these terms are defined in Governmental Accounting Standards
Board Statement54, Fund Balance Reporting and Governmental Fund Type Definitions; and
WHEREAS, only the Committed, Assigned and Unassigned categories, which include
those resources without a constraint on spending or for which the constraint on spending is
imposed by the City of Cheyenne itself, are termed Unrestricted Fund Balance; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne distinguishes its operating budget in the General Fund
between recurring operating expenditures (hereinafter referred to as “Operating Expenditures"),
and support services and non-recurring special project budgets; and
WHEREAS, the Government Finance Officers Association of the United States and
Canada recommends that cities maintain an Unrestricted Fund Balance of noless than sixty (60)
days of Operating Expenditures; and
WHEREAS, it is the intent of the Governing Body to achieve and maintain an Unrestricted
Fund Balance in the General Fund of the City of Cheyenne equal to or exceeding sixty (60) days of
Operating Expenditures.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OFCHEYENNE, WYOMING THAT Resolution4738isrescinded.
BEIT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT the Cityof Cheyenne will maintain an
Unrestricted Fund Balance in the General Fund in an amountequal to or exceeding sixty(6o)days
of Operating Expenditures.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED THAT expenditures of the Unrestricted Fund Balance in
the General Fund below the sixty (60) day reserve amount is limited to emergencies and other
unforeseen expenditures which must be approvedby the Governing Body through a
re-appropriation of the General Fund Budget.
