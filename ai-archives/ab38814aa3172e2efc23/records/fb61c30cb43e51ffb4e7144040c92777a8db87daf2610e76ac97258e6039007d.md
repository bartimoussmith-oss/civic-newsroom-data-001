RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 14, 2019

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:02 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Case, Mr. Cook,
Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Roybal, Mr. Shanor, and Mr. White.  Absent:  Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Luna, and Dr.
Rinne.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Logan Sharp, Assistant City Attorney; Robin
Lockman, City Treasurer, and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of
allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook live.
Mayor Orr explained it is the governing body’s intention to go into executive session for the
purpose of evaluating proposed remaining sections.

President Bill Hinz, Local 279, advised the Union is agreeable to signing off on Sections
#32, 33, and 44.  Upon inquiry, Chief Hoggatt confirmed proposed changes relating to purchase
of dress uniforms.

Mr. Hinz requested a recess at 6:06 p.m. and Union members vacated chambers to Room
104 for the purpose of caucus.

Mayor Orr reconvened the special session at 6:38 p.m.

 Mr. Hinz proposed the following:  Agreement for a 2-year contract term and requested a
guarantee of additional staffing for the purpose of limiting overtime relating to Kelly Day request.

Mr. Hinz requested a recess at 6:42 p.m. and Union members vacated chambers to Room
104 for the purpose of caucus.

Mayor Orr reconvened the special session at 6:49 p.m.

Mr. Hinz advised the Union continues to propose increase of staffing levels by 3 members
for fiscal years 2020 and 2021, a 3½ % wage increase for fiscal years 2019 and 2020, Kelly Day
proposal, and stated they do not support City’s counter offer to reduce E.O.D. compensatory time
from 480 to 240 hours in Section #22.

Mayor Orr requested City staff obtain written clarification relating to retirement
calculations involving Union members who are out on workers compensation.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Case moved to meet in executive
session, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing
body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body present, the
City Clerk, Assistant City Attorney, City Treasurer, and Chief of the Fire and Rescue Department
would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the special
meeting was stopped; executive Session convened at 7:05 p.m. in the Mayor’s Conference Room]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
7:58 p.m.  Mayor Orr advised the City requests the Union submit all proposals and counter offers
in writing and reported the City supports signing off on Sections #15, 17, 31, 33, 43, and 44.  Mr.
Hinz stated the Union has concerns with Sections #15 and 17 relating to staffing levels and
overtime compensation.

Mr. Hinz requested a recess at 8:06 p.m. and Union members vacated chambers to Room
104 for the purpose of caucus.

Mr. Hinz advised agreement to signing off on Sections #32, 33, and 44 and stated for the
record the Union would not be signing off on Sections #15 and 17.  For the record, Mayor Orr
stated the City supported the original Union proposals for Sections #15 and 17.

Discussion included request of written proposals and counter offers and confirmation was
received that Section #31 has been signed off on by both parties.

Mayor Orr stated the purpose of the special meeting had been met, and there being no
business previously noted for the special meeting, adjourned the meeting at 8:21 p.m.

Submitted by,

Kristina F. Jones,

City Clerk

This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle

March 21, 2019
