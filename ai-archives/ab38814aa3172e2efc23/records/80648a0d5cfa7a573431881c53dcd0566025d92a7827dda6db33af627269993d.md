APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

UDC 2.4.2 Annexations

PlanCheyenne

BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting annexing of 481 acres of land for future industrial development. The County
Assessor presently shows the land as part of three unplatted parcels. The land is currently undeveloped in
the County. A zone change and plat are proposed concurrently with this annexation. All three applications
are anticipated to come to final consideration before the Governing Body at the same meeting.

Land annexed to the City requires a City zone. The petition to annex has been accompanied by a submission
to change the zoning from County A-2 Agricultural and Rural Residential to the City zones of AG Agricultural,
LI Light Industrial, and HI heavy Industrial.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned A-2 Agricultural in the County. The property is concurrently proposed to
be annexed and rezoned to the AG Agricultural, LI Light Industrial, and HI heavy Industrial zones. The AG
zone is generally described as a primarily agricultural zone intended for use where land is to be held in a
natural state or as a holding zone until development is appropriate.  The HI and LI zones are intended for
industrial development and is appropriate near regional transportation routes and to larger land areas
that are capable of providing substantial buffers and located remote from other differing and
incompatible land uses.

future development once it is built. By being in City limits, the property will receive access to
