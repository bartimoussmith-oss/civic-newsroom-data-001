Dear Mayor and Members of City Council:
This letter serves as Texas Roadhouse Holdings, Inc.'s ("TXRH Holdings") introduction
and request that Cheyenne's licensing authority approve the transfer of the Retail Liquor License
for Texas Roadhouse from Texas Roadhouse of Cheyenne, LLC to TXRH Holdings.
TXRH Holdings and Texas Roadhouse of Cheyenne, LLC are merging, with TXRH
Holdings as the surviving entity.TXRH Holdings will continue to operate as Texas Roadhouse
and anticipates that the general public will not even be aware of the transition as all employees
and operations will remain precisely the same following the merger on December 31, 2013. This
merger is being done in an effort to simplify the Texas Roadhouse operations nationwide and
bring all of the operations under the ownership of TXRH Holdings.The transfer of this retail
liquor license is, of course, a key aspect of themerger.
We are confident that Texas Roadhouse will continue to be successful in Cheyenne and
we look forward toa profitable partnership with the Cityfor manyyears to come.
