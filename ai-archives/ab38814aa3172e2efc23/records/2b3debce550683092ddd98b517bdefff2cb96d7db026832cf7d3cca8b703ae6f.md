RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
January 9, 2012
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Ms. Ash, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Collins, Mr. Pierson, Dr. Rinne,
Mr. Spiker and Mr. Valdez.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk; Lois Huff, City Treasurer,
and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.
ELECTION OF CITY COUNCIL PRESIDENT FOR 2012.  Mayor Kaysen noted for the
record that, pursuant to City Code, he would be conducting the elections for City Council President
and Vice-President but would not be voting.   Mayor Kaysen stated nominations for election of the
office of Council President was in order.  Mr. Valdez moved to nominate and elect Mr. Collins as
City Council President for 2012, seconded by Mr. Pierson. Mr. Brown moved that nominations
cease, seconded by Mr. Allen.  Motion to cease nominations carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members
of the governing body with the exception of Mayor Kaysen who did not vote.  Mr. Collins advised
he is looking forward to serving as Council President for 2012 and noted there are many projects to
address during the upcoming year.  Motion to nominate and elect Mr. Collins carried.  Voting “yes”
-- all members of the governing body with the exception of Mayor Kaysen who did not vote.
ELECTION OF CITY COUNCIL VICE-PRESIDENT FOR 2012.  Mayor Kaysen stated
nominations for election of the office of Council Vice-President was in order.  Mr. Collins moved
to nominate and elect Mr. Pierson as City Council Vice-President for 2012, seconded by Ms.
Broyles.  Mr. Collins moved that nominations cease, seconded by Mr. Brown.  Motion to cease
nominations carried.  Voting “yes”-- all members of the governing body with the exception of Mayor
Kaysen who did not vote.  Mr. Pierson advised he considered it an honor to serve as a part of Council
leadership.  Motion to nominate and elect Mr. Pierson carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body with the exception of Mayor Kaysen who did not vote.
On behalf of the governing body, Mayor Kaysen recognized outgoing 2011 Council President
Dr. Rinne, and presented him with an engraved gavel plaque.  He also expressed appreciation to Mr.
Brown for serving as Council Vice-President for 2011.
