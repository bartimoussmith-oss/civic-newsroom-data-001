A.Project Description
The City of Cheyenne, Wyoming is seeking qualification statements and service proposals from
consulting groups for the Pavement Condition Survey, Assessment and Inventory Services.The
proposals should be designed to providethe City with a clear understanding ofthe scope of work to

Introduction and Background
The City of Cheyenne's pavement manageiment program has historically been operated
through the Metropolitan Planning Organization(MPO).InFY 2010 theMPO changed from
STANTEC to Lucity(formally GBA master series)pavement management software(PMS).
The city haschosento assume the pavement management program tocreate a betterQC/QA
of our road network. The City will continue using Lucity as our PMS and schedule a
Pavement Condition Survey and Assessment and Inventory Service period every four (4)
years.In order to maximize ongoing investment, the City of Cheyenne believes it maybe
better toemphasize a cost benefit analysis(CBA)rather thanPCI alone.By modeling the
return on CBA, network needs maybe indentified objectively, in terms of maximum CBA
rather based solely on a certain level of PCI.
The successful proposer will be expected to commit, at a minimum, to perform the tasks
described in theFY2010 MPO Data Collection Project Scope of Work, whichis attached as
Exhibit D.However, proposers are encouraged to identify other work activities, tasks or
techniques which, intheview ofthe proposer, are necessary to provide acomplete, valid, and
useable pavement condition survey, analysis, and inventory.
The successful proposer must have working experience in roadway data collection and the
Lucity PMS.
