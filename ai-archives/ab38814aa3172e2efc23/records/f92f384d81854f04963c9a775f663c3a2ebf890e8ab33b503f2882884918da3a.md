```text
FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
Due to the Presidents Day holiday, a meeting of the Finance Committee was instead held on
Wednesday, February 18, 2026, in City Council Chambers and via electronic meeting, starting at
12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS:
Ken Esquibel, Chairman; Dr. Kathy Emmons; Jeff White (via
Zoom); and Lawrence Wolfe. Absent: Mark Moody.
AGENDA ITEMS
19.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
c)
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyeime and TDSi for
professional design services for the repurposing of the Historic Pumphouse.
(General Fund Reserves)
Mr. Wolfe moved to approve in an amount of $198,903.00, seconded by Mr. White. Mr. Wolfe
moved to amend by substitute (letter) dated February 18, 2026, seconded by Mr. White. Motion
to amend failed with Dr. Emmons and Mr. White voting “NO,” and Mr. Wolfe voting “YES.”
Main motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Jason Sanchez, Community Recreation & Events Director, provided a brief history of the
relocation of the City’s Clean and Safe Department and noted the issues with their current location.
Committee member Wolfe provided a substitute and overview of changes included in the scope of
work.
David Hopkinson, Deputy City Attorney, provided information related to the process of
document approval by the City Attorney’s office.
Committee members Emmons and White
commented on the item. Public comments were made by Maren Kallas, Historic Cheyenne Inc.;
and Megan Stanfill, Alliance for Historic Wyoming.
d)
Utility Easement Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Microsoft
Corporation for grant of perpetual right-of-way and temporary easement for
underground fiber lines. (Revenue to the City)
Dr. Emmons moved to approve in an amount of $10.00, seconded by Mr. Wolfe. Motion carried
by unanimous voice vote.
Jean Anderson-Shrednik, Parks & Greenway Planner, provided a staff report and Brad Emmons,
AVI P.C., provided additional infonuation. Committee member Emmons commented on the item.
```
