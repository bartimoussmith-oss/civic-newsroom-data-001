```text
SECTION 3.  EXCLUSIVE BARGAINING AGENT

The paid Members of the Cheyenne Fire Rescue Department, the regularly constituted
Fire Department of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, by a majority vote, have selected the
I.A.F.F. Local Union No. 279 as the sole and exclusive bargaining agent for all of the Members
of the Department, except the Fire Chief.

through 27-10-109.

The City shall not enter into any other agreement with Members serving as firefighters,
individually or collectively, or with any other organization, which in any way conflicts with the
provisions of this Collective Labor Agreement.
```

```text
SECTION 8.  GRIEVANCE PROCEDURES

A grievance is defined as a dispute or difference of opinion between the City and a
Member, Members, or the Union over the meaning, interpretation, application, or the inequitable
application of an expressed provision of the Agreement.  Any Member may file a grievance
under this Agreement protesting the exercise of Management Rights, but to prevail, the Member
must establish a violation by the City of the specific terms of this Agreement.  Any grievance
filed against the City shall be in writing and if a Member’s grievance, it shall contain the name
and position of the affected Member(s).  The grievance shall contain a clear and concise
statement of the grievance, the issue or issues involved, the relief sought, the date the incident or
violation took place, and the specific section or sections alleged to have been violated.

For a grievance to be considered timely, the Union’s Grievance Committee must submit
it in writing within ten business days of the date the grievant knew of the event giving rise to the
dispute, following the steps below:
Step 1

The Union’s Grievance Committee may submit the grievance in writing to the respective
Division Chief who will then schedule a meeting with the grievant and two members of the
Union’s Grievance Committee within ten business days to discuss the grievance and attempt to
resolve the grievance.  A written decision from the Division Chief shall be delivered to the
grievant, Union, and Fire Chief within five business days of the meeting.  If the decision fails to
satisfy the grievant and/or Union, either may initiate Step 2 of the grievance process.

Step 2
```

```text
SECTION 17.  HOURS OF WORK

The average work week for Platoon Personnel shall be fifty-six hours computed on a
twenty-four-day basis.  The three-shift system shall be maintained with twenty-four-hour tours of
duty, and the existing schedule shall be continued, which will include scheduled work time from
the hours of 0800 to 1700 with one hour off for lunch and one hour for physical fitness training.

Beginning January 7, 2023, the employer shall begin the work shifts per work period as
follows: the stated work period for purposes of complying with the Fair Labor Standards Act
shall be twenty-four days.  Each Member shall be assigned to work shifts that total 192 hours of
work per period.  The assigned schedule shall be a 48/96 shift schedule, three platoon system in
which employees work two consecutive twenty-four hour shifts for a total of forty-eight hours
and have ninety-six consecutive hours off. The maximum number of consecutive hours worked
by members shall be ninety-six hours.

Day Personnel will be primarily scheduled to work four 10-hour days (40-hour
work week) as defined in the definitions.  Shifts may begin at 0700 hours and may end at 1800
hours. This time may be adjusted to meet the needs of the Fire Department or the Member with
the approval of the Fire Chief.  The schedule may have one week day, and shall have Saturday,
Sunday, and holidays off.  If a holiday falls on a Member’s regularly scheduled day off, the
Member shall receive the day before the holiday or the next regularly scheduled work day off, as
determined by the Fire Chief.
```

```text
SECTION 19.  INCLEMENT WEATHER CONDITIONS

It is the responsibility of each Member of the Department to make an honest effort to
report to work as scheduled.  During periods of severe inclement weather, Members are
classified as essential personnel.

If a Member cannot report for duty as scheduled without an unreasonable risk to his or
her safety, the Member shall immediately notify an Officer in Charge at Headquarters by the
hours specified in the rules and regulations to report the Member’s status.  The following will
apply to:
Shift Personnel:

The Officer in Charge shall notify the Member who has to be relieved of duty and place
that Member on holdover until properly relieved.

The affected Member shall be:
1.
Granted two hours of administrative leave without penalty to report to
duty.  If a Member cannot arrive safely in the two hours, the Member may request from
the Fire Chief or Division Chief of Operation an additional two hours of administrative
leave without penalty (maximum of four hours administrative leave).
2.
If the Member is still unable to safely report to duty by noon, he or she
may obtain an emergency time swap for the period needed to report to duty or utilize
accrued vacation or E.O.D. time in hour increments until he or she is able to report to
duty.
```

```text
SECTION 35.  RESIDENCY

Members will be required to be a resident and be domiciled within a sixty mile radius of
```
