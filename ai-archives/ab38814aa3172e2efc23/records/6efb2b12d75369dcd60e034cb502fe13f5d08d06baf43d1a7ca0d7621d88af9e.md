```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

June 9, 2021
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-28-21 for furnishing Three (3) New 2021-2022 Cab &
Chassis (Low Cab Forward/Cabover) With 40-Yard Front Loader Refuse
Collection Body Installed, for the City of Cheyenne, Sanitation Division
______________________________________________
Four (4) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.
The Four (4) Bids were received from:
1. Peterbilt of Wyoming.  Cheyenne, Wyoming.
a. (Bid in Compliance.  5 Bid Options Submitted.  2 Bid Options Met Spec 100%, 3
Options With Bid Exceptions).
2. Floyd's Truck Center.  Gillette, Wyoming.
a. (Bid in Compliance.  4 Bid Options Submitted.  No Bid Option Met Spec 100%.)
3. McCandless Truck Center.  Cheyenne, Wyoming.
a. (Bid in Compliance, 1 Bid Option Submitted.  No Bid Option Met Spec 100%).
4. Jackson Group Peterbilt.  Billings, Montana.
a. (Bid in Compliance.  4 Bid Options Submitted.  1 Bid Option Met Spec 100%, 3
Options With Bid Exceptions.)
The overall lowest bid was submitted by Peterbilt of Wyoming (w/Kois Brother – Heil Half Pack).
Although the lowest overall, the Bid contained a Bid Exception to the following specification:
“Y  N  1.
A hinge type or sliding hopper access door is installed on curb side for
safety, and allow access to hopper.”
Peterbilt of Wyoming (w/Kois Brother – Heil Half Pack) marked “ N ” to this Bid Specification,
as the door on their proposed option is mounted on the Street Side, not the Curb Side.  This item
WAS NOT brought up for review or modification by the City at the Mandatory Pre-Bid
Meeting, or by the Question Submission Deadline.  The City of Cheyenne has rejected this bid
option, as it did not meet the written Bid Specification.
The lowest responsive bid was submitted by Peterbilt of Wyoming (w/ Ameri-Tech – Labrie
Wittke Starlight HD).
```

```text
The bids were reviewed by Vicki Nemecek (Public Works), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), Steven
Pauli (Sanitation), and Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance).  Their recommendation is to accept the
lowest responsive bid from Peterbilt of Wyoming (w/ Ameri-Tech – Labrie Wittke Starlight HD),
for Three (3) New 2021-2022 Cab & Chassis (Low Cab Forward/Cabover) With 40-Yard Front
Loader Refuse Collection Body Installed, Less Trade Ins, Plus 3 Year Remote Diagnostics, for a
bid award amount of $689,094.00.
Basis for overall award was determined on Cost/Each, Less Trade Ins.  After application of the 5%
Preference for Resident Bidders, Peterbilt of Wyoming was determined to be the Lowest
Responsive Bidder, as a Certified Resident Contractor/Supplier.

Funding:
023-14-1417-43-43102 (Solid Waste Fund / Equipment)

Total $689,094.00
```
