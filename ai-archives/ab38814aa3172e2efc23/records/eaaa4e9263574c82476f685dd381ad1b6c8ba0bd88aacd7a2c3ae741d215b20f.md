Planning Commission
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
October 10, 2024
PZ-24-00077 PC 10.10.24 Mtng Packet
Attachments:
Cate Cundall, Associate Planner, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of
Dustin and Sara Smith, submitted a Subdivision Permit and Plat application for
Prairie Sage Acres, located at 1253 Road 146, Burns, WY. The purpose of the
application is to divide this 40.0 acre parcel into four residential tracts.The current
residence and outbuildings will remain.Water and sanitary sewer will be provided by
individual wells and septic systems. The subdivision will be primarily accessed of
Road 146, with Tract 3 being accessed via a 60 foot utility and access easement.
Shane Hansen, applicant's agent, was present to answer any questions the
Commissioners may have.
Vice Chairman Watkins opened the Public Hearing for comments. Hearing no
comments the hearing was closed.
Jack Stadel moved, seconded by Mark Smith to recommend approval of
the Subdivision Permit and Plat for Prairie Sage Acres to the Laramie
County Board of Commissioners and adopt the findings of facts a and b of
the staff report with no conditions. Vote 4-0.
Stadel, Watkins, Gaspar, Smith
Aye:
Caughey
