ORDINANCE NO. ____________________

ENTITLED: “AN ORDINANCE VACATING A PORTION OF THE RIGHT-OF-WAY OF
BRADLEY AVENUE AT THE INTERSECTION OF BRADLEY AVENUE AND EAST
14TH STREET.”

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1.
That the property described as a portion of Bradley Avenue, Original City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, and more particularly described in Exhibit A, attached hereto and incorporated
herein, be and is hereby vacated as shown on the attached Vacation Map.

Section 2.
The vacation of a portion of Bradley Avenue is in the best interest of the City of
Cheyenne and its residents.

Section 3.
That said vacated right-of-way shall revert to the adjacent property owners
pursuant to Wyoming State Statutes.

Section 4.
The said vacated land shall be subject to any easements, reservations and
restrictions of record and as represented on the attached Vacation Map.

Section 5.
The City Clerk is directed to record this ordinance, together with the Vacation
Map, with the County Clerk and Ex-Officio Register of Deeds for Laramie County, Wyoming.

Section 6.
That the City Engineer’s office is directed to change the Official City Map to
reflect this vacation and the Official Zoning Map designating the vacation request for the property
described in Exhibit A as zone CB Community Business District.

Section 7.
Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable.  If any phrase, clause, sentence, paragraph or section of this ordinance is declared
unconstitutional, such unconstitutionality shall not affect any of the remaining phrases, clauses,
sentences, paragraphs and sections.

Section 8.
That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its publication.
