WHEREAS, W.S.§ 16-4-111(a) requires the Governing Body to make the necessary

hearing required by W.S.  16-4-109(b); and
WHEREAS, due to the complexity of the budgeting process, it is appropriate that the

by the budget officer; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body would benefit from receiving individual departmental
estimates of all proposed expenditures, other than those of the Cheyenne Fire&Rescue Department,

Governing Body to make strategic decisions regarding the City'sfinances.
