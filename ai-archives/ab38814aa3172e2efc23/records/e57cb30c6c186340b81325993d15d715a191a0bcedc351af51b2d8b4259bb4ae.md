TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager

March 23, 2022
SUBJECT:
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and Waste
Management of Colorado, Inc., for the Recycling Services Project
______________________________________________
One (1) Responsive Proposal was submitted under Request for Proposal (RFP) #7-22, for the
Recycling Services Project.

The proposal was received from:
1. Waste Management of Colorado, Inc.,  Fort Collins, Colorado.

Upon review of the one (1) proposal by the evaluation panel (consisting of 3 members), the
evaluation panel has recommended acceptance of the proposal submitted by Waste Management
of Colorado, Inc., for a project award amount not-to-exceed $200,000.00.  This Agreement is based
on actual quantities recycled, and is billed in accordance with the Fee Schedule attached as Exhibit
A, to the Professional Services Agreement.  If future Contract renewals are executed after the
original term of the Agreement ends, additional funds may need to be added as well.

Basis for award was based on a Qualifications based review and scoring of proposals.  Waste
Management of Colorado, Inc., submitted the highest scoring proposal, therefore their sealed
pricing envelope was opened & reviewed.

PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT
for
RECYCLING SERVICES (RFP 7-22)

This Professional Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made and entered into by and between
the City of Cheyenne, located at 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and
WASTE MANAGEMENT OF COLORADO, INC., located at 5887 S. TAFT HILL ROAD,
FORT COLLINS, COLORADO 80526 (“Consultant”).  This Agreement shall become
effective upon the date of the last signature affixed hereto.

RECITALS

In consideration of the mutual covenants and obligations herein expressed, it is agreed by and
between the parties as follows:

1.
SCOPE OF WORK
The Consultant agrees to provide services as described for the above-mentioned project,
in accordance with City of Cheyenne Request for Proposal (RFP) #7-22, incorporated
herein by reference, and the Consultant’s submitted proposal, incorporated herein by
reference.

2.
TIME OF PERFORMANCE
The work included in this Scope of Work will be completed by April 15, 2023.

3.
COMPENSATION
This is a unit price, not-to-exceed Agreement.  The City shall pay for actual services
rendered.  In consideration of the services to be performed pursuant to this Agreement,
the Consultant will bill the City and the City agrees to pay Consultant a not to exceed
amount of Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000.00) as stated in Exhibit A, Fee
Schedule.

4.
CONSULTANT RESPONSIBILITY
In providing services under this Agreement, the Consultant shall perform in a manner
consistent with that degree of care and skill ordinarily exercised by members of the same
profession currently practicing under similar circumstances. Upon notice to the
Consultant, the Consultant will, without additional compensation, correct those services
not meeting such a standard.

5.
COMPLIANCE WITH LAWS
The Parties agree that they will perform their obligations as provided in this Agreement
in accordance with all applicable laws and ordinances.

6.
DRUG-FREE WORKPLACE
By Stefanie Boster at 11:04 am, Mar 23, 2022
