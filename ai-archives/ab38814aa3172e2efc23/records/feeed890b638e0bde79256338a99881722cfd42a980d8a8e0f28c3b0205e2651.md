AMENDMENT TO CONTRACT BETWEEN
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND CHARLES E. BALES

This Amendment to City Contract #3920 is made and entered into by and between the City
of Cheyenne, a municipality organized and existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming, whose
address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001 (“City”), and Charles E. Bales, whose
address is 3618 Charles Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82009 (“Mr. Bales”).

RECITALS

WHEREFORE Mr. Bales is the current owner of property (“the property”) which is
contiguous to, but not annexed to, the City.  The property is more specifically described as
follows:

Lots 6 and 7, Robitaille Subdivision, and all of that part of Robitaille Court and
Wills Road adjacent to Lots 6 and 7, and that portion of Charles Street from the
west right-of-way line of Wills Road easterly 135 feet, Laramie County, Wyoming.

WHEREFORE, on March 21, 2000, pursuant to City Contract # 3920, Mr. Bales applied
to annex the property to the City in order to obtain City sewer service because his private sewer
system had failed. Mr. Bales was not able to finance the construction of street improvements
including street, sidewalk, curb and gutter, which were required by the City as a condition
precedent to annexation and availability of City sewer service.

WHEREFORE, the City recognized that it was important to annex Mr. Bales’ property
because such action would resolve a serious health and safety problem, diminish county pockets
and add residents to the tax rolls of the City.

WHEREFORE, as a result, the City and Mr. Bales entered into City Contract # 3920
wherein he acknowledged that he is obligated to pay all costs associated with the construction of
street improvements including street, sidewalk, curb and gutter, upon the City’s request.  He also
acknowledged that the improvements will be made when his property is sold or subject to a
Development Action.

WHEREFORE, the definition of a “Development Action” means an action improving a
site with a structural improvement requiring a building or other permit and intended to be a
permanent improvement of the Development as its operation is contemplated herein (i.e., vertical
data center buildings or permanent road or utility installation, but not including permanent
improvements by a public or private utility provider, and not including site grading of the
Property). “Development Action” is used in the UDC, but not defined in City Contract #3920. The
Parties agree to use the foregoing definition for purposes of interpretation of any application of
such term under the UDC for purposes of all matters related to this Amended Contract.

by a gravel street, Mr. Bales has discovered that the costs associated with the construction of street
