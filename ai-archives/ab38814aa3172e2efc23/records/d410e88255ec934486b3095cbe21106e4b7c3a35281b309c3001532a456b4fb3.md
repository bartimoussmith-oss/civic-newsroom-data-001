SUBJECT: City of Cheyenne Transit Facility Change Order #2

Dear Renae,

We have been working diligently on the Transit Facility Site Plan. Approximately a month
ago we submitted the final site plan and the construction plans to the city for the site
plan review (SPR) and engineering plan review (EPR). At that time, we had expended
most of our original design budget plus the approved amendment for the site plan
assuming that any comments after the EPR and SPR would be relatively minimal. That
was not the case and we have now addressed the comments for the EPR and SPR and
resubmitted while incurring additional hours to complete the project. Additionally, as
the EPR was processed we have been made aware that drainage easements will be
required.
To date we have billed $47,482.75 (through June 30th) of our $56,225.00 not to exceed
contract and have incurred approximately an additional $8,200 in design fees in the
month of August.  After resubmitting, we are hopeful that any comments from the city
will be minor and that we can continue forward to bidding and construction
administration of the project. As it currently stands, if we bill for our additional design
effort, we will approach the not to exceed contract value and we still do not have an
approved site plan or engineering plans. Additionally, we are still under contract to
provide limited support during bidding and construction administration.
I am requesting an increase to our not to exceed fee to account for the additional
work required to resubmit the EPR and SPR, prepare the required drainage easements,
and to provide some budget to account for minor final comments and assist with
preparing the project for bid. I’ve provided a summary of what we are requesting
below.
