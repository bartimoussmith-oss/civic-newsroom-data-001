MEMO

Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council

Jeanie Shrednik, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner
Subject: Resolution authorizing the submission of an application by the City of Cheyenne for
federal funding through the Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP)
administered by the Wyoming Department of Transportation, for the purpose of
construction for a Greenway path for pedestrians and bicycles from the Sun Valley
Open Space into Kiwanis Community Park.

Date:
June 18, 2024

Background:

The item for consideration is an authorization from Council to submit a TAP grant application for
construction of a portion of the Sun Valley to Kiwanis Park Greenway Connector. A grant
application for construction of this segment was submitted last year, unfortunately the project was
not funded.

The Sun Valley to Kiwanis Park Connector will be approximately 2.4 miles and will run from the
existing Greenway trailhead located at Baldwin Drive, which is south of the Sun-Valley
subdivision and east of N. College Drive, then head east toward the Kiwanis Community Park.
Per discussions with WYDOT representatives last fall, the city has moved forward this year to
complete the full design and has obtained all the needed right-of-way purchases with expectation
of utilizing the TAP funds for construction in the next two years. This connector has been discussed
and listed as a priority in several planning documents since 2000 and with the purchase of the
Kiwanis Park property and other properties abutting the UPRR, the Greenway Advisory
Committee has agreed that it is a top priority for use of 2021 6th Penny funding.   Based on the
estimated total cost, the project will be completed in two phases and Phase 1, Baldwin to
Gettysburg, is expected to have a total cost of $1,900,000.00.  If the grant is approved, the city
would pay $900,000.00 (47%) with 2021 6th Penny funding and the TAP Grant would pay
$1,000,000.00. (53%).  Submission of the grant to include a resolution by the Governing Body is
due July 15, 2024.

RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A
RESOLUTION
AUTHORIZING
THE
SUBMISSION
OF
AN
APPLICATION BY THE CITY OF CHEYENNE FOR FEDERAL
FUNDING THROUGH THE TRANSPORTATION ALTERNATIVES
PROGRAM ADMINISTERED BY THE WYOMING DEPARTMENT OF
TRANSPORTATION FOR THE PURPOSE OF CONSTRUCTION FOR A
GREENWAY PATH FOR PEDESTRIANS AND BICYCLES FROM THE
SUN VALLEY OPEN SPACE INTO KIWANIS COMMUNITY PARK."

WHEREAS, the governing body for the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the
Transportation Alternatives Program (TAP) administered by the Wyoming Department of
Transportation, to assist in funding construction (Project) for a Greenway path for pedestrians
and bicycles from Sun Valley Open Space into the Kiwanis Community property;

WHEREAS, the governing body for the City of Cheyenne recognizes the need for the
Project;

WHEREAS, TAP requires that federal funding criteria be met, and the City of Cheyenne
agrees to ensure satisfaction of all requirements;

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne acknowledges that if funded, the Project shall be
completed prior to December 31, 2027;

WHEREAS, the MPO Policy Committee has included this Project as an amendment to
the Transportation Improvement Plan (TIP) for 2024-2027;

WHEREAS, the governing body for the City of Cheyenne requests a grant of one million
dollars ($1,000,000) from TAP for the cost of construction for the proposed Project;

WHEREAS, the governing body for the City of Cheyenne agrees to provide nine hundred
thousand dollars ($900,000.00) from the 6th Penny Sales Tax for the required local match of
10% and additional costs for the project; and

WHEREAS, the governing body for the City of Cheyenne acknowledges TAP is funded
on a reimbursement basis and all invoices must be 100% paid by the City of Cheyenne prior to
reimbursement through TAP. The City of Cheyenne acknowledges that failure to comply with
this requirement may result in cancellation of the award and repayment by the City of Cheyenne
of all funds reimbursed.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that a funding application requesting one million dollars
($1,000,000) in Federal TAP funding be submitted to the Wyoming Department of
Transportation, Transportation Alternatives Program, for consideration to assist in funding for
the Project, and that the Mayor and City Clerk are authorized to execute all documents associated
with the grant application.
