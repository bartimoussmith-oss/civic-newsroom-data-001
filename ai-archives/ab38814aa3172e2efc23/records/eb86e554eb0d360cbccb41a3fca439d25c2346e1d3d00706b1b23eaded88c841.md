August 18, 2015

Dear Property Owner:

The purpose of this letter is to notify you of proposed amendments to the “Major Street Plan
Official Map” for the City of Cheyenne.  You are being notified because you are an owner of
property adjacent to the planned Christensen Road extension between Commerce Circle and U.S.
Highway 30. A map is enclosed to show you the proposed Official Map Amendment.

A public hearing will be held at the City Planning Commission on Monday, September 21, 2015
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers, 2101 O’Neil Avenue.  The purpose of the public hearing is to
describe the mapping changes and give all interested persons an opportunity to make comment
on the proposed amendments to the official map for Christensen Road and to the entire official
map for the City.

The creation of an official map is allowed by State Statutes §15-1-501 et seq.  The purpose of an
official map is to show the locations of collector and arterial streets which have already been
approved or proposed new streets or street extensions that have been surveyed based on a major
street plan, also known as PlanCheyenne.  The placing of any street or street lines upon the
official map does not in itself constitute the opening or establishment of any street or the taking
or acceptance of any land for street purposes.

Please call, email, or write if you have any questions or comments.  I can be reached at 307-638-
4385, or tmason@cheyennempo.org .

Sincerely,

Tom Mason, Director

Metropolitan Planning Organization
2101 O’Neil Ave.
Cheyenne, WY 82001
www.plancheyenne.org
