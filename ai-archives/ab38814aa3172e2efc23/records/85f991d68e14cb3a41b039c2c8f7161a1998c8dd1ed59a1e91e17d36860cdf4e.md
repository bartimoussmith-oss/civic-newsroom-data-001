Wyoming (“City of Cheyenne”), the Cheyenne-Laramie County Corporation for Economic
Development, a Wyoming non-profit corporation and statutorily-authorized community

limited partnership organized under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“Granite Peak”).
RECITALS
(A)
On or about December 1, 2013, the City of Cheyenne applied for a Wyoming
Business Ready Community-Business Committed Loan (the “Loan”) in the amount of Five Million
Dollars ($5,000,000.00).  In the processing of said application, it has been determined that the City
of Cheyenne will receive a portion of the proposed Loan funds in the form of a Business Ready
Community-Business Committed Grant (the “Grant”) from the Wyoming Business Council
(“WBC”).  The City of Cheyenne will distribute the Loan and Grant proceeds to Granite Peak
pursuant to the terms of this Agreement, the terms and conditions of a Business Ready Community-
Business Committed Loan Agreement in the amount of Three Million Dollars ($3,000,000.00) (the
“Loan”), and the terms and conditions of a Business Ready Community-Business Committed Grant
Agreement in the amount of Two Million Dollars ($2,000,000.00) (the “Grant”).  Said Loan and
Grant funds shall be used exclusively to fund a portion of the costs of construction of a rail spur (the
“Project”) within the Swan Ranch Industrial Park, Cheyenne, Laramie County, Wyoming;
(B)
The Swan Ranch Industrial Park includes approximately Four Thousand (4,000) acres
of land which are potentially subject to development into industrial rail-served lots.  The Swan
Ranch Industrial Park is located generally west and south of the junction between Interstate 25 and
Interstate 80, Cheyenne, Wyoming;
(C)
When the City of Cheyenne submitted its application for a Wyoming Business Ready
Community-Business Committed Loan, it was contemplated that the rail spur to be constructed
pursuant to this Agreement would be approximately 3.8 miles (20,200 feet) in length.  At the time
the City submitted its application, it was understood that the purpose of the rail spur was to connect
existing internal rail facilities which have been constructed by Granite Peak adjacent to a Burlington
Northern/Santa Fe Railroad rail line, which is located generally south of the Swan Ranch Industrial
Park, to internal rail facilities which are currently being constructed by Granite Peak adjacent to a
Union Pacific Railroad Company rail line, which is located generally west of the said industrial
park.  It has since been determined that the rail spur should be re-designed and it is anticipated that
the original purpose of the Project may be accomplished by constructing a rail spur which will be
shorter than 3.8 miles (20,200 feet).  If the combined value of the Loan and Grant funds exceed the
cost of constructing the re-designed rail spur, and subject  to the approval of the WBC, the parties
wish to expend all available Loan and Grant funds for the construction of rail facilities within the
Swan Ranch Industrial Park  In particular, Granite Peak anticipates that excess Loan and Grant funds
