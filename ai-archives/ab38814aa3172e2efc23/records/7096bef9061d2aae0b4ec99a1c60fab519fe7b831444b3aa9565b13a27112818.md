Move to approve the rezoning of a portion of the east half of Section 25, Township 14 North, Range 66

BACKGROUND:

This zone change application proposes changing the zoning from County A-1 Agricultural and Rural
Residential to MR Medium-density Residential for 4.6 acres of vacant land in concert with an annexation. The
land is anticipated to developed as a single-unit residential uses, with associated streets and open areas.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary and existing
infrastructure exists in the neighborhoods to the west. Development will require infrastructure
extensions and improvements.  The Future Land Use Designation for this property is Urban
Residential. The optimal zoning for the proposed future land use designation is any of the
residential zones, including the MR zone.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1.

UDC-21-00116
May 3, 2021
2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

The existing zoning to the south is AR and outside of City limits. The property to the north and
east is zoned A-1 Agricultural and Rural Residential, and is also outside of City limits. The
properties to the west are zoned MR Medium-density Residential and P Public District within
City limits. The future land uses anticipated by the Comprehensive Plan and other official plans
for this area anticipate higher-intensity, urban-style residential uses, and the MR zone is both
compatible with existing zoning in the area and with the future land use designation.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The current County A-1 zone allows limited development options and is intended for low-
intensity rural uses. Generally, parcels in this zone district are not expected to connect to urban
services. The proposed MR zone allows a variety of more urban development options. Also, as
the property is being annexed into the City, a City zone is required.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The standards of Article 4 apply to MR zoning. Due to the undeveloped nature of the land to the
north and east, the subject property would be expected to meet all the standards of Article 4.
The zone change is submitted in concert with a platting application, and the associated plat
shows the area developing in accordance with the standards of Article 4.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The MR zone is intended to develop with a variety of urban-style residential uses. The MR zone
is applicable to areas designated Urban Residential by the Future Land Use Map, and is intended
to be within ½ mile of commercial centers. This property is anticipated to develop with urban-
style uses in the future. The property is within a mile of existing commercial centers located at
Campstool and Christensen and at US 30 and Whitney. The property is about ½ mile away from
an anticipated commercial center which would be located at the intersection of US 30 and
Christensen.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.
