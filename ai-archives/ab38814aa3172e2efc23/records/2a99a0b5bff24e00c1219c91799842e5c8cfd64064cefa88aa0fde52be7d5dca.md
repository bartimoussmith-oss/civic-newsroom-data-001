BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of an expedited preliminary plat for 1.7 acres of land to better align the
Tray More and Waterford Boulevard intersection. The four parcels adjacent to this right-of-way were
unbuildable parcels in the original Waterford Square plat and they continue to be so with this plat. All
agreements, ownership, and restrictions associated with Lots 28 and 29, Block 7, and Lots 1 and 31, Block 5
Waterford Square will be continued to all lots associated with this plat. The intersection adjustment was
stipulated as part of the approval process for Waterford Square, 2nd Filing. The proposed realignment will
allow the intersection to better meet intersection standards.

SURROUNDING LAND USE AND ZONING:

The property is currently zoned NR-3 Neighborhood Residential  High Density. The NR-3 zone is
intended for primarily for high-density, urban residential uses, located close to existing or planned
commercial centers. This zone should be supported by a highly connected street network with street
design types that have a high degree of pedestrian amenities.

UDC-21-00209

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Expedited Plat, the Commission
must make the following findings required in UDC 2.1.2.d and UDC 2.1.3.d a detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan. (UDC 2.1.2.d.1)

PlanCheyenne supports an effective transportation system with appropriate access alignments
and separations. This plat proposes adjusting the Tray More and Waterford intersection to
improve the transportation network and properly align the streets.

The future land use map designates this area of the City with the future land use category of
Mixed-use Commercial and Mixed-use Residential. All lots created by this plat are unbuildable
and are to be used as open space. The provision of open space is an important part of new
developments.

The application complies with this review criterion.

2) The application reflects sound planning and urban design principles with respect to existing
and potential future development in the vicinity, including consideration of current and future
streets, open spaces, blocks and lots, and public utilities or facilities. (UDC 2.1.2.d.2)

The realignment of the Tray More and Waterford intersection improves traffic flow and good
intersection design is an important planning principle.

The application complies with this review criterion.

3) The division of land meets the purposes, intent, and all applicable standards in Article 4. (UDC
2.1.2.d.3)

Transportation Network:
The transportation network in this area of the City is well established and no additions to the
street network are proposed. This plat proposes adjusting the Tray More Boulevard right-of-way
such that the intersection of Tray More and Waterford is better aligned.
Community Facility Fees:
The land being replatted as part of this platting action was originally platted as part of
Waterford Square. The fees and Development Agreement established with Waterford Square
apply to this plat, including the fee payment schedule. No other fees are required for this plat.
Open Space:
This area was reserved for open space and right-of-way by the original Waterford Square plat,
and the proposed plat continues to utilize the entirety of land within the plat boundary for
either open space or right-of-way.
Required Improvements:
This division of land would allow future development to meet all required improvement
standards. The plat provides or maintains sufficient easements for the current utilities.

The application complies with this review criterion.

4)
Any phasing proposed in the application is clearly indicated and demonstrates a logical and
well planned development pattern. This may include consideration of amenities and public
facilities of the subdivision within each phase compared to the anticipated timing or the
likelihood of future phases being constructed as planned. (UDC 2.1.2.d.4)

Phasing is proposed  the central and western portions of this plat are proposed to be
developed first, in concert with development west of Trey More, and the eastern portion is
proposed to be developed later, concurrent with development east of Trey More. The western
portion of the surrounding neighborhood is closer to existing City infrastructure, so it is logical
that the western portion of this plat be the first portion developed. Should the eastern portion
be developed differently, the first-phase development of the western portion would still be
logical.

The application complies with this review criterion.

5) The impacts identified in specific studies or technical reports that were prepared or requested
to be prepared in association with the application, and whether the findings and
recommendations of these reports, or any modifications or conditions on approval of the
application, can mitigate these impacts. (UDC 2.1.2.d.5)

The applicant submitted Drainage and Traffic Worksheets. Engineering found no major issues
with the project as presented. The project is part of the larger Waterford Square development
which submitted studies previously.

The application complies with this review criterion with conditions.

6) Lots proposed for development shall be buildable lots according to the existing or proposed
zoning district standards, result in design and development patterns that meet the Intent and
Applicability statements of the existing or proposed zoning district, and not aggravate
nonconforming setbacks or currently adopted building and fire code access and separation
requirements. (UDC 2.1.2.d.6)

None of the lots proposed by this plat are buildable; however, these lots were originally platted
as unbuildable lots in the original Waterford Square Plat.

The application complies with this review criterion.

7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and accepted by the
City shall have a clearly defined entity responsible for ongoing maintenance.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.7)

None of the proposed lots would be owned by the City. The MOU (City Contract 6406)
establishes that an HOA will maintain these lots.

The application complies with this review criterion.
