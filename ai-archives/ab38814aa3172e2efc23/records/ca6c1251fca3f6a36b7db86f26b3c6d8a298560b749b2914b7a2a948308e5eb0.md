AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO CONTRACT NO. 5737
USE AGREEMENT BETWEEN
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING
AND
ZAYO GROUP, LLC
f/k/a 360NETWORKS, INC.

The following sections of Contract No. 5737 between the City of Cheyenne and Zayo
Group, LLC (formerly known as 360networks, Inc.) dated September 28, 2011 are hereby
amended:

1. Section 1. Definitions.  For the purposes of this agreement, the following terms
shall mean:

b. Company means Zayo Group, LLC, 360networks (USA) inc., a Nevada
Corporation, a Delaware Limited Liability Company, authorized to do business
in the State of Wyoming.

2. Section 2. Permission Granted.

a. The City hereby grants to the Company limited permission to install a fiber
optics telecommunications cable along the public right-of-way in Cheyenne,
Wyoming, for a distance of approximately 24,516 linear feet.  The specific area
where the utility corridor will be installed is contained in the utility corridors
proposed in Exhibits A and B to this agreement, and as specified in applicable as-
builts.  The Company may install additional fiber optic telecommunications
cable along public rights-of-way within the City provided that: (1) the
Company obtains the prior written approval of the City Engineer pursuant
to the issuance of right-of-way encroachment permits in all circumstances
provided for under Chapters 12.04 and 12.12 of the Cheyenne City Code,
and (2) the Company’s installation of the additional fiber optic
telecommunications cable complies with the terms of this agreement.

b.  This Agreement shall have a an initial term of five (5) years commencing upon
execution of this Amendment No. 1 (the “Amendment No. 1 Term”).

3. Section 5. Legal Construction.

This agreement shall not be construed as a comprehensive franchise agreement.
Its terms are limited to the areas and lengths shown in Exhibits A and B, and
any additions thereto as permitted by the City Engineer pursuant to Section
2 of this agreement. approximate 24,516 linear feet of public right-of-way. Any
expansion beyond that initial fiber optics installation shall require another
agreement with the City of Cheyenne.

4. Section 7. Compensation.

Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. 5737 -- Page 2 of 3

As consideration for this the original agreement, Company shall pay to the City
two dollars and fifty cents ($2.50) per lineal foot of conduit or related facilities in
the City right-of-way as shown in Exhibit A, due once as-builts are completed
pursuant to Section 4 of this agreement, and upon each anniversary date of this
agreement until it expires or terminates. Company may pay the entire amount due
of $306,450.00 in one lump sum upon execution of this agreement.

As consideration for this amendment, the Company shall supply the City six
(6) fibers exclusively for the City’s own governmental, non-commercial use
without the right of resale along the routes highlighted in Exhibit B.    For
the avoidance of doubt, no additional consideration shall be required for
Company’s construction and installation of the facilities related to Exhibit B
(as specified in as-builts), for the Amendment No. 1 Term.

As consideration for any future expansions pursuant to Section 2 of the
agreement, the Company shall pay to the City two dollars and fifty cents
($2.50) per lineal foot of additional conduit or related facilities in the City
right-of-way, due once as-builts are completed pursuant to Section 4 of the
agreement, and upon each anniversary date of the agreement until it expires
or terminates.

All other aspects of the original agreement remain unchanged and in full force and effect.

This Amendment shall be in full force and effect when executed by all parties.
