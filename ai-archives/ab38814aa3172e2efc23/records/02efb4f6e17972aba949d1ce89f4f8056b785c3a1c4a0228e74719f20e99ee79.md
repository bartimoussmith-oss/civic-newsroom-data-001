APPROVED
By Michael R. O’Donnell at 3:41 pm, Jul 21, 2021J
RESOLUTION NO.
ENTITLED:
“A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE BOARD
OF PUBLIC UTILITIES TO DISCHARGE UNCOLLECTIBLE DEBTS IN
THE AMOUNT OF $1,138.02 PURSUANT TO WYO. STAT. § 16-4-502.”
WHEREAS, Wyo. Stat. § 16-4-502 provides for discharge of uncollectible debts upon
proper certification to the Governing Body; and
WHEREAS, the City Treasurer has prepared the required certification for uncollectible
debts on the books of the Board of Public Utilities (Board), a copy of which is attached to and
incorporated as part of this Resolution; and
WHEREAS, the debts have been reviewed by the appropriate Board staff and collection
efforts have failed; and
WHEREAS, the Board desires to remove the uncollectible debts from its books as
expediently as possible.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City Treasurer is authorized to discharge the uncollectible
debts in the amount of$ 1,138.02 from the books ofthe City of Cheyenne Board of Public Utilities.
THIS RESOLUTION shall be in full force and effect from and after its passage in
accordance with the laws of the State of Wyoming.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED this
day of
,2021.
Patrick Collins, Mayor
(SEAL)
Attest:
Kristina F. Jones, City Clerk
