ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION TO ENHANCE THE GOVERNING BODY’S OVERSIGHT
OF ITS PUBLIC INVESTMENT IN AND TO IMPROVE SERVICE AT THE
CHEYENNE ANIMAL SHELTER.”
WHEREAS, on June 1$, 201$, the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming (City), entered into an
Agreement with the Cheyenne Animal Shelter/Services (CAS) to provide both animal control and
animal shelter services; and
WHEREAS, the City provides $505,000.00 to CAS to provide these services; and
WHEREAS, CAS is directed by a volunteer Board of Directors, which in turn hires a
President/CEO to oversee and manage daily operations; and
WHEREAS, a recent highly-publicized incident involving an animal attack on a staff
member, and a subsequent training exercise involving the same canine, led to both the canine’s
euthanasia and allegations of animal abuse against the CAS President/CEO; and
WHEREAS, the CAS Board investigated the matter, and held at least two lengthy executive
sessions to discuss the matter, and finally decided to suspend the President/CEO; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body ofthe City of Cheyenne has determined that better board
governance, CAS stafftraining, and more prompt, consequential action by the CAS Board following
the incident could have potentially prevented or more effectively resolved this situation.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, that the City Attorney be directed to work with counsel for CAS to propose an
amendment to the City-CAS contract that adds the following three policy directives into the existing
contract:
1.
Annual governance training for the CAS Board;
2.
An initial audit, and regularly scheduled audits thereafter, of the CAS policies and
procedures by a nationally-recognized organization engaged in the business of animal protection,
control, and shelter services; and
3.
Develop a written crisis management plan to be incorporated into the contract
between CAS and the City. The crisis management plan should include specific actions to be taken
by the CAS Board in the event of a crisis. The plan should provide for the prompt notification of
the crisis to the Governing Body through the City’s appointed board member, and should allow for
his or her participation. The plan will take into consideration the need for the Governing Body’s
involvement in resolving the crisis and account for the CAS Board’s policies and procedures.
