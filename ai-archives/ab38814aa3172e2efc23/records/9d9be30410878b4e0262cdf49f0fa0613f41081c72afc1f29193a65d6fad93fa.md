MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING
BETWEEN LARAMIE COUNTY COMMUNITY COLLEGE AND
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING

1.
Parties. This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is made and entered into by and
between Laramie County Community College (LCCC), whose address is 1400 E College
Drive, Cheyenne, WY 82007, and the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, (City) whose
address is 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001.

2.
Purpose. To set out the responsibilities for LCCC’s CDL “Your Truck” Training
Program to utilize the City’s truck/trailer while receiving instructions from a LCCC
trainer using the City’s location as the base for this training.

LCCC will provide a CDL instructor exclusively to the City in order to provide the
following training:
• Theory training (online) (30 days to complete 17-20 hours of online
training – from home, place of work, etc.)
• Simulator training for shifting, turning, driving, and backing (up to 8
hours – provided on LCCC campus)
• Lecture training (in person) (lectures vary in length, 1-3 hours – provided
on LCCC campus)
• Behind the wheel training (BTW) (in person)
o BTW training can be adjusted based on trainee’s previous
experience and capabilities
o Class A CDL training – Not to exceed 60 hours of BTW training
o Class B, C, and Hotshot training – Not to exceed 50 hours of BTW
training

BTW training does not include all additional training hours that the
trainee receives.  Only BTW hours are logged into the Training Provider
Register (TPR) at the conclusion of training.

Trainee to instructor ratio is 2:1 (maximum)

The program will utilize the City’s trucks and trailers to perform the BTW training. The
BTW training will occur on rural, suburban, city, state highways, and US highways
including the LCCC campus and LCCC training site at the Cheyenne airport as part of
the CDL curriculum.  The radius of training shall be limited 100 miles and is limited to
the State of Wyoming.  In addition, the truck/trailer will be used to demonstrate an
inspection at a Port of Entry during the BTW training.  The City shall ensure that the
vehicle will be registered with the proper authorities.

3.
Term of MOU. This MOU shall commence upon date of last signature and shall remain
in full force and, thereafter, shall automatically renew, unless otherwise terminated.

By Stefanie Boster at 3:43 pm, Nov 14, 2022
