Dear Mayor Collins and Members of City Council;
I am writing on behalf of Billy Jack’s Pizza Pub regarding our company’s entry into a new market
in Cheyenne, Wyoming and to introduce our company to you. Our company redefines fast
casual dining backed by top-notch customer service. We are currently operating in markets
located in Nebraska and exploring locations in Voming and Colorado. We are excited to be
expanding our reach to the Cheyenne area.
Our first location in Cheyenne will be located at the Cheyenne Regional Airport. We are excited
to be associated with this essential transportation hub which will contribute to many sectors of
the state and city’s economy and add to the quality of life for Cheyenne residents, businesses
and visitors.
The benefit of our fast casual dining restaurant being located in the Cheyenne Regional Airport
terminal will help increase the direct economic return to the city of Cheyenne and the greater
Laramie County region as our target customers will be those traveling to/from Cheyenne and
those who call Cheyenne home.
Billy Jack’s Pizza Pub and our fifty local Cheyenne investors look forward to bringing a feeling of
a hometown gathering place to the growth at the Cheyenne Regional Airport. We also look
forward to working with our community partners to benefit all citizens in the Cheyenne
community.
Please feel free to contact me at your earliest to discuss any questions you may have.
