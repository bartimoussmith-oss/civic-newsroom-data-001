```text
RESOLUTION NO. ____________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION DIRECTING ALL REVENUE FROM CONTRACT NO.
5902 TO BE EQUALLY DISBURSED BETWEEN THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE AND THE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTITLITIES AND
EARMARKED
TO
DEVELOP,
PLAN,
BUILD
AND
MAINTAIN
RECREATION FACILITIES AT THE BELVOIR RANCH AND WATER
DEVELOPMENT EXPANSION PROJECTS FOR THE BOARD OF PUBLIC
UTILITIES.”

WHEREAS, the Belvoir Ranch was originally equally purchased by the City of Cheyenne and
The Board of Public Utilities for many purposes, including recreation and water development
expansion; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne and The Board of Public Utilities
desires to establish a funding mechanisms to develop, plan, build, and maintain recreation
infrastructure and facilities at the Belvoir Ranch and water development expansion projects for the
Board of Public Utilities; and

WHEREAS, the Belvoir Ranch currently has a separate line item in the City of Cheyenne Budget
which is managed by the Public Works Department; and

   WHEREAS, the Board of Public Utilities currently operates, maintains and manages irrigation
water wells and water rights on the Belvoir Ranch annually within their budget which is managed by
the Water Treatment Division; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of City of Cheyenne and The Board of Public Utilities wishes
to direct the revenue from City Contract No. 5902 with NextEra Energy to be equally disbursed and
earmarked for the purposes of planning, constructing and maintaining recreation facilities at the Belvoir
Ranch and water development expansion projects for the Board of Public Utilities; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body requests a financial plan for the facilities, including the amount
of revenue received from City Contract No. 5902 and the anticipated use for the earmarked funds,
before any City expenditures are incurred; and

WHEREAS, the Public Works Department is directed to coordinate with the Planning and
Development Department and Community Recreation and Events Department to develop and execute
the plan for the earmarked funds; and

WHEREAS, the official Board of the City of Cheyenne Board of Utilities will review plans for
the Board’s water development expansion projects, including the amount of revenue received from
City Contract No. 5902 and the anticipated use for the earmarked funds, before any Board expenditures
are incurred; and

WHEREAS, the Board of Public Utilities is directed to follow the recommendations for water
development expansion projects as discussed in the most recent copy of the Board’s Master Plan in
addition to the Board’s three-year budget projection plan; and

By Alessandra at 2:18 pm, Mar 08, 2019
```
