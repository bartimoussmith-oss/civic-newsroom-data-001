COMMITTEE OF THE WHOLE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Committee of the Whole was held on Wednesday, May 22, in Council Chambers
and via electronic meeting, starting at 6:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: President Ken Esquibel, Chairman; Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Richard
Johnson, Pete Layboum Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Tom Segrave (via Zoom), and Jeff White
(via Zoom). Absent: Bryan Cook.
AGENDA ITEMS
21.
ORDINANCE —2 READING
-- Appropriating moneys for the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming for the various purposes in conducting the municipal government of said City
and fixing the amount of general and special taxes as part of the revenue required to meet
the said appropriation, all for the Fiscal Year beginning July 1, 2024 and ending June 30,
2025. (SPONSOR
MR. ESQUIBEL)
Mr. White joined the meeting via Zoom at 6:03 p.m.
Mr. Roybal moved to approve on 2 reading, seconded by Mr. Johnson.
Mr. Roybal moved to
increase the City Council Department’s Ward I Discretionary Account by $11,666.67, the Ward II
Discretionary Account by $11,666.67 and the Ward III Discretionary Account by $11,666.66.
I
also move to decrease the Miscellaneous Department’s Insurance and Bonding line item by
$35,000.00, seconded by Mr. Johnson. Motion to amend carried with Dr. Rinne, Mr. Segrave, and
Mr. White voting “NO”. Main motion, as amended carried by unanimous voice vote.
Mayor Patrick Collins provided a brief overview of the proposed fiscal year 2025 budget noting
various revenues, expenses, and the addition positions.
Mayor Collins thanked Wyoming State
Representative Dan Zwonitzer for leading the charge in raising the State distribution from $105
million to $146 million that passed the legislature. Mayor Collins also thanked all the departments
and City Treasurer, Robin Lockman, and her team for their work on the budget and the presenting
document. Dr. Aldrich voiced appreciation to staff for their transparency during the budget process
and continued diligence in seeking out grant opportunities. Robin Lockman provided information
related to the number of operating days in reserves which currently is 227 days.
Committee
members Rinne and Johnson commented on the proposed budget and discussion included the
recent salary study and salary increases; payment of employees out of the Capital Project Funds;
preparing the City for a recession; and parking revenue decreases.
Following the motion to
increase Ward discretionary funds, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, provided information related
to the over budgeting of funds in the insurance and bonding line item and advised that discretionary
funds not used from FY 2024 would be carried over to FY 2025. Committee members Aldrich,
Johnson; Layboum,
Rinne,
Roybal,
Segrave,
and White commented
on the amendment.
Discussion included: funds expended in FY 2024; projects from FY 2024; and the need for funds
beyond $20,000.00.
Additional discussion included that related to the Transit Fund.
