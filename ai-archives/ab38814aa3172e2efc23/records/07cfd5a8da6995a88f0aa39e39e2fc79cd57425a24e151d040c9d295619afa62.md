RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

February 11, 2020

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00 p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of meeting in Executive Session
involving personnel matters relating to collective labor bargaining with the Cheyenne Professional
Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR
- Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Mr. Esquibel, Mr. Laybourn, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr.
Roybal, Mr. Shanor and Mr. White. Absent:  Mr. Case and Mr. Cook.  Also present: Kris Jones,
City Clerk; Michael O’Donnell, City Attorney; Alessandra McCoy Fakelman, Deputy City
Attorney; Robin Lockman, City Treasurer and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.
The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised she would entertain a motion to go into executive session.  Pursuant to
Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Shanor moved to meet in executive session, seconded
by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present.

Mayor Orr announced there will be no action taken by the governing body as a result of
the executive session, therefore, the special meeting will be officially adjourned following closure
of the executive session.

Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body present, the City Clerk,
City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, Fire and Rescue Department Fire Chief and the City
Treasurer would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio recording of the
special meeting was stopped; Executive Session convened at 6:03 p.m. in Council Chambers]

Following closure of the executive session, Dr. Rinne, Council President, reconvened the
special meeting at 7:07 p.m.  [City Clerk note: Mayor Orr relinquished duties of the Chair to Dr.
Rinne, Council President, at 6:41 p.m. due to a previous commitment]

Dr. Rinne stated the purpose of the special meeting had been met, and there being no
business previously noted for the special meeting, adjourned the meeting at 7:08 p.m.

Submitted by,

Kristina F. Jones,

City Clerk

This notice is available in alternative, accessible formats upon request.

Published:
Wyoming Tribune-Eagle

February 15, 2020
