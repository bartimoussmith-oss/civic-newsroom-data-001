PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Public Services Committee was held on Tuesday, March 19,2024, in Committee
Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Scott Roybal, Chairman, Pete Laybourn, Dr. Rinne, and Tom
Segrave.
AGENDA ITEMS
9.
ORDINANCE _3rd READING
— Amending Section 10.12.160, Authority to Impound
Illegally Parked Vehicles, and Section 10.12.170, Immobilization, of Chapter 10.12,
Administration and Enforcement, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the Municipal Code
of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, modifying specified enforcement provisions regarding
vehicle impoundment.
Mr. Segrave moved to approve on 3I and final reading, seconded by Mr. Laybourn. Motion carried
by unanimous voice vote.
Captain David Janes, Police Department, provided a staff report and John Brodie.
Chairman
Roybal and committee member Rinne commented on the item.
