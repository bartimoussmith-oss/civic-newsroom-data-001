APPENDIX  “A”
SOUTHEAST WYOMING FOSTER GRANDPARENT PROGRAM
Program:
Helping Community
Mission:
The Foster Grandparent Program provides opportunities for limited income persons
60 years of age and over to give supportive person-to-person service in health,
education, welfare and related settings to help alleviate the physical, mental or
emotional problems of children having exceptional or special needs.
Program Purpose:
To provide meaningful volunteer opportunities to low-income senior citizens
to serve children with special needs by providing one-on-one attention
needed by the individual children who are at risk of academic failure.
