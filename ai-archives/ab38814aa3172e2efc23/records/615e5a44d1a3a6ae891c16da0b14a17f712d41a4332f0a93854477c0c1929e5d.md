Annual Reports:  Consultant shall submit a draft copy (electronic is acceptable) of a
comprehensive annual report to the City by March 1 of each contract year.  Consultant shall
incorporate City comments and submit one electronic and one paper copy of final report (if paper
draft, page changes are acceptable) to the City and paper and electronic deliverables to WDEQ
per their requirements by April 1 of each contract year.

Event Reports:  Consultant shall submit a draft copy (electronic is acceptable) of a brief
summary event report within 30-days of receipt of complete and accurate analytical results for
each April, August and December monitoring event.  Consultant shall incorporate City
comments and submit one paper copy and electronic deliverables to WDEQ per their
requirements not later than 30-days following submission of draft.

Compensation rates will remain the same as original contract as outlined in Exhibit B,
Compensation.

IV.  Summary:

This modification increases the amount of the contract by $209,220 and extends the contract for
one additional three-year period.  This modification includes groundwater sampling & analysis,
groundwater gauging, methane monitoring and reports at the same frequency and number as
included in original contract.  The total cost of this contract will not exceed $418,440 without a
written change order signed by both parties.
