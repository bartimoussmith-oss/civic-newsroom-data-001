RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

July 22, 2024

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers and via electronic conference meeting.  Present were: MAYOR
– Patrick Collins, COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Bryan Cook, Ken Esquibel,
Pete Laybourn, Dr. Mark Rinne, Scott Roybal, Tom Segrave and Jeff White (via Zoom). Absent:
Mr. Johnson.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda). Mr. Roybal moved to approve, seconded by Dr. Aldrich.  Motion
carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present.

[CA] Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on J uly 8, 2024.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.

 ORDINANCE –3rd READING – Amending Section 10.16.150, Parking Violation
Hearings and Appeal Procedures, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming,
modifying the procedures governing the Administrative Review of Parking Citations, making
conforming amendments  and repealing an obsolete provision.  Reporting for Public Services
Committee, Mr. Roybal moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by Mr. Segrave.
Motion carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body present. (#4581)

 ORDINANCE –3rd READING – Creating Section 10.60.015, Paid On -Street Parking
Districts; Establishment, Administration, of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming, establishing the Paid On- Street Parking Districts Program and making conforming
amendments as specified.  Mr. Roybal moved to approve on third and final reading, seconded by
Mr. Segrave. Mr. Roybal moved to amend by substitute dated July 10, 2024, seconded by M r.
Segrave. Motion to amend by substitute carried. Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present. Main motion as amended by substitute carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Public comment was made by: Jared Schaefer. Comments were made by
the following members of the governing body:  Dr. Aldrich, Mr. Cook, Mr. Esquibel  and Mr.
Segrave (#4582)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Certifying compliance with Wyoming Statute § 15 -1-402 for the City-
initiated annexation of tracts of land situate in portions of Sections 4 and 5, T13N, R67W, 6th
P.M., Laramie County, Wyoming (also known as ‘North Range Business Park’, located northwest
of the I-80 and Roundtop Road interchange). Motion (per Consent Agenda) carried. (#6418)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Expressing the support of the Governing Body for the submission of a
petition by the Governing Body to the Downtown Development Authority for inclusion of property
in the Downtown Development District. Motion (per Consent Agenda) carried. (#6419)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Supporting the Clean Crow Creek Initiative on behalf of the Governing
Body for the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming – referred to Public Services Committee. ( Sponsors –
Dr. Aldrich, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Roybal, and Mr. White)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Amending the City of Cheyenne 2023 Program Year Annual Action
Plan, prepared by the Cheyenne Housing & Community Development Office – referred to Finance
Committee. (Sponsor – Mr. Esquibel)

[CA] RESOLUTION – Authorizing submission of an application to the Wyoming State Loan
and Investment Board for a loan from the Wyoming Drinking Water State Revolving Fund for the
Hypochlorite Generation Project in the amount of four million dollars and authorizing the Director
of the Board of Public Utilities to execute the application and all related documents – referred to
Finance Committee. (Sponsor – Dr. Rinne)

[CA] Minimum Revenue Guaranteed Payment Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and
the Cheyenne Regional Air Service Focus Team (CRAFT) for Fiscal Year Ending June 30, 2025.
(2021 Specific Purpose Option Tax Fund) . Motion (per Consent Agenda) carried  (in an amount
not to exceed $400,000.00). (#8010)

 Land Lease between the City of Cheyenne and Swan Ranch Cheyenne Rail, L.L.C. which
