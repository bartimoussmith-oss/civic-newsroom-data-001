RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

February 26, 2020

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Rocky Case, Pete
Laybourn, Mike Luna, Dicky Shanor, and Jeff White.  Absent:  Bryan Cook, Ken Esquibel, Dr.
Mark Rinne, and Scott Roybal.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Michael O’Donnell, City
Attorney; Alessandra McCoy-Fakelman, Deputy City Attorney, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer,
and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr requested those who may be speaking to use the microphones so that their
comments can be heard.  Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available
on Facebook live and stated that Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings
Act allows the governing body to meet in executive session to consider or tender offers regarding
salaries, wages or other terms and conditions of employment during negotiations with the fire
fighters union and that any motion approved by the governing body to move into executive session
during the special meetings would be pursuant to this section of statute.  Mayor Orr further advised
if the governing body met in executive session, they would be meeting in the Mayor’s Conference
Room, and that Fire Union representatives were welcome to use Committee Room 104 for the
purpose of meeting to caucus.

TJ Haws, Union representative, advised copies of their counter proposal will be presented
to members of the governing body immediately following duplication. Comments were made
regarding process of transferring documents back and forth in either hard or electronic format and
it was mutually agreed upon to use hard copies moving forward.  Mayor Orr requested all hard
copy documents be ready for presentation prior to the beginning of future meetings.

Mayor Orr offered brief legislative updates during the pause while waiting for Union
documents to be duplicated.

Upon inquiry by Union representatives, Mayor Orr and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and
Rescue Department, provided information relating to the City’s proposal for Sections #3-Exclusive
Bargaining Agent, #8-Grievance Procedures, #13-Management Rights, #15-Department Staffing,
and #17-Hours of Work.  Discussion included provided answers to specific questions.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Shanor moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body
present, the City Clerk, City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer, and Chief of the Fire
and Rescue Department would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio
