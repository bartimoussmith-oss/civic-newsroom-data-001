CONTRACT NO. _______

THIS AGREEMENT is made by and between the THE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, hereinafter referred to as “BOPU,” and POLO RANCH
COMPANY, hereinafter referred to as “Polo Ranch.”

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Polo Ranch and the City of Cheyenne are parties to a Drilling and Water
Use Agreement entered into in 1955; and

WHEREAS, over the past several years there have been numerous disputes between the
parties which have led to litigation and multiple court cases; and

WHEREAS, the Wyoming courts have upheld the validity of the 1955 agreement and the
City’s interpretation of its meaning; and

WHEREAS, the Polo Ranch now seeks to develop twelve (12) lots, for a total of not
more than twelve (12) wells, in the Quarter Circle Five subdivision; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has an interest in additional development for the City
and its residents; and

WHEREAS, the parties wish to enter into this Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is hereby
acknowledged by each party, the parties agree as follows:

1. BOPU/City agrees to allow Polo Ranch to develop the twelve (12) lots with (12) twelve
corresponding wells in the Quarter Circle Five subdivision. See attached Exhibit.
2. This change in use does not affect the validity or enforceability of the City’s water rights
under the 1955 Agreement and does not increase the City’s obligations under the 1955
Agreement.
3. This change in use applies to Polo Ranch’s entire allocation of water per the formula
under the 1955 Agreement.
4. BOPU/City shall have no obligation to provide water to or for the Polo Ranch, for any
use, including water for the lots in Quarter Circle Five, in excess of that determined by
the formula in the 1955 Agreement.
5. The deduction from the Polo Ranch’s annual water entitlement for the Quarter Circle
Five subdivision lots shall remain .7 acre feet per lot.
6. The Polo Ranch agrees to add deed restrictions to each lot sold to prevent further splitting
of the twelve (12) lots.
7. The Polo Ranch agrees to restrict the size of the lawns of the twelve (12) lots within
Quarter Circle Five subdivision to a maximum of 6,500 square feet.
By Stefanie Boster at 2:54 pm, Feb 18, 2022
