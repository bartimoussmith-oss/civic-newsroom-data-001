MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From:
Charles W. Bloom, AICP, Planning and Development Director
Subject: Memorandum of Understanding between the City of Cheyenne and the Downtown
Development Authority
Date:
September 23, 2024

The item for consideration is a Memorandum of Understanding between the City and the Downtown
Development Authority (DDA).

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral: September 23, 2024
•
Finance Committee: October 7, 2024
•
Final consideration: October 14, 2024

The purpose of this MOU is to coordinate to the repair of sidewalks identified as trip hazards by the City
Engineer’s office and to allow the City to receive reimbursement for all sidewalk repairs made by the City’s
contractor. Reimbursement to the City will come from the Downtown Development Authority (DDA) and
the property owner at a 50:50 ratio.  Pursuant to Cheyenne City Code 12.04.120.b the City will be
responsible for any   curb and gutter repair associated with a sidewalk reconstruction. Responsibilities
outlined in the MOU are summarized below.

DDA Responsibilities:
•
Property owner notification on behalf of the City Engineer.
•
Collect payment from affected property owners in advance of work and relay payment to the City
for services.
•
Coordinate with the City’s contractor the timing repairs to minimize business impact.
•
Coordinate the project with the City.

City Responsibilities:
•
Follow its procurement policy to identify as contractor qualified to make sidewalk, curb, and gutter
repairs.
•
Contract with a contractor to complete repairs to curbs, gutters, and sidewalks in problem areas.
•
Seek reimbursement from the DDA for the cost of the repairs made (Not to exceed the DDA’s
$100,000 budget allocation).

At the time of preparation of this memo, the DDA Board had not considered this MOU. The MOU will be
reviewed by the DDA Board at their September 19, 2024, meeting. An update and any necessary
substitute MOU will be provided at the September 23, 2024, Finance Committee meeting.

WHEREAS, the City’s miscellaneous concrete program funding has been considered to
assist DDA property owners in funding curb and gutter repairs;

WHEREAS, DDA staff have considered DDA Capital Improvement Grant (CIG) funding
and have determined that that grant program is not an appropriate funding source.
Notwithstanding, the DDA has designated up to one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) in its
budget to fund sidewalk repairs;

WHEREAS, DDA staff will work with property owners to fund fifty (50%) of sidewalk
repairs and will work with property owners to fund attendant curb and gutter repair ; and,

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

1.
Purpose.  The purpose of this MOU is for the City and the DDA to coordinate for
the repair of the sidewalks identified as trip hazards by the City Engineer’s Office, the necessary
repair of curb and gutter associated with the sidewalk repairs, and to receive appropriate
reimbursement for all sidewalk repairs made by the City’s contractor.

2.
Term.  This MOU shall commence on the date last executed by the duly
authorized representative of each party to this MOU and shall remain in full force until
terminated as provided herein.

3.
Payments.  The DDA will reimburse the City for its costs in completing the
sidewalk repairs in the specified locations in an amount not to exceed one hundred thousand
dollars ($100,000).

4.
Responsibilities of the Downtown Development Authority ("DDA").  The
DDA shall:
a.
 Provide notice to identified property owners of the program and their
fiscal responsibility if they elect not to participate in this program, as per Cheyenne City
Code.
b.
Be responsible for securing payment from property owners for their
portion of the cost of these repairs prior to commencement of work.
c.
Reimburse the City for the actual cost of sidewalk repairs made by any
contractor under the program in a total amount not to exceed one hundred thousand
dollars ($100,000).
