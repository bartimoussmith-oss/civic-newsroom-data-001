RESOLUTION NO. ________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE ‘CRUISE THE LEGEND’ EVENT
HELD IN CHEYENNE.”

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” is a Saturday night downtown event that was started in
April 2020 as an idea for the community to break the social isolation caused by COVID-19; and

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” is a COVID safe community activity and is good, clean
family fun, and is promoted by Visit Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” draws classic and antique vehicles, is an opportunity to
see some amazing vehicles, and is reminiscent of growing up in Cheyenne; and

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” raises funds for non-profit organizations and there are
approximately twenty-nine different non-profit organizations that will benefit this year; and

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” is an effort to bring the community to the downtown
area and businesses and restaurants in the downtown area benefit from the increased foot traffic;
and

WHEREAS, “Cruise the Legend” is scheduled to begin Saturday, April 17, 2021, and
end on Saturday October 30, 2021; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne supports the “Cruise the
Legend” event.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne hereby supports the “Cruise
the Legend” event.
