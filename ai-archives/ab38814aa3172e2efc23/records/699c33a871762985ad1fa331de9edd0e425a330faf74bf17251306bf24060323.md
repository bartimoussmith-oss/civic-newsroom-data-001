PUDC-25-122

September 2, 2025
RECOMMENDED MOTION:

Move to recommend the Governing Body approve the assigned zoning for land south of Dell Range
Boulevard, west of Dry Creek, east of North College Drive, and north of Cheyenne Street to AR
Agricultural Residential, MR Medium-density Residential, MUR Mixed-Use Residential, and MUB Mixed-
use Business as shown in the attached zone change map, noting that the project meets the review
criteria for approval.

APPLICABLE CITY CODE SECTION(S) AND PLANS:

•
UDC 2.2.8 Assigned Zoning

BACKGROUND:
The City is annexing parcels that are 100% surrounded by current City Limits as part of Councils goals to
annex county pockets. Annexation of a parcel requires a city zone to be established. Per UDC 2.2.8 a zone will
be assigned based on the underlying county zone and per the policy document adopted by the city.

ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The assigned zone is the most analogous City zone to the current zone when considering
allowed uses. Zoning for standard County zones shall be assigned based on the policy
document, while zones not listed on the policy document and terminated City zones shall be
assigned a City zone on a case-by-case basis. (UDC 2.2.8.d.1)

The underlying county zones at the time this project was started are AR Agricultural Residential,
MR Medium-density Residential, MU Mixed-use and PUD Planned Unit Development. The most
analogous city zones are AR-Agricultural Residential, MR Medium-density Residential and MUR
Mixed-use Residential according to the policy document for the corresponding county zones.
The PUD was analyzed by Staff and the closest city zone was determined to be MUB Mixed-use
Business. The PUD only allows one specific use which is ministorage. The MUB zone allows
ministorage as a conditional use. Staffs reasoning for going with MUB is because the property to
the west was once originally part of the PUD before it was annexed to the City and is currently
zoned MUB, both properties are owned by the same organization, and both properties are part
of the development. Rather than give a property split zoning staff believe that having both as
MUB would be beneficial.

The Assigned Zoning policy document is attached to the staff report as attachment number two.
The County PUD is also attached as attachment number three.

Establishment of the City zone matches the policy document; therefore, the item complies with
review criterion 1.

STAFF REVIEW:

Through the review process, there were no comments.
