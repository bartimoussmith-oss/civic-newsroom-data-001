10. Consideration of a correction of the record for a zone change for Tract

Bryce Hamilton, Planning, said on Dec 5, 2023, staff presented applications for two
zone changes, a vacation, administrative plat approval, and approval of use for an RV
storage unit for Toybox Storage. Mr. Hamilton pointed out staff had made an error in
the proposed language for the zone change motion which was approved by the board
at the Dec 5 meeting. He noted the error in the board's previously approved motion
could result in Tract 1 of Larimore Subdivision not receiving one of the two intended
zone changes. He said staff wanted to ensure the record of proceedings included the
correct language approving the required zone change.
A motion was made by Commissioner Heath, seconded by Commissioner
Holmes, to adopt the findings of fact a, b, and c of the staff report and
approve the zone change from CB to MU for Tract 1, Larimore

vote:
Lovett, Malm, Heath, Holmes

Thompson

12. PUBLIC HEARING regarding a subdivision permit and plat for Open
View Ranch, situated at the N1/2 of Sect 7, T14N, R63W, of the 6th
PM, Laramie County, WY.
24 - 005
Open View Ranch, Subdivision Permit and Plat
Attachments:
Bryce Hamilton, Planning, said Steil Surveying Services, LLC, on behalf of Whiskey
Hill, LLC, submitted a subdivision permit and plat application for property located on
the east side of County Rd 142, between County Rds 212 and 213, about one mile
south of Hillsdale. He said the submitted application is to subdivide the 397.73-acre
property into 22 residential lots of roughly 14.22 acres each. The southeast portion of
the property on the S1/2 of Sect 7 would remain unplatted.
Mr. Hamilton said based on evidence provided, staff recommends approval of the
subdivision permit and plat with the adoption of findings of fact a and b, and the
condition that a non-adverse Chapter 23 recommendation is provided by DEQ prior
to recordation.
Chairman Lovett opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Melissa Anderson,
Laurie Jackson and Wyatt Jackson residents, raised questions and concerns about
school capacities, EMT services, postal service, water issues, the board's long-range
plan, road maintenance, additional traffic, snow removal, and need for more study of
wildlife in the area.
Commissioner Health explained how outside entities, such as school and fire districts,
are given the opportunity to comment on the application. If there is no comment, then it
is assumed there are no issues. Commissioner Health said EMT services would be
through AMR and postal service is not under the board's control. Commissioner
Holmes addressed the water issue, stating the AMEC memo recommendation is five
acres for the recharge rate of normal household usage. The proposed tracts exceed
the recommendation. He noted the state engineer monitors the water table level.
Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Thompson closed the hearing.
A motion was made by Commissioner Holmes, seconded by
Commissioner Heath, to adopt findings of fact a and b of the staff report
and to approve the subdivision permit and plat for Open View Ranch with
the one condition as outlined in the staff report. The motion carried by the
following vote:
