Subject: North Range Business Park Annexation

Background:

The item for consideration is a resolution to allow the City to proceed with a City-initiated annexation
pursuant to Wyoming Statute § 15-1-402. North Range Business Park utilizes outside user agreements
with BOPU to provide services. These agreements stipulate that the land owners agree to be annexed
once contiguous with the City. With an annexation earlier in 2024, the City annexed land south of the
park, making the business park contiguous to City limits for the purposes of state statute.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and referral
July 8, 2024
•
Public Services Committee
July 16, 2024
•
Final consideration

July 22, 2024

On March 29, 2024, City staff mailed a notice to the affected agencies pursuant to the Outside User
Agreement dated May 5, 2005, and recorded at Book 1882, Page 18.

According to state statute, the City may annex a property under 40 acers without landowner consent, as
long as a majority of owners within the annexation do not protest the annexation. Any property over 40
acres which pays property taxes and is not 100% surrounded by City limits requires written consent from
the landowner. The properties meeting these requirements serviced by BOPU have given written
consent in their Outside User Agreement. There is one adjacent property which does not currently have
an Outside User Agreement which has been added to this annexation for convenience of the landowner,
and that owner has given written consent to this annexation.

Annexation also requires establishing a City zone. The current County zone is a PUD and requires
consideration to properly modify into City zoning.

Attachments:
1. Resolution
2. Map of properties
3. Existing Outside User Agreements
4. Written consent for property over 40 acres not subject to an Outside User Agreement
