MEMO
To:
Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council

Subject: Contract Modification #4 to Contract #7437, to add a parking requirement
Date:
November 25, 2024

Background:
The City designated land located northeast of the intersection of Morrie Avenue and Lincolnway as
surplus on July 27, 2020 (Resolution #6076). On February 22, 2021, the Governing Body authorized the
sale and transfer of the property to Brandon Swain (Resolution #6124). The purchase and sale
agreement subsequently entered into (Contract #7437) contained 5 conditions the buyer had to meet
with the development of the property (Section 7):
a. City has a right of first refusal on any sale of the property.
b. Buyer must apply for a zone change and a future land use map amendment.
c. Development of the property must include a mixed-use building, at least 3 stories in height and
at with at least 12 residential units.
d. Development of the property must be compatible with Holliday Park.
e. Buyer agrees to subdivide the Real Property from the Holliday Park property

In reviewing the various development scenarios for the site, the Buyer has requested the UU Overlay
zone for height and setback flexibility; however, the UU zone also allows for significant parking
flexibility. At Public Services Committee on November 19, 2024, the Public Services Committee
expressed a desire to allow the setback and height flexibility, but not the parking flexibility.

As the City is the landowner selling the property, and as the City has already placed conditions for the
development of the property, the City may add more mutually-agreeable conditions on the contract for
the sale of the property. This contract modification adds an additional condition on the Buyer’s
responsibilities to develop the site, that is: the buyer must meet the parking standards of the Unified
Development Code regardless of the overlay.

The item for consideration is a Contract Modification.
