Service:
Peak Wellness Center Emergency Mental Health Services operated 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
An agreement with the Cheyenne Police Department provides for Peak staff to assist law enforcement
officers with all emergency mental health detentions, thus reducing the amount of time that officers
are required to wait in the hospital emergency room.
This service is available to anyone in need and is accessed by calling Peak Wellness Center.  After-
hours calls go to an answering service that connects the caller with the on-call therapist.  Due to the
increasing number of calls and the speciality skills required for this emergency service, we have
assigned the responsibility for it to our Intensive Case Management Team during the after-hours
coverage times.  This has proved to be very advantageous to clients, law enforcement and other
referral sources, hospital staff, and Center staff.
