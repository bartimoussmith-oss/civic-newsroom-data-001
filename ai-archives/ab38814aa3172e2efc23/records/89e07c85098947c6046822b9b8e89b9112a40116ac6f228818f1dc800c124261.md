```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

City of Cheyenne Governing Body
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

August 4, 2021
SUBJECT:
Final Fiscal Year 2021 Re-Appropriation to be considered for approval

On August 9, 2021, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee
to re-appropriate funds for last year’s City of Cheyenne budget per W.S. 16-4-112 and 16-4-113.
Below is more detailed information regarding this re-appropriation request.

GENERAL FUND - TRANSFERS FROM OTHER DEPARTMENTS
Fire Department exceeded their Fiscal Year 2021 approved budget
• $60,000 – the expenditures in the Fire Department’s payroll accounts exceeded their
budget.  This is a result of health insurance coverage changes, firefighters becoming
eligible for new “additional pays”, and other unanticipated payroll costs.   The Police
Department has excess funds and will be used to cover this shortage.

GENERAL FUND – ADDITIONAL REVENUE AND RESERVES
Parking Garage Bond Refunding
• The city needs to budget $5,880,000 in Fiscal Year 2021 for the following:
o $5,800,000 toward debt defeasance of the 2012 Series Bonds (to be paid from the
Miscellaneous Department through the “Loan and Bond Payment” account).
o $80,000 for the cost of bond issuance (fees for bond counsel, the municipal advisor,
the trustee/paying agent as well as arbitrage rebate calculations).
• To pay for these costs, the City sold the new 2021 Series Bonds, which were purchased by
three local banks for $5,455,000, as follows:
o $1,210,000 from ANB Bank,
o $1,855,000 from Wyoming Bank and Trust, and
o $2,390,000 from Platte Valley Bank.
• The difference, or $425,000, was paid for by the City’s 2012 Bond Reserve Fund, held at
Wyoming Bank and Trust.  This reserve fund is meant to help offset the cost of the final
“balloon” payment on April 1, 2028.  This account balance was $970,500, which was
liquidated and closed on April 1, 2021.  A new 2021 Bond Reserve Fund was opened and
only required $545,500 of the liquidated funds as the new balloon payment is less than the
previous balloon payment.  The difference between these two amounts ($425,000) was
essentially added to General Fund reserves.  Therefore, the amount needed has been
budgeted from “Revenue from Reserves” to cover the difference.
```

```text
OTHER FUNDS - RESERVES
Annexation Loan Fund interest costs
The City of Cheyenne received a request on May 6, 2021 to refund a deposit made in 1999 for
annexation improvements.  This deposit, in the amount of $8,068.50, was made on August 16,
1999.  Ordinance 4221 was approved on March 26, 2018 which directed the following:  if the off-
site infrastructure improvements are not constructed by the tenth fiscal year after the cash deposit
was made, the person (or legally entitled representative) making such cash deposit may request a
refund of their deposit, plus interest earned.  Therefore, I calculated the interest owed based on
historical one-year treasury rates since 1999 in the amount of $4,241 and paid this, plus the original
deposit, to the requestor.
As always, if you have any questions, please give me a call at 637-6336 or email at
rlockman@cheyennecity.org.
```
