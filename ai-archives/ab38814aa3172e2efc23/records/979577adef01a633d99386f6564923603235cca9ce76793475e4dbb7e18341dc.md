Esteemed members of Cheyenne City Council and Mayor Collins,
My name is Brian Eicholtz.
I represent Capitol Cuisine LLC and am pleased to come
before you with a proposal of my application for a Retail License to be used at Ike’s Bar & Grill
at 122 w 6 St. Cheyenne WY 82007. My intent is to provide alcoholic beverages with amazing
food in a safe and entertaining environment. Adding a sorely needed establishment to the
South Side of Cheyenne for residents to enjoy a late night drink at a friendly and safe
environment.
I was born and raised in Cheyenne WY on a glorious August evening in 1978. Graduated
from Cheyenne Central in 1997. (Don’t hold that against me Mr. Mayor). Lettering in Basketball
and Soccer. Sticking to my home roots attended the University of Wyoming. After graduation
I returned with my family and was the Bar Manager of Jacksons All American Sports Bar & Grill
here in Cheyenne. 1 year later I was given the promotion of Area Coach to South Eastern
Wyoming Pizza Hut. After 7 years on the job, I decided
I had had enough of the cooperate life
and decided on a personal venture of Capitol Cuisine located at 1720 Capitol Ave here in
Cheyenne WY. After 3 years of supporting and loving downtown Cheyenne I was graced the
opportunity to renovate and create my true passion. Ike’s Bar & Grill was born. Since October
of 2020 I have invested countless hours and finances to create my dream. New flooring,
bathrooms, paint and countless other modifications. We have also been approved to add an
outdoor dining patio.
I look forward to many more years as both a resident of Cheyenne and a
proud member of Downtown Cheyenne.
I am the proud parent of 2 children and 6 stepchildren. With our split family household,
we see All parts of Cheyenne.
My wife Kaylan is not only the glue of our family but finds time
to help at the restaurant and keep up with her career as the Events and Marketing Director of
the Cheyenne Depot Museum. Kaylan is also a graduate of both Cheyenne Central and the
University of Wyoming. Poke pride runs deep in our household.
I appreciate everyone’s time as you entertain my passion.
I cannot imagine a more
