Section 6.  That Section 5.7.10 of the UDC is amended to read as follows:

5.7.10 [RESERVED] Recreational Vehicle Parks and Campgrounds

In districts where recreational vehicle parks and campgrounds are allowed, they shall meet the
following standards in addition to all other lot and building standards for the district.

a. All property lines and street frontages shall have a level 2 landscape buffer as specified in UDC
6.3.5.b.

b. Accessory uses such as laundry buildings, service retail stores, manager's offices, storage
buildings, caretaker facilities, cabins, and other uses common with recreational vehicle parks
and campgrounds shall be permitted.

c. A network of vehicular accessways shall be provided and individual sites shall not have direct
access to a public street. All accessways shall be paved with either asphalt, concrete, permeable
pavers, or a combination thereof. Accessways shall not encroach into a required landscape
buffer, except as necessary to provide access to the site.

d. A developed outdoor recreational area(s) shall be provided and shall be at least 10% of the
gross development area or 4,500 square feet, whichever is greater. Outdoor recreational spaces
shall be comprised of playgrounds, picnic areas, trails, sports facilities, and similar amenities.

e. One fenced pet area(s) per acre of developed site shall be provided and shall be at least 700
square feet in area.

f. At least one (1) canopy tree within fifteen (15) feet of each space shall be provided.

g. Designated areas for tents or cabins shall provide a minimum campsite area of five hundred
(500) square feet per tent or cabin. Campsite areas may be consolidated when tents or cabins
are clustered, provided that the required square footage per unit is maintained, and the area is
exclusive of drive aisles, parking spaces, or common access ways.
