Consideration to Purchase between the City of Cheyenne, and Critical Facilities Technology /
Schneider Electric, for the Cheyenne Public Safety Center, Modular Power Revitalization Service.

This purchase is to revitalize the Uninterrupted Power Supply (UPS) system and server batteries
at the Cheyenne Public Safety Center, that keep the buildings servers operational in the event of a
loss of power.

This vendor has hands on experience working on the intricate server and battery system at the
Cheyenne Public Safety Center, therefore they are recommended as a Single Source for this
revitalization.

The total amount of the purchase is $92,526.00.

One Time State Distribution Funds
