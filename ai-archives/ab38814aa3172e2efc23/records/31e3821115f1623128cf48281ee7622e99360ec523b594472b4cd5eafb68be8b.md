Consideration of a resolution approving Wyoming Horse Racing, LLC,
to expand its operations at 5640 High Plains Rd.
25 - 032
Resolution, Wyoming Horse Racing, LLC, Expansion
Swan Ranch
Attachments:
County Attorney Mark Voss gave an overview of the request. He said it was an
expansion of an existing location. Voss said if the board approves the request, the
applicant would need to go before the WY Gaming Commission for final approval.
Nick Hughes, applicant, spoke about reasons for expanding the current location.
Hughes talked about the future expansion of the location and using the Historic Horse
Racing (HHR) revenue to build a theater, convention meeting rooms, and hotel.
Hughes said the expansion will go in phases. He noted the economic benefits to the
county and state.
Commissioner Hollingshead asked about the time frame for the build-out phases.
Hughes said the construction would be dependent on the weather and may take 18
months to two years.
Chairman Malm opened the hearing for PUBLIC COMMENT. Maeke Ermarth,
resident, spoke about the project. She said the project is moving too fast to make a
final decision and expressed concerns about transparency, potential for money
laundering and water.
Chairman Malm said the applicant to go to the Gaming Commission. He said the
applicant presented the project details in Nov 2024 and before the city council about
three months ago. He said the project is located in Cheyenne and BOPU will supply
the water. He stated the expansion will bring out-of-state dollars and support the
growing tourism industry.
