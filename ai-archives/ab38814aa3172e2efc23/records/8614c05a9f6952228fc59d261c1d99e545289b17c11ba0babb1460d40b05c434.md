AMENDMENT TO CITY CONTRACT NO. 7155
SPONSORSHIP AGREEMENT BETWEEN THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
AND SPRADLEY BARR TOYOTA
This agreement amends City Contract No. 7155, dated March 25, 2020, between the City of
Cheyenne, Wyoming, and Spradley Barr Toyota, to replace the current language in paragraph 5,
Events, with the following language:
5.
Events.
A.
As a 2020-2021 Platinum Venue Sponsor to assist in purchasing a film projector, in
exchange for Thirty-Five Thousand Dollars ($35,000.00) of sponsorship revenue to the City of
Cheyenne, Spradley Barr Toyota will receive:
•
Promotional vendor space at all 2020-2021 film series shows (i.e., one car outside
of Civic Center) two hours prior to the show and removed following the show.
•
Four Facebook posts, two organic and two sponsored.
•
Logo recognition on one indoor banner.
•
Logo recognition on one exterior venue banner.
•
Promotional material may be distributed to film series attendees (i.e., flyer, keychain,
etc.). Promotional material must be provided by the sponsor to the Cheyenne Civic
Center marketing team one week prior to showing.
•
Logo recognition in out-of-town marketing endeavors. (E-mail blasts to out of
town/state patrons. E-blasts are distributed to approx. 30,000 people.)
•
Four comp tickets per film series show.
•
Pre-recorded announcement before each show. Sponsor will be recognized by name
in the pre-show overhead announcement.
•
Sponsor commercial to be played before each film and in the lobby. Assets must be
provided by the sponsor to the Cheyenne Civic Center marketing team one week
prior to showing.
All other terms and conditions of the existing agreement shall remain unchanged and in full
force and effect.
1
By Alessandra at 10:07 am, Jun 18, 2020
