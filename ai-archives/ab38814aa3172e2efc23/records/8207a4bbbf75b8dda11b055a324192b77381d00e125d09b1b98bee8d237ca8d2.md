```text
ANALYSIS OF APPLICATION CONFORMANCE WITH REVIEW CRITERIA:

Should the Planning Commission wish to recommend approval of the Zoning Map Amendment, the
Commission must make the following findings required in UDC 2.2.1.d. A detailed analysis is below:

1) The application is in accordance with the development policies and physical patterns
identified in the Comprehensive Plan.  (UDC 2.2.1.d.1)

PlanCheyenne encourages growth within the Urban Service Boundary and the maximization of
existing infrastructure. This property is within the Urban Service Boundary, in an area with
existing infrastructure. PlanCheyenne also encourages infill development.

The Future Land Use designation for this property is currently Public; however, it is anticipated
that the designation for this area of the community will be changed to Mixed-use Commercial
(MU-C). The MU-C designation recognizes that the location will likely redevelop by private
enterprise once the fire station is relocated. Furthermore, the MU-C designation matches the
Future Land Use designations to the north and west.

Based on the policies and proposed future land use designation of the subject property,
Community Business zoning is appropriate at this location. Community Business is an acceptable
zone within the MU-C Future Land Use designation as it fulfills the commercial component of
the designation,

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 1, with conditions.

2) The application is compatible with nearby property, specifically considering existing zoning,
the Comprehensive Plan, and any other official plans and policies created under the guidance
of that plan for these areas; (UDC 2.2.1.d.2)

Several of the surrounding properties to the north are zoned Community Business and are
developed with commercial uses. Furthermore, the properties to the west that are zoned
Mixed-use Business Emphasis and are also developed with commercial uses. The surrounding
properties to the north and west carry a Mixed-Use Commercial Emphasis which anticipates
predominantly commercial development. Lastly, the location adjacent to E. Lincolnway further
reinforces the suitability of the site for commercial uses.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 2.

3) The suitability of the subject property for development according to the standards and uses of
the existing zoning district, compared to the suitability of the subject property for
development according to the standards and uses of the proposed zoning district (UDC
2.2.1.d.3)

The Public zone is crafted for use by public entities, such as the City of Cheyenne. With the
planned relocation of the fire station, it is anticipated the City will dispose of the subject
property to a private entity for commercial development. The Community Business zone will
afford the future developer a variety of opportunities for redevelopment compared to the
current Public zone.
```

```text
UDC-22-00030

February 22, 2022
The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

Community Business zoning typically anticipates larger suburban-style blocks, consistent with
the current site at 2.18 acres. The existing pattern is compatible with the proposed zoning and
staff is comfortable that the subject property will meet the requirements of Article 4 should
future redevelopment require a subdivision process.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The Intent of the Community Business district is for retail, employment, service, and civic uses
that may serve a variety neighborhoods. The Applicability of the district states that the CB
district is appropriate in areas with moderate- or large-scale businesses can be anticipated. The
location of the subject property along a major arterial, E. Lincolnway, and size of the lot, 2.18
acres, yield conditions conducive to moderate-scale commercial businesses that will serve the
surrounding neighborhood.

The Community Business zone is required to meet the Large Scale Commercial Design Standards
of 6.8. Staff is confident that the future development of the subject property will be able to
meet these requirements.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The existing property has adequate area and site conditions to meet the standards of the
Community Business district. There are no current applications for development of the subject
property and staff is satisfied that the various uses permitted in the CB district will be able to
meet the applicable standards at the subject property.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 6.

7) The review and recommendations of other reviewing bodies. (UDC 2.2.1.d.7)

Staff presented the zoning map amendment to the Project Advisory Team on Thursday February
10. No comments were received.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 7.
```
