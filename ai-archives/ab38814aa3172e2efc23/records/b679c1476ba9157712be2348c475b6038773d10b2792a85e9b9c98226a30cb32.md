BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:

Section 1.
That on January 7, 2013, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission reviewed the
proposed zone change, which is the subject of this ordinance, and determined that a zone change to MUB
Mixed-Use with Business Emphasis District meets the criteria specified in Section 2.2.1.d of the Unified
Development Code.

Section 2.
That the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission recommended, in accordance
with Section 2.2.1 Zoning Map Amendment, Section 5.1.2 Zoning Districts Established, and Section
5.1.3 Official Zoning Map of the Unified Development Code, that the zoning classification for the real
property described as Lot 4, Block 486, Original City, Cheyenne, Wyoming be changed to MUB Mixed-
Use with Business Emphasis District

Section 3.
That the zoning classification of the real property described in Section 2 of this
ordinance is hereby changed to MUB Mixed-Use with Business Emphasis District.

Section 4.
That the City Engineer or his designated representative is hereby directed to alter
and amend the Adopted Official Zoning Map as described in Section 5.1.3 of the Unified Development
Code, to comply with the change of zoning classification as described in Section 3 of this ordinance
upon notification by the City Clerk’s office of publication of said ordinance.

Section 5.
Sections, paragraphs, sentences, clauses and phrases of this ordinance are
severable. If any phrase, clause, sentence, paragraph or section of this ordinance is declared to be
unconstitutional or otherwise invalid, such unconstitutionality or invalidity shall not affect any of the
remaining phrases, clauses, sentences, paragraphs and sections.

Section 6.
That this ordinance shall be in full force and effect upon its publication.
