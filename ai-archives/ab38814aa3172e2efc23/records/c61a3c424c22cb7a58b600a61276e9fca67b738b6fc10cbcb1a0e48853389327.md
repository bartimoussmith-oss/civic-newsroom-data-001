BACKGROUND:

The applicant is requesting approval of a preliminary plat for roughly 0.15 acres of land. The subject property
is vacant. The proposed plat would subdivide the parcel into four townhouse lots. These lots would meet the
requirements to be considered AD1 Lots, which are permitted in the MR Medium-density Residential zone.

Townhome (“attached”) lots are not allowed front access if the lot is less than 36’ wide. The plat does not
provide an access easement or alley for access to the central lots. These lots would need relief from UDC
4.3.6.c to allow front access. Should the Planning Commission and the Governing Body find the development
pattern acceptable and approve the plat, a subdivision standards waiver would be granted by Staff for access
to Cheyenne Street.

Although the plat as proposed meets all dimensional standards in the UDC for AD1 lot types, the specific
product desired by the applicant would require setback relief. Should the Planning Commission and the
Governing Body find the development pattern acceptable and approve the plat with the understanding of
the product proposed, an administrative adjustment would be granted by Staff for reduced rear setback.

All lots proposed for development are buildable lots in this zone district. The proposed lots
would be considered AD1 Lot Types for townhouse style development. The lots being created
are shallow lots which would only allow a property to have a 23’ deep dwelling unit, if the unit
were to meet all setbacks (a 58’ deep lot with a 15’ front setback, 20’ rear setback). There are
dwelling units in the City which are as little as 20’ deep; however that is not the product the
applicant is proposing. The applicant has requested relief for the rear setback as their proposed
product would encroach by 10-feet into the rear setback. Should the Planning Commission and
Governing Body accept the reduction in the rear setback, Staff will grant the Administrative
Adjustment with the approval of this plat.

The Administrative Adjustment Review Criteria is attachment number 6. Also attached is the
applicant’s requested adjustment.

The application complies with this review criterion.

7) All parcels not proposed as buildable lots and which are not dedicated to and accepted by the

All proposed lots are buildable lots. The Plat does not propose any lots subject to this criterion.

Review criterion not applicable.

8) The application will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development for
adjacent property considering the existing zoning and Comprehensive Plan policies for that
property.  (UDC 2.1.2.d.8)

This plat will not create any undue influence on existing or potential development. While the
lots proposed are smaller than all of the lots in the area, there are other lots that have multiple
units on a single lot.

The application complies with this review criterion.
