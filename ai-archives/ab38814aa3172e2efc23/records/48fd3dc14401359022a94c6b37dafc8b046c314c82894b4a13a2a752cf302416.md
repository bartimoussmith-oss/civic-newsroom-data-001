MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

March 9, 2022
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-16-22 for furnishing Bituminous Asphalt Hot Mix, Tack
Oil, and Cold Mix on an annual basis, for the City of Cheyenne, Street & Alley
Division
______________________________________________
Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) bids were received from:

1. Knife River d/b/a JTL Group Inc. (Cheyenne, WY)
2. Simon Contractors (Cheyenne, WY)

The bids were reviewed by Randy Hickman (Street & Alley), Craig LaVoy (Public Works), and
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive Bid from Knife River d/b/a JTL Group
Inc. for the purchase of Bituminous Asphalt Hot Mix ($382,500.00), Tack Oil ($14,000.00), and
Cold Mix ($38,000.00) on an annual basis, for a total cost of $434,500.00.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-32302 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Asphalt)

$434,500.00
