Subject:
Scale House and Administration Building Construction-Happy Jack Landfill
Construction Cost Increase
In recent months, the unforeseen volatility of themarket due to theCOvID-19 pandemic and
other related factors has led to increased costs and increased delays related to the above
referenced project.As a result, the construction contractor for the Happy Jack Landfill scale
to several construction items.The following summarizes theimpacted costs and delays.
Due to unforeseen material lead time delays related to theCOVID-19 pandemic, Synergy
Construction (Synergy)is constructing the new Happy Jack Landfill scale house building on site,
rather than procuring a prefabricated building asrequired in the design documents.The
prefabricated building manufacturer experienced steel and other material lead time delays that
significantly delayed the estimated delivery date of thescale house building.The delivery date
delay meant that it was unlikely that the building would be constructed in2021 as required in
Synergy's construction contract with the City.The cost to construct the building onsite is more
than originally proposed but completes theproject on schedule.
In addition, four othercost increases havebeen experienced by Synergy for steel, asbestos
abatement, wall extension, and electrical upgrades for a building onsite.The steel cost increase
is a result ofthe structural steel supplier(Vulcraft)charging anincreased pricefor the steeljoists
and decking on the administration building due to unforeseen changes in market prices.The
asbestos abatement cost increase is aresult of unexpectedly encountering asbestos in the drywall
of the break room building tobe demolished.The extension of the foundation walls was the result
ofa design change made during the on-site scale house building construction.The electrical
upgrade is a result of providing power to HVAC and building on site electrical needs. The price
increases are summarized below:
PCO#1-Scale house construction cost increase:$26,385.98
PCO #2-Steel cost increase:$6,932.52
PCO #3-Asbestos cost increase:$8,779.51
PCO #4-Extending walls 1-foot:$13,461.05
PCO #5-Electrical:$7180.74
Total change order request =$62,730.80
