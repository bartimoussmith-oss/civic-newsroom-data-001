A Resolution Certifying the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study
______________________________________________________________________________

The Cheyenne MPO is pleased to have completed the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study which will
amend the Connect 2045 Master Transportation Plan regarding the future design and reconstruction of
Powderhouse Road between Dell Range and US 85. The Cheyenne MPO retained AVI to complete this
study and address roadway deficiencies, traffic safety problems, traffic volume growth, multimodal
needs, drainage constraints, and aligned roadway functionality and character with existing and
planned land uses and desired character.

After extensive public participation, the plan was finalized and presented to the MPO Citizens
Advisory and Technical Committee as well as the City Planning Commission, all of which
recommend adoptions.

Please reach out to me directly if you have any questions regarding this study.  I can be reached at 638-
4384 and gstevens@cheyennempo.org.

WHEREAS, Powderhouse Road between Dell Range and US 85 is classified as a Minor
Arterial/Major Collector, with jurisdictional responsibility by both the City of Cheyenne and
Laramie County;

WHEREAS, Powderhouse Road between Dell Range and US 85 is a corridor that varies
in width and currently carries between roughly 1,000 and 8,000 vehicles a day, which is expected
to significantly increase by 2045;

WHEREAS, the Cheyenne Metropolitan Planning Organization (MPO) retained AVI
consultants on July 25, 2022, to develop the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study;

WHEREAS, the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study identified and addressed roadway
deficiencies, traffic safety problems, traffic volume growth, multimodal needs, drainage
constraints, and aligned roadway functionality and character with existing and planned land uses
and desired character;

WHEREAS, the Powderhouse Road Corridor Study and resulting preliminary design
provides the City of Cheyenne, Laramie County, and future developers adjacent to the corridor
with a strategy of investments to meet the transportation and utility needs along the corridor into
the future;

WHEREAS, public involvement for the project consisted of two public meetings, October
4, 2022 and June 14, 2023, to gather feedback on issues and recommendations;

WHEREAS, the plan was developed with the assistance and guidance of a Project Steering
Committee, and presentations to the MPO’s Citizen and Technical Advisory Committees during
the plan’s development;

WHEREAS, the MPO Citizens Advisory and Technical Committees reviewed the
Powderhouse Road Corridor Study and recommended adoption by the MPO Policy Committee;
and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Planning Commission held a public hearing on October
16, 2023, accepted public comments, and recommended approval.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
8:08 pm, Oct 16 2023
