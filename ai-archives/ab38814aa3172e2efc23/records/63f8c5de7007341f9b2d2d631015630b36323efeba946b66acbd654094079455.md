12.20.330 Double-depth burials.

No double-depth burials shall be permitted in any cemetery unless specifically provided by
this code or other ordinances of the city.

Double-depth burials shall be permitted in Beth El cemetery only in burial spaces not
already occupied by a single-depth burial.

A. Double depth burial fee (where allowed)

$   100

12.20.350  Stillborn infants

Stillborn infants may be buried in a part of the city cemetery designated for that purpose,
in which case no charge shall be made for either the burial permit or lot certificates.  The applicant
shall pay thirty-five ($35.00) one hundred forty ($140) for the expense of opening and closing
the grave, unless the applicant is indigent.
