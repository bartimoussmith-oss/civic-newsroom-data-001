MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Richard Kaysen, and City Council
FROM:

Sara Vasquez, Asst. Director of Purchasing
DATE:

March 1, 2013
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #R-20-13, for furnishing Three Pickup Trucks for Street &
Alley Division.
______________________________________________
We had four bidders respond to this bid, and the bid tab is attached.

The bids were reviewed by Dennis Bell, Jim Elias, and the Street & Alley crew, and the
recommendation is to accept the bid from Spradley Barr Motors, Inc. of Cheyenne, WY for a
total bid amount including the options for the Bluetooth package and the trade-ins of
$104,690.00.  There were two bidders that were lower.
Cowboy Dodge had a lower bid, but they did not meet specifications set by the using department.
We asked for certain manuals that are necessary for the Fleet Maintenance Division, and they
said they cannot provide them.  Spradley will be providing an online subscription for these
manuals which the price was included in their bid.  Cowboy Dodge did not provide this
information until after the bid opening.  We cannot accept anything after the bid opening.

When the bid was sent to all the possible bidders, they were also told about a pre-bid that was
going to be held on February 12, 2013.  The pre-bid was not mandatory, but highly
recommended.  If they were not able to come to the pre-bid, they were to contact myself, or
Dennis Bell with any issues that they may have on the bid.  Spradley Barr was the only company
to show, and Halladay Motors was the only company to call with a question.

Halladay Motors did not submit a bid bond, therefore, we could not accept their bid which was
lower than Spradley’s.

Funding:
021-26-2616-42-43201  (1% Sales Tax 11-14)

020-26-2618-42-43904  (1% Sales Tax 07-10)
