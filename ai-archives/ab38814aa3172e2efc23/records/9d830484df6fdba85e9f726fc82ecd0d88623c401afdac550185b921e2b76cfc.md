Background:

The item for consideration is a Development Agreement associated with the Whitney Ranch 3rd Filing
final plat.  The final plat is a separate item on this agenda and will be considered at the Public Services
Committee on April 6, 2021.

The proposed Governing Body review schedule is as follows:
•
Introduction and Referral: March 22, 2021
•
Public Service Committee: April 6, 2021
•
Final Consideration: April 12, 2021

On July 27, 2020, the Governing Body approved the preliminary plat with multiple conditions, two of
which (#3 and #4) are satisfied by this agreement.  Conditions #3 and #4 read as follows:
“3. Prior to approval of the final plat(s), the applicant/developer shall enter into an agreement
with the City regarding impacts to Cheyenne Fire Rescue. The agreement shall identify the
applicant/developer’s proportionate share of the cost of extending service (land, facilities, and
equipment) to the area and shall be determined based on the ISO Fire Suppression Rating
Criteria and evaluation of said criteria.
4. Prior to Engineering Plan Review approval, the applicant/developer shall enter into an
agreement with the City regarding design and construction of roadway improvements (such as
traffic signals, curbs, gutter, pavement, additional lanes, traffic calming devices, etc.). The
applicant/developer shall pay their proportionate share of costs for design and improvements
based on their impact to the system as identified in the Traffic Study submitted with this plat.”
City Planning and Development, City Engineering, and Cheyenne Fire Rescue have been working with the
Developer and their agent to draft an agreement that satisfactory meets conditions #3 and #4.  The
other conditions have been met and are detailed in the related final plat staff report.

Public Safety Fees:
For the purpose of meeting condition #3 staff and the developer utilized the 2015 TichlerBise Impact Fee
Study.  The 2015 study identified a fee for new residential development based on square footage of
finished square footage.   The 2015 values were averaged for the anticipated housing types in Whitney
Ranch (between 1,100 and 2,100 finished square feet) and were adjusted for inflation utilizing the
construction cost index for twenty (20) cities, as issued by the current Engineering News Record which is

Dell Range Boulevard / Van Buren Avenue Signal:
For the purpose of meeting condition #4, staff and the developer utilized the traffic study that was
submitted with the Whitney Ranch Development. The traffic study indicated that the development
would warrant off-site improvements, including signalization, of the intersection of Dell Range
Boulevard and Van Buren Street.  The study further elaborated that intersection improvements,
including a signal, should be shared as follows: City 15.5% and the Developer 84.5%.  The estimated cost
of a signal installation is $225, 796 (based on the average of the last three traffic signal bids accepted by
the City).   The City’s share is $34,998 and the Developer’s share is $190,798.

Staff Recommendation:

Staff recommends the Governing Body approve the agreement as it satisfies preliminary plat conditions
3 and 4.
