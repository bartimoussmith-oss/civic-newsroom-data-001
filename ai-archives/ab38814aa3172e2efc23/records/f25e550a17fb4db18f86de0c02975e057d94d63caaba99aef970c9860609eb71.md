```text
RESOLUTION  _________
ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION CERTIFYING THAT THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE IS IN AGREEMENT ON TWO PROJECTS TO BE
SUBMITTED BY THE TOWN OF ALBIN TO THE STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT
BOARD; 2013-2014 COUNTYWIDE CONSENSUS BLOCK GRANT FUNDING FOR A
CURB, GUTTER AND CONCRETE REHAB PROJECT, AND 2015-2016
COUNTYWIDE CONSENSUS BLOCK GRANT FUNDING FOR THE LARSON ROAD
RECONSTRUCTION PROJECT. ”
Whereas,  the Laramie County Board of Commissioners support the two projects and the
town of Albin desires to participate in the State Loan and Investment Board’s Countywide
Consensus Block Grant program; and
Whereas, the rules of the State Loan and Investment Board require that application for funds
under this program be made by the Board of County Commissioners and agreed to by
municipalities representing at least 70% of the county’s incorporated population; and
Whereas, the Governing Body of Cheyenne represents greater than 70% of the incorporated
population of Laramie County;
THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING
That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne is in agreement with the two Albin
Projects; the Curb, Gutter and Concrete Rehab Project (Countywide Consensus Block Grant
funding 2013-2014), and the Larson Road Reconstruction Project, (Countywide Consensus
Block Grant funding 2015-2016).
PASSED, APPROVED AND ADOPTED THIS 27th DAY OF APRIL, 2015

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor
Attest:
________________________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
Consens 13-14 & 15-16 Co  RES Albin.wpd
```
