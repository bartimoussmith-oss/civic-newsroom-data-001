Process to review benefits and make recommendations to Council
•
Employee Benefits Committee (EBC) has been meeting since Fall 2017 (12+ meetings)
-
All meetings are open to the public; meetings and activities promoted through ‘Stall Talk’
•
Employee benefits survey
— March 2018— over 30% rate of return
-
Survey results and all comments forwarded to Council on March 30
•
Presentations about benefit structure and funding options (fully insured vs. partially self-
funded)
•
Meetings with United Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield and UC-Health to discuss funding,
benefits, and opportunities for the City
•
Health questionnaire for all employees to gather data for underwriting by carriers
-
Data sent to UHC, Blue Cross, Allied, CIGNA for both fully-insured and partially self-insured
quotes; over 32 stop-loss carriers contacted in conjunction with self-funding
•
EBC met several times in late April to review responses, ask questions, seek information
Proposal/Recommendation from EBC
Major factors considered:
•
Premium cost — including increase from previous year
•
Benefit structure — address areas of concern to employees
•
Providers in network from whom to seek medical treatment
•
Customer service
Conclusions:
•
Self-funding not economically feasible this year.
•
CIGNA not at option; highest quote plus significant loss of providers in-network.
•
UHC was negotiated down to either a lower rate or a decrease in the out-of-pocket maximum
to $3,500/$7,500. No other benefit changes were proposed. Limited provider network and
customer service remain major concerns.
•
Blue Cross Blue Shield offered a competitive rate, benefit enhancements, the broadest
network, and brick-and-mortar building in Cheyenne. EBC recommends this option.
