ORDINANCE NO. ________

ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE DEFINING THE TERMS AND CONDITIONS OF
EMPLOYMENT FOR SWORN OFFICERS OF THE CHEYENNE POLICE
DEPARTMENT FOR THE FISCAL YEAR 2023-2024."

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  Definitions.

EMPLOYEE/EMPLOYEES - Employee or employees as used in this ordinance means
sworn police officers, below the rank of chief.

Section 2.  Applicability.

The terms and conditions of employment as set forth herein shall apply to all employees as
defined herein.

Section 3.  Purpose.

The purpose of this ordinance is to define the terms and conditions of employment for
employees.  The rights contained herein are in addition to any rights an employee may have under
the Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual (as that document may be amended at any time,
before or after passage of this ordinance), and applicable federal, state and local laws.  In areas of
conflict between this ordinance and established federal, state or local laws, the latter shall govern.
This ordinance comprises the exclusive statement of employee terms and conditions of
employment.  Any other agreement with or representation made to any employee by a supervisor,
agent or officer of the City of Cheyenne which is in conflict with or in addition to this ordinance
is specifically disclaimed by the City of Cheyenne, except for the Cheyenne Police Department
Policy Manual and except for agreements entered into by and between supervisory personnel and
employees establishing specific job expectations, requirements or discipline which are part of the
employee’s official personnel file maintained by the Human Resources Department.

Section 4.  Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual and Benefits Policy.

The Cheyenne Police Department Policy Manual including the benefit policy set forth in
Exhibit A, as they exist or are amended from time to time, are incorporated herein and made part
of these terms and conditions of employment and shall apply only to sworn police officers.

Section 5.  Compensation.

The compensation paid to employees is set forth in Exhibit B, which is attached and
incorporated by reference herein.  The compensation appropriate for the aforementioned
employees shall be computed with reference to the grade of the individual employee.  Assignment
of employees to the appropriate grade under the new plan will be made on a case by case basis,

Section 6.  Continuation of Employment.

Any employee covered by this ordinance who continues his or her term of employment
during and beyond the term of this ordinance shall be entitled to advancement annually (subject to
the provisions of Section 5), as set forth in Exhibit B, upon recommendation of the employee’s
supervisor, if the mayor and council approve funds to pay the increases.

Section 7.  Health, Medical, Dental, and Life.

The City will negotiate with or seek bids from qualified health insurance providers and will
enter into a contract with a qualified carrier or carriers to provide employees with a medical
insurance plan, which is on file in the Human Resources Department, for fiscal year 2023-2024.
Each uniformed City employee eligible for health insurance coverage shall contribute ten percent
(10%) of the monthly health insurance premium attributable to the level of coverage selected by
that employee.  This payment will be a payroll deduction.

The City will maintain dental insurance on employees pursuant to the dental plan on file in
the Human Resources Department.  The City will pay premiums for employees.  If an employee
wishes to maintain dental insurance under this plan for eligible dependents as defined in Appendix
B of the dental plan, he or she may do so by paying the premium for the additional person(s)
insured.

The City will pay premiums for term life insurance on all employees in accordance with
the term life insurance plan on file in the Human Resources Department and City Clerk’s Office.

Section 8.  Term.

The terms and conditions of employment set forth in this ordinance shall apply for fiscal
year 2023 – 2024 and shall be approved as part of the budget process.  This ordinance will
terminate upon passage of a replacement ordinance.

Section 9.  Repeal of Other Ordinances.

EXHIBIT A

EMPLOYEE WAGE AND BENEFIT POLICY

THIS POLICY COVERS ALL COMMISSIONED OFFICERS OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
POLICE DEPARTMENT.

1.
MONTHLY WAGES.

a.
Wages.

Effective July 1, 1997, all commissioned officers became covered by the City of Cheyenne
pay plan.

All step increases shall be on the officer’s original date of employment or promotion date.

b.
Lateral Transfer Program.

Lateral entry candidates must be currently certified as a Wyoming peace officer or in a
state with a reciprocity agreement with Wyoming.  If not currently certified in Wyoming, lateral
entry candidates must take and pass the Wyoming POST challenge test, as well as the Wyoming
criminal law and domestic violence courses offered through the Wyoming Law Enforcement
Academy.

Subject to these requirements, the department offers a lateral transfer program for officers
with prior qualifying law enforcement experience.  All officers will be hired at a Grade 201 - Step
1.  Officers completing a minimum of six months of the department’s eighteen-month probationary
period may, at the chief’s discretion, be placed in the step program according to their experience
as follows:

2 to 4 years of experience:
Grade 201 - Step 3

4 to 6 years of experience:
Grade 201 - Step 4

6 + years of experience:
Grade 201 - Step 5

c.
Specialty On Call Pay.

Effective July 1, 2004, eligible employees will be entitled to receive specialty on call pay.
Employees are limited to receiving compensation for a maximum of two specialties per month.

An eligible employee is one who serves as a first line supervisor (field training officer) or
is required to be available for callout and does not receive any other compensation for being on
call.

d.
Longevity Pay.

Effective January 1, 2022, eligible employees will be entitled to receive longevity pay
based on years of service.  Longevity raises will be effective on the employee’s anniversary month.

2.
OVERTIME.

a.
Patrol Officers and Patrol Sergeants.

All sergeants and officers who are called in for overtime duties will receive a minimum of
two and one-half hours of overtime pay, excepting court appearances which are limited to two
hours of overtime pay or compensatory time if the time spent is two hours or less.  All court time
in excess of two hours is to be paid at time and one-half the classification rate.  All patrol officers
and patrol sergeants shall be limited to eighty hours of accumulated compensatory time, at which
time, they must take paid overtime until they reduce below the eighty-hour level.

All officers assigned acting sergeant duties will be compensated two hours overtime for
each shift.  All sergeants assigned acting lieutenant duties will be compensated one-hour straight
time for each shift.

Should the acting sergeant, lieutenant or captain duties extend beyond thirty consecutive
days, the department may temporarily promote the officer, and place the officer in the appropriate
pay grade of the new position.

3.
HOLIDAYS.

The officers and sergeants of the City of Cheyenne Police Department will be entitled to
those paid holidays that are set by Personnel Rules and Regulations of the City of Cheyenne, as
amended from time to time, and as enjoyed by personnel of other departments of the City, to
include “Special Holidays” or other paid time off afforded to other City employees.

The rate of pay for these holidays will be computed on the basis of 2,080 hours per year.
The officer working a holiday has the option of taking overtime pay or compensatory time at one
and one-half times the classification rate.  If the holiday falls on an officer’s regular day off, he
will be given eight hours of straight pay or compensatory time.  Likewise, if the holiday falls on
the officer’s vacation, or while on sick leave, the holiday will not be counted as vacation time or
sick leave.

4.
SICK LEAVE.

Each officer shall accumulate sick leave time at the rate of ten hours for each month of
continuous employment to a maximum of six hundred forty hours.

Officers who have completed four years of continuous service and terminate from the City
shall receive 50% of their accrued sick leave at their regular base rate, not to exceed 240 hours.  In
the case of retirement, an employee will be reimbursed fully for his or her accrued sick time up to
240 hours.

5.
SICK BANK.

The Sick Bank is to be used by officers in the case of serious illness or injury which is not
covered under Worker’s Compensation, and after they have exhausted their accumulated sick
leave.

The Sick Bank is to be established on the effective date of this policy with a balance of
1,200 hours contributed by the City.  The Sick Bank will maintain a balance limited to 1,200 hours.
This balance to be renewed by the City, if any days are used, on January 1st of each year.

A contributory pool (Sick Pool) of 4,160 maximum sick hours will be established on the
effective date of this policy.  Sworn officers with excess sick leave (over 640 hours) may contribute
their excess sick leave to the Sick Pool January 1st of each year.

The Sick Bank and Sick Pool are to be administered by the Sick Bank Board.   This Board
will consist of the Chief of Police, the City Human Resources Director and the President of the
Police Protective Association or his designee.

Policies and procedures for administering the Sick Bank and Sick Pool are to be made by
the Sick Bank Board, including renewing depleted balances.  These policies and procedures are to
be in writing and available, upon request, to all sworn officers.  Request for Sick Bank/Sick Pool
hours are to be made in writing to the Sick Bank Board, in accordance with their policies and

6.
VACATION.

Each officer shall be entitled to a vacation of eighty-eight hours after one year of
continuous service, and shall be entitled to an additional eight hours of vacation for each year of
uninterrupted service provided that an officer is entitled to earn no more than 216 hours of vacation
in any one year.  Insofar as possible, vacation time shall be determined by division and squad, with
seniority rank having preference within each division and squad.

Not more than 296 hours of accumulated vacation leave can be carried from one calendar
year to the next calendar year.  Accumulated vacation leave in excess of 296 hours on January 1
of each year shall be forfeited.

On separation from service, an officer shall be paid for all accumulated vacation hours
earned and not taken, including the year of separation, proportionate to the time employed in that
year.

7.
PERSONAL DAY.

Each full-time uniformed police officer with one year of continuous service will be
awarded one personal day each fiscal year.  A personal day shall be defined as one scheduled
workday.

This personal day may be used by the employee at his or her discretion, with supervisory
approval.  Personal days must be used in the year in which they are awarded.  Personal days cannot
be broken into increments of less than a normal working day.  They do not carry over from one
year to the next.

8.
REDUCTION IN FORCE.

Officers laid off in accordance with the provisions of this section will be eligible for rehire
or reinstatement into a position of the same classification in the inverse order of layoff.  If rehired
or reinstated, however, no officer shall accumulate time in service during the period of lay off.  An
officer laid off under these provisions shall be protected for a period of twenty-four months from
time of separation, pursuant to state statute.  The department shall notify protected officers of any
vacancy as soon as known to the department.  The officer shall notify the department with-in five
days of his or her receipt of notice of his or her intent and must report at least fifteen days after
receipt of notice.
