MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Patrick J. Collins, City of Cheyenne
Members of the City Council, City of Cheyenne

FROM:
Doug Klahn, Construction Manager

DATE:
September 2, 2024

SUBJECT:
Contract Modification #1 to Asphalt Preservation LLC. for the 2024 Surface Seal
#1 project.

Please find the attached proposed contract modification for the above referenced city project.
Construction and Engineering Staff have discussed the additional work required to complete this
project and determined that the costs associated with the supply and installation of this material is
in accordance with the original contract unit pricing. Additionally, this contract modification is
beneficial to the city as it requires no additional mobilization costs and ensures that this project is
completed to the city’s specifications. The additional cost associated with this work is estimated
at $193,638.42. In addition, this will require an extension of the completion date to November 15,
2024. Staff respectfully request your approval of this contract modification.

CITY ENGINEER’S OFFICE
2101 O’Neil Avenue
Cheyenne, Wyoming 82001
