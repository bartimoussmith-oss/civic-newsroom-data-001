RESOLUTION NO. _________
ENTITLED:
“A RESOLUTION AMENDING RESOLUTION NO. 5521 AND AUTHORIZING
SUBMISSION OF AN APPLICATION TO THE WYOMING BUSINESS
COUNCIL, BUSINESS READY COMMUNITY GRANT AND LOAN PROGRAM
FOR FUNDING THROUGH THE DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT GRANT AND
LOAN PROGRAM FOR AN ELEVATED WALKWAY.”
WHEREAS, Resolution No. 5521, adopted by the Governing Body at its August 26, 2013
meeting inadvertently omitted wording with respect to the loan component of the project which is
the subject of this Resolution; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne desires to participate in the
Wyoming Business Council Business Ready Community/Downtown Development Program to assist
Spirit Hospitality, LLC, in constructing an elevated pedestrian access walkway from the Jack R.
Spiker Parking Facility across Pioneer Avenue to a location within Block 392, Original City, City
of Cheyenne, 1620 Pioneer Avenue, Cheyenne, Wyoming (“Block 392”); and
WHEREAS, the purposes of the elevated pedestrian access walkway includes providing
pedestrian access for the general public from the Spiker Parking Facility to the streets, sidewalks,
and businesses located in and around the 1600 block of Pioneer Avenue and to provide access to and
from the Spiker Parking Facility for guests of a Hilton Gardens Hotel which Spirit Hospitality, LLC,
proposes to construct; and
WHEREAS, the eligibility requirements of the Business Ready Community/Downtown
Development Program include the provision that a portion of eligible program funds may be
distributed in the form of a loan to the City of Cheyenne as the grant applicant; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will request Business Ready Community/Downtown
Development Program grant funds in the amount of Five Hundred Sixty-Two Thousand Two
Hundred Forty-Eight Dollars ($562,248.00) and loan funds in the amount of One Hundred Eighty-
Seven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($187,500.00), said loan funds to be re-paid by the City of
Cheyenne over a twenty (20) year period with simple interest in the amount of one half of one
percent (.5%) compounded annually; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to said terms and conditions, the annual debt service owed by the City
of Cheyenne to the Wyoming Business Council will be in the approximate amount of Nine
Thousand Eight Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars ($9,875.00) annually; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will secure the loan through a non-exclusive lease of the
elevated pedestrian access walkway to Spirit Hospitality, LLC, for use by its guests with annual
lease payments for Years 1 through 4 in the amount of Ten Thousand Dollars ($10,000.00) and
annual lease payments for Years 5 through 20 in the amount of  Fourteen Thousand Seven Hundred
and Fifty Dollars ($14,750.00); and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will use revenue recaptured through this project,
estimated at Seventy-Eight Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($78,500.00) over the twenty (20) year
lease term, to support the City of Cheyenne’s West Edge Project; and
WHEREAS, said grant and loan funds will be used by the City of Cheyenne to construct the
elevated walkway, which will be retained in the ownership of the City as public pedestrian access
infrastructure; and
WHEREAS, the use of grant and loan funds for the construction of an elevated pedestrian
access walkway satisfies the eligibility criteria which have been established by the Wyoming
Business Council for the Business Ready Community/Downtown Development Program; and

WHEREAS, the eligibility requirements of the Business Ready Community/Downtown
Development Program also include the requirement that the grant applicant provide a grant match
and the City’s match will be provided through the loan component of the grant application as
described above and through capital investments of Spirit Hospitality, LLC; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will follow state procurement standards, inclusive of W.S.
§ 15-1-113 and W.S. § 16-6-101, et seq., in the construction of the elevated pedestrian access
walkway; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will comply with the Wyoming Preference Act (W.S. §§
16-6-201 through 16-6-206) and all related state statutes in the construction of the elevated
pedestrian access walkway; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recognizes the construction of an
elevated walkway will provide public benefit; and
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, that a loan application in the amount of One Hundred Eighty-Seven Thousand
Five Hundred Dollars ($187,500.00), subject to and including the terms and conditions set forth
above, be submitted to the Wyoming Business Council for the purpose of assisting in the funding
of the construction of the elevated pedestrian access walkway as described above.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne, or such other City
official as may be designated by the Mayor, is authorized to sign an application for this grant and
loan request and that the Mayor, or his designated representative, is authorized to execute a grant
agreement and loan documents as required by the Wyoming Business Council in the event that grant
and loan funds, as described herein, are awarded to the City of Cheyenne for this project.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that Resolution No. 5521 is amended to the extent that
Resolution No. 5521 is inconsistent with this Resolution.
ADOPTED THIS __________day of October, 2013.
