Annexation Area – This annexation area contains a 6.71-acre parcel of vacant land that is
located between the existing Super 8 motel and the Burlington Northern-Santa Fe railroad on
West Lincolnway (US Highway 30). It is a long, narrow parcel with approximately 200 feet
of frontage on West Lincolnway and extends north more than 1,500 feet. On the west side of
the property is a vacant railroad right-of-way and beyond that the BNSF railroad, which
crosses over West Lincolnway. West of the railroad is a Subaru dealership with a Motel 7
north of it on Westland Road. The BNSF railroad curves northeast. North of the subject
property is vacant land. East of the subject property is the existing Super 8 motel, which is
under the same ownership as the subject property, vacant land that was once part of the
Hitching Post, the Hitching Post property itself, and the Cheyenne Ice & Events Center. To
the south across Lincolnway is the Union Pacific railroad.

CURRENT STATUS:  The property is currently vacant land. Several mature pinion pine trees exist
on the site near West Lincolnway, as well as other trees across the site, the west side of which slopes
sharply downward.

Improvements: The landowner will be required to comply with Chapter 1.16 of the Cheyenne City
Code, which includes provisions for offsite improvements. Street improvements and a detached
sidewalk are in place though access improvements may be required in coordination with WYDOT
since Lincolnway is a US highway.
Sewer service is available to the property currently at the east side of the property adjacent to the
east, which is under the same ownership as the subject property. Water lines exist in the adjacent
Lincolnway. Subsequent development actions on this property may trigger additional improvements
to be required. For example, landscaping including street trees and irrigation will be required at the
time of development.
Platting: The property is currently unplatted. The owners have submitted a preliminary plat for the
property, which will be processed concurrently with the annexation.
Zoning:  The property is zoned CB, Community Business.
Other plans:  The subject property is also included in the Missile Drive Corridor Plan, 2010, which
states that “The long term vision for the corridor is a mixed use district that is an extension of
downtown.” Figure 1 of that plan includes the north part of the subject property in a “potential
economic development area.” Figure 2 indicates that hotel development is anticipated on the south
portion of the property and the north portion could potentially include a mix of different types of
residential development within a grid network of streets anchored by a transit stop and a new
entertainment district to include the Ice & Events Center. As stated on page 2 of the plan: “Areas
east of the BNSF crossing take on the character and scale of downtown, including a finer grained
street network that encourages pedestrian connections across Missile Drive, mixed use building
forward environments that have an address on Missile Drive, and greenway enhancements along the
Crow Creek, which provide pedestrian connectivity along the entire corridor.”
More specifically, the plan shows extensions of 17th, 18th, and 19th Streets on the north portion of this
property, which should be planned for when the property is platted. If no access to the north portion
of the property is included, development of this area will be difficult, making it more likely to remain
undeveloped.

ANNEXATION ELIGIBILITY DATA/ANALYSIS:  Before any territory is eligible for
annexation, the governing body of any city or town, at a hearing as provided in W.S. 15-1-405,
shall find that:
1)
The annexation of the area is for the protection of the health, safety, and welfare of
the persons residing in the area and in the city or town. Findings:  Future required
connections to City services in the Urban Area helps prevent overuse of the aquifer
and possible contamination from septic systems. Urban densities are most
appropriately served by full-time professional fire services and City police.
2)
The urban development of the area sought to be annexed constitutes a natural,
geographical, economical, and social part of the annexing city or town. Findings:
The property is approximately 55% contiguous to the existing City boundary. The site
is located within the PlanCheyenne comprehensive plan area, which includes the
following land use designations on the future land use plan: Mixed-Use Commercial.
3)
The area sought to be annexed is a logical and feasible addition to the annexing city
or town and the extension of basic and other services customarily available to the
residents of the city or town shall, within reason, be available to the area proposed to
be annexed. Findings:  The City has the capacity to serve more people and businesses
with its water and sewer system. This area can be supported by City services, such as
fire protection, police protection, and garbage collection. The proximity to existing
urban development is an opportunity to provide City services in a fiscally responsible
manner.  PlanCheyenne advances urban development in this area and this area is
located in the 201 boundary both of which would require new development to be
connected to City sewer. Sewer service is available east side of the property adjacent
to the east and water service is available in Lincolnway.
4)
The area sought to be annexed is contiguous with the annexing city or town, or the
area meets the requirements of W.S. 15-1-407. Findings:  The property is
approximately 55% contiguous with existing City limits.
5)
If the city or town does not own or operate its own electric utility, its governing body
is prepared to issue one (1) or more franchises as necessary to serve the annexed area
pursuant to W.S. 15-1-410. Findings:  Franchises are in place to serve this property.
6)
The annexing city or town, not less than twenty (20) business days prior to the public
hearing required by W.S 15-1-405 (a), has sent by certified mail to all landowners and
affected public utilities within the territory a summary of the proposed annexation
report as required under subsection (c) of this section and notice of the time, date, and
location of the public hearing required by W.S. 15-1-405 (a). Findings:  The required
information was sent October 14, 2011, to meet the requirement of this provision.
