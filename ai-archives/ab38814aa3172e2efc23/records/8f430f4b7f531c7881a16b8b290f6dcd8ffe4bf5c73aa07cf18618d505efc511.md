CITY OF CHEYENNE
CHEYENNE PUBLIC SAFETY CENTER
CONTRACT MODIFICATION NUMBER TWO TO CITY CONTRACT NO.6115BETWEEN
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND FCI CONSTRUCTORS OF WYOMING, LLC, FOR
INTERIOR DEMOLITION FOR THE CHEYENNE PUBLIC SAFETY CENTER PROJECT
Whereas, on December 24,2013, the City of Cheyenne("City")and FCI Constructors of
Wyoming, LLC,("FCI")entered into City Contract No.6115(C MAR Agreement")under
which FCI will act as Construction Manager at Risk ("CMAR")for the construction of the
Cheyenne Public Safety Center("Project"); and
Whereas, the Project is still under architectural design and, upon completion of the
architectural design and other contract documents, the Project Architect will submit to the City
and FCI, a final Cost Estimate for the Project under Section 1.4.8.1 of the CMAR Agreement;
and
Whereas, following the receipt of the architectural design andfinal Cost Estimate from
the Project Architect, FCI will submit a proposed Guaranteed Maximum Price("GMP")to the
City's Governing Body for approval pursuant to Sections 1.4.8.2 and 2.4.6 of the CMAR
Agreement; and
Whereas, pursuant to the definition of"Fast Track Project"contained in Section 1.4.6 and
the Fast Track Project provisions of Section 2.1.6 of the CMAR Agreement, the City has, after
consultation with the Project Architect, directed FCI to proceed with bidding for interior
demolition prior to the completion of a final Cost Estimate and priorto the submission ofa
proposed GMP; and
Whereas, following public bid processes, FCI has selected a sub-contractor to perform
interior demolition for the price of $ 652,475.00, which includes contingency fees as provided for
in Section 1.4.7 of the CMAR Agreement; and
Whereas, pursuant to Section 2.4.4.2 of the CMAR Agreement, the purpose of this
Contract Modification Number Two is to authorize FCI to incur thecostof$652,475.00for
interior demolition and topermit FCI toissue a Notice to Proceed toits sub-contractor, Brisco, a
Wyoming corporation, to perform this work; and
Whereas, it is mutually understood and agreed that the sums to bepaid byFCI to Brisco
for interior demolition will be included in the final G MP to be submitted by FCI pursuant to
Section 2.4.6of the C MAR Agreement.
