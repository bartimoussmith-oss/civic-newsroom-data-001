PUBLIC SERVICES COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Public Services Committee was held on Tuesday, August 6, 2024, in Committee
Room 104 and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Scott Roybal, Chairman; Pete Laybourn; and Tom Segrave.
Absent: Dr. Rinne.
AGENDA ITEMS
10.
RESOLUTION
— Supporting the Clean Crow Creek Initiative on behalf of the Governing
Body for the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming. (SPONSORS
— DR. ALDRICH, DR. RINNE,
MR. ROYBAL, AND MR. WHITE)
Mr. Segrave moved to adopt, seconded by Mr. Laybourn. Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Councilwoman, Ward III and co-sponsor of the item, provided an overview
of the proposed resolution. Committee members Laybourn and Segrave commented on the item.
Public comments were made by Tom Cobb, City Engineer; Brian Deureloo, Frog Creek Partners;
and Jeff Geyer, Laramie County Conservation District.
There being no further items for the agenda to come before the Public Services Committee, the
meeting was adjourned at 12:26 p.m.
Submitted by,
J9M 6zth
Jennifer McClelland
Executive Assistant to the Cheyenne City Council
