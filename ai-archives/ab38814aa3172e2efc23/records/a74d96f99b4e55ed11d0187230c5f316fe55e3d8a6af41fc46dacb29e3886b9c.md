RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
 September 22, 2014
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles, Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne (arriving at 6:04
p.m.), Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez and Ms. Williams.  Also present: Kristy Anderson, Deputy City
Clerk; Lois Huff, City Treasurer, and Dan White, City Attorney.  The pledge of allegiance was
recited.
Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.) Upon request by Mr. Brown, and pursuant to support received by
Ms. Broyles and Ms. Williams, Agenda Item #13(c) was removed from the Consent Agenda. Ms.
Broyles moved to approve remaining consent agenda items, seconded by Ms. Williams.  Motion
carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on September 8, 2014.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest in vouchers payable to Ameri-
Tech HVAC Services) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Shanor moved to approve payment of
vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Luna.  Upon inquiry, Lois Huff, City Treasurer; Mayor
Kaysen; Rick Parish, Parks and Recreation Director, and Jim Elias, Public Works Director, provided
information relative to the following vouchers:  JM Rodent Control; Brant Audiology, LLC; Harris
Enterprise Resource Planning; Professional Financ; Qian Yaling, and Ron Little Valley Wholesale
Nursery. Motion carried. Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present in Council
Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown returned to Council Chambers.
Following approval of vouchers, Mayor Kaysen noted for those present in the audience, Agenda Item
#12(a), Preliminary Plat for Thomas Heights, would have no further discussion on it at the meeting
since it was approved on the Consent Agenda.
ORDINANCE  - Vacating a portion of Alexander Avenue and 13th Street, adjacent to Blocks
444, 445, 452 and 453, Original City, Cheyenne, Wyoming (located south of E. Lincolnway, west
of Dunn Avenue). Reporting for Public Services Committee, Mr. Shanor moved to approve on
second reading, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body.
ORDINANCE - Pursuant to Section 2.2.1, Zoning Map Amendments, Section 5.1.2, Zoning
Districts Established, and Section 5.1.3, Official Zoning Map, of the Cheyenne Unified Development

Under Other Business:
Major Dorr spoke about development in different areas of Cheyenne and expressed concerns
regarding the need for a grocery store in West Cheyenne.  Upon inquiry, Matt Ashby, Planning and
Development Director, explained that with the West Edge development project, prospects for any
type of a grocery store would be on a smaller scale.
Ms. Williams moved to reconsider the vote taken at the Sept. 8, 2014 City Council meeting
for an “Application requesting the establishment of a Residential Critical Traffic and Parking Area
in the vicinity of the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center,” seconded by Mr. Allen.  It was advised
the purpose of the motion to reconsider was to amend the application to extend the area’s boundaries
as recommended by the City Traffic Engineer.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body.  Mayor Kaysen advised the main motion to approve the Residential Critical Traffic
and Parking Area was now back before the governing body.  Deputy City Clerk Kristy Anderson read
the entitlement into the record as follows: “Application requesting the establishment of a Residential
Critical Traffic and Parking Area in the vicinity of the Cheyenne Regional Medical Center.”  Ms.
Williams moved to postpone to Oct. 13, 2014, seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Motion to postpone carried.
Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body.  Mayor Kaysen announced due to postponement,
the item will be reviewed at the next Public Services Committee meeting which will be held
Tuesday, Oct. 7, 2014 at noon, Room 122, of the Municipal Building.
There being no further business to come before the governing body, the meeting was
officially adjourned at 7:42 p.m.
