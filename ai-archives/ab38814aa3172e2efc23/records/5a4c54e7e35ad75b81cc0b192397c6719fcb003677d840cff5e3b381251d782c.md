RESOLUTION NO. _____________

Entitled:
“A RESOLUTION SUPPORTING THE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES’
APPLICATION TO THE WYOMING STATE LOAN AND INVESTMENT
BOARD FOR A LOCAL GOVERNMENT PROJECT ARPA GRANT FOR
THE WATER RECLAMATION FACILITIES HYDRAULIC CAPACITY
PROJECT IN THE AMOUNT OF $1,984,000.00 AND AUTHORIZING THE
DIRECTOR OF THE BOARD OF PUBLIC UTILITIES TO EXECUTE THE
APPLICATION AND ALL RELATED DOCUMENTS”

WHEREAS, W.S. § 15-7-401, provides for a Board of Public Utilities (BOPU) to
manage, operate, maintain, and control municipal waterworks, sanitary sewer system and
sewage disposal plants;

WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Section 2.68.010, establishes that the BOPU has the
exclusive authority to manage, operate, maintain and control the Cheyenne Municipal
Waterworks, Sanitary Sewage System and Sewage Disposal Plants of the City and to make all
rules and regulations necessary for the safe, economical and efficient operation and
maintenance of the above facilities;

WHEREAS, all sanitary sewer wastewater, including storm water inflow and
infiltration, flow to the Crow Creek Water Reclamation Facility (WRF) and the Dry Creek WRF
where the combined flow is treated and discharged to Crow Creek or additionally treated and
used as recycle water supply;

WHEREAS, during storm events, Crow Creek elevation rises and Crow Creek WRF is
unable to discharge to the creek;

WHEREAS, the relocation of the Crow Creek WRF outfall location will allow the plant
to continue to operate during times of high Crow Creek water elevation;

WHEREAS, the 2013 Water and Wastewater Master Plan recommends the installation
of equalization basins at Dry Creek WRF to store water during high storm flow events and
allow the plant to treat the water after the storm subsides;

WHEREAS, the BOPU desires to pursue the WRF Hydraulic Capacity Project
(Project) to install equalization basins at Dry Creek WRF and relocate the outfall pipeline at
Crow Creek WRF;

WHEREAS, the Local Government Project ARPA Grant program required that certain
criteria be met, as described in the State Loan and Investment Board’s Rules and Regulations
governing the program, and to the best of our knowledge the application meets those criteria;

WHEREAS, the Project cost estimate for engineering, construction, and other
administrative costs is $3,100,000.00, which includes engineering services, easements,
construction and miscellaneous costs;

WHEREAS, the Project meets the criteria and is eligible for consideration of grant
funding from the Water & Sewer ARPA Grant Program in the amount of $1,984,000.00, sixty-
four percent (64%);

WHEREAS, the BOPU is committed to paying the remainder of PROJECT costs from
revenue from the Sewer Enterprise Fund in the amount of $1,116,000.00, thirty-six percent
(36%);

WHEREAS, if awarded the ARPA grant, the funds shall only be used to cover costs
incurred during the period beginning on March 3, 2021 and ending on September 1, 2026; and

WHEREAS, the BOPU recognizes that the PROJECT is necessary and that it is
essential to maintain reliable operation of Dry Creek and Crow Creek WRFs during high storm
events.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, that an application be submitted to the Wyoming State Loan
and Investment Board for a Local Government Project ARPA Grant for an amount not to exceed
$1,984,000.00 for project costs such as, but not limited to, engineering services, construction,
administration, and all related costs of the Water Reclamation Facilities Hydraulic Capacity
Project;

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Mayor, the City Clerk, and the Director of
the Board of Public Utilities are hereby designated as the authorized representatives of the City
of Cheyenne to act on behalf of the City of Cheyenne in all matters related to this application
and related documents, including any amendments to the Project Agreement.
