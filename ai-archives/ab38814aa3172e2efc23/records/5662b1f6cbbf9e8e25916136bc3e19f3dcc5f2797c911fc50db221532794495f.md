MEMO

TO:

Honorable Mayor Collins and members of the City Council

FROM:
Charles W. Bloom, AICP - Director

SUBJECT:
Required reporting of park fees, public safety fees, and Core Development
Incentivization Program activities for fiscal year 2021

DATE:
October 10, 2022

The Unified Development Code and municipal code require reporting of fees and expenses related to park
fees (UDC § 4.2.5.5, Park Fees) and public safety fees (UDC § 4.6.3.d, Public Safety Fees) and a
summary of activities related to the City of Cheyenne Core Development Incentivization Program Core
Fee Waiver (Municipal Code § 15.08.031.E).  Not required, but included in this report, is revenue from
the Sweetgrass Future Road Improvement fund.  These reports are provided upon fiscal year finalization
by the City Treasurer.

Full details of all revenues and expenses are attached and individual accounts are summarized below.

Park Fees:

Park Fees collected were $702,319 and expenditures totaled $148,711. Details are provided in the
attached report. No park fee collected may be used for routine maintenance of public parks or overhead
or general fund purposes.

Park Acquisition and Infrastructure (PAI) fees totaled $141,163. These funds may only be used for
park land acquisition and/or base infrastructure supporting the service area.  Park Enhancement (PEA)
fees totaled $561,156.  These funds may only be used for enhancements to new or existing parks
supporting the service area.  Service areas vary depending on the open space type being supported and
are summarized in Table 4-16 of the Unified Development Code.

The total balance of all PAI accounts one June 30, 2022, was: $593,422.76 and all PEA accounts was:
$820,926.72.

Public Safety Fees:

Public Safety Fees are collected with development for the purpose of public safety and related
infrastructure.  Public safety fees do not fund operating or personnel expenses.  Public Safety Fees
collected were $719,616. In FY 2022 no expenses were incurred.

City of Cheyenne Core Development Incentivization Program Core Fee Waiver:

The Core Development Incentivization Program which was initially established as a development
incentive for areas surrounding downtown for fiscal year 2019 and was reestablished on November 27,
2021, through June 30, 2025.  The program allows for up to $100,000 in permit fees to be waived in a
fiscal year with a $5,000 fee reimbursement cap for individual projects within the project area.

Program activity is summarized as follows:

1. A project located at 211 E. 19th Street waiving $ $2,747.25 fees for a project with a valuation
of $150,000. The associated permits were: Mechanical, Plumbing, Electrical, and Commercial
Building Permit (including roof) (BP-21-03081).
2. A project located at 1722 Carey Avenue waiving $553.16 fees for a project with a valuation
of $21,000. The associated permit was: Commercial Building Permit (including roof) (BP-21-
03081).

Total reimbursements for FY 2022 were $3,300.41 for the two permits. $50,000 was budgeted for FY
2023.

Sweetgrass Future Road Improvement Fund:

The Sweetgrass Future Road Improvement fund had a closing balance of $63,464. This fee was
established with the 2018 Sweetgrass PUD Annexation Master Agreement. Per the agreement 90% of
revenue shall be spent within or within one quarter mile of the Sweetgrass development. The remaining
10% are not geographically restricted and may be used for transportation matters at the City’s direction.
These fees may be adjusted annually based upon the Engineering News- Record’s annual 20-city average
Construction Cost Index.  Although this is not required for reporting, it has been included in this update.
