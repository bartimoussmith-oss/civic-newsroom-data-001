RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
 Office of City Clerk
July 25, 2016
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in regular session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. in Council Chambers.  Present were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL
MEMBERS - Jim Brown, Richard Johnson, Mike Luna, Scott Roybal, Dicky Shanor and Jeff White.
Absent: Bryan Cook, Dr. Mark Rinne and Annette Williams.  Also present: Carol Intlekofer, City
Clerk, and Amanda Esch, representing Davis and Cannon, LLP, Interim City Attorney.  The pledge
of allegiance was recited.
Consent Agenda.  (All agenda items listed with the designation of [CA] are considered to be
routine items by the governing body and will be enacted by one motion. There will be no separate
discussion on these items unless a member of the governing body so requests and support by two
other members is received.  Any item removed from the Consent Agenda will be considered in its
normal sequence on the agenda.)  Mr. Luna moved to approve Consent Agenda items, seconded by
Mr. Johnson.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present with the
exception of Mr. Shanor voting “no”.
[CA]
Minutes from Regular Meeting of the Governing Body on July 11, 2016.  Motion (per
Consent Agenda) carried.
Mr. Luna moved to approve payment of vouchers as presented, seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Mr.
Luna moved to divide the question to allow for separate action on a voucher payable to Ameri-Tech
HVAC Services, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the
governing body present.  Mr. Brown declared a conflict of interest (business interest in voucher
payable to Ameri-Tech HVAC Services) and vacated Council Chambers.  Mr. Luna moved to
approve the voucher payable to Ameri-Tech HVAC Services in the amount of $41,028.34, seconded
by Mr. Johnson.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present in
Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr. Brown returned to Council Chambers.
Mr. Luna moved to divide the question to allow for separate action on a voucher payable to High
Country Harley-Davidson, Cheyenne, seconded by Mr. White.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all
members of the governing body present.  Mr. Roybal declared a conflict of interest (business interest
in voucher payable to High Country Harley-Davidson, Cheyenne) and vacated Council Chambers.
Mr. Luna moved to approve the voucher payable to High Country Harley-Davidson, Cheyenne, in
the amount of $3,470.73, seconded by Mr. Shanor.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members
of the governing body present in Council Chambers.  Following announcement of the vote, Mr.
Roybal returned to Council Chambers.  Main motion to approve payment of vouchers, excluding the
two vouchers previously approved for Ameri-Tech HVAC Services and High Country Harley-
Davidson, Cheyenne, carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing body present.

nd
Chapter 8.60, Nuisances, of Title 8, Health and Safety, of the Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming.  Mr. Johnson moved to approve on second reading, seconded by Mr. White.  Inquiry was
made regarding how many property owners might be affected due to proposed new wording
contained in Section 8.60.090 (A), Snow, ice or slush as a nuisance, that states “Every person who
owns or occupies any property abutting a right-of-way that contains a sidewalk, any portion of which
sidewalk is located within ten (10) feet of any boundary of the property, shall, within a twenty-four
(24) hour period after a snowfall, remove snow, ice and slush from each such sidewalk and maintain
each such sidewalk free of the same”.  Chief Brian Kozak, Police Department, stated he was aware
of one particular area and that other areas may surface in the future.  Upon inquiry on whether
notification to affected property owners would be provided, Officer Emmet Lusher, Code
Enforcement, Police Department, explained the notification process that would be utilized.  In
response to query on whether there was a method to compile an inventory and map areas that may
be affected by the wording change, Mayor Kaysen advised he will communicate with City
Engineering/GIS staff to ascertain whether an inventory/map could be generated prior to the next
Public Services Committee meeting.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” -- all members of the governing
body present.
