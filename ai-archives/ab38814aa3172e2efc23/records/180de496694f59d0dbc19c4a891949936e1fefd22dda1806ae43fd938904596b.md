Bid #E-19-24 was solicited for One (1) New Sliding Axle Transportation Trailer for the Street &
Alley Division.
One (1) bidder responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.  The Bid was received
from:
1.
Peterbilt of Wyoming

The bids were reviewed by Bryan Bell (Fleet Maintenance), Bill Gonzales (Street & Alley), Vicki
Nemecek (Public Works), and their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive bid from
Peterbilt of Wyoming.

The Bidding Documents contain a Surcharge and Escalator – Special Provision, that allows for the
review of surcharges related to the following items:  Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
and/or Body surcharges applied to the cost of the vehicles after the bid opening date & Model year
escalators.  The Surcharge & Escalator Contingency, is for an amount not-to-exceed Fifteen
Percent (15%), of the total value of  requested items on the Bid.  Calculated on the base cost of
trailer, the Surcharge amount to be included with this bid is $18,405.00.

The total requested bid award is for $131,345.00.
$120,300.00 Base Bid, Less (-$7,000.00) Trade In, Plus Surcharge Contingency $18,405.00.
