Planning Commission Recommendation:
The Planning Commission heard this item at their November 20, 2023 meeting and voted 3-0 to
recommend the Governing Body approve this application.

The Planning Commission originally heard this item in concert with building façade materials
requirements, but divided the question and postponed the section regarding windows. This Ordinance
reflects the windows section of the original suite of façade amendments.

UDC 6.7.5.b.1 requires openings (defined in this section as transparent windows) on the ground floor of
commercial, mixed-use, and urban loft buildings in certain zone districts. The design standards of 6.7 are
generally applicable to when development is pulled closer to the street, and the windows requirement is
an attempt to help activate the pedestrian space adjacent to the building. The windows are required on
60%-90% of the ground-level façade. Staff has received feedback from developers that the windows
portion of the design standards in 6.7 are not necessarily appropriate to multifamily development. The
proposed text amendment essentially makes ground-floor residential areas exempt from the windows
requirement. Should the Council wish to have some sort of minimum windows requirement, a smaller
percentage might be appropriate. For example, UDC 6.6.4.b.2 (multifamily design) requires 15%
windows on the entire façade, UDC 6.7.5.b.3 (a part of this section) allows upper level windows to
occupy as little as 20% of the façade, and UDC 6.9.4.b (for suburban commercial buildings) allows
arcades, awnings, or other such features to count for openings.

ORDINANCE NO. ________

ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING SUBSECTION 6.7.5.b OF THE UNIFIED
DEVELOPMENT CODE (UDC), CHANGING BUILDING FAÇADE
DESIGN REQUIREMENTS TO REDUCE REQUIRED WINDOWS ON
URBAN LOFT MULTIFAMILY RESIDENTIAL AND MIXED-USE
RESIDENTIAL DEVELOPMENT."

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne finds that the text amendment
that is the subject of this ordinance meets the criteria specified in Section 2.4.1.d of the Unified
Development Code.

Section 2.  That on November 20, 2023, the Planning Commission conducted a public
hearing, reviewed the proposed text amendment, and recommended approval of said text
amendment.

Section 3.  That Subsection 6.7.5.b Facade Openings, of Subsection 6.7.5 Building Design,
of Section 6.7 Small Scale Commercial and Mixed-use Design Standards, of Article 6 Design
Regulations, of the Unified Development Code of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby
amended as set out in this section.

b. Façade Openings. Each facade facing a street shall have openings at street level and upper
levels according to the following:
1. Between 60% and 90% of all street-level facades between 2’ and 10’ above grade shall
be transparent with views to the interior of the building. No window starting at a level
of greater than 3.5’ above the street level should be included in the calculation. Where
the interior operation or program of the building warrants screening from the street, a
closed display window of at least 2.5 feet deep may be substituted along up to 50% of
the linear distance of the façade. Any section of a building with residential dwelling
units on the floor that predominately covers the space between 2’ and 10’ above grade
are exempt from this requirement in the areas where the residential units are located.
2. No more than 20’ of building frontage shall exist at the street level without window or
door openings. On secondary or support streets that are not the primary pedestrian and
retail streets of the Center, the Director may approve facades with greater than 20’ of
building frontage without window and door openings.
3. Upper level openings shall occupy between 20% and 50% of the façade and consist of
punched and regularly spaced openings to create a rhythm and pedestrian scale for the
façade.
4. Corner buildings with two street frontages may designate one frontage as the primary
frontage; however, at least the first 30’ of secondary frontage from the corner shall
meet the above minimum opening requirements.

RECOMMENDED MOTION:

Move to recommend the Governing Body approve text amendments to the Unified Development Code

BACKGROUND:

The City’s Unified Development Code (UDC) was adopted by the Governing Body on January 23, 2012, by
Ordinance Number 3943 with an effective date of April 30, 2012.  The Unified Development Code is the
regulatory document carrying out the vision of PlanCheyenne and is reviewed annually pursuant to UDC
1.1.7 [Annual Review Requirement] to determine if any particular standard or regulation is timely,
relevant and adequately addresses current market conditions. In addition to the annual review, the
Planning and Development Director and City Engineer are authorized under UDC 2.4.1.b.3 to bring
forward text amendments on their own initiative.

City Staff brought forward a text amendment proposing changing various façade requirements for
multifamily buildings. The question was divided and the ‘windows’ portion of the façade requirements
amendment was postponed. This Staff Report is for the ‘windows’ portion of the multifamily-centric
façade amendments.

Multifamily buildings are called ‘Urban Lofts’ in certain scenarios, applying the design requirements of 6.7
in these cases. Section 6.7 requires display windows on 60% to 90% of street-level façades. This proposed
code change proposes removing the display window requirement for Urban Lofts (multifamily) and mixed-
use buildings where multifamily on the ground floor. Although this particular amendment was not
requested by the Affordable Housing Task Force nor by any specific request by members of the public;
however, Staff has run into this issue multiple times with development actions and felt that it was apropos
to the request to make façade requirements make more sense for multifamily development.
