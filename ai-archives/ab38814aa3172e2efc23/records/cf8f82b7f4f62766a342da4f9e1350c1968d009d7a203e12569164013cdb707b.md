FIRST AMENDMENT TO CITY CONTRACT 7118
 THE MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING BETWEEN
THE CITY OF CHEYENNE AND SADDLE RIDGE, LLC

1.
Parties.  This First Amendment to the Memorandum of Understanding (the "Amendment")
is made and entered into by and between Saddle Ridge, LLC (“Saddle Ridge”), 506 Shoshoni
Street, Cheyenne, Wyoming, 82009, and the City of Cheyenne, a municipality organized and
existing under the laws of the State of Wyoming (“City”), 2101 O’Neil Avenue, Cheyenne,
Wyoming, 82001.

2.
Purpose.  The purpose of this Amendment is to extend the time for performance of the
party’s respective obligations described in the Memorandum of Understanding due to mutual
delays relating to Covid 19.

3.
Recitals.

a.
Saddle Ridge and the City entered into a Memorandum of Understanding, also
known as City Contract number 7118, with an effective date of January 24, 2020 (the “MOU”).

b.
The MOU outlined each party’s responsibilities for certain improvements related to
the construction of the Saddle Ridge Subdivision, 7th and 9th Filings.  The MOU states that it
shall not extend past December 31, 2021.

4.
Amendment.  For good and valuable consideration, the receipt of which is hereby
acknowledged, pursuant to paragraph 7(a) of the MOU, the parties hereby amend paragraph 3 of
the MOU and extend the term of the MOU to December 31, 2022.

5.
No Other Amendments.  Except as set forth in this Amendment, the MOU shall remain
unaffected and shall continue in full force and effect in accordance with its terms.  If there is
conflict between this Amendment and the MOU, the terms of this MOU shall prevail.

6.
Signatures.  In witness whereof, the parties to this Amendment, through their duly
authorized representatives, have executed this Amendment on the days and dates set out below
and certify that they have read, understood and agreed to the terms and conditions of this
Amendment as set forth herein.

The effective date of this Amendment is the date of the signature last affixed to this page.

(signature page follows)

By Stefanie Boster at 3:22 pm, Feb 08, 2022
