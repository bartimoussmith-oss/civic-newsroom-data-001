```text
RESOLUTION NO. ____________

ENTITLED:
“A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE BUDGET OF THE DOWNTOWN DEVELOPMENT
AUTHORITY FOR THE FISCAL YEAR BEGINNING JULY 1, 2012 AND ENDING JUNE
30, 2013.”

WHEREAS, the Downtown Development Authority (“DDA”) was created pursuant to Ordinance
#2804 adopted by the Governing Body on July 9, 1984;

WHEREAS, Wyoming Statute §15-9-218 provides that the governing body shall approve the
proposed budget of the DDA after administrative review by City staff;

WHEREAS, Cheyenne City Code, Chapter 2.76.050(C), provides that the DDA budget shall be
subject to alteration, modication and approval by the governing body in
accordance with the policies of the City;

WHEREAS, City staff has reviewed the proposed budget received from the DDA;

NOW, THEREFORE BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING, that the proposed budget for the fiscal year ending June 30, 2013 of the Downtown
Development Authority as set forth in the attached spreadsheet entitled “Cheyenne Downtown
Development Authority, Fiscal Year 2013 Budget” in the total amount of $626,220 is hereby
approved.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED this __________day of ______________, 2012.

_____________________________

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor

(SEAL)
Attest:

________________________________________
Carol Intlekofer, City Clerk
```
