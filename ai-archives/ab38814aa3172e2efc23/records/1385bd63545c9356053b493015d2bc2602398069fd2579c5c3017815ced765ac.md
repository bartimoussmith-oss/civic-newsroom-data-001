RESOLUTION NO. __________
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION DIRECTING CITY STAFF TO PLAN AND EXECUTE
THE COMPLETION OF THE 17TH STREET LIGHTING PROJECT.”
WHEREAS, at the primary election held on August 21, 2012 in Laramie County, the voters
approved specific purposes to be funded by the 1% Specific Purpose Sales and Use Tax (“voter-
approved projects”); and
WHEREAS, pursuant to City Resolution No. 5374, attached hereto and incorporated by
reference as Exhibit A, any excess funds and interest earned thereon (“excess tax funds”) shall be
used for operation and maintenance of the voter-approved projects or any other legal purpose with
respect thereto; and
WHEREAS, pursuant to City Resolution No. 5983, attached hereto and incorporated by
reference as Exhibit B, any excess funds and interest earned thereon (“excess tax funds”) shall be
used for operation and maintenance of the voter-approved projects or any other legal purpose with
respect thereto; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Governing Body desires to see the completion of the 17th
Street lighting project with these excess tax funds; and
WHEREAS, thirty-three thousand two hundred fifty-five dollars ($33,255.00) remains in the
unexpended 2012 specific purpose sales and use tax fund to be used for the 17th Street lighting
project; and
WHEREAS, an additional appropriation of a not-to-exceed amount of two hundred fifty
thousand dollars ($250,000.00) in unexpended excess tax funds from the 2012 specific purpose sales
and use tax fund shall be used to complete the 17th Street lighting project; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Governing Body directs the City Engineer and the
Planning and Development Director of the City of Cheyenne to plan and execute the completion of
the 17th Street lighting project with these additional funds.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that:
1.
The funds in the amount of thirty-three thousand two hundred fifty-five dollars
($33,255.00) that remain in the unexpended 2012 specific purpose sales and use tax fund shall be
used for the 17th Street lighting project.
2.
An additional appropriation of a not-to-exceed amount of two hundred fifty thousand
dollars ($250,000.00) in unexpended excess tax funds from the 2012 specific purpose sales and use
tax fund shall be used to complete the 17th Street lighting project.
3.
The City Engineer and the Planning and Development Director of the City of
Cheyenne are directed to plan and execute the completion of the 17th Street lighting project with
these additional funds.
PRESENTED, READ, AND ADOPTED this ______ day of ________________, 2021.
__________________________________________
Patrick Collins, Mayor
