MEMORANDUM
TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

February 21, 2023
SUBJECT:
Request to Purchase Two (2) Used SUV’s, for the City of Cheyenne, Compliance
Department
______________________________________________
BACKGROUND INFORMATION ABOUT PURPOSE OF PROPOSED PURCHASE:
The purpose for this Request to Purchase is to expedite the acquisition of two needed vehicles for
the Compliance Department.  One vehicle will be used for a new Compliance Building Inspector
position, and one vehicle will be used for an existing Compliance Building Inspector position.  The
vehicle for the current position has been experiencing multiple mechanical failures, and has been
out of service numerous times over the recent past.

PROCUREMENT INFORMATION & PROCESS:
The City of Cheyenne, Compliance Department, is requesting spending authority in an amount
not-to-exceed $70,000.00 for the purchase of  two (2) used Mid Size SUV’s.  Each vehicle is
budgeted at $35,000.00 each. Due to the current market for used vehicles and equipment, timing
is of the essence when a used vehicle becomes available on the used vehicle market.  Additionally,
the lead times for new vehicles are growing longer and longer – often exceeding 1 year.
In accordance with the City of Cheyenne Purchasing Policy, “Selection will be made only from
vehicles or equipment meeting or exceeding the minimum requirements set forth by the
department/division. If the amount exceeds $35,000.00 the expenditure must be approved by the
Governing Body.”
This is a preemptive request for approval by the Governing Body, to grant spending authority when
a used vehicle that meets all of the minimum specifications is located & identified.  In order to
ensure that the City can make an offer on a vehicle in a timely manner, without placing a
Consideration of Award on City Council Agenda (3-4 Week Process), the Compliance Department
is requesting this spending authority in advance of beginning the search for a vehicle that meets
the needs of the Department.  This will provide the City the ability to expeditiously secure used
vehicles when suitable units are located.  In coordination with the City of Cheyenne Fleet
Maintenance Division, minimum specifications have been established for this purchase and they
are attached to this memorandum.
This Request to Purchase is being concurrently advertised in the Wyoming Tribune Eagle, in
accordance with the intent of Wyoming Statute § 15-1-113.

Funding:
020-26-2610-42-43101 (One Cent Sales Tax 19-22 / Capital Equipment Fund)
