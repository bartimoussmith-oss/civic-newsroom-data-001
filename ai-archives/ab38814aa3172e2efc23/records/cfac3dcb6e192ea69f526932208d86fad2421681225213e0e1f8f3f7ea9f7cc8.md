TO:

Mayor Patrick Collins, and City Council
FROM:

TJ Barttelbort, Purchasing Manager
DATE:

April 3, 2024
SUBJECT:
Consideration of Bid #E-15-24 for furnishing Bituminous Asphalt Hot
Mix, Tack Oil, and Cold Mix on an annual basis, for the City of Cheyenne,
Street & Alley Division
 ______________________________________________

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Two (2) bids were received from:

1.
JTL Group Inc. d/b/a Knife River (Cheyenne, WY)
2.
Simon Contractors (Cheyenne, WY)

The bids were reviewed by Bill Gonzalez (Street & Alley), Mike Schlabs (Street & Alley), and
Vicki Nemecek (Public Works).

Their recommendation is to accept the lowest responsive Bids for each Produce.
1. JTL Group Inc. d/b/a Knife River for the purchase of:
a. Bituminous Asphalt Hot Mix ($382,500.00)
b. Cold Mix ($56,250.00)
i. Total = $438,750.00

2. Simon Contractors for the purchase of:
a. Tack Oil ($14,400.00)
i. Total = $14,400.00

The total requested bid award for both Bidders is $453,150.00.

Funding:
020-26-2616-42-32302 (1% Sales Tax 19-22 / Asphalt)

$453,150.00
