Background:
The item for consideration is an Outside User Agreement.  This is approved by resolution.  Typically, a
resolution is introduced and referred to committee prior to final consideration.  If this resolution
followed the standard process, final consideration would be June 12, 2023.
The applicant has indicated they an emergency exists due to the septic system being damaged by others
which has resulted in an immediate health hazard. Specifically, the septic system
was
located on an adjacent parcel under different ownership and the new owner has damaged the leach
field to a point where the system does not function. Staff requests that this item be considered upon
introduction and not referred to committee due to the extenuating circumstances.
This property is 100% surrounded by City limits, and it is
to initiate the annexation of all County pockets. If an annexation petition were filed by the landowner by
the next submittal deadline, May 24th, the annexation would not be complete until mid-August.  Filing
would only be possible if the applicant had managed to create the necessary annexation maps required
for filing and paid all application fees ($2,275). City-initiated annexations take time and staff resources
to complete, and while the parcel will be annexed to the City in the next year or so, the health issue
created by the removal of a portion of the septic system creates more urgency for this parcel. Even if
City Staff suddenly focused on this parcel to annex, the timelines involved for either annexation scenario
would mean it would be months before annexation was complete.
Approval of this resolution would allow for the property owner to immediately connect to City sewer.
BOPU has indicated that the standard outside user agreement fee (150%) would still apply; however;
the additional 1/3rd of the cost would be refunded to the landowner if the property is annexed by the
City or the applicant within two years of resolution approval.
A reimbursement agreement for public infrastructure improvements for street and sewer was filed on
this property on April 19, 2012.  Connection to City sewer or landowner-initiated annexation is
considered a development action under the prior reimbursement notice of infrastructure
reimbursement. The 2012 infrastructure costs assigned to this lot were $8,939.39 for streets and

RESOLUTION NO. __________
ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION APPROVING SANITARY SEWER SERVICE, BY

WYOMING (LOCATED NORTHWEST OF THE INTERSECTION OF

WHEREAS, Myers, Austin Et Al ("the Owner") is the owner of the property in Laramie
County, Wyoming, more specifically described as: the southerly 127' of Lot 1, Block 1, Rogers
Subdivision, Laramie County, Wyoming (per annexation Ord #3821, bk 2078 pg 380), addressed
as 3730 Rawlins Street;
WHEREAS, the Owner’s property is outside of City limits and was originally developed
with a well and septic system;
WHEREAS, a portion of the Owner’s septic system was on a parcel to the north of their
current parcel which was annexed to the City in 2008 (Ord #3821, bk 2078 pg 380), said
annexation resulting in the creation of two parcels for tax purposes;

WHEREAS, the creation of two tax parcels created an assumption the two tax parcels
could be sold off separately, which assumption was acted upon by a previous landowner which
did indeed sell off the properties separately;

WHEREAS, the owner of the northern parcel has removed the portion of the Owner’s
septic system on their property, creating a health hazard on the Owner’s property;

WHEREAS, it is the City of Cheyenne City Council’s stated goal to initiate the annexation
of all County pockets (land 100% surrounded by City limits, but not within the City’s jurisdiction).
However, such annexations take time and resources to complete;

WHEREAS, the subject property is a County pocket (land 100% surrounded by City
limits, but not within the City), therefore it is only a matter of time before the subject property will
be annexed to the City;

WHEREAS, pursuant to Resolution No. 3577, BOPU was instructed to present to the
Governing Body for its review any application for the provision of water and/or sanitary sewer
services to any individual or entity outside the corporate limits of the City of Cheyenne;

WHEREAS, the Owner will approve and execute applicable Outside User Agreement(s)
upon approval by the Governing Body pursuant to Resolution No. 3577 and application of the
Owner if approved by the BOPU Board; and,
