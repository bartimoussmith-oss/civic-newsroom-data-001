RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE

Office of City Clerk

March 10, 2020

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne convened in a special meeting on this date
beginning at 6:00p.m. in City Council Chambers for the purpose of collective labor bargaining
with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters, International Association of Fire Fighters, Local
No. 279.  Present were:  MAYOR - Marian J. Orr; COUNCIL MEMBERS – Rocky Case, Mike
Luna, Dr. Mark Rinne, Dicky Shanor, and Jeff White.  Absent:  Bryan Cook, Ken Esquibel, Pete
Laybourn, and Scott Roybal.  Also present: Kris Jones, City Clerk; Michael O’Donnell, City
Attorney; Alessandra McCoy-Fakelman, Deputy City Attorney, Robin Lockman, City Treasurer,
and Chief Greg Hoggatt, Fire and Rescue Department.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Orr advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook live
and stated that Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings Act allows the
governing body to meet in executive session to consider or tender offers regarding salaries, wages
or other terms and conditions of employment during negotiations with the fire fighters union and
that any motion approved by the governing body to move into executive session during the special
meetings would be pursuant to this section of statute.  Mayor Orr further advised if the governing
body met in executive session, they would be meeting in the Mayor’s Conference Room, and that
Fire Union representatives were welcome to use Committee Room 104 for the purpose of meeting
to caucus.

 Michael O’Donnell, City Attorney, advised the City and Union would be signing off on
the closed agreed upon sections prior to the City presenting its counteroffer to Union
representatives.  Sections closed include:  1-Parties, 2-Definitions, 5-Separability, 6-Emobodiment
of Agreement, 7-Re-Negotiations, 9-Non-Discrimination, 10-Annexation of Areas into the City,
11-Voluntary Demotion, 12-Union Representation, 14-Strikes, 23-Working in a Higher Job Rank,
24-Specialized Emergency Response Teams, 25-Conversion of Benefit Hours, 27-Vacations, 29-
Berevement Leave, 30-Leave of Absence, 31-Health, Dental and Life Insurance, 32-Uniforms, 33-
Personal Protective Equipment, 34-Union Dues, 36-Residency, 38-Union Use of City Facilities,
39-Alcohol and Controlled Substance Police, 40-Family and Medical Leave Policy, 42-Safety and
Health, 43-Longevity Pay, and 44-Shift Trainers.

Mayor Orr, upon inquiry from Kevin West, Union Representative, advised additional
meetings could be considered, noting the negotiation timeframe ends on March 20, 2020.

Mayor Orr asked that parties involved in the negotiation process exercise caution regarding
the Covid-19 virus and to please stay home if they become ill.

Mayor Orr further advised that there will be some occasions where the Union will retreat
for the purpose of caucusing and the governing body would either go into Executive Session or
recess during those time periods.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Shanor moved to meet in
executive session, seconded by Case.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the
governing body present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body
present, the City Clerk, City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer, and Chief of the Fire
and Rescue Department would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio
recording of the special meeting was stopped; executive session convened at 6:13 p.m. in the
Mayor’s Conference Room]

Following closure of the executive session, Mayor Orr reconvened the special meeting at
6:22 p.m.

Mayor Orr provided brief summary on each remaining open section relating to the City’s
counteroffer.  The following Sections contained a revised offer from the City: bundled offer for
15-Department Staffing, 17-Hours of Work, 18-Trading Shifts, 20-Holidays, and 35-Mileage
Allowance and individual offers for sections; 3-Exclusive Bargaining Agent, 8-Grievance
Procedures, 13-Management Rights, 19-Inclement Weather Conditions, 21-Classsification of
Employment and Basic Wages, 22-Overtime, 26-Wellness/Fitness Program, 28-Sick Leave, 37-
Time Off for Union Business and 41-Prevailing Rights.  Mr. O’Donnell advised the remaining
open sections are re-offered as originally offered by the City:  Sections 2-Definitions, 23-Working
in a Higher Job Rank, 24-Specialized Emergency Response Teams, and 41-Prevailing Rights.
(Section 4-Term of Agreement is undetermined at this time of either a 1 or 2 year agreement).

Mayor Orr clarified that although the City supports the concept of forming a “Squad” with
personnel for the Fire Department, the City is not including it in the contract at this time.   Upon
inquiry from Mr. O’Donnell, Mr. West confirmed Sections 46-Military Leave, 47-Tuition
Reimbursement and 48-Parental Leave were previously closed.

Upon Union request, Michael O’Donnell, City Attorney, provided details relating to the
City’s offer for section #22-Overtime.

Union representatives requested a 30-minute recess to review the City’s counteroffer at
6:34 p.m.  Members of the Governing Body stood in recess.  Mayor Orr, at the request of Union
representatives, granted an extension of time for an additional 20 minutes at 7:02 p.m.

Following the recess, Union representatives provided a verbal explanation of their
counteroffer for the remaining open sections.

Pursuant to Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x), Mr. Luna moved to meet in executive
session, seconded by Case.  Motion carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body
present.  Mayor Orr identified, in addition to members of the governing body present, the City
Clerk, City Attorney, Deputy City Attorney, City Treasurer, and Chief of the Fire and Rescue
Department would also be present at the executive session.  [City Clerk note: Audio recording of
the special meeting was stopped; executive Session convened at 7:28 p.m. in the Mayor’s
Conference Room]
