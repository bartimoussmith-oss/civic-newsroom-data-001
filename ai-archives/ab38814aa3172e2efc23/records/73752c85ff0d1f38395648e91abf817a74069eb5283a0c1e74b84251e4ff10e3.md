```text
MEMORANDUM
TO:

The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:

Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:

March 4, 2024
SUBJECT:
Re-Appropriation to be considered for approval

On March 11, 2024, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance Committee
to re-appropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2024 City of Cheyenne Budget per W.S. § 16-4-
112 and 16-4-113.  Below is more detailed information regarding this re-appropriation request.

GENERAL FUND
1. Wage Increases.  In November 2023, salary adjustments were approved for 116 city
employees through resolution #6367.  As I reported to you on December 1, 2023, four
additional employees were identified that should have been included.  These employees
had previously moved to a different job title, but their job duties did not change, and
therefore their earlier city work experience should have been weighed more heavily.  Since
that email was sent, Human Resources has identified an additional seven employees. This
reappropriation will increase the applicable budget line items for the remaining six months
of FY 2024 at a cost of $18,155 from General Fund reserves and $47,395 from all other
funds.

2. Repairs to the City’s Activity Center.   A request of $45,000 from General Fund reserves
is being requested for Facilities Maintenance to make repairs to the City’s Activity Center,
which include fixing the sewer line, replacing the hot water tank, and installing a new door.
These repairs will allow Animal Control to move into the building.

3. Spiker Parking Structure Security Services.  A request of $15,500 from General Fund
reserves is being requested to hire professional security services at a cost of $3,100 for five
months to be provided at the Spiker Parking structure.

4. Thomas Heights Settlement.  The Governing Body approved a settlement on the Thomas
Heights lawsuit agreeing that the city will complete drainage improvements under an
established upon timeline.   An estimate to modify a nearby detention pond and install a
sewer system is $350,000 to be paid from General Fund reserves.
```

```text
5. Cheyenne National Cemetery Road Improvements Project.  $4,500 is being requested
from General Fund reserves to reimburse the City Engineer’s professional services account
for geotechnical services provided for improvements to the road at the Cheyenne National
Cemetery.  The remaining estimated project amount of $245,000 will also be paid from
General Fund reserves.

6. US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Climate Pollution Reduction Grant
Application Assistance (CPRG).  The Mayor approved a contract with the Rocky
Mountain Institute (RMI) to assist with a time sensitive grant application for the US EPA
CPRG Program Implementation Grant, which is due April 1st.   $60,000 is being requested
from General Fund reserves.

7. Purchase of BEAST Facility.  The Governing Body approved the purchase of the BEAST
facility in the amount of $4,259,075 ($4,250,000 was approved through Contract #7896,
plus $9,075 in closing costs approved through Contract Modification #1).  General Fund
reserves are being requested to cover this purchase.

8. BEAST Facility Property/Liability Insurance.  $4,250 is being requested from General
Fund reserves for property/liability insurance coverage of the new BEAST Facility.

9. Axon Body Worn Video Cameras for Police Officers.  The Cheyenne Police Department
received approval from the Governing Body to purchase 120 new body worn cameras for
police officer staff.  The total equipment cost is $800,795, which is to be paid over a five-
year period.  The fiscal year 2024 payment is $160,160 and is being requested from General
Fund reserves.

10. New Boilers for the Municipal Building.  $240,292 is being requested from General Fund
reserves to pay for the replacement of the Municipal Building boilers. The total cost to
purchase the boilers was $215,292 and was conducted through an emergency procurement
with Ameri-Tech.  An additional $25,000 is being requested to pay for professional
contract services to hire a mechanical engineer to assist with proper installation of the new
boilers.

OTHER FUNDS
The City of Cheyenne has received the following new grants and donations, or is reappropriating
unspent existing grants, and are included on this reappropriation:
Local and State Grant Fund (011) – new grants received:
 $4,000 – Visit Cheyenne Marketing Grant
```

```text
 $125,720 – US Department of Energy, Energy Efficiency and Conservation Block Grant
(EECBG) Program, for Civic Center lighting
Other non-General Fund items being requested:
1. Development Impact Fees Fund (013) – An amount of $700,000 is being requested for
the potential land purchase of a new fire station 6 location.  This will be paid for by the
public safety infrastructure fees held in this fund’s reserves.

2. Recreation Programs Fund (014) – An amount of $20,000 is being requested to complete
the Botanic Gardens Master Plan Update, which will be paid from miscellaneous
donations.  An additional amount of $44,000 is being requested to purchase equipment for
the new Gymnastics Facility.  The source of funds is donations from the Cheyenne
Gymnastics Parent Booster Club.

3. Miscellaneous Federal Grant Fund (025) – The Governing Body approved $2.5 million
in American Rescue Plan Federal Recovery (ARPA) funds for construction of the City’s
new gymnastics facility.  If costs exceed $2.5 million in the current fiscal year, additional
funds will be reappropriated.

4. Special Purpose Option Tax Fund (030) – An amount of $1 million dollars is requested
to be paid from the West Edge District 2017 Ballot Special Purpose Option Tax Fund for
City Contract #7782 with DHM Design Corporation for the Reed Avenue Corridor Project.
```
