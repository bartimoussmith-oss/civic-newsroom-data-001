EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:

This zone change application proposes changing two contiguous lots located at the corner of Campstool
Road and Hutchins Drive from CB Community Business to LI Light Industrial.  This land totals about 4.72
acres.  This land is currently vacant, and the applicant proposes developing the property with a large
equipment retailer (specifically tractors) and equipment services facility.

PlanCheyenne generally considers LI zoning an appropriate zone in areas designated Industrial by the
Future Land Use Map.  The LI zone is compatible with the surrounding area and the proposed
development on the parcels in question.

The LI zone district is intended for intensive employment uses that may have accessory commercial,
service or office uses, but cannot readily fit into other general business or mixed-use areas due to the
nature or intensity of operations.  Uses in the LI zone district often have impacts such as noise, outdoor
storage, and freight needs that make them incompatible in other areas of the community.  The proposed
development as well as the current commercial development in this area match the development patterns
anticipated by areas zoned LI.

Due to meeting the review criteria of Section 2.2.1.d of the Cheyenne Unified Development
Code, staff recommends approval of this zone change.

The proposed development is located in an area that is relatively undeveloped but has been
designated Industrial on the Future Land Use Map.  The adjacent property to the south is a large
equipment retailer with outdoor sales, similar to the proposed project.  The property to the east

important factors in siting industrial areas.
The proposed LI zone district is not required to meet any design standards.  Setbacks and lot
coverage requirements for the current CB zone district are identical to those of the proposed LI
zone district.  The application has the ability to meet the required setbacks and lot standards
established by the proposed LI zone.

6. ZONING: FUTURE DEVELOPMENT - The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable
standards for development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options in the
event that any specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval.
The applicant is proposing rezoning this land with the intent to develop the property with a large
equipment retailer (specifically tractors) and equipment services facility which includes outdoor
sales.  The applicant is pursuing a Conditional Use Application for outdoor sales concurrently with
this zone change application.  The proposed development is able to meet all standards of the
proposed zone district.  The LI zone would allow a variety of commercial and low-intensity
industrial uses including but not limited to office space, general retail, grocery store, or artist
studios in the event the specific development proposed in association with this zone change
application is not executed.
