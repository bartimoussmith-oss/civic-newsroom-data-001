16. Presentation of Wyoming Downs "Gateways to Wyoming East and
West."
24 - 027
Presentation, Gateway East, LLC
Attachments:
Eric Nelson, Wyoming Downs, gave an overview of the project.
Chairman Lovett opened the meeting for PUBLIC COMMENT. Linda Coatney,
resident, asked how much development was planned at Terry Bison Ranch, whether
the development would be primarily in Wyoming or Colorado, where customers would
come from, and if it would compete with Cheyenne Frontier Days (CFD).
Mr. Nelson said plans include hotels, expansion of the RV facilities, an indoor arena,
rodeo facilities, an Indian relay, and a race track facility. He said development would
be on both sides of the Wyoming/Colorado state line, but the majority would be on the
Wyoming side. Mr. Nelson noted the targeted customers would be from Colorado. He
said he hopes to partner with CFD.
Hearing no further PUBLIC COMMENT, Chairman Lovett closed the comment period.
The presentation was received and filed.
