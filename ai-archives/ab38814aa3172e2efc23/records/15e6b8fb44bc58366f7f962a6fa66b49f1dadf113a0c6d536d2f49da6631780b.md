SUBJECT: Notice of Emergency Procurement -Traffic Signal-Logan & Pershing
BACKGROUND:
A traffic accident occurred on March 20,2021, at the intersection of East Pershing Boulevard &
Logan Avenue.The traffic accident caused substantial damage to the traffic signal pole. The
traffic signal pole was damaged so severely, that the pole was at risk of falling.
EMERGENCY PROCUREMENT/PURCHASING POLICY:
In accordance with the City of Cheyenne Purchasing Policy, Page16, Emergency Procurement,
"In the event of a sudden or unexpected emergency situation where it is imperative that immediate
actionbe taken to prevent serious damage tohealth, welfare or safety of the public, the Mayormay
authorize purchases of goods or services exceeding the prescribed limits of these Purchasing
Procedures, provided that the emergencyis present, immediate and existing, and not a condition
which may or may not arise in the future or a condition which reasonably may be foreseen in time
to advertise for bids; and that such emergency procurement be made with such competition as is
practicable under the circumstances."
The Memorandum provided to the City Purchasing Office, dated March22,2021, isincluded with
this Notice of Emergency Procurement.
COURSE OF ACTION:
City of Cheyenne Traffic Engineer An is s a Gerard, recommended the following course of action
to remedy this emergency situation:
There are currently Two (2) new uninstalled traffic signal poles in town for another signal project

new traffic signal poles to facilitate the emergency replacement at the Pershing & Logan
