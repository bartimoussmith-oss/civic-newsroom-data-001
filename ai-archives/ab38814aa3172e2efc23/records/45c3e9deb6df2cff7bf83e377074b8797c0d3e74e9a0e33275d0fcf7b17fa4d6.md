Section 1.
That, pursuant to Wyo. Stat.
§
15-7-407, the Board of Public Utilities
(“BOARD”) shall fix the rates for water and sanitary sewer services furnished to customers, and
any revision thereof is subject to the governing body’s review, modification, and approval.
Section 2.
That the BOARD is authorized by State Statute and City Code to fix rates,
fees, and penalties for water and sanitary sewer services furnished to customers to:
(a)
Pay interest charges and principal payments on all outstanding water and sanitary sewer
loans and bonds as they are due;
(b)
Pay for the operation and maintenance of the water and sanitary sewer systems,
including salaries, wages and benefits, and the cost of all materials and supplies
necessary for repairs, replacements, and upgrades to the systems;
(c)
Cover all miscellaneous expenses;
(d)
Cover all usual extensions and enlargements, together with a reasonable allowance for
emergency and unforeseen expenses; and
(e)
Provide and maintain a depreciation fund for each of the water and sanitary sewer
departments.
Section 3.
That the BOARD has determined that an increase in the water and sanitary
sewer rates are justified to ensure adequate revenue is generated in order to pay for costs associated
with those services rendered.
Section 4.
That, pursuant to City Code Section 13.04.140, rates and charges for the
consumption of city utility services, charges and fees for connections, inspections, penalties, shutoff
penalties and meter installations shall be established by the BOARD, approved by the Governing
Body and filed with the City Clerk and business office of the BOARD.
Section 5.
That, pursuant to City Ordinance No. 4308, adopted on June 8, 2020, prior to
the beginning of each fiscal year, the BOARD will calculate new rates for water and sewer services
based on the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index, All Urban Consumers, Water and
Sewer Maintenance and the Wyoming Cost Living Index
— Southeast Region and prepare new rate
schedules for Water and Sewer services.
Section 6.
That the Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Price Index, All Urban
Consumers, Water and Sewer Maintenance for the calendar year 2020 was 3.3% and the Wyoming
Cost of Living Index
— Southeast Region for the second quarter of 2020 was 1.1%; and the
Engineering News Record Construction Cost Index (ENR CCI) for calendar year 2020, was 1.7%.
Section 7.
That the BOARD established by Resolution No. 202 1-01, adopted April 26,
2021, revised Water rates to be charged to users of the Cheyenne Water System as shown and set
forth on the resolution attached hereto as Exhibit A and incorporated by reference.
Section 8.
That the BOARD established by Resolution No. 2021-02, adopted April 26,
2021, revised Sewer rates to be charged to users of the Cheyenne Sewer System as shown and set
forth on the resolution attached hereto as Exhibit B and incorporated by reference.
Section 9.

2021, revised Water and Sewer Connections Fees, System Development Fees, Pump Station
