RESOLUTION NO. _______
ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION ADOPTING THE WEST EDGE VISIONARY BLUEPRINT AS A WORKING
DOCUMENT AND GUIDE TO THE COMMUNITY’S GOALS OF MANAGING STORMWATER IN
THE LOWER CAPITOL BASIN, MITIGATING BROWNFIELDS, CREATING MEANINGFUL PUBLIC
SPACES, AND FACILITATING PRIVATE INVESTMENT IN THE NEAR WEST SIDE OF
DOWNTOWN CHEYENNE.”
WHEREAS, the impacts from a storm event centered over the Lower Capitol Basin could cost the
Cheyenne community more than $70 million directly, and over $100 million including indirect costs; and
WHEREAS, the voters of Laramie County have approved $4.8 million in 6th Penny Optional Sales Tax
funds to begin addressing stormwater management in the Lower Capitol Basin; and
WHEREAS, it is desirable to leverage local tax dollars with other, non-local funds to extend the impact
and efficacy of local funding; and
WHEREAS, the West Edge Visionary Blueprint is the embodiment of citizen input received at three open
house meetings, the comments and discussion of 200 registered website users, and 1200 unique visitors
to the online Mindmixer web platform, along with commentary received during a public comment
period; and
WHEREAS, the West Edge project has been guided by representatives from the City of Cheyenne,
Laramie County, and the Downtown Development Authority/Mainstreet Board; and
WHEREAS, the West Edge Visionary Blueprint acknowledges the need to maintain a flexible approach to
siting stormwater improvements to maximize opportunities for efficient property acquisition costs; and
WHEREAS, the flexible approach enables implementation of incremental and segmented solutions
tailored to match potential funding sources; and
WHEREAS, stormwater infrastructure can serve a dual purpose as civic open space, with the potential to
function as a neighborhood amenity capable of attracting reinvestment; and
WHEREAS, a variety of stormwater feature concepts are illustrated in the West Edge Visionary Blueprint
that can be used to create an interconnected system of detention and water quality features; and
WHEREAS, redevelopment concepts supported by potential civic amenities have been illustrated to
demonstrate the potential of the West Edge District; and
WHEREAS, the City and Coalition Partners have received a $1 million U.S. Environmental Protection
Agency Assessment Grant to fund planning and environmental assessments in an effort to encourage
redevelopment and investment; and

WHEREAS, a portion of the Assessment Grant funding has been utilized to conduct environmental
assessments on 24 parcels to aid property owners in characterizing the presence of any contaminants
and to streamline future real estate transactions; and
WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has been awarded a $1.2 million U.S. Environmental Protection Agency
Cleanup Revolving Loan Fund to assist property owners in mitigating environmental issues; and
WHEREAS, the West Edge Visionary Blueprint also contains potential tools for consideration which may
enable further advancement of the primary project components, many of which will require further
research and consideration.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING:
That the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne hereby adopts the West Edge Visionary Blueprint as
the working document and guide to the community’s goals of managing stormwater in the Lower
Capitol Basin, mitigating brownfields, creating meaningful public spaces, and facilitating private
investment in the near west side of Downtown Cheyenne.
BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED, that the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne recommends that the West
Edge Visionary Blueprint be used to guide and shape the improvement and revitalization of the West
Edge and Lower Capitol Basin areas.
PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED THIS __ DAY OF _____, 2014.
