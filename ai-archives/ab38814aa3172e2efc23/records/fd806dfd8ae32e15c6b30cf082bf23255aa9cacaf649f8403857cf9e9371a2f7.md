FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Tuesday, July 22, 2025, in Council Chambers
and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMITTEE MEMBERS: Pete Layboum, Chairman; Ken Esquibel; Mark Moody; and Scott
Roybal.
AGENDA ITEMS
15.
ORDINANCE —2’ READII’JG
— Defining the terms and conditions of employment for
sworn
officers of the
Cheyenne
Police
Department
for
Fiscal
Year
2025-2026.
(SPONSORS - DR. RINI4E AND MR. SEGRAVE)
Mr. Roybal moved to approve on 2 reading, seconded by Mr. Moody.
Motion carried by
unanimous voice vote.
Captain David Janes, Police Department, provided a staff report.
22.
LEASES/CONTRACTS/LEGAL:
a)
Memorandum of Understanding between City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police
Department, and Laramie County School District Number One for the School
Resource Officers Program. (Revenue to the City)
Mr. Moody moved to approve, seconded by Mr. Esquibel.
Motion carried by unanimous voice
vote.
Sergeant Robert Peete, Police Department, provided a staff report and committee member Esquibel
commented on the item,
b)
Professional Services Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and GLM Design
Group, LLC, for the Clear Creek Inline Stormwater Detention Facility project.
(2023-2026 Optional One Percent Sales Tax Fund and FEMA BRIC Grant)
Mr. Esquibel moved to approve in an amount not to exceed $403,742.16, seconded by Mr. Moody.
Motion carried by unanimous voice vote.
Wes Bay, City Drainage Engineer, provided a staff report.
Chainnan Layboum and committee
members Moody and Roybal commented on the item.
