```text
UDC-22-00029
March 7, 2022
zone is intended for more automobile-centric development. The MUB zone allows a variety of
commercial and residential uses, while the CB zone is more commercial focused. This location
along a major thoroughfare is more likely to develop with  commercial-style uses. The
specifically-proposed use associated with this zone change is allowed in both zone districts.

The proposed zone change complies with review criterion 3.

4) The ability of the subject property and surrounding vicinity to be developed according to the
Subdivision Standards in Article 4 considering the proposed zoning (UDC 2.2.1.d.4)

The existing block is within the anticipated size for regional commercial development associated
with CB zoning. However, it is much larger than anticipated for mixed-use development. Mixed-
use development anticipates smaller blocks than regional commercial centers, so the proposed
CB zoning is compliant with block sizes, while the current MUB zoning is not. The block is largely
already developed and limited options are available to create meaningful street network
connections. No additions to the street network are proposed.

There are no existing approved accesses to the subject property, and it is unlikely WYDOT would
allow another access off of College at this location (no matter the zoning). Development of the
property would require access off of Charles or a shared access with an adjacent property.

Infrastructure (streets and utilities) exist in the area, but improvements may be required with
development.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 4.

5) The ability of the application to support the Intent and Applicability statements of the
proposed zoning district and achieve the Design Objectives of any specific standards. (UDC
2.2.1.d.5)

The CB zone is intended for larger commercial uses serving the community as a whole and is
applicable in activity centers. Internal circulation between adjacent sites is encouraged. The CB
zone applies commercial design standards.

This property is adjacent to CB zoning (to the north), and the proposed zone change is
effectively an expansion of an existing CB zone district which meets the intent and applicability
of the CB zone.

The proposed zone change for the subject property complies with review criterion 5.

6) The ability of all potential future development to meet all applicable standards for
development in the proposed zoning district, including other development options if any
specific development proposed in association with the application is not executed after
approval. (UDC 2.2.1.d.6)

The specific development proposed in concert with this rezoning is a commercial business which
would be allowed in either the current or proposed zone. The purpose of the zone change is to
modify the required setbacks and building design standards. Should the proposed development
not be executed after approval, there are also a number of commercial uses which would be
eligible to be established at this location.
```
