Attachment “B”

CONTINUING CONTROL ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

The Cheyenne Transit Program. is purchasing a vehicle(s) for use in its Transportation Program with a grant
from the Wyoming Department of Transportation (WYDOT) using Federal Transit Administration (FTA) pass
through funding.  As a condition of this grant agreement with WYDOT, the Cheyenne Transit Program
understands that the vehicle must be used in the manner described in the grant agreement and that this vehicle(s)
has a minimum useful life of five (5) years or 100,000 miles.  Additional details for the vehicle use, disposal
and useful life can be found in the Use of Project Equipment section of the grant agreement and the State
Management Plan.  Signing below indicates that you acknowledge and understand these requirements and will
present this form to your respective County Clerk during vehicle licensing.

Vehicles purchased all or in part with federal and/or state funds through the WYDOT and the FTA cannot be
used as collateral or traded without the express permission in writing from WYDOT.  During the useful life of
the vehicle(s), the Cheyenne Transit Program and the WYDOT must certify to the FTA that the vehicle is being
maintained and used in accordance with the grant agreement.  The Cheyenne Transit Program shall present a
copy of this form to the Laramie County Clerk along with the title paperwork when licensing the vehicle to
ensure the appropriate steps are taken to ensure the continuing control of federally funded assets.

TO:  LARAMIE COUNTY CLERK

To be in compliance with the FTA and the WYDOT for grant funding, the statement below should be conveyed
on the face of the vehicle title.  It is the Cheyenne Transit Program’s responsibility to advise title issuing
agencies a vehicle was purchased with funding.

Please place the following statement in a conspicuous place on the face of the title for this vehicle(s):

“WYDOT Grant – Restricted”

By:

Signature

Printed Name and Title

Cheyenne Transit Program

Date
