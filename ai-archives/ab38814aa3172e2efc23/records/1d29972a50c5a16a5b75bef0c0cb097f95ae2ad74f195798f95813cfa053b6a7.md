MEMORANDUM
TO:
The Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and City of Cheyenne Council Members
FROM:
Robin Lockman, City Treasurer
DATE:
September 12, 2024
SUBJECT:
Final Fiscal Year 2024 reappropriation to be considered for approval
On September 23, 2024, the Governing Body will be referring a resolution to the Finance
Committee to reappropriate funds for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2024 City of Cheyenne Budget per
W.S. § 16-4-112 and 16-4-113.
I am requesting an increase of $1,000,000 to both the revenue and expenditure budgets of the self-
insurance fund.  This request relates to last fiscal year.  We are still waiting for the incurred but
not reported (IBNR) determination from Blue Cross Blue Shield (BCBS) of Wyoming.  IBNR is
a term used by insurance companies and self-insured plans to describe the amount of money they
may owe to claimants who have had a covered loss but have not yet filed a claim.  IBNR is an
estimate because insurers don’t know how many losses have occurred or how severe each loss is.
As this is an unknown amount, I have overestimated the amount of budget that may be required.
For comparison, last year’s IBNR was $750,000.  Once we receive this estimate from BCBS in
mid-October, I will post it to the general ledger and will finalize and disseminate the annual report
of the City’s self-insurance fund.
Please feel free to contact me at 637-6336 if you have questions.
