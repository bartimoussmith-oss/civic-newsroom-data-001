```text
RESOLUTION NO.________

ENTITLED:  “A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE TO
APPROVE AND FILE THE WYOMING DIVISION OF VICTIM
SERVICES FUNDING APPLICATION STATE FISCAL YEAR 2023 AND
2024, JULY 1, 2022 TO JUNE 30, 2024.”

WHEREAS, pursuant to Wyo. Stat. Ann. § 1-40-118, all grantees receiving VOCA,
VAWA and FVSPA funds are required to submit additional subgrantee reports to DVS in
accordance with deadlines established through the Office on Violence Against Women and the
Family Violence Prevention and Services Program; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance Program will
apply for funds to provide basic, direct services to victims of crime and be in good standing with
the Wyoming Division of Victim Services Rules. Awarded applications are funded with one, or a
combination of, State Domestic Violence and Sexual Assault funds, State Victim/Witness funds,
State Child Advocacy funds, State Salary funds, Federal Violence Against Women Act (VAWA)
Services, Training, Law Enforcement Officers, and Prosecution (STOP) funds, Sexual Assault
Services Program (SASP) funds, federal Victim of Crime Act (VOCA) funds and Federal Family
Violence Prevention Services Act (FVPSA) funds; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance
Program will submit the application through the WyoGrants System by Tuesday, March 15, 2022
for State Fiscal Years 2023 and 2024; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne, Cheyenne Police Department Victim Assistance
Program will be required to submit a DUNS number and register the DUNS number with the
System for Award Management (SAM) database; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne will be required to meet a 20% cash or in-kind match.
Applicants will not be required to provide match information at the time of application.  Upon
receipt of the award notice, applicants will be required to submit a final detailed budget, including
match, based on actual award amounts as part of their award stipulations and submit a match
certification form; and

WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne, the City of Cheyenne,
Cheyenne Police Department Victims Assistance, understand the long-term obligations of the
Wyoming Division of Victim Services Funding Application State Fiscal Year 2023 and 2024, July
1, 2022 to June 30, 2024.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the Governing Body approves the funding application and
application process through the Wyoming Division of Victim Services State Fiscal Year 2023 and
2024 (July 1, 2022 to June 30, 2022.)
By Stefanie Boster at 8:27 pm, Feb 08, 2022
```
