Date:
February 6, 2024

Background:
This is an amendment to the original Development Agreement made between the Developer of
Niobrara Energy Park, COLL-IND LLC and the City and County in 2012, Resolution 5295.  As
described in this resolution and on page 3 of the original Development Agreement document,
installation of an 8-foot sidewalk along the east side of Energy Drive to Fox Farm Road was
required.  It is the opinion of staff that construction of this segment of sidewalk is not in the
best interest of the City or County based on plans for future installation of storm sewer pipe in
this area as any sidewalk installed will likely be removed and/or damaged in the future.  City
staff, the County and the Developer have agreed that the Developer should  pay the City
$60,000.00 toward construction of a Greenway connector alternative as shown in the attached
exhibits.

RESOLUTION NO. __________

ENTITLED: "A RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING THE MAYOR TO EXECUTE AN
AMENDMENT TO THE DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT BETWEEN
THE BOARD OF COUNTY COMMISSIONERS OF LARAMIE COUNTY,
WYOMING, THE CITY OF CHEYENNE, AND COLL-IND, LLC
REGARDING THE NIOBRARA ENERGY PARK."

WHEREAS, On July 11, 2011, the Governing Body approved Resolution #5295
authorizing the Mayor and the City Clerk to sign a County Final Plat and Development Agreement
for the Niobrara Energy Park;

WHEREAS, on May 7, 2012, Mayor Richard Kaysen signed the Development Agreement
between COLL-IND, LLC ("Developer"), City of Cheyenne and the Board of Commissioners of
Laramie County, Wyoming and the document was recorded in the Laramie County Clerk's office,
reference # 590831, Bk 2274, page 343;

WHEREAS, a master plan has been completed for the Fox Farm Road Corridor that may
include certain storm water drainage improvements that may impact the Energy Drive right-of-
way planned improvements of the Development Agreement;

WHEREAS, the original Development Agreement describes improvements to be installed
by the developer to include an 8’ concrete sidewalk along the east side of Energy Drive. The area
that this sidewalk is to be installed in appears to be the most likely corridor for a future storm sewer
pipe from the Fox Farm road-way improvement project;

WHEREAS, all parties agree that it would be cost effective to defer the installation of the
sidewalk until after the Fox Farm Road project improvement has been completed;

WHEREAS, all parties agree that the Fox Farm Road project timeline is uncertain and that
any concrete poured today would likely be destroyed in the future; and,

WHEREAS, the Developer has offered to pay the amount of sixty thousand dollars
($60,000) to the City of Cheyenne toward the construction of an alternative Greenway connection
as set forth in Exhibit A, attached, to be located within the right-of-way of east of Allison Road,
south of ARP Elementary School and north of E. Allison Road to make an alternative transportation
connection into the business park.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY
OF CHEYENNNE, WYOMING, that the Mayor is hereby authorized and directed to execute the
Amendment #1 to the Niobrara Energy Park Development Agreement and such other documents
as necessary to effectuate the same.
