```text
FINANCE COMMITTEE
MINUTES
A meeting of the Finance Committee was held on Tuesday, May 6, 2025, in Council Chambers
and via electronic meeting, starting at 12:00 p.m. Those in attendance were as follows:
COMMIflEE MEMBERS: Pete Laybourn, Chairman; Ken Esquibel; Mark Moody; and Scott
Roybal.
AGENDA ITEMS
Chairman Laybouni called the meeting to order at 12:00 p.m. and immediately relinquished the
chair to Mr. Moody for item #6 due to a conflict of interest on the item.
6.
APPLICATIONS/LICENSES/PERMITS:
a)
Application for a winery liquor license filed with the City Clerk’s Office for
Wagonhound Wine LLC d/b/a Wagonhound Wine Company, 1610 Capitol Ave.,
Cheyenne, WY.
Mr. Esquibel moved to approve, seconded by Mr. Roybal. Motion carried by unanimous voice
vote.
Kylie Soden, City Clerk, provided a staff report and Jackson Laybourn, applicant, provided
additional infonnation. Committee members Esquibel and Roybal commented on the item.
Following the vote on item #6, Chairman Laybourn rejoined and assumed the role as Chair ofthe
meeting at 12:03 p.m.
9.
ORDNANCE 3M READING — Amending and updating the Solid Waste Fee Schedule in
accordance with Section 8.44.010 of the Municipal Code of the City of Cheyenne,
Wyoming and repealing Ordinance No. 4571.
Mr. Roybal moved to approve on 3d and final reading, seconded by Mr. Esquibel. Motion carried
with Mr. Esquibel and Mr. Roybal voting “YES” and Mr. Moody voting “NO.”
Craig LaVoy, Public Works Deputy Director, provided a staff report and committee members
Esquibel and Moody commented on the item.
22.
CONSIDERATION OF BIDS/PURCHASES/REQUESTS FOR PROPOSALS:
d)
Consideration to purchase between the City of Cheyenne and Advanced Security
Technologies LLC, for the purchase of nine (9) Modular Vehicle Barrier Systems
and Trailer Packages. (General Fund Reserves)
```
