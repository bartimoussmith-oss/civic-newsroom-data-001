SUBJECT:
Investment Policy Statement

On January 25, 2022, the City’s Investment Committee, which is comprised of the Mayor, City
Treasurer, City Council President and Finance Committee Chair, voted to recommend approval of
revisions made to the City’s Investment Policy Statement.
The City is fortunate to have Mr. Patrick Fleming, the Chief Investment Officer for the State of
Wyoming, and Mr. Jeffrey Prince, a local investment advisor, to serve as non-voting  members of
this Committee.  They provided their investment expertise in making suggested changes to the
policy statement.  In addition, the City’s new Investment Advisor, Federated Hermes, reviewed
the City’s policy statement to ensure we comply with Wyoming State Statutes.
As always, if you have any questions, please give me a call at 637-6336 or email at
rlockman@cheyennecity.org.

City of Cheyenne, Wyoming
Investment Policy Statement
Standards of Care
All participants inthe investment process shall be held to the highest standards of professional and
ethical conduct.Each participant should be committed tohonesty, integrity, and accountability in
all of his or her decisions and actions relating to the investment process.
Compliance with Laws, Rules, Regulations and Policies: All participants in the investment
process must comply with any and all applicable laws, rules and regulations and the terms and
provisions of this Investment Policy.
Prudence: The standard of care to be applied by all participants in the investment process shall
be the “prudent person” standard.
Any person, persons, or third parties investing or managing the City'sfinancial assets, shall doso
as a prudent investor would, by considering the purposes, terms, distribution requirements, and
other circumstances, and exercising reasonable care, skill and caution in all decisions and actions.
Conflicts of Interest: All participants in the investment process have a duty to avoid financial,
business or other relationships whichmight cause a conflict with the performance of their duties
and could reasonably be expected to interfere with their duties or their ability to make unbiased
and objective recommendations and decisions.Each participant should conduct himself orherself
so as to avoid even the appearance of such conflicts of interest.
To avoid conflicts of interest, all participants in the investment process shall, at a minimum:
·refrainfrom personal and business activity that could conflict with proper execution and
management ofthe investment program, orthat could impair their ability to make impartial
investment decisions;
·disclose to the Mayor of the City of Cheyenne any material interest in financial institutions
withwhich they conduct business; and
refrain from undertaking any personal investment transactions with the same individual
(e.g., investment advisor) with whom business is conducted on behalf of the City.
Internal Controls: The City Treasurer shall establish awritten system of internal guidelines and
controls, which shallbe reviewed and approvedby the investment committee and the City's
independent auditor.The controls shall be designed to prevent the loss of public funds arising
fromfraud, employee error, misrepresentation bythird parties, unanticipated changes in financial
markets, orimprudent actions by the Investment Officer and participants in the investment process.
The guidelines and controls should include, but not belimited to:
·frequent reconciliation of investment and accounting records;
·
written confirmation of transactions;
·annual independent reviews by an external auditor to assure compliance with this policy;
·appropriate training for participants in the investment process; and
·a list of approved financial institutions, depositories, and broker/dealers (see below).
