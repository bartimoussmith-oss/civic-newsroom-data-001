RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk

March 3, 2022

The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne met in special session on this date beginning
at 6:00 p.m. at the Historic Depot, 121 W. 15th Street, and via electronic conference meeting, for
the purpose of collective labor bargaining with the Cheyenne Professional Fire Fighters,
International Association of Fire Fighters, Local No. 279.  Present were: MAYOR – Patrick
Collins, COUNCIL MEMBERS – Dr. Michelle Aldrich, Bryan Cook (arriving at 6:05 p.m.), Ken
Esquibel, Richard Johnson, Pete Laybourn (arriving at 6:05 p.m.), Dr. Mark Rinne (arriving at
6:05 p.m.), Scott Roybal, Tom Segrave and Jeff White.  Also present: Kylie Soden, Deputy City
Clerk; Stefanie Boster, City Attorney; Robin Lockman, City Treasurer, and Chief John Kopper,
Cheyenne Fire and Rescue.  The pledge of allegiance was recited.

Mayor Collins advised the meeting would be audio recorded and available on Facebook
live and stated that Wyoming State Statute §16-4-405(a)(x) of the Public Meetings Act allows the
governing body to meet in executive session to consider or tender offers regarding salaries, wages
or other terms and conditions of employment during negotiations with the fire fighters union and
that any motion approved by the governing body to move into executive session during the special
meetings would be pursuant to this section of statute.

Mayor Collins provided opening comments noting he has been meeting with the Fire
Department for labor management discussions which have resulted in a better understanding of
the functions of their department.  Mayor Collins expressed appreciation to Chief John Kopper for
the work he does with the Union and his representation of the Fire Department.  The proposed
Ground Rules were agreed upon and signed by the City and Union. [Clerk’s note: City’s
Spokespersons are: Mayor Patrick Collins, City Attorney Stefanie Boster and City Council
President Scott Roybal.  Union’s Spokespersons are: Bill Hinz, Chris Thoms and Vincent
McQueeney.]

Following signing of the Ground Rules by Mayor Collins and Union Spokesperson Chris
Thoms, the proposed Collective Bargaining Agreement was exchanged between the parties.
Mayor Collins briefly reviewed the City’s proposal which included changes to the following areas
and Sections: update cover page; update table of contents; Section 2, Definitions - strikethrough
on-call language and shift trainer, add Specialty Team definition; Section 4, Terms of Agreement
– update date of contract; Section 8, Grievances – strikethrough Division Chief language, remove
Operations, Training, and Prevention; Section 13, Management Rights – add #10 and #11
pertaining to the City’s right to establish and determine specialized emergency response teams and
establish job descriptions; Section 15, Staffing – under Company Strength, update equipment
dispatched to structural fires to sixteen members without an aerial truck and seventeen members
with an aerial ladder truck; Section 21, Classification of Employment – strikethrough on-call
Prevention language and update Day Personnel’s Specialty pay; Section 22, Overtime – add Fire
Chief language to monitor and add overtime conversion for Day Personnel; Section 24, Specialized
