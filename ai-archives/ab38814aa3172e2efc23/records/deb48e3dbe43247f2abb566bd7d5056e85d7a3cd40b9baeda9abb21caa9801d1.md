▪The Reed Avenue Corridor improvements were first
proposed in the West Edge Visionary Blueprint and
later codified in the West Edge Area-Wide Plan to
support economic redevelopment of Cheyenne’s west
side.
▪In 2018, the Cheyenne MPO developed the Reed
Avenue Corridor Plan, incorporating extensive public
input and partially funded by the 6th Penny Sales Tax.
▪Over a four-year period, the West Edge planning and
projects attracted $17 million in external state and
federal funding through a strategic grant approach.
▪For every $1 of public investment, the West Edge has
attracted $6 in private investment, demonstrating
strong confidence in the area’s revitalization and robust
return on public investment.

Why Reed Avenue Corridor - 6th Penny Project
Proposal?
▪Transform Reed Avenue into a safe, accessible, and
vibrant corridor.
▪Aligns with City of Cheyenne’s long-term
infrastructure, community, and council goals.
▪Fulfills 2017 Voter Commitments
▪Advances the City’s original promise to complete the Reed
Avenue Corridor, as outlined in the 6th Penny ballot initiative.
▪Demonstrates accountability and follow-through on publicly
supported infrastructure priorities.
▪Supports Private Investment & Economic Growth
▪Enhances infrastructure to unlock adjacent private development
potential.
▪Improves access, safety, and aesthetics — key factors in attracting
and retaining businesses.
▪Addresses Critical Infrastructure Needs
▪Resolves long-standing stormwater and utility issues.
▪Improves multimodal access and public safety for pedestrians,
cyclists, and vehicles.
▪Total Estimated Cost:  $14.9 M (2025 Dollars)
▪Based on 30% Concept Design Plans
▪20% Contingency
▪Design uncertainty,
▪Market volatility,
▪Unforeseen conditions,
▪Inflation
