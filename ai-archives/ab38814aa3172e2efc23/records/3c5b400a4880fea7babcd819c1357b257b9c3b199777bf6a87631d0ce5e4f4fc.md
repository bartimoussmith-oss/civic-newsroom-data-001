Special Provisions.
A.
State Parks and the City shall develop along-term lease for the property, which will

use and management of the site, length of the lease, termination clauses, external
partnerships, and other general provisions.
B.
Uponpassage of legislation designating the Site as a State Historic Site, the City
shall introduce, through City Council, a measure to amend thelong-term lease of
the Sitewith the United States Department of Agriculture-Agricultural Research
Service (USDA-ARS). This request shall include the return of sections of property
identified by the survey as well as buildings identified by the USDA.Such a
request would need to be referred to committee, and pass the voting process, and
will reference the formal land survey and the transfer of several historic buildings.
C.
Upon passage of a lease modification referenced above in Section 7.B, the City
shall approach USDA-ARS with the proposed lease modifications, beginning the
process to return land and assets to the Cityfor inclusion into along-term lease with
State Parks.
