MEMO

To: Honorable Mayor Patrick Collins and Members of the City Council
From: Jeanie Shrednik, PLA, Parks and Greenway Planner
Subject: Cooperative Agreement between the City of Cheyenne and WDEQ for a FY2024 Clean
Water Act, Section 319, Non-Point Source Implementation Grant to assist in construction of the
Kiwanis Park Trail and Constructed Wetlands Project.  (FY2024 CWA Section 319), Amount to
the City $ 112,904.00.

Date: February 18, 2025

Background:

This project will include construction of water quality wetlands, gravel trails and installation of
educational signage within the City owned Kiwanis Community Park site. The intent is to
provide a water quality wetlands feature along a portion of Dry Creek.  Dry Creek is a tributary
of the Crow Creek Water Shed. The project will add to existing wetlands and open water on the
site, provide gravel trails for the public to view nature and wildlife and educate the public on the
many functions of wetlands and their effect on both water quality and wildlife habitat.

The project has been supported by and will be matched with funding from the following;

Kiwanis Club of Cheyenne $80,000.00
Cheyenne High Plains Audubon Society $2,500.00
Cheyenne Greenway Foundation $3,000.00
Women’s Civic League of Cheyenne $800.00
Laramie County Conservation District $1,000.00
City of Cheyenne 6th Penny for Kiwanis Park Purchase and Improvements $185,000.00

The current funding in the Kiwanis Park 6th Penny account is approximately $ 500,000.00

Enclosures:
