ORDINANCE NO. ________

ENTITLED: "AN ORDINANCE AMENDING CHAPTER 8.68, CAMPING ON PUBLIC
PROPERTY, OF THE MUNICIPAL CODE OF THE CITY OF
CHEYENNE, WYOMING, EXPANDING THE GENERAL PROHIBITION
ON CAMPING TO ALL PUBLIC PROPERTY OF THE CITY, CREATING
A PERMITTING PROCESS, MAKING CONFORMING AMENDMENTS
AND REPEALING OBSOLETE PROVISIONS."

BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE,
WYOMING:

Section 1.  That Section 8.68.025, Sleeping on Sidewalks, Streets, Alleys or Within
Doorways Prohibited, and Section 8.68.035, Camping Allowed with Lawful Permit; Permit
Administration, of Chapter 8.68, Camping on Public Property, of Title 8, Health and Safety, of the
City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, are hereby created to read:

8.68.025 - Sleeping on sidewalks, streets, alleys or within doorways prohibited.

A. No person shall sleep on any public sidewalk, street or alleyway at any time as a matter of
individual and public safety.

B. No person may sleep in any pedestrian or vehicular entrance to public or private property
abutting a public sidewalk.

C. Any person who violates this section is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of
fifty dollars ($50.00). Any person found in violation of this section may also be
immediately removed from the premises. Upon conviction for a violation of this section, if
a defendant demonstrates that after receiving the citation and before the hearing, they
meaningfully engaged with public or private resources or service providers to address the
reasons that led them to be in violation, the court shall consider that information when
determining the appropriate sentence.

8.68.035 - Camping allowed with lawful permit; permit administration.

A. Notwithstanding the prohibitions imposed under Section 8.68.030 of this chapter, a person
may camp on public property of the city upon the issuance of a camping permit by the city
clerk and payment of the associated permit fee as provided by this section. All applications
for a camping permit shall be submitted to the city clerk on forms provided by the clerk
and shall be submitted not less than ten (10) business days before the date the camping will
occur. No permit may be issued under this section except upon the approval of the clerk
and the director of the community recreation and events department.

B. Before the issuance of any camping permit under this section, the director of the
community recreation and events department shall:

Section 2.  That Chapter 8.68, Camping on Public Property, of Title 8, Health and Safety,
of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming, is hereby amended to read:

8.68.010 - Legislative findings and intent.

A. It is found and declared that:

1. The act of camping on public property tends to endanger the health and safety of those
engaged in such camping as well as the public at large;

2. Campsites positioned on public property of the city of Cheyenne, including on
undeveloped parcels, public rights-of-way, under bridges and on or near sidewalks, also
undermine the public's ability to use that property for its intended purpose;

3. Camping on public property also creates unsafe and unsanitary living conditions which
pose a threat to the peace, health and safety of the people who are camping and the
community;

4. Crow Creek serves as a vital component of the drainage network of the city of
Cheyenne, offers valuable habitat for local flora and fauna and the creek's health
requires special attention;

5. 4. Some individuals have come to increasingly rely on public property adjacent to
Crow Creek for the purpose of camping and the establishment of campsites creates
significant hazards for Cheyenne, including damaging water quality through the
introduction of trash, human waste and other materials;
