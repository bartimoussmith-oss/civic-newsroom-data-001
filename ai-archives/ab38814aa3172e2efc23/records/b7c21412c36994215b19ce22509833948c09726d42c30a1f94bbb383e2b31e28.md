RESOLUTION NO.  _________________________________

ENTITLED: “A RESOLUTION APPROVING THE CITY OF CHEYENNE’S 2013 - 2014
ANNUAL ACTION PLAN PREPARED BY THE CHEYENNE HOUSING
AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT OFFICE.”

WHEREAS, the Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) provides
funding to communities to address housing and urban development problems; and

WHEREAS, the City of Cheyenne has been designated by HUD as an Entitlement
Community, and is eligible to receive an annual allocation of CDBG (Community Development
Block Grant) funds based on the Comprehensive Consolidated Community Development Plan
which was adopted by the Governing Body on May 10, 2010; and

WHEREAS, the 2013 - 2014 Annual Action Plan has been developed in compliance with
the City’s Citizen Participation Plan, which requires citizen input and two public hearings
including review and recommendations by the Housing and Community Development Office
Advisory Council.

NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the City of Cheyenne’s 2013 - 2014 Annual Action
Plan is approved.  A copy of the 2013 - 2014 Annual Action Plan is attached to this resolution.

BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Governing Body, Mayor, City Clerk are
authorized to submit the 2013 - 2014 Annual Action Plan, along with certificates, and execute all
HUD contracts and third-party agreements with operating and sponsoring agents necessary to
implement and accomplish the 2013 - 2014 Annual Action Plan.

PRESENTED, READ AND ADOPTED ON THIS _______ DAY OF _____________,
2013.

______________________________

Richard L. Kaysen, Mayor

(SEAL)
ATTEST:

______________________________________
Carol A. Intlekofer, City Clerk
