10.60, Parking Limitations, of Title 10, Vehicles and Traffic, of the City of Cheyenne, Wyoming,
are hereby amended to read:

10.16.150 - Parking violation hearings and appeal administrative review procedures.

A. Civil actions to enforce this chapter may be adjudicated by an administrative hearing
officer actively licensed to practice law in the State of Wyoming designated by the mayor.

A. Any person liable for a parking citation may request administrative review of the citation
by contacting the police department parking administration manager in person, by
telephone or mail not more than fifteen (15) days after the date of the issuance of the
citation. The request shall specify the citation for which the person seeks review and
provide the reasons for seeking administrative review. Under this section, a request for
review made by telephone shall be considered received as of the day it was made if received
by a police department employee between the hours of 8:00 a.m. and 5:00 p.m. of any day,
except on Saturday, Sunday or a city holiday.
