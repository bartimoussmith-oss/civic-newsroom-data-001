______________________________________________
Bid #E-14-22 was solicited for furnishing Two (2) New 2022 4WD 72” Front-Mount Mowers, for
the City of Cheyenne, Community Recreation & Events Department- Parks Division.

Two (2) bidders responded to this bid, and the bid tabulation is attached.

The Bids were received from:
1. LL Johnson Distributing Company.  Denver, CO.

2. Heartland Kubota.  Sheridan, WY.

The bids were reviewed by Sam Payne (Parks), and Jason Sanchez (CRE).  Their recommendation
is to accept the lowest responsive bid from Heartland Kubota, for the purchase of Two (2) New
2022 4WD 72” Front-Mount Mowers.  The total cost, less Trade Ins, is $74,350.00.

Additional Background Information:
The Governing Body approved Bid E-1-22 on August 23, 2021, to Heartland Kubota for the
purchase of Two (2) New 2022 4WD 72” Front-Mount Mowers, for the City of Cheyenne,
Community Recreation & Events Department- Parks Division.  The Kubota Tractor Corporation
ceased production of the awarded model due to problems with the operation of the DPF (Diesel
Particulate Filter) system on the F3990 series mowers.  As Heartland Kubota was unable to fulfill
this order due to unavoidable problems with manufacturing, the City cancelled the awarded of Bid
E-1-22, and resolicited under this Bid E-14-22.

Funding:

(Specific Purpose Option Tax 2012 & 2017 Ballots / Greenway Maintenance)
