Planning Commission
Minutes of the Proceedings - Final
January 23, 2025
The current use is vacant residential land and is situated in the LU Zone District. Tracts 1
and 2 will be accessed via a shared easement off Road 143 and Tracts 3 and 4 would do
the same. The existing private access easement would continue to serve the remaining lot
to the east as well as the neighbors to the south and east.
Agency comments are being addressed and the exhibit updated to reflect any changes.
Staff recommends approval to the Laramie County Board of Commissioners with two
conditions.
1. The plat is revised to make access spacing off Road 143 compliant with the LCLUR.
2. The plat is revised to move the ROW dedication to the north border rather than the
south.
Vice Chairman Watkins opened the Public Hearing. Comments were heard from the
following:
*  Amy Lounsbury at 1671 Road 143 has concerns on water runoff and possible road
damage.
*  Casey Epler at 1856 Road 143 has concerns that the existing culverts will not be able to
handle the additional    runoff and impede the improvements being made to Lodgepole
Creek.
*  Brad Waufle at 1683 Road 143 wants to know what the impacts will be to the existing
road and make needed improvements to the private easements and that the new roads
would be designed by an engineer.
Shane Hansen, agent for the applicant, responded to questions from the Commission
regarding additional culverts added to the road design and drainage concerns from
neighbors.
Hearing no further comments the meeting was closed and a motion and discussion called
for.
Jack Stadel moved, seconded by Johnny J. Tafoya, II moved to adopt
findings of fact a, b, and c of the staff report and to recommend approval
of the Subdivision Permit and Plat for Pee-Wee Ponderosa Subdivision
with the two conditions outlined in the staff report. Vote 4-0.
Stadel, Watkins, Gaspar, Tafoya II
Aye:
Caughey
Absent:
Adjournment
There being no further business the meeting was adjourned at 4:02 p.m.
CERTIFICATION
