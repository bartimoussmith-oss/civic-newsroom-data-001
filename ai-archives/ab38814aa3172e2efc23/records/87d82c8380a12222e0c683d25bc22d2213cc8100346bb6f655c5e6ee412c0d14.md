WHEREAS, Wyo.Stat.Ann. 39-15-204(a)(ii) allows counties in Wyoming to impose an
approval of qualified electors in accordance with Wyo.Stat.Ann.$ 39-15-203(a)(i)(3); and
WHEREAS, the 1% Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee has proposed that an optional one
percent (1%) sales tax be imposed for specific purposes authorized by the qualified electors of
Laramie County, Wyoming; and
WHEREAS, the specific projects itemized in this resolution will enhance the ability of the
City of Cheyenne to discharge core functions including, fire protection, drainage control, safe and
efficient streets, recreation and economic development; and
WHEREAS, the Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne has adopted the following non-
prioritized list of preferred one percent (1%) specific purpose projects for consideration by the 1%
Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee.
NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVEDBY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE
CITY OF CHEYENNE, WYOMING, that the following non-prioritized list of specific projects be
adopted and presented to the 1%Specific Purpose Sales Tax Committee for its consideration and
electors of Laramie County during the May 2,2017 special election:
1.
Belvoir Ranch-Big Hole Trail Development Project: projects supporting public access
supporting infrastructure in the amount of Two Million Five Hundred Thousand
Dollars ($2,500,000.00).
2.
Railroad; associated drainage work; and installation of a new water main in the
amount of Seven Million Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($7,500,000.00).
3.
Downtown Core Plan Project: to modernize and improve two alleys(1.north of the
Hynds Building, and 2.north of the Plains Hotel) and a depot plaza spray park in the
amount of Two Million Three Hundred Fifty Thousand Dollars ($2,350,000.00).
4.
East Park Land Project: to purchase and preserve land for a future community park
