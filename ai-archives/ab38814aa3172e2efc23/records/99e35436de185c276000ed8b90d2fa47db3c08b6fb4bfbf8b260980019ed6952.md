RECORD OF PROCEEDINGS FOR THE GOVERNING BODY
OF THE CITY OF CHEYENNE
Office of City Clerk
January 7, 2013
The Governing Body of the City of Cheyenne assembled for its First Regular Meeting for
2013 on this date at 3:00 p.m. in the Cheyenne Civic Center.  Purpose of the First Regular Meeting
was to recognize and administer oaths of office to those individuals who were elected, or re-elected,
at the most recent general election to serve as members of the governing body, as well as provide for
certain administrative functions.  Rod Hottle provided introductory comments and served as master
of ceremonies prior to the meeting being officially convened.
Father August Koeune, Pastor, St. Mary’s Cathedral, delivered the invocation.
The Honorable Michael K. Davis, Justice of the Wyoming Supreme Court, administered the
oath of elected office to Richard L. Kaysen as Mayor,  followed by administration of oath of elected
office for the following newly elected and re-elected City Council members:  Jimmy Valdez and
Annette Williams, Ward I; Bryan M. Cook and Dicky Shanor, Ward II, and Jim Brown and Mike
Luna, Ward III.
Mayor Kaysen officially called the First Regular Meeting to order at 3:10 p.m.  Those present
were: MAYOR - Richard L. Kaysen; COUNCIL MEMBERS - Mr. Allen, Mr. Brown, Ms. Broyles,
Mr. Cook, Mr. Luna, Dr. Rinne, Mr. Shanor, Mr. Valdez and Ms. Williams.  Also present were Carol
Intlekofer, City Clerk, and Dan White, City Attorney.  Following the pledge of allegiance, led by
members of the Mayor’s Youth Council, Mayor Kaysen provided welcoming comments and
remarked on the community role of the governing body and future financial challenges due to the
economy.  He expressed appreciation to those individuals in the community who serve as elected
public officials; to City staff who assisted with the “Sine Die” and current meeting arrangements;
to Justice Davis, Father Koeune, Mr. Hottle, and members of the Mayor’s Youth Council and
Cheyenne Public Employees Association who participated and assisted with the meeting and
reception, to the families of governing body members for their support, and to Wyoming citizens
serving in the military.
Election of City Council President for 2013.  Mayor Kaysen announced, pursuant to City
Code, he would be conducting the election but would not be participating in the vote.  Ms. Broyles
moved to nominate and elect Dr. Rinne as City Council President for 2013, seconded by Mr. Brown.
Mr. Brown moved that nominations cease, seconded by Mr. Valdez.  Motion to cease nominations
carried.  Voting “yes” – all members of the governing body with the exception of Mayor Kaysen who
did not vote.  Motion to nominate and elect Dr. Rinne carried.  Voting “yes”-- all members of the
governing body with the exception of Mayor Kaysen who did not vote.
Election of City Council Vice President for 2013.  Mayor Kaysen announced, pursuant to
City Code, he would be conducting the election but would not be participating in the vote.  Dr. Rinne
moved to nominate and elect Ms. Broyles as City Council Vice President for 2013, seconded by Mr.
Allen.  Mr. Brown moved that nominations cease, seconded by Mr. Cook.  Motion to cease
